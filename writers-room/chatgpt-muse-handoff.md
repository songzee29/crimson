## Locked canon guardrails

### Zee, origin, and power
- Zee Darkheart's title is **First Natural Tribrid**, not Quadbrid.
- Witch, Vampire, and Wolf natures are awake from birth. Dormant Dragon blood is inherited from Aurelia.
- The Convergence Vault says **“Four”** and **“Three awake”** without destiny/prophecy framing.
- Victor's single attempt to kill newborn Zee already happened. Zee returned. Do not add repeated kill attempts or a recurring death/resurrection cycle.
- There is one prophecy, privately known to Victor. Victor is King Victor Darkheart, not Alaric.
- Aurelia Darkheart is Zee's mother, protector, and safe place.
- Crimson magic belongs to Zee, not House Darkheart. Zee absorbs and evolves into Crimson Evolutions; he does not copy other people's powers.
- Zee's time/age power can alter a living person's biological age only. It cannot rewrite history, undo events, reverse death, or prevent Aurelia's eventual death.
- At age ten, Zee's Dragon signs include crimson-black horns, permanent Crimson scales that spread with emotion/danger/combat, and Dragon energy. No full Dragon transformation or adult wings at that stage.
- Zareth's Teal-and-Gold Dragon Mana / Dragon Link catalyzes Zee's dormant Dragon blood but does not supply it.

### Family, ages, and relationships
- Zee and Zareth share a birthday and are the same age. Zareth Drakonis is the true Dragon Prince of House Drakonis, not secretly Dragon–Witch before transformation. At age 21, he can shift between Dragon and human while retaining his powers.
- Ner Azrion uses he/him, has Pure Gold Mana, and is Zee's lover. No Nymeris romance or jealousy triangle.
- Ner is a father and carries/births Zayrlen and Azren; Ner is not Zee's son.
- Zee has exactly three sons:
  1. **Aidan Seyrion Darkheart** — mother Queen Nymeris Thalryn.
  2. **Zayrlen Keal Darkheart** — son of Zee and Ner; Dragon–Witch–Wolf, crimson-gold eyes, crimson/gold Wolf form, true Dragon form.
  3. **Azren Neyron Darkheart** — son of Zee and Ner; Dragon–Witch–Vampire, Ner's exact Pure Gold Witch Mana; true Dragon form remains TBD.
- Nymeris Thalryn, Queen of Nhalaris, and Zee are chosen family, not romantic partners. Her Ocean/Abyss/hereditary Siren blood comes through water, deep pressure, and resonance and is distinct from Ner's Bloodsong.
- Aidan's maternal Ocean/Abyss/Siren nature is unknown/unbound; when Zee hid Aidan in Vesper, he sealed only Witch/Vampire/Wolf. Rez alone besides Zee knows Aidan's parentage.
- Rez is Zee's best friend and Aidan's uncle, not Zee's son. The earlier age-adjustment changed Rez's living biological age from 17 to 10 while retaining memories, training, and personality; treat him as living age ten afterward, not “effectively older.”
- The absolute no-target boundary includes Ner, Rez, Zareth, Kael, Tessa, Amara, family, and close friends.
- After Aidan is hidden, Zee acts against outsiders who threaten his people, not random outsiders or his own people.
