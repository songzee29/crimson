## Locked character, lineage, and relationship canon

### Zee Darkheart
- Correct title: **First Natural Tribrid**, not Quadbrid.
- Witch, Vampire, and Wolf natures are naturally active from birth. Dragon blood inherited from Aurelia is a fourth natural blood nature, initially dormant.
- The Convergence Vault says “Four” and “Three awake”; this is not a prophecy or destiny framing.
- Zee's Crimson magic belongs to Zee, not House Darkheart.
- Zee does not copy other people's powers. He absorbs/evolves into Crimson Evolutions.
- After Victor's single newborn kill attempt, Zee returned and cannot permanently die. Do not repeat the kill attempt or establish a recurring death/resurrection cycle.
- Time/age power changes the biological age of a living person only. It cannot rewrite history, undo events, reverse death, or prevent Aurelia's eventual death.
- Around age 10, Zee develops crimson-black horns, permanent Crimson scales that spread under emotion/danger/combat, and Dragon energy. At this stage he does not have a full Dragon transformation or adult wings.
- Zee's protected circle / absolute no-target boundary includes Ner, Rez, Zareth, Kael, Tessa, Amara, family, and close friends. In his darker ruler phase he targets outsiders who threaten his people, not random outsiders or his own people.

### Victor and Aurelia Darkheart
- The king is **Victor Darkheart**, not Alaric.
- Aurelia Darkheart is Zee's mother, protector, and safe place.
- Victor initially behaves fatherly, then becomes afraid after a private prophecy known only to him.
- There is one prophecy. Victor's attempt to kill newborn Zee happened once and must not be repeated.
- A later Victor return / apparent-death thread and his testing of Zee through a Zerlin scenario are current plotting ideas; preserve the original single newborn kill event.

### Zareth Drakonis
- Zareth is the missing Dragon Prince who appeared beside newborn Zee on the same birthday. He is not one of Zee's sons.
- Zareth is a true Dragon of House Drakonis, not secretly Dragon–Witch before transformation.
- His Dragon Mana / Link is Teal-and-Gold. It catalyzes Zee's dormant Dragon blood but does not give Zee Dragon blood.
- At age 21, Zareth can shift between Dragon and human forms while retaining powers. Later Phoenix–Dragon–Witch Tribrid canon retains both Dragon and human forms.

### Ner Azrion and Rez
- Ner uses he/him pronouns, has Pure Gold Mana, and is Zee's lover. There is no Nymeris jealousy or love triangle.
- Ner is the biological father and gestational/birth parent of Zee and Ner's two sons, Zayrlen and Azren.
- Rez is Zee's best friend and Aidan's uncle, not Zee's son.
- At the Ch. 24 snapshot, Zee, Zareth, and Ner are 10 while Rez is 17. Later, Zee's unstable wish-response to Rez wanting to stay with his best friend changes Rez's biological age from 17 to 10 while preserving memories, training, and personality. Canon should treat Rez as living at biological age 10 afterward, not as “effectively older.”
- Ner is older than Zee and Zareth in later canon.

### Aurelia Veyrath and related Houses
- Aurelia Veyrath / House Veyrath is distinct from House Drakonis. Do not rename Veyrath to Vaelorian.
- Aurelia Veyrath is a true Dragon–Witch hybrid with a true Dragon form; Lucien did not know.
- Sevran is Aurelia's cousin. Theron is her older brother; another character named Theron is separate.
- Khandra Parbat replaced Nanda Parbat.
- The League of the Black Order replaces the former assassin organization.
- The Godslayer era begins at Chapter 94; Cassel Rhune is Zee's first true Godslayer encounter.
- Zee's formal term is **Black Divinity**; “Dark Divinity” is not the formal name.
