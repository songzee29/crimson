## Zee Darkheart — nature, powers, and limits

- Correct title: **First Natural Tribrid**, not Quadbrid.
- Witch, Vampire, and Wolf natures are naturally awake from birth. Dragon blood is inherited from Aurelia but initially dormant, making it a fourth blood nature, not a fourth awake nature.
- The Convergence Vault says “Four” and “Three awake.” Do not frame that as destiny or prophecy.
- Zee's Crimson magic belongs to Zee; it is not a House Darkheart power.
- Zee does not copy other people's powers. He absorbs and evolves into Crimson Evolutions.
- Zee cannot permanently die after Victor's single newborn kill attempt. Victor already killed Zee once and Zee returned. There must not be repeated kill attempts or a recurring death/resurrection cycle.
- Time/age power can alter a living person's biological age only. It cannot change history, undo events, reverse death, or reverse Aurelia's eventual death.
- At age 10, Zee develops crimson-black horns, permanent Crimson scales that spread with emotion/danger/combat, and Dragon energy. He does not yet have full Dragon transformation or adult wings.
- Zareth's Teal-and-Gold Dragon Mana and Dragon Link catalyze Zee's dormant Dragon blood; they do not give Zee Dragon blood.
- The later true unified species reveal is **Abysscin**: Zee's true nature is revealed when he fully transforms into his Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix true Phoenix form. At that on-page reveal, the story establishes Abysscin as what Zee has become, rather than merely a collection of accumulated Witch/Vampire/Wolf/Dragon/Phoenix traits.
- “Dark Divinity” is the formal concept; “Black Divinity” is Zee's own term.
- Zee's no-target boundary is absolute for Ner, Rez, Zareth, Kael, Tessa, Amara, family, and close friends.
- In his later dark ruler phase, after Aidan is hidden, Zee acts against outsiders who threaten his people, but does not target random outsiders or his own people.
