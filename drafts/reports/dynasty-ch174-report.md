## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes
"Nine is the nursery's name for what they counted. I don't have another yet." / "Then I'll call you Nine until you do. The leaving kind." — this exchange is the emotional peak of the whole Gate School arc, and it earns it. The "uncounted" answer to the clerk's ledger is a perfect refusal — the master of the count refusing the one thing he was made for — and Tessa writing the refusal into the ledger ("the house files everything, even refusals") is a wonderful beat of paper-as-weapon. A name, even withheld, would make the stakes more personal.
