## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

Zee's standing-by-the-door sequence reads like weather, and the final paragraph — the walls "keeping his childhood's price for him, and keeping it diligently, the way good servants keep everything they're given, whether or not the master asked" — lands the theme of the whole arc: the file doesn't forget. He's the hand that holds the blood and the chalk, and the chapter treats him as functionary where the other scenes treat him as person. Pacing is tight throughout; the dread escalates (toll → knock → cord → the walls holding the note) without repeating itself.
