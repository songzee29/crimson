## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes
Nine's three-line refusal is the chapter's heart, and the signature under it — "Nine. The leaving kind." — is the kind of line that makes a whole character arc land in one breath. The girl's answer to the debt ("They can't collect children who know how to leave. You taught us the leaving. Teach it to the paper too.") earns its chill because the chapter has shown us the leaving as a taught thing, not a metaphor. One thing I'd want more of: the war-marshal's own position. Pacing is excellent; the bench scenes breathe, and Zee's dusk entrance reframes the whole chapter from a bureaucratic defeat into a deliberate play — the reveal that he routed the requisition on purpose is perfectly timed.
