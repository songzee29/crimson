## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

Chilling, in the best way. The central concept — the market can't collect Rez's death, so it collects the *standing*, and the bidding curdles into *veneration* — is the sharpest idea in the whole batch: "owning the one death the market couldn't collect was the closest the file had ever come to immortality." Victor's bid landing from the letter-box, "like a second heartbeat," is a genuine gut-punch, and "The cradle-night king. The Crimson Assassin. On the same page, by law, once a year, forever." is a dread that will linger. Keeping Rez a line item — nobody speaks *to* him, only *about* him — is ice-cold and exactly right; the one thing I'd want more of is a single crack in it, one flicker where the asset is a man, because the chapter earns it by withholding it. The auction-house pacing is deliberate and never drags.
