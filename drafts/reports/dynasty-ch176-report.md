## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes
This is a quiet, devastating chapter — the best kind of dread is procedural, and "Sign them, and read each name aloud before the pen moves. Let the paper hear what it's taking" is a line that will stay with readers. The girl's certificate carrying a *replacement* name, not a misspelling — "a name for a child who had never existed" — is the chapter's sharpest beat; it turns the whole intake system from carelessness into design. The closing image, the two rolls side by side ("the numbers matching, the names refusing to"), is a perfect thesis for the whole arc: the certified lie against the unshowable truth. One thing I'd want more of: Zee's absence. The chapter hangs the question of whether the King is told — I'd like one beat earlier in the chapter (Nine or Tessa deciding *not* to carry this upstairs) so the final question feels like a decision, not an afterthought.
