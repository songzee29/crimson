## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

This is the most emotionally grounded chapter of the six, and the one I'll remember longest. Ner holding his own drawer against the King — "That drawer stays mine" — and Zee, who kills men for less than a look, nodding *once*: that's the whole marriage in one gesture. "The silence... between us." / "The keeping of it." is beautiful, and the boys' voices coming through the nursery door like "tide through a cracked hull" gives the domestic stakes a physical ache. The dread Zee voices — "the file will never learn" what one mother proved with a question about dancing — is the paper war's thesis in miniature. What confused me: the "swept a ruin" phrase and the courted/kept ambiguity (see Pass 1) pulled me out for a beat. One thing I'd want more of: Ner's hand in the letter itself — we hear *about* "dances badly, with everything in it," but one quoted line of the mother's hand would make the drawer feel real.
