## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

**What landed:** The opening arithmetic — "not as a feeling but as an arithmetic" — is the best sentence in the batch so far; it turns hunger into bookkeeping, which is scarier than fangs. "He had taught it where the hinges were" is the chapter's thesis and it earns the title. The Ner exchange is the emotional center: Zee *asking* rather than telling is new, and Ner's counter-condition ("Rez stays") is a perfect one-line character beat that keeps his agency intact. Rez's closing read on Victor ("A man who'll do that can't be starved on command") is the kind of line that reframes the whole arc — it makes the self-harm legible as defiance, not despair. The bread-and-kitchen ending after all that darkness is a genuine exhale; the contrast does the work.

**What confused:** The kill-count arithmetic — "seven men dead" against eight named conspirators took a second pass to resolve (Sera Quill presumably the eighth non-"man," but neither bible genders Sera or Pell, so a reader can't be sure). A cleaner count or one named beat would land the "list is done" premise faster. Also: Rez asks "How many nights?" and Zee answers "One... This was the first" — but the prose earlier says "the way hours went in the blackout nights" (plural), implying earlier blackout nights. A reader may trip on whether this is the first blackout or the first *self-feeding* night. (Context supports the latter; a half-line of clarity would fix it.)

**Pacing:** Clean three-act shape — setup (the list, the arithmetic), the bite, the missing hour — and it never stalls. The self-feeding sequence is long enough to hurt and short enough not to wallow.

**One thing I'd want more of:** The missing hour is the hook that will carry readers into the next chapter, and right now it's pure dread with zero shape. One more sensory breadcrumb — a smell besides ash, a sound — would make the unopened door itch harder without explaining it.

**No editing offered** — this is a read, not a rewrite.
