## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

This chapter's emotional engine is the mercy kill, and it lands. The priest breaking sanctuary *from the inside* is the strongest beat in the chapter — one action that tells you everything about what the Order has done to this city. The bell striking wrong and staying wrong is the perfect haunting aftermath: the city sanctifying a thing Zee hates being forgiven for. That's the R-rated moral murk this series runs on, and Ink is swimming in it.

Pacing is tight — the chapel sequence never stalls, and "seven" as a refrain gives the kill a ledger-like coldness that fits a revenge arc.

What confused me: the opening counts six buried and tonight seven, but never names the six on the page — a reader holding the locked order in mind will do the math and wonder why Pell isn't among the dead (he's the accountant, still alive). That's a continuity gap, not a prose gap, but it reads as a gap either way.

One thing I'd want more of: Senn himself. Right now he's a vial, a grinding voice, and a thank-you — the horror of the marrow is doing all the emotional work. Even one line of who Senn was before the Order made him desperate would make Zee's kindness cost him more.

No editing offered — just the read.

## Pass 3 — Proofread fixes

- "Zee picked it up and crushed it in his fist, and his blood — crimson-black, steaming — took the glass dust and the last of the marrow **both**, and the embers breathed" — "both" is doing nothing; cut it: "took the glass dust and the last of the marrow".

- "and underneath it the embers breathed — in and out, in and out — and the window that was off its hinges **stood open on the night**" — "open on the night" is an unusual construction; "open to the night" reads cleaner.
- "the hunger coiled down, sated and patient" — "coiled down" is slightly off from the earlier established "the hunger coiled"; consider "the hunger coiled, sated and patient" for consistency.
- Dialogue formatting otherwise clean: punctuation inside quotes throughout ("King," "Please." "Don't,"). The repeated bare "Don't" between priest and Zee is an intentional echo, not an error.
