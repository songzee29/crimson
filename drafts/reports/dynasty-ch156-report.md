## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

What landed: the tourniquet. A man waking on a roof with blood on his mouth and finding he'd tied a bandage around his own arm mid-blackout — some part of him "doing triage even through the blackout" — is the single best image in the batch so far. It says everything about Zee's core without a speech. The second best beat is Zee actually telling Ner everything, unprompted, blood still on his hands — that confession is where the character's ruthless/protective contrast earns its weight.

Ner's "no third choice" speech is strong and earned — "You don't get to decide what I'm allowed to survive, Zee. That's mine." It lands harder because Rez's earlier "He'll hate that" / "He'll live through it" set the fuse. This is the Ch 46–49 agency theme paying off on the page.

What confused me: the chapter wants it both ways on the blackout's precision — Zee locked himself away fearing random slaughter, yet the hunger beelined to Vane's exact den. If the starving thing can read sealed ledgers by smell, nothing in this story stays hidden from Zee ever again, and that's a problem the draft doesn't yet feel. Also: "The eight are dead" is stated flatly by Zee, but Pell is alive offstage delivering ledgers — so the "eight" can't all be dead unless Pell doesn't count, which no one has established.

Pacing: taut. The roof-to-den-to-bedroom movement works; the scene never stalls. The feather coda is chilling precisely because it's quiet.

One thing I'd want more of: the roof wake-up itself. We get "Dawn. The Quarter below him, wet and gold" and immediately go down the fire escape. One more breath up there — the torn shirt strip, whose it might have been, Zee making himself look — would let the horror land before the den explains it.
