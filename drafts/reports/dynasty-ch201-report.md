## Pass 2 — Beta reader notes

The tide-glass concept is the star — "It breaks into *readings*" is a gorgeous premise, and the vocabulary-vs-grammar distinction ("the fragments keep the words. They refuse to keep the assembly") landed hard. The eleven-second kill sequence is genuinely frightening, and the third agent's laughing trap speech ("It needs *you to need it*") is the best beat in the chapter — the Order weaponizing Zee's own need for the record is deliciously cruel. Zee's refusal to bid at the end feels earned rather than passive. Pacing runs slow-burn into violence into dread, and it works. One thing I'd want more of: the tutor's voice — his "filing the absence" discipline is the chapter's intellectual spine, and I'd follow him anywhere.
