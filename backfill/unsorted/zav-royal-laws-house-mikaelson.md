# 🩸 THE ROYAL LAWS OF THE CRIMSON DYNASTY 🩸  
## *The Eternal Decrees of House Mikaelson*

---

# **LAW I — FAMILY ABOVE ALL**

No crown stands above blood.

No kingdom stands above family.

Any ruler of House Mikaelson who sacrifices family for power is no true king and shall lose claim to the Crimson Throne.

> *“Power fades. Blood remains.”*  
— Zee Mikaelson

---

# **LAW II — PROTECT THE WEAK**

The Crimson Dynasty was born from suffering.

Because of this, the dynasty shall never prey upon the innocent nor allow oppression beneath its rule.

Humans, witches, wolves, vampires, hybrids, and all supernatural beings shall have equal protection beneath crimson law.

Any noble who abuses power shall answer directly to the throne.

---

# **LAW III — LOYALTY IS SACRED**

Betrayal against House Mikaelson is the highest crime in existence.

Punishment for treason includes:
- exile from the kingdom
- removal of immortal status
- or judgment by the reigning Sovereign

However…

Mercy may be granted if redemption is earned through sacrifice and truth.

---

# **LAW IV — THE THRONE MUST BE EARNED**

No heir inherits the Crimson Crown through blood alone.

Every ruler must prove:
- strength
- wisdom
- emotional control
- devotion to the people

A weak ruler destroys kingdoms faster than war.

---

# **LAW V — MONSTERS CHOOSE WHAT THEY BECOME**

The dynasty rejects the belief that darkness defines destiny.

Every creature born cursed, feared, or broken still possesses the right to choose who they become.

This law was written personally by Zee after his ascension.

> *“We are not evil because we were hurt.”*

---

# **LAW VI — NEVER HIDE AGAIN**

The Crimson Dynasty shall never return to chains, secrecy, or fear.

The supernatural world has the right to exist openly beside humanity.

No king or queen may force the kingdom back into hiding.

---

# **LAW VII — THE BALANCE MUST HOLD**

Creation and destruction must coexist.

No ruler may pursue absolute domination over reality itself.

Even the Sovereign must answer to balance.

This law was added by Lucien after the War of Abyssion.

---

# **LAW VIII — CHILDREN OF THE DYNASTY ARE SACRED**

No child of House Mikaelson may be used as a weapon, political tool, or sacrifice.

The dynasty remembers too well what pain does to children.

Any violation of this law invokes immediate judgment from the throne.

---

# **LAW IX — THE STORM ALWAYS WATCHES**

The Crimson Storm surrounding New Orleans is not merely magic.

It is legacy itself.

A reminder that every ruler carries the weight of those who came before them.

Kings may fall.
Empires may burn.

But the dynasty endures.

---

# **LAW X — LOVE IS NOT WEAKNESS**

The final and most important law.

Written jointly by Zee and Lucien.

> *“The universe feared us because we survived.”*  
> *“But it respected us because we loved anyway.”*

No ruler of the Crimson Dynasty shall ever abandon their humanity in pursuit of greater power.

Because the moment love dies…

Monsters win.

---

# 👑 THE CRIMSON OATH 👑

Every royal heir must swear this oath before the throne:

> “I swear by blood and storm…  
> To protect this family.  
> To defend this kingdom.  
> To carry our pain without spreading it.  
> To rule with strength… but remain capable of love.  
> I am Crimson born.  
> And I shall endure.” 🩸
