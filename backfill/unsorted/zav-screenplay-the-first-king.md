```screenplay
                     THE CRIMSON DYNASTY
                      Episode I / Film I
                     “THE FIRST KING”

                           Written by
                         Zee Mikaelson


FADE IN:


EXT. NEW ORLEANS – NIGHT

Black clouds consume the sky.

Thunder shakes the city violently as crimson lightning tears through the heavens above modern-day New Orleans.

People stop in the streets.

Phones rise.

Fear spreads.

Because the storm above the city isn’t natural.

It’s watching.

A deep, distorted WHISPER echoes faintly through the wind:

                         WHISPER (V.O.)
               Blood remembers.


CUT TO:


EXT. ST. LOUIS CEMETERY – NIGHT

Rain pours endlessly over ancient graves.

The cemetery gates CREAK open slowly on their own.

Candles extinguish one by one.

A group of armed hunters carefully move through the darkness carrying silver weapons and enchanted flashlights.

Their leader, MARCUS REED (40s, hardened, scarred), grips a strange black dagger.

                         MARCUS
               Stay close.
               We get the artifact and leave.

Another hunter nervously looks around.

                         HUNTER
               You really think he’s real?

Marcus stops walking.

Thunder BOOMS overhead.

                         MARCUS
               Doesn’t matter.
               Tonight we kill the myth.


Suddenly—

Every flashlight flickers red.

The wind stops completely.

Silence.


Then...

FOOTSTEPS.


Slow.

Heavy.

Echoing through the cemetery.


The hunters turn toward the darkness between the tombs.

A tall figure slowly emerges beneath crimson lightning.

Black coat flowing.

Eyes glowing faint red.

Beautiful.

Terrifying.

ZEE MIKAELSON.


The storm itself seems alive around him.

One hunter panics instantly and raises a weapon.

                         HUNTER
               FIRE!


GUNSHOTS explode through the cemetery.

But the bullets stop in midair.

Frozen.

Floating inches from Zee’s face.

The hunters stare in horror.

Zee looks almost bored.

                         ZEE
               Silver bullets.
               Cute.


With a flick of his fingers—

The bullets reverse direction violently.

SCREAMS.

Bodies hit the ground instantly.


Marcus backs away trembling.

                         MARCUS
               What the hell are you?

Zee slowly walks closer.

Lightning illuminates ancient scars across his hands.

Pain hidden beneath immortality.

                         ZEE
               Depends who’s asking.

Marcus shakily raises the black dagger.

The blade glows with ancient symbols.

For the first time…

Zee pauses.


                         MARCUS
               They said this could kill you.

Zee stares at the dagger silently.

Then smirks.

Not amused.

Offended.

                         ZEE
               Everybody says that.


Inhuman speed.

Zee appears directly in front of Marcus.

Grabs the dagger effortlessly.

CRACK.

Marcus collapses screaming as his wrist snaps instantly.


Zee examines the weapon carefully.

Recognition flashes across his face.

Old memories.

Old fear.


                         ZEE (quietly)
               Where did you find this?

Marcus laughs weakly through pain.

                         MARCUS
               You should be afraid.
               They’re coming for your family.


The storm above the cemetery intensifies violently.

Zee’s eyes darken.

For one brief second—

Something monstrous moves beneath his skin.


                         ZEE
               Big mistake.


Suddenly the cemetery trembles.

Graves crack open.
Wind explodes outward.

The hunters panic.


And then—

A calm voice interrupts.


                         ELIAS (O.S.)
               Zee.


Everything stops instantly.


Zee turns.

Standing near the cemetery gates beneath an umbrella is ELIAS.

Elegant.
Calm.
The only person in the world completely unafraid of Zee.

The storm softens around him naturally.

Elias glances at the dead hunters.

                         ELIAS
               Again?

Zee shrugs slightly.

                         ZEE
               They started it.

Elias notices the dagger immediately.

His expression changes.

                         ELIAS
               …Where did they get that?

For the first time…

Zee looks genuinely concerned.


SMASH CUT TO:


INT. CRIMSON THRONE ROOM – NIGHT

Massive gothic doors BURST open.

Lucien turns toward the entrance sharply.

Rez sits lazily across the throne armrest drinking from a crystal glass.

Nyra studies glowing magical symbols nearby.

Kael sharpens a blade silently.


Zee enters holding the black dagger.

The room immediately feels colder.

                         LUCIEN
               Dad?

Zee tosses the dagger onto the throne steps.

Dark energy spreads from it instantly.

Everyone reacts.

Even Rez stops joking.


                         NYRA
               That’s impossible.

                         KAEL
               I thought those were destroyed.

Lucien looks toward Zee carefully.

                         LUCIEN
               What is it?

Zee stares at the weapon silently.

Then finally says:

                         ZEE
               A warning.


Lightning explodes outside the palace windows.

The crimson sky shakes violently.


And somewhere far beyond reality itself...

Something ancient opens its eyes.


CUT TO BLACK.


TITLE CARD:

              THE CRIMSON DYNASTY


COMING SOON.
```
