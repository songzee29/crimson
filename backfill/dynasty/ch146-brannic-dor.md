# Chapter One Hundred Forty-Six

## Brannic Dor

Brannic Dor knew Selk Varren was dead.

He had received the message before sunrise.

No explanation.

No detailed report.

Only three words written across a strip of black paper.

**SELK Varren. DEAD.**

Brannic had burned the message immediately.

Then he doubled the guards.

Tripled the perimeter.

Opened every old weapons case he had kept hidden since the operation.

For years, he had told himself that if Zee Darkheart ever came looking for him, he would be ready.

He had believed that because preparation had once worked.

Chapter 128 had proven it.

They had studied Zee.

They had understood his Wolf instincts.

They had learned how he tracked.

How he moved.

How he reacted when cornered.

They had built compounds that disrupted his senses.

They had learned which scents confused him.

Which irritants weakened his concentration.

Which traps forced his Wolf nature into predictable movement.

Brannic had been proud of that work.

Until Selk died.

Now he stood inside an underground fortress surrounded by steel doors, scent burners, false tunnels, hidden hunters, and enough anti-Wolf compounds to poison an entire battlefield.

He looked at the weapons arranged before him.

Silvered hooks.

Wolfbane capsules.

Scent-nullifying powder.

Blood masking agents.

Chemical irritants.

Pressure traps.

Decoy trails.

Everything had a purpose.

Everything had been designed for one creature.

One man.

One Wolf.

Brannic slowly tightened the straps around his forearms.

“Let him come.”

A guard swallowed.

“Lord Dor…”

“What?”

“The reports say Selk wasn't killed with Witchcraft.”

Brannic looked at him.

“What was used?”

The guard hesitated.

“Black fire.”

Brannic's expression hardened.

“Fire doesn't change the fundamentals.”

The guard said nothing.

Brannic turned back toward the weapons.

“No.”

He picked up a Wolfbane blade.

“It just means Varren made a mistake.”

Far above them…

Zee walked alone.

No army followed him.

No guards.

No family.

No throne.

He had left Darkheart without announcing where he was going.

He did not need to.

Brannic's scent had already been given to him.

Merek had provided the location.

Selk had provided the next piece.

And Zee had spent enough years hunting monsters to know that fear left traces.

Brannic's fortress was buried beneath an abandoned hunting estate several miles beyond the eastern territories.

Zee reached the entrance just after nightfall.

He stopped.

The old man had prepared well.

Zee could smell the compounds before he even entered.

Wolfbane.

Chemical irritants.

Old blood.

Dozens of different animals.

Human sweat.

Leather.

Steel.

The entire underground structure had been turned into a maze designed to confuse one particular predator.

Zee smiled faintly.

Not because it was funny.

Because it was familiar.

They had really kept studying him.

He pushed open the first door.

A cloud of scentless powder exploded across the corridor.

Zee walked through it.

Nothing happened.

A second trap triggered beneath his feet.

Iron spikes shot upward.

Black-crimson flame appeared around Zee's boots.

The spikes melted before reaching him.

He continued walking.

Behind a wall, someone whispered.

“He didn't react.”

Another voice answered.

“Wait.”

A third trap activated.

The corridor filled with a chemical mist designed to interfere with supernatural scent recognition.

Zee stopped.

For half a second, the old version of him might have adjusted.

Tracked differently.

Changed forms.

Searched for another sensory anchor.

Instead…

Two black-crimson wings unfolded from his back.

The air changed.

The mist burned away.

Not exploded.

Not scattered.

Burned.

Every molecule of it seemed to disappear beneath the controlled heat radiating from the Phoenix manifestation.

The guards watching through the hidden passages stared.

One whispered:

“What is that?”

Zee looked directly toward the wall.

The guard froze.

Zee smiled.

“You built all this for a Wolf.”

The wall exploded inward.

Black-crimson fire rolled through the chamber.

The guards scattered.

Zee stepped through the flames.

He was still human.

Mostly.

Dark Phoenix wings stretched behind him.

Scarlet light burned underneath the black-crimson fire crawling across his shoulders.

His eyes had changed.

Not into the eyes they remembered.

Something deeper.

Older.

Wrong for every calculation they had made.

Brannic watched from the next chamber.

His face went pale.

“You…”

Zee walked toward him.

“You studied me.”

Brannic raised his weapon.

“You were a Witch.”

Zee kept walking.

“A Vampire.”

Another step.

“A Wolf.”

Another.

“A Dragon.”

Zee tilted his head.

“Among other things.”

Brannic fired.

Three Wolfbane bolts.

Zee didn't dodge.

The first struck his chest.

The second struck his shoulder.

The third struck beneath his ribs.

Brannic smiled.

Then the smile disappeared.

The bolts dissolved.

Not broke.

Not fell out.

They simply burned away.

The wounds closed beneath them.

Brannic stared.

Zee pulled the last bolt from his side.

The metal glowed.

Then vanished inside black-crimson flame.

Brannic backed away.

“No.”

Zee's wings spread.

“You prepared for everything you knew.”

Fire gathered between his fingers.

“You just never knew me.”

Brannic ran.

Zee let him.

That was the mistake.

The underground fortress suddenly became a hunting ground.

Brannic knew every passage.

Every door.

Every hidden tunnel.

He triggered traps behind him.

Steel jaws snapped shut.

Scent bombs detonated.

Wolfbane clouds filled corridors.

Hidden hunters fired from walls.

Zee moved through all of it.

Black Phoenix fire burned away the compounds.

His wings carried him over the traps.

His pressure alone forced hidden attackers out of their positions.

One hunter fired a crossbow.

Zee caught the bolt.

Black flame crawled over his fingers.

He looked at the man.

“You weren't on Merek's list.”

The hunter dropped his weapon.

“I was hired by Brannic.”

Zee stared at him.

“Leave.”

The man didn't need to be told twice.

Zee continued.

Brannic reached the deepest chamber.

He slammed his hand against a stone panel.

The floor opened.

A massive pit appeared beneath Zee.

Iron chains shot upward from every direction.

Brannic pulled a lever.

The chains wrapped around Zee's wings.

For the first time…

Zee stopped.

Brannic breathed heavily.

“Yes.”

He smiled.

“I knew it.”

The chains tightened.

“They may not understand what you are now…”

He pulled another lever.

“…but I know how to restrain movement.”

Zee looked at the chains.

Then at Brannic.

“You really don't.”

Black-crimson fire erupted.

The chains turned white.

Then melted.

Brannic's smile vanished.

Zee stepped forward.

The floor beneath him cracked.

Brannic reached for another weapon.

Zee crossed the distance before he could draw it.

One hand caught Brannic by the throat.

The other remained at his side.

No transformation.

No explosion.

No dramatic display.

Just overwhelming Phoenix pressure.

Brannic's feet left the floor.

“You're going to answer some questions.”

Brannic struggled.

“You think I'll help you?”

“I don't need help.”

Zee's grip tightened.

“I need information.”

Brannic coughed.

“About what?”

“The others.”

Brannic went still.

Zee's expression remained calm.

“Selk gave me something.”

“He's dead.”

“I know.”

“And you think he told you everything?”

“No.”

Zee's wings folded slowly behind him.

“That's why you're still breathing.”

Brannic stared at him.

“You're killing everyone?”

“Everyone who mattered.”

Brannic swallowed.

“You're insane.”

“No.”

Zee's voice became quieter.

“I'm organized.”

Brannic looked away.

Zee waited.

Eventually, Brannic spoke.

“Vaeren Thoss.”

Zee's eyes narrowed.

“What about him?”

“He paid more than Merek.”

“For what?”

“Logistics. Information. Protection.”

Zee said nothing.

Brannic continued.

“Thoss had access to Vampire networks. He helped arrange movement through territories we couldn't enter openly.”

“Next.”

“Kaldrek Vane supplied the weapons.”

“What weapons?”

“Dragon-resistant equipment. Specialized restraints. Armor.”

Zee's expression changed slightly.

“Next.”

“Sera Quill.”

“What did she do?”

“Intelligence.”

Brannic looked at Zee.

“She maintained files on you.”

“How much?”

“Enough.”

Zee's fingers tightened.

Brannic immediately continued.

“Jorren Vale led the strike.”

Silence.

That name landed differently.

Zee remembered the voice.

The commands.

The rotations.

The moment they had decided he was weakened enough.

**Break the target.**

**Withdraw on confirmation.**

Zee stared at Brannic.

“Where is Jorren?”

Brannic shook his head.

“I don't know.”

“Lie again.”

“I swear.”

Zee released him slightly.

Brannic coughed.

“Jorren disappeared after the operation.”

“Why?”

“I don't know.”

“Who does?”

Brannic hesitated.

“Neris Pell.”

Zee remembered the name.

“Next.”

“Drovak Senn handled movement. Safehouses. Routes. Transportation.”

Brannic's breathing became weaker.

“And Kaldrek?”

“You already asked.”

“No.”

Zee's eyes hardened.

“I asked what he supplied.”

Brannic understood.

“You want to know where he is.”

“Yes.”

“He moved north.”

“How long ago?”

“Three months.”

Zee released him.

Brannic dropped to his knees.

For one brief second, he believed he might live.

Then he looked up.

“You got your answers.”

Zee stared down at him.

“You helped put me on that floor.”

Brannic said nothing.

“You helped build the traps.”

Nothing.

“You helped study how to break me.”

Brannic's face tightened.

“It was a contract.”

Zee smiled without warmth.

“I know.”

Brannic stared at him.

“It wasn't personal.”

“I know that too.”

Black-crimson fire appeared around Zee's hand.

“That doesn't make it disappear.”

Brannic's eyes widened.

Zee stepped closer.

“You went home after.”

Brannic said nothing.

“You ate.”

The fire grew.

“You slept.”

Brannic's breathing shook.

“You forgot.”

Zee's eyes burned.

“I didn't.”

The Phoenix fire consumed Brannic.

There was no scream lasting long enough to echo.

Only black-crimson flame.

Then silence.

Zee stood alone in the chamber.

The fire disappeared.

His wounds were already gone.

His wings folded.

For several seconds, he simply looked at the place where Brannic had been.

Then he turned.

He walked out.

By midnight, the remaining six had begun receiving messages.

Not official ones.

Not organized ones.

Rumors.

Whispers.

Broken communications.

One message contained only:

**BRANNIC IS GONE.**

Another:

**SELK WAS NOT THE FIRST.**

Someone else received a much shorter warning.

**THE PHOENIX.**

At a fortified estate in the north, Vaeren Thoss stopped pouring wine.

At another location, Kaldrek Vane closed an old weapons ledger.

Sera Quill burned three folders.

Neris Pell stared at a message for nearly a minute before deleting it.

Drovak Senn immediately began changing routes.

And somewhere far from all of them…

Jorren Vale received the news.

He read it once.

Then again.

His face did not show fear.

Not yet.

He simply whispered:

“Two.”

The number hung in the darkness.

Selk.

Brannic.

Two down.

Six remaining.

Jorren slowly opened an old chest.

Inside was a weapon he had not touched in years.

The weapon he had carried the night Zee Darkheart was broken.

His fingers rested against the handle.

For the first time in years…

Jorren wondered whether the man they had defeated back then still existed.

Outside the window, something black crossed the moon.

Jorren looked up.

Nothing was there.

But he knew.

Zee had begun counting.

And this time…

there would be no withdrawal order.
