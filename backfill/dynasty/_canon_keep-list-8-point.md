# The 8-point keep-list (post-Ch-104 canon lock)

> **Source:** "Crimson Dynasty Writers Room" (ChatGPT export), message 716, assistant, 2026-09-08 02:09. The writers-room canon lock after Chapter 104 closed the Legacy bridge. Includes the Zee-age-34 derivation (Zee ~27 at Aidan's birth + ~7 years = ~34). Status: CANON LOCK (writes-room record; Zee continued the story under it).

---

Yeah. Now that Chapter 104 closed the direct Legacy bridge, **Crimson Dynasty can snap back to being Zee’s book.** Here’s what we’re keeping right now:

1. **Zee Darkheart stays the center of everything.** This is still his entire life story: his reign, wars, friendships, family, enemies, evolution, darkness, victories, mistakes, and eventually whatever his final Dynasty-era fate becomes. Aidan does **not** take over this book.

2. **The core family stays active:** Ner is still Zee’s lover and Witch Bloodsong; Zareth is still his Dragon brother with the exclusive Dragon Link; Rez is still his brother by choice, Crown Prince of Vesper, and Witch–Vampire; Nymeris is still Queen of Nhalaris and chosen family. She remembers her relationship with Zee and everything they built together—she simply no longer remembers Aidan or being his mother. Rez remains the only person besides Zee who knows the truth.

3. **Darkheart keeps growing.** We keep the kingdom, refugees, Safe Roads, hybrids, politics, wars, assassinations, rebuilding, and Zee’s philosophy that Darkheart judges what people **choose to do**, not what blood they were born with. Kael, Tessa, Amara, and the hybrid races remain part of the world.

4. **Zee’s power/evolution story continues.** First Natural Tribrid forever; Witch/Vampire/Wolf fully active; Dragon fully awakened but no Dragon form, wings, or tail; Crimson Demonic Dragon remains his evolved Dragon state. He keeps adapting through combat, blood, supernatural exposure, and survival. There is still no System for Zee himself.

5. **Gods and Godslayers stay in the story.** Cassel opened the true Godslayer era. Dark Divinity remains a force Zee cannot steal or possess, although his body can develop resistance to being harmed by it. Gods, Godslayers, kingdoms, monsters, assassins, demons, Dragons, and other independent threats can continue hitting Zee from different directions.

6. **The Aidan material is now mostly CLOSED.** We have explained his birth, why Zee sealed Witch/Vampire/Wolf, why Ocean/Abyss/Siren remained dormant and unbound, why the world forgot him, why only Zee and Rez remember, why he was hidden in Vesper, and why “Uncle Rez” is a real childhood memory. The Legacy material already establishes Rez as Aidan’s uncle/guardian and the bridge between Zee’s age and Aidan’s later age. fileciteturn5file3L715-L799 We do **not** need to start telling Crimson Legacy inside Dynasty now.

7. **Aidan stays alive offstage in Vesper.** Rez raises him as the ridiculously spoiled royal ward/little-brother figure 😂 while keeping him away from large natural bodies of water. Zee can secretly check on him occasionally, but we should not keep cutting away to Aidan’s childhood. When the established future point comes where Rez is trapped in the Crimson Book, we preserve it—the Legacy canon already has Rez trapped after the Founder’s final preparations and later guiding Aidan after the Vampire Curse awakens. fileciteturn5file3L675-L689

8. **We keep Zee’s larger family future open.** Zee is still intended to have **three sons total**, Aidan being one of them. The other two sons have not been properly developed in the Dynasty story yet, so we still have that entire part of Zee’s life available rather than making Aidan his only child.

And one BIG timeline consequence from Chapter 104: because we watched Aidan grow to about **seven years old**, Dynasty just naturally moved forward several years. Zee was around 27 when Aidan was born, so we are now roughly around **Zee age 34**. That’s actually useful because we’re well into adult-King Zee now and can make the next portion of the book feel like a new period of his life without doing another forced giant time skip.

So basically:

**The Legacy bridge is finished. 🔒  
Aidan is safe in Vesper. 🔒  
Uncle Rez is established. 🔒  
Now we go back to wars, monsters, gods, kingdoms, family, Zee’s other future children, his evolving Dynasty, and Zee becoming the legendary man history eventually remembers.** 🔥
