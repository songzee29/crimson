Chapter Eighty

The Kingdom That Came to Darkheart

By the time Zee turned twenty-three, Darkheart had outgrown Victor’s walls.

New streets spread south.

Wolf families built along the forest line.

Vampires claimed the old underground wine tunnels and turned them into homes.

Witches expanded the outer wards instead of arguing about who belonged inside them.

Kael trained guards.

Tessa ran battlefield medicine.

Neither liked being called historic.

Both were.

The attacks kept coming.

So did the people.

One morning, five hundred refugees appeared outside Darkheart.

Not fleeing one battle.

Fleeing a kingdom.

Zee stood above the gate with Rez, Ner, and Zareth.

Zareth had arrived in human form, dark clothes edged with Drakonis markings.

He looked over the crowd.

“That is not a caravan.”

Rez nodded.

“That is a town.”

Ner’s Bloodsong brushed the people below.

Fear.

Exhaustion.

Injuries.

Children.

“No immediate threat.”

Zee looked toward the banners.

House Ardyn.

A Wolf kingdom.

Or what remained of one.

Their ruler had ordered every Witch living among the clans surrendered after discovering Darkheart’s new hybrids.

The Wolves refused.

Civil war followed.

Now an entire border province had walked away.

Their leader approached the gate.

A broad Wolf woman named Commander Sera Rhone.

She stopped before Zee.

“We want sanctuary.”

Zee looked behind her.

“You brought soldiers.”

“Seventy.”

“Families?”

“Four hundred twenty-nine.”

“Witches?”

“One hundred six.”

“Wolves?”

“The rest.”

“And your king?”

Sera smiled without humor.

“Wants our heads.”

Zee’s pupils narrowed.

“Why mine?”

“Because we heard Darkheart does not care what blood someone is born with.”

Zee looked toward Ner.

Then Kael standing below.

Then Tessa.

Darkheart’s reputation had become law before Zee ever wrote it.

He looked back at Sera.

“Enter.”

Merek nearly choked.

“Majesty, five hundred people?”

Zee glanced at him.

“Build.”

Merek stared.

Zee smiled faintly.

“You knew what job this was becoming.”

“I did not know you intended to collect entire provinces.”

“I didn’t.”

Sera overheard.

“That is disappointing.”

Rez laughed.

⸻

House Ardyn attacked six days later.

Not Darkheart Castle.

The new settlement outside it.

Smart.

The outer wards were unfinished.

Zee was in the eastern district when the first explosion hit.

By the time he reached the wall, Sera’s people were already fighting.

Good.

Darkheart did not wait for its King anymore.

Kael hit one assault line with Witchcraft and Vampire speed.

Tessa tracked infiltrators through smoke by scent.

Ner stood on the wall.

His voice carried across the battlefield.

“DOWN.”

Bloodsong hit a group of attacking Wolves.

Seven collapsed.

Not unconscious.

Bodies obeying before minds could recover.

Ner looked almost annoyed by how effective it had become.

Rez shouted from the western tower.

“Siege line moving north!”

Zareth answered through the Link before Zee could.

I see it.

Then aloud:

“I have the siege engines.”

His human body shifted.

True Dragon form erupted above the walls.

Massive wings opened.

Teal-and-Gold fire struck the empty ground in front of the siege crews.

Warning.

Most fled.

Two engines kept moving.

Zareth destroyed them.

Zee smiled.

Then smelled poison.

Behind him.

A blade entered between his ribs.

The assassin whispered:

“Monster.”

Zee looked down.

Then back.

Crimson-black scales had slowed the strike enough.

He grabbed the assassin’s wrist.

“You people desperately need new material.”

Snap.

The man screamed.

Zee bit him.

Fed.

Supernatural blood.

Wolf.

Foreign Witch poison.

His body separated both.

The poison burned.

Then failed.

Zee dropped the assassin.

Alive.

Barely.

“Who sent you?”

“Ardyn.”

“Obviously.”

Zee looked toward the attacking army.

Then the refugees behind Darkheart walls.

The answer came easily.

“Tell your king something.”

The assassin coughed.

“What?”

Zee’s crimson-and-gold eyes became slits.

“These people chose Darkheart.”

He leaned closer.

“That means he lost them.”

⸻

House Ardyn withdrew before sunset.

Not destroyed.

Defeated.

Sera stood beside Zee afterward.

“You could conquer them now.”

“Yes.”

“You won’t?”

“No.”

“Why?”

Zee looked at the wounded being carried home.

“Because I do not want their kingdom.”

Sera studied him.

“What do you want?”

Zee looked toward Darkheart.

Old Witch towers.

New Vampire districts.

Wolf settlements.

Refugee roads.

Hybrid defenders.

His answer surprised even him.

“For mine to survive.”

That night, the five hundred refugees became Darkheart citizens.

No blood oath.

No ownership.

Choice.

The borders moved again.

Not because Zee conquered land.

Because people brought their lives with them.

And the old world began realizing something worse than King Zee creating hybrids.

He was creating a place people were willing to abandon kingdoms for.
