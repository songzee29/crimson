Chapter One Hundred Fifteen

The Eighth Month

Darkheart buried five people after the Demon attack.

Three soldiers.

One Wolf scout.

One Witch healer.

No children.

That mattered.

It did not make the five smaller.

Zee stood through every burial.

Elias stood beside him.

Not behind.

The Demon–Witch scars remained visible along his throat.

His original deep-blue Mana occasionally flickered beneath his skin when emotion rose.

Nobody in Darkheart stared anymore.

That took less than a week.

Darkheart had become difficult to impress.

⸻

Elias returned to his old rooms.

They were exactly as he left them.

Mostly.

Rez had stolen two books.

Zareth had broken a chair.

Kael denied eating the emergency food.

Ner had apparently stored three boxes inside without asking.

Elias stood in the doorway.

“This is disrespectful.”

Zee leaned against the wall.

“You were missing.”

“That does not make my room public property.”

“It apparently did.”

Elias looked at the broken chair.

“Zareth.”

“Yes.”

“How?”

“He sat down.”

Elias stared.

“He can control his strength.”

“Usually.”

“I hate all of you.”

“Good.”

Elias entered.

His fingers brushed the old desk.

There were knife marks in one corner.

He remembered making them at sixteen because Zee had dared him to stab a moving target while blindfolded.

A shelf still held one of Aurelia’s old spellbooks.

Not important enough for royal archives.

Important enough for Elias to keep.

He touched the spine.

Then stopped.

Zee did not say anything.

Neither did Elias.

Aurelia remained dead.

Memory did not require resurrection.

After a moment, Elias asked:

“Did you think I was dead?”

“Yes.”

“How long?”

“Six weeks.”

“That was not what I asked.”

Zee looked at him.

Elias understood.

Every day.

Zee had thought it possible every day.

Elias sat on the edge of the bed.

“They changed my scent.”

“Yes.”

“My Mana.”

“Yes.”

“My blood.”

“Yes.”

“You still came.”

“Ner found the prisoners.”

Elias smiled faintly.

“Of course he did.”

Zee looked toward the floor.

“I should have found you sooner.”

Elias’s expression changed.

“No.”

Zee looked up.

“I was escorting Darkheart families.”

“Yes.”

“They survived.”

“Yes.”

“I did my job.”

“And you were taken.”

“That was their choice.”

Zee said nothing.

Elias leaned back.

“Do not make yourself responsible for every cage in the world just because you can break most of them.”

Zee smiled faintly.

“That sounded annoyingly reasonable.”

“I have been friends with you for too long.”

There.

No retelling needed.

No grand correction.

Elias had belonged here long before Demon blood entered him.

And he remained here now.

⸻

Ner entered the eighth month during a thunderstorm.

Zee knew before Tessa confirmed anything.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat changed.

Again.

Not weaker.

Heavier.

The unborn child’s Dragon nature had begun responding to pressure in the sky.

Every thunderclap made the heartbeat accelerate.

Every flash of lightning caused faint Crimson-and-Gold Witch patterns to move beneath Ner’s skin.

The Wolf rhythm remained strongest at night.

Dragon heat strongest during storms.

Witch resonance strongest around Ner.

Not Ner’s exact Pure Gold Mana.

Zayrlen’s own developing magic carried both fathers visibly.

Crimson.

Gold.

One expression.

Ner stood on the balcony during the storm.

Zee found him there.

“You are wet.”

“I noticed.”

“Come inside.”

“No.”

“Ner.”

“I like the rain.”

Zee looked at the lightning.

“You are carrying a child with Dragon blood.”

“Yes.”

“Standing outside during a magical thunderstorm.”

“Yes.”

“That seems irresponsible.”

Ner turned.

“You murdered an entire Phoenix kingdom and came home with wings.”

“That was different.”

“How?”

“I was not pregnant.”

Ner stared.

Zee realized he had lost the argument.

Zayrlen moved.

A pulse of heat rolled from Ner.

The rain around his body turned briefly to steam.

Both men froze.

Ner looked down.

“Well.”

Zee stepped closer.

Another thunderclap.

Crimson-and-Gold energy moved.

A Wolf-shaped pressure briefly appeared behind Ner.

Not a transformation.

Not even a complete projection.

Instinct.

Four-legged.

Low.

Protective.

Then gone.

Zee’s Wolf nature responded immediately.

He looked toward the place it had been.

Ner watched him.

“What?”

“He is going to have a true Wolf form.”

Ner smiled.

“You sound surprised.”

“I am not.”

“You are.”

“Only by the color.”

“What color?”

Zee looked deeper.

The developing Wolf pressure had not resembled his own near-black Wolf.

Not even close.

Crimson dominated the outline.

Gold moved through it.

Both fathers.

Zee touched Ner.

“He is going to be Crimson and Gold.”

Ner went quiet.

His hand covered Zee’s.

“Both of us.”

“Yes.”

The rain kept falling.

Zee smiled.

That particular inheritance felt right.

⸻

The first real pregnancy complication arrived twelve days later.

Not labor.

Power.

Ner collapsed during council.

One second he was insulting a trade proposal.

The next, Pure Gold Mana exploded from his body.

The entire chamber went bright.

Zee was across the table before Ner hit the ground.

Phoenix wings blocked everyone else instinctively.

“Ner.”

“I am awake.”

Good.

Zee listened.

Ner’s heartbeat.

Fast.

Zayrlen’s.

Faster.

Too fast.

Tessa pushed past Zee’s wing.

“Move.”

“No.”

“Move or I remove it.”

Zee moved.

Tessa knelt.

Gold-white healing light passed over Ner.

Then recoiled.

Not injury.

Overload.

“What?”

Tessa looked at the magic moving beneath Ner’s skin.

“He is drawing too much.”

Ner breathed through the pressure.

“Who?”

Zee stared.

“Zayrlen.”

The unborn child’s Witch foundation had latched onto Ner’s Pure Gold Mana.

Not stealing it.

Using it as the nearest structure to develop against.

Too aggressively.

Ner’s own magic kept answering.

Parent feeding child.

Instinctively.

And if it continued—

Ner would burn himself empty.

Zee’s face hardened.

“Can you stop it?”

Tessa shook her head.

“Not without disrupting the pregnancy.”

Ner grabbed Zee’s wrist.

“Do not touch his nature.”

“I was not going to.”

“You were thinking about it.”

“I think about many things.”

“Badly.”

Even now.

Good.

Zee placed his hand over Ner.

Crimson Sovereign senses opened completely.

Witch.

Vampire.

Wolf.

Then Dragon.

Phoenix.

He could feel the child.

Zayrlen was not attacking Ner.

He did not know how.

The developing Witch nature had simply found Pure Gold Mana and recognized:

Father.

Source.

Safety.

It kept reaching.

Zee closed his eyes.

Then fed something else into the space.

Not Vampire.

Never.

Not Phoenix.

Not Demon.

Those did not belong to Zayrlen.

Dragon first.

His own inherited Veyrath blood.

Heat entered gently.

Zayrlen reacted.

The pull on Ner’s Mana weakened.

Then Zee added Witch resonance.

Crimson.

Not creating a new nature.

The Witch nature already belonged to their son by birth.

Zee simply gave it another parent to recognize.

Crimson met Gold.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat slowed.

Ner’s Mana stopped flooding outward.

Silence.

Then—

a tiny magical pulse moved back through Zee’s hand.

Crimson.

Gold.

Zee opened his eyes.

Ner looked at him.

“Did he just push you?”

“Yes.”

Ner smiled weakly.

“Good.”

Zee stared.

“Why good?”

“Because you needed it.”

Tessa sighed.

“He is definitely yours.”

⸻

The incident changed the pregnancy.

After that, Zee regularly fed controlled Witch resonance into Zayrlen’s developing magic.

Not power.

Not an awakening.

Balance.

Two fathers.

Two magical patterns.

Ner’s Pure Gold.

Zee’s Crimson Witch structure.

Zayrlen settled between them.

The child’s own Mana remained uniquely his.

Crimson and Gold.

Neither father erased.

Neither copied exactly.

Except for the things meant to pass cleanly.

Dragon blood.

Wolf instinct.

Witch nature.

Their son was becoming himself before either could meet him.

⸻

Zareth arrived two days after hearing Ner had collapsed.

He entered in human form.

No joke.

No sarcasm.

“Is he safe?”

Ner nodded.

“Yes.”

“And Zayrlen?”

“Yes.”

Only then did Zareth relax.

His gaze moved toward Zee.

You nearly lost your mind.

No.

Liar.

Zee ignored him.

Elias entered carrying food.

Rez arrived from Vesper before dinner.

Kael.

Tessa.

Amara.

Rhyven.

No council had been called.

They simply came.

Family did that.

Elias set food down.

Ner looked at the tray.

“I am not dying.”

Tessa responded immediately.

“You are eating.”

“I already ate.”

“When?”

Ner paused.

Tessa pointed toward the food.

“Exactly.”

Rez sat beside him.

“Do what she says.”

Ner looked offended.

“You are the worst person here.”

Zareth looked toward Zee.

“Debatable.”

Rhyven quietly stole food from the edge of the tray.

Amara slapped his hand.

Kael took the same piece while they argued.

Elias watched all of them.

Then laughed.

Zee looked around the room.

Chosen family.

Old blood.

New blood.

Natural Tribrids.

Created Tribrids.

Hybrids that had never existed before.

People who remained exactly what they had always been.

Nobody mattered more because they had more races inside them.

That law had to remain.

Power changed bodies.

It did not decide family.

⸻

Then Zayrlen kicked hard enough for Ner to flinch.

Everyone stopped.

Ner stared around the room.

“No.”

Rez smiled.

“No what?”

“No one is touching me.”

Zareth raised his hand.

“I was not—”

“You absolutely were.”

Elias smiled.

“May I?”

Ner considered.

Then nodded.

Elias went first because he had nearly died before getting the chance.

He placed one hand against Ner.

Zayrlen moved.

Elias smiled.

“Strong.”

Kael received permission next.

Then Tessa.

Amara.

Rhyven hesitated at the edge of the room.

Ner noticed.

“You too.”

Rhyven blinked.

“I am not family.”

Silence.

Zee looked at him.

Rhyven realized his mistake.

Amara crossed her arms.

“You live here.”

Kael added:

“You fight here.”

Elias said:

“You steal food here.”

Rhyven looked at the bread in his hand.

Rez smiled.

“Unfortunately, that qualifies.”

Rhyven looked toward Zee.

Zee shrugged.

“Darkheart has low standards.”

Ner laughed.

Then held out his hand.

Rhyven approached.

Carefully.

His palm touched Ner.

Zayrlen moved.

Crimson Phoenix heat inside Rhyven reacted—

then immediately quieted.

Not compatible.

Not part of the child.

Zayrlen’s developing nature recognized something foreign and ignored it.

Rhyven smiled faintly.

“He does not like me.”

Zee listened.

“No.”

Rhyven looked up.

Zee’s smile appeared.

“He does not know you yet.”

Different.

Rhyven nodded.

He could live with that.

⸻

Later, when everyone finally left, Zee lay beside Ner.

One Phoenix wing covered both of them.

Ner rested against his chest.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat filled the quiet.

Eighth month.

Alive.

Strong.

Growing.

Ner spoke into the darkness.

“You know he is going to be trouble.”

“Yes.”

“You sound proud.”

“Yes.”

“He is going to break things.”

“Yes.”

“Probably bite someone.”

“Definitely.”

Ner smiled.

Then became quiet.

“He gets to know us.”

Zee’s expression changed.

“Yes.”

“Both of us.”

“Yes.”

“No hiding.”

“No.”

“No pretending.”

“No.”

Ner took Zee’s hand and placed it over their son.

Zayrlen pushed back.

Zee smiled in the dark.

For once, the future did not feel like something he needed to erase before it arrived.

It felt like someone he was waiting to meet.
