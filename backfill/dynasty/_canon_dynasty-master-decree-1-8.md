# 📜 THE CRIMSON DYNASTY MASTER DECREE

## PREAMBLE

The Crimson Dynasty Master Decree is the supreme canon governing the Crimson Dynasty Universe.

All worldbuilding, characters, races, kingdoms, magic, lore, and future stories must obey these decrees unless a later decree officially amends them.

The Master Decree contains only permanent canon.

Story ideas, outlines, chapter planning, and evolving concepts belong within the Crimson Dynasty Codex and are not considered canon until formally added to this Decree.

---

# 📜 DECREE 1

## The Age of Kingdoms

Crimson Dynasty takes place several thousand years before the events of Crimson Legacy.

This era is known as **The Age of Kingdoms**.

The world is ruled by ancient royal kingdoms rather than a unified supernatural civilization.

Each kingdom possesses its own traditions, history, magical culture, and royal bloodline.

---

# 📜 DECREE 2

## The First Natural Tribrid

Zee Darkheart is the First Natural Tribrid.

He is born as a Witch, Vampire, and Wolf simultaneously.

He is not transformed into a Tribrid, nor does he awaken the three races separately.

His existence from birth is unprecedented and impossible according to the natural laws of the world.

Zee cannot permanently die.

He continually endures, adapts, grows stronger, and evolves throughout his life.

---

# 📜 DECREE 3

## The Birth of the First Tribrid

Upon Zee Darkheart's birth:

- The sky turns crimson.
- Blood rains from the heavens.
- Witches across the world lose their powers.
- Wolves descend into uncontrollable feral madness.
- Vampires sense an ancient supernatural awakening.
- Zee's magic leaks from his body.
- His teeth sharpen into fangs.
- His eyes glow a golden-crimson.

The world witnesses the impossible.

This event becomes one of the greatest supernatural catastrophes in recorded history.

---

# 📜 DECREE 4

## The World's Judgment

From the day of his birth, Zee Darkheart is judged before committing a single act.

Across the kingdoms he becomes known by titles including:

- The First Tribrid
- The Monster
- The Abomination
- The Curse

The world's fear is directed toward Zee's existence rather than his actions.

---

# 📜 DECREE 5

## The Father of Hybrids

As the First Natural Tribrid, Zee Darkheart possesses the unique ability to create the original hybrid races through his blood.

These include:

- Witch–Vampire Hybrids
- Vampire–Wolf Hybrids
- Wolf–Witch Hybrids

This authority belongs exclusively to Zee and remains distinct from the Crimson Bloodline and Crimson races established by Aidan Darkheart in Crimson Legacy.

---

# 📜 DECREE 6

## The Royal Kingdoms

The two primary royal kingdoms of Crimson Dynasty are:

### House Darkheart

An ancient Witch Kingdom.

Ruled by:

- King Victor Darkheart
- Queen Aurelia Darkheart

House Darkheart is not defined by crimson magic.

---

### House Vesper

Known throughout the Age of Kingdoms as:

**The Kingdom of the Azure Flame.**

House Vesper is an ancient Witch Kingdom whose royal bloodline wields Blue-White Mana.

The kingdom is renowned for discipline, precision, mastery, honor, and wisdom.

Ruled by:

- King Lucien Vesper
- Prince Rez Vesper

---

# 📜 DECREE 7

## Mana Traditions

Mana colors do not determine morality.

They represent unique magical identities.

Current known traditions include:

- Zee Darkheart — Crimson Mana
- House Vesper — Blue-White Mana
- Ner — Golden Mana

Each tradition possesses its own identity and history.

---

# 📜 DECREE 8

## The Founding Circle

The emotional foundation of Crimson Dynasty is centered upon Zee Darkheart's earliest relationships.

These include:

- Queen Aurelia Darkheart — Mother
- Prince Rez Vesper — Brother by choice
- Ner — Best friend and Zee's first secret love

These relationships permanently shape Zee's life and journey.
