Chapter Seventy-Two

The Man Who Wanted the King Alive

Dren Solvar did not hide.

That made Zee suspicious immediately.

He owned an estate outside the coastal city of Valen.

Open gates.

Visible guards.

No blood wards.

No hidden fortress.

No attempt to run.

Ner looked at the estate from the ridge.

“I hate this.”

Zee smiled.

“Good.”

“That was not encouragement.”

“I know.”

Rez had stayed in Vesper.

The aqueduct attack had failed, but three Covenant members remained loose inside his kingdom.

His problem.

His hunt.

Zareth remained in Drakonis.

The Link carried distant mountain heat and irritation at being excluded from a problem that specifically involved Zee’s blood.

Zee sent back amusement.

Then looked toward Ner.

“You stay outside.”

Ner stared.

“No.”

“Dren wants me alive.”

“Yes.”

“So I give him what he wants.”

“That sentence is terrible.”

Zee’s pupils narrowed into slits.

“I want to see how.”

Ner crossed his arms.

“You are using yourself as bait.”

“Yes.”

“You are King.”

“Yes.”

“You are impossible.”

“Yes.”

Ner sighed.

“Fine.”

Zee blinked.

“That was easy.”

“I am coming in thirty seconds after you.”

“That defeats the point.”

“Twenty.”

Zee smiled.

“Thirty.”

“Twenty-five.”

“Done.”

They kissed once.

Quick.

Then Zee disappeared down the ridge.

⸻

Dren Solvar waited in the garden.

Human.

At least mostly.

Zee smelled Vampire blood in him.

Not enough to become Vampire.

Repeated doses.

Enhancement.

Other blood too.

Wolf.

Witch.

Something reptilian.

Dren had been experimenting on himself.

Interesting.

He sat beneath a white pavilion drinking tea.

“King Darkheart.”

Zee approached.

“Dren.”

“You came alone.”

“No.”

Dren smiled.

“At least you are honest.”

Zee sat opposite him.

No weapon visible.

Four guards behind Dren.

Two archers on the roof.

One Witch beneath the pavilion floor.

Three heartbeats behind the garden wall.

Zee cataloged and dismissed them.

Dren poured tea.

Zee did not touch it.

“Poison?” Dren asked.

“Yes.”

Dren laughed.

“Very good.”

“Common paralytic under the lavender.”

“Less common suppression agent beneath it.”

Dren’s smile widened.

“And?”

Zee inhaled.

“Dragonbane.”

The smile vanished.

There.

“You really can smell that?”

“Yes.”

Dren set the cup down.

“You are magnificent.”

Zee’s face cooled.

“Do not.”

“Do not what?”

“Talk about me like a specimen.”

Dren studied him.

“But that is exactly what you are.”

Wrong.

Zee almost killed him there.

Did not.

Information first.

“What do you want with my blood?”

“To understand transformation.”

“Ner.”

“Yes.”

“God blood.”

“Yes.”

“Your Dragon evolution.”

“Yes.”

Dren leaned forward.

“You drank things that should have destroyed you.”

“And?”

“You became more yourself.”

That sentence made Zee pause.

Dren noticed.

“Demon blood.”

“Dragon blood.”

“Divine blood.”

“Different poisons.”

“Different supernatural species.”

“You do not accumulate foreign powers.”

“You rewrite pressure.”

Zee said nothing.

Dren smiled.

“I understand you better than most.”

“No.”

“Why?”

“Because you think understanding my blood means understanding me.”

The smile thinned.

Zee leaned back.

“What were you planning?”

Dren’s eyes sharpened.

“To capture you.”

“Alive.”

“Yes.”

“How?”

“Would you like to know?”

Zee smiled.

“Yes.”

Dren lifted one finger.

The garden exploded.

⸻

Silver-black cables launched from beneath the stone.

Not magic.

Mechanisms.

Fast.

Zee moved.

One wrapped his ankle.

Another caught his wrist.

A third hit his chest.

The cables activated.

Suppression.

Dragonbane.

Vampire restraints.

Wolf irritants.

Witch-breaking sigils.

Everything at once.

Zee hit the ground.

Dren stood.

“Like that.”

Zee looked down.

Interesting.

His Vampire strength failed first.

Witchcraft became heavy.

Wolf senses flooded with artificial scent.

Dragon blood burned.

Good design.

Dren approached.

“You have been attacked by specialists.”

“I hired engineers.”

Zee flexed one hand.

The cable tightened.

Pain.

His Crimson adaptations reacted.

Not instantly.

Not enough.

Good.

Dren crouched.

“You see?”

Zee looked at him.

Human pupils now.

Calm.

“You made one mistake.”

Dren smiled.

“Which?”

“You built restraints for everything you know I am.”

Zee’s hand closed.

Nothing happened.

Dren laughed.

Then Zee smiled.

“Separately.”

His Wolf nature found balance first.

He stopped fighting the restraint and rolled with its tension.

The Vampire suppression expected direct resistance.

None came.

His Witch nature used one tiny gesture.

Two fingers.

Barely moving.

A sigil on the nearest cable rotated half an inch.

Wrong alignment.

The Dragonbane load shifted.

Crimson Demonic Dragon heat surged into the exposed weakness.

One cable snapped.

Dren stopped laughing.

Zee moved.

Vampire speed returned only partially.

Enough.

He caught Dren by the throat.

The other cables dragged behind him.

Dren gasped.

Zee headbutted him.

Horn edge opened his scalp.

Blood.

Mixed blood.

Artificial.

Zee smelled every layer.

Ner’s Golden barrier erupted around the garden.

Twenty-five seconds.

Exactly.

“Show-off,” Zee said.

Ner entered.

“You are tied to a statue.”

“Temporarily.”

Dren shouted:

“NOW!”

The hidden fighters moved.

Chaos.

The underground Witch cast first.

Ner’s Gold pierced the floor and broke the spell structure before it rose.

One archer fired.

Zee flicked two fingers.

The arrow bent.

Hit the second archer’s bow.

Wolf attackers came from the wall.

Zee tore one remaining cable free through brute force.

Not Vampire alone.

Dragon durability let the restraint cut deep without stopping him.

He caught the first Wolf by the throat.

Threw him.

Second attacked Ner.

Wrong.

Ner spoke one low Bloodsong note.

The Wolf’s circulation stumbled.

His body dropped to one knee.

Ner sealed him in Gold.

“Alive,” Ner said.

Zee looked toward Dren.

“For now.”

Dren ran.

Zee let him.

Ner looked over.

“You are letting him go.”

“Yes.”

“Why?”

Zee’s pupils shifted into slits.

“Wolf.”

Understanding hit Ner.

“Oh.”

Zee transformed.

⸻

Dren reached the coastal forest.

He believed the trees would help.

They did.

Zee wanted the hunt.

Near-black Wolf form moved through wet brush without sound.

No horns.

No scales.

No magic.

Just Wolf.

Dren used scent blockers.

Zee tracked broken fern.

Dren crossed rock.

Zee tracked displaced insects.

Dren entered seawater.

Zee circled ahead.

Waited on the beach.

Dren emerged twenty minutes later.

Saw Zee.

Stopped.

The fear was exquisite.

Zee walked toward him slowly.

Dren backed into the surf.

“You need me.”

Wolf Zee showed teeth.

Dren pulled a blood-filled injector from his coat.

“Do you know what this is?”

Zee shifted back.

Human form returned.

He looked at the vial.

Dragon.

Demon.

Vampire.

Mixed.

Badly.

“You made a cocktail.”

Dren laughed.

“A weapon.”

“No.”

Zee stepped closer.

“A mistake.”

Dren injected himself.

His body reacted immediately.

Veins blackened.

Eyes turned red.

Bones shifted beneath skin.

Strength surged.

Then his heartbeat lost rhythm.

Zee watched.

Dren screamed.

Supernatural bloods fought inside him.

No Crimson biology to rewrite them.

No stable inheritance.

Just incompatibility.

Dren lunged anyway.

Zee caught the first punch.

Barely.

Strong.

Then another.

Dren became faster.

More unstable.

Zee’s assassin instincts stopped trying to match force.

Weakness.

The blood mixture kept pulling Dren’s left shoulder too high.

His right leg compensated.

Heart skipping every fourth beat.

Zee waited.

One.

Two.

Three.

Skip.

He struck the sternum exactly then.

Dren collapsed.

Zee caught him.

“Interesting.”

Dren coughed blood.

“You see?”

“Yes.”

“Then save me.”

Zee stared.

“Why?”

“I can give you everything.”

“Already have your ledgers.”

“You need my research.”

“No.”

“You could become—”

Zee’s face went cold.

“I decide what I become.”

He bit.

Dren screamed.

Zee drank.

Not because Dren’s blood offered some miraculous new power.

Because Zee wanted to know what artificial supernatural blood tasted like inside a failed vessel.

The answer was chaos.

His body separated everything immediately.

Foreign Vampire enhancement.

Wolf residue.

Witch infusion.

Dragon blood.

Demon corruption.

Nothing stable enough to keep.

Crimson biology rejected most of it.

That was useful too.

Not every supernatural blood source deserved adaptation.

Sometimes the correct evolution was refusal.

Zee drained Dren completely.

Then dropped the body into the surf.

Dead.

Second Covenant name finished.

⸻

Ner found Zee on the beach.

Blood at his mouth.

Waves around his boots.

“You killed him.”

“Yes.”

“Learn anything?”

Zee looked toward the corpse.

“My blood is smarter than his research.”

Ner laughed.

“That sounded arrogant.”

“It is also true.”

Ner stood beside him.

“What now?”

Zee pulled the Covenant list from his coat.

Three names remained.

Keira Morn.

Father Caldus.

And one other.

Then the Dragon Link slammed into him.

Not danger.

Urgency.

Drakonis.

A face.

Zareth showed him a captured attacker.

A mark burned into the man’s chest.

Zee recognized it from the Covenant records.

Not Dren.

Not Mara.

The third name.

Keira Morn.

Zee looked east.

Ner sighed.

“Of course.”

Zee smiled.

“Next.”

The world was making this efficient.
