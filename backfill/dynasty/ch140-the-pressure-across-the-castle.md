Chapter One Hundred Forty

The Pressure Across the Castle

Years moved again.

Not gently.

Not evenly.

Zayrlen grew.

The little Crimson-and-Gold Wolf that once chased its own tail in the rain became larger.

Sharper.

Faster.

His Human form changed with him.

Childhood softened into adolescence.

His face lengthened.

His shoulders broadened.

His Witch Mana strengthened.

His Wolf control became reliable enough that he could transform without falling over afterward.

Most of the time.

Darkheart stopped seeing a child running through its halls.

It started seeing a young Prince.

Zee did not.

Not entirely.

To Zee, the boy who stood nearly eye-to-eye with him on some days was still somehow the same baby who once grabbed his horn with both hands.

Zayrlen hated when Zee said that.

Zee said it anyway.

⸻

By his teenage years, Zayrlen had become dangerous.

Not theoretically.

Actually.

His Crimson-and-Gold Mana could crack reinforced Witch wards.

His Wolf form could outrun most ordinary supernatural trackers.

His strength had grown far beyond what anyone expected from his age.

Dragon scales appeared more often now.

Not just during anger.

Sometimes during combat.

Sometimes during stress.

Sometimes for no obvious reason at all.

His body temperature ran hotter.

His presence became heavier.

Some days the Crimson Demonic Dragon inside Zee responded to Zayrlen from rooms away.

Zareth noticed it too.

Never forced it.

Never scheduled anything.

Never tried to make the Dragon emerge.

It would come when it came.

Nobody knew when.

That answer still irritated Zayrlen.

⸻

He was in his room when it happened.

Alone.

No training.

No battle.

No ritual.

No argument.

Nothing dramatic.

At first.

Zayrlen sat at the edge of his bed reading a field report he had stolen from Rez.

Not technically stolen.

Borrowed without asking.

Which Rez would later insist was theft.

Zayrlen disagreed.

He turned one page.

Then stopped.

Something moved beneath his skin.

He looked down.

A Crimson-and-Gold scale appeared along the back of his hand.

Then another.

Zayrlen frowned.

“Not now.”

The scales spread anyway.

He stood.

Heat filled the room.

Too fast.

His Wolf senses sharpened violently.

His Witch Mana flickered.

The candles blew out.

Then relit Crimson-and-Gold.

Zayrlen looked toward the mirror.

His eyes had changed.

Crimson-Gold.

Brighter.

Slit.

Dragon.

His heartbeat accelerated.

“Dad?”

Quiet.

No one answered.

He had not called loudly.

Not yet.

He looked down as another line of scales crossed his forearm.

The room creaked.

Then something inside him pushed outward.

Hard.

Zayrlen gasped.

⸻

Across the castle, Zee was arguing with Victor.

Naturally.

Victor had somehow decided that Darkheart’s western defense towers were aesthetically offensive.

Zee had informed him that nobody cared.

Victor had informed Zee that architecture mattered.

Zee had informed Victor that he had been dead for years and had missed his opportunity to file complaints.

Rez was laughing.

Ner was pretending not to.

Then Zee felt it.

Small.

A pressure.

Deep inside Darkheart.

He stopped speaking.

Victor noticed.

“What?”

Zee looked toward the opposite side of the castle.

Nothing obvious.

Darkheart held too many powerful creatures for every pressure shift to mean danger.

Witches.

Vampires.

Wolves.

Hybrids.

Dragons visiting.

Rhyven.

Elias.

Ner.

Zareth, when present.

Zee pushed the sensation aside.

“Nothing.”

Victor stared.

“You stopped insulting me.”

“Do not get used to it.”

Rez smiled.

Then the pressure grew.

Fast.

The glass in the council chamber trembled.

Everyone stopped.

Ner looked up.

Pure Gold Mana reacted.

Victor’s expression changed.

“That is not nothing.”

Zee was already standing.

The pressure doubled.

Then tripled.

A ward somewhere deep in the castle activated.

Another answered.

Windows shook.

Stone groaned.

Supernatural heartbeats throughout Darkheart accelerated.

Zee’s Vampire hearing sharpened.

Wolf instincts surged.

Witch Mana rose automatically.

Then the Crimson Demonic Dragon inside him reacted so violently that black-crimson scales flashed across his throat.

Zee froze.

Not fear.

Recognition.

Blood.

His blood.

His son’s blood.

His head snapped toward the far side of the keep.

“Zayrlen.”

Then Zee disappeared.

⸻

Vampire speed tore through the castle.

No announcement.

No guard escort.

No strategy.

Nothing else mattered.

Zee crossed two corridors.

One stairwell.

A balcony.

Another hall.

Every step closer made the pressure worse.

Crimson-and-Gold Mana leaked beneath doors.

Stone cracked.

Wards bent inward toward one room.

Zayrlen’s room.

Zee hit the door.

It exploded off its hinges.

Then he stopped.

For one second.

Because his son was changing.

⸻

Zayrlen stood in the center of the room.

Barely.

Crimson-and-Gold scales covered one arm completely.

Half his chest.

His neck.

Part of his face.

Claws had emerged.

Not Wolf claws.

Dragon.

Larger.

Curved.

His back arched.

Bones shifted beneath the skin.

His breathing came too fast.

Crimson-and-Gold Witch Mana flooded the room without direction.

The Wolf inside him reacted badly.

Instinct fighting instinct.

One true form already known.

Another trying to emerge.

Zayrlen looked at Zee.

And for perhaps the first time in years—

he looked young.

Scared.

“Dad.”

Zee crossed the room.

The Dragon pressure hit him hard enough to slow even him.

Furniture shattered behind Zayrlen.

A window cracked.

Gold light flashed beneath Crimson scales.

Zayrlen grabbed his own arm.

“I can’t stop it.”

Zee reached him.

“Look at me.”

“I am trying.”

“No.”

Zee caught his face carefully between both hands.

Dragon heat burned against Zee’s palms.

Did not matter.

“Look at me.”

Zayrlen’s Crimson-Gold eyes found his.

“I can’t stop it.”

Zee heard the panic.

Not Prince.

Not fighter.

Son.

That changed everything.

Zee lowered his voice.

“Then stop trying.”

Zayrlen stared.

“What?”

The room shook again.

His shoulder shifted violently.

He cried out.

Zee stayed close.

“Stop fighting it.”

“It hurts.”

“I know.”

“You do not know.”

That hit.

Because Zayrlen was right.

Zee had Dragon blood.

Real.

Biological.

Awakened.

Evolved into the Crimson Demonic Dragon.

But Zee had never become a true Dragon.

Never had a full Dragon body.

Never experienced this.

He could not say:

Do what I do.

He had nothing to demonstrate.

So he did the only thing left.

He stayed.

⸻

Zayrlen’s back bowed again.

More scales erupted.

The room filled with a low Dragon growl that did not come from his throat alone.

Zee held him steady.

“Listen to me.”

“I am.”

“No. You are listening to the pain.”

Zayrlen clenched his jaw.

“The Dragon is not attacking you.”

Another shift.

Zayrlen nearly dropped.

Zee caught him.

“It feels like it.”

“I know.”

“You said you didn’t.”

“I do not know the transformation.”

Zee’s voice remained steady.

“But I know you.”

Zayrlen looked at him.

That landed.

Zee continued.

“You learned this with the Wolf.”

Zayrlen’s breathing hitched.

“When you fought the Wolf, you panicked.”

“I was a kid.”

“You are still my kid.”

“Dad.”

“Not the point.”

Despite everything, Zayrlen almost laughed.

Good.

Zee saw it.

Held on.

“The Dragon is not something trying to take you.”

Zayrlen’s claws dug into the floor.

“It is you.”

Silence.

The pressure surged.

Zee moved closer.

“So stop trying to beat yourself.”

Zayrlen stared.

Fear.

Pain.

Understanding beginning.

“What if I cannot come back?”

“You will.”

“You do not know that.”

“No.”

Zee did not lie.

“Then how—”

“Because if you cannot, I will stay until you can.”

That silenced him.

Zee’s hands remained against his face.

“However long it takes.”

Zayrlen’s eyes burned.

Zee spoke softly.

“Let go.”

⸻

Zayrlen closed his eyes.

Stopped resisting.

The change became violent.

But different.

Not war anymore.

Release.

Crimson-and-Gold light erupted through the room.

Zee stepped back only because Zayrlen’s body needed space.

His spine lengthened.

Human shoulders broadened into Dragon structure.

Arms extended.

Hands became massive forelimbs.

Claws tore through stone.

Wings burst outward.

Real Dragon wings.

Crimson membrane threaded with Gold.

No Phoenix flame.

No Phoenix feathers.

The walls cracked.

The ceiling groaned.

Zee raised one hand.

“Hold.”

Witchcraft reinforced the room long enough.

Not to stop Zayrlen.

To give him room to become himself.

His neck lengthened.

Jaw shifted.

Teeth became Dragon fangs.

A tail formed behind him.

True Dragon anatomy.

Nothing borrowed from Zareth.

Nothing demonic.

Nothing Phoenix.

Crimson-and-Gold scales spread across an enormous body.

The transformation kept growing.

And growing.

And growing.

Zee stared.

Then smiled.

Pride hit harder than fear ever could.

⸻

A true Dragon filled what remained of Zayrlen’s chamber.

Young.

Enormous.

Majestic.

Crimson and Gold.

The scales carried deep Crimson across the body with brilliant Gold along the chest, face, wing ridges, and limbs.

Crimson-Gold eyes opened.

Massive wings struggled for space.

His tail knocked over what remained of a table.

Zayrlen looked down at himself.

Then immediately panicked again.

A Dragon sound shook the entire room.

Zee stepped closer.

“Hey.”

The Dragon froze.

Zee looked up at his son.

Huge.

Powerful enough to terrify kingdoms someday.

Still his baby.

“You did it.”

Zayrlen lowered his head.

Carefully.

Zee put one hand against the Dragon’s snout.

Heat.

Blood recognition.

Family.

The Crimson Demonic Dragon inside Zee responded so strongly his own eyes flashed.

No jealousy.

No sadness.

Nothing but pride.

“My baby is a Dragon.”

Zayrlen made a sound that was probably intended as:

Dad.

It came out as a thunderous rumble.

Zee smiled.

“Yeah.”

The Dragon nudged him.

Too hard.

Zee slid three feet.

He stared.

Zayrlen froze.

Zee looked offended.

“Control yourself.”

The Dragon made another low sound.

Zee narrowed his eyes.

“You are laughing.”

Zayrlen absolutely was.

⸻

Nobody else entered.

Not yet.

They felt it.

Everyone in Darkheart did.

Zareth, miles away in Drakonis, felt Zee’s emotional explosion through the Dragon Soul Link but did not see the transformation itself.

Victor stood in the damaged council chamber staring toward the far tower.

Rez looked at Ner.

Ner already knew.

“That is him.”

Rez nodded.

“No one goes.”

Victor looked toward them.

“Why?”

Ner answered:

“Because Zee is already there.”

That was enough.

The first moment belonged to father and son.

⸻

Zayrlen remained in Dragon form for almost an hour.

At first he could barely move without breaking something.

Then he learned how to fold his wings.

Mostly.

How to shift his weight.

How not to destroy the floor.

Eventually the pressure began easing.

Zee stayed beside him the entire time.

No lesson plan.

No lecture.

Just presence.

When Zayrlen finally tried changing back, Zee said:

“Do not force it.”

The Dragon looked at him.

“You know what happened last time.”

Zayrlen huffed hot air directly into Zee’s face.

Zee stared.

“Disrespectful in every form.”

Then the Dragon body began to fold.

Crimson-and-Gold light pulled inward.

Wings vanished.

Tail shortened.

Scales receded.

Bones returned.

And suddenly a teenage Zayrlen collapsed forward.

Zee caught him before he touched the ground.

Immediately.

Of course.

⸻

“Dad, I am fine.”

“No.”

“I am.”

Zee grabbed his face.

Checked his eyes.

“Dad.”

His breathing.

Heartbeat.

Hands.

Arms.

Shoulders.

Zayrlen groaned.

“I just became a Dragon and you are checking me like I fell down stairs.”

“You became a Dragon.”

“Yes.”

“My point.”

“That does not make sense.”

Zee touched his forehead.

Zayrlen pushed his hand away.

“Stop.”

“No.”

“I am not five.”

“Unrelated.”

Zayrlen stared.

Then smiled despite himself.

Zee pulled him into a hug.

Teenager.

Dragon.

Prince.

Did not matter.

Zayrlen hugged him back.

For a few seconds, neither said anything.

Then Zee whispered:

“You did good.”

Zayrlen closed his eyes.

“Yeah?”

“Yes.”

That mattered more than everything else.

⸻
