Chapter Eighty-Six

The Prince Vesper Chose

Rez returned to Vesper with fangs.

The city noticed.

Of course it did.

He had barely crossed the southern gate before whispers began moving faster than his horse.

The Crown Prince had died.

The Crown Prince had returned.

The Crown Prince was now Vampire.

The Crown Prince belonged to Zee Darkheart.

That last rumor nearly made Rez turn around and bite someone.

Nearly.

King Lucien called council before sunset.

Twelve Vesper nobles filled the Azure Chamber.

Rez stood beside his father.

Blue-White Mana rested around his fingers.

His heartbeat was slower now.

His senses were sharper.

And every person in the chamber smelled nervous.

Lord Casmir spoke first.

“You cannot inherit Vesper.”

Rez looked at him.

“That was fast.”

“You are no longer purely Vesper.”

Rez glanced toward Lucien.

“Did we have purity requirements yesterday?”

Lucien hid a smile.

Casmir did not.

“You carry Darkheart blood.”

“I carry Zee’s blood.”

“Exactly.”

“No.”

Rez stepped forward.

Blue-White Mana brightened.

“My Witch nature is mine.”

His fangs descended.

“The Vampire nature is mine.”

He looked around the chamber.

“And Vesper is still mine to protect.”

Another noble stood.

“You could be compelled.”

Rez laughed.

“Try.”

Silence.

Nobody volunteered.

Good.

Casmir’s face tightened.

“You expect us to accept that nothing changed?”

Rez became serious.

“No.”

That quieted them.

“Everything changed.”

His voice lost the humor.

“I hear your heartbeats.”

Several nobles stiffened.

“I smell fear.”

Worse.

“I heal faster. I am stronger. I hunger.”

He looked toward his father.

“But I still remember every street I defended while bleeding beside you.”

Then back at the council.

“I remember who stayed.”

His eyes settled on Casmir.

“And who hid.”

Casmir went pale.

Rez smiled faintly.

“So yes.”

“I changed.”

He stepped closer.

“But I did not become Zee.”

Blue-White Mana filled the room.

“I became more Rez.”

Lucien finally stood.

“That is my heir.”

The chamber went silent.

Decision made.

Not by Zee.

Not by Darkheart.

By Vesper.

⸻

The challenge came anyway.

Three nobles withdrew support.

One demanded a succession trial.

Rez accepted.

Not because he needed to.

Because refusing would make the argument survive.

The trial was simple.

Vesper magic.

No Vampire strength.

No blood.

No compulsion.

Only Witchcraft.

Lord Casmir’s chosen champion entered the arena smiling.

Rez entered irritated.

Zee watched through a communication mirror from Darkheart.

Ner stood beside him.

“This feels unfair.”

Zee frowned.

“For Rez?”

“For the other man.”

Rez heard through the mirror.

“I hate both of you.”

The bell rang.

The champion attacked.

Blue lightning.

Rez raised one hand.

Blue-White Mana split it.

Second spell.

Rez redirected it.

Third.

He did not move.

The champion became desperate.

Rez became bored.

Then he finished it.

“Down.”

Blue-White force struck the arena.

The champion hit stone.

Done.

No Vampire nature used.

No fangs.

No borrowed blood.

Vesper Witchcraft.

Rez walked toward Casmir.

“Anything else?”

Casmir said nothing.

Rez smiled.

“Good.”

The crowd began chanting his name.

Not Hybrid.

Not Vampire.

Rez.

That mattered.

⸻

Two weeks later, someone tried to assassinate him.

Politics had clearly learned nothing.

The poison was designed for Vampires.

It ignored Witch biology.

Rez smelled it before drinking.

He looked at the cup.

Then at the servant carrying it.

The servant began crying.

“They have my daughter.”

Rez’s anger vanished.

“Who?”

“Men from House Casmir.”

There.

Not another kingdom.

Not Zee.

Vesper’s own problem.

Rez did not kill the servant.

He found the daughter.

Then found the men.

Casmir had not personally ordered the attempt.

His younger brother had.

That distinction saved Casmir.

It did not save his brother.

Rez handled that himself.

By morning, House Casmir remained standing.

Its guilty members did not.

When Zee heard, he smiled.

Ner looked at him.

“What?”

“He is getting meaner.”

“Your influence.”

“No.”

Zee’s smile widened.

“Rez has always been mean.”

Through a communication mirror, Rez raised one finger.

“I can hear you.”

Ner laughed.

Good.

Vesper had chosen its prince.

Fangs included.
