Chapter Eighty-One

The Third Beginning

The first Wolf-Witch was created four months later.

Her name was Amara Rhone.

Sera’s younger sister.

Twenty-six.

Wolf scout.

Terrible liar.

Excellent tracker.

No Witchcraft.

At least none anyone knew about.

The attack that changed her came during a night patrol beyond Darkheart’s new southern boundary.

Demon raiders struck a refugee camp.

Not for Zee.

Not for hybrids.

For people.

Bodies to sell.

Darkheart defenders stopped them.

Amara stayed behind to cover the evacuation.

By the time Zee arrived, the Demons were dead.

Amara was dying.

Again.

Too late.

Again.

Sera knelt beside her sister.

Blood covered both hands.

“What happened?”

Tessa answered.

“Demon blade through the chest.”

“Vampire blood?”

“Won’t take.”

“Witch healing?”

Kael shook his head.

“Something is consuming the magic.”

Zee crouched.

Amara opened one eye.

“You look terrible.”

Zee almost smiled.

“You have a hole in your chest.”

“Still prettier.”

Sera laughed once through tears.

Then broke.

“Zee.”

He knew.

Ner stood behind him.

Kael and Tessa too.

Proof.

Two lives already.

Two changes.

Still no certainty.

Zee looked at Amara.

“You know what happened to them.”

She glanced toward Kael.

Then Tessa.

“Yes.”

“You may change.”

“Probably.”

“You may still die.”

“Less appealing.”

Sera grabbed her hand.

“Amara—”

Amara looked at her sister.

“I want to live.”

Simple.

Zee opened his wrist.

Amara drank.

Once.

Twice.

The Demon wound reacted.

Black energy surged through her chest.

Her Wolf nature exploded outward.

Claws.

Fangs.

A growl deep enough to shake her body.

Then—

magic answered.

Not Zee’s.

Not borrowed.

Hers.

Green-white Witch light burst from Amara’s hands.

Everyone froze.

Sera stared.

“Amara?”

Amara screamed.

The Demon corruption burned away.

Wolf healing accelerated.

Witch Mana flowed into the wound beside it.

Two natures.

Not Vampire.

Wolf.

Witch.

Zee pulled his wrist away.

Amara’s heartbeat steadied.

Ner’s eyes widened.

“Zee.”

“I know.”

“No.”

Ner moved closer.

Pure Gold spread around Amara.

“There was Witch blood already inside her.”

Sera frowned.

“That’s impossible.”

Ner shook his head.

“Dormant.”

Old.

Buried somewhere in the family line.

Zee’s blood had not invented Witchcraft from nothing.

It had awakened what was already waiting.

Amara opened her eyes.

Wolf-gold.

Then Witch light flashed through them.

She looked down at her hands.

A small green flame hovered above one palm.

“Oh.”

Kael smiled.

“That seems inconvenient.”

Tessa elbowed him.

Amara stared at Zee.

“What did you do?”

Zee looked at Kael.

Witch-Vampire.

Then Tessa.

Vampire-Wolf.

Then Amara.

Wolf-Witch.

Three beginnings.

Three people.

Three lives that should have ended.

“I gave you a chance.”

Amara looked at the flame.

Then at her claws.

Sera laughed through tears.

“You are going to be unbearable.”

Amara smiled weakly.

“I already was.”

Good.

Still her.

⸻

The name came weeks later.

Wolf-Witch.

Again simple.

Again accurate.

Again chosen by the person living it.

Amara refused every dramatic alternative.

Kael suggested Moon Witch.

She threw a cup at him.

Tessa suggested Fangcaster.

Amara threatened murder.

Zee approved of neither.

Ner laughed hardest.

By winter, Darkheart had three original hybrids living inside its borders.

Kael Marrin.

Witch-Vampire.

Tessa Vale.

Vampire-Wolf.

Amara Rhone.

Wolf-Witch.

None created as soldiers.

All created because Zee refused another grave when his blood might offer life.

That distinction did not survive outside Darkheart.

Foreign reports said:

THE TRIBRID HAS COMPLETED THREE HYBRID BLOODLINES.

Another:

DARKHEART CAN NOW COMBINE THE THREE GREAT SUPERNATURAL RACES.

Another:

KING ZEE IS BUILDING AN ARMY THAT WILL REPLACE WITCHES, VAMPIRES, AND WOLVES.

False.

Terrifying.

Effective.

Then six kingdoms closed their borders to Darkheart citizens.

Two began mobilizing soldiers.

A Vampire court offered an alliance.

Three Wolf clans requested sanctuary.

A Witch House secretly asked whether Zee would save their dying heir.

And somewhere beyond the eastern mountains, an unknown kingdom issued a bounty for one thing.

Not Zee’s death.

His blood.

Zee read the notice in the throne room.

Rez stood beside him.

Ner leaned against the table.

Zareth, human for once, read over Zee’s shoulder.

“How much?” Zareth asked.

Zee looked at the number.

Then frowned.

“That is insulting.”

Rez laughed.

Ner stared at him.

“Someone wants to bleed you dry.”

“Yes.”

“And your concern is the price?”

“It should be higher.”

Zareth nodded seriously.

“I agree.”

Ner closed his eyes.

“I hate all of you.”

Zee smiled.

Then folded the bounty notice.

His eyes shifted into slits.

Three hybrids now existed because people tried to kill Darkheart.

Three new bloodlines.

Three survivors.

The world had feared what Zee might create.

So it attacked.

And the attacks created exactly what it feared.

Zee looked toward the growing kingdom beyond the windows.

“Let them come.”

Ner’s expression sharpened.

“Careful.”

Zee looked at him.

Then smiled.

Not reckless.

Not young.

Not waiting either.

“They keep teaching me what my people need.”

And that was the problem.

Every attack made Darkheart harder to kill.

Every loss made Zee more willing to change the rules.

And the Age of Hybrids had only begun.
