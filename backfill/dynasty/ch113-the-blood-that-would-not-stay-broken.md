Chapter One Hundred Thirteen

The Blood That Would Not Stay Broken

The Glass Mountains reflected sunlight like knives.

The Order of the Hollow Crown had built beneath them because the crystalline stone disrupted ordinary magical tracking.

It did not disrupt Zee.

Not anymore.

The King’s Hunt read movement.

Blood Mark recognized the signature carried by the dead infiltrator.

Witch sight found artificial seams in the mountain.

Wolf instinct smelled people living where no settlement should exist.

Phoenix wings carried Zee over the last ridge.

Behind him came Darkheart forces.

Kael.

Amara.

Rhyven.

Ner remained with the second line exactly as promised.

For eleven minutes.

Then he walked forward.

Zee landed beside him.

“You said second line.”

“This is the second line.”

“It moved.”

“So did I.”

Zee stared.

Ner smiled.

Pregnant.

Dangerous.

Impossible.

Zee loved him.

“Stay behind me.”

“No.”

“Ner.”

“I will stay beside you.”

Zee considered arguing.

Then the mountain opened.

The Order attacked.

Argument over.

⸻

Glass soldiers emerged from concealed gates.

Not actual glass.

Witches wearing mirrored armor designed to reflect magical targeting.

Behind them came experimental creatures.

Not stitched corpses this time.

Living failures.

A Vampire with Demon bone growing through his spine.

A Witch whose arms had become blackened claws.

A Wolf with two hearts beating out of rhythm.

The Hollow Crown had learned from the bone vessels.

Not how to create.

How to break more carefully.

Rhyven saw the Wolf.

His expression changed.

“Alive?”

Zee listened.

“Yes.”

The creature charged.

Rhyven met it.

Not to kill.

To stop.

Wolf form struck Wolf form.

Rhyven’s Phoenix vitality absorbed the impact.

The altered Wolf snapped at his throat.

Rhyven rolled beneath it.

Crimson fire moved through his claws.

He deliberately kept the flames away from flesh.

Amara joined him.

Two Wolves pinning one.

Prisoner.

Not enemy.

Zee moved past them.

Good.

His people had learned his distinctions too.

⸻

The Order’s Witches raised mirrored barriers.

Zee did not slow.

Vampire speed.

Witch sight.

One structural weakness.

“Sever.”

The first barrier failed.

The second reflected the command.

Interesting.

The returning magic struck Zee.

His own severing force cut across his shoulder.

Blood opened.

Phoenix flame sealed part of it.

The Order’s Magister smiled from behind the third barrier.

“We studied you.”

Zee looked at the wound.

Then smiled.

“Not enough.”

He flew.

Straight upward.

The barrier had been designed for frontal Witch assault.

Not a winged predator entering from the ceiling.

Zee came through crystal.

Crimson Phoenix wings shielded his face.

Dragon durability took the impact.

Wolf instinct found the Magister before dust cleared.

Vampire speed closed distance.

One synchronized movement.

The Magister barely raised his hand.

Zee broke it.

“Where are the prisoners?”

Silence.

Heart Command pressed.

“Answer.”

“Below.”

“How many?”

“Forty-eight.”

“Children?”

The man’s heart changed.

Zee heard the lie forming.

“Answer correctly.”

“Six.”

Zee’s expression disappeared.

The Magister started begging before Zee touched him again.

Smart.

Useless.

⸻

Ner reached the lower chambers first.

Not because Zee allowed it.

Because Bloodsong found forty-eight terrified rhythms beneath the mountain and Ner followed them.

His Pure Gold Mana opened the first door.

Inside were cages.

He went very still.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat changed.

Fast.

Wolf agitation.

Ner placed one hand over his stomach.

“I know.”

The nearest prisoner looked up.

A young Witch man.

Black veins covered half his face.

One eye burned red.

The other remained blue.

His hands shook uncontrollably.

Ner approached.

The prisoner recoiled.

“Do not touch me.”

“I was not going to.”

“They put Demon marrow in me.”

Ner’s expression hardened.

“How long?”

“Twenty days.”

“Your Witchcraft?”

“Still there.”

“What is failing?”

The man laughed weakly.

“Everything.”

His heartbeat stumbled.

Ner immediately called through Bloodsong.

Not words.

A signal Zee recognized from floors above.

Urgent.

Zee arrived seconds later.

He looked at the prisoner.

Then smelled it.

Witch.

Demon.

Neither stable.

The Order had tried combining them through marrow transplantation.

It had failed.

The Demon source was consuming the Witch structure.

The Witch Mana was burning the Demon tissue.

The body sat between them.

Dying.

Tessa was still several chambers away.

Zee crouched.

“Name.”

The prisoner blinked.

“What?”

“Your name.”

“Elias.”

Zee’s expression changed for half a second.

Then settled.

Not prophecy.

Not future connection.

Just a name.

“Elias what?”

“Elias Marr.”

“Do you want to live?”

The Witch stared.

“Yes.”

“Do you understand living may mean changing?”

A pause.

“Yes.”

“Do you understand I cannot promise what shape?”

Fear.

Then resolve.

“Yes.”

Zee looked at Ner.

Ner already understood.

“Two bloodlines.”

“Witch and Demon.”

“Their version is killing him.”

“Yes.”

Ner looked at Elias.

Then back at Zee.

“Can yours make them stop fighting?”

Zee looked deeper.

His blood answered.

Demon source.

Not a birth nature inside him.

A stable pattern integrated into his Crimson Demonic Dragon evolution.

Enough to create from.

Witch.

One of his original natures.

He could feel the bridge.

This did not need three.

It needed two done correctly.

Zee cut his palm.

“Drink.”

Elias hesitated.

Then obeyed.

⸻

The change was uglier than Rhyven’s.

No fire rebirth.

No Dragon core.

Demon biology fought through the bones.

Elias screamed.

His Witch Mana exploded around the cage.

Ner raised Pure Gold barriers around the nearby prisoners.

Zee held Elias down.

The man’s blackened veins spread.

Then stopped.

Crimson blood reached them.

Did not erase Demon.

Did not erase Witch.

Rewrote the conflict.

Witch Mana began forming channels around the Demon structure instead of attacking it.

Demon marrow stopped consuming the magic and became an anchor for it.

Elias’s hands transformed.

Black claws appeared.

Then receded when he panicked.

His eyes changed.

Both became blue again.

Then thin black rings formed around the irises.

His Witch Mana returned.

Not Gold.

Not Crimson.

His original deep blue.

But now black demonic sigils lived inside it.

One stable expression.

Elias screamed once more.

Then the two rhythms became one.

Witch.

Demon.

Stable.

A new race.

Zee listened.

Alive.

Strong.

Still Elias.

He released him.

Elias lay shaking.

“What am I?”

Zee looked at the blue Witch light curling around black claws.

“Demon–Witch.”

Ner stared.

“Another first.”

“Yes.”

Elias looked terrified.

Zee crouched.

“You are not theirs.”

The Witch looked at him.

“They made this.”

“No.”

Zee’s eyes hardened.

“They broke you.”

Then he offered his hand.

“I made sure you survived it.”

Elias took it.

⸻

The Hollow Crown realized its lower prison had been breached.

Every experiment was ordered terminated.

That killed any possibility of mercy.

Glass vents opened.

Black gas flooded the cages.

Ner smelled it first.

“Poison.”

Zee’s wings opened.

“Hold your breath.”

Ner looked offended.

“I am Vampire.”

“Still.”

Zee slammed both Phoenix wings outward.

Crimson fire entered the ventilation channels.

Not into the prisoners.

Into the poison.

The gas ignited.

Burned away before reaching the cages.

Above them, bells sounded.

Execution protocol.

Zee looked toward the ceiling.

His expression became calm.

Rhyven arrived carrying an unconscious Wolf over one shoulder.

Amara behind him with two children.

Kael kicked open another cage.

“They are killing their own prisoners.”

Zee’s eyes became slits.

“Get everyone out.”

Kael looked at him.

“And you?”

Zee’s altered horns glowed faintly.

“Closing another institution.”

No one asked what that meant.

⸻

The Order of the Hollow Crown died in its own monastery.

Not every person inside.

Prisoners lived.

Servants who had not participated were removed.

Two young initiates who dropped their weapons and had never touched a captive were restrained instead of killed.

Distinctions remained.

But the researchers?

Dead.

The guards who mutilated prisoners?

Dead.

The Witches who designed forced bloodline grafts?

Dead.

The officers who ordered termination?

Dead.

The Magister who funded the child experiments?

Zee found him beneath the central dome.

The man had surrounded himself with twelve mirrored barriers.

Zee landed before them.

The Magister smiled nervously.

“You cannot break all twelve before the mountain collapses.”

Zee looked upward.

Cracks already spreading.

Then toward the prisoners evacuating through Ner’s Gold corridors.

“You are right.”

The Magister relaxed.

Zee raised one hand.

“Silence.”

Every spoken activation inside the barriers died.

Then another hand.

“Sever.”

Not all twelve.

One connection.

The shared anchor.

All twelve barriers failed together.

The Magister stared.

Zee stepped through.

“You learned from me.”

“Yes.”

Zee grabbed him.

“You should have learned the important part.”

“What?”

“Hurt my people.”

Crimson Phoenix flame spread across Zee’s hand.

“You die.”

The Magister burned.

Zee did not look away.

⸻

The mountain began collapsing.

Ner was still inside.

Zee felt his heartbeat.

Then Zayrlen’s.

Both moving.

Too slow.

“Ner.”

Bloodsong answered through the stone.

Working on it.

Zee launched downward.

Phoenix wings folded tightly through the corridor.

He found Ner beneath a broken arch, Pure Gold Mana holding twenty prisoners beneath a shield.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat hammered.

Not distress.

Reaction.

The unborn Wolf felt danger.

Crimson and Gold light spread across Ner’s abdomen.

Then something impossible happened.

A Dragon pulse rolled outward.

Heat.

Not Phoenix.

Dragon.

The broken arch above Ner cracked again.

Before Zee reached him, Pure Gold Witch Mana surged from Ner.

For one second, another magical rhythm moved beneath it.

Not Ner’s.

Zayrlen’s.

Crimson-gold.

The collapsing stone shifted sideways.

Barely.

An instinctive unborn magical reaction.

No conscious casting.

No awakened nature.

Just developing power responding to danger.

Zee landed.

His face changed.

Ner looked down at himself.

“Did he just—”

“Yes.”

“Is that bad?”

“No.”

Zee grabbed the stone.

Dragon strength lifted.

“Later.”

Together they cleared the corridor.

Ner maintained Gold around the prisoners.

Zee used Phoenix wings as a shield against falling glass.

Rhyven carried the injured.

Elias Marr walked under his own power.

Blue-black Witch Mana held one side of the tunnel.

New blood.

Old family.

Unborn life.

All moving together.

They escaped seconds before the monastery collapsed.

⸻

Darkheart brought forty-eight prisoners home.

Six children.

All alive.

Three were too damaged for transformation and remained under Tessa’s care.

Two asked Zee to change them.

He refused.

They did not need it to survive.

That answer spread too.

Zee was capable of creating races.

That did not mean he would give anyone whatever bloodline they wanted.

Rhyven understood.

Elias understood faster.

He spent the first week terrified of sleeping.

Demon instincts came with dreams.

Witch Mana answered emotion more sharply.

But neither side tried to destroy the other anymore.

His deep-blue magic now carried black sigils naturally.

When he cast, his claws sometimes appeared.

When anger rose, Demon strength entered his body.

When Witch precision returned, the Demon response followed structure instead of chaos.

One race.

Demon–Witch.

New.

Stable.

Alive.

⸻

The world learned about him within weeks.

It learned the wrong lesson again.

Rulers began listing what Zee could now create.

Phoenix.

Dragon.

Demon.

Witch.

Vampire.

Wolf.

Simple combinations.

Tribrids.

Unknown future variants.

The lists grew.

Zee ignored them.

He cared about something else.

Ner entered the seventh month.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat had become loud enough that Zee sometimes heard it from across a room.

On the next full moon, it changed again.

Ner woke before Zee.

Crimson and Gold light filled the bedroom.

Not bright.

Soft.

Alive.

Zee opened his eyes.

Ner sat upright with one hand over his stomach.

“What is he doing?”

Zee listened.

Wolf rhythm.

Witch structure.

Dragon heat.

All three.

Natural.

Synchronized before birth only as developing instinct, not active power.

Zee moved closer.

Then stopped.

Ner looked at him.

“What?”

Zee’s Phoenix wings had opened.

Completely.

Curved around the bed.

A shield.

Ner stared.

Then slowly smiled.

“You did it again.”

Zee looked at the wings.

“So?”

“You are shielding us from nothing.”

Zee listened beyond the walls.

No threat.

No assassin.

No spell.

Nothing.

He slowly folded one wing.

Ner caught the other.

“Leave it.”

Zee looked at him.

Ner leaned against the crimson feathers.

Warm.

Not burning him.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat settled beneath them.

For a few minutes, the Age of New Blood did not matter.

The kingdoms experimenting in secret did not matter.

The next race did not matter.

The next war did not matter.

There was only Zee.

Ner.

And the son growing between Crimson and Gold.

Then the eastern alarm bell rang.

Zee closed his eyes.

Ner sighed.

“The world has terrible timing.”

“Yes.”

Zee stood.

Phoenix wings opened.

Ner stood too.

Seven months pregnant.

Pure Gold Mana already gathering around one hand.

Zee looked at him.

Ner raised an eyebrow.

“Do not.”

Zee almost smiled.

Then they went to see who had made the next mistake.
