Chapter One Hundred Thirty-Four

The Father Who Came Back

Zee found him at dusk.

Not by sight.

By blood.

The trail had been impossible all day.

No stable scent.

No heartbeat pattern he could trust.

No ordinary Witch signature.

No Phoenix trail that behaved like Pyrathis.

Every method gave him the same infuriating answer.

Known.

Kin.

Wrong.

Zee stopped at the edge of an abandoned stone field beyond Darkheart’s western roads.

The old ruins had once belonged to a minor Witch garrison.

Now half the walls were gone.

Trees grew through the barracks.

Wind moved through broken arches.

Nothing else.

Zee stood quietly.

No visible fire.

No open wings.

No announcement.

He had learned.

“If you are here,” he said, “you have ten seconds before I stop being polite.”

Nothing.

Zee smiled faintly.

“Good.”

Then the first black-crimson blade of Witchfire struck from behind.

⸻

Zee moved before impact.

Barely.

The attack cut through his coat and burned across the scales beneath.

Scarlet fire followed the wound.

Not his.

The familiarity hit immediately.

His own Phoenix blood surged in answer.

Victor remained hidden behind distortion.

No face.

No name.

Only the same impossible magical presence.

Zee turned.

“Found you.”

A second strike came from above.

Then a third from the left.

Different angles.

Old Darkheart Witchcraft.

Zee knew the architecture even though the exact spells had evolved beyond anything used in Victor’s reign.

That made no sense.

He answered with Witch precision.

“Sever.”

The nearest sigil split.

Victor countered.

“Close.”

The broken spell folded back together.

Zee’s expression changed.

That word.

That rhythm.

Old.

Familiar in a place memory did not want to touch.

Victor attacked again.

Zee opened his Phoenix wings.

Black-crimson feathers now mixed through the Crimson.

Scarlet blood-fire erupted.

Victor’s own flame met it.

The collision shook the ruins.

Zee slid backward.

Victor did too.

For one breath, neither moved.

Then Zee looked toward the concealed figure.

“Who taught you Darkheart Witchcraft?”

No answer.

Wrong choice.

Zee disappeared in Vampire speed.

⸻

Victor anticipated the direct attack.

Zee did not finish it.

He vanished halfway.

Wolf instinct changed angle.

Khandra Parbat changed rhythm.

Victor turned toward where Zee should have been.

Zee came from below.

Dragon strength drove one shoulder into Victor’s ribs.

They crashed through a wall.

Victor rolled.

Zee followed.

Claws came out.

Victor caught his wrist.

Black-crimson Phoenix flame exploded between their hands.

Both recoiled.

The blood resonance became unbearable.

Zee stared at his own palm.

Then at Victor’s concealed one.

Related.

Not metaphorically.

Biologically.

His Vampire senses screamed ancestry.

His Witch Mana pulled toward the stranger.

His Phoenix fire kept answering before Zee commanded it.

Even his Wolf hesitated.

Enemy.

Kin.

Enemy.

Kin.

Zee hated the contradiction.

He attacked harder.

⸻

Victor’s concealment survived the first three collisions.

Not the fourth.

Zee stopped trying to break the entire spell.

He watched.

Waited.

Let Victor cast.

Then struck one point.

“Sever.”

The distortion cracked across the chest.

Victor immediately reinforced it.

Too late.

Zee saw a jawline.

Older.

Familiar.

Impossible.

He froze.

Victor saw the recognition begin.

Zee’s face changed.

“No.”

Victor stepped back.

Zee did not move.

The concealment failed completely.

Victor Darkheart stood inside the ruins.

Alive.

Older.

Scarred.

Black-crimson fire moving around one hand.

Scarlet beneath.

Zee stared.

For once, nothing came out.

No threat.

No sarcasm.

No violence.

Just shock.

Victor looked exactly wrong.

Alive when he should not be.

Breathing when Zee knew he had stopped.

Standing when Zee had already buried that chapter of his life.

Zee’s voice came quietly.

“I killed you.”

Victor said nothing.

Zee took one step closer.

His Crimson-Gold eyes did not leave Victor’s face.

“I killed you.”

“I know.”

That voice.

Real.

Not illusion.

Not mimicry.

Not resurrection spell wearing a corpse.

Victor.

Zee’s expression emptied.

“How?”

Victor’s black-crimson flame moved once.

Then died down.

“Death woke something in me.”

Zee stared at the fire.

Then at Victor.

Phoenix.

The realization came piece by piece.

Victor died.

Then returned.

Phoenix bloodline.

Dormant.

Inherited.

The deeper fire inside Zee.

His own awakening.

Everything turned at once.

Zee’s face changed.

“You.”

Victor did not answer fast enough.

Zee looked toward the black-crimson fire.

Then remembered Darkheart burning.

The royal residence.

The nursery.

The false strike toward Zayrlen.

His voice dropped.

“You attacked my home.”

Victor inhaled.

“Yes.”

“You aimed at my son.”

“No.”

Zee’s eyes became slits.

“You made me believe you did.”

Victor held his ground.

“Yes.”

Silence.

Worse than shouting.

⸻

Victor spoke first.

“It was a test.”

Zee stared at him.

Victor continued because he had already crossed too many lines to stop halfway.

“I watched you with the boy.”

Zee’s entire body went still.

Victor saw that and immediately knew he had chosen the worst possible wording.

“You watched my son.”

“Yes.”

“You watched us.”

“Yes.

“And then you attacked Darkheart.”

Victor’s jaw tightened.

“I needed to know what you had become.”

Zee laughed once.

No humor.

“You could have asked.”

“I did not know if you would let me live long enough.”

“You were correct.”

Victor almost smiled.

Did not.

“I needed to see what you would choose when fear came.”

Zee’s expression vanished.

There.

The real wound.

Victor continued.

“I remembered what I chose.”

“Do not.”

“I remembered what you were.”

Zee stepped closer.

“I said do not.”

Victor kept going.

“You were impossible. The world reacted to your birth. I was afraid of what you would become.”

“And you murdered me.”

“Yes.”

No excuse.

That mattered.

Not enough.

Victor looked toward Zee’s face.

“I wanted to know whether you would become the same kind of father when faced with an impossible child.”

Zee stared.

The words landed slowly.

Then violently.

“You used Zayrlen.”

Victor’s face tightened.

“I never intended to harm him.”

“You used him.”

“I controlled every lethal edge.”

“You used my son.”

“Zee—”

“You watched my two-month-old child.”

Scarlet-black fire appeared along Zee’s wings.

“And decided to make me think he was about to die.”

Victor did not answer.

Could not.

Zee’s voice became very quiet.

“You should have stayed dead.”

Then he attacked.

⸻

The second fight had no testing in it.

No uncertainty.

No need to identify.

Father.

That made everything worse.

Zee hit Victor with Vampire speed and Dragon force hard enough to break three ribs instantly.

Victor answered with a blast of Abyssal Titan Phoenix Sovereign fire that tore Zee through two stone walls.

Zee came back before the rubble settled.

Wolf aggression.

Witch precision.

Crimson Sovereign fully synchronized.

Phoenix wings slicing through smoke.

Victor met him with Witchcraft.

“Still.”

Zee’s body slowed.

“Break.”

The command shattered.

Victor’s eyes widened.

Zee crossed the distance.

Fangs sank into Victor’s shoulder.

Victor roared and exploded into black-crimson fire.

Zee was thrown off.

Blood in his mouth.

His father’s blood.

That made the resonance unbearable.

Something inside Zee unlocked completely.

⸻

Victor felt it first.

His own bloodline answered Zee’s bite.

The ancient Phoenix inheritance roared between them.

Then Zee’s acquired Crimson Phoenix blood surged through it.

Not yielding.

Rewriting.

Witch.

Vampire.

Wolf.

Dragon.

Crimson Demonic Dragon.

Phoenix.

Crimson Sovereign.

Everything inside Zee stopped behaving like separate layers.

Victor stared.

“No.”

Zee’s wings opened.

Too wide.

The black-crimson feathers spread rapidly.

Scarlet blood-fire flooded every plume.

His bones shifted.

This time Zee did not force them back.

His spine lengthened.

Shoulders tore into new structure.

Arms changed.

Legs folded into enormous taloned limbs.

Humanoid shape disappeared beneath fire.

The transformation swallowed him.

Not silhouette.

Not projection.

Real.

Complete.

⸻

The Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix opened its wings.

The ruins vanished beneath shadow.

Massive.

Black-crimson feathers layered across a colossal body.

Scarlet-crimson blood-fire moved beneath them like living veins.

Every feather burned without consuming itself.

Crimson-Gold eyes opened in a head large enough to look down on Victor from above.

Not ordinary Phoenix eyes.

Zee’s eyes.

Predatory.

Recognizable.

The beak curved brutally.

When it opened—

fangs showed inside.

Not decorative.

Predatory Vampire inheritance rewritten into the form.

Crimson Witch power cracked across the Phoenix body like lightning.

Sigils appeared between feathers without being cast separately.

Wolf instinct lived in the stance.

Dragon force lived in the bones.

Phoenix fire lived everywhere.

Crimson Sovereign did not disappear.

It existed within the whole.

Not as the species.

As Zee’s perfected Witch-Vampire-Wolf synchronization inside something larger.

The Crimson Demonic Dragon remained part of him too.

Not erased.

Integrated.

Victor stood beneath that impossible creature and finally understood.

This was not a Tribrid carrying too many acquired powers.

This was one biology.

One supernatural organism.

Unified.

New.

Victor whispered:

“Abysscin.”

Zee’s Phoenix head lowered.

Victor stared up.

“The First Natural Abysscin.”

The words carried awe despite himself.

Not title.

Species.

Zee had been born the First Natural Tribrid.

That history remained.

But this—

this was what he had become.

Victor looked at the enormous Phoenix body.

Then at the scarlet-black fire.

“The Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix.”

The form.

Not the species.

Different.

Clear.

Zee’s roar answered.

The ruins shook.

Victor smiled despite the danger.

“My son.”

That was the wrong thing to say.

⸻

Zee hit him.

The Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix moved with horrifying speed for something that size.

One talon struck Victor before his Witchcraft finished forming.

Bones broke.

He crossed the field.

Hit stone.

Rose in black-crimson fire.

His own Abyssal Titan Phoenix Sovereign power erupted fully.

Victor did not transform into Zee’s form.

His bloodline remained his own.

Ancient.

Powerful.

Scarlet-black flame towered around him.

Witch sigils formed in layers.

He launched everything at Zee.

Zee burned through it.

Not immune.

Not untouched.

His feathers tore.

Blood-fire scattered.

Victor’s magic burned through one side.

Regeneration answered instantly.

Frighteningly.

The form rebuilt itself while attacking.

Victor finally understood how dangerous Zee’s rewrite had become.

The Abysscin was not merely stronger.

It was coherent.

No internal argument.

No borrowed structures fighting for priority.

One creature.

Zee.

⸻

Victor struck the Phoenix throat with a Witch spear.

Zee caught it in his beak.

Bit down.

The spear shattered.

Fangs snapped through enchanted metal.

Victor stared.

“That is excessive.”

Zee answered with scarlet fire directly into his chest.

Victor screamed.

His own Phoenix inheritance prevented immediate destruction.

Not enough.

Zee hit him again.

Talon through abdomen.

Wing strike breaking the remaining ward.

Witch pressure crushing every escape line.

Victor tried to rise.

Zee’s Wolf instinct anticipated it.

A talon pinned him.

Crimson-Gold eyes looked down.

Victor breathed hard.

Blood on his mouth.

Fire across his body.

Still powerful.

Still his father.

Still the man who had killed a newborn because fear felt easier than love.

Victor looked up.

“I know the test was wrong.”

Zee’s Phoenix mouth opened.

The layered voice came through with a cry beneath it.

“You used my son.”

“Yes.”

“You attacked my people.”

“Yes.”

“I killed you once.”

Victor looked tired.

“I remember.”

Zee lowered his head.

“Then remember this too.”

The Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix drove one talon through Victor’s heart.

Then Witch power detonated through the wound.

Victor’s body convulsed.

Phoenix fire surged.

Failed.

His heart stopped.

His eyes went empty.

Real death.

Again.

Zee held him there long enough to know.

No heartbeat.

No breath.

No Witch response.

No Phoenix fire.

Dead.

Victor Darkheart died by his son’s hand for the second time.

⸻

Zee stood over the body.

Still in true Phoenix form.

Scarlet blood-fire moved across the enormous black-crimson feathers.

No triumph.

No peace.

No closure.

Only fury slowly cooling into something uglier.

Confusion.

His father had been alive.

His father had resurrected.

His father had carried the same ancient Phoenix inheritance.

His father had watched him.

Tested him.

Used Zayrlen to do it.

Then called him—

Abysscin.

Zee stared at the corpse.

The name sat inside his mind.

Not understood.

But recognized somewhere deeper than language.

Then the Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix threw its head back and screamed.

The sound crossed miles.

Darkheart heard it.

Zareth felt it through the Dragon Soul Link.

Ner felt the change in Zayrlen’s heartbeat.

Rez looked toward the western horizon.

Something had happened.

Something enormous.

Zee remained there.

Phoenix.

True.

Complete.

For the first time.

And Victor Darkheart lay dead beneath him.

Again.

⸻
