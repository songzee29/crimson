Chapter One Hundred Thirty-Three

How Far a Father Goes

Zee did not sleep.

Tessa wanted him examined.

Elias wanted blood samples.

Rez wanted the attack mapped.

Zareth wanted to hunt.

Ner wanted the unknown attacker dead.

Zee wanted all of it.

In that order?

No.

That was the difference now.

Before Chapter One Hundred Twenty-Eight, Zee might have flown into the forest immediately.

Now he sat at the council table while scarlet-black fire moved slowly through black-crimson feathers along both wings.

He let everyone think he was staying.

Then he lied.

⸻

Rez studied the attack points.

“Four external manifestations. One royal strike.”

Elias nodded.

“No conventional casting trail.”

Zareth’s eyes narrowed.

“The fire interacted with Zee’s blood.”

“Yes.”

Ner sat nearby with Zayrlen asleep against him.

He looked toward Zee.

“What did it feel like?”

Zee answered carefully.

“Familiar.”

Again.

That word.

He hated it more every time.

“Blood familiar?”

Rez asked.

“Yes.”

“Family?”

Zee’s expression changed.

“My family is here.”

Not all of it.

He did not know that.

Elias looked toward the wings.

“Whatever woke inside you reacted before the attack actually hit.”

Zee nodded.

“Blood calling to blood.”

“Probably.”

Zee hated probabilities too.

⸻

A messenger entered.

Rez took the note.

Read.

Then looked toward Zee.

“Merek Voss passed through Ravelin Cross nine days ago.”

Small.

Concrete.

Ambush thread still alive.

“Direction?” Zee asked.

“Southwest.”

“Alive?”

“Yes.”

Good.

Not tonight.

Different threat.

Separate.

Zee looked back toward the western window.

The thing that attacked Darkheart had no connection to Merek.

He felt that instinctively.

Not proof.

Enough not to merge threads carelessly.

That would come later.

⸻

“Zee,” Ner said.

He looked toward him.

“You are planning something.”

“No.”

Zayrlen made a tiny sound in his sleep.

Ner smiled faintly.

“Even he knows that is a lie.”

Zee stood.

Everyone watched.

“I am not chasing the attacker.”

Rez immediately distrusted that sentence.

“What are you doing?”

“Giving it something to watch.”

Now Rez smiled.

Good.

⸻

An hour later, Darkheart moved visibly east.

Rez took twenty guards.

Kael joined him.

Elias lit false tracking sigils.

Zareth launched from the eastern wall in true Dragon form.

Huge.

Obvious.

Impossible to miss.

Anyone watching would believe Darkheart had identified an eastern trail.

That was intentional.

Zee went west.

Alone.

Without visible Phoenix fire.

He suppressed the strange bloodline as much as he could.

His wings folded tightly.

Then vanished beneath concealment and clothing.

Wolf scent.

Vampire hearing.

Witch perception.

King’s Hunt.

No spectacle.

No obvious pursuit.

He moved like Nanda Parbat had taught him.

Quiet.

Patient.

Wrong direction.

⸻

Victor realized the deception thirty minutes too late.

He stood near an old river crossing west of Darkheart.

Black-crimson fire barely visible beneath one palm.

His concealment remained intact.

He felt Zee’s blood far away.

East.

Then—

closer.

Victor turned.

Nothing.

A Wolf scent trail moved north.

False.

A Witch signature moved east.

False.

Phoenix heat flared south.

False.

Victor’s expression tightened.

Zee had changed.

Not just grown stronger.

Changed.

He was no longer giving the battlefield one version of himself.

Victor almost smiled.

Then remembered why.

Someone had beaten his son badly enough to teach him.

The smile died.

⸻

Zee followed the biological familiarity instead.

Not scent exactly.

Not heartbeat.

Something beneath both.

Every time he moved closer, his blood reacted.

Wolf instinct hesitated.

Enemy.

Kin.

Enemy.

Kin.

His Vampire senses recognized an ancestry note he had never consciously smelled before.

His Witch Mana resonated around certain trees.

Phoenix fire kept trying to ignite along his fingertips.

Zee clenched both hands.

“Stop.”

The fire obeyed.

For three seconds.

Then returned.

He hated this.

⸻

Victor moved.

Zee heard it.

Barely.

One displaced pebble.

One branch shifting wrong.

He did not follow directly.

Instead Zee turned north.

Made noise.

Let his damaged coat scrape bark.

Then stopped behind a ruined retaining wall.

Waited.

Nothing.

Long enough that most people would assume the trail was lost.

Zee remained still.

Then Victor crossed behind him.

Wrong move.

Zee attacked.

⸻

Witchcraft hit first.

“Hold.”

Victor countered without revealing himself.

A black-crimson sigil shattered the command.

Zee’s eyes widened.

Old Witchcraft.

Darkheart Witchcraft.

Not identical.

Related.

He struck again.

“Break.”

Victor answered with:

“Still.”

The two commands collided.

The forest cracked between them.

Zee moved through the impact.

Vampire speed.

Victor’s Phoenix reflex pulled him backward.

Not far enough.

Zee caught fabric.

Victor tore free before his face entered the moonlight.

No clear view.

Only a hand.

Older.

Scarred.

Then Phoenix fire erupted between them.

Victor’s black-crimson flame.

Zee’s Crimson fire.

They collided.

The world exploded scarlet.

⸻

Zee screamed.

Not pain alone.

Transformation pressure.

His heartbeat thundered.

The hidden inheritance inside him opened wider than ever before.

Black-crimson feathers spread across both wings.

Scarlet veins ran through them like liquid fire.

The wings expanded.

Larger.

Broader.

Predatory.

His cracked horn healed completely.

The lingering ache in his ribs vanished.

The damaged eye cleared.

Every remaining injury from Chapter One Hundred Twenty-Eight burned away beneath violent regeneration.

Dragon scales glowed from underneath.

Scarlet fire poured from between them.

Massive talons made from blood-flame formed over Zee’s hands.

His spine arched.

Bones shifted.

Not enough to break.

Enough to change intention.

Something inside his body was trying to become something else.

Bigger.

Winged.

Ancient.

A colossal Phoenix outline erupted around him.

Black-crimson.

Scarlet.

Crimson-Gold eyes.

A hooked predatory face.

Fangs suggested inside the shape where a normal Phoenix should not have them.

Witch lightning cracked around its body.

Zee’s mouth opened.

A Phoenix cry came out beneath his voice.

Victor stared.

Stunned.

His own flame bent toward Zee.

Not submission.

Recognition.

Kinship.

Then alteration.

Victor understood.

The bloodline inside Zee had stopped simply awakening.

It was fusing.

His son’s acquired Crimson Phoenix blood was rewriting the inheritance into something new.

Something Victor had never possessed.

Something no old name fully covered.

The beginning of a Crimson Abyssal Phoenix.

⸻

Zee’s body tried shifting farther.

Shoulders changed.

Wing joints widened.

Scarlet fire flooded his throat.

His fingers curled beneath phantom talons.

The Phoenix silhouette closed around him.

Too much.

Too fast.

No mastery.

Zee forced his knees straight.

“No.”

His voice layered with another cry.

The fire surged.

Victor watched.

Zee clenched both fists.

Witch discipline.

Crimson Sovereign control.

Wolf grounding.

Vampire focus.

Dragon endurance.

Father.

Home.

Zayrlen.

That was the anchor.

Not fear.

Purpose.

Zee forced the change backward.

Bone settled.

Hands remained hands.

Wings remained physical Phoenix wings instead of something larger.

The enormous silhouette flickered.

Then stayed behind him without swallowing him.

Zee breathed hard.

Scarlet-black fire poured from his mouth once.

Then stopped.

His eyes remained blazing.

He looked toward Victor’s concealed position.

“What are you?”

Victor did not answer.

Zee stepped forward.

Victor stepped back.

Then Zee smiled.

Cold.

“You are afraid.”

Victor hated that he was right.

⸻

Zee changed direction.

Not toward Victor.

Past him.

Victor realized too late.

Zee had noticed the escape route.

He cut it off.

The post-defeat lesson.

Do not chase where the enemy wants.

Take away where they intend to go.

Victor turned.

Zee was already there.

Wings spread.

Scarlet-black fire behind him.

“You attacked my home.”

Silence.

“You aimed at my son’s room.”

Victor’s face remained hidden beneath distortion.

Zee’s voice dropped.

“I am going to kill you.”

Victor believed him.

Completely.

That was when he made the final test.

And hated himself for needing it.

⸻

Victor raised one hand.

Not toward Zee.

Toward Darkheart.

A Witch sigil formed high above the trees.

Far away on the horizon—

scarlet-black flame erupted.

An illusion.

A resonance.

Perfectly shaped to mimic the royal defenses breaking.

Then came another signal.

A distorted magical pulse patterned after the nursery wards.

Impact.

Collapse.

Distress.

Zee’s head snapped toward Darkheart.

Victor watched him.

Here.

Enemy almost caught.

One step from the answer.

Revenge within reach.

Home apparently burning.

Zayrlen possibly inside.

Zee did not hesitate.

Not even one heartbeat.

He abandoned Victor.

Phoenix wings exploded open.

Scarlet-black fire ripped through the forest.

Zee launched toward Darkheart.

Gone.

Victor stood alone.

The test ended.

Complete.

⸻

He chose the boy.

Victor closed his eyes.

Every time.

There was no excuse left.

No prophecy.

No impossible biology.

No fear of what the child might become.

Zee had just stood close enough to finally seize the person who attacked his home—

and left him alive the instant his son appeared threatened.

Victor remembered his own newborn.

Zee.

Small.

Impossible.

Terrifying.

Victor had chosen himself.

His crown.

His fear.

His control.

Zee chose the child.

Victor’s jaw tightened.

The shame did not redeem him.

It only removed one more lie.

He was still proud.

Still angry.

Still afraid of what would happen when Zee finally learned who he was.

Because Victor had crossed Zee’s law now.

He had attacked Darkheart.

Worse—

he had made Zee believe Zayrlen was in danger.

Victor understood exactly what that meant.

If he revealed himself tonight, Zee would probably attack before listening.

And Victor could not honestly call that unreasonable.

Not anymore.

⸻

Zee hit Darkheart like falling scarlet fire.

He landed in the royal courtyard.

Stone cracked.

Black-crimson feathers flared.

Scarlet blood-flame poured from his wings.

“NER.”

Ner appeared at the upper passage with Zayrlen in his arms.

Alive.

Safe.

Completely unharmed.

Zee stopped.

The illusion broke inside his mind.

False.

Manipulated.

His face became terrifying.

Ner saw it.

“What happened?”

Zee crossed the courtyard.

“The attacker lied.”

Ner immediately understood.

“About Zayrlen.”

“Yes.”

Pure Gold Mana flared.

Not at Zee.

At the idea.

“I hate this person.”

“Yes.”

Good.

⸻

Zee reached for Zayrlen.

Ner handed him over.

The baby recognized him instantly.

Heartbeat changed.

Relaxed.

Tiny hand caught against one black-crimson feather now lying over Zee’s shoulder.

The scarlet fire moved around Zayrlen’s fingers.

Did not burn.

Zee froze.

Everyone nearby noticed.

Ner stared.

Zayrlen pulled the feather closer.

Then made a tiny happy sound.

Zee looked down at him.

“You are supposed to be impressed.”

Zayrlen chewed on his own fist.

Ner laughed.

“He is two months old.”

“Still rude.”

Zayrlen grabbed the feather again.

Scarlet fire curled around his hand like silk.

No Phoenix inheritance in the child.

No awakening.

No transfer.

Only Zee’s power recognizing family and refusing harm.

Zee stared.

Then his entire expression softened.

Daddy Zee won.

Completely.

Ner smiled.

“There he is.”

“Do not.”

“You melted.”

“No.”

“You absolutely did.”

Zayrlen made the happy sound again.

Zee looked down.

“Traitor.”

⸻

Rez returned from the false eastern search shortly afterward.

One look at Zee’s transformed wings stopped him.

Black-crimson feathers.

Scarlet blood-fire moving persistently between them.

No longer a flicker.

No longer one strange feather.

A permanent change during active power.

Elias arrived and stared.

Zareth landed behind him.

The Dragon Soul Link slammed into Zee’s newest sensations.

His face changed.

“You nearly transformed.”

“Yes.”

“Into what?”

“I do not know.”

Elias stepped closer.

“The inherited signature?”

“Wide awake.”

“Mastered?”

Zee laughed once.

“No.”

Good.

That answer mattered.

Tessa arrived.

Looked at Zee.

Then at the completely healed remnants of his Chapter One Hundred Twenty-Eight injuries.

Her eyes narrowed.

“You are healed.”

“Yes.”

“I dislike that.”

“Why?”

“Because I did not do it.”

Fair.

She examined the wing.

No damage.

Horn fully restored.

Scales complete.

Eye clear.

Regeneration had changed dramatically.

Not ordinary Crimson Phoenix anymore.

Something deeper had taken over repair.

Elias looked toward the scarlet fire.

“It is fusing.”

Zee’s expression hardened.

“With what?”

“We still do not know.”

Zareth looked at the wings.

“But it is yours now.”

Zee glanced toward Zayrlen.

The baby still had one fist wrapped around a feather.

“Yes.”

That much was obvious.

⸻

Far outside Darkheart, Victor watched from one final ridge.

Too far for Zee to detect.

Darkheart glowed below.

Victor saw movement.

Small.

The silhouette of Zee holding Zayrlen near the window.

Black-crimson scarlet fire moving around him.

The baby completely unafraid.

Victor’s chest tightened.

He had his answer.

All the way.

That was how far Zee would go.

Not to control the child.

Not to kill what frightened him.

Not to make him smaller.

To protect him.

Everything outward.

All violence pointed away from the boy.

Victor closed his eyes.

“He chose the boy.”

Quiet.

No pride.

Not yet.

Too much guilt for that.

He turned from the ridge.

Not retreat forever.

Delay.

Because now he knew exactly what Zee had become.

And exactly what Zee would do if Victor revealed that the attacker who had threatened Darkheart and made him believe Zayrlen was in danger—

was his dead father.

Victor walked away before sunrise.

Black-crimson fire burned once across his hand.

Scarlet beneath.

The old bloodline.

The original inheritance.

Still his.

But inside Zee—

it was becoming something else.

Something larger.

Something the ancient Abyssal Titan Phoenix Sovereign bloodline had never produced.

⸻

Inside Darkheart, Zee sat beside the nursery window.

Zayrlen slept against him.

Ner nearby.

Scarlet-black Phoenix fire moved quietly through Zee’s feathers.

Persistent now.

Controlled enough not to spread.

Not mastered enough to disappear when he wanted.

His body felt different.

Hotter.

Deeper.

Like something colossal had folded itself beneath his skin and was waiting for space.

He still did not know its name.

Did not know its source.

Did not know his father lived.

Did not know Victor had been watching.

He knew only three things.

The power was inherited.

It was fusing with his Crimson Phoenix blood.

And something inside him had almost become a Phoenix instead of merely carrying one.

Zayrlen shifted.

Tiny fingers caught another feather.

Zee looked down.

The fire moved around the baby’s hand.

Gentle.

Ner smiled.

Zee stared at his son.

Monster to the world.

Father here.

Outside, one enemy had already taught Zee he could lose.

Somewhere else, Merek Voss remained alive and moving through Ravelin Cross.

And somewhere beyond Zee’s knowledge, a father who had once chosen fear was walking away from the son who had just proven he would choose his own child every time.

The Crimson Abyssal Phoenix had not awakened completely.

But now—

it was no longer sleeping.
