CRIMSON DYNASTY — LOCKED CANON SUMMARY

1. Setting

* Crimson Dynasty takes place thousands of years before Crimson Legacy during the Age of Kingdoms.
* The world is ruled by ancient kingdoms and royal Houses.
* This era is darker, older, and far more dangerous than Crimson Legacy.

⸻

2. Zee Darkheart

* Zee Darkheart is the First Natural Tribrid.
* He is born as a Witch, Vampire, and Wolf simultaneously.
* He does not awaken the three races separately.
* He cannot permanently die.
* Every attempt to kill him only causes him to adapt, evolve, and become stronger.
* Zee is the creator of the original hybrid races:
    * Witch–Vampire
    * Vampire–Wolf
    * Wolf–Witch
* This is completely separate from Aidan’s later creation of the Crimson Bloodline and Crimson races.

⸻

3. The Birth of the First Tribrid

Upon Zee’s birth:

* Blood rains from the sky.
* Witches lose their powers.
* Wolves descend into uncontrollable madness.
* Vampires sense an ancient supernatural awakening.
* Zee’s magic leaks from his body.
* His eyes glow golden-crimson.
* His teeth sharpen into fangs.
* The world names him:
    * The First Tribrid
    * The Monster
    * The Abomination
    * The Curse

⸻

4. House Darkheart

* House Darkheart is an ancient Witch Kingdom.
* House Darkheart does not possess crimson magic.
* Crimson magic belongs exclusively to Zee.
* Zee’s crimson mana is completely unknown to the world.

⸻

5. House Vesper

Official title:

The Kingdom of the Azure Flame

* House Vesper is an ancient Witch Kingdom.
* Their signature magic is Blue-White Mana.
* Blue-White Mana represents discipline, elegance, precision, mastery, and balance.

⸻

6. Royal Family of House Darkheart

King Victor Darkheart

* Zee’s father.
* Terrified of the prophecy.
* Calls Zee:
    * Monster
    * Abomination
    * Curse
* Zee’s first and greatest childhood antagonist.

Queen Aurelia Darkheart

* Zee’s mother.
* Zee’s only true safe place.
* Loves him unconditionally.
* Her eventual suffering becomes one of the major turning points in Zee’s life.

⸻

7. Royal Family of House Vesper

King Lucien Vesper

* King of the Kingdom of the Azure Flame.
* Treats Zee as one of his own sons.
* Represents wisdom, honor, and chosen family.

Prince Rez Vesper

* Zee’s best friend.
* Brother by choice.
* Uses Blue-White Mana.

⸻

8. Nereus Azrion

Full Name:

Nereus Azrion

* Son of Zee’s personal maid.
* Zee’s best friend.
* Zee’s secret first love.
* Uses Golden Mana.

Naming Rule:

* Zee calls him “Ner.”
* Friends generally call him “Nereus.”
* Formal settings use “Azrion” or appropriate formal titles.

⸻

9. Mana Colors

Zee

* Crimson Mana

Rez

* Blue-White Mana

Nereus

* Golden Mana

Mana colors are unique magical identities and do not determine morality.

10. Dragon Kingdom

* Dragons are an ancient civilization with their own kingdom.
* Dragons are capable of shifting between:
    * Dragon Form
    * Human Form
* Dragons possess their own form of Dragon Magic.
* Dragons are classified by:
    * Age
    * Color

⸻

11. Dragon Classification

By Age

* Hatchling
* Young
* Adult
* Elder
* Ancient
* Primordial

By Color

Different dragon colors represent different lineages and magical affinities.

⸻

12. Prince Zareth Drakonis

Full Name:

Prince Zareth Drakonis

* Crown Prince of the Dragon Kingdom.
* Born on the exact same day as Zee.
* Eventually becomes known simply as:

Zee’s Dragon

* He and Zee become genuine friends over time.
* He is not a mount.
* Nobody rides dragons.

⸻

13. Core Theme of Book One

Book One has no single primary villain.

Instead:

Zee is born into the world, and from that day forward everything tries to kill him.

This includes:

* His father
* Kingdoms
* Witches
* Monsters
* Demons
* Dragons with differing views
* The dangers of the ancient world

Every day is another fight.

Every fight changes Zee.

Every attempt to destroy him only makes him evolve.

⸻

14. Zee’s Legend

The world remembers Zee as:

* The Monster
* The Abomination
* The First Tribrid

His people remember him as:

* Their True King
* Their Hero
* Their Protector

The same man becomes two different legends depending on who tells the story.
