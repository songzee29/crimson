Chapter One Hundred Sixteen

The Birth of the Crimson-Gold Prince

Zayrlen chose the middle of the night.

Of course he did.

Ner woke Zee by grabbing one of his horns.

Hard.

Zee’s eyes opened instantly.

His hand closed around Ner’s wrist before instinct recognized who touched him.

Then he heard it.

Ner’s heartbeat.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat.

And a third rhythm between them.

Not another heart.

Magic.

Crimson and Gold pulsed through the room.

Zee sat upright.

Ner released his horn.

“Something is happening.”

Zee listened harder.

Zayrlen’s heartbeat accelerated.

Ner inhaled sharply.

Then bent forward.

Pain.

Zee was out of the bed immediately.

“Ner.”

“I know my own name.”

Another contraction hit.

Pure Gold Mana exploded across the walls.

The windows shook.

Zee’s Phoenix wings opened reflexively.

Ner glared through the pain.

“Do not start.”

“You are in labor.”

“I noticed.”

“I am getting Tessa.”

“Good.”

Zee disappeared.

Then returned less than two seconds later carrying Tessa.

She looked furious.

“I have legs.”

“No time.”

“You could have warned me.”

“Ner is in labor.”

Tessa’s irritation vanished.

She turned.

Ner was already standing.

Barely.

Gold light moved beneath his skin.

A deep Crimson pulse followed.

Then heat rolled through the chamber.

Tessa stared.

“That is new.”

Zee heard Zayrlen’s heartbeat strike again.

Strong.

Fast.

Wild.

“No.”

Tessa looked at him.

“What?”

“He has done that before.”

The heartbeat changed again.

Wolf.

Zee knew the rhythm now.

Hunt.

Moon.

Instinct.

But underneath it—

Dragon heat.

And surrounding both—

Witch structure.

Three.

Together.

Their son was ready.

⸻

Darkheart woke before anyone rang a bell.

The Witches felt it first.

Every ward surrounding the inner castle turned Gold.

Then Crimson.

Then both.

Not broken.

Answering.

Wolves throughout the capital lifted their heads.

No frenzy.

No fear.

Recognition.

Something new had entered the edge of their senses.

Dragons were farther away.

Except one.

Zareth woke in Drakonis with Zee’s name already in his mind.

The Dragon Link opened.

He is coming.

Zee did not need to explain who.

Zareth was out of bed instantly.

Rez received a messenger five minutes later.

Kael woke when every Vampire in the eastern barracks complained that the castle suddenly smelled like lightning.

Elias woke because his Witchcraft started flickering without permission.

Amara woke because every Wolf near the keep began pacing toward the same building.

Darkheart understood.

The Prince was coming.

⸻

Ner refused to lie down.

Tessa argued for twelve minutes.

Then stopped wasting time.

“Fine. Stand.”

“Thank you.”

“You are impossible.”

“I live with Zee.”

Tessa looked toward him.

“That does explain environmental damage.”

Zee ignored them.

He had one hand against Ner’s back.

The other rested over their son.

Every contraction changed Zayrlen’s supernatural rhythm.

Witch.

Dragon.

Wolf.

Not taking turns.

Responding differently to the same life.

The Witch nature reacted to Ner.

Pure Gold Mana surrounded the developing magical structure like a living cradle.

The Dragon blood reacted to Zee.

Heat rolled between father and son.

The Wolf—

the Wolf reacted to both.

Pack.

Family.

Home.

Zee’s own Wolf instinct became almost unbearably protective.

His claws extended once.

Ner noticed.

“Put those away.”

Zee looked down.

Then retracted them.

“I did not do that intentionally.”

“I know.”

Another contraction.

Ner’s hand crushed the wooden frame beside him.

The entire side snapped.

Tessa looked at it.

Then at Ner.

“Still not fragile.”

Ner breathed hard.

“I have been saying that for months.”

⸻

Hours passed.

Not peacefully.

Zayrlen’s Dragon blood complicated everything.

The child’s body temperature climbed.

Then Ner’s climbed with it.

Tessa used cooling magic.

It evaporated.

Zee used controlled Witchcraft to draw heat away.

Zayrlen reacted by producing more.

Ner glared at Zee.

“He is fighting you.”

“He is not conscious enough to fight me.”

“He is your son.”

Zee considered.

“Fair.”

Pure Gold Mana suddenly burst from Ner.

A circle appeared beneath his feet.

Not deliberately cast.

Tessa stepped back.

Elias, standing outside the chamber, felt it immediately.

“So that is normal?”

Kael looked at him.

“Nothing in that room has ever been normal.”

Rez had arrived by then.

He stood beside Zareth near the closed doors.

Zareth could feel Zee through the Link.

Not fear for himself.

Something much worse.

Father’s fear.

Rez looked toward him.

“He is terrified.”

Zareth nodded.

“He hides it badly through the Link.”

Rez stared at the door.

“Good.”

Zareth looked at him.

“Good?”

“Maybe fatherhood will finally humble him.”

From inside the room Zee shouted:

“I CAN HEAR YOU.”

Rez smiled.

“No, it won’t.”

⸻

Then everything changed.

Ner screamed.

Not anger.

Pain.

Zee felt Zayrlen’s heartbeat stumble.

Once.

His entire body went cold.

“Tessa.”

“I hear it.”

Another beat.

Wrong.

Another.

Slower.

Zee’s Crimson Sovereign senses opened completely.

Witch.

Vampire.

Wolf.

Dragon.

Phoenix.

Everything.

He looked inside the supernatural structure around his son.

The problem was immediate.

Zayrlen’s three natures were no longer waiting quietly.

Birth itself had triggered them.

Not full awakening.

Not mastery.

Instinctive activation.

Dragon durability wanted the body hardened for transition.

Wolf nature wanted movement.

Witch magic was reacting to Ner’s Mana.

All three were pulling at once.

Too much pressure around a body that had not taken its first breath.

Zee’s expression changed.

“Tessa.”

“I cannot separate them.”

“Do not.”

She looked at him.

“They are fighting.”

“No.”

Zee listened deeper.

Not fighting.

Crowding.

Three natural bloodlines.

All belonging there.

None foreign.

None created.

None needing to be rewritten.

His son did not need transformation.

He needed room.

Zee placed both hands against Ner.

“Zayrlen.”

Ner looked at him.

“He cannot understand you.”

“I know.”

Zee closed his eyes.

His Witch nature reached first.

Not into Zayrlen.

Around him.

Space.

Structure.

“Open.”

Magic obeyed.

Not Ner.

Not the child.

The pressure around them.

The Gold circle beneath Ner widened.

Crimson lines joined it.

Zayrlen’s Witch nature immediately stabilized.

One.

Then Zee let his Dragon blood rise.

Not Phoenix.

Not Demon.

Only Dragon.

Veyrath.

Inheritance.

Father to son.

Heat answered heat.

The Dragon pressure eased.

Two.

Then Wolf.

Zee did not cast.

Did not command.

He growled.

Low.

Deep.

Not Human.

Not threat.

Pack call.

Ner’s Bloodsong answered instinctively.

Pure Gold resonance moved through the chamber.

Two fathers.

One child.

Zayrlen’s Wolf rhythm slowed.

Three.

The heartbeat corrected.

Tessa exhaled.

Ner almost collapsed.

Zee caught him.

Ner grabbed Zee’s neck.

“If you ever tell anyone I almost fell—”

“You broke furniture.”

“I will kill you.”

“Noted.”

Tessa stepped closer.

“Save the threats.”

Another contraction struck.

This one different.

Tessa’s eyes widened.

“Now.”

⸻

Outside the room, every Wolf in Darkheart went silent.

Zareth stood.

Rez stopped pacing.

Elias’s deep-blue Mana flickered.

Kael looked toward the door.

Amara’s eyes changed.

Something moved through the castle.

Not pressure like Aidan’s birth.

Not a supernatural alarm reaching across the world.

This was closer.

Warmer.

Belonging to Darkheart itself.

The wards turned fully Crimson and Gold.

The stone beneath the keep warmed.

Then came the sound.

A newborn cry.

Everything stopped.

⸻

Inside the room, Zee forgot how to move.

Tessa was speaking.

Ner was breathing.

Magic filled the air.

None of it mattered.

Because there was another heartbeat now.

Not beneath Ner.

Separate.

Small.

Fast.

Alive.

Tessa held their son.

Zayrlen Keal Darkheart.

He was covered in blood.

Angry.

Loud.

Perfect.

Ner stared.

For the first time in hours, he said nothing.

Tessa smiled.

“Father?”

Neither moved.

She looked between them.

“You are both fathers. One of you take him.”

Ner laughed weakly.

Then held out his arms.

“Me.”

Tessa placed Zayrlen against him.

The crying stopped.

Immediately.

Ner froze.

Zayrlen opened his eyes.

Crimson.

Gold.

Mixed completely through both irises.

Zee’s eyes.

But smaller.

New.

His.

Ner’s face broke.

Not dramatically.

Just enough.

“Zee.”

Zee had already seen.

Crimson-gold.

Their son looked at Ner without understanding anything.

Then one tiny hand closed around his finger.

Pure Gold Mana flickered around both.

Ner laughed through tears.

“Hello, Zayrlen.”

Zayrlen made a quiet sound.

Zee stood beside them.

Unable to look away.

Ner looked up.

“You are doing the statue thing.”

Zee did not answer.

“Take your son.”

That moved him.

Zee reached carefully.

More carefully than he had ever touched anything.

Ner placed Zayrlen in his arms.

The child’s heartbeat accelerated.

Zee heard it.

Smelled him.

Blood.

Magic.

Heat.

Wolf.

Dragon.

Witch.

All natural.

All his.

All Ner’s.

Zayrlen stared up at him.

Then grabbed one of Zee’s fingers.

Zee stopped breathing.

Again.

Years disappeared.

Aidan had done the same thing.

That memory came.

Then passed.

This moment belonged to Zayrlen.

Zee lowered his head.

“Hello, son.”

Zayrlen’s tiny hand tightened.

Zee smiled.

Completely.

No King.

No Sovereign.

No Blood Hunter.

No Phoenix monster.

Father.

⸻

Then Zayrlen sneezed.

A spark of Crimson-and-Gold magic burst from his nose.

Zee stared.

Ner laughed so hard he immediately regretted it.

“Ow.”

Tessa looked offended.

“That child has been alive for three minutes.”

Zee looked at the scorched blanket.

“He has control problems.”

Ner stared at him.

“He is three minutes old.”

“Still.”

“Do not start.”

Zayrlen yawned.

Then something shifted beneath his skin.

Tiny patches of scales appeared along one shoulder.

Not permanent.

Crimson edged faintly in Gold.

They faded seconds later.

Zareth felt it through the open Dragon Link with Zee.

His voice entered Zee’s mind immediately.

Dragon.

Zee looked toward the door.

Yes.

Then the Wolf came.

Not a transformation.

An instinctive pressure.

Behind Zee’s arms, for one heartbeat, the shape of a future true Wolf appeared.

Four-legged.

Powerful.

Not Zee’s near-black Wolf.

Crimson fur.

Deep, rich Crimson.

Gold running through the coat like living sunlight.

Crimson-gold eyes.

Then the image disappeared.

Zee went still.

Ner had seen it too.

“Crimson and Gold.”

“Yes.”

“Exactly.”

Zee looked down at their son.

Both fathers.

Visible before Zayrlen could even understand what color was.

Then magic moved again.

This time—

Witch.

A tiny pulse spread from Zayrlen.

Crimson and Gold spiraled together.

Not Ner’s exact Pure Gold.

Not Zee’s Crimson Witchcraft.

His own.

Tessa whispered:

“Three.”

Zee nodded.

Dragon.

Witch.

Wolf.

A naturally born Tribrid.

Not created.

Not altered.

Not transformed.

Born.

Zayrlen Keal Darkheart.

The first naturally born Dragon–Witch–Wolf Tribrid.

But history remained intact.

Zee Darkheart was still the First Natural Tribrid.

The first being ever born with Witch, Vampire, and Wolf fully awake from birth.

Zayrlen did not replace him.

He continued what Zee had begun in an entirely new direction.

Different blood.

Different combination.

Different person.

His son.

⸻

The doors finally opened.

Rez entered first.

Obviously.

Zareth nearly pushed him aside.

Ner immediately pointed.

“Do not crowd me.”

Both stopped.

Elias came behind them.

Kael.

Amara.

Tessa looked toward the growing group.

“Why is everyone here?”

Rez looked offended.

“A Prince was born.”

Ner raised an eyebrow.

“A child was born.”

Rez smiled.

“Both.”

Good answer.

Zareth stared at Zayrlen.

His new Phoenix–Dragon–Witch senses reacted immediately.

But it was the Dragon inside him that came forward most strongly.

Not Drakonis.

He recognized that at once.

Veyrath through Zee.

Aurelia’s blood.

Zareth looked at Zee.

No words.

The Link carried enough.

Aurelia should have seen him.

Yes.

She should have.

But she was not here.

Zee would not turn Zayrlen’s birth into another funeral.

He looked down at his son.

Life.

Not death.

Forward.

⸻

Rez approached.

“Can I hold him?”

Ner looked toward Zee.

Zee looked at Rez.

Rez frowned.

“Why are you both looking at me like that?”

“You spoil children.”

Rez stared.

“I have no idea what you mean.”

Zee did.

He said nothing.

“Fine,” Ner said.

Rez smiled immediately.

He took Zayrlen.

The newborn stared at him.

Rez stared back.

“Hello.”

Zayrlen grabbed his collar.

Rez froze.

Then smiled.

“Oh, you are going to be trouble.”

Zee folded his arms.

“Give him back.”

“I have had him for four seconds.”

“Enough.”

Ner laughed.

Elias looked at Zee.

“You really are going to be insane.”

“I am reasonable.”

Every person in the room disagreed at once.

“No.”

Zayrlen slept through it.

⸻

Darkheart heard before sunrise.

No secrecy.

No concealment.

No erased record.

The bells rang.

Every district.

Every settlement near enough to hear.

A Prince of House Darkheart had been born.

His name was spoken openly.

ZAYRLEN KEAL DARKHEART.

Son of Zee Darkheart.

Son of Ner Azrion.

No shame.

No hidden parentage.

No false story.

No one took his name away.

Witches lit Crimson-and-Gold lanterns.

Wolves howled from the walls.

Vampires raised cups of blood and wine.

Children who had left gifts outside the royal chambers ran through the streets screaming that the baby had finally arrived.

Darkheart celebrated him before he ever saw the kingdom celebrating back.

⸻

Near dawn, everyone finally left.

Even Tessa.

After threatening Zee three separate times.

Ner lay exhausted against the pillows.

Zee sat beside him.

Zayrlen slept between them.

Tiny.

Warm.

One hand closed beneath his chin.

The faintest Crimson-and-Gold magic breathed around him with each heartbeat.

Ner watched Zee listening.

“You can stop checking.”

“No.”

“He is right there.”

“I know.”

“He is breathing.”

“I know.”

“You can see him.”

“Yes.”

Ner smiled.

“Hopeless.”

Zee touched one finger to Zayrlen’s hand.

The baby grabbed it in his sleep.

Ner looked at them.

“He knows you.”

Zee swallowed.

“Yes.”

That was the part that mattered.

Zayrlen would know him.

Not as a stranger.

Not as Uncle Rez’s friend.

Not as a hidden truth waiting beneath erased history.

Father.

Zee Darkheart could carry his son through his own halls.

Teach him.

Argue with him.

Watch him shift for the first time someday.

Watch that Crimson-and-Gold Wolf run across Darkheart land.

See him learn Witchcraft.

See the Dragon blood of Aurelia continue in a child she would never meet.

Zee would not have to love this son from the shadows.

Ner reached across Zayrlen and took Zee’s hand.

“He is here.”

Zee looked at him.

Then down at their son.

“Yes.”

Outside, Darkheart’s bells continued ringing.

Not in warning.

Not for death.

Not because another enemy had reached their walls.

Celebration.

Zayrlen Keal Darkheart slept through all of it.

Dragon.

Witch.

Wolf.

Crimson and Gold.

Born to two fathers who had chosen him before his heartbeat existed.

Born into a kingdom already waiting to know his name.

And for the first time in Zee Darkheart’s life—

one of his sons entered the world without Zee needing to make the world forget him.
