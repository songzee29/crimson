# OPERATION: THE DARKNESS RISES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Zee is becoming darker.

**Only gradually.**

Do NOT turn Zee into a mindless monster.

Do NOT make him lose control.

Do NOT make him evil for the sake of being evil.

His darkness comes from **deliberate choices**.

Every target should reveal another layer of the Zee who came out of Chapter 128.

The deeper Zee gets into the list, the less mercy he has for the people who directly participated in what happened to him.

He is still Zee.

Still a father.

Still a King.

Still capable of love.

Still capable of restraint.

But the people on this list are discovering something new:

**Zee remembers.**

And he is no longer interested in forgiving them.

# OPERATION: THE ABYSSCIN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The remaining targets prepared for the Zee they knew.

They know about:

- Witchcraft
- Vampire abilities
- Wolf abilities
- Dragon abilities
- combinations of those abilities
- Zee's known combat habits
- his known weaknesses
- information gathered during the original operation

They do **NOT** properly understand his Crimson Black Abysscin Phoenix.

This remains one of Zee's biggest advantages.

Zee should continue using the **Crimson Black Abysscin Phoenix** during the lick-back.

Not constantly for spectacle.

Strategically.

The targets should gradually realize:

**They prepared for the wrong Zee.**

Brannic Dor is the immediate next target.

Zee gets his location.

He goes personally.

No army.

No entourage.

No warning.

No permission.

**Zee hunts Brannic.**

He does not understand the Abysscin.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
# BRANNIC'S DEFENSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Brannic should have prepared heavily.

His location should contain things specifically designed around what he knows about Zee:

- scent disruption
- Wolfbane
- false trails
- decoy scents
- anti-Wolf compounds
- traps
- hidden hunters
- sensory interference
- old information gathered from the original operation

Brannic should genuinely be competent.

Do NOT make him stupid just so Zee can win.

He should be dangerous.

He should know what he is doing.

The problem is that he prepared for powers Zee doesn't need to use.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Black-crimson wings.

Scarlet energy burning beneath the darkness.

Abysscin Phoenix pressure.

Controlled Phoenix fire.

**This was never in their original calculations.**

Possible exchange:

Brannic:

# THE FIGHT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Make the fight longer than Selk's.

Brannic should actually test Zee.

He should use the environment.

He should attempt to force Zee into situations designed around the old operation.

Zee dismantles those counters one by one.

But Zee does not simply overpower everything immediately.

Let Brannic have moments where he thinks an old tactic is working.

Then reveal that Zee has another answer.

The fight should show the evolution of Zee's combat thinking.

Each target should feel different.

> Zee arrives → target fights → Zee wins → target dies.

**Selk:** suppression and preparation.

**Brannic:** hunting, Wolf counters, sensory warfare.

**Sera:** intelligence, strategy, psychological warfare, years of observation.

**Jorren:** direct battlefield history and personal involvement.

**Neris:** information and secrets.

**Drovak:** logistics, movement, escape routes, operational infrastructure.

Their differences should make the lick-back progressively more interesting.
