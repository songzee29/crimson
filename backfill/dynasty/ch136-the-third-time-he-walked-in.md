Chapter One Hundred Thirty-Six

The Third Time He Walked In

Victor returned through the front gate.

That was somehow worse.

No concealment.

No hidden fire.

No distant Witch sigils.

No attack.

He simply walked toward Darkheart like a man arriving somewhere he had already been invited.

The guards disagreed.

Spears lowered immediately.

Wards activated.

Wolf sentries growled.

Witch circles opened across the stone.

Victor stopped before the outer arch and looked around at the weapons pointed toward him.

Black-crimson fire moved once across his fingers.

Scarlet beneath.

Every nearby ward screamed.

One guard shouted:

“Identify yourself.”

Victor looked toward the keep.

“Victor Darkheart.”

Silence.

Then everything became more dangerous.

⸻

Zareth felt him first.

Teal-and-Gold Mana erupted around him before anyone finished delivering the message.

Victor.

Alive.

Again.

The Dragon Soul Link snapped toward Zee instantly.

Not panic.

Warning.

Rage.

Rez heard the name and stopped halfway through a conversation.

Ner looked up from Zayrlen.

Pure Gold Mana filled the nursery.

Elias cursed.

Kael reached for a blade.

Amara’s Wolf came forward in her eyes.

Rhyven’s Phoenix-Wolf fire rose around his claws.

Tessa said:

“Absolutely not.”

Then Zee walked into the courtyard.

Victor was waiting beneath the gate.

Alive.

Again.

Zee stopped.

For several seconds he said nothing.

Not shock this time.

No confusion.

He understood exactly what he was looking at.

His father.

The man he had killed.

Twice.

Standing upright for a third appearance like death had become an inconvenience.

Zee stared.

Victor waited.

Zee finally spoke.

“I just killed you.”

“Yes.”

The answer was immediate.

That made it worse.

Zee took another step.

“Completely.”

“Yes.”

“I put my foot through your heart.”

“I remember.”

“I watched you stop breathing.”

“Yes.”

“I checked.”

“I know.”

Zee stared at him.

Then looked toward the sky as though asking the universe for patience it had no intention of giving.

When he looked back down:

“Why are you here?”

Victor’s expression remained composed.

“I came back.”

“That was not the question.”

“I wanted to see you.”

Zee’s face went blank.

Ner arrived behind him.

“That may be the worst possible answer.”

Victor looked toward Ner.

Ner’s expression made the boundary obvious.

Do not.

Victor wisely looked back at Zee.

Zee took one more step.

Then punched him.

⸻

The courtyard exploded into movement.

Victor hit the stone.

Rolled.

Black-crimson Phoenix fire erupted around him.

Zee was already there.

No full Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix transformation.

No need.

Vampire speed.

Dragon force.

Crimson Sovereign.

One fist struck Victor across the mouth.

Victor blocked the second.

Witchcraft cracked between them.

“Hold.”

“Break.”

The commands collided.

Scarlet-black fire surged.

Zee drove a knee into Victor’s ribs.

Victor answered with a Phoenix blast that forced Zee backward.

Zareth moved.

Zee snapped:

“No.”

Zareth stopped.

Barely.

This was theirs.

For now.

Victor rose.

Blood at his mouth.

“You can kill me again.”

Zee advanced.

“I intend to.”

Victor wiped the blood away.

“And then I will wake up again.”

Zee stopped.

Only for a second.

Then hit him again.

Victor slammed into the wall.

“That did not sound convincing.”

Zee followed.

“You have died twice.”

“Yes.”

“By me.”

“Yes.”

“And you keep coming back.”

“Yes.”

Zee grabbed him by the throat.

Victor did not struggle.

Much.

“You are beginning to understand.”

Zee’s grip tightened.

Victor’s eyes darkened.

“If you crush my throat, I will die.”

“Good.”

“And then I will resurrect.”

Zee stared at him.

Victor continued:

“And come back.”

Ner folded his arms.

“This is deeply irritating.”

Rez arrived beside him.

“For once, I agree with Victor on something.”

Zee looked toward Rez.

Rez lifted both hands.

“Only the factual part.”

Zee looked back at Victor.

His fingers remained around his throat.

One twist.

That would do it.

Maybe.

Again.

Then what?

Another corpse.

Another resurrection.

Another return.

Another argument.

Another murder.

Victor’s face was already beginning to look annoyingly patient.

Zee released him so suddenly Victor almost fell.

Everyone noticed.

That mattered.

The first time.

Zee had deliberately chosen not to kill him.

Not mercy.

Not forgiveness.

Efficiency.

Victor rubbed his throat.

“That was almost mature.”

Zee’s eyes sharpened.

“Do not ruin it.”

Fair.

⸻

Zee stepped back.

His voice changed.

King now.

Not son.

“You are alive because killing you has become inconvenient.”

Victor nodded once.

“Understood.”

“No.”

Zee moved closer.

“You do not understand yet.”

Scarlet-black fire rose along Zee’s wings.

Not transformation.

Warning.

“You do not approach Zayrlen.”

Victor’s face changed slightly.

Zee caught it.

“Without permission from me or Ner.”

Ner spoke immediately:

“Preferably never.”

Victor glanced toward him.

Ner did not blink.

Zee continued.

“You do not test me.”

Victor nodded.

“You do not test Darkheart.”

Another nod.

“You do not test my family.”

“Yes.”

“You do not create false attacks.”

“Yes.”

“You do not use my son in any plan, lesson, demonstration, question, experiment, threat, or fucking metaphor.”

That one landed.

Victor’s expression hardened with something close to shame.

“Yes.”

Zee stepped closer until only inches separated them.

“If you threaten my people again, I will kill you even if I have to do it every week for the next century.”

Victor looked at him.

“I believe you.”

“Good.”

Silence.

Then Victor said:

“I did not come to threaten anyone.”

Zee’s face remained cold.

“Then do not.”

⸻

A guard near the gate looked between them.

“Majesty?”

Zee turned.

The poor man immediately regretted speaking.

“What?”

“Is he…”

The guard looked at Victor.

Then back at Zee.

“Allowed inside?”

Zee stared at Victor.

Victor waited.

Ner waited.

Rez looked suspiciously entertained.

Zareth was not entertained at all.

Zee exhaled through his nose.

“Yes.”

Victor started forward.

Zee pointed at him.

“Do not make me regret that before dinner.”

Victor stopped.

Then continued more slowly.

Darkheart watched its former King walk into the territory of the son he had once tried to erase.

Alive.

Again.

And Zee watched him like reality itself had personally decided to become annoying.

Victor crossed the gate.

No crown.

No authority.

No welcome.

Just survival.

That was enough to make the truth unavoidable.

Victor was back.

⸻
