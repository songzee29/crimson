Chapter One Hundred Fourteen

The Friend They Took

The eastern alarm was not an invasion.

That made it worse.

Zee reached the outer wall first.

Crimson Phoenix wings spread behind him.

Ner arrived seconds later with Pure Gold Mana around one hand and seven months of pregnancy doing absolutely nothing to improve his willingness to follow instructions.

Kael came from the southern barracks.

Amara from the training fields.

Rhyven reached the wall in a blur of Wolf speed and lingering Crimson heat.

Then Elias Marr walked out of the medical wing.

Zee turned immediately.

“You should be resting.”

Elias looked at him.

“You have been saying that to me since we were children.”

There it was.

Not prisoner.

Not stranger.

Elias.

Family.

Long before the Hollow Crown had carved Demon marrow into him, Elias Marr had been sitting at Darkheart tables.

Long before Zee became King, Elias had been one of the Witches who slipped Ner stolen kitchen wine during royal dinners.

He had trained beside Kael.

Lost money to Rez.

Argued theology with Zareth until the Dragon threatened to eat his books.

Aurelia had known him by name.

He had attended her funeral.

He had stood beside Zee when Victor’s throne finally emptied.

And six weeks before the Glass Mountains—

Elias had disappeared while escorting three Darkheart families through the northern roads.

They had found the families.

Alive.

They had not found Elias.

Until the Hollow Crown.

Whatever the Order had done to him had changed his scent, his blood, his Mana, and almost everything supernatural Zee would normally have recognized at a distance.

But Elias Marr had never been new to Darkheart.

They had simply gotten him back in pieces.

Zee looked at the fading black veins beneath Elias’s throat.

“You almost died three days ago.”

“Four.”

“That does not improve it.”

“No.”

Elias stepped beside him.

“But if the alarm is about me, I would prefer to hear why.”

Zee’s eyes narrowed.

“Why would it be about you?”

A guard approached.

Then handed him a blackened piece of bone.

It was still warm.

Elias saw it.

His expression changed.

“I know that mark.”

⸻

The bone had been driven into Darkheart soil like a message post.

Three symbols were carved across it.

None were Witch.

Rhyven smelled it and immediately backed away.

“Demon.”

Elias nodded.

“Old Demon.”

Zee looked toward him.

“How old?”

“Older than the kind the Hollow Crown was using in their cages.”

Ner touched the edge with Gold.

The bone reacted.

Black flame snapped toward his hand.

Zee caught Ner’s wrist and pulled him back.

Ner stared.

“I was fine.”

“Yes.”

“You do understand I can still use magic.”

“Yes.”

“You are doing the thing.”

“What thing?”

“Pregnancy insanity.”

Zee looked at the bone.

“That was hostile.”

“It was a spark.”

“It touched you.”

“It nearly touched me.”

“Exactly.”

Elias looked between them.

“I missed this.”

Ner turned.

“You were imprisoned for six weeks.”

“Yes.”

“And this is what you missed?”

“No. I also missed food.”

Fair.

Zee looked at the symbols.

“Translate.”

Elias’s humor disappeared.

He crouched.

Deep-blue Witch Mana gathered around his fingertips.

Black demonic sigils appeared inside it.

The symbols reacted.

His new nature recognized them.

Not because the Demon source owned him.

Because it was part of him now.

Elias read slowly.

“Return stolen marrow.”

Zee went still.

Elias continued.

“Return the vessel carrying it.”

Silence.

Rhyven looked toward him.

“You.”

“Yes.”

The final line blackened beneath Elias’s fingers.

He read it.

“Or the settlement burns until nothing bearing Darkheart blood remains.”

Zee smiled.

No humor.

“That was stupid.”

⸻

The marrow inside Elias had not come from a random Demon corpse.

The Hollow Crown had stolen it from something powerful enough that its bloodline could still feel the loss.

The source did not care that Elias had been tortured.

It did not care that the Order was dead.

It cared that part of its blood now existed beyond its ownership.

Elias stood beside Zee in the council chamber while maps were unrolled.

“You are not giving me back.”

Zee looked at him.

“That was never an option.”

“I know.”

“Then why say it?”

“Because you looked like you were planning to kill an entire region.”

“Only if necessary.”

Elias stared.

Ner sat nearby with both feet on another chair.

“He means that.”

“I know.”

Zee looked at Elias.

“How much do you remember about the marrow?”

“Pain.”

“Useful.”

“Very.”

Elias ignored him.

“The Order called it Sovereign Demon marrow.”

Zee’s expression sharpened.

“Not related to my Sovereign state.”

“No.”

“Good.”

“Different use of the word.”

Elias pointed toward the mountains east of Darkheart.

“They said the source came from a Demon ruler beneath the Iron Chasm.”

Amara frowned.

“A kingdom?”

Elias shook his head.

“A dominion.”

Zareth entered through the balcony because doors remained beneath him in spirit even when he was Human.

Teal-and-Gold Mana flickered around his hands.

Phoenix vitality had already erased the last visible Dragonbane damage.

“What did I miss?”

Rez entered from the opposite door.

“Apparently Elias is being repossessed.”

Elias looked at him.

Rez stopped.

For the first time since Elias returned, neither man joked.

Rez crossed the room.

Then punched him in the chest.

Not hard.

Elias winced anyway.

“What was that for?”

“You vanished.”

“I was captured.”

“You could have avoided that.”

“Excellent advice.”

Rez grabbed him.

The hug lasted only a few seconds.

Long enough.

When he released him, Rez looked at the black ring around Elias’s eyes.

“You look terrible.”

“You became Vampire while I was gone.”

“Yes.”

“You look worse.”

“Good. You are definitely still Elias.”

Zee watched them.

This was why the Hollow Crown had died.

Not because it experimented on some stranger.

Because it had taken someone who belonged at this table.

Someone who had belonged there for years.

The Demon–Witch first had not been given to a random man the world would forget.

It had happened to Elias Marr.

Chosen family.

And whoever wanted him now had made the mistake of assuming Zee would negotiate over one of his own.

⸻

The attack began at dusk.

No army appeared.

The shadows changed.

Darkheart’s eastern forest turned black beneath a sky that was still red with sunset.

Every animal fled west.

Wolves on the wall growled.

Vampires smelled sulfur.

Witches felt something push against the lower foundations of their magic.

Then the trees opened.

Demons walked through.

Not mutated Humans.

Not experimental things.

True Demons.

Tall.

Armored.

Some with horns.

Some with bone plates.

Some almost Human until they smiled.

At their center walked a man in black iron.

His horns curved backward.

His eyes were completely red.

He looked directly at Elias.

“There.”

Elias stepped onto the wall.

Zee’s wing blocked half his body automatically.

Elias looked at it.

“Seriously?”

“Yes.”

The Demon ruler ignored the exchange.

“My marrow.”

Elias’s claws emerged.

Black.

His deep-blue Witch Mana circled them.

“My body.”

The Demon smiled.

“No.”

Zee’s pupils narrowed.

That single word almost ended diplomacy before it began.

The Demon looked toward him.

“Zee Darkheart.”

“You know me.”

“Everyone does now.”

His gaze moved to the Crimson Phoenix wings.

“King who drinks races.”

Zee smiled.

“Only rude ones.”

Ner muttered:

“That was almost funny.”

Zee ignored him.

The Demon ruler pointed toward Elias.

“The flesh is irrelevant. The marrow belongs to House Varkesh.”

Elias’s expression hardened.

Zee’s voice went flat.

“He said no.”

“You cannot deny blood its master.”

“Yes, I can.”

“How?”

Zee opened his wings.

“Violently.”

The Demon army moved.

So did Darkheart.

⸻

Elias fought beside Zee.

Not behind him.

The first Demon struck with a bone sword.

Elias caught the wrist.

His Witch Mana formed a deep-blue binding around the arm.

Black demonic sigils grew through the spell.

The binding did not collapse under Demon strength.

It tightened.

Elias looked surprised for half a second.

Then smiled.

“Oh.”

He twisted.

The Demon’s arm broke.

Black claws entered its chest.

Elias froze.

Instinct told him to rip the heart out.

Witch precision told him exactly where.

Both answered together.

He killed the Demon.

Then stared at his hand.

Zee landed beside him.

“Problem?”

“No.”

Elias’s breathing changed.

“I liked that more than I expected.”

Zee looked at the corpse.

“Welcome home.”

No judgment.

Elias had been part of Darkheart long enough to know that answer was genuine.

Another Demon came.

Elias raised one hand.

“Hold.”

Deep-blue Witchcraft erupted.

Black sigils hardened through it like a skeletal frame.

The Demon stopped mid-lunge.

Elias’s eyes widened.

His new biology was finding itself through combat.

Witch structure.

Demon force.

Not alternating.

One race.

Demon–Witch.

He closed his hand.

The spell crushed the attacker’s armor into its ribs.

Elias smiled.

This time he did not apologize for it.

⸻

Rhyven tore through the left flank in true Wolf form.

Crimson fire ran through his claws.

Kael used Witchcraft and Vampire speed to break the center.

Amara’s Wolf patrols hunted anyone trying to reach civilians.

Zareth shifted.

True Dragon form erupted above the eastern wall.

Teal-and-Gold scales.

Original Dragon wings.

Phoenix fire tracing their edges.

Witch sigils spinning between his horns.

His Dragonfire struck the forest beyond the walls.

Not randomly.

Precisely.

A Witch-shaped structure contained the blast and prevented it from spreading toward Darkheart homes.

Zareth felt the control.

Then sent through the Link:

I am becoming annoyingly good at this.

Zee answered:

You are welcome.

I regret surviving.

Zee almost smiled.

Then the Demon ruler hit him.

⸻

The impact threw Zee through the outer watchtower.

Stone collapsed.

His wings folded around his body before the debris landed.

Phoenix feathers took the first impact.

Dragon durability took the second.

Vampire healing repaired what remained.

Wolf instinct had already tracked the Demon ruler before Zee stood.

Witch sight found the magic beneath the black armor.

Everything moved together.

Perfect.

Mastered.

Zee stepped from the ruins.

Crimson fire ran across his wings.

The Demon ruler stared.

“You contain too much.”

Zee tilted his head.

“I keep hearing that.”

The Demon attacked.

Black force split the ground.

Zee flew above it.

One wing struck.

The Demon caught it.

Bad choice.

Crimson Phoenix flame traveled into his gauntlet.

The armor began burning.

He released Zee.

Zee landed.

“Silence.”

The Demon’s next spoken invocation failed.

His eyes widened.

Zee’s Vampire speed crossed the distance.

Wolf instinct read the counterattack.

Dragon strength broke the man’s breastplate.

Witchcraft severed the defensive enchantment beneath it.

Then Zee stopped.

Not because he had to.

Because Elias stood behind the Demon.

The ruler looked toward him.

Elias’s claws were out.

Deep-blue Mana covered in black sigils surrounded both hands.

The ruler laughed.

“You think his stolen marrow makes him my equal?”

Elias looked at Zee.

No words.

Zee understood.

This death belonged to him if he wanted it.

Zee stepped back.

The Demon ruler’s smile vanished.

“You allow the vessel to decide?”

Zee’s expression became cold.

“He was deciding before you arrived.”

Elias attacked.

⸻

The Demon ruler was stronger.

Much stronger.

Elias knew it immediately.

His first strike was blocked.

The second nearly broke his spine.

The third opened his chest.

Blood hit the ground.

Zee did not interfere.

Not yet.

Elias had chosen the fight.

Family did not mean denying him the right to finish it.

The Demon grabbed Elias by the throat.

“You carry a fragment.”

Elias’s hands tightened around the arm.

“No.”

His Witch Mana flared.

“I carry me.”

A deep-blue circle formed beneath both of them.

Black demonic sigils appeared inside the lines.

The ruler looked down.

Too late.

Elias smiled.

“Fall.”

The circle inverted.

Not gravity.

Demonic force shaped through Witch law.

The ruler’s knees hit stone.

Elias tore free.

His claws entered the broken breastplate Zee had already opened.

The Demon ruler caught his arm.

Elias’s bones cracked.

His face tightened.

Then his Demon strength answered.

Not enough to overpower.

Enough to hold.

His Witch side saw the wound.

His Demon side supplied the force.

Together.

Elias drove deeper.

The ruler gasped.

Elias whispered:

“You do not own what survived you.”

Then ripped out the heart.

The Demon ruler fell.

The remaining Demons felt it.

Some stopped.

Some fled.

Some fought harder.

Darkheart killed the ones who continued attacking.

The rest escaped east.

Zee let them.

They had seen enough.

⸻

Elias stood over the body.

Bleeding.

Shaking.

Alive.

Zee approached.

“You good?”

“No.”

“Excellent.”

Elias laughed once.

Then winced.

“You have gotten worse at comfort.”

“I was never good.”

“That is true.”

Ner reached them.

Zee immediately listened.

Two heartbeats.

Ner stared.

“Do not start.”

“I did nothing.”

“You made the face.”

“What face?”

“The heartbeat face.”

Elias looked between them.

“You have a heartbeat face?”

“Yes,” Ner said.

“No,” Zee said.

Zayrlen kicked.

All three felt the small surge of Crimson and Gold magic.

Elias froze.

Then looked at Ner.

“That was him?”

“Yes.”

For the first time since his rescue, the black edge in Elias’s expression softened completely.

“Can I?”

Ner considered.

Then nodded.

Elias placed one hand carefully against Ner.

Zayrlen moved again.

Elias laughed.

Not bitter.

Not exhausted.

The old laugh.

The one Zee remembered from years before the Hollow Crown.

“There you are.”

Ner looked at him.

“You have not met him.”

“I was talking to myself.”

“Concerning.”

“Very.”

Zee watched Elias.

Alive.

Changed.

Family.

Still himself.

The Order had not created Elias Marr.

They had only injured him.

Zee had given his oldest friend enough new blood to survive what they did.

The distinction mattered.

It always would.

⸻
