# **ZEE: THE FIRST TRIBRID KING**  
## **Part II — The War Against Nature**

---

The storm above New Orleans lasted for thirteen days.

No sunlight.  
No stars.  
Only thunder and crimson lightning tearing across the heavens like the sky itself feared what was coming.

Inside the obsidian throne room, Zee sat motionless.

His crown rested crooked against dark curls, one hand gripping the arm of his throne while the other held an old photograph.

A faded picture of him and his mother before everything went wrong.

Before blood.

Before immortality.

Before the world turned him into a legend.

A servant entered carefully, terrified to even breathe too loudly.

> “My king…”

Zee didn’t look up.

> “Speak.”

> “The northern covens have gathered.”

Silence.

> “And?”

The servant swallowed hard.

> “They’ve awakened… *her.*”

For the first time in weeks, Zee’s expression changed.

Not fear.

Something worse.

Recognition.

# **Chapter 9 — The Witch in White**

Deep beneath the forests of Eastern Europe, sealed inside a cathedral older than most civilizations, a woman opened her eyes.

White irises.

Veins glowing gold.

Magic ancient enough to bend reality itself.

Her name was Seraphine.

The first witch ever created.

Nature’s original servant.

And the only being capable of killing Zee.

For centuries she had slept, bound away because her power threatened the balance of the world itself.

But now nature had become desperate.

Because Zee was growing stronger.

Too strong.

Every vampire he defeated added to his influence.
Every war increased his legend.
Every act of survival made him harder to destroy.

Nature realized something terrifying:

Zee was no longer surviving against fate.

He was overpowering it.

So nature created its answer.

---

# **Chapter 10 — The Crimson Kingdom**

New Orleans belonged to Zee now.

Not publicly.

Humans still lived their ordinary lives above ground.

But underneath the city…

Another kingdom existed.

A supernatural empire carved into ancient tunnels and forgotten catacombs.

Vampires guarded the entrances.
Werewolves patrolled the outer territory.
Witches bound to Zee maintained magical barriers around the kingdom.

Candles lit endless halls of black stone.

Every wall carried murals depicting the rise of the Tribrid King:
- the boy in darkness
- the wolf beneath the moon
- the vampire crowned in blood

But despite the loyalty surrounding him…

Zee trusted almost nobody.

Power had taught him one lesson repeatedly:

Love was temporary.

Fear lasted forever.

---

# **Chapter 11 — Ghosts Don’t Die**

Late one night, Zee wandered alone through the royal halls.

He could hear thousands of heartbeats above him in the city.

But another sound stopped him cold.

A laugh.

Soft.

Familiar.

Selene.

His body froze.

Slowly, he turned toward the empty corridor.

And there she stood.

Not alive.

Not dead.

A spirit.

Her silver dress moved like smoke as tears filled her eyes.

> “You finally built your kingdom.”

Zee stared silently.

Even after centuries, seeing her still hurt.

> “You’re not real.”

> “Maybe not.”

She stepped closer.

> “But your pain is.”

His jaw tightened.

> “Why are you here?”

Selene looked at the throne room behind him.

> “Because darkness is swallowing you alive.”

For the first time in years, anger cracked through Zee’s calm mask.

> “Darkness?” he snapped.
> “The world made me this way.”

The walls trembled.

Candles exploded.

Thunder roared above the city.

But Selene didn’t flinch.

> “No,” she whispered.
> “The world hurt you.”
>
> “You chose what came after.”

Those words cut deeper than any weapon ever had.

And before Zee could answer…

She vanished.

Leaving him alone with his rage.

# **Chapter 12 — The Hunt Begins**

Seraphine arrived in New Orleans at sunrise.

And immediately, supernatural creatures started dying.

Entire vampire nests burned to ash.

Wolves collapsed from magical curses.

Witches loyal to Zee lost access to their powers entirely.

Nature itself obeyed her.

Flowers bloomed where she walked.
Storms calmed above her.
The earth moved for her footsteps.

She wasn’t merely powerful.

She was balance incarnate.

And she came with a message:

> “Bring me the Tribrid King…
>
> or this city falls.”

Fear spread through Zee’s kingdom for the first time in centuries.

Because unlike ordinary enemies…

Seraphine truly might win.

# **Chapter 13 — The Boy Beneath the Monster**

That night, Zee stood on a balcony overlooking New Orleans.

Rain poured against his black coat as red lightning flashed behind him.

His most loyal general, Marceline — a vampire warrior he had saved centuries ago — approached carefully.

> “The kingdom’s preparing for war.”

Zee nodded silently.

Then asked something unexpected.

> “Do you think I’m evil?”

Marceline blinked.

> “My king—”

> “Answer me.”

She hesitated.

Because nobody had ever dared speak honestly to him.

But she finally whispered:

> “I think… you became what you needed to survive.”

Zee looked toward the city lights below.

> “That’s not an answer.”

Silence stretched between them.

Then she quietly said:

> “No.”
>
> “I think you’re a wounded boy pretending to be an unkillable god.”

For once…

Zee had no response.

---

# **Chapter 14 — War of the Immortals**

The battle began at midnight.

The sky cracked open with crimson storms as Seraphine’s forces attacked the underground kingdom.

Magic collided with vampire speed.
Wolves tore through enchanted soldiers.
The streets above descended into chaos.

And in the center of it all stood Zee.

Black crown.
Glowing crimson eyes.
Blood dripping from silver claws.

A king born from pain.

Seraphine faced him across the battlefield dressed entirely in white.

Light against darkness.

Balance against evolution.

She raised her hand.

Every supernatural creature nearby collapsed instantly.

Except Zee.

He smiled faintly.

> “You’ll need more than nature to stop me.”

Then he attacked.

The impact shattered the street beneath them.

Buildings trembled.
Windows exploded for blocks.
The Mississippi River itself surged violently against the shore.

For the first time in existence…

Nature fought something it could not control.

And deep inside the chaos…

Zee felt something terrifying.

Not rage.

Not hunger.

Freedom.
