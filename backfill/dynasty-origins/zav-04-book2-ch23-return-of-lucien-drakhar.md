## Chapter 23 — The Return of Lucien Drakhar

The moment Lucien Drakhar opened his eyes...

The world remembered fear.

Not ordinary fear.

Ancient fear.

The kind buried in blood.

The kind passed down through generations.

The kind kingdoms write myths about because the truth is too terrifying.

Across the northern seas, storms erupted without warning.

Entire mountain ranges shook.

Creatures hidden beneath the earth fled their nests.

Ancient monsters that hadn't moved in centuries suddenly awakened and began running.

Running.

Not hunting.

Not fighting.

Running.

As if something worse had returned.

Deep within the Hollow Throne, Aiden nearly fell to one knee.

The Codex exploded with warnings.

### WORLD EVENT DETECTED

### THE CRIMSON PRINCE HAS AWAKENED

### WARNING

### WARNING

### WARNING

Aiden grabbed his head.

Every page inside the Codex began turning on its own.

Faster.

Faster.

Faster.

The system was panicking.

Actually panicking.

Rez stared in disbelief.

"I've never seen it do that."

Seraphina's expression darkened.

She already knew what this meant.

The impossible had happened.

Lucien was alive.

And worse...

He wasn't fully awake.

---

Far to the north, Lucien stepped from his frozen prison.

Thousands of years.

Thousands of years trapped in silence.

Thousands of years dreaming.

Remembering.

Reliving wars.

Losses.

Betrayals.

The death of friends.

The disappearance of family.

The destruction of everything he loved.

His silver eyes glowed brighter.

Then crimson.

Then silver again.

His power surged uncontrollably.

The glacier around him exploded.

Entire mountains vanished beneath waves of crimson energy.

A forest fifty miles away turned to ash.

The sky cracked with red lightning.

Lucien staggered.

Not from weakness.

From confusion.

From disorientation.

His body remembered war.

His mind remembered grief.

And for a brief moment...

He forgot who he was.

## Chapter 24 — The Quest to Survive

The Hollow Throne shook violently.

Cracks spread across the palace walls.

Outside, the sky darkened.

A crimson storm formed on the horizon.

Seraphina immediately stood.

"No."

Something Aiden never thought possible.

"No no no..."

Aiden looked toward her.

"What?"

"He's losing control."

Silence.

Rez immediately understood.

His face lost all color.

"Oh that's bad."

0%

Aiden's heart skipped.

Evolution.

A real evolution.

Not a skill.

An evolution.

The same kind that transformed Lucien.

And somehow...

The system had tied it to surviving him.

## Chapter 25 — The Monster They Called A Hero

Three days later...

Lucien arrived.

Not at the Hollow Throne.

Not yet.

But close enough.

Every step he took changed the landscape.

Rivers boiled.

Storms formed.

Mountains split apart.

Entire armies sent to investigate disappeared.

No survivors.

No witnesses.

Only destruction.

The stories called him a hero.

The stories called him a prince.

The stories called him a legend.

They forgot something important.

Lucien Drakhar was still Zee's son.

And Zee had never been safe.

---

That night Aiden stood atop the ruined palace walls.

Watching the northern horizon.

The crimson storm had grown larger.

Closer.

Angrier.

Every flash of lightning illuminated the silhouette of a man walking.

Just walking.

Yet the world broke beneath him.

Aiden swallowed.

For the first time since opening the Codex...

He wasn't excited.

He wasn't curious.

He was scared.

Actually scared.

Because deep down he understood something.

If Lucien wasn't stopped...

Nobody would survive long enough for Valthoros to matter.

## Chapter 26 — Father and Son

As the storm approached, Seraphina finally told them the truth.

The truth history never recorded.

The truth nobody wanted remembered.

Lucien had inherited something from Zee.

Something even Zee struggled with.

The Crimson Rage.

An evolution hidden deep within House Drakhar.

A state where power multiplied endlessly.

Strength.

Speed.

Magic.

Everything.

But at a cost.

Control.

When Zee was young, he nearly destroyed entire kingdoms before learning to master it.

Lucien inherited an even stronger version.

And unlike Zee...

Lucien had spent thousands of years asleep.

Thousands of years alone.

Thousands of years trapped inside his own mind.

All that grief.

All that loneliness.

All that rage.

Had nowhere to go.

Until now.

Aiden looked toward the storm.

Toward the approaching prince.

Toward the man he hoped would become family.

Then the Codex appeared one final time.

### FINAL WARNING

### LUCIEN DRAKHAR DETECTED

Distance:
17 Miles

16 Miles

15 Miles

The number kept dropping.

Every second.

Every heartbeat.

Closer.

Closer.

Closer.

Then—

The storm suddenly stopped.

The wind died.

The world became silent.

And a new voice echoed across the night.

A calm voice.

A dangerous voice.

A broken voice.

A voice carrying thousands of years of pain.

> "Father..."

Silence.

> "Where are you?"

Aiden felt chills crawl down his spine.

Because this wasn't a monster.

This wasn't a villain.

This was a son.

A son who had lost everything.

A son searching for someone who could never answer.

And that somehow made him far more terrifying.

Black armor.

Silver eyes.

Long crimson hair moving in the wind.

Lucien Drakhar.

The Prince of Crimson.

The Son of Zee.

The Strongest Warrior in Drakhar history.

His eyes slowly lifted toward the Hollow Throne.

Toward Seraphina.

Toward Aiden.

Toward the last survivors of his bloodline.

For one brief moment...

His eyes became clear.

Recognition flashed across his face.

Hope.

Relief.

Family.

Then—

The Crimson Rage surged again.

The ground exploded.

The palace cracked.

The sky turned red.

And Lucien screamed.

A scream filled with enough power to shake continents.

The Codex immediately activated.

### SURVIVAL QUEST STARTED

### NEW EVOLUTION UNLOCK CONDITION ACTIVE

Objective:
Help Lucien Remember Who He Is

Reward:
???

Hidden Reward:

Aiden stared at the screen.

Then at the approaching prince.

Then back at the screen.

Then back at Lucien.

And finally said the only reasonable thing possible.

"Oh... we're completely screwed."
