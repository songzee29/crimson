# **ZEE: THE FIRST TRUE TRIBRID KING**  
## **Part VII — The Crimson Dynasty**

---

# **Chapter 46 — A Kingdom No Longer Empty**

The Crimson Kingdom changed after Rez’s transformation.

For centuries the palace had been cold:
stone halls
silent guards
fear disguised as loyalty

But now?

It felt alive.

Music echoed through the halls again.
Arguments broke out over dinner.
Lucien raced through corridors while Elias yelled at him to stop using magic indoors.

And somehow…

The terrifying Tribrid King found himself surrounded by chaos instead of loneliness.

Rez made it worse.

On purpose.

One afternoon Zee entered the throne room expecting reports about supernatural rebellions.

Instead he found:
- Lucien teaching wolves card games
- Elias reorganizing ancient grimoires
- Rez sitting on the throne eating chips

Zee stared silently.

Rez looked at him casually.

> “What?”
>
> “The throne’s comfortable.”

The guards looked horrified.

Lucien burst into laughter.

And after a long pause…

Even Zee smirked.

Something impossible was happening to the immortal king.

He was healing.

---

# **Chapter 47 — The Witch Who Mocked Death**

Not everyone entered Zee’s life through violence.

Some arrived through pure chaos.

Her name was Nyra Vale.

A powerful runaway witch with silver braids, sharp eyes, and absolutely no survival instincts.

She broke into the Crimson Kingdom trying to steal magical artifacts.

Which normally would’ve gotten someone executed instantly.

Instead?

She accidentally insulted Zee to his face.

> “You’re taller than I expected.”
>
> “…Still dramatic though.”

The throne room froze.

Rez nearly choked laughing.

Lucien loved her immediately.

And strangely…

Zee didn’t kill her.

Nyra eventually admitted the truth:
she was fleeing a coven that experimented on young witches.

The same kind of cruelty Zee once survived himself.

So he offered her sanctuary.

Nyra accepted.

And over time she became:
- Lucien’s magical mentor
- Elias’s closest friend
- Rez’s constant partner in causing problems

Eventually the kingdom stopped calling her “guest.”

She became family.

---

# **Chapter 48 — The Wolf General**

Then came Kael.

A werewolf warrior nearly seven feet tall who once tried assassinating Zee during the early wars centuries ago.

The fight lasted three days.

Kael lost.

Badly.

But instead of killing him, Zee respected his loyalty to his people and spared him.

Ever since then, Kael served as protector of the kingdom.

Gruff.
Terrifying.
Emotionally awkward.

Lucien called him:
> “The angry uncle.”

Kael pretended to hate it.

He absolutely did not.

One night during patrol, Kael admitted something quietly to Zee:

> “This kingdom feels different now.”

Zee glanced toward the palace where laughter echoed through open windows.

Then answered softly:

> “That’s because it finally became a home.”

---

# **Chapter 49 — The Found Family of Monsters**

Years passed.

And slowly…

The Crimson Kingdom became something the supernatural world had never seen before.

Not an empire built purely on fear.

But refuge.

Runaway witches arrived seeking safety.
Abandoned wolves found pack bonds again.
Vampires tired of endless violence came searching for peace.

Humans even began secretly working alongside supernatural creatures inside the city.

For the first time in history…

Species stopped living entirely divided.

And all of it existed because a broken boy once refused to stay broken.

The world still feared Zee.

But now?

They also respected him.

Because despite all his power…

The king protected people the world threw away.

Just like someone once should’ve protected him.

---

# **Chapter 50 — Family Dinner**

One rare peaceful evening, the entire family gathered for dinner inside the royal hall.

And it was absolute chaos.

Rez and Nyra argued over cheating during cards.
Lucien accidentally set napkins on fire laughing.
Kael threatened to throw everyone outside if they didn’t calm down.

Elias sat beside Zee smiling softly at the disaster around them.

Then quietly asked:

> “What are you thinking about?”

Zee looked around the table.

At his son.
His brother.
His friends.
The man he loved.

Family.

Something he once believed he’d never deserve.

His voice came out quieter than expected.

> “I spent centuries trying to conquer the world.”
>
> “Turns out…”
>
> “This was the only thing I actually wanted.”

Elias squeezed his hand gently beneath the table.

And for once…

The immortal king looked peaceful.

---

# **Chapter 51 — The Threat Beyond Heaven**

But peace attracts attention.

Far beyond earth…
beyond magic…
beyond nature itself…

Something ancient awakened.

Celestial beings known only as **The Thrones** finally noticed Zee’s existence.

Entities older than creation.

Guardians of cosmic balance.

And they were horrified.

Because the Tribrid bloodline was spreading.

Growing.

Uniting supernatural species in ways never meant to happen.

To them…

Zee wasn’t merely dangerous anymore.

He was changing destiny itself.

And so the Thrones made their judgment:

> “The Crimson Dynasty must be erased.”

---

# **Chapter 52 — The King’s New Fear**

Late that night, Zee stood alone on the palace balcony watching storms roll across the horizon.

But this time…

Even he looked unsettled.

Elias approached quietly.

> “Something’s wrong.”

Zee nodded slowly.

> “I can feel them.”

> “Who?”

The wind suddenly stopped entirely.

Even magic itself felt nervous.

Then Zee answered with rare uncertainty:

> “Something older than gods.”

For the first time in centuries…

The First Tribrid King realized his family’s story was becoming larger than earth itself.

And somewhere beyond the stars…

Ancient cosmic powers were already preparing for war against the dynasty of monsters who dared reshape the world. 😮‍💨
