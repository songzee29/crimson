# **ZEE: THE CRIMSON DYNASTY**  
## **Part XIV — The Weight of the Crown**

---

# **Chapter 108 — The Silence Zee Left Behind**

At first…

The kingdom survived Zee’s ascension beautifully.

Lucien ruled wisely.
Serina balanced his darker instincts.
Lyra and Cassius brought life back into the palace halls.

And every crimson storm overhead reminded the world:

The Sovereign still watched.

But eventually…

Watching wasn’t enough.

Because the moment Zee stepped beyond reality itself, the universe noticed something dangerous:

The dynasty was vulnerable again.

Not weak.

But grieving.

And enemies always attacked grief.

---

# **Chapter 109 — The King Who Never Slept**

Lucien changed slowly over the centuries.

Not visibly.

Emotionally.

The responsibilities never stopped:
wars between supernatural territories
cosmic breaches opening across dimensions
human rebellions against immortals
rogue celestial creatures attacking cities

Every day demanded something from him.

A decision.
A sacrifice.
A punishment.

And unlike Zee…

Lucien cared deeply about every loss.

That became his greatest strength.

And eventually…

His greatest weakness.

Serina noticed it first.

The late nights.
The silence.
The way Lucien stared at the throne after meetings as though it physically hurt him.

One night she touched his face gently.

> “You need rest.”

Lucien laughed weakly.

> “Kings don’t get rest.”

That answer sounded too much like Zee.

And it terrified her.

---

# **Chapter 110 — Cracks in the Dynasty**

The wars grew worse.

A new empire known as **The Hollow Dominion** rose beyond Europe:
immortal conquerors enhanced through stolen cosmic energy.

They believed the Crimson Dynasty had grown soft through peace and family.

Their leader, Emperor Vael, openly challenged Lucien’s rule:

> “Your father ruled through fear.”
>
> “You rule through attachment.”
>
> “That makes you weaker.”

Lucien answered the challenge personally.

The war lasted eighty-seven years.

Eighty-seven years of:
burned kingdoms
dead immortals
collapsing dimensions
constant violence

And every victory cost Lucien something.

A piece of patience.
A piece of softness.
A piece of himself.

---

# **Chapter 111 — Cassius the Wild Prince**

Meanwhile Cassius grew into exactly the kind of prince nobody could control.

He fought recklessly.
Loved recklessly.
Lived recklessly.

But beneath all the chaos…

He was loyal to the dynasty completely.

He and Lyra became inseparable:
battle partners
best friends
sometimes each other’s only emotional support

Cassius often dragged her away from royal responsibilities just to make her laugh.

One night while sitting atop the palace towers, Lyra admitted quietly:

> “I think Lucien’s breaking.”

Cassius looked toward the throne room windows sadly.

> “Yeah.”
>
> “And nobody knows how to stop it.”

---

# **Chapter 112 — The Princess of Eternity Suffers**

Lyra carried her own burden.

Everyone feared her power still.

Even allies looked at her carefully sometimes.

Because unlike Zee or Lucien…

Lyra’s abilities continued evolving naturally without struggle.

She could:
- rewrite magical laws instinctively
- heal broken dimensions
- destroy celestial beings effortlessly

And the more powerful she became…

The lonelier she felt.

Because nobody truly understood her anymore.

Not even Lucien.

The only person who sometimes did?

Cassius.

Which frightened them both.

---

# **Chapter 113 — The Day Serina Died**

Everything shattered during the Siege of Prague.

The Hollow Dominion launched a surprise attack using anti-cosmic weapons created from void remnants.

Lucien and Serina fought side by side like always.

Until Vael targeted the one weakness Lucien never protected well enough:

Love.

The blade pierced Serina directly through the heart.

Lucien caught her before she fell.

The battlefield went silent.

And suddenly…

The prince everyone loved disappeared.

Because grief turned Lucien into something colder instantly.

Serina touched his face weakly as blood stained her hands.

> “Don’t become lost…”

Tears streamed down Lucien’s face violently.

> “Please stay.”

But immortality had limits even now.

And Serina died in his arms.

---

# **Chapter 114 — The Son Becomes the Father**

After Serina’s death…

Lucien changed permanently.

The kingdom felt it.

Storms darkened constantly.
His cosmic energy became unstable.
Entire rooms froze when he entered them emotionally overwhelmed.

He stopped sleeping entirely.

Stopped laughing.

Stopped speaking unless necessary.

And slowly…

He became terrifying in the exact same way Zee once had.

The wars worsened because Lucien stopped showing mercy.

Entire enemy bloodlines vanished overnight.
Cities surrendered purely from hearing he was approaching.

Even allies grew nervous.

One night Rez confronted him directly.

> “This isn’t you.”

Lucien’s eyes glowed sharply.

> “You think I don’t know that?”

Rez stepped closer carefully.

> “Your father went through this too.”

That broke something inside Lucien instantly.

Because unlike everyone else…

Lucien never wanted to become Zee.

Not the broken version.

Not the lonely version.

Yet somehow…

He already had.

---

# **Chapter 115 — Lyra’s Fear**

Late one evening Lyra found Lucien sitting alone inside Zee’s old throne room.

Darkness surrounded him.

The throne looked too large somehow.

Like it still belonged to someone else.

Lucien didn’t look up when she entered.

> “You should be resting.”

Lyra ignored him.

Then quietly whispered:

> “I miss him too.”

Silence.

Finally Lucien spoke.

His voice cracked slightly.

> “I used to think father was unstoppable.”
>
> “Now I understand.”
>
> “He was just tired.”

That hurt Lyra more than any war ever had.

Because she realized the truth:

Zee didn’t leave because he stopped loving them.

He left because protecting existence cost him everything.

---

# **Chapter 116 — Endless War**

Centuries passed.

And the wars never truly ended.

Every victory created another enemy.
Every peace created another rebellion.

Lucien carried the kingdom alone longer than anyone realized.

And eventually…

Even kings break.

---

# **Chapter 117 — The Son Finally Falls Apart**

It happened during the Burning of Rome.

The Hollow Dominion returned stronger than ever.
Cassius nearly died protecting civilians.
Lyra exhausted herself holding reality together.

And Lucien?

Lucien stood alone against an army for three straight days.

By the end…
his armor was shattered
his hands trembling
his soul exhausted

And for the first time in centuries…

The King of the Crimson Dynasty dropped to his knees.

The battlefield froze watching him.

Rain poured heavily across ruined streets.

Lucien looked upward toward the crimson storms above.

Toward the place where Zee once watched over them.

And finally…

The son broke.

Tears streamed down his face violently as centuries of grief crushed him all at once.

> “PLEASE…”

His voice cracked across the heavens.

> “I CAN’T DO THIS ANYMORE.”

The storm trembled.

Lucien’s breathing became uneven.

Broken.

Desperate.

> “I LOST HER.”
>
> “I LOST MYSELF.”
>
> “I’M TRYING.”
>
> “I’M STILL TRYING.”

Every soldier stood frozen.

Because kings were never supposed to cry like this.

But Lucien wasn’t speaking as a king anymore.

He was speaking as a son.

A terrified exhausted son begging for his father.

> “Dad…”
>
> “Please come back.”
>
> “Please save me.”
>
> “I don’t know how to carry this without you.”

The crimson sky shook violently.

And somewhere beyond reality itself…

Something ancient heard him. 😮‍💨
