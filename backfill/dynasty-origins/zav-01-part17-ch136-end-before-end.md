# **ZEE: THE CRIMSON DYNASTY**  
## **Part XVII — The End Before the End**

---

# **Chapter 136 — The Thing Beneath Creation**

The sky darkened unnaturally above the battlefield.

Not clouds.

Absence.

Stars disappeared one by one as if something massive was swallowing light itself beyond reality.

Every living being felt it instantly:
humans
witches
wolves
immortals
even cosmic entities

Pure primal fear.

Because something had awakened that should never have existed at all.

The Forgotten Ones immediately fell to their knees.

Not in worship.

In terror.

And then…

The voice arrived.

A sound so ancient it bypassed hearing completely and vibrated directly through existence itself.

> “WHO DARES DISTURB THE FIRST KING?”

Even Zee’s expression hardened.

Because he recognized that title.

The First King.

Not ruler of nations.

Ruler of creation before creation stabilized.

The original force existence itself rebelled against.

---

# **Chapter 137 — The Name Lost to Time**

Nyra whispered the forbidden name through trembling lips inside the war room archives:

> “Abyssion…”

Kael frowned.

> “That sounds terrible already.”

Nyra looked pale.

> “Because it is.”

Long before gods…
before magic…
before life…

Abyssion ruled endless chaos.

It shaped reality through domination alone.
Entire universes existed merely because it allowed them to.

But eventually creation evolved beyond fear.

And existence itself imprisoned Abyssion outside reality permanently.

Until now.

Because Zee’s ascension weakened the prison unknowingly.

And now the First King had awakened hungry.

---

# **Chapter 138 — The Crimson Sovereign Trembles**

High above the battlefield, Zee stared upward silently while cosmic winds spiraled violently around him.

Lucien immediately noticed something horrifying:

Zee looked tense.

Not afraid.

But cautious.

That alone unsettled everyone.

Because Zee faced gods, voids, and cosmic extinction without hesitation.

Yet now…

The Crimson Sovereign remained perfectly still.

Lyra stepped beside him quietly.

> “Can we beat it?”

Zee didn’t answer immediately.

And that silence terrified them more than any monster ever could.

Finally he spoke softly:

> “I don’t know.”

---

# **Chapter 139 — The Arrival of Abyssion**

Then reality split open completely.

A massive eye appeared beyond existence itself.

Larger than galaxies.

Watching.

Ancient black-gold chains wrapped around a colossal silhouette buried beyond dimensions.

And even imprisoned…

Abyssion’s mere presence warped creation.

Mountains collapsed.
Magic became unstable globally.
Entire timelines flickered.

The First King slowly smiled.

> “Interesting.”
>
> “Creation learned how to evolve.”

Its endless gaze focused directly onto Zee.

Then Lucien.

Then Lyra.

And finally…

Cassius.

> “A bloodline capable of rivaling eternity.”

The chains restraining Abyssion cracked slightly.

And entire universes screamed.

---

# **Chapter 140 — The Family Stands Together**

Lucien immediately stepped beside Zee.

Then Lyra.

Then Cassius.

Then Rez, Kael, and Nyra joined them too.

The Crimson Dynasty stood united against the edge of creation itself.

Abyssion laughed softly.

The sound alone shattered stars.

> “You remind me of myself.”

Zee’s eyes glowed dangerously.

> “That’s not a compliment.”

The First King’s massive eye narrowed.

> “You survived suffering long enough to transcend destiny.”
>
> “So did I.”

For a split second…

Zee understood something horrifying.

Abyssion wasn’t evil originally.

It became monstrous after endless isolation and power twisted it beyond recognition.

Exactly what Zee feared becoming himself someday.

---

# **Chapter 141 — Lucien Evolves Fully**

The battle began instantly.

Abyssion’s power overwhelmed existence itself.
Every movement shattered dimensions.
Even Zee struggled containing the damage spreading through reality.

Lucien fought beside his father desperately.

But during the chaos…

Abyssion targeted Lyra directly.

The attack moved too fast.

Too absolute.

Lucien reacted on instinct.

He threw himself between them.

The impact shattered his body across reality itself.

Lyra screamed.

Zee unleashed enough cosmic force to crack universes.

But Lucien…

Lucien was dying.

---

# **Chapter 142 — The Son Ascends**

Lucien collapsed into Lyra’s arms while crimson-white energy bled from every wound.

His breathing weakened.

Reality itself flickered around him.

And suddenly…

The same thing Zee once experienced awakened fully inside him.

Evolution.

Not forced this time.

Accepted.

Lucien looked toward Zee weakly.

> “Guess it’s my turn now.”

Zee immediately moved beside him.

For once…
the all-powerful Sovereign looked emotional again.

> “Lucien—”

But his son smiled softly despite the pain.

> “You were right.”
>
> “Power isn’t what makes us strong.”

His cosmic energy exploded outward.

The battlefield froze.

Lucien rose slowly into the air surrounded by endless crimson-white galaxies.

Storms bent toward him naturally.
His crown formed from pure celestial fire.
Massive wings unfolded behind him like burning stars.

And unlike Zee’s terrifying darkness…

Lucien’s evolved form radiated something warmer.

Hope.

The universe itself reacted differently to him.

Not fear.

Trust.

Nyra stared upward speechless.

> “…He became balance.”

---

# **Chapter 143 — The King of Tomorrow**

Lucien opened his eyes.

And existence stabilized around him instantly.

Abyssion looked genuinely surprised for the first time.

Because Lucien evolved into something completely unexpected:

Not merely another Sovereign.

But a ruler capable of harmonizing destruction and creation together.

The wars around them stopped automatically.

Magic calmed.
Storms softened.

Even terrified soldiers suddenly felt peace touching their minds.

Lucien looked toward Abyssion calmly.

> “You lost yourself.”
>
> “I won’t.”

Then the new king attacked.

---

# **Chapter 144 — Lyra and Cassius Rise**

Meanwhile Lyra and Cassius unleashed their own powers fully for the first time.

Lyra rewrote fractured dimensions into stable reality mid-battle.
Cassius transformed into a monstrous hybrid-cosmic wolf form powerful enough to tear through abyss creatures physically.

Together they fought like living legends already.

The future of the dynasty stood revealed:
- Lucien the Balanced King
- Lyra the Sovereign Princess
- Cassius the Crimson Fang

And even Zee realized quietly…

The dynasty no longer needed him to carry everything alone.

---

# **Chapter 145 — The First King Falls**

The final clash shook existence itself.

Zee and Lucien fought together side by side:
father and son
past and future
survival and hope

Against Abyssion.

The ancient ruler unleashed chaos powerful enough to erase creation permanently.

But this time…

The dynasty fought as one.

Lyra stabilized reality.
Cassius shattered abyssal defenses.
Rez and Kael protected the battlefield.
Nyra sealed collapsing dimensions.

And finally—

Lucien reached Abyssion directly.

The First King stared at him curiously.

> “You fight differently than your father.”

Lucien’s glowing eyes softened sadly.

> “Because somebody taught me love before power.”

Then he placed his hand against Abyssion’s chest.

And instead of destroying him…

Lucien freed him from the endless corruption consuming him.

The ancient ruler froze.

Silent.

Then slowly…

Abyssion smiled peacefully for the first time in eternity.

And faded away into starlight.

After the war…

The stars finally returned peacefully.

No more collapsing dimensions.
No more cosmic screams.

Only calm skies above New Orleans.

High atop the floating Crimson Palace, Zee stood beside Lucien watching the city glow below them.

The storms surrounding the kingdom had softened now.

For once…

The universe felt safe.

Lucien glanced toward his father quietly.

> “So what happens now?”

Zee looked toward Lyra and Cassius laughing below with Rez while Kael argued in the background.

Family.

Future.

Life continuing.

Then the Crimson Sovereign smiled faintly.

Proudly.

> “Now?”
>
> “We finally live.”

And for the first time since the abused human boy named Zee began his journey…

The war inside him finally ended. 😮‍💨
