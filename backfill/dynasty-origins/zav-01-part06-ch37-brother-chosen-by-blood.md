# **ZEE: THE FIRST TRUE TRIBRID KING**  
## **Part VI — The Brother Chosen by Blood**

---

# **Chapter 37 — Before the Crown**

Long before Zee became a king…

Before the storms followed his anger…

Before the world feared his name…

There was Rez.

Darez Laurent.

Called “Rez” by everyone who knew him.

He grew up three apartment buildings away from Zee back when life was still small enough to fit inside broken neighborhoods and streetlights.

Rez was loud where Zee was quiet.
Fearless where Zee was angry.
Funny when the world felt unbearable.

When Marcus screamed at Zee as a child…

Rez would throw rocks at the apartment windows just to distract him long enough for Zee to escape.

When other kids feared Zee…

Rez sat beside him anyway.

And one rainy night after Zee accidentally shattered a streetlamp with his emotions, terrified he was becoming a monster, Rez simply shrugged and said:

> “Cool.”
>
> “Do it again.”

It was the first time Zee laughed after years of pain.

They became brothers long before immortality entered their lives.

But life separated them eventually.

When Zee disappeared into the supernatural world, Rez believed his best friend had died.

And for centuries…

That was the lie Zee allowed him to keep.

Because loving mortals always ended in grief.

---

# **Chapter 38 — The Human Who Found the King**

One night, centuries later, a black SUV pulled up outside the Crimson Kingdom.

The guards immediately sensed something strange.

Human heartbeat.

But absolutely no fear.

The doors opened.

And out stepped a man in a leather jacket holding a cigar.

Older now.
Scar across his eyebrow.
Same reckless confidence.

Rez.

The guards surrounded him instantly.

> “State your business.”

Rez looked past them toward the massive palace doors.

Then smirked.

> “Tell Zee his brother’s here.”

Inside the throne room, Zee froze.

Because after centuries…

He recognized that voice immediately.

---

# **Chapter 39 — Brothers Reunited**

The throne room doors burst open.

Rez walked in casually while supernatural soldiers watched nervously.

And there sat Zee:
black crown
crimson eyes
storm magic flickering behind him

The terrifying Tribrid King himself.

Rez stared for a long moment.

Then pointed at the throne.

> “…You really went full vampire emperor, huh?”

Silence.

Then—

Zee laughed.

Not the cold controlled laugh of a ruler.

A genuine laugh that echoed through the throne room.

Before anyone could react, Zee crossed the room instantly and pulled Rez into a crushing hug.

The entire kingdom stared in disbelief.

Because nobody touched the king casually.

Nobody joked with him.

Nobody treated him like *Zee.*

But Rez did.

Always had.

---

# **Chapter 40 — The Last Piece of Humanity**

Over the following weeks, Rez became part of the palace naturally.

Elias loved him immediately.

Lucien practically worshipped him.

And somehow, despite being completely human, Rez was one of the only people alive capable of calming Zee during his darker moments.

When Zee spiraled into rage…

Rez grounded him.

When the king isolated himself…

Rez dragged him out of the throne room by force.

One night during a storm, Rez sat beside Zee on the palace balcony drinking whiskey.

> “You know,” Rez muttered,
> “for an immortal nightmare king, you’re still dramatic as hell.”

Zee smirked faintly.

Then grew quiet.

> “You should leave.”

Rez blinked.

> “What?”

> “The people around me don’t survive forever.”

Rez stared at him for a moment before laughing softly.

> “Zee…”
>
> “You were protecting me long before you became immortal.”
>
> “I’m still here.”

But deep down…

Zee was terrified.

Because fate always destroyed the things he loved most.

---

# **Chapter 41 — The Enemy Beneath the Earth**

Far beneath New Orleans…

Something ancient awakened.

Older than witches.
Older than nature’s balance.

A creature sealed away during the first supernatural wars:
**Malakar.**

The Devourer.

An immortal entity that fed on supernatural power itself.

And Zee’s existence had finally become powerful enough to wake it.

Malakar wanted one thing:

Consume the Tribrid bloodline.

Starting with the humans closest to the king.

---

# **Chapter 42 — The Night Rez Died**

It happened fast.

Too fast.

Rez and Lucien were returning from the human side of the city when shadows swallowed the street entirely.

Cars shut down.
Lights died.
Magic vanished.

Then Malakar appeared.

A massive figure made of living darkness and bone.

Lucien attacked instantly, but the creature threw him aside effortlessly.

Rez grabbed the boy protectively.

> “RUN!”

But Malakar’s claws pierced directly through Rez’s chest.

Lucien screamed.

And miles away…

Zee felt it happen.

The sky above New Orleans exploded crimson instantly.

---

# **Chapter 43 — The King’s Breaking Point**

By the time Zee arrived, the street looked like a warzone.

Lucien was injured.
Buildings destroyed.

And Rez…

Rez was dying.

Zee dropped to his knees beside him.

For a moment, the immortal king couldn’t breathe.

Not again.

Not someone else.

Not his brother.

Rez coughed blood weakly before laughing painfully.

> “Damn…”
>
> “That thing hit hard.”

Zee’s hands trembled as he pressed them against the wound trying desperately to heal him.

But even his power couldn’t stop death completely.

For the first time in centuries…

Panic consumed him.

> “Stay with me.”
>
> “Rez, LOOK AT ME.”

Rez’s fading eyes locked onto his.

> “You know…”
>
> “I always knew you’d become something insane.”

Tears burned down Zee’s face.

> “Don’t do this.”

Rez smiled weakly.

> “You’re still my brother.”
>
> “King or monster.”
>
> “Always.”

His heartbeat slowed.

And something inside Zee shattered.

---

# **Chapter 44 — The Fourth Tribrid**

The storm above the city became violent enough to shake skyscrapers.

Every supernatural creature in New Orleans felt Zee losing control.

Because grief always made him stronger.

More dangerous.

More unstable.

Zee looked down at Rez dying in his arms…

And made his decision instantly.

> “No.”
>
> “I’m not losing you too.”

He bit into his own wrist deeply.

Ancient crimson blood poured into Rez’s mouth.

Then Zee unleashed forbidden magic directly into his soul.

The transformation began violently.

Bones cracked.
Veins glowed red.
The air exploded with supernatural energy.

Lucien backed away in shock.

Because unlike Elias and Lucien’s transformations…

Rez’s was different.

Wild.

Unstable.

Powerful.

Because his bond with Zee had existed long before immortality.

The magic recognized him not as servant…

But family.

---

# **Chapter 45 — Rise of the Crimson Brother**

Three nights later…

Rez woke up starving.

His eyes flashed crimson-gold.

Wolf venom burned beneath his skin.
Magic surged through his veins.
Vampire instincts sharpened every sense.

And standing beside him was Zee.

Silent.
Exhausted.
Terrified.

Rez sat up slowly.

Then looked at his hands glowing with energy.

> “…You turned me into a supernatural menace.”

Zee blinked.

Rez grinned.

> “Honestly?”
>
> “Kinda love it.”

For the first time since the attack…

Zee smiled again.

Then Rez stood up and pulled him into a hug.

> “You saved me.”
>
> “That’s what brothers do.”

And deep down…

The immortal king realized something important.

Family was no longer the weakness his enemies believed it was.

It was becoming his greatest strength.

Because now the world didn’t face one immortal tribrid king.

It faced a dynasty. 😮‍💨
