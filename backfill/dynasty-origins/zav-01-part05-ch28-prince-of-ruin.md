# **ZEE: THE FIRST TRUE TRIBRID KING**  
## **Part V — The Prince of Ruin**

---

# **Chapter 28 — The Child Born From a King**

Lucien Mikaelson was not normal.

Even among tribrids.

By age ten:
- he could compel vampires without speaking
- wolves obeyed him instinctively
- magic bent around his emotions naturally

And worst of all?

He inherited Zee’s rage.

The palace servants feared his temper.

One moment he’d be laughing with Elias in the gardens…

The next, candles exploded because someone lied to him.

Zee saw too much of himself in the boy.

And that terrified him.

Because Zee remembered what pain had turned *him* into.

He refused to let the world do the same thing to Lucien.

---

# **Chapter 29 — The Soft Heart of Kings**

Unlike Zee, Elias remained gentle even after becoming immortal.

He became the balance inside the kingdom.

Where Zee inspired fear…
Elias inspired loyalty.

He learned magic slowly.
Protected humans secretly.
Built safe places for supernatural runaways.

People started calling him:
> “The Heart of the Crimson Kingdom.”

And honestly?

Zee adored him for it.

Some nights the terrifying Tribrid King would abandon meetings, wars, and politics just to sit quietly beside Elias in their library.

Because after centuries of violence…

Peace felt addictive.

But peace never lasted long for monsters like them.

---

# **Chapter 30 — The Prophecy Returns**

One winter evening, an ancient witch arrived at the palace gates half-dead and covered in blood.

Her final words chilled the kingdom:

> “The child must die.”

Zee’s eyes glowed instantly.

> “Explain.”

The witch trembled violently.

> “Your son…”
>
> “He is not merely tribrid.”
>
> “He is the end.”

Silence swallowed the throne room.

Then she revealed the prophecy hidden even from nature itself:

> “The First King would shake the world…”
>
> “But the son born from love and immortality…”
>
> “Will either unite all species…”
>
> “Or erase them forever.”

Even Zee felt cold hearing it.

Because deep down…

He knew Lucien’s power kept growing unnaturally fast.

Too fast.

---

# **Chapter 31 — Father and Son**

Later that night, Zee found Lucien sitting alone on the palace roof watching lightning storms over New Orleans.

The boy looked upset.

> “Everybody fears me.”

Zee stood beside him silently.

> “Fear comes naturally to powerful creatures.”

Lucien looked down.

> “Did they fear you too?”

Zee laughed softly.

> “They still do.”

The boy hesitated before asking:

> “Am I becoming a monster?”

That question nearly broke Zee’s heart.

Because he remembered asking himself the same thing once.

So the immortal king knelt in front of his son and answered honestly:

> “No.”
>
> “But the world will try to convince you that you are.”

Lucien’s crimson eyes shimmered slightly.

> “Then how do I stop becoming one?”

Zee looked toward the city lights below.

Then quietly whispered:

> “Hold onto the people you love.”
>
> “That’s the only thing that saved me.”

---

# **Chapter 32 — The Order of Dawn**

The attacks returned months later.

But this time…

Different.

An ancient organization emerged from hiding:
**The Order of Dawn.**

Older than vampires.
Older than covens.
Older than most kingdoms.

Their mission had remained unchanged for thousands of years:

Destroy supernatural abominations before they consume the world.

And to them?

The Mikaelson family represented the greatest threat in existence.

Their leader, Cassian Vale, stood before his army wearing armor forged from enchanted silver.

In his hand rested a weapon created specifically to kill tribrids.

A black spear carved from the bones of extinct gods.

And carved into the weapon were three names:

- Zee
- Elias
- Lucien

---

# **Chapter 33 — The Fall of the Palace**

The Order attacked at sunrise.

The kingdom exploded into chaos.

Walls collapsed beneath enchanted artillery.
Ancient barriers shattered.
Even Zee’s strongest warriors fell under Dawn weapons.

For the first time in centuries…

The Crimson Kingdom was losing.

Zee tore through enemy soldiers mercilessly, moving like death itself across the battlefield.

But Cassian wasn’t trying to defeat Zee.

He was distracting him.

Because hidden deep inside the palace…

Assassins had already reached Lucien.

---

# **Chapter 34 — Blood of the Prince**

Lucien fought viciously despite his age.

Magic burst uncontrollably around him.
Wolf claws ripped through armored hunters.
His vampire speed blurred through hallways.

But there were too many.

A hunter stabbed silver into his shoulder.

Another wrapped enchanted chains around him.

Lucien screamed—

And something ancient answered.

The entire palace shook violently.

Then suddenly…

Everything went still.

Too still.

The assassins slowly turned around.

And froze.

Zee stood in the doorway.

Silent.

Covered in blood.

Eyes glowing brighter than anyone had ever seen before.

Not red anymore.

Pure crimson-white.

The air itself distorted around him.

Because this wasn’t the King.

This was a father watching someone hurt his child.

And the world had made that mistake before.

---

# **Chapter 35 — Wrath Beyond Nature**

The massacre lasted less than sixty seconds.

Nobody survived.

Not the hunters.
Not the assassins.
Not even the walls around them.

Zee ripped through the palace like a living apocalypse.

Cassian watched in horror as his entire army disappeared.

> “What are you?!”

Zee slowly walked toward him holding Lucien protectively in one arm.

> “I’m the consequence of your fear.”

Cassian threw the god-bone spear directly at Zee’s heart.

The weapon pierced his chest.

The battlefield gasped.

For one moment…

The immortal king stood frozen.

Blood dripping slowly from the wound.

Then Zee looked down at the spear…

And snapped it in half with one hand.

Fear spread across every surviving member of the Order.

Because the impossible had happened again.

Zee had evolved.

Even weapons meant for gods no longer worked on him.

---

# **Chapter 36 — The King’s Promise**

After the battle, the ruined kingdom stood silent beneath falling ash.

Elias treated Lucien’s wounds while Zee stared across the destruction.

His family approached carefully.

Lucien looked shaken.

> “They’ll never stop hunting us… will they?”

Zee was quiet for a long time.

Then finally answered:

> “No.”
>
> “They won’t.”

Elias gently touched his hand.

> “Then what do we do?”

The Tribrid King looked toward the horizon as storms gathered again.

And something ancient moved behind his crimson eyes.

Not fear.

Not anger.

Decision.

> “We stop surviving.”
>
> “And start building a world where nobody can threaten this family again.”

The thunder roared above New Orleans.

And somewhere deep beneath the earth…

Old powers began awakening in response to the King’s declaration.

Because even after all this time…

Zee’s story was still only beginning. 😮‍💨
