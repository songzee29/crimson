## **Part XVI — The Beginning of the Return**

# **Chapter 126 — The Kingdom Breathes Again**

After Rome, something shifted across the dynasty.

Not peace.

But hope.

Lucien returned to New Orleans different somehow:
still grieving
still scarred
still carrying centuries of war on his shoulders

Yet steadier.

The palace noticed immediately.

He started speaking during dinners again.
Started training with Cassius and Lyra personally.
Started walking through the city instead of hiding inside war rooms.

For the first time since Serina’s death…

The kingdom saw its king living instead of merely surviving.

And above the city, the crimson storms glowed warmer.

As though Zee himself approved.

---

# **Chapter 127 — Lyra’s Visions**

But Lyra kept seeing things.

At first they came during sleep:
fractured stars
bleeding dimensions
a throne floating in darkness

Then the visions worsened.

She’d suddenly freeze during conversations.
Hear whispers inside the wind.
See shadows moving behind reality itself.

One night she collapsed during a royal meeting.

Silver-crimson energy exploded around her violently as ancient symbols burned through the throne room walls.

Lucien caught her immediately.

> “Lyra!”

Her eyes opened slowly.

Terrified.

> “Something’s coming.”

The room went silent.

Because Lyra never feared anything.

# **Chapter 128 — The Forgotten Ones**

Nyra eventually uncovered the truth hidden deep within forbidden cosmic archives.

And even she looked disturbed reading it.

> “Before the Void Court…”
>
> “Before the Thrones…”
>
> “There existed something older.”

Kael crossed his arms uneasily.

> “Older than reality again?”
>
> “I’m getting tired of hearing that.”

Nyra ignored him.

Then quietly whispered the name:

> **The Forgotten Ones.**

Ancient rulers erased from existence long before creation stabilized.

Not dead.

Buried outside reality itself.

And now…

The barriers keeping them trapped were weakening.

Why?

Because Zee’s final evolution accidentally shook every layer of existence.

Including their prison.

---

# **Chapter 129 — The Cost of the Sovereign**

Far beyond dimensions…

Zee stood alone.

Watching.

Waiting.

The Crimson Sovereign had become so powerful that reality itself struggled containing him fully anymore.

Stars bent toward him instinctively.
Time fractured around his presence.
Entire universes shifted when he moved emotionally.

And worst of all?

He could feel his humanity slowly slipping further away.

That terrified him more than any enemy ever had.

Because every day it became harder remembering:
warmth
fear
hunger
sleep

Human things.

The only reason he still remained himself at all…

Was his family.

Their voices anchored him.

Their love kept him from becoming something cold and infinite.

But now?

He felt the Forgotten Ones awakening.

And for the first time in ages…

Even Zee felt uncertain.

---

# **Chapter 130 — Cassius and Lyra**

Meanwhile Cassius and Lyra grew closer than ever during the chaos.

Not romantically.

Something deeper.

Two heirs born carrying impossible expectations.

They understood each other in ways nobody else could.

One night they sat atop the floating palace towers overlooking New Orleans glowing beneath endless storms.

Cassius glanced toward her quietly.

> “You scared?”

Lyra stared at the stars.

> “Terrified.”

He smirked faintly.

> “Good.”
>
> “Means you’re still sane.”

She laughed softly.

Then unexpectedly leaned against his shoulder.

For a moment…

The weight of destiny disappeared.

No titles.
No prophecies.

Just two young immortals trying desperately not to drown beneath their family legacy.

---

# **Chapter 131 — The First Crack**

Then reality broke.

Literally.

A massive fracture opened above the Atlantic Ocean stretching across the sky itself.

And from it emerged something horrifying:
towering beings wrapped in black oceans of starlight
faces hidden beneath shattered crowns

The Forgotten Ones.

Human militaries attacked immediately.

Entire fleets vanished instantly.

Celestial beings tried intervening.

They were erased effortlessly.

The world descended into panic.

Because these entities weren’t conquering.

They were reclaiming existence itself.

---

# **Chapter 132 — Lucien Leads the War**

The Crimson Dynasty mobilized instantly.

Lucien stood at the front lines once again:
armor glowing crimson-white
storms spiraling around him

Beside him fought:
Lyra
Cassius
Rez
Nyra
Kael

Family.

The battle shattered continents.

Cassius tore through creatures with reckless fury.
Lyra repaired collapsing dimensions while unleashing cosmic destruction simultaneously.
Lucien fought like a king possessed.

But the Forgotten Ones were overwhelming.

Every creature destroyed simply reshaped itself again.

And slowly…

The dynasty began losing ground.

---

# **Chapter 133 — The Last Message**

During the battle, Lyra suddenly froze.

Her silver-crimson eyes widened violently.

Then she whispered:

> “…Grandfather.”

Everyone stopped.

The sky above the battlefield darkened completely.

Not with storm clouds.

With presence.

And suddenly…

Every Forgotten One turned upward in fear.

Fear.

The same creatures that terrified gods were afraid now.

Because something far worse had arrived.

---

# **Chapter 134 — The Return of the One True King**

The heavens split open.

Crimson galaxies poured across the sky like living oceans.

Reality bent downward as footsteps echoed through dimensions themselves.

Then Zee descended.

Not as the broken boy.
Not as the Tribrid King.
Not even as the Crimson Sovereign.

But as all of them combined.

Infinite cosmic wings stretched behind him.
His crown burned brighter than dying stars.
Storms wrapped around his body like living creatures.

And his eyes…

His eyes still looked human.

The battlefield fell completely silent.

Even Lucien looked overwhelmed seeing him physically again.

Because no matter how powerful they’d become…

Zee still felt impossible.

One Forgotten One tried attacking immediately.

Zee looked at it once.

ONCE.

And the creature disintegrated across every timeline simultaneously.

The remaining entities recoiled.

Because the being standing before them wasn’t merely powerful anymore.

He was consequence itself.

---

# **Chapter 135 — Father and Son Reunited**

Lucien slowly approached him through the ruined battlefield.

For a moment…

Neither king spoke.

Then Lucien laughed weakly through tears.

> “You always come back dramatic.”

Zee smirked faintly.

> “Runs in the family.”

And finally…

The son hugged his father again.

Not as rulers.

Not as legends.

Just family reunited after centuries of pain.

Zee held him tightly.

Proudly.

Then quietly whispered:

> “You survived.”
>
> “I knew you would.”

Lucien nearly broke apart hearing that.

But before emotions could fully settle—

The stars above them began dying again.

Because the true leader of the Forgotten Ones…

Was finally waking up. 😮‍💨
