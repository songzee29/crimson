## Chapter 33 — The Secret Zee Took To His Grave

The memory world trembled.

Cracks spread through the sky.

Entire pieces of Lucien's mind began collapsing into darkness.

Yet Aiden couldn't move.

He stood frozen before the black throne.

Before the thing sitting upon it.

The shadow looked almost human.

Almost.

Its shape shifted constantly.

One moment it appeared as a man.

The next as smoke.

The next as a living void.

Its golden eyes never blinked.

Never looked away.

Never stopped smiling.

Aiden finally found his voice.

"What are you?"

The creature chuckled.

A low sound.

Ancient.

Mocking.

"Wrong question."

The throne groaned beneath it.

"Ask who I am."

Aiden felt every instinct screaming at him to run.

But he stayed.

The creature leaned forward.

And smiled wider.

"I am the secret your precious ancestors buried."

The world shook.

Aiden's heart pounded.

The shadow laughed.

"Oh yes."

Its golden eyes narrowed.

"Zee never told them."

"Never told Lucien."

"Never told Seraphina."

"Never told anyone."

The creature stood.

The entire memory realm darkened.

And then it said the impossible.

"I am the first monster House Drakhar ever created."

## Chapter 34 — Before House Drakhar

The throne vanished.

The darkness swallowed everything.

Suddenly Aiden found himself standing inside another memory.

Much older.

Far older than any memory he'd ever witnessed.

Before the Crimson Dynasty.

Before the Ashborn.

Before kings.

Before empires.

Before House Drakhar.

A young man stood alone on a battlefield.

Covered in blood.

Broken.

Dying.

Aiden immediately recognized him.

Zee.

Not King Zee.

Not the First Crimson King.

Just Zee.

Young.

Angry.

Alone.

The version history never remembered.

The version that existed before the legend.

Aiden watched as Zee fell to one knee.

His body battered.

His enemies closing in.

Death approaching.

Then the sky opened.

Something descended.

Something ancient.

Something impossible.

Golden light flooded the battlefield.

The shadow beside Aiden smiled.

"I remember this day."

The younger Zee looked up.

And for the first time...

Fear appeared in his eyes.

The golden light spoke.

Not with words.

With promises.

Power.

Strength.

Survival.

Everything Zee wanted.

Everything a desperate young man would accept.

Everything a dying boy couldn't refuse.

The shadow's smile widened.

"He said yes."

Silence.

"He accepted."

Aiden stared.

"No."

The shadow laughed.

"Oh yes."

The battlefield disappeared.

The memory shattered.

And suddenly Aiden understood.

House Drakhar hadn't started with greatness.

It started with a bargain.

## Chapter 35 — The Price of Power

The memory shifted again.

Years passed in seconds.

Aiden watched Zee grow stronger.

Watched him become a warrior.

A king.

A legend.

Every battle made him stronger.

Every victory made him stronger.

Every evolution pushed him further.

But something else grew too.

The darkness.

The thing inside him.

The shadow standing beside Aiden.

The first monster.

The original corruption.

The hidden cost.

Aiden watched Zee realize the truth.

Watched him discover what he'd done.

And for the first time...

The great king looked terrified.

Not for himself.

For his children.

For Lucien.

For Seraphina.

For the future.

The shadow laughed.

"He finally understood."

The memory showed Zee building seals.

Ancient seals.

Powerful seals.

Desperate seals.

One after another.

Trying to contain the thing growing inside him.

Trying to stop what he'd unleashed.

Trying to protect his family.

Aiden's chest tightened.

Because now everything made sense.

The hidden history.

The erased records.

The sealed memories.

Zee wasn't hiding glory.

He was hiding guilt.

## Chapter 36 — Why Lucien Was Chosen

The shadow slowly walked around Aiden.

Like a predator circling prey.

"Do you know why I was sealed inside Lucien?"

Aiden remained silent.

The creature answered anyway.

"Because he was the strongest."

A vision appeared.

Lucien.

Young.

Powerful.

Unbreakable.

The greatest heir House Drakhar had ever produced.

The shadow smiled.

"Your ancestor thought his son could contain me."

The vision darkened.

Aiden watched Zee and Lucien arguing.

Not as king and prince.

As father and son.

A painful conversation.

One neither wanted.

One both understood.

Aiden couldn't hear the words.

But he understood enough.

Lucien knew.

Lucien volunteered.

The realization hit like a hammer.

Lucien chose this.

He chose imprisonment.

He chose isolation.

He chose sacrifice.

Not for power.

Not for glory.

For his family.

For the bloodline.

For everyone.

The shadow rolled its eyes.

"Disgustingly noble."

The memory shattered.

And for the first time...

Aiden felt genuine sorrow for the Crimson Prince.

Thousands of years trapped.

Thousands of years carrying a burden nobody knew existed.

Thousands of years suffering alone.

## Chapter 37 — The Heir's Choice

Outside the memory realm...

Lucien's body continued destroying the Hollow Throne.

Entire towers collapsed.

The kingdom itself was beginning to die.

Seraphina fought desperately.

Nyra protected civilians fleeing the ruins.

Rez searched for answers.

And Aiden stood at the center of Lucien's mind.

Face-to-face with the truth.

The shadow sat back upon the throne.

Relaxed.

Confident.

Patient.

"You're wondering what happens next."

Aiden didn't answer.

The creature smiled.

"I escape."

The throne room trembled.

"Lucien breaks."

The cracks spread.

"The bloodline falls."

The world darkened.

"And then Valthoros wins."

Silence.

The shadow's golden eyes narrowed.

"But there is another option."

Aiden already hated where this was going.

The shadow pointed directly at him.

"You."

The word echoed.

"You carry the Codex."

Another echo.

"You carry Zee's blood."

Another.

"You carry the future."

The shadow stood.

And for the first time...

Its smile disappeared.

"You can take Lucien's place."

Silence.

The offer hung in the air.

Terrible.

Tempting.

Dangerous.

Aiden's heart pounded.

Because he knew what it meant.

If he accepted...

Lucien would be free.

The bloodline would survive.

But Aiden would spend eternity carrying the monster.

Alone.

Just like Lucien.

The Codex suddenly appeared.

A new quest materialized.

### LEGENDARY QUEST

### THE HEIR'S CHOICE

Option One:
Save Yourself

Option Two:
Save Lucien

Reward:
Unknown

Failure:
Unknown

The shadow smiled once more.

"Choose carefully, little heir."

And somewhere outside the collapsing memory realm...

Lucien Drakhar screamed.

A scream filled not with rage.

But pain.

The pain of a man who had carried the world's burden for far too long.

And for the first time...

Aiden wasn't sure what the right answer was.
