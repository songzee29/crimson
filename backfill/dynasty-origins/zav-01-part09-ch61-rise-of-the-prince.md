# **ZEE: THE CRIMSON DYNASTY**  
## **Part IX — The Rise of the Prince**

---

# **Chapter 61 — The Heir of the End**

Lucien Mikaelson had stopped being a child centuries ago.

Now he stood nearly eye-to-eye with Zee himself:
sharp black curls
crimson-white eyes
power radiating from his skin like heat from fire

And unlike Zee…

Lucien smiled more.

Which somehow made him even more dangerous.

Because people never realized how terrifying he truly was until it was too late.

He inherited:
- Zee’s rage
- Elias’s heart
- Rez’s recklessness
- Nyra’s intelligence
- Kael’s brutality in battle

A perfect storm born from the Crimson Dynasty.

And the supernatural world had begun whispering something unsettling:

> “The prince may surpass the king.”

Even Zee heard the rumors.

He never denied them.

---

# **Chapter 62 — The Weight of a Crown**

One storm-filled evening, Zee summoned Lucien to the throne room alone.

The ancient hall glowed with crimson firelight while thunder shook the kingdom outside.

Lucien approached calmly.

> “You called for me?”

Zee studied his son silently for a long moment.

Not as a father.

As a king.

Then finally spoke:

> “You’re ready.”

Lucien’s expression shifted slightly.

Because he understood immediately.

The throne.

The kingdom.

The dynasty.

One day it would all become his responsibility.

But Lucien didn’t look excited.

He looked uncertain.

> “You built all of this.”
>
> “How am I supposed to replace you?”

Zee stood from the throne slowly.

The room darkened instinctively around him.

> “You don’t replace me.”
>
> “You become something greater.”

---

# **Chapter 63 — The Prince’s Desire**

But Lucien carried secrets.

Because despite his loyalty to the dynasty…

There was something he wanted for himself.

Someone.

Her name was Serina.

A human.

A musician from the French Quarter with a laugh capable of calming even Lucien’s darker impulses.

She knew nothing about supernatural politics.
Nothing about cosmic wars.
Nothing about the dynasty ruling beneath New Orleans.

And Lucien loved her desperately.

Which meant the universe immediately decided to ruin it.

Because enemies had learned from history.

The easiest way to hurt the Crimson bloodline…

Was through love.

---

# **Chapter 64 — The Rebellion of Ash**

A new faction rose against the dynasty:
**The Ashborn.**

Descendants of survivors from every war Zee ever fought.

Children of witches, hunters, and vampires who lost families to the Crimson King’s rise.

They believed the dynasty had become too powerful.

Too untouchable.

And they feared Lucien most of all.

Because unlike Zee…

Lucien inspired devotion naturally.

People followed him willingly.

The Ashborn leader, Valen Drakkar, made one thing clear:

> “Kill the prince before he becomes unstoppable.”

And unfortunately…

They discovered Serina.

---

# **Chapter 65 — Like Father, Like Son**

Lucien found out too late.

The Ashborn kidnapped Serina during one of her performances downtown.

By the time Lucien reached their hideout…

She was already bleeding.

Bound in enchanted chains.

Terrified.

Valen smiled when the prince arrived.

> “There he is.”
>
> “The heir to the apocalypse.”

Lucien’s eyes glowed instantly.

> “Let her go.”

Valen laughed.

> “Or what?”
>
> “You’ll become your father?”

Silence.

Then Lucien smiled.

Slowly.

Dangerously.

And suddenly…

Even the Ashborn realized they’d made a mistake.

Because Lucien didn’t merely inherit Zee’s power.

He inherited his fury too.

---

# **Chapter 66 — The Crimson Prince Awakens**

The lights exploded.

Walls cracked apart.

Magic spiraled violently around Lucien as his rage triggered something sleeping inside him.

For years Zee had restrained his son carefully.

Trained him.
Balanced him.
Protected him from the darker parts of their bloodline.

But now?

Lucien let go.

Completely.

His eyes burned bright crimson-white.
Shadow energy wrapped around his body like living fire.
The ground beneath him blackened instantly.

And unlike Zee…

Lucien’s power felt sharper.

Faster.

More chaotic.

The Ashborn attacked anyway.

None survived.

Lucien tore through them with terrifying precision:
magic
fangs
claws
cosmic force

Valen barely had time to scream before Lucien grabbed him by the throat.

> “You touched what belongs to me.”

The building shook violently.

> “Now die for it.”

And with one pulse of energy…

Valen disintegrated into ash.

---

# **Chapter 67 — The Fear of the Future**

When Lucien carried Serina home, the Crimson Kingdom fell silent watching him.

Because everyone sensed it.

The prince had crossed a line.

Not into evil.

Into certainty.

The same certainty Zee once gained when he stopped allowing the world to take things from him.

Later that night, Zee found Lucien standing alone on the palace balcony.

The city glowed beneath them while storms rolled overhead.

> “You killed them all,” Zee said quietly.

Lucien didn’t deny it.

> “They would’ve kept coming.”

Silence stretched.

Then Lucien finally asked:

> “Does it ever stop?”
>
> “The fear of losing people?”

Zee looked toward the horizon.

His answer came softer than expected.

> “No.”
>
> “You just become willing to burn the world to protect them.”

Lucien laughed weakly.

> “That sounds unhealthy.”

> “It is.”

For a moment they stood there not as kings…

But as father and son.

Two monsters shaped by love and violence equally.

---

# **Chapter 68 — The King’s Decision**

Weeks later, the dynasty gathered inside the throne room.

Elias.
Rez.
Nyra.
Kael.
Lucien.

Family.

Zee stood before the Crimson Throne wearing his black crown while red lightning flashed outside.

Then finally…

The immortal king removed the crown.

The entire room froze.

Zee turned toward Lucien slowly.

> “Kneel.”

Lucien hesitated.

Then obeyed.

And for the first time in history…

The Crown of the Crimson Dynasty touched another ruler.

The throne itself reacted violently.
Storms roared.
Magic surged through the kingdom.

Because the dynasty recognized its future king.

Zee rested a hand on Lucien’s shoulder.

> “You were born from love instead of pain.”
>
> “That already makes you stronger than me.”

Lucien looked up quietly.

> “And if the world comes for this kingdom?”

Zee’s crimson-white eyes glowed in the darkness.

A faint smile crossing his face.

> “Then remind them whose blood runs through your veins.”

---

# **Final Scene — The New Era**

Later that night, Lucien stood alone on the palace balcony wearing the crimson crown for the first time.

Below him stretched:
the kingdom
the city
the world his family built

And beside him stood Serina holding his hand.

But far away…

New enemies already whispered his name in fear.

Because the age of Zee Mikaelson was no longer ending.

It was evolving.

Again.

And the supernatural world slowly realized a terrifying truth:

The first king was unstoppable.

But his heir?

Might become something even worse. 😮‍💨
