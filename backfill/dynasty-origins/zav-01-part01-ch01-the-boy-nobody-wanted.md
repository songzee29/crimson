# **ZEE — The First Tribrid King**  
*A Dark Fantasy Origin Story*  

---

## **Chapter 1 — The Boy Nobody Wanted**

Before the legends… before the blood… before kingdoms whispered his name in fear…

Zee was just a boy.

He grew up in the dead corners of New Orleans, hidden behind broken apartments and streets stained with rainwater, smoke, and violence. His mother, Alina, worked endlessly to keep food on the table, but the world had already hardened her into someone cold. Love in their home came in fragments — sometimes a meal left on the counter, sometimes silence instead of screaming.

His stepfather Marcus was worse.

Cruel. Bitter. Drunk on failure.

Every time Zee spoke too loudly, dreamed too big, or defended himself, Marcus reminded him exactly “what kind of monster” he’d become.

> “You got darkness in you, boy.”

And maybe he was right.

Because strange things happened around Zee.

When he got angry, lights burst.  
When he cried, windows cracked.  
Animals stared at him too long before running away.

The neighborhood kids feared him. Adults whispered. Priests avoided eye contact.

Zee grew up believing something inside him was wrong.

He just didn’t know *what.*

---

# **Chapter 2 — Blood on Royal Street**

Everything changed the night he turned 17.

Marcus came home drunk again. Angry again.

But this time, he hit Alina harder than ever before.

Something inside Zee snapped.

Not emotionally.

Physically.

His heartbeat stopped.

For one terrifying second, the room fell silent.

Then his eyes glowed crimson.

Marcus froze.

The shadows in the room moved on their own.

And Zee… lost control.

When it was over, the apartment looked like a massacre. Walls ripped apart. Furniture splintered. Blood everywhere.

Marcus was dead.

And Zee stood over the body trembling — not from fear…

But hunger.

That’s when they found him.

Three strangers emerged from the darkness:
- a vampire
- a witch
- and a werewolf

Each one stared at him with horror.

Because Zee carried traits from *all three species.*

Impossible.

Abomination.

Prophecy.

The vampire woman whispered:

> “He shouldn’t exist.”

---

# **Chapter 3 — The Truth Hidden by Monsters**

Zee learned the truth slowly.

His mother had once belonged to a secret bloodline tied to ancient witchcraft. His biological father — a man Alina never spoke about — had been descended from one of the oldest werewolf clans in existence.

But the vampire side…

That was the impossible part.

Years ago, while pregnant, Alina had survived an attack by an ancient vampire tied to forbidden magic. The child inside her absorbed the cursed blood instead of dying.

Zee wasn’t born naturally.

He was created through accident, prophecy, and darkness.

A loophole in nature itself.

The first human capable of becoming:
- vampire
- witch
- and werewolf

All at once.

A tribrid.

Nature hated loopholes.

And nature would eventually try to destroy him.

---

# **Chapter 4 — The First Transformation**

At 18, Zee triggered the werewolf curse after accidentally killing a gang member protecting his best friend, Lena.

The pain nearly killed him.

Bones shattered. Skin tore open. He screamed until his voice disappeared entirely.

But unlike ordinary wolves…

His transformation awakened something ancient.

The vampire blood inside him activated.

He died during the transformation.

And came back.

When he woke up, he could hear heartbeats through walls. Smell fear from miles away. Feel magic crawling beneath the earth.

But something else awakened too.

A hunger unlike anything he'd ever known.

Not just for blood.

For power.

---

# **Chapter 5 — The City of Enemies**

News spread quickly through the supernatural world.

Witches wanted him dead because he violated nature.
Vampires feared him because he couldn’t truly die.
Werewolves saw him as either savior or curse.

Every faction hunted him.

And Zee learned quickly:

Power never attracts peace.

It attracts war.

He spent years running through cities:
- Chicago
- New Orleans
- Prague
- Paris
- Tokyo

Leaving bodies, enemies, and legends behind him.

Some called him:
> The Crimson King.

Others called him:
> The Beast of Three Souls.

But no matter how feared he became…

He still carried the broken boy from that apartment inside him.

---

# **Chapter 6 — Love & Betrayal**

Then he met Selene.

A witch capable of calming the darkness inside him.

For the first time, Zee felt human again.

She taught him music. Laughter. Peace.

And for a while, he stopped running.

Until the truth came out.

Selene had been sent to kill him from the beginning.

The witches believed Zee’s existence would eventually destroy the balance of life itself.

But she fell in love with him instead.

The coven gave her a choice:
- kill Zee
- or watch everyone she loved die

So she betrayed him.

Not with a blade.

With poison.

A dagger enchanted by ancient magic capable of weakening immortals.

The night she stabbed him, Zee didn’t scream.

He just looked hurt.

Truly hurt.

> “I would’ve burned the world for you.”

Selene cried as she whispered:
> “That’s exactly why they fear you.”

Her coven slaughtered her anyway.

And that night…

Zee became something far worse than a monster.

He became cold.

---

# **Chapter 7 — Rise of the Tribrid King**

Centuries passed.

Zee stopped hiding.

He built his own kingdom beneath New Orleans — a supernatural empire hidden in darkness.

Vampires bowed to him.
Wolves followed him.
Even witches feared speaking his name.

He wore black crowns forged from enchanted silver.

Storms followed his anger.

His enemies discovered terrifying truths:
- Stakes couldn’t kill him.
- Magic barely contained him.
- Wolf venom strengthened him.
- Even death rejected him.

He was no longer a loophole.

He was evolution.

And yet…

The throne never healed him.

Immortality only gave him more time to remember every betrayal.

Every death.

Every moment he was treated like something unworthy of love.

---

# **Chapter 8 — The Prophecy**

Ancient witches eventually uncovered a final prophecy:

> “When the immortal king with three souls fully embraces his darkness…  
> the world itself will kneel or burn.”

Some believed Zee would destroy humanity.

Others believed he would save the supernatural world from itself.

But nobody knew the truth.

Not even Zee.

Because deep inside the unkillable king…

The scared human boy still existed.

The one who once wanted nothing more than a family that loved him.

---

# **Final Chapter — The Throne of Shadows**

One storm-filled night, Zee sat alone in his throne room as red lightning split the sky behind him.

Thousands feared him.

Millions knew his legend.

Yet he had never felt more alone.

A servant nervously asked:
> “My king… what do you desire?”

Zee looked toward the dark city below.

Then quietly answered:

> “Peace.”
>
> “But monsters like me are never allowed to keep it.”

The thunder shook the kingdom.

His crimson eyes glowed in the darkness.

And somewhere far away…

Nature itself began preparing for war against the first immortal tribrid king.
