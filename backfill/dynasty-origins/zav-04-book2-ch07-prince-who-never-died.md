## Chapter 7 — The Prince Who Never Died

The fire burned low as midnight settled over the ruined forest. Aiden sat motionless, staring into the flames while Nyra's words echoed through his mind.

**Zee Drakhar's first son.**

It shouldn't have been possible.

Everything Aiden had learned through the Codex, the Temples, and the memories of the bloodline pointed toward Lucien Drakhar as a legendary figure from a distant age. A hero. A prince. A symbol.

Not someone who could still be alive.

Yet Rez's reaction told him otherwise.

The ancient spirit had gone silent.

Not thoughtful.

Not curious.

Afraid.

For the first time since Aiden had opened the Codex, Rez looked genuinely unsettled.

"Tell me everything," Aiden finally said.

Nyra took a slow breath before speaking.

"My family guarded the truth for generations. The Forgotten Branch wasn't erased because we were weak. We were erased because we knew something the rest of the bloodline didn't."

The fire crackled between them.

"The last records of Lucien were lies."

Aiden felt his stomach tighten.

"What do you mean?"

Nyra stared into the darkness beyond the camp.

"The stories say he disappeared after the Final Crimson War."

"They do."

"They're wrong."

The forest suddenly felt colder.

"My ancestors believed Lucien survived."

Silence settled over the camp.

"And not only survived."

Her crimson eyes met Aiden's.

"They believed he became something else."

Aiden frowned.

"What does that mean?"

Nyra looked away.

"I don't know."

And somehow that answer was worse.

---

The next morning they traveled east.

The Codex had activated before sunrise, revealing a new destination hidden far beyond the mapped territories.

A place called **The Hollow Throne.**

Neither Aiden nor Rez had ever heard of it.

Which immediately made Aiden suspicious.

Whenever the Codex revealed something Rez didn't know, bad things usually followed.

The journey took them through ancient valleys untouched by civilization.

Ruined towers rose from the earth like broken teeth.

Statues stood half-buried beneath vines and centuries of neglect.

Everywhere they went, Aiden felt the same sensation.

Like someone was watching.

Not the Observer.

Something closer.

Something physical.

Following them.

Waiting.

Three nights into the journey, Aiden finally caught it.

A figure standing atop a distant cliff.

Watching.

Motionless.

The moment Aiden noticed it, the figure vanished.

No movement.

No flash of magic.

Simply gone.

Even Rez saw it.

"Did you—"

"Yep."

"What was that?"

Rez frowned.

"I have absolutely no idea."

That answer worried Aiden more than the figure itself.

---

On the sixth day, they found the first sign.

A battlefield.

Ancient.

Forgotten.

Stretching across an entire valley.

Broken weapons littered the landscape.

Cracked armor rested among the grass.

The remains were so old they should have turned to dust centuries ago.

Yet somehow they remained.

Preserved.

Waiting.

The Codex immediately activated.

### LEGACY LOCATION DISCOVERED

**The Valley of Princes**

Reward: Legacy Experience

Warning: Bloodline Memory Ahead

Aiden stepped forward.

The moment his foot touched the battlefield, the world disappeared.

---

The vision struck him instantly.

Thousands of warriors filled the valley.

The sky burned crimson.

Magic shattered mountains.

Monsters roared across the horizon.

And at the center of it all stood two figures.

One wore black armor and a crown of crimson fire.

Zee Drakhar.

The First Crimson King.

The second figure stood beside him.

Younger.

Taller.

Silver-eyed.

Carrying a blade made from living darkness.

Lucien Drakhar.

The Prince of Crimson.

The son history had turned into a legend.

Aiden watched as father and son stood shoulder to shoulder against an impossible army.

The battlefield trembled beneath their power.

Entire armies refused to advance.

Not because they lacked orders.

Because they were terrified.

Then something happened.

Something the histories never recorded.

A third figure appeared.

A man cloaked in gold.

A man with burning golden eyes.

The same eyes Aiden had seen in the mysterious traveler months ago.

The same eyes connected to the Observer.

The vision distorted.

Cracked.

Shattered.

As if the memory itself was being hidden.

As if something didn't want him seeing what happened next.

Before the vision vanished completely, Aiden heard only one sentence.

A sentence spoken by Zee himself.

> "If I fall, protect the bloodline. If you fall... protect the future."

Then the vision ended.

Aiden stumbled backward into reality.

Breathing hard.

Heart racing.

The Codex flashed violently.

A new message appeared.

### MAIN QUEST UPDATED

**Find Lucien Drakhar**

Status: Active

Difficulty: Unknown

Failure: Extinction of House Drakhar

Aiden stared at the screen.

Then looked toward the distant mountains on the horizon.

Somewhere beyond them...

A prince lost to history was waiting.

And for the first time...

Aiden wasn't searching for answers.

He was searching for family.
