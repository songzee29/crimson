## **Part III — The King Nature Couldn’t Kill**

# **Chapter 15 — Rage of the Tribrid**

The streets of New Orleans burned.

Cars overturned.
Buildings cracked apart.
The sky churned with black clouds and blood-red lightning as Zee and Seraphine clashed like gods at war.

Every punch from Zee shattered concrete.

Every spell from Seraphine split the earth itself.

But something was changing.

Zee was getting stronger.

Too strong.

Because rage fed every side of him:
- the vampire’s hunger
- the wolf’s fury
- the witch’s raw magic

And tonight…

Zee was furious.

Seraphine launched a wave of ancient magic capable of erasing immortals from existence.

The blast swallowed entire buildings.

For one horrifying second, the battlefield went silent.

Then—

A crimson glow emerged from the smoke.

The ground trembled.

And Zee stepped forward untouched.

Veins of glowing red energy spread across his skin like cracks in molten stone.

His eyes no longer looked human.

They looked ancient.

Predatory.

Wrong.

Even Seraphine took a step back.

> “Impossible…”

Zee tilted his head slowly.

Then smiled.

Not kindly.

Not mercifully.

But like something finally unleashing itself after centuries of restraint.

> “You people keep making one mistake.”
>
> “You think pain weakens me.”

The storm exploded outward.

Every window in the French Quarter shattered instantly.

And Zee vanished.

---

# **Chapter 16 — The Monster They Created**

Seraphine barely reacted before Zee appeared directly in front of her.

FASTER than any vampire.
STRONGER than any wolf.
MAGIC heavier than any witch alive.

His fist hit her hard enough to launch her through three buildings.

The city shook.

Everyone watching froze in horror.

Because Zee wasn’t fighting carefully anymore.

He was angry.

Truly angry.

For centuries, he’d endured:
- betrayal
- loneliness
- hunters
- prophecies
- fear
- endless attempts to erase him

And now nature itself dared judge him?

No.

He was done being hunted.

Seraphine rose from the rubble, blood spilling from her lips.

> “You are an abomination!”

Zee walked toward her slowly as lightning crawled over his body.

> “Maybe.”
>
> “But your world created me.”

The air around him distorted.

Streetlights exploded.

Magic users nearby collapsed to their knees choking from the pressure of his power.

Even vampires older than kingdoms felt fear crawling through their veins.

Because Zee’s energy no longer felt supernatural.

It felt catastrophic.

---

# **Chapter 17 — The True Awakening**

Seraphine screamed ancient words in a forgotten language.

Golden chains erupted from the earth, wrapping around Zee’s arms, throat, and chest.

Nature itself trying to bind him.

For a moment…

It worked.

The city grew still.

Then Zee remembered:
Marcus.
His mother crying.
Selene dying.
Centuries alone on a throne no one truly shared.

And something inside him finally snapped completely.

A pulse of crimson energy detonated from his body.

The chains shattered instantly.

The shockwave flattened entire city blocks.

Clouds spun violently overhead as the sky turned pitch black.

Then the impossible happened.

Zee began floating above the battlefield.

Not through spellcasting.

Through raw power alone.

His eyes glowed like burning stars.

His veins became rivers of crimson light.

The wolf inside him howled.
The vampire hungered.
The witch magic merged with both.

No balance.
No restraint.

Perfect union.

The first TRUE Tribrid had finally awakened.

And the world felt it.

Across continents:
- witches lost control of spells
- wolves transformed unwillingly
- vampires collapsed in terror

Every supernatural creature alive instinctively understood one thing:

A king had risen.

---

# **Chapter 18 — Fall of Nature’s Servant**

Seraphine looked terrified now.

Not because she might lose.

Because she realized nature had already lost.

> “What are you…?”

Zee descended slowly through the rain.

His voice echoed unnaturally.

Almost inhuman.

> “I am what comes after your balance fails.”

Seraphine attacked desperately:
fire
lightning
ancient curses
death spells

None of it mattered.

Zee walked through every attack untouched.

Then finally…

He grabbed her throat.

The entire storm froze instantly.

Silence swallowed the city.

Seraphine gasped as Zee lifted her effortlessly.

> “For my entire life…”
>
> “Everything wanted me dead.”

His grip tightened.

> “But I survived.”

Cracks of red energy spread through Seraphine’s glowing skin.

> “Not because I’m lucky.”
>
> “Not because of prophecy.”

His crimson eyes burned brighter than the storm.

> “Because kings do not kneel to fear.”

And with one final burst of power—

He absorbed her magic.

All of it.

Nature’s own energy poured into the Tribrid King.

Seraphine screamed before dissolving into ash.

The first witch was gone.

And Zee became something even nature could no longer define.

---

# **Chapter 19 — The One True King**

The storm ended instantly.

Moonlight finally touched New Orleans again.

The surviving supernatural creatures slowly looked upward.

And there he stood above the city:
Black crown.
Dark coat flowing in the wind.
Red eyes glowing against the night sky.

Untouchable.

Unkillable.

Victorious.

One by one…
wolves bowed.
Vampires knelt.
Witches lowered their heads.

Not because they were forced to.

Because they knew resistance was meaningless now.

Zee had defeated nature itself.

Marceline approached carefully.

> “My king… what happens now?”

Zee stared over the city silently.

The old Zee might’ve answered with vengeance.

But after everything…
all the suffering…
all the loneliness…

He finally understood something.

Power alone would never heal him.

Yet weakness would only destroy him again.

So he chose something new.

Rule.

Not as a monster.

Not as a savior.

But as something greater.

A king who would ensure nobody like him ever suffered alone again.

He turned toward his kingdom.

And for the first time in centuries…

The rage inside him quieted.

Just slightly.

> “Now?”
>
> “Now the world learns my name.”

The red lightning flashed one final time behind the throne of the immortal Tribrid King.

And across history…

No one would ever forget it.
