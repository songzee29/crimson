# **ZEE: THE FIRST TRUE TRIBRID KING**  
## **Part IV — The Ones He Refused to Lose**

---

Centuries passed after the Fall of Seraphine.

The supernatural world no longer questioned Zee’s rule.

They feared it.

Respected it.

Worshipped it.

Stories about the Tribrid King spread across every continent:
- the immortal crowned in shadows
- the king who defeated nature
- the monster death itself could not claim

But legends never told the full truth.

Because beneath the throne…
beneath the storms and crimson eyes…

Zee was tired.

Immortality had become heavy.

Every century felt colder than the last.

Until *him.*

---

# **Chapter 20 — The Man Who Didn’t Fear the King**

His name was Elias Laurent.

Human.

A bookstore owner in New Orleans.

Soft-spoken.
Sharp-tongued.
Too brave for his own good.

The first time they met, Elias accidentally spilled coffee on Zee’s black coat.

The entire café froze in terror.

Everyone in New Orleans knew who Zee was.

Even humans whispered stories about the man with red eyes.

But Elias just looked annoyed.

> “You’re staring like you’ve never seen coffee before.”

Zee blinked.

Nobody talked to him like that.

Not servants.
Not enemies.
Not kings.

For the first time in decades…

The Tribrid King laughed.

A real laugh.

And that terrified his guards more than any war ever had.

---

# **Chapter 21 — Learning How to Live Again**

Elias treated Zee differently.

Not like a god.
Not like a weapon.
Not like a prophecy.

Just… Zee.

He dragged him through ordinary human things:
- late-night food runs
- old movies
- crowded festivals
- dancing in the kitchen at 2 AM

At first Zee hated it.

Then slowly…

He began loving it.

Because Elias reminded him of something immortality almost erased:

Humanity.

For once, Zee wasn’t the king.
Wasn’t the monster.
Wasn’t the Tribrid feared by nature itself.

He was just a man in love.

And eventually…

For the first time in his endless existence…

Zee let himself have a family.

---

# **Chapter 22 — The Boy with Crimson Eyes**

His son was born during a thunderstorm.

The entire kingdom felt it instantly.

Magic trembled.
Wolves howled.
Vampires became restless.

Because the child carried Zee’s blood.

His name was Lucien.

Human… mostly.

At least for now.

He had Elias’s smile.

But Zee’s eyes.

Tiny hints of crimson appeared whenever he cried.

The moment Zee held him, something inside the immortal king shattered completely.

Not from pain.

From love.

Pure overwhelming love.

And suddenly…

Zee understood fear again.

Because now he had something the world could take from him.

---

# **Chapter 23 — The Weakness of Kings**

Enemies watched carefully.

For centuries Zee had been untouchable because he cared about almost nothing.

But now?

Now he had:
- a human boyfriend
- a human son
- something fragile

And fragile things could be broken.

The attacks started slowly.

Assassins near Elias’s bookstore.
Poisoned gifts left at the palace.
Hunters stalking Lucien from the shadows.

Zee slaughtered anyone responsible.

Entire covens disappeared overnight.

Vampire clans were erased from existence.

But the attacks kept coming.

Because fear had spread across the supernatural world once again.

They believed Lucien might become even stronger than Zee someday.

And they refused to let that happen.

---

# **Chapter 24 — The Night Everything Burned**

It happened on Lucien’s eighth birthday.

The palace was filled with music and candlelight.

Lucien laughed while Elias danced with Zee in the grand hall.

For one perfect moment…

The Tribrid King was happy.

Then the screaming started.

Explosions ripped through the kingdom walls.

Ancient anti-magic weapons tore through Zee’s guards.

A coalition had formed:
witches
hunters
rebellious vampires
rogue wolves

All united by one goal:

Kill the king’s family.

Zee moved instantly.

Blood.
Claws.
Magic.

Bodies hit the floor before most attackers even saw him move.

But then—

A hunter’s blade pierced Elias’s chest.

Everything stopped.

Zee caught him before he hit the ground.

Blood spread across Elias’s white shirt.

Lucien screamed.

And the entire palace began shaking violently.

Because Zee…

Had lost control.

---

# **Chapter 25 — Wrath of the Immortal Father**

The sky over New Orleans turned black instantly.

Not clouds.

Darkness itself.

Every supernatural creature in the city collapsed as Zee’s power exploded outward.

The walls cracked.
The earth split.
Rivers surged backwards.

Zee held Elias trembling in his arms.

And for the first time in centuries…

The King cried.

Real tears mixed with blood and rain.

Elias weakly touched his face.

> “Don’t let me die…”

That sentence destroyed whatever restraint Zee had left.

The hunters realized too late what they’d done.

Because this wasn’t the rage of a king anymore.

This was the rage of a man about to lose his family.

And nothing in existence was more dangerous.

---

# **Chapter 26 — The Birth of a New Bloodline**

Zee knew there was only one way to save them.

One forbidden way.

He looked at Lucien.
Then Elias.

And for the first time since becoming immortal…

The Tribrid King was afraid.

Because turning them meant destroying their human lives forever.

But losing them?

Impossible.

So Zee made his choice.

He bit Elias first.

Ancient tribrid venom mixed with immortal blood flowed into his body.

Then Lucien.

The palace trembled as Zee fed them both his blood.

The transformation began immediately.

Bones cracked.
Magic surged.
Eyes glowed crimson.

The entire kingdom felt history changing again.

Not one tribrid.

Three.

A new bloodline born directly from the First King himself.

And nature…

Nature screamed.

---

# **Chapter 27 — The Family of Monsters**

Three days later, Elias opened his eyes.

Crimson.

Powerful.

Immortal.

Lucien awakened beside him, small veins glowing red beneath his skin as supernatural energy pulsed around him.

Zee stood nearby silently.

Terrified they’d hate him.

Because he stole their humanity to save them.

Elias slowly approached him.

Then touched Zee’s face gently.

> “You chose us.”

Zee’s voice cracked slightly.

> “I couldn’t lose you.”

Lucien wrapped his arms around both of them.

And in that moment…

The most feared being in existence finally understood something.

Power wasn’t his greatest strength.

Love was.

Because love had turned a broken abused boy…

Into a king willing to fight the entire world for his family.

---

# **Final Scene — The Crimson Throne**

Years later, the world spoke of them in whispers:

- Zee, the immortal Tribrid King
- Elias, the king consort born from love
- Lucien, the child destined to surpass them all

Together they ruled New Orleans from the shadows.

Untouchable.

Unbreakable.

A family forged through pain, rage, immortality…

And devotion.

And every enemy who threatened them learned the same terrifying truth:

The world should have feared Zee alone.

But now?

The king was no longer alone. 😮‍💨
