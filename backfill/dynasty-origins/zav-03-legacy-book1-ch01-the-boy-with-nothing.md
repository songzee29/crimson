# 🩸 THE CRIMSON LEGACY 🩸
## Book I: *The System of the First King*
### Chapter 1 — The Boy With Nothing

---

Long after the age of Zee Mikaelson...

Long after the Crimson Dynasty became legend...

The world forgot what the First King truly looked like.

Statues remained.

Stories remained.

Songs remained.

But the truth faded.

Because legends always grow larger than reality.

---

Yet one thing never changed.

The name.

---

**Zee Mikaelson.**

The First King.

The Eternal King.

The King Who Taught the Universe to Survive.

---

And every child in the world grew up hearing that name.

Especially one boy.

---

His name was **Aiden Vale.**

Age: 15.

Status: Nobody.

Power: None.

Family: Gone.

---

The war had taken everything.

Not a world-ending war.

Not a cosmic war.

Just one cruel enough to leave children orphaned.

His parents died protecting civilians during a border conflict between two supernatural territories.

Heroes.

Forgotten heroes.

The kind history never remembers.

---

Aiden lived alone now.

In a tiny apartment near the lower districts beneath the floating remains of old Crimson Territory.

Every morning looked the same.

Wake up.

Work.

Survive.

Sleep.

Repeat.

---

No magic.

No wolf gene.

No vampire blood awakening.

Nothing.

---

In a world where supernatural gifts were common...

Aiden had none.

---

The only thing his parents left behind was an ancient crimson book.

Old.

Dusty.

Heavy.

Impossible to open.

---

For years he tried.

Nothing worked.

Not keys.

Not spells.

Not brute force.

---

So it sat beside his bed.

A useless reminder of people he missed.

---

Until the day everything changed.

---

# Chapter 2 — Blood

Aiden was angry.

Again.

---

Three older boys cornered him after work.

Mocking him.

Laughing at him.

Calling him powerless.

Worthless.

Weak.

---

And the worst part?

They weren't wrong.

---

Aiden fought back.

Lost.

Badly.

---

By the time he got home his lip was bleeding and his knuckles were swollen.

---

He slammed the apartment door.

Threw his backpack across the room.

Then kicked the old crimson book.

---

It crashed against the wall.

---

A sharp metal corner sliced his palm.

---

"Ow—"

---

A single drop of blood landed on the cover.

---

Silence.

---

Nothing happened.

---

Then...

---

The room shook.

---

Aiden froze.

---

The crimson crest on the cover ignited.

Ancient symbols spread across the book like veins of living fire.

---

The apartment lights exploded.

Windows rattled violently.

---

The book opened itself.

---

Aiden stumbled backward.

---

Pages flipped rapidly.

Thousands of pages turning on their own.

Faster.

Faster.

Faster.

---

Until—

---

Everything stopped.

---

A crimson screen appeared directly in front of his eyes.

---

### CRIMSON LEGACY SYSTEM

### BLOODLINE VERIFIED

### DESCENDANT CONFIRMED

### WELCOME HOME

---

Aiden stared.

---

"...What?"

---

Then another line appeared.

---

### INITIALIZING SYSTEM GUIDE...

---

### ...

---

### ...

---

### GUIDE FOUND

---

### REZ

---

A voice immediately exploded inside his head.

---

> "OH FOR THE LOVE OF—"

---

Aiden screamed.

---

"WHO SAID THAT?!"

---

Silence.

---

Then a man appeared floating upside down in front of him.

Only visible to him.

---

Dark clothes.

Annoyed expression.

Crossed arms.

---

The man stared at Aiden.

Aiden stared back.

---

Long silence.

---

Then—

---

> "Seriously?"

---

Aiden blinked.

---

> "You're the descendant?"
>
> "This is who the book waited for?"
>
> "We're doomed."

---

Aiden pointed.

---

"WHO ARE YOU?!"

---

The man sighed dramatically.

---

> "Rez."
>
> "Royal Guardian."
>
> "Former Hero."
>
> "Professional Problem."
>
> "Current Prisoner."

---

Aiden blinked again.

---

"...Prisoner?"

---

> "Long story."

---

A new screen appeared.

---

### USER STATUS

Name: Aiden Vale

Age: 15

Bloodline Status: Mikaelson Descendant

Strength: Poor

Magic: None

Combat Skill: Terrible

Confidence: Critically Low

---

Rez stared at the screen.

---

Then at Aiden.

---

Then back at the screen.

---

> "Wow."

---

Aiden frowned.

---

"What?"

---

Rez pointed.

---

> "I've seen houseplants with better stats."

---

# Chapter 3 — The First Quest

Aiden nearly threw the book out the window.

---

But before he could...

A new notification appeared.

---

### MAIN QUEST RECEIVED

### SURVIVE

Objective:
Remain alive for 30 days.

Reward:
Minor Vampire Regeneration

Failure:
Death

---

Aiden froze.

---

"...Death?"

---

Rez nodded.

---

> "Yep."

---

"...That's insane."

---

> "Welcome to the Crimson Legacy."

---

Aiden stared at the glowing screen.

---

Then asked the question that would change everything.

---

"...What exactly is this book?"

---

For the first time...

Rez didn't joke.

---

The immortal's expression became serious.

---

He looked toward the ancient pages.

Toward the names hidden inside.

Toward centuries of history.

---

And quietly said:

---

> "Kid..."
>
> "You're holding the greatest secret the Mikaelsons ever left behind."

---

Outside...

Thunder rolled across the sky.

---

And somewhere far away...

Something ancient felt the Codex awaken.

---

Something that had been waiting thousands of years.

---

Waiting for a descendant.

---

Waiting for the Legacy.
