# 🩸 THE CRIMSON DYNASTY — CONTINUED 🩸  
## *Part X — The Crown Grows Heavy*

---

# Chapter 28 — The Storm Season

Months passed.

And New Orleans changed with Zee.

The skies above the city no longer behaved naturally.

Weather followed his emotional state now:
- anger brought crimson lightning
- anxiety caused endless rain
- grief darkened daylight itself

People across the kingdom began calling it:
> *The Storm Season.*

Children feared it.

The elders understood it.

The Crimson Sovereign was unraveling slowly.

---

# Chapter 29 — The Weight of Immortality

Immortality had once sounded beautiful to Zee.

Now it felt exhausting.

Because immortality didn’t just preserve memories.

It preserved pain.

Every death.
Every scream.
Every mistake.

Nothing faded naturally anymore.

One night Zee stood alone inside the royal archives staring at old portraits:
- Serina smiling beside Lucien
- young Lyra asleep on his shoulder
- Rez grinning after a battle
- Elias before the gray streaks in his hair appeared

Time touched everyone except Zee.

And that hurt more than war ever did.

---

# Chapter 30 — Elias Grows Older

Elias remained human longer than anyone expected.

Magic slowed his aging.
The dynasty protected him endlessly.

But time still moved.

Slowly.

Cruelly.

One evening Elias struggled climbing the palace stairs after a council meeting.

Tiny thing.

Barely noticeable.

Except Zee noticed immediately.

And froze.

Because for the first time in centuries…

The Crimson Sovereign looked helpless.

---

# Chapter 31 — Fear

That night Zee barely spoke.

Elias eventually found him standing outside beneath violent storms overlooking the city.

Lightning flashed across Zee’s face.

His expression looked terrified.

Not angry.

Terrified.

Elias stepped beside him quietly.

> “Talk to me.”

Zee’s jaw tightened.

Then finally—

> “I can fight gods.”
>
> “I can survive universes collapsing.”
>
> “But I can’t stop time.”

The storm intensified violently around the palace.

Elias gently grabbed his hand.

> “Zee—”

> “Don’t.”

Zee’s voice cracked slightly.

> “Don’t tell me to accept it.”
>
> “I’m so tired of losing people.”

And for the first time in forever…

The King cried.

Not loudly.

Not dramatically.

Just silent tears disappearing into crimson rain.

---

# Chapter 32 — Lucien Watches

Lucien witnessed more than Zee realized.

He saw:
- the insomnia
- the fear
- the restraint
- the emotional exhaustion

And slowly…

Lucien began understanding the terrible truth of leadership.

A king’s greatest battle wasn’t enemies.

It was endurance.

Still—

Lucien also noticed something dangerous happening.

Zee had started isolating himself again.

Which meant the darkness inside him was winning ground.

---

# Chapter 33 — The Ancient Hunger Awakens

Then the feeding incidents started.

At first:
small blackouts
missing memories
destroyed training rooms

Then worse.

Several prisoners beneath the palace were found drained completely of blood and magic.

Not killed.

Consumed.

Nyra examined the bodies silently.

Then looked toward Lucien with visible concern.

> “This wasn’t rage.”

Lucien frowned.

> “Then what was it?”

Nyra hesitated.

> “Instinct.”

---

# Chapter 34 — The Beast Beneath the Crown

That answer terrified them.

Because Zee’s tribrid nature had evolved beyond balance.

The wolf wanted dominance.
The vampire wanted hunger.
The cosmic power wanted transcendence.

And all three instincts were beginning to merge together.

Creating something entirely new.

Something ancient.

Something predatory.

---

# Chapter 35 — The First Loss of Control

It happened during a royal gathering.

Music echoed through the throne hall.
Family laughed.
The kingdom celebrated peace.

Then suddenly—

Zee froze mid-conversation.

Every candle extinguished instantly.

The room temperature dropped violently.

And everyone felt it.

Hunger.

Pure overwhelming hunger.

Zee’s breathing became uneven.
His pupils vanished completely into glowing crimson-black light.

He looked toward a wounded guard nearby.

And for one horrifying second…

The Sovereign stopped seeing a person.

He saw prey.

---

# Chapter 36 — Lucien Intervenes

Before anyone could react—

Lucien stepped directly between them.

Fearlessly.

Their eyes locked.

Father and son.

Monster and king.

The entire palace trembled around them.

Lucien spoke calmly despite the terror gripping the room.

> “Fight it.”

Zee’s claws slowly extended.

Storms exploded outside.

> “Move.”

Lucien stood his ground.

> “No.”

Reality cracked across the throne room floor.

Several royals panicked.

Cassius immediately shielded Lyra instinctively.

Rez reached for his weapons.

But Lucien never moved.

> “You taught me monsters still have choices.”
>
> “So choose.”

Silence.

Long painful silence.

Then finally—

Zee screamed.

Not at Lucien.

At himself.

The force shattered every window in the palace.

And then he vanished into the storm.

---

# Chapter 37 — The Hunt for the King

Three days passed.

No Zee.

The storms worsened globally.

Earthquakes shook supernatural territories.
Magic became unstable worldwide.
Creatures fled forests in terror.

Because the Crimson Sovereign wasn’t merely missing.

He was hunting.

And nobody knew whether he still recognized friend from enemy anymore.

---

# Chapter 38 — Elias Finds Him

Everyone searched.

But Elias found him first.

Deep within the Louisiana swamps.

Kneeling beside an entire destroyed supernatural militia camp.

Bodies everywhere.

The air smelled like blood and burnt magic.

Zee sat motionless in the center of the destruction.

Hands trembling.

Eyes hollow.

When Elias approached…

Zee immediately backed away.

Like a wounded animal.

> “Don’t come near me.”

Elias ignored him.

Again.

Because that’s what love had always looked like between them.

Staying.

Even when it was terrifying.

---

# Chapter 39 — The Truth

Zee finally admitted what he feared most.

> “I liked it.”

Elias froze.

Zee looked sick saying it.

> “The power.”
>
> “The hunting.”
>
> “The fear.”
>
> “Part of me enjoyed it.”

His voice cracked.

> “What if this is who I really am underneath everything?”

That question broke Elias more than the destruction around them.

Because after centuries…

Zee still secretly believed he was fundamentally broken.

---

# Chapter 40 — “No. You’re Just Tired.”

Elias slowly sat beside him in the mud and rain.

No fear.

No judgment.

Just honesty.

> “Zee.”
>
> “Monsters don’t hate hurting people.”
>
> “You do.”

The storm weakened slightly.

Elias continued softly:

> “You’ve been carrying everyone for centuries.”
>
> “Kings break too.”

Zee stared downward silently.

Then finally whispered:

> “I don’t know how to stop carrying it.”

Elias leaned against him gently.

> “Then let us carry some of it too.”

And somewhere deep inside the Crimson Sovereign…

The boy who survived abuse, war, immortality, and godhood…

Finally stopped trying to suffer alone. 🩸
