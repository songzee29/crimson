# 🩸 THE CRIMSON LEGACY 🩸
## Book I: *The System of the First King*
### Chapter 77 — The Ascent to the Second Temple

---

The corridor behind Aiden disappeared.

---

No flash of light.

No sudden transition.

---

One moment he stood inside the First Temple.

The next...

He stood alone beneath an endless twilight sky.

---

A cold breeze drifted across the mountains.

---

For the first time in what felt like weeks...

Everything was quiet.

---

No quests.

No warnings.

No enemies.

---

Just silence.

---

Aiden stood there for a long moment.

---

Listening.

---

The wind moved through distant pine trees.

Snow drifted lazily from the cliffs above.

---

Peace.

---

A rare thing.

---

And somehow...

It felt stranger than all the monsters.

---

Rez appeared beside him.

---

Neither spoke.

---

For once...

Even Rez seemed content to enjoy the quiet.

---

Aiden sat down on a nearby rock.

---

The First Temple stood behind him.

---

Ancient.

Proud.

Unmoving.

---

The place had tested him.

---

Broken him.

---

Changed him.

---

And now it felt almost sad to leave.

---

> "I passed."

---

Rez glanced over.

---

> "You did."

---

Aiden stared at the horizon.

---

> "Does it ever get easier?"

---

Rez laughed softly.

---

Not his usual sarcastic laugh.

---

A tired one.

---

> "No."

---

Aiden groaned.

---

> "That's not encouraging."

---

> "You wanted honesty."

---

> "Fair."

---

Silence returned.

---

Then Aiden asked the question that had been bothering him for days.

---

> "Why are you helping me?"

---

Rez froze.

---

The smile vanished.

---

Aiden noticed immediately.

---

Because for the first time...

Rez didn't have a joke ready.

---

The immortal looked away.

---

Toward the mountains.

---

Toward memories.

---

Old memories.

---

> "Because somebody helped me once."

---

Aiden waited.

---

Rez rarely talked about himself.

---

Almost never.

---

> "Who?"

---

A long pause followed.

---

Then—

---

> "Lucien."

---

Aiden blinked.

---

The name wasn't unfamiliar.

---

Lucien Mikaelson.

---

The son of Zee.

---

One of the greatest legends of the Dynasty.

---

Rez smiled faintly.

---

> "When everyone expected him to become his father..."
>
> "He spent years trying to become himself."

---

The words lingered.

---

Because they sounded familiar.

---

Very familiar.

---

Aiden thought back to Zee's memory.

---

*"You are not a replacement."*

---

The same lesson.

---

The same burden.

---

Different generations.

---

Same struggle.

---

# Chapter 78 — A Quiet Lesson

---

The next morning arrived slowly.

---

Clouds drifted across the mountains.

---

The sky glowed silver.

---

Aiden expected another quest.

---

Another challenge.

---

Another fight.

---

Instead—

---

Nothing happened.

---

The Codex remained silent.

---

Hours passed.

---

Still nothing.

---

> "Did it break?"

---

Rez looked offended.

---

> "The Codex survived ten thousand years."

---

> "So that's a no?"

---

> "That's a very dramatic no."

---

Aiden laughed.

---

Then frowned.

---

> "So what am I supposed to do?"

---

Rez pointed toward a nearby cliff.

---

> "Sit."

---

Aiden blinked.

---

> "That's it?"

---

> "That's it."

---

> "You're kidding."

---

> "Nope."

---

Aiden stared.

---

Rez stared back.

---

Neither moved.

---

Finally—

---

Aiden walked over and sat.

---

Minutes passed.

---

Then an hour.

---

Then another.

---

Nothing happened.

---

No system messages.

---

No rewards.

---

No hidden bosses.

---

Just wind.

---

Just sky.

---

Just stillness.

---

Eventually—

---

Aiden realized something.

---

For the first time since opening the Codex...

He wasn't chasing power.

---

He wasn't worrying about levels.

---

He wasn't afraid.

---

He was simply existing.

---

And somehow...

That felt important.

---

Very important.

---

# Chapter 79 — The Message Hidden in Silence

---

That evening...

The Codex finally reacted.

---

Not with a quest.

---

Not with a reward.

---

A single line appeared.

---

### LESSON COMPLETE

---

Aiden frowned.

---

> "Lesson?"

---

Another line appeared.

---

### POWER WITHOUT PEACE BECOMES OBSESSION

---

Silence.

---

The words lingered.

---

Simple.

---

Yet heavy.

---

Rez nodded slowly.

---

> "A lot of people never learn that."

---

Aiden thought about Zee.

---

Lucien.

---

The Ashborn King.

---

All the legends he'd seen.

---

All the power they carried.

---

And all the pain.

---

Maybe this lesson wasn't about magic at all.

---

Maybe it was about surviving the weight of it.

---

As darkness settled over the mountains...

Aiden looked toward the distant horizon.

---

Far away.

---

Beyond the clouds.

---

Beyond the snow.

---

A faint golden light flickered.

---

Small.

---

Almost invisible.

---

The Second Temple.

---

Waiting.

---

Not calling him.

---

Not demanding anything.

---

Simply waiting for him to arrive when he was ready.

---

And for the first time since this journey began...

Aiden wasn't in a hurry.

---

He sat beneath the stars.

---

Listening to the wind.

---

While somewhere deep inside the Codex...

Something ancient smiled.

---

Not because Aiden had grown stronger.

---

But because he had finally learned how to slow down.
