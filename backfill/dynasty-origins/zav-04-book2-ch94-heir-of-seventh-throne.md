# BOOK II: THE FORGOTTEN BLOOD

## Chapter 94 — The Heir of the Seventh Throne

The Seventh Mark burned.

Aiden fell to one knee.

Pain exploded through his body.

Not physical pain.

Something deeper.

Older.

The pain of memory.

The pain of inheritance.

The pain of carrying something that was never meant to be forgotten.

The Golden City shook violently.

The empty Seventh Throne continued awakening beneath the city.

Ancient runes erupted across reality.

Entire dimensions began glowing.

The Celestial Dragon lowered its massive head.

Its countless golden eyes remained fixed upon Aiden.

Watching.

Judging.

Waiting.

Then the dragon spoke again.

Its voice echoed across creation.

> "Rise."

The command wasn't loud.

Yet existence itself obeyed.

Aiden felt power surge through him.

The Seventh Mark ignited.

Golden flames erupted around his body.

The Codex appeared.

Pages turning faster than ever before.

Then a notification emerged.

### ANCIENT AUTHORITY RECOGNIZED

### HEIR VERIFIED

### ACCESS TO THE SEVENTH THRONE GRANTED

The entire forum froze.

The First Guardian immediately stood.

The Observer looked horrified.

Even Valthoros stopped smiling.

Aiden slowly rose.

His eyes glowing crimson and gold.

And for a brief moment...

The silhouette behind him looked exactly like Zee.

## Chapter 95 — Why the Dragon Knows Zee

The Celestial Dragon stared at Aiden.

Then laughed.

The sound shook galaxies.

Not from power.

From genuine amusement.

The dragon's enormous eyes softened.

Almost fondly.

Then it said something nobody expected.

> "You look like him."

Aiden frowned.

"Zee?"

The dragon snorted.

Entire stars scattered from the force.

> "He always hated when I called him that."

Silence.

Complete silence.

Aiden blinked.

Lucien blinked.

Seraphina blinked.

Even Kael looked confused.

The dragon tilted its head.

> "To me, he was just Zee."

The impossible creature smiled.

> "A stubborn child who refused to stay dead."

---

A memory appeared above the city.

A young Zee stood before the Celestial Dragon.

Much younger than any version Aiden had seen.

Barely more than a teenager.

Covered in wounds.

Bleeding.

Defiant.

The dragon looked much the same.

Ancient.

Powerful.

Impossible.

And somehow...

The young Zee was arguing with it.

Actually arguing.

Aiden watched in disbelief.

"Father really did that?"

Lucien groaned.

"That sounds exactly like him."

The memory continued.

The dragon offered Zee power.

Zee refused.

The dragon offered protection.

Zee refused.

The dragon offered immortality.

Zee refused.

Finally the dragon became annoyed.

And Zee smiled.

The exact smile House Drakhar had inherited.

The smile that usually preceded disaster.

The dragon sighed.

> "That was the day I realized he would either save existence..."
>
> "Or give it a headache."

## Chapter 96 — The Ancient Enemy Behind All Things

The laughter faded.

The memories vanished.

And the dragon's expression darkened.

Immediately.

The atmosphere changed.

Every Ancient One grew silent.

The Seven Thrones vibrated.

Even the living stars dimmed.

The dragon slowly turned toward the darkness beyond the First Door.

Toward the place even Ancient Ones feared.

Then it spoke.

> "The time for stories is ending."

The Golden City trembled.

Aiden felt dread creeping into his chest.

Because the dragon sounded worried.

And beings like this didn't worry easily.

The dragon's eyes narrowed.

> "You believe Valthoros is the enemy."

The Devourer folded his arms.

"To be fair..."

A pause.

"I've earned that reputation."

Nobody argued.

---

The dragon continued.

> "Valthoros destroys worlds."

Its gaze shifted toward the darkness.

> "But something else destroys possibilities."

Silence.

The First Guardian lowered his head.

The Observer closed his eyes.

They already knew.

The dragon slowly raised one claw.

A vision appeared.

A universe.

Then another.

Then another.

Millions of timelines.

Millions of futures.

Millions of possibilities.

All thriving.

All alive.

Then darkness touched them.

Everything vanished.

Not destroyed.

Removed.

As though those futures had never existed.

The vision ended.

The dragon whispered a single name.

A name that caused the Seven Thrones themselves to shake.

> "Nihryx."

The name echoed across existence.

And reality shuddered.

## Chapter 97 — Kael's Greatest Test

Kael stood silently beside Zee.

The little boy watched everything.

The dragon.

The Thrones.

The Ancient Ones.

The coming darkness.

And for the first time...

He looked older.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

The weight of everything was beginning to settle upon him.

Then the dragon turned toward him.

The massive creature lowered its head.

Its countless eyes focused on the child.

For several moments neither spoke.

Then the dragon asked:

> "Do you know why you were born?"

Kael slowly shook his head.

The dragon smiled gently.

> "Good."

The answer surprised everyone.

Kael looked confused.

The dragon continued.

> "Because your purpose is not your prison."

Silence.

The child listened carefully.

The dragon's voice softened.

> "You were created to stop Nihryx."

Kael's eyes widened.

The dragon immediately raised a claw.

Stopping his panic before it could begin.

> "Created for a purpose."
>
> "Not defined by it."

The words settled over the city.

A lesson.

Not just for Kael.

For everyone.

The dragon looked toward Aiden.

Toward Lucien.

Toward Seraphina.

Toward Zee.

Then back toward Kael.

> "The moment purpose becomes identity..."
>
> "Freedom dies."

The child slowly nodded.

And for the first time...

Kael smiled without fear.

## Chapter 98 — The Throne That Chose Aiden

The Seventh Throne finally emerged.

The entire city shook.

Golden stone rose from beneath the forum.

Ancient runes ignited.

Reality bent around it.

The throne dwarfed mountains.

Dwarfed worlds.

Dwarfed imagination.

Yet somehow...

It felt familiar.

As though it had always been waiting.

Always been watching.

Always been expecting him.

The Codex appeared one final time.

### SEVENTH THRONE ACTIVATED

### TRUE HEIR DETECTED

### ASCENSION AVAILABLE

Aiden stared.

The word made his stomach drop.

Ascension.

That sounded permanent.

Dangerous.

Suspicious.

The exact kind of thing he normally regretted.

---

The throne called to him.

Not with power.

Not with promises.

With belonging.

The same feeling he experienced in the Hall of Kings.

The same feeling he felt with House Drakhar.

Connection.

Legacy.

Family.

The Crimson Legacy wasn't about ruling.

It wasn't about conquering.

It wasn't about becoming stronger.

It was about carrying others forward.

The throne understood that.

Which was why it chose him.

Not because he was perfect.

Not because he was powerful.

Because he always looked for another way.

---

Aiden slowly stepped forward.

The throne glowed.

The Seven Thrones awakened completely.

The Ancient Ones stood.

The Celestial Dragon bowed its head.

Even Zee smiled.

Proud.

The way a father might smile.

The way a king might smile.

The way a legend might smile when the next chapter finally begins.

Then the Codex revealed a final message.

### BOOK II MAIN QUEST UPDATED

### THE COSMIC AGE

Objective:
Unite The Seven Thrones

Objective:
Stop Nihryx

Objective:
Protect Every Future

Difficulty:
Impossible

Reward:
The Next Age

Failure:
All Possibilities End

The message faded.

The stars dimmed.

The darkness beyond the Door moved.

And somewhere beyond reality...

Something ancient opened its eyes.

Something that had been sleeping since before creation.

Something that had finally noticed Aiden Drakhar.
