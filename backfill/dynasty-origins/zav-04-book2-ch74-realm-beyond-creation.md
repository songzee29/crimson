## Chapter 74 — The Realm Beyond Creation

The celebration lasted only a few minutes.

Not because anyone wanted it to end.

Because reality reminded them it hadn't forgotten.

The First Door remained open.

The darkness beyond it remained awake.

And whatever existed on the other side was still watching.

The Hall of Kings had stabilized, but nobody mistook stability for safety.

Aiden stood near the edge of the city, staring toward the impossible horizon beyond the Door.

The darkness wasn't moving.

Yet somehow it felt alive.

Observing.

Learning.

Waiting.

The Observer appeared beside him.

Silent as always.

For several moments neither spoke.

Then Aiden finally asked the question that had been bothering him since the beginning.

"What exactly is beyond that Door?"

The Observer looked toward the darkness.

His expression remained unreadable.

Yet his voice carried something unusual.

Respect.

Fear.

Wonder.

All at once.

"When creation began..."

The ancient being said quietly.

"It wasn't empty."

Aiden frowned.

"What does that mean?"

The Observer's eyes narrowed.

"It means something was already there."

The answer sent chills down Aiden's spine.

Because he understood immediately.

Creation wasn't first.

Something came before.

Something older.

Something the universe itself had been built around.

Or perhaps...

Built to contain.

---

Far beyond the Door...

The giant eye slowly closed.

But before it disappeared...

Aiden thought he saw something.

Not a monster.

Not a god.

A person.

A silhouette standing within the darkness.

Watching.

Waiting.

Smiling.

Then it was gone.

---

## Chapter 75 — The Heir's Evolution

The Codex appeared without warning.

Golden pages floated around Aiden.

The system felt different now.

More alive.

More connected.

As though restoring House Drakhar had unlocked something hidden deep within its foundation.

The pages turned.

One after another.

Then stopped.

A single notification appeared.

### UNIQUE EVOLUTION AVAILABLE

Current Title:
The Crimson Heir

Evolution Path:
The Crimson Legacy

Status:
Ready

Aiden stared.

The title alone felt important.

Different.

Ancient.

Then new text appeared.

### WARNING

This evolution cannot be reversed.

Aiden immediately laughed.

"That's usually how all of these work."

The Codex ignored him.

### The Crimson Legacy is not power.

### The Crimson Legacy is responsibility.

### Accept?

YES

Aiden pressed it.

Immediately.

---

The Hall vanished.

The city vanished.

The Door vanished.

Everything disappeared.

Aiden found himself standing in complete darkness.

Then voices appeared.

Thousands of voices.

Every Drakhar.

Every ancestor.

Every life connected to the bloodline.

He felt their hopes.

Their fears.

Their victories.

Their failures.

Their dreams.

And suddenly he understood.

The true inheritance of House Drakhar wasn't strength.

It wasn't immortality.

It wasn't magic.

It was memory.

The ability to carry others forward.

The ability to preserve what mattered.

The ability to leave the world better than they found it.

Golden light erupted around him.

The transformation began.

And somewhere deep within the Codex...

A title unseen for ages awakened.

### EVOLUTION COMPLETE

### NEW TITLE ACQUIRED

THE CRIMSON LEGACY

## Chapter 76 — The Hidden Truth About Rez

Aiden's evolution shook the Hall.

Not violently.

But deeply.

The entire city reacted.

Ancient structures glowed.

Forgotten memories awakened.

And then something unexpected happened.

The Hall reacted to Rez.

Everyone turned.

Including Rez.

The Hall of Kings had begun glowing around him.

Ancestors stared.

Whispering.

Confused.

Concerned.

The Observer narrowed his eyes.

Lucien looked intrigued.

Seraphina looked suspicious.

Rez looked uncomfortable.

"Why is everyone staring at me?"

Nobody answered.

Because something impossible had appeared.

A name.

Floating above him.

Ancient.

Hidden.

Sealed.

The Codex immediately activated.

### BLOODLINE DETECTED

Rez froze.

Aiden froze.

Everyone froze.

The screen continued.

### UNKNOWN LINEAGE IDENTIFIED

### ACCESS RESTRICTED

### ACCESS RESTRICTED

### ACCESS RESTRICTED

The Observer actually looked shocked.

Which might have been the most alarming thing Aiden had seen all day.

Rez slowly looked around.

"What?"

The Hall trembled.

Then an ancient voice echoed from somewhere deep within the city.

A voice belonging to no Drakhar.

A voice older than the Hall itself.

> "The Last Keeper has returned."

Silence.

Complete silence.

Rez blinked.

Twice.

Then pointed at himself.

"Me?"

## Chapter 77 — The First Expedition Beyond the Door

While the mystery surrounding Rez grew...

Another problem remained.

The Door.

Nobody could ignore it forever.

Eventually someone would have to investigate.

Eventually someone would have to learn the truth.

Unfortunately...

That someone became Aiden.

Against his will.

As usual.

---

Several hours later, a small group gathered near the entrance.

Aiden.

Lucien.

Seraphina.

Rez.

And surprisingly...

Kael.

The child stood quietly beside Zee.

His silver eyes fixed upon the darkness.

Unlike before, reality no longer unraveled around him.

For the first time...

He seemed stable.

Peaceful.

Whole.

Zee placed a hand on his shoulder.

"You don't have to come."

Kael shook his head.

The little boy looked toward the Door.

Then toward his family.

Then smiled.

"I don't want to be alone anymore."

Nobody argued after that.

---

The expedition began.

One step.

Then another.

Then another.

The moment they crossed the threshold...

Everything changed.

Gravity disappeared.

Time disappeared.

Distance disappeared.

Reality itself seemed uncertain.

The universe behind them vanished.

And before them stretched an endless sea of stars.

Not normal stars.

Living stars.

Watching stars.

Thinking stars.

Aiden stared in disbelief.

Because beyond creation...

Something impossible existed.

An entirely different reality.

---

## Chapter 78 — The Crimson Legacy Expands

The stars watched them.

Millions of them.

Billions.

Every light felt aware.

Conscious.

Alive.

The sensation was deeply unsettling.

Lucien immediately pointed at one.

"That one blinked."

"It absolutely did not."

"It did."

"It didn't."

"It did."

Even at the edge of existence, Lucien found ways to annoy people.

---

Then the stars parted.

Slowly.

Deliberately.

Creating a pathway.

A road of light stretching endlessly into the unknown.

The Observer appeared once more.

His gaze fixed on the distant horizon.

For the first time in ages...

He seemed uncertain.

Then he spoke.

Quietly.

Almost reverently.

"Nobody has walked this road since before creation."

Silence followed.

Then the ancient being looked toward Aiden.

Toward the Crimson Legacy.

Toward the future of House Drakhar.

And said:

"Whatever waits at the end..."

A pause.

"...will change everything."

Far ahead, beyond the stars...

A colossal city of gold slowly emerged from the darkness.

Larger than worlds.

Older than time.

And at its center stood a throne.

Occupied.

Waiting.

Watching their approach.
