## Chapter 28 — Surviving the Son of Zee

The scream didn't stop.

It echoed across the kingdom.

Across the mountains.

Across the oceans.

Across the world.

Every window inside the Hollow Throne shattered instantly.

Ancient towers collapsed.

Entire sections of the ruined kingdom fell into the crater below.

Aiden was thrown backward through a stone wall.

Pain exploded through his body.

Dust filled the air.

His ears rang.

For several seconds he couldn't hear anything.

Then the Codex appeared.

### HEALTH CRITICAL

### SUGGESTION:
RUN

Aiden groaned.

"Helpful."

Outside, Lucien continued screaming.

His power flooded the landscape like a tidal wave.

The crimson storm above had become a living thing.

Lightning crashed from the heavens.

Each bolt carved miles-long scars into the earth.

The world itself seemed unable to contain him.

Because Lucien wasn't simply powerful.

He was power.

The accumulated strength of thousands of years.

Thousands of battles.

Thousands of evolutions.

Thousands of victories.

And now all of it was erupting at once.

Like a volcano finally breaking.

Like a star collapsing.

Like a god waking up angry.

---

Seraphina landed beside Aiden.

Her armor was cracked.

Blood ran down her forehead.

Yet she barely seemed to notice.

Her eyes never left Lucien.

"My brother is still in there."

The words surprised Aiden.

Because they weren't spoken with certainty.

They were spoken with hope.

The kind of hope someone clings to when they're terrified of the alternative.

Then Lucien's head slowly turned toward them.

His silver eyes glowed.

For one brief second...

Recognition appeared.

"Sera..."

The word was barely a whisper.

Seraphina froze.

Tears immediately filled her eyes.

"Lucien."

A smile almost appeared on his face.

Almost.

Then the Crimson Rage swallowed him again.

---

The explosion that followed erased half the mountain.

## Chapter 29 — The Strongest Drakhar

Aiden didn't see the attack.

Nobody did.

One second Lucien stood a mile away.

The next second he was directly in front of them.

The ground beneath him collapsed.

Space itself seemed distorted.

The sheer speed was impossible.

Lucien's fist moved.

Aiden reacted instantly.

Ashfire exploded around him.

Witch runes activated.

Shadow magic surged.

Every ability he possessed erupted at once.

It wasn't enough.

Not even close.

The impact launched him across the kingdom.

He crashed through six buildings.

Then eight more.

Then finally stopped inside a ruined tower.

The entire left side of his body felt numb.

The Codex flickered wildly.

### DAMAGE RECEIVED

ERROR

### DAMAGE RECEIVED

ERROR

### DAMAGE RECEIVED

ERROR

The system couldn't even calculate how hard he had been hit.

Aiden slowly stood.

Blood dripped from his mouth.

His vision blurred.

And in that moment...

He finally understood why Lucien had become a legend.

Not because he was Zee's son.

Because he was Lucien.

A monster even history couldn't forget.

## Chapter 30 — The Memory Hunt

Aiden knew he couldn't beat him.

Seraphina knew it.

Rez knew it.

Even the Codex knew it.

The quest wasn't defeat Lucien.

The quest was help him remember.

Which meant somewhere inside the Crimson Prince...

The real Lucien was trapped.

Fighting.

Screaming.

Trying to come back.

The question was how.

Then Aiden noticed something.

Every time Seraphina spoke...

Lucien hesitated.

Every time he heard a familiar name...

His attacks slowed.

Only for a second.

But it was enough.

Aiden immediately opened the Codex.

Pages began flipping.

Searching.

Scanning.

Looking through every memory it possessed.

Then suddenly—

A new skill appeared.

### TEMPORARY ABILITY UNLOCKED

### MEMORY WALKER

Description:
Enter the memories of another.

Risk:
Extreme.

Possible Outcome:
Death.

Aiden stared.

Then looked toward Lucien.

Then back at the skill.

Then back at Lucien.

"Why is every useful ability trying to kill me?"

Rez shrugged.

"Tradition?"

## Chapter 31 — Inside the Mind of a Prince

The moment Aiden activated Memory Walker...

Everything disappeared.

The battlefield vanished.

The kingdom vanished.

The storm vanished.

He found himself standing somewhere else.

A beautiful place.

A garden.

Sunlight.

Flowers.

Peace.

For a moment Aiden thought the ability had malfunctioned.

Then he saw him.

A young Lucien.

Maybe ten years old.

Running through the garden.

Laughing.

Actually laughing.

Not a warrior.

Not a prince.

Just a kid.

A father chased after him.

Pretending not to be able to catch him.

Pretending.

Because everyone knew who the father was.

Zee Drakhar.

The First Crimson King.

Aiden watched silently.

The memory felt real.

Too real.

Zee eventually caught Lucien and lifted him onto his shoulders.

The young prince laughed.

The sound hit Aiden harder than any attack.

Because he had never imagined Lucien happy.

Every story had painted him as a warrior.

A hero.

A legend.

But here...

He was just someone's son.

Then the memory darkened.

The sky turned red.

The garden began crumbling.

And somewhere in the distance...

Something roared.

Not Lucien.

Not Zee.

Something else.

Something ancient.

Something hungry.

Aiden immediately recognized the feeling.

Valthoros.

## Chapter 32 — The Thing Inside the Rage

The garden shattered.

Darkness spread across the memory.

Trees withered.

The sky cracked.

The world decayed.

Aiden ran.

The memory itself was collapsing.

As he moved deeper, he saw fragments of Lucien's life.

Wars.

Victories.

Losses.

Friends dying.

Kingdoms falling.

Entire generations disappearing.

Thousands of years of pain.

Thousands of years alone.

Then he found the source.

At the center of Lucien's mind stood a black throne.

And sitting upon it...

Wasn't Lucien.

A shadow sat there.

Smiling.

Watching.

Waiting.

The same smile Aiden had seen in visions before.

The same ancient hunger.

The same endless malice.

The shadow slowly opened its eyes.

Golden eyes.

Terrible eyes.

The shadow smiled wider.

"Interesting."

Aiden froze.

Because the voice wasn't Lucien's.

The voice wasn't Valthoros'.

The voice belonged to something else.

Something that had been hiding.

Watching the bloodline.

Manipulating events from the beginning.

Then the shadow spoke again.

And the words made Aiden's blood run cold.

> "Tell me, little heir..."
>
> "Do you know why Zee sealed me inside his son?"

The memory world cracked apart.

And for the first time since opening the Codex...

Aiden realized the truth might be far worse than anyone imagined.
