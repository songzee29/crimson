## Chapter 17 — The Conversation No Drakhar Survived

Nobody moved.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody breathed.

The message remained suspended before them.

### THE OBSERVER WISHES TO SPEAK WITH THE HEIR

Aiden stared at the screen.

His stomach twisted.

Every warning.

Every vision.

Every secret.

Every death.

Every mystery.

Somehow they all led here.

To this moment.

To this conversation.

Seraphina stood immediately.

For the first time since her release...

She looked alarmed.

"No."

The word echoed through the throne room.

Aiden looked toward her.

"What?"

"You cannot answer."

Rez nodded.

Instantly.

Immediately.

Without hesitation.

"Listen to her."

Aiden frowned.

"Why?"

Neither answered.

Not right away.

Because both already knew the truth.

Nobody knew what happened to those who spoke with the Observer.

The ancient records ended there.

Entire bloodlines vanished.

Entire kingdoms disappeared.

Stories stopped.

History ended.

The Observer remained.

Aiden slowly looked back toward the message.

Then another notification appeared.

### CONTACT REQUEST EXPIRING

### 00:59

A countdown.

The Observer was waiting.

The room grew silent.

Fifty-eight seconds.

Fifty-seven.

Fifty-six.

Aiden made his choice.

He raised his hand.

And pressed ACCEPT.

The world vanished.

## Chapter 18 — Beyond The Stars

Darkness.

Endless darkness.

No floor.

No sky.

No time.

No sound.

Aiden floated within a void so vast it made reality itself feel small.

Then he saw them.

Stars.

Billions.

Trillions.

Entire galaxies spinning around him.

Entire universes unfolding before his eyes.

The scale was impossible.

His mind struggled to comprehend it.

Then a voice spoke.

Not from above.

Not from below.

From everywhere.

And nowhere.

"Aiden Drakhar."

The stars trembled.

Aiden turned.

For a moment he saw nothing.

Then—

A figure appeared.

Simple.

Ordinary.

A man sitting upon a stone bench.

No armor.

No crown.

No divine aura.

Nothing.

The Observer looked human.

Which somehow terrified Aiden more.

The man smiled.

"Hello."

Aiden remained standing.

Tense.

Ready.

Waiting.

The Observer laughed softly.

"You expected something more dramatic."

"You've been watching me."

The smile never left.

"Yes."

"Why?"

The Observer looked toward the stars.

And for the first time...

The smile disappeared.

"I was hoping you wouldn't survive."

Silence.

Aiden's blood ran cold.

## Chapter 19 — The First Truth

The Observer stood.

Slowly.

Calmly.

The stars moved around him as if reality itself obeyed his presence.

"You misunderstand me."

Aiden said nothing.

The Observer continued.

"When Zee Drakhar appeared..."

The stars shifted.

A vision formed.

Ancient battlefields.

Ancient kingdoms.

Ancient wars.

"I watched."

The vision changed.

A younger Zee appeared.

Then Lucien.

Then Seraphina.

Then countless others.

The Drakhar bloodline.

Growing.

Evolving.

Becoming stronger.

Becoming something new.

Something impossible.

The Observer sighed.

"I thought your bloodline would destroy itself."

Silence.

"But it didn't."

The stars darkened.

The vision shifted again.

Showing countless dead civilizations.

Countless fallen kingdoms.

Countless extinct races.

"They all fell."

Aiden stared.

The Observer's eyes grew distant.

"Tyranny."

The vision shifted.

"Greed."

Another shift.

"Pride."

Another.

"Fear."

Endless examples.

Endless failures.

Then the vision returned to Zee.

Standing.

Unbroken.

The Observer's expression changed.

Almost thoughtful.

"Your ancestor disappointed me."

Aiden blinked.

"What?"

A faint smile returned.

"He proved me wrong."

## Chapter 20 — The Enemy Behind The Purifiers

The stars vanished.

The void became silent once more.

Aiden folded his arms.

"If you're not the enemy..."

The Observer laughed.

A genuine laugh.

"You think I created the Purifiers."

"Didn't you?"

"No."

The answer came instantly.

Without hesitation.

Without deception.

"No."

For the first time...

Aiden realized something.

The Observer hated them.

Not feared.

Not disliked.

Hated.

The stars erupted.

Showing another vision.

A dark world.

A dying world.

A world consumed by black fire.

Entire civilizations burned.

Entire realities collapsed.

And standing within the destruction—

Something moved.

A shadow.

Massive.

Ancient.

Hungry.

The Observer's face hardened.

"That."

The shadow turned.

Aiden immediately felt sick.

His instincts screamed.

His blood screamed.

His soul screamed.

Run.

The Observer pointed.

"That is the reason I watched your bloodline."

The shadow smiled.

Aiden wished it hadn't.

"The Purifiers served it."

Silence.

"The Hunter serves it."

Another pause.

"The wars served it."

The Observer finally looked directly at him.

"And one day..."

The stars went dark.

"That thing will come for House Drakhar."

## Chapter 21 — The Name Of The Devourer

The shadow remained.

Watching.

Waiting.

Even through the vision.

Aiden hated it.

More than the Hunter.

More than the Purifiers.

More than anything he had ever seen.

The Observer finally spoke.

"The oldest enemy."

The void trembled.

"The first destroyer."

Reality itself seemed reluctant to hear the name.

As if the universe remembered.

As if the universe feared.

The Observer took a breath.

Then said it.

"Valthoros."

The stars shattered.

The vision ended.

Everything vanished.

The void collapsed.

Aiden found himself standing once more inside the Hollow Throne.

Back beside Seraphina.

Back beside Nyra.

Back beside Rez.

Only a second had passed.

Yet he felt years older.

The Codex immediately activated.

### WORLD QUEST UNLOCKED

### THE SHADOW OF VALTHOROS

Status: Active

Objective: Survive

Reward: Unknown

Failure: Extinction

The room fell silent.

Then Seraphina slowly stood.

Her silver eyes locked onto Aiden.

And for the first time since her release...

She looked afraid.

"What did it tell you?"

Aiden stared at the screen.

At the impossible quest.

At the name now burned into his mind.

Then he answered.

"It wasn't warning us about itself."

Silence.

"It was warning us about something worse."

## Chapter 22 — The Prince Awakens

Thousands of miles away...

Beyond the northern seas.

Beyond the frozen kingdoms.

Beyond the edge of every map.

A mountain split open.

Ancient ice shattered.

An entire glacier collapsed.

Deep within the darkness...

A pair of silver eyes opened.

The world trembled.

The oceans stirred.

The stars shifted.

A voice echoed from within the frozen abyss.

A voice unheard for thousands of years.

A voice belonging to a prince.

A voice belonging to a legend.

A voice belonging to the son of Zee Drakhar.

Lucien slowly rose from his throne of ice.

And smiled.

Because after thousands of years...

He had finally felt it.

The bloodline had awakened.

And his family was calling him home.
