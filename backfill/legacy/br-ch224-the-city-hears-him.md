Across New Orleans...

Every witch suddenly clutched their chest.

Every magical barrier flickered.

Every enchanted object began vibrating.

The old cemeteries...

Awakened.

The bayou waters turned crimson.

The Mississippi River flowed backwards...

For one impossible second.

Children who had never used magic before suddenly cried because they felt something they couldn't explain.

One little girl looked toward the city skyline.

"...Someone's hurting."

Her mother hugged her tightly.

Not realizing...

The entire city could feel Aidan.
