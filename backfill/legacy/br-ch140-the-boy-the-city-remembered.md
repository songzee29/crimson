By sunset...

News had spread across New Orleans.

Nobody knew Aidan's name.

But everyone was talking about...

"The boy who ran into the fire."

An old woman placed flowers outside the burned building.

"He saved my grandson."

A firefighter shook his head.

"I've never seen anyone move like that."

Children began telling the story before the day had even ended.

"The fire couldn't catch him."

"He fought the monster."

"He smiled after saving everybody."

Aidan heard none of it.

He sat alone on the warehouse roof.

His body ached.

His magic felt drained.

Lyra climbed up beside him.

She handed him a bottle of water.

"You did good today."

Aidan looked down at the city.

"I got lucky."

She smiled.

"No."

"You chose people before power."

For a long moment...

Neither of them spoke.

Below them...

New Orleans slowly returned to life.

Jazz drifted through the evening air.

The city was healing.

And for the first time...

The people had unknowingly witnessed the beginning of a new legend.

Not the Crimson Devil.

Not the Crimson King.

Just...

A young man who ran into the fire when everyone else ran away.

---

Far away...

Inside a quiet training hall hidden beneath a Veil fortress...

A young swordsman lowered his practice blade.

A messenger entered.

"The Living Curse in New Orleans..."

"...was destroyed."

The young man asked only one question.

"By the heir?"

The messenger nodded.

The swordsman closed his eyes.

Then smiled.

"So..."

"He's finally becoming worthy."

He picked up his sword once more.

"Tell the Council..."

"...I'll handle him when the time comes."

The messenger hesitated.

"They asked for your name to be added to the operation."

The young man slid the blade back into its sheath.

"Then add it."

He turned toward the doorway.

His face remained hidden by the shadows.

"The heir has begun growing."

"So it's time..."

"...for his future nemesis to begin growing too."

The two young men had still never truly met.

But destiny had already begun sharpening them against one another.

And neither realized that one day, both of their lives would be forever defined by a single moment... and a single death.
