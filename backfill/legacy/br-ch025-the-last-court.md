The throne was no longer empty.

It was full.

Dozens of men and women stood before it.

Some wore crowns.

Others carried staffs.

Some wore armor stained with blood.

Every one of them carried the Darkheart Crest.

Not one looked alike.

Different ages.

Different races.

Different eras.

Yet they all shared the same eyes.

Determined.

Burdened.

Tired.

The throne itself remained empty.

Then...

A young king walked forward.

Not Zee.

Someone far older.

He turned toward the gathered rulers.

"The House stands divided."

No one answered.

He continued.

"We have forgotten why we were given authority."

One ruler argued.

"We were chosen to rule."

The young king shook his head.

"No."

"We were chosen..."

"...to answer."

The memory froze.

The Legacy wasn't showing Aidan history.

It was teaching him philosophy.
