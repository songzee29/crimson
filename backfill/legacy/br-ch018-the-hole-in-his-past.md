Aidan laughed.

Not because it was funny.

Because it was impossible.

---

"I wasn't erased."

Lyra didn't answer.

She simply stared at him.

Waiting.

---

"I have friends."

Nothing.

---

"I go to school."

Silence.

---

"I've lived here my whole life."

Still nothing.

---

The longer she stayed quiet, the worse he felt.

---

Finally, Lyra spoke.

"Tell me your earliest memory."

---

Aidan opened his mouth.

Stopped.

Thought.

---

A birthday party.

No.

That wasn't right.

---

A school field trip.

No.

---

A family dinner.

No.

---

Every memory he reached for felt incomplete.

Like pages torn from a book.

---

His stomach tightened.

---

"That's not possible."

---

Lyra looked away.

"The erased never notice."

---

Aidan's pulse quickened.

---

"What do you mean?"

---

"The mind fills gaps."

---

The candlelight flickered across her face.

---

"If someone removes ten years from your life..."

She paused.

---

"...your brain invents a bridge."

---

Silence.

---

Aidan suddenly couldn't remember his mother's face.

---

Not clearly.

---

He knew he had one.

Of course he did.

---

Yet every detail felt blurry.

Distant.

---

Like trying to remember a dream.

---

For the first time since finding the Crimson Book...

Aidan felt genuine fear.

---

Not fear of monsters.

---

Fear of himself.
