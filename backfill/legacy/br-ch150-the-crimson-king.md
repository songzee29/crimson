Aidan stood.

Barely.

His legs threatened to collapse.

His heartbeat thundered in his ears.

His hands had stopped shaking.

Not because he recovered.

Because he had become too tired to notice.

He looked at Cassian.

Then quietly said...

"...You know why you'll never understand Elias?"

Cassian frowned.

"...What?"

Aidan's eyes burned.

"...Because he would've stopped fighting..."

"...if it meant protecting someone else."

A long silence.

"...You..."

"...only know how to fight for yourself."

---

Cassian's face twisted with rage.

He roared.

Charging forward.

The swamp exploded beneath his feet.

Aidan charged too.

Not because he had strength left.

Because he had **nothing** left.

Their fists collided.

The impact split the clouds.

Lightning exploded across the sky.

The shockwave rolled through Louisiana.

Miles away...

People looked toward the horizon.

Wondering...

What kind of monsters were fighting out there.
