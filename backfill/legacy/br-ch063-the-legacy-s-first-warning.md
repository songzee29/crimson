As they prepared to leave the chamber, the System interrupted.

Not like before.

The entire interface darkened.

Every notification vanished.

Only one line remained.

---

> **《FOUNDER'S WARNING》**

---

The Guardian looked sharply at Aidan.

"What is it?"

Aidan stared ahead.

"It says..."

His voice grew quiet.

"...Founder's Warning."

The Guardian's face changed.

For the first time...

Fear.

Real fear.

"What does it say?"

Aidan read slowly.

---

> **Do not mistake my path for yours.**

> **If you become me...**

> **The Crimson Legacy has failed.**

---

Silence.

Even the Sanctuary seemed to hold its breath.

The Guardian lowered his head.

A faint smile appeared.

"So..."

"He left that behind."

Aidan looked at him.

"You knew?"

The Guardian shook his head.

"No."

"But that..."

He looked toward the empty throne.

"...sounds exactly like something Zee would say."

---

As Aidan stood there, the words lingered in his mind.

Not because they frightened him.

Because they gave him permission.

He didn't have to become another Zee.

He had to become someone worthy of carrying the Crimson Legacy.

And somewhere, deep within the System, beneath the interface and the quests and the ancient code of the bloodline...

A new, empty page quietly appeared.

Not for Zee.

Not for the forgotten son.

But for **Aidan**.

A page waiting to record a legacy no one—not even the Crimson System—could yet predict.
