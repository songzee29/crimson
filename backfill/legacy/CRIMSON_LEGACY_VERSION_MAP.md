# Crimson Legacy — Master Version Map

**Purpose:** Reconstruct the complete history of Crimson Legacy from its earliest recovered conception through the latest current-version material, while preserving every distinct draft and every continuity branch.

**Status:** Working archive index. This is a navigation and classification document, not canon and not a replacement manuscript. No existing chapter, canon file, directive, or conversation extraction is being overwritten or deleted.

## The rule for this recovery

Do not choose one “best” draft and discard the others. Do not merge incompatible chapters. Do not treat a chapter number as a universal position: Chapter 1, Chapter 2, and other numbers restart across books, conversations, rewrites, and branches.

Every recovered item must retain:
- its original source conversation and date where available;
- its title and chapter number as used in that source;
- its book/arc/track label as recorded, or **unconfirmed** if the source does not establish one;
- whether it is a distinct version, a partial continuation, a duplicate copy, an outline/directive, or a manuscript chapter;
- its relationship to later versions, without declaring older material canon;
- continuity conflicts and unresolved gaps rather than silently repairing them.

## Version status labels

- **EARLIEST RECOVERED** — oldest surviving version found so far; not necessarily the first ever written.
- **DISTINCT VERSION** — prose or events differ materially.
- **BRANCH** — intentionally or explicitly separate continuity.
- **REWRITE / RESTART** — new pass or numbering run.
- **CONTINUATION** — text continues a chapter or scene rather than representing a complete standalone chapter.
- **DUPLICATE COPY** — same recovered text stored in more than one location.
- **PARTIAL / EXCERPT** — only part of the chapter survives in this extraction.
- **CURRENT CANDIDATE** — appears to match a recent/current track, pending comparison with the latest directives.
- **UNASSIGNED** — evidence is insufficient to place it safely.

## Current-version authority

The latest locked Zee directive controls the present story where it clearly applies. Older manuscript prose remains valuable historical evidence but does not override a later ruling. A chapter that feels familiar is a lead to investigate, not proof that it is current canon.

For example, the archive includes Book Three Soul Link scenes in which Rez is already speaking to Aidan. Those scenes must remain in their own historical track; the current timing rule that Rez remains asleep until Vampire Arc Chapter 7 must not be overwritten by an older draft.
