Outside...

The fire suddenly stopped spreading.

The entire street became silent.

Too silent.

Aidan felt it before he saw it.

Someone had arrived.

Slow.

Measured footsteps echoed through the smoke.

Cassian.

His sword remained sheathed.

His eyes met Elias's.

For the first time in years...

Neither brother spoke.

The world seemed to disappear around them.

Just two brothers.

Standing on opposite sides of a promise.

Elias smiled first.

"...You got taller."

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"So did you."

Aidan looked between them.

His mind raced.

"...You know each other?"

Neither answered.

Cassian finally stepped closer.

Rain hissed against the burning streets.

"I came to take the Heir."

Elias slowly pushed himself upright despite the crushing beam.

"...Then you'll have to go through me."

Cassian closed his eyes.

For the first time...

Pain crossed his face.

Not anger.

Not hatred.

Pain.

"I was hoping..."

"...you wouldn't say that."

He rested one hand on the hilt of his sword.

The air itself seemed to hold its breath.

Aidan's heart pounded.

The Witch Authority stirred violently beneath his skin.

The System flashed once.

Then twice.

Then began to flicker.

Something deep inside the Crimson Legacy recognized this moment.

Not because the curse had broken...

But because destiny had finally arrived.

The next decision...

Would change every life on that battlefield forever.
