The morning sun rose over New Orleans.

The city looked peaceful.

Children walked to school.

Streetcars rolled through the French Quarter.

Coffee shops filled with laughter.

Everything appeared normal.

Except...

Every shadow in the city pointed in the wrong direction.

No matter where the sun stood...

Every shadow leaned toward Aidan.

People whispered.

Some crossed themselves.

Others pretended not to notice.

The Circle noticed immediately.

Malik looked down.

"...Tell me I'm seeing things."

Sienna swallowed.

"...You're seeing it too."

Aidan looked behind himself.

His shadow...

Wasn't following him.

It stood still.

Watching him.

Then...

It moved.

Half a second later than he did.

Before anyone could react—

It returned to normal.
