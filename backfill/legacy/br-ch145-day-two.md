The sun rose...

Neither man noticed.

The swamp had become unrecognizable.

Ancient cypress trees lay uprooted.

The river had changed direction.

Entire sections of earth had collapsed beneath the force of their battle.

The birds had stopped singing hours ago.

Even nature...

Had abandoned the battlefield.

---

Cassian stood breathing heavily.

His shirt was soaked in blood.

Most of it...

His own.

The Darkheart Brand beneath his sleeves burned faintly.

Across from him...

Aidan stood with his sword lowered.

Blood streamed from a cut above his eye.

His left shoulder hung unnaturally low.

His right hand trembled.

Not from fear.

From exhaustion.

Neither man had slept.

Neither man intended to.

---

Cassian laughed.

A tired...

Broken laugh.

"...You're slowing down."

Aidan wiped blood from his mouth.

"So are you."
