The following afternoon...

Aidan and Lyra wandered through the French Quarter.

Music echoed from every direction.

Tourists packed the sidewalks.

For just a little while...

Everything felt normal.

Until an old blind man reached out and grabbed Aidan's wrist.

Hard.

"You came back."

Aidan froze.

"What?"

The old man slowly lifted his head.

His cloudy eyes couldn't see.

Yet somehow...

they seemed to look directly into Aidan.

"I remember your heartbeat."

Lyra immediately stepped between them.

"Sir, you've got the wrong person."

The old man ignored her completely.

He whispered one word.

"...Prince."

Aidan's heart skipped.

"What did you call me?"

The old man smiled sadly.

"I wondered how long it would take..."

"...before the blood remembered itself."

Then...

he released Aidan's wrist.

The moment Aidan turned around—

the old man was gone.

Not walking away.

Gone.

Lyra looked around in disbelief.

"Where did he go?"

Aidan didn't answer.

Because the System had appeared.

Not in its normal crimson colors.

In deep black and red.

was beginning to find him.

I also have a thought for where we should go next, and I think it could become one of the best arcs in the series.

Instead of immediately jumping back into Veil battles, we introduce **other people who knew Zee**. Not people who dump exposition, but people whose lives were permanently changed by him. Some loved him. Some hated him. Some owe him everything. Some blame him for losing everything.

That way, before Aidan ever learns who Zee truly was, the reader experiences **multiple versions of Zee** through the eyes of others. By the time the father reveal finally comes, readers will realize they never really knew him at all—and neither does Aidan. I think that would make the world feel much larger and much more alive.
