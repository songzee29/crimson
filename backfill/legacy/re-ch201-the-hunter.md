Three Veil Hunters moved silently through Jackson Square.

Silver weapons drawn.

Magic charms wrapped around their wrists.

The oldest whispered,

"Stay close."

"The reports say it's only one vampire."

Another laughed nervously.

"One vampire doesn't wipe out thirty squads."

Before the third could answer...

The oldest froze.

"...Don't..."

"...move."

---

A shadow landed behind them.

No sound.

No warning.

Only...

Presence.

---

One hunter slowly turned.

His eyes widened.

"...Run."

Too late.

---

Aidan moved once.

The first hunter hit the ground before he realized he'd been struck.

The second raised a silver blade.

Aidan caught it.

Silver burned his hand.

The smell of his own burning flesh filled the air.

He didn't react.

He simply snapped the blade in half.

Then drove the broken piece through the hunter's shoulder.

Not to kill.

To remove the obstacle.

The third hunter fired a blessed round.

It pierced Aidan's side.

He looked down at the wound.

Curious.

The flesh hissed.

Then...

Closed.

His crimson eyes lifted again.

The hunters finally understood.

This wasn't an ordinary vampire.
