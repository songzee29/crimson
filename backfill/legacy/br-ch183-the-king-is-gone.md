Aidan slowly lifted his head.

Fresh blood stained his lips.

His crimson eyes looked brighter now.

Clearer.

Stronger.

Not because he had become himself again.

Because the hunger had been satisfied.

For the moment.

---

Lyra reached him first.

She collapsed in front of him.

Tears streamed down her face.

"...Please..."

She reached toward his hand.

"...Come back..."

Aidan looked at her.

For several long seconds...

Nothing happened.

Then...

Very slowly...

He lowered his gaze.

Not at her face.

At the tears falling onto her hands.

His head tilted.

Confused.

He didn't know why.

But something about her crying...

Made his chest hurt.

Not physically.

Somewhere deeper.

A place the hunger couldn't reach.

---

Then the feeling vanished.

He turned away.
