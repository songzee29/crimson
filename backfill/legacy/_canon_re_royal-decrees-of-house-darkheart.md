# 👑 THE ROYAL DECREES OF HOUSE DARKHEART

## I. The First Decree — Blood Is Forfeit

> **"By the First Decree of House Darkheart... your blood is forfeit."**

The oldest and most feared decree.

When spoken, the victim's own blood betrays them. It is no longer under their command, but under the authority of House Darkheart.

Legends say the first king used it to end a war in a single sentence.

---

## II. The Second Decree — Your Name Is Forgotten

> **"By the Second Decree of House Darkheart... your name is forgotten."**

This is worse than death.

The condemned is erased from history.

Records fade.

Statues crack.

Songs are never sung.

Generations remember that someone existed...

But never remember who.

To House Darkheart, this is the greatest punishment imaginable.

Because legacy is sacred.

---

## III. The Third Decree — Let Silence Judge You

> **"By the Third Decree of House Darkheart... let silence judge you."**

The condemned loses every voice except their own.

No spirit answers.

No magic answers.

No ally hears their cries.

Even the world itself seems to turn away.

A punishment of complete isolation.

---

## IV. The Fourth Decree — The Crown Rejects You

> **"By the Fourth Decree of House Darkheart... the crown rejects you."**

Any authority the condemned possesses begins to abandon them.

Kings lose kingdoms.

Generals lose armies.

Leaders lose loyalty.

Power slips through their fingers until they stand alone.

---

## V. The Fifth Decree — Carry Your Sins

> **"By the Fifth Decree of House Darkheart... carry every life you have taken."**

Every innocent life the condemned has ended becomes a living burden upon their soul.

Not hallucinations.

Not ghosts.

A constant supernatural weight that can never be escaped.

The more blood they've spilled...

The heavier they become.

---

## VI. The Sixth Decree — Walk Beneath No Light

> **"By the Sixth Decree of House Darkheart... walk beneath no light."**

Wherever the condemned goes...

Hope fades.

Fire dims.

Blessings weaken.

They become an exile from every place that once welcomed them.

---

## VII. The Seventh Decree — Your Throne Is Ash

> **"By the Seventh Decree of House Darkheart... your throne is ash."**

Everything built upon the condemned's ambition begins to crumble.

Empires fail.

Dynasties collapse.

Victories become meaningless.

It is a judgment against pride.

---

## VIII. The Eighth Decree — Death Shall Wait

> **"By the Eighth Decree of House Darkheart... death shall wait."**

The condemned is denied release.

Neither life nor death fully claims them.

They must endure the consequences of their actions before either will accept them.

Among House Darkheart, this is considered more terrifying than execution.

---

## IX. The Ninth Decree — Let the World Bear Witness

> **"By the Ninth Decree of House Darkheart... let the world bear witness."**

Every hidden crime.

Every betrayal.

Every lie.

Is revealed before those whose judgment matters most.

No secret survives.

No mask remains.

Truth itself becomes the punishment.

---

## X. The Tenth Decree — Judgment Complete

> **"By the Tenth Decree of House Darkheart... judgment is complete."**

No one knows exactly what this decree does.

The records end here.

Ancient kings refused to write beyond its title.

Only one sentence remains beneath it:

> **"No ruler has spoken the Tenth Decree twice."**

No explanation.

No surviving witness.

Only silence.

---

# 🔒 One final rule I want to lock in.

These decrees should **never** become attacks Aidan throws around in every fight.

They are **constitutional powers of the Head of House Darkheart**.

A king doesn't invoke them because he's angry.

He invokes them because he has reached a judgment from which there is no return.

That keeps them rare, terrifying, and worthy of the legend we've been building.

😈 Out of all of them, I think these three will become iconic:

- **The First Decree:** *"Your blood is forfeit."*
- **The Second Decree:** *"Your name is forgotten."*
- **The Tenth Decree:** *"Judgment is complete."*

Those feel like the kind of words that make even ancient monsters stop smiling.
