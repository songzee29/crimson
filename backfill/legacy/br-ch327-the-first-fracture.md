Aidan woke before dawn.

His heart raced.

His hands shook.

He walked to the bathroom.

Looked into the mirror.

His reflection was normal.

He reached for the sink.

Then froze.

For less than a second...

His reflection blinked...

After he did.

The delay was almost imperceptible.

But it happened.

Aidan stared.

The reflection smiled.

He hadn't.

Then it whispered.

Not aloud.

Inside the mirror.

> **"Soon..."**

The smile vanished.

Everything returned to normal.

The Witch Authority surged through his hands, shattering the mirror into crimson dust before it could say another word.

Aidan stood breathing heavily.

Looking at his own trembling hands.

He whispered the one sentence that no one else heard.

> **"...I'm starting to lose."**

Far away, beneath the Hall of Kings, the Soul Anchor pulsed.

The sleeping Witch Hybrid vessel's fingers curled for the very first time.

And in the endless darkness, Rez frowned in his sleep.

Though he still could not wake...

He finally sensed what Zee had feared all along.

Not that Aidan would become a vampire.

But that if he faced the Vampire Authority **alone**...

There might not be enough of Aidan left for anyone to bring back.
