That evening...

The Guardian met Aidan again.

This time he placed six candles on a stone table.

"No enemies tonight."

Aidan looked confused.

"No fighting?"

The Guardian shook his head.

"Magic."

He lit the first candle.

"A Witch doesn't throw power around."

He lit the second.

"A Witch listens."

Third.

"A Witch studies."

Fourth.

"A Witch adapts."

Fifth.

"A Witch protects."

Finally...

He held the sixth candle but didn't light it.

"And the last lesson..."

He handed it to Aidan.

"...you'll learn on your own."

Aidan stared at the unlit candle.

The Guardian turned and walked away.

Leaving him alone beneath the moon.

The System appeared.

---

**Meditation Available**

Would you like to begin?

---

Aidan smiled.

"...Yeah."

He sat cross-legged.

Closed his eyes.

And for the first time...

He didn't ask the System for power.

He asked himself a question.

*"What kind of Witch do I want to become?"*
