Three days later.

The city had begun rebuilding.

Barricades disappeared.

Businesses reopened.

Tourists returned to streets that would never know how close New Orleans had come to falling.

Officially…

Lucien Vale had never existed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Inner Circle gathered inside an abandoned warehouse overlooking the river.

No celebration waited for them.

Only silence.

Maps covered the table.

Reports from every surviving coven and hunter network lay scattered across the room.

Malik broke the silence first.

“The organized vampire network is finished.”

“Eighty-seven confirmed locations.”

“All abandoned.”

“Eighteen lieutenants confirmed dead.”

“The rest scattered.”

Draco nodded.

“Without Lucien, no one possesses enough authority to reunite them.”

“The empire is over.”

Rowan closed the final report.

“Then we succeeded.”

No one answered.

Because the room did not feel victorious.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra quietly looked toward the empty chair where Aidan should have been.

“He didn’t come.”

“No,” Elias said.

“He won’t.”

Not because he was angry.

Not because he was avoiding them.

Simply because the meeting served no purpose to him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian rested both hands on the table.

“We’ve spent years trying to keep him alive.”

His voice remained steady.

“Now…”

He searched for words that refused to come.

“…I don’t know what we’re trying to save.”

No one challenged him.

Not even Lyra.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco unfolded a weathered map of North America.

One location had been marked decades earlier.

A single crimson circle.

He looked at it for a long time before rolling the map shut again.

“He’s already moving.”

“Toward what?” Rowan asked.

Draco met his eyes.

“I don’t know.”

“And that’s the first time in thirty-two years I’ve had to say those words.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Miles away…

Aidan stood alone on a cliff overlooking the Gulf.

The wind carried the scent of salt and distant rain.

His gaze remained fixed on the horizon.

The hunger had not weakened.

It had grown quieter.

Sharper.

More patient.

No longer a storm.

A constant companion.

A chime echoed once inside his mind.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Primary Vampire Objective

Complete

Next Directive

Pending…

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification faded.

Aidan remained motionless.

Far below, the tide rolled toward the shore.

For a single instant…

The waves divided around the reflection of the cliff before continuing toward the beach.

The sea kept its silence.

So did Aidan.

Book Two had survived its darkest night.

But the cost of that survival now stood alone, looking toward a horizon only he could see.
