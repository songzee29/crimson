That same night...

Lyra returned to the Hall of Kings.

Alone.

She couldn't shake the fear growing in her chest.

She stood before the sealed vampire door.

The inscription had changed again.

Now it read:

> **"The King smiles..."**

A pause.

Then new words carved themselves beneath it.

> **"...while the Vampire hungers."**

Lyra took a step back.

"No..."

The ring on Aidan's hand suddenly burned.

Miles away.
