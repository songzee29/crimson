The cavern beneath the cathedral stretched farther than the light could reach.

Aidan landed without stumbling.

Across from him, Lucien brushed stone dust from his coat.

He smiled.

“I finally have you alone.”

Aidan’s expression never changed.

“You’ve had me since the cathedral.”

Lucien chuckled.

“So you noticed.”

“I noticed everything.”

They moved.

No warning.

No wasted motion.

Lucien’s fist cut toward Aidan’s temple.

Aidan slipped inside the strike.

His elbow shattered Lucien’s ribs.

Lucien twisted with the impact instead of resisting it.

His heel caught Aidan beneath the jaw.

Both men separated.

Neither looked injured.

Neither looked surprised.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Above them, the Inner Circle fought their way through the remaining elite vampires.

Without Lucien’s commands, the formation faltered.

Small mistakes became larger ones.

Cassian cut down another attacker.

“They’re losing coordination!”

“They’ve lost their commander,” Rowan answered.

“But they haven’t lost purpose.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Below…

Lucien circled slowly.

“I’ve watched every battle you’ve fought.”

“I know your instincts.”

“I know how you think.”

“You eliminate the greatest threat first.”

“You protect your allies only when strategically necessary.”

“You’ve become wonderfully predictable.”

Aidan stepped forward.

“No.”

Lucien frowned.

“I’ve become faster.”

The distance between them disappeared.

Lucien barely raised his guard before Aidan’s fist drove through it.

Bone cracked.

Lucien stumbled backward.

For the first time…

He looked genuinely surprised.

“You’ve adapted.”

“I survived.”

“That’s not the same thing.”

“It is.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

High above, Elias finally forced the cathedral doors open.

Moonlight poured into the ruined sanctuary.

The surviving vampires hesitated.

One looked toward the shattered floor.

Another toward the exit.

Then…

One after another…

They fled.

Not to regroup.

To escape.

Draco watched them go.

“It’s over.”

Cassian looked into the darkness beneath the broken floor.

“No.”

“Not yet.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lucien wiped blood from his mouth.

His jaw healed.

His smile returned.

“I misjudged your growth.”

“But not your limits.”

He lunged again.

Aidan caught his wrist.

Twisted.

The arm snapped.

Lucien drove his other fist into Aidan’s chest.

The impact echoed through the cavern.

Neither stepped back.

Lucien laughed.

“You still feel pain.”

“I do.”

“Good.”

Aidan’s grip tightened.

“So do you.”

He tore Lucien’s broken arm free.

There was no rage.

No satisfaction.

Only efficiency.

Lucien staggered.

His arm began regenerating.

More slowly this time.

His smile faded.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

“The Command Structure…”

Lucien breathed heavily.

“…will survive me.”

“No.”

“They’ve already broken.”

Lucien hesitated.

For the first time since the battle began…

He listened.

There were no coordinated footsteps above.

No signals.

No commands.

Only scattered fighting.

His empire had already begun collapsing.

Not because Aidan hunted it.

Because everything had depended upon Lucien remaining alive.

“You…”

Lucien whispered.

“…planned that.”

Aidan answered simply.

“Yes.”

Lucien had believed he was controlling the battlefield.

He had never realized he had been isolated from his own command.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian finally reached the edge of the collapse.

He looked down.

“Aidan!”

His voice echoed through the cavern.

Aidan looked up.

Only briefly.

Cassian smiled despite the destruction.

“We’re here.”

“You don’t have to do this alone.”

Silence.

For one impossible heartbeat…

Cassian saw the face of the friend he had trusted for years.

Then it vanished beneath the predator’s calm.

Aidan looked away.

“Stay out of my hunt.”

Nothing more.

Cassian’s hand slowly slipped from the broken stone.

He didn’t call again.

He finally understood.

The brother he had followed into impossible battles…

Was gone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra arrived beside him.

She looked down at Aidan.

No plea left her lips.

No declaration.

No promise.

She simply watched the man she loved turn his back on every hand reaching toward him.

Love had crossed every battlefield with him.

It could not cross this one.

She quietly lowered her eyes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Malik closed the last of his healing runes.

Logic had failed.

Every argument.

Every explanation.

Every strategy.

None of them had reached Aidan.

Only altered his calculations.

He stopped searching for the right words.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Elias cleaned the blood from his blade.

Loyalty had never once been questioned between them.

Yet even loyalty had become another battlefield asset.

He accepted the truth with silent grief.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Rowan rested both hands upon his staff.

Every lesson.

Every warning.

Every year spent teaching the Crimson Heir…

Had created a survivor.

Not the young man he hoped would inherit them.

He could teach no farther.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco looked from the shattered cathedral…

To the lone figure below.

For decades he had searched for the Crimson Heir believing destiny would be enough.

Now he understood.

Finding Aidan had never guaranteed saving him.

Destiny had only brought them to the edge.

Nothing beyond it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lucien slowly rose one final time.

His breathing had become uneven.

His regeneration faltered.

“I finally understand.”

Aidan stood waiting.

Lucien smiled weakly.

“I spent decades preparing for the Crimson Heir.”

“I prepared for every version of the man.”

He laughed once.

Quietly.

“I never imagined…”

“…the thing that would kill me…”

“…was something beyond the man.”

His eyes met Aidan’s one last time.

“I never understood you.”

“No.”

Aidan stepped forward.

“You understood someone who no longer exists.”

His hand pierced Lucien’s chest.

Closed around the vampire’s heart.

Crushed.

Lucien’s body froze.

Crimson cracks spread across his skin.

His eyes never left Aidan.

Then…

His body collapsed into ash.

The last commander of New Orleans’ hidden vampire order died without another word.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A sharp chime echoed once inside Aidan’s mind.

No one else heard it.

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Hidden Requirement Fulfilled

…

The notification vanished before another line appeared.

Aidan didn’t react.

Above him…

The cathedral stood.

The city survived.

Lucien Vale was dead.

Yet as the Inner Circle looked down at the lone figure standing among the ashes…

Not one of them believed they could bring Aidan Darkheart back anymore.
