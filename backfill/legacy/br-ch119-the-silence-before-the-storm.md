Days passed.

No emergencies.

No monsters.

No hidden catacombs.

For the first time in months...

Life almost felt ordinary.

Almost.

---

The Crimson Circle had begun meeting every Friday night at Malik's warehouse.

What had once been a cursed ruin was slowly becoming something else.

A home.

Old couches lined one wall.

Weapons rested on handmade racks.

Ancient books sat beside pizza boxes.

Lyra rolled her eyes.

"You seriously left your sword next to the microwave."

Malik shrugged.

"It fits."

"It absolutely does not."

"It spiritually fits."

Sienna laughed from across the room.

"I've known you for two weeks..."

"...and somehow that makes perfect sense."

Everyone laughed.

Even Aidan.

The sound echoed through the warehouse.

For a brief moment...

He forgot about curses.

Forgot about bloodlines.

Forgot about destiny.

He was just...

Happy.

---

Across the room, the Guardian watched quietly.

He didn't interrupt.

He simply smiled.

Because this...

This was what Zee had always fought to protect.

Not kingdoms.

Not power.

Moments like this.
