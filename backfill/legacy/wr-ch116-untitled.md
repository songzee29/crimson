The disciplined vampires attacked without a single battle cry.

They moved like soldiers.

Pairs.

Trios.

Each position supported another.

They had prepared for this fight.

Aidan met the first attacker head-on.

The vampire’s blade flashed toward his neck.

Aidan caught the wrist.

The bone shattered in his grip.

Before the body hit the floor, he seized another by the throat and threw him through a stone pillar.

The chamber erupted into chaos.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian intercepted two vampires rushing Rowan.

Steel rang through the cellar.

These opponents adapted.

They feinted.

Retreated.

Forced openings for one another.

“They’ve trained together!” Malik shouted as golden runes burst across the floor.

A wave of crimson fire answered him.

The runes exploded apart.

“They know witchcraft!” Malik growled.

“No,” Rowan corrected as he deflected another spell.

“They know how to fight witches.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra crashed into a vampire trying to flank Elias.

Her claws tore across his chest.

The wound healed almost instantly.

Without hesitation, she ripped his head free.

This time…

He stayed down.

“Remove the head!” she shouted.

“They’re not like the Guardians!”

The Inner Circle adjusted immediately.

Every movement became faster.

Sharper.

Years of surviving together showed once again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan stood at the center of the room.

Three elite vampires surrounded him.

One smiled.

“We finally found you.”

Aidan’s expression never changed.

“You’ve been looking.”

“For a very long time.”

They attacked together.

The first lost an arm.

The second lost his jaw.

The third landed a kick powerful enough to drive Aidan through a stone wall.

Dust filled the air.

For the first time in days…

Aidan laughed.

A short, quiet laugh.

He rose slowly from the rubble.

Blood trickled from the corner of his mouth.

He wiped it away.

“You hit harder.”

The predator’s smile widened.

“Good.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco watched from across the battlefield.

“He’s enjoying this.”

Rowan answered without taking his eyes off the fight.

“No.”

“He enjoys finally needing to try.”

Draco wasn’t sure that was any better.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A vampire lunged toward Malik from behind.

Before anyone could warn him—

Aidan appeared.

One hand pierced straight through the attacker’s chest.

He pulled his arm free.

The body collapsed.

Malik looked up.

“You saved me.”

Aidan didn’t even look at him.

“You were necessary.”

Then he vanished back into the battle.

Malik stood frozen.

Necessary.

Not friend.

Not family.

Necessary.

The word lingered longer than the battle itself.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The elite vampire who had first spoken stepped backward.

He studied Aidan carefully.

“So it’s true.”

“The humanity is gone.”

For the first time…

His confidence faded.

“We’re too late.”

He immediately turned.

“Retreat!”

Every surviving vampire withdrew at once.

No hesitation.

No panic.

Only discipline.

Within seconds…

The cellar stood empty.

Cassian lowered his swords.

“Why would they run now?”

Draco looked toward Aidan.

“They came to confirm something.”

“What?”

Draco’s voice grew quieter.

“They wanted to see whether Aidan was still a man…”

“…or had finally become the monster they were waiting for.”
