As they prepared to leave...

Malik frowned.

"...That's weird."

"What?"

"There are four chairs."

Lyra looked down.

"...So?"

Malik pointed.

"We only asked for three."

Aidan looked.

He was right.

A fourth plate.

A fourth glass.

A fourth chair.

Already set.

Like someone had been expected.

The waitress walked over.

"Oh."

She looked genuinely confused.

"I could've sworn someone else was with you."

Silence.

Aidan felt a chill run through him.

The System appeared.

Aidan said nothing.

Neither did Lyra.

Malik simply shrugged.

"Weird."

He grabbed a mint from the table.

"Free candy."

"You're impossible."

Outside...

Across the street...

The silver-haired woman watched from beneath an umbrella.

She smiled to herself.

"So..."

"The Circle begins."

Then she walked away before anyone noticed she had ever been there.
