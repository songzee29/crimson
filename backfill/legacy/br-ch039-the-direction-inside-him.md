The subway room felt smaller than it was.

Not physically.

Perceptually.

Like the air itself was leaning toward Aidan.

Lyra saw it too now.

The change wasn’t loud. It wasn’t dramatic.

It was *attention.*

Aidan stood still, eyes unfocused, as if something far away had tugged a thread inside him and he was trying to decide whether to follow it.

“What did you see?” Lyra asked again, more carefully this time.

Aidan blinked once.

“I don’t know how to explain it,” he said.

“Try.”

A pause.

Then:

“A woman.”

Lyra’s expression tightened immediately.

“And a child,” Aidan added.

Silence dropped between them.

Not empty silence.

Heavier silence.

Lyra took a slow step closer.

“Where?”

“I don’t know,” Aidan said. “It wasn’t a place. It felt like… something behind a place.”

The Witch Sight flickered again in his eyes.

Not activated.

Triggered.

The room distorted slightly at the edges, like reality was trying to decide whether it should open or close.

Lyra lowered her voice.

“Aidan… when you saw them, did you feel anything?”

He hesitated.

Longer than before.

Then:

“I felt like I should follow it.”

Lyra went still.

That was the first real break.

Not loss.

Not confusion.

**Pull.**

Something inside him still existed.

Just not fully formed.
