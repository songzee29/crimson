## 2. Author instructions that changed the opening

The key rule is to follow the author correction, not merely the most recent prose.

1. **Curse order reset:** Zee directly says the assistant forgot the Witch side/System must come first and orders a clean restart. Vampire comes later; Wolf follows through its own trigger.
2. **Physical activation:** Zee challenges the draft for announcing bloodline detection before Aidan has touched the book. Contact and blood must precede identification.
3. **Crimson color:** Zee corrects the black book. The intended object is crimson.
4. **Rez stays asleep:** Rez does not speak or become a conscious companion during the opening. His awakening is gated by the Vampire Curse.
5. **System privacy:** only Aidan can see/hear the System. Other characters may observe him looking or pausing, but must not see the interface.
6. **Book disappears after opening:** once Aidan opens the book, the physical object disappears; the System/Legacy relationship continues within Aidan. Later canon clarifies System and Legacy are distinct but joined.
7. **No generic leveling ladder:** the System is a living legacy interface with quests, records, abilities, rewards, and authority progression; it is not a generic level 1–100 menu.
8. **Evolution is not a menu choice:** Aidan's evolution follows emotional state and control, with the triggers/costs specified by the author.

## 4. The current opening rule set, derived from later author locks

This is the controlling checklist for classifying opening drafts, not a new decree:

- Current book color: **crimson**.
- Activation: Aidan physically touches the book; blood is obtained; activation follows.
- System: visible/audible to Aidan alone.
- Book: physical object disappears after it opens.
- Rez: dormant in the opening; no visible Rez; no premature speech.
- Curse sequence: Witch/System → Vampire after Aidan's first true death → Wolf, restrained by the sacred bond until the locked release condition.
- Rewards and abilities: System grants them during the appropriate stage; it later shifts toward blueprint/mastery behavior when the full tribrid state is reached.
- Source handling: every opening is retained under its original title, date, source ID, and track.

- Treating all June 11 Chapter 1s as rewrites of the exact same scene; they differ in setting, mechanics, and characterization.

- Treating the June 30 Blackwood Academy opening as the same manuscript as the June 11 subway opening.

- Claiming a chapter extraction is an exact full-response copy without checking the source body.
