No light filled the prison.

No explosion echoed through the earth.

Instead...

Every sound...

Stopped.

The dripping blood.

The cracking stone.

Even the wind.

Reality itself seemed to pause.

Waiting.

Watching.

Azrath took one final step.

Outside the prison.

Freedom.

He inhaled slowly.

"...I had forgotten..."

He closed his eyes.

"...what the world smelled like."

---

Then...

He looked upward.

Not toward the Veil.

Not toward the Sanctuary.

Toward New Orleans.

Toward one soul.

A smile slowly returned.

"...Crimson King."

Another pause.

"...Let's see..."

"...if your hope survives me."
