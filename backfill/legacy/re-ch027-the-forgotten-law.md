One final memory remained.

This time...

The throne wasn't empty.

Zee sat upon it.

Not as the legendary Crimson King.

Just...

A father.

He looked exhausted.

His crown rested beside him instead of on his head.

He held the Crimson Legacy in his hands.

Slowly closing it.

"I hope..."

His voice cracked.

"...I built enough."

He stared toward the empty hall.

As if he knew someone would one day stand where Aidan now stood.

"I don't know your face."

"I don't know your voice."

"I don't know whether you'll forgive me."

Aidan couldn't breathe.

Zee smiled sadly.

"But I know one thing."

He gently placed his hand over the closed Crimson Legacy.

"If this House survives..."

"...it won't be because of me."

"It'll be because someone finally becomes the king..."

"...I never could."

The room trembled.

Not because of power.

Because of love.

Then Zee said something Aidan would never forget.

> **"The greatest lie ever told about House Darkheart..."**

A long silence followed.

> **"...is that we were feared because we were monsters."**

His crimson eyes slowly lifted.

> **"No..."**

> **"We were feared..."**

Another pause.

> **"...because we judged monsters without becoming them."**

Silence.

Aidan felt tears running down his face.

Then...

Zee looked directly at him.

Not through time.

Not through memory.

At him.

As if he had known this moment would come.

> **"If you ever find yourself sitting where I once sat..."**

He glanced at the empty throne.

"...remember this."

A soft smile.

The kind only a father could give.

> **"The throne doesn't make the king."**

> **"The choices do."**

The memory ended.
