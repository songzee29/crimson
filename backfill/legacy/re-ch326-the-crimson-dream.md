That night...

Aidan dreamed again.

He stood inside the Hall of Kings.

Only...

It wasn't empty.

Every throne was occupied.

Not by kings.

By vampires.

Ancient.

Silent.

Watching him.

At the center...

The black throne remained empty.

One figure stood beside it.

Face hidden beneath a crimson hood.

It slowly raised one hand.

Not threatening.

Inviting.

Then it spoke.

Its voice sounded ancient.

Like thousands of years of hunger wrapped into a whisper.

> **"Why do you starve?"**

Aidan answered immediately.

"...Because I'm not you."

The figure laughed.

Softly.

Sadly.

Then replied...

> **"You misunderstand."**

It slowly pointed toward Aidan's chest.

> **"I am you."**

The dream shattered.
