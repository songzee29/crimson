That night...

Aidan entered the Crimson Archive alone.

Ancient books lined endless shelves.

The Legacy appeared before him.

Not as a command.

Not as a reward.

As a single page.

It slowly turned by itself.

A new heading appeared.

---

> **CURRENT ERA**

## **THE RETURN OF THE OLD WORLD**

---

> **History does not repeat itself.**

---

> **It remembers.**

---

The page closed.

Aidan stood in silence for a long time.

Then he reached for an old journal once written by Zee.

Not to become him.

But to understand the shoulders upon which House Darkheart had been built.

Outside...

A storm gathered beyond the horizon.

Not over House Darkheart.

Not over Black Hollow.

But somewhere between them.

History was drawing breath.

And the Second Judgment...

Had quietly begun.
