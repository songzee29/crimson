Aidan slowly approached.

Every step echoed.

The closer he came...

The heavier the air became.

Not threatening.

Expectant.

The throne wasn't asking him to sit.

It was...

Watching him.

Aidan reached out.

Before his fingers touched the armrest...

The room changed.

The walls disappeared.

The ceiling vanished.

The Hall dissolved into endless stars.

Then...

He wasn't alone anymore.

Twelve figures stood around him.

Transparent.

Ancient.

Each wearing a different crown.

Each carrying unimaginable authority.

None of them spoke.

Until...

The youngest among them stepped forward.

Zee.

Not the king history remembered.

Not the warrior.

Not the legend.

Just...

A father.

He smiled.

"...You've grown."

Aidan's breathing caught.

"...Dad?"

The illusion smiled again.

"I wish..."

"...I could answer."

"But this isn't me."

"It's only a memory."

Aidan lowered his head.

"...Figures."
