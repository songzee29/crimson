The celebration had ended.

The music was gone.

The banners still waved proudly above the Crimson Castle.

But...

Everything felt different.

Not because the kingdom had changed.

Because the world had.

The Crimson Legacy floated before him.

It no longer looked like the book he had first opened all those years ago.

The cover had changed.

The old crimson leather had become darker.

Ancient silver runes wrapped around its spine.

A faint crimson aura pulsed from every page.

It had evolved.

Just as he had.

---

Merchants opened their shops.

For the first time...

It was the heart of a growing civilization.
