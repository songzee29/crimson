Aidan suddenly grabbed his chest.

Pain exploded through his body.

Not the burning agony of vampire hunger.

Not the backlash of witchcraft.

Something deeper.

Every muscle tightened.

His bones ached.

His hearing sharpened until he could hear wolves howling from mountains over fifty miles away.

His sense of smell flooded with thousands of scents at once.

He dropped to one knee.

Rez was beside him instantly.

"Aidan!"

Then froze.

The Sacred Seal she had woven so many years ago...

Was shaking.

Not breaking.

Shaking.

Fine crimson cracks spread through the invisible magic wrapped around Aidan's soul.

The Wolf Authority...

Had begun pushing back.
