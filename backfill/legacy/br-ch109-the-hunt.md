Cassian ran.

Out of the cathedral.

Across empty streets.

Away from Azrath.

Away from everyone.

His senses had become monstrous.

Every heartbeat echoed like thunder.

Every pulse called his name.

He grabbed his own head.

"No..."

"I won't..."

The hunger answered.

A deer.

Hidden in the woods outside the city.

Its heartbeat.

Strong.

Alive.

Cassian moved without thinking.

One step.

Then another.

Then...

He disappeared.

The trees exploded apart behind him.

---

Moments later...

Silence returned.

The forest stood still.

One frightened bird flew into the night.

Then...

A scream.

Not human.

Animal.

Abruptly cut short.
