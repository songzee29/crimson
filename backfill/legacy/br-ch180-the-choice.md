Dust filled the cathedral.

Everyone scattered.

The massive crimson hand continued dragging its owner toward the surface.

The Judge charged forward alone.

His greatsword ignited with ancient crimson fire.

He looked back one final time.

Not at the Guardian.

Not at the Circle.

Only at Aidan.

Then he gave one final command.

> **"LIVE."**

He slammed his sword into the colossal arm.

The explosion that followed lit the entire cathedral like a second sun.

The shockwave hurled Aidan, Lyra, Malik, Sienna, and Elias into the underground river.

The current seized them instantly.

Everything became darkness.

The last thing Aidan saw before the water swallowed him...

Was the ancient Judge standing alone...

Holding back something so powerful that even Zee had chosen to leave it sleeping.

Then the river carried them away.

Far from the cathedral.

Far from the Second Seal.

Far from the truth.

As unconsciousness claimed him, one final notification flickered weakly across his vision.
