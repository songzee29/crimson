Cassian returned to the monastery.

The burned page was gone.

The room was exactly as he'd left it.

Except...

Someone was sitting in a chair near the window.

Reading.

Cassian's hand shot toward his sword.

"...Who are you?"

The man closed the book.

Smiled.

"You already know."

Cassian's heartbeat stopped.

"...Azrath."

The man sighed dramatically.

"I've missed hearing my own name."

He stood.

Perfect posture.

Perfect suit.

Perfect smile.

"I owe you my freedom."

Cassian couldn't move.

"You..."

"...were the voice?"

Azrath nodded.

"For a little while."

Cassian's grip tightened.

"You manipulated me."

Azrath tilted his head.

"Did I?"

Silence.

"I never asked you to envy Aidan."

"I never asked you to kill Elias."

"I never asked you to hate."

Every sentence landed like a blade.

"I simply..."

"...listened."

Cassian said nothing.

Because every word was true.
