The messenger held out the scroll.

Aidan accepted it.

The wax seal dissolved the moment his fingers touched it.

Only one sentence waited inside.

Written in impossibly ancient handwriting.

---

> **"The throne has remained empty long enough."**

---

Nothing else.

No signature.

No explanation.

The paper immediately crumbled into ash.

The messenger slowly stood.

"My duty is complete."

Aidan stopped him.

"Wait."

"Who is the First Vampire?"

The messenger smiled sadly.

"I have never seen him."

"No one has."

"Then how did you receive the message?"

"I woke up."

He turned toward the city gates.

"And the words were already written inside my mind."

Before anyone could ask another question...

The messenger collapsed.

Not dead.

Gone.

His body dissolved into crimson mist.

The wind carried it away.

Leaving behind only one thing.

A single drop of blood.

It landed on the stone street.

Aidan stared at it.

His throat suddenly tightened.

For one impossible heartbeat...

Every instinct inside him screamed:

**Drink it.**

His fingers twitched.

His pupils narrowed.

The Witch Authority flared across both hands so violently that crimson runes burst into the air around him.

Lyra immediately noticed.

"Aidan?"

He stepped backward.

"No."

His voice sounded strained.

"No..."

The drop evaporated before anyone else could touch it.

Aidan closed his fist until his knuckles turned white.

He had won.

But...

It hadn't felt like victory.

---

That night...

Alone...

The Journal opened.

Not in front of Aidan.

Inside the Crimson Legacy itself.

Where no human eyes could read.

It wrote three sentences.

> **Operation: Rise of the King is exceeding all projections.**

A second line appeared.

> **The Witch Authority has reached its intended pinnacle.**

Then...

For the first time in hundreds of years...

The Journal hesitated before writing the final line.

> **Beginning observation...**

> **...of the Fall.**

The ink stopped.

Far beneath the Hall of Kings...

A chain snapped.

Not loudly.

Softly.

Like the sound of someone quietly unlocking a door from the inside.

And in the darkness...

Rez turned in his sleep again.

His brow tightened.

His lips moved.

No sound came out.

But the readers would know exactly what he was trying to say.

He was still fighting.

Still reaching.

Still trying to get back to Aidan...

Before whatever was waking on the other side of the seal reached him first.
