The Guardian entered quietly.

"My King."

Aidan turned.

"The eastern villages have completed their reconstruction."

"The southern covens have sworn loyalty."

"The northern watch reports no Veil activity."

One by one...

The reports continued.

Not battles.

Not emergencies.

Progress.

Aidan listened to every report.

Not because he had to.

Because every village...

Every family...

Every citizen...

Was now his responsibility.

When the meeting ended, the Guardian smiled faintly.

"You've become a ruler."

Aidan answered,

"I'm still learning."

The Guardian nodded.

"A king who believes he's finished learning..."

"...is already beginning to fall."
