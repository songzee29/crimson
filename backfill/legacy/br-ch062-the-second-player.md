That evening...

Aidan walked alone through Jackson Square.

The little girl from before ran over carrying another drawing.

"I made you something!"

Aidan smiled.

"What is it?"

She proudly held it up.

It showed House Darkheart standing beneath a crimson moon.

Below it she had written:

**HOME**

Aidan knelt.

"This is beautiful."

She grinned.

"My grandpa says a house isn't where you're born."

"It's where people choose you."

Aidan felt something tighten in his chest.

"...Your grandpa sounds wise."

"He is!"

She pointed behind her.

"He's right—"

She stopped.

Her smile faded.

"...Huh?"

There was no one there.

Only an empty bench.

Aidan looked around.

No elderly man.

No footprints.

Nothing.

Just a black feather resting where someone had been sitting.

He picked it up.

Cold.

Unnaturally cold.
