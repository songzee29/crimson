Cassian met Azrath again beneath the abandoned cathedral.

He slammed his fist against the stone wall.

"You lied."

Azrath tilted his head.

"About?"

"You said strength would matter."

Azrath smiled.

"I never said that."

Cassian froze.

Azrath stepped closer.

"I said..."

"...hope matters."

He pointed toward New Orleans.

"So tell me..."

"...what's stronger?"

"A king..."

"...or a kingdom that stops believing in him?"

Cassian looked toward the city lights.

For the first time...

He understood the game.

Azrath wasn't trying to defeat Aidan.

He was trying to make the world reject him.
