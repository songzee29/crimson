The old theater had collapsed.

Half the roof had caved in.

Flames reached toward the night sky.

Aidan arrived seconds later.

"Elias!"

From beneath the rubble—

"I'm down here!"

Aidan dropped to his knees.

Crimson Threads shot into the debris, searching for gaps.

Then...

He found him.

Pinned beneath a massive support beam.

Alive.

Barely.

Aidan immediately began lifting the beam.

It wouldn't move.

More Threads.

More magic.

Nothing.

The beam groaned but refused to lift.

Elias looked at him and smiled.

"...You look terrible."

Aidan laughed despite himself.

"Shut up."

"I probably should."

"You definitely should."

For one brief second...

It almost felt normal again.

Then another explosion shook the building.

Dust rained from above.

Elias looked toward the ceiling.

"...We've got about thirty seconds."

Aidan pushed harder.

Veins stood out across his neck.

The beam finally moved...

An inch.

Then stopped.

"No..."

The Witch Authority surged again.

The System appeared.

Aidan ignored it.

"Elias!"

"I'm trying!"

"I know."

Another explosion.

The ceiling cracked.

Elias reached out.

Grabbing Aidan's wrist.

"Listen to me."

"No."

"Listen."

"I SAID NO!"

Elias's voice remained calm.

"You remember what I told you?"

Aidan's eyes filled with tears.

"...Don't."

"You can't save everyone."

"I CAN SAVE YOU!"

Silence.

Then Elias smiled.

"...You still don't get it."

Aidan shook his head violently.

"No..."

"My job..."

"...was never to be saved."

"It was to make sure..."

"...you kept becoming the man I knew you could be."

The words struck harder than any blade.
