Broken.

Glitching.

Unable to stabilize.

Cassian remained standing.

His sword hung loosely at his side.

For the first time since becoming Commander of the Veil...

He lowered his head.

Not in defeat.

In mourning.

His eyes remained fixed on Elias.

His older brother.

The man who had taught him to throw a baseball.

The man who used to shield him from bullies.

The man who used to carry him home after he scraped his knees.

Gone.

Because of him.

---

Aidan finally spoke.

Quietly.

"You don't deserve to carry him."

Cassian looked up.

Aidan stepped forward.

Every Veil hunter instinctively raised a weapon.

The Witch Authority answered.

Hundreds of crimson runes ignited beneath the street.

Every weapon in the hunters' hands began vibrating violently.

One by one...

They lowered them.

Not by choice.

By instinct.

No one wanted to challenge whatever Aidan had become in that moment.
