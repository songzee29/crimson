The Circle immediately split apart.

There wasn't a choice.

Too many people.

Too many fires.

Too many monsters.

Aidan looked at Elias.

For a brief moment...

Neither of them spoke.

They both knew.

This battle would change everything.

Elias smiled.

The same warm smile he'd worn since the day they met.

"Race you."

Aidan couldn't help laughing.

"You're impossible."

"I know."

Then Elias took off running...

Straight toward the largest fire.

Aidan ran the opposite direction...

Toward the sound of screaming children.

Neither looked back.

Because both believed...

They would see each other again before sunrise.

---

Far above the burning city...

The Smiling Man stood atop St. Louis Cathedral.

Watching the flames spread across New Orleans.

He quietly moved another chess piece.

Then whispered...

> **"The first domino has fallen."**

---

The Journal, resting back at the warehouse, opened on its own.

Ink spread across a fresh page.

Slowly.

Painfully.

As if the words themselves didn't want to be written.

---

> **The greatest curses...**

A pause.

Another line appeared.

> **...do not begin with hatred.**

The final line formed.

> **They begin with loss.**

The page stopped writing.

And somewhere in the city...

Aidan felt it.

Not through the System.

Not through magic.

Just...

A feeling in his chest.

Like the world had taken one deep breath...

Before screaming.

**The Witch Curse had not broken yet.**

But for the first time...

It was close enough that destiny could almost touch it.
