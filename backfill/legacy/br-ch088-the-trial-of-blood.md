A low rumble shook the chamber.

The stone floor split apart.

Crimson light erupted from beneath the cracks.

The crowns began floating into the air.

One by one.

Then...

They melted.

Liquid metal flowed across the floor, gathering into a single enormous figure.

A knight.

Nearly ten feet tall.

Forged entirely from crimson steel.

Its helmet had no face.

Only darkness.

It drew a massive sword.

The blade scraped across the stone, sending sparks into the air.

Then it spoke.

Its voice echoed from everywhere at once.

---

Malik looked around desperately.

"...Can we vote for leaving?"

The knight raised its sword.

"No."

The answer was immediate.

Then it charged.

The entire chamber shook.

Aidan pushed Malik aside just before the sword carved a trench through solid stone.

The impact threw everyone off their feet.

"So that's a no..." Malik muttered.

Aidan raised both hands.

Crimson circles ignited beneath the knight.

**Crimson Shackles!**

Dozens of cursed chains erupted from the ground.

Wrapping around the giant's legs.

The knight stopped.

For exactly one second.

Then—

The chains exploded.

As though they had never existed.

Aidan stared.

"It..."

"...broke them."

The knight swung again.

The world blurred.

Only to realize it had already turned around.

The sword slammed into his ribs.

Pain exploded through his body.

He crashed through three stone pillars.

Blood filled his mouth.

"Aidan!"
