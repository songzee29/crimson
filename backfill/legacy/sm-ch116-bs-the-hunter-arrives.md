## Chapter One Hundred Sixteen — The Hunter Arrives

No one noticed him.

No one remembered seeing him pass.

His black eyes studied every face.

Children.

Parents.

Workers.

Students.

He ignored them all.

Then...

He stopped.

Across the street...

Aidan laughed at something Malik had said as they left the academy gym.

The Hunter watched silently.

So...

That's him.

Not impressive.

Instead...

He felt almost nothing.

The Heir looked completely human.

That would make measuring him much easier.

Not yet...

I will wait.

A King protects his people.

Only his people?

Anyone who needs protection.

The Guardian remained expressionless.

Then what separates a King from an ordinary good man?

Aidan fell silent.

The river flowed gently beside them.

Minutes passed.

Finally...

Responsibility.

The Guardian's eyes softened.

Explain.

A good man chooses to help when he can.

A King accepts responsibility even when helping costs him everything.

Now...

It's a burden accepted willingly.
