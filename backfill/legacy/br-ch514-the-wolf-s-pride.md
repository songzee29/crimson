Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf sat quietly.

Watching.

It wasn't looking at Aidan.

It was watching the others.

Rez refusing to fall.

Lyra refusing to give up.

Kael protecting Mara.

Darius protecting everyone.

Even Cassian standing against the darkness.

The Wolf slowly smiled.

> **"...This..."**

> **"...is a true pack."**

It looked toward Aidan.

> **"...I once believed..."**

> **"...power created loyalty."**

Another heartbeat echoed.

> **"...You have taught me..."**

> **"...that loyalty creates power."**

Another crack spread gently across the Sacred Seal.

Not from violence.

From trust.

The Wolf no longer wished to escape.

It wished...

To stand beside its king.
