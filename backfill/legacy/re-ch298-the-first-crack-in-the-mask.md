> **Specimen 001**

The oldest vampire ever captured.

Without opening his eyes.

"...You're the one..."

Cassian frowned.

"The one what?"

"...Who smells like the King's grief."

"I don't know what you're talking about."

"You will."

> **"Tell the Crimson King..."**
