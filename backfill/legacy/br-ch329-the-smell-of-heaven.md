The Guardian led the Circle to an abandoned monastery.

The Veil believed an ancient relic had awakened.

Inside...

Everything felt wrong.

No darkness.

No curses.

Only silence.

An old priest stepped from the shadows.

His beard reached his waist.

His eyes were completely white.

He looked at Aidan...

And immediately began crying.

Not from fear.

From pity.

"...Poor child..."

Aidan frowned.

"Do you know me?"

The priest nodded slowly.

"I know what follows you."

"What?"

The old man's trembling hand pointed behind Aidan.

"...Hunger."

The entire room became cold.

The priest continued.

"I buried the last Crimson King."

The Guardian's eyes widened.

"You knew Zee?"

The priest smiled sadly.

"I knew the man..."

"...before the monster."

Silence swallowed the room.
