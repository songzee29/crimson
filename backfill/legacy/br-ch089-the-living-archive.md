The celebrations throughout House Darkheart continued long into the night.

The Queen had returned.

The kingdom rejoiced.

But deep beneath the castle...

Inside the Crimson Archive...

Only Aidan stood before the ancient pedestal where the Crimson Legacy rested.

Rez remained quietly at his side.

Neither of them spoke.

The Living Archive slowly opened.

Its pages turned without haste.

Not to grant power.

Not to unlock an ability.

To record history.

Crimson words began writing themselves across the page.

---

# **📜 CONSTITUTIONAL RECORD**

## **Authority & System Constitutional Rule**

**The Crimson Legacy is not the source of the Crimson King's power.**

It is the **Living Archive of House Darkheart.**

Its purpose is to:

- Record.
- Preserve.
- Judge.
- Document.

The journey of the Crimson King.

His Authorities.

His Crimson Laws.

His Blood Arts.

His Evolutions.

His Ascensions.

And every achievement yet to come.

---

The pages continued writing.

---

## **Constitution Confirmed**

**The System never activates the Crimson King's powers.**

**Only Aidan Darkheart does.**

The Living Archive merely witnesses what the King creates.

It does not create it.

---

Another page turned.

---

# **The Three Authorities**

### **Crimson Witch Authority**

The Crimson King shapes Crimson Laws through his own will.

His hands form the Law.

His voice declares the decree.

Only after the Law exists...

The Crimson Legacy records it.

---

### **Crimson Vampire Authority**

This Authority requires no command.

Speed.

Strength.

Regeneration.

Blood Arts.

Blood manipulation.

Every instinct of the vampire flows naturally through the Crimson King.

The Legacy simply records what has already occurred.

---

### **Crimson Wolf Authority**

The Wolf does not command.

The Wolf guides.

It sharpens instinct.

Awareness.

Senses.

The hunt.

Combat.

The decision...

Always belongs to Aidan.

---

The Living Archive became brighter.

Another page slowly revealed itself.

---

# **First Crimson Ascension**

When the Crimson King reaches the **First Crimson Ascension**...

The Three Authorities shall no longer function as separate active powers.

They shall become...

**One Crimson Existence.**

From that moment forward...

There shall be no visible distinction between:

- Witch.
- Vampire.
- Wolf.

No different colors.

No different energies.

No different signatures.

Every ability...

Every technique...

Every Law...

Every Blood Art...

Every instinct...

Shall manifest as one.

**Crimson.**

The world will no longer know which Authority the Crimson King is using.

Because there will no longer be three.

There will simply be...

**Crimson.**

---

The final page wrote itself.

---

> **Historical Notice**

> **The Living Archive will continue preserving the Three Authorities separately for the history of House Darkheart.**

> **The Crimson King, however, shall no longer experience them as separate powers after the First Crimson Ascension.**

> **He shall simply command...**

## **Crimson.**

---

The pages closed.

The Archive became silent once more.

Aidan rested his hand upon the cover.

"So that's what this place truly is."

Rez smiled.

"It always was."

"It was never trying to make you stronger."

"It was making sure..."

"...that no one could ever rewrite your story."

Aidan nodded.

He finally understood.

The Crimson Legacy had never made kings.

It simply made certain...

That history remembered them.

The crimson glow slowly faded.

The Living Archive had fulfilled another constitutional record.

And the next chapter of House Darkheart...

Would be written not by the Archive...

But by the Crimson King himself.
