The bridge finished rising.

It stretched into absolute darkness.

No end.

No light.

Only crimson mist.

The System remained silent.

No quest.

No objective.

No warning.

Elias walked beside Aidan.

"What are you thinking?"

Aidan didn't answer immediately.

Then...

"I think he's alive."

"And if he isn't?"

Aidan looked at the bridge.

"...Then I need to know."

Lyra stepped forward.

"We're coming."

"No."

She frowned.

"What?"

"If this is a trap..."

"I won't risk all of you."

Malik folded his arms.

"You know that's not how this works."

Sienna nodded.

"We're already involved."

Elias smiled.

"You still haven't figured it out."

Aidan looked at him.

"Figured out what?"

Elias laughed.

"You're not walking this road by yourself anymore."

One by one...

They stepped beside him.

Lyra.

Malik.

Sienna.

Elias.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody needed to.

They had already made their decision.

The Journal quietly opened in Aidan's hand.

New ink slowly appeared.

---

> **A king who walks alone may reach his destination...**

The sentence paused.

Another line formed beneath it.

> **...but a king who inspires others changes the journey itself.**

The pages stopped moving.

Aidan stared at the words.

Then slowly smiled.

Not because he felt stronger.

Because...

For the first time since finding the Crimson Book...

He no longer felt alone.

He looked at his family.

Then toward the endless crimson bridge.

He took the first step.

The others followed without hesitation.

Far ahead, hidden within the darkness, two crimson eyes slowly opened.

Watching.

Waiting.

Patient.

The Second Seal had not merely awakened.

It had been waiting centuries...

For the Heir to finally choose **his people over his fear.**

And somewhere far above, the Veil captain quietly looked toward the New Orleans skyline and whispered into the wind:

> **"Keep walking, Darkheart..."**

> **"...the world is almost ready to meet you."**

The storm was no longer approaching.

It had begun.
