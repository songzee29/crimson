Azrath slowly walked past him.

Hands behind his back.

Like a professor teaching a lesson.

"You want something."

Cassian didn't answer.

Azrath smiled.

"No..."

"You've always wanted something."

He stopped at the broken window.

Looking toward the city.

"You don't want revenge."

"You don't even want justice."

Another pause.

"You want to stand..."

"...where he stands."

Cassian whispered,

"...I deserve it."

Azrath smiled wider.

"There it is."

The truth.

---

"You think the Crimson Legacy chose the wrong king."

Cassian remained silent.

Silence...

Was answer enough.

---

Azrath looked back.

His smile vanished for the first time.

"If I offered you the strength..."

"...to prove it..."

"...would you take it?"

Cassian looked him directly in the eyes.

"...Yes."

Not hesitation.

Not fear.

Just certainty.
