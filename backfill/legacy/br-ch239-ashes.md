After everyone had left...

Only the Circle remained.

The sun was beginning to set.

Aidan stepped forward.

He placed Elias' firefighter badge on top of the casket.

"You always said..."

His voice was rough from silence.

"...someone had to run toward the fire."

A faint smile crossed his face.

"You idiot."

A tear fell onto the polished wood.

"You never stopped."

He rested one hand on the casket.

Then stepped back.

The funeral pyre was lit.

Orange flames climbed into the evening sky.

No crimson.

No magic.

Just ordinary fire.

Exactly how Elias would've wanted it.

The Circle watched until nothing remained but glowing embers.
