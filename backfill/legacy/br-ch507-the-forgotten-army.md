The ash before them suddenly shifted.

Thousands of figures slowly rose from the ground.

Not skeletons.

Not ghosts.

People.

Men.

Women.

Children.

All wandering aimlessly.

Blank expressions covered every face.

The little boy gasped.

"...Mom?"

A woman slowly turned toward him.

She stared blankly.

No recognition.

No love.

Nothing.

She didn't know who he was.

The boy fell to his knees crying.

The Ash King's Dominion hadn't simply taken people.

It had stolen...

Their identities.
