That night...

Cassian entered the ruins of his home alone.

He dug through the ashes.

Looking.

Searching.

For anything.

Hours later...

He found a small wooden box.

Inside...

A single ring.

Untouched by the fire.

His partner's promise.

His future.

Everything he had rebuilt...

Reduced to one object.

Cassian fell to his knees.

For the first time in centuries...

He cried.

Not for the house.

For the peace that died with it.

---

Inside his soul...

The Darkheart Brand pulsed.

Not with anger.

With memory.

Aidan's final words echoed again.

> **"You shall never know peace..."**

Cassian screamed into the empty night.
