# Chapter Seventy-Six: Between

The Great Hall stood silent.

Every candle leaned toward the same point.

Aidan's body never moved.

No breath stirred his chest.

No heartbeat answered Helena's earlier examination.

No trace of Witch magic remained around him.

He lay exactly as he had when Blackwood carried him home.

Dead.

Yet...

Something was wrong.

Rowan slowly stepped back toward the bier.

The others remained outside the closed doors.

He placed two fingers against Aidan's wrist.

Nothing.

He closed his eyes and reached with every thread of perception he possessed.

Nothing.

No life.

No death.

Only...

Stillness.

Ancient.

Absolute.

His hand slowly withdrew.

A faint crimson shimmer crossed Aidan's vision for less than a heartbeat.

Then vanished.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Observation Recorded.

Death Condition...

Incomplete.

Access Restricted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The words disappeared before they could be understood.

Aidan never opened his eyes.

...

Outside the Great Hall...

Cassian paced alone beneath the covered walkway.

Rain began falling again.

He welcomed it.

Anything to drown out the silence.

Rowan stepped through the doors behind him.

"What is it?"

The old master did not answer immediately.

Finally...

"He hasn't crossed."

Cassian frowned.

"What does that mean?"

"I don't know."

"You always know."

"No."

Rowan looked toward the closed doors.

"This time..."

His voice was quieter than Cassian had ever heard.

"...I don't."

...

Hours passed.

Nothing changed.

The candles continued burning.

The silence continued growing heavier.

Blackwood mourned.

No miracle came.

No healing answered.

No spell responded.

The academy slowly accepted what it had already believed.

Their Crimson Witch was gone.

Only Cassian couldn't leave.

Every time he walked away from the Great Hall...

He turned back.

Every time.

As though something inside him refused to abandon the place.

He hated the feeling.

He couldn't explain it.

...

Near dawn...

The silence broke.

Not outside.

Inside.

One breath.

Weak.

Impossible.

Cassian froze.

He looked toward the closed doors.

Another breath.

Then stillness again.

He shoved the doors open.

The Great Hall remained empty.

Candles burned quietly.

Aidan had not moved.

Yet...

Cassian knew what he had heard.

He stepped closer.

"Aidan..."

No answer.

He reached for his friend's hand.

Cold.

Then—

Aidan's fingers closed weakly around his wrist.

Cassian's heart stopped.

Crimson-gold eyes opened only halfway.

Clouded.

Distant.

Barely aware.

They searched the room without recognition.

Then...

Found Cassian.

For one fragile moment...

Recognition returned.

Aidan tried to speak.

Nothing came.

Blood touched the corner of his lips.

His body trembled once.

Then stilled.

Cassian leaned closer.

"I'm here."

Aidan looked directly into his eyes.

He understood.

Not everything.

Only enough.

Enough to know who stood beside him.

Enough to know what had to happen.

His voice emerged as little more than a whisper.

"Cassian..."

Tears blurred Cassian's vision.

"Don't."

Aidan's eyes never left his.

He could no longer lift his hand.

Could no longer move.

Only speak.

Very slowly.

Very clearly.

"By the Final Decree... of House Darkheart..."

The candles darkened.

Every flame turned crimson.

"You shall... never know peace..."

The words seemed to echo far beyond the Great Hall.

"...for your soul... shall never outrun... my judgment."

Silence.

The final word settled over the hall like falling ash.

Cassian closed his eyes.

He understood.

Not why.

Not how.

Only that Aidan had entrusted him with an impossible burden.

His hand shook as it rested against Aidan's chest.

"I'm sorry."

It was the only thing he had left to say.

Aidan's expression never changed.

There was no anger.

Only exhausted acceptance.

He had spent every piece of himself protecting everyone else.

There was nothing left to spend.

Cassian drew one slow breath.

Then completed the final act.

Aidan's body relaxed.

The faint awareness in his eyes disappeared.

The last thread holding him between life and death was severed.

True silence filled the Great Hall.

For one endless heartbeat...

The world itself seemed to stop.

Then—

Deep within the darkness beyond thought...

Something ancient opened its eyes.
