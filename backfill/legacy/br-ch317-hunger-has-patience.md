The next morning...

The Circle investigated another vampire nest.

Everything seemed ordinary.

Until Aidan entered.

Every vampire froze.

One by one...

They knelt.

Not out of fear.

Reverence.

An ancient instinct older than memory.

One old vampire began crying.

Actual tears.

He looked at Aidan...

Like someone seeing the sun after centuries of darkness.

His voice shook.

> **"...Our King..."**

Aidan stepped backward.

"I am not your king."

The old vampire smiled sadly.

"No..."

"...not yet."

Before anyone could question him...

His body turned to ash.

The smile remained on his face until the end.
