Then...

Something impossible happened.

A single drop of Aidan's blood fell onto Elias' chest.

Not because Aidan cast a spell.

Because his trembling hands couldn't stop shaking.

The Witch Authority surged.

The cracked Vampire Seal answered.

Not fully.

Just...

Enough.

The collision created a pulse.

No one saw it.

No one felt it.

Except...

Deep within the Crimson Legacy.

A thread of crimson light reached into the endless darkness.

Searching.

Searching...

Searching...

Until it found a sleeping soul.

Rez.

Still unconscious.

Still dreaming.

The thread gently wrapped around him.

Not pulling him free.

Just...

Connecting him.

Then the thread shot back toward reality.

It struck an invisible point beside Elias' body.

The air twisted.

Reality folded inward.

Magic condensed.

Flesh.

Bone.

Blood.

A form.

Not Elias.

Not Rez.

An empty body.

A vessel.

It collapsed onto the rain-soaked pavement.

Lifeless.

Unmoving.

The System completely froze.

The words glitched beyond recognition.

No classification appeared.

No explanation.

No quest.

No reward.

Nothing.

Because...

The Crimson Legacy had just witnessed something it had no language for.

---

The crimson thread remained.

One end tied to Aidan's soul.

One end tied to Rez's sleeping soul.

And between them...

The silent Witch Hybrid vessel.

Unfinished.

Waiting.

As thunder rolled over New Orleans, the Journal back at the warehouse opened one final time.

Ink spread across a fresh page without a hand touching it.

Only one sentence appeared.

> **"On this night, grief rewrote destiny."**

The page closed.

The city would never be the same again.
