One month later...

New Orleans had begun healing.

The buildings could be rebuilt.

The streets could be cleaned.

But names...

Names were harder to repair.

People recognized him.

Not because they knew his face.

Because they remembered the night crimson lightning split the heavens.

The night one young man carried another through a burning city.

Not sad.

Just...

Different.

Again.

"The smoke detector likes you too."
