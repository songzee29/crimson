The crimson light slowly faded.

Silence.

No one moved.

No one breathed.

Even the wind refused to disturb the battlefield.

Then...

A single drop of blood struck the stone.

**...Drip.**

Every eye turned toward Valerius.

A deep crimson slash stretched diagonally across his chest.

Not fatal.

Clean.

Perfect.

He slowly looked down.

Then...

Laughed.

"...You truly did it..."

Across from him...

Aidan remained standing.

Barely.

His right arm hung uselessly.

His chest had been pierced straight through.

One knee threatened to buckle.

Royal Regeneration fought desperately to keep him alive.

Blood poured from his body.

Yet...

He never looked away from the First Blood Sovereign.
