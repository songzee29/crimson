The obsidian door slowly disappeared back into the stone.

Not because Aidan had failed.

Because he had passed.

The chamber trembled softly before becoming still once again.

Lyra let out a long breath.

"I don't think I've ever been happier to walk away from a door."

Malik laughed weakly.

"I don't think I've ever been happier to still be alive."

Aidan smiled...

Then winced.

His ribs reminded him the fight wasn't free.

The System quietly appeared.

Aidan frowned.

"Fourteen percent?"

He almost laughed.

"I nearly died..."

The System answered.

---

---

Malik noticed him staring ahead again.

"The thing again?"

Aidan nodded.

"It just told me I only fought at fourteen percent."

Malik blinked.

"...You almost died at fourteen percent?"

Lyra folded her arms.

"I'm choosing to find that encouraging."

Aidan wasn't.

If fourteen percent looked like this...

What would one hundred percent become?
