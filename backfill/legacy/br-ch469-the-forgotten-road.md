Night fell.

Aidan, Cassian, Rez, Lyra, Kael, Mara, and Darius rode together beyond Black Hollow's northern border.

For the first time...

Aidan's newest companions rode beside the old generation.

Kael scanned the tree line constantly, hand on the hilt of his blade.

Mara quietly traced protective runes into the air, reinforcing the group against unseen magic.

Darius rode at the rear, making sure no one became separated.

Hours passed.

Then...

The road simply...

Appeared.

Old stone.

Covered in vines.

None of Black Hollow's scouts had ever reported it.

Cassian whispered...

"...I've searched this forest a hundred times."

The Wolf answered from within Aidan.

> **"...You searched with vampire eyes..."**

> **"...Not wolf eyes."**
