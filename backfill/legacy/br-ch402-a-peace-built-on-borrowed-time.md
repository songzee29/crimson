Spring finally reached Black Hollow.

For years...

Cassian had helped rebuild the forgotten valley.

Stone by stone.

Home by home.

The broken settlement had become a thriving coven.

Children raced through the streets chasing one another with wooden swords.

Young vampires trained in the courtyard.

Laughter echoed from every home.

The life Cassian had once believed impossible...

Now surrounded him.

He stood outside his house, watching it all.

His partner stepped beside him and gently rested their head against his shoulder.

"You did this."

Cassian smiled.

"...No."

"We all did."

For just a moment...

The emptiness faded.

He almost felt peace.

Almost.
