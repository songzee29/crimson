The next morning...

The Circle answered another distress call.

An old church outside New Orleans had become cursed.

Nothing unusual.

Until they arrived.

There were no bodies.

No blood.

No monster.

Only words.

Written across the altar in crimson.

> **THE CRIMSON KING CANNOT SAVE YOU.**

Malik looked around.

"...Who did this?"

No answer.

Aidan slowly raised his hand.

The Witch Authority spread across the room.

His fingers moved with practiced precision.

One word.

"Reveal."

The crimson writing vanished.

Beneath it...

Another message appeared.

Older.

As if the first had only been covering it.

> **HE WILL FAIL YOU...**
>
> **...LIKE EVERY KING BEFORE HIM.**

Lyra felt a chill.

"...That's not a curse."

The Guardian nodded.

"No."

His face darkened.

"...That's a challenge."
