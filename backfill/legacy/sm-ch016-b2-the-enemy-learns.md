# Chapter 16: The Enemy Learns

The academy did not return to normal.

No bells signaled the end of danger.

No celebration followed the battle beneath the mountain.

Instead...

Blackwood Academy became a fortress.

Students slept in rotating shelters closer to the central wards.

Faculty patrolled through every hour of the night.

Even meals were eaten in shifts.

The enemy had not broken the academy.

It had changed how everyone lived.

...

Three more tunnels were discovered before sunrise.

Not dug upward.

Around them.

"They're mapping us," Rowan said quietly.

The realization settled over every instructor.

"They know where our strongest wards are."

"They avoided them," Helena added.

"They're looking for weaknesses."

Cassian knelt beside one freshly carved tunnel.

His good hand brushed the stone.

"They've also stopped leaving tracks."

Aidan noticed something else.

The walls no longer carried random claw marks.

They formed deliberate patterns.

Not language.

Measurements.

Distances.

The creatures were recording the academy.

"They're studying us."

No one questioned him.

Not anymore.

...

The eastern patrol left before dawn.

Aidan insisted on going despite Helena's protests.

"You can barely breathe."

"I can still fight."

"That's not the same thing."

"No."

He tightened the straps around his ribs.

"It isn't."

The patrol moved silently through the forest.

No one spoke unless necessary.

Aidan walked in front.

Not because he wanted to.

Because his hearing reached farther than everyone else's.

A twig snapped.

Very faint.

Behind them.

His hand rose immediately.

Everyone froze.

Cassian slowly turned.

Nothing.

Seconds later...

A shadow darted between two trees.

Gone before anyone else fully saw it.

"It was watching," Malik muttered.

"It wanted us to see it," Aidan answered.

The words chilled the group.

...

Another shadow appeared.

Then another.

Never attacking.

Always retreating.

Leading.

"Don't follow," Rowan ordered.

"They're separating us."

The realization came too late.

A scream echoed through the woods.

Not ahead.

Behind.

They spun.

A younger patrol witch stumbled from the trees, bleeding from deep claw wounds.

"We were ambushed..."

She collapsed.

The creatures had never intended to attack Aidan's group.

They had used one patrol to draw another into a trap.

"They're learning," Lyra whispered.

"They already have."

...

The rescue force reached the ambush site minutes later.

Broken trees.

Shattered barriers.

Blood.

No bodies.

The surviving patrol members were gone.

Taken.

Not killed.

Aidan knelt beside a torn strip of academy robes.

Fresh blood stained the cloth.

His eyes fixed on it instantly.

The scent struck him like a wave.

Warm.

Fresh.

His stomach tightened.

His breathing slowed.

He hated himself for noticing.

Then—

Movement.

His head snapped left.

"Down!"

Everyone dropped.

A volley of sharpened stone spikes erupted from hidden trenches where they had been standing.

The traps had been placed while the creatures retreated.

They had expected pursuit.

Malik stared at the shattered trees.

"They planned for us."

Aidan looked toward the empty forest.

"They're beginning to think like hunters."

...

That night...

No one slept.

The missing patrol remained missing.

The academy's confidence had been wounded more deeply than its walls.

Aidan stood alone atop the eastern tower.

Below him...

Students huddled around lanterns.

Trying to pretend everything was alright.

The second heartbeat answered softly.

THUMP.

He closed his eyes.

Not to listen.

To remember.

Every battle.

Every mistake.

Every injury.

Every person he had almost failed.

When he opened them again...

He noticed something he hadn't before.

The eastern wall.

Two blind angles.

If he had seen them...

So had the enemy.

# Chapter 17: The Breaking Line

Blackwood moved before sunrise.

Not to attack.

To prevent another disappearance.

Under Aidan's direction, the eastern patrol routes changed completely.

No straight lines.

No predictable timing.

Every patrol overlapped another.

Every team remained within signal range.

"The creatures watched us yesterday," Rowan told the assembled guards.

"So today..."

He looked toward Aidan.

"...we make yesterday worthless."

The patrols departed.

Hours passed.

Nothing.

By midday even Malik began smiling.

"Maybe we finally—"

Aidan stopped walking.

His head turned toward the wind.

"No."

The scent reached him first.

Smoke.

Fresh earth.

Fear.

Blood.

Not much.

Enough.

"They're west."

Cassian frowned.

"How can you—"

The distant academy horn interrupted him.

One blast.

Then two.

Western wall.

They had ignored the east entirely.

"They watched us change formations," Rowan realized as they ran.

"They wanted us to reinforce the east."

"And they struck where we became weakest," Aidan finished.

By the time they reached the western perimeter...

Part of the outer wall had already collapsed.

Not from overwhelming force.

From beneath.

A tunnel had opened directly under the foundation.

Students scrambled to evacuate as stone continued breaking apart.

One of the creatures burst upward.

Not to fight.

To herd people.

A second emerged farther away.

Driving frightened apprentices toward the first.

"They're separating the students!" Lyra shouted.

Aidan's body moved instantly.

Not toward the closest creature.

Toward the trapped students.

Threat Assessment.

The greatest danger wasn't the enemy.

It was the collapsing wall above terrified children.

"Hold!"

The fractured battlements froze.

Hundreds of tons of stone hung motionless overhead.

His ribs screamed.

Blood seeped through his bandages.

The Law trembled.

He couldn't hold it forever.

"Move!"

Rowan and Helena rushed the students through the opening.

Malik and Cassian intercepted the advancing creatures.

Despite his damaged arm, Cassian fought with ruthless precision, forcing one creature away from the evacuation route.

The second creature never engaged Aidan.

Instead...

It circled behind him.

Waiting.

Watching.

Learning.

It had recognized he couldn't abandon the collapsing wall.

It attacked the one place he couldn't leave.

Lyra saw it first.

"Aidan!"

She threw herself between them.

The creature's claws ripped across her side.

She cried out as crimson spread across her robes.

Time stopped.

Aidan stared.

The scent of fresh blood reached him before the sound of her pain.

Everything narrowed.

The world became that single crimson stain.

The second heartbeat roared.

THUMP.

For one terrifying instant...

His feet started moving toward Lyra.

Not to help.

Toward the blood.

His fingers curled.

His breathing deepened.

Then he saw her eyes.

Not afraid of the creature.

Afraid for him.

The moment shattered.

"No."

The word escaped through clenched teeth.

With every ounce of will he tore his gaze away from the blood.

The suspended wall crashed down behind the last escaping student.

Dust swallowed the battlefield.

The creatures retreated into hidden tunnels before anyone could pursue.

Again.

They had achieved exactly what they wanted.

Not victory.

Knowledge.

From the drifting dust, Rowan looked at the collapsed western wall.

"They've stopped trying to defeat us."

Cassian bound his wounded arm tighter and looked toward the dark tunnel disappearing beneath the academy.

"They're teaching us where to look..."

Aidan knelt beside Lyra as Helena began treating the deep wounds across her side.

His hands trembled.

Not from fear.

From the effort it had taken...

Not to look at the blood again.

Far below Blackwood Academy...

The unseen hunters retreated through the mountain.

They had learned something invaluable.

The Crimson Witch would sacrifice himself for the people he loved.

And if they could not break his body...

They would begin breaking his heart.
