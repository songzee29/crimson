Far beneath the city...

A massive chamber stretched into darkness.

Hundreds of cloaked figures stood in absolute silence.

At the center...

A throne carved from black crystal.

Empty.

A hooded figure stepped forward.

"The heir grows."

Another answered.

"The Witch Authority nears completion."

A third voice echoed through the chamber.

"Good."

A cold laugh followed.

"Let him complete it."

The room fell silent.

One hunter looked up.

"...Why?"

The voice answered.

"Because..."

"...the moment the Witch Curse finishes..."

"...the First Fang will begin calling him."

Another pause.

"And when the Vampire Curse awakens..."

The hooded figure slowly smiled beneath the shadows.

"...the world will finally remember why it feared the Crimson Bloodline."

Somewhere above...

Aidan looked out over New Orleans from the church steps.

He had no idea how many eyes were now watching him.

The Veil.

The Guardian.

The silver-haired woman.

The old man with the crimson cane.

Ancient enemies.

Forgotten allies.

And forces that had been waiting centuries for an heir to finally appear.

He took a slow breath.

The city lights reflected in his eyes.

The Crimson System remained silent.

For once...

There was no quest.

No warning.

Just the quiet understanding that the storm was coming.

And whether Aidan was ready or not...

It had already begun.
