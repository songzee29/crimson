The sanctuary became quiet once more.

The candles no longer bent toward Aidan.

The ancient memories retreated into the stone.

It was as though the Sanctuary had decided it had revealed enough.

Aidan stood before the crimson box one last time.

His hand hovered above it.

"...I'll come back."

The words escaped him before he realized he had spoken them.

The System immediately appeared.

Unlike before, the interface looked... different.

The clean silver borders that normally framed each notification had deepened into dark crimson. Small runic symbols drifted behind the text before disappearing.

**Walk the path your blood remembers.**

"No objective?"

No explanation.

No percentages.

No map marker.

"You did it again."

"...Sorry."

"Whatever you're seeing... it's changing."

"How do you know?"

"You stop looking confused."

"...Now you look like you're listening."

Because she was right.

The System wasn't just displaying information anymore.

It felt like it was... waiting for him to decide.
