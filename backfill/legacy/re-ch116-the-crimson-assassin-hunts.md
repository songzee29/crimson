Without another word...

Rez's crimson cloak moved with the wind.

He looked toward Aidan.

"I'll check it."

Aidan nodded.

"Don't engage unless you have to."

Rez smirked.

"You know me."

Inside Aidan's mind—

*That's exactly why I said it.*

Rez laughed.

Then disappeared.

Not in a flash of magic.

Not in an explosion.

Simply...

Gone.

The Crimson Assassin moved through House Darkheart like a shadow.

Silent.

Watching.

Listening.

Protecting.

---

Minutes later...

Rez stood atop the outer wall overlooking the forest.

The night was calm.

Too calm.

He closed his eyes.

Listening.

Then...

He heard footsteps.

Slow.

Measured.

Someone wasn't hiding.

They wanted to be found.

Rez stepped down from the wall.

His crimson eyes locked onto the darkness.

"Come out."

A figure emerged from between the trees.

Their face remained hidden beneath a hood.

They stopped just beyond the castle's border.

Neither moved.

Neither spoke.

The wind passed between them.

Finally...

The stranger smiled.

"I was wondering..."

"...if the Crimson Assassin still existed."

Rez's expression never changed.

"You've found him."
