The hunters suddenly stopped.

Not because Aidan defeated them.

Because someone walked between them.

The young swordsman.

Rain rolled off his black coat.

His sword remained sheathed.

The hunters immediately stepped aside.

One whispered,

"Captain..."

The title surprised Aidan.

Captain?

The swordsman looked at the battlefield.

Then at the wounded hunters.

"You've embarrassed yourselves."

One hunter protested.

"But sir—"

He raised one finger.

Silence.

He slowly looked at Aidan.

"You've improved."

Aidan wiped blood from his mouth.

"You keep saying that."

"Because it's true."

The swordsman glanced toward the warehouse.

"You have people waiting for you."

Aidan frowned.

"What?"

The captain finally rested one hand on his sword.

"For the next sixty seconds..."

"...I'm the only thing standing between them..."

"...and the hunters behind me."

Silence.

"You have a choice."

He met Aidan's eyes.

"Fight me."

"Or run back to the people you care about."

Every muscle in Aidan's body tightened.

His instincts screamed.

Trap.

But...

If he stayed...

The hunters would reach the warehouse.

Lyra.

Malik.

Everyone.

He couldn't risk it.

Aidan took one step backward.

Then another.

Before disappearing into the rain.

The captain watched him leave.

Then quietly smiled.

"He made the correct decision."

The hunters looked confused.

"We let him escape?"

The captain answered coldly.

"No."

"We measured him."
