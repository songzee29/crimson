Midnight descended without darkness.

The crimson-black light surrounding the old oak had become its own sky.

Every ward protecting Blackwood Academy glowed beneath it like stars struggling against an eclipse.

No one slept.

No one expected to.

Faculty, staff, and students who could wield even the smallest amount of magic stood together throughout the grounds, reinforcing barriers that weakened with every passing minute.

Headmaster Aldren stood at the center of the academy courtyard.

His staff rested against the ancient stone.

“Tonight…”

“…Blackwood stands.”

No speech followed.

None was needed.

⸻

Master Rowan found Aidan waiting beneath the western cloister.

The old traveler carried no staff.

No books.

No maps.

Only silence.

Aidan looked at him.

“You said one lesson remained.”

Rowan nodded.

“It does.”

The two walked through the empty corridor overlooking the courtyard.

Neither hurried.

Below them, they could see Lyra helping younger students remain calm.

Cassian quietly repairing damaged ward circles beside Professor Helena.

Malik and Elias carrying enchanted lanterns from barrier to barrier.

Darius and Kael coordinating volunteers with remarkable composure.

Everyone had found their place.

Rowan stopped.

“What do you see?”

“My friends.”

“No.”

“My family.”

Rowan smiled.

“Better.”

⸻

He turned toward Aidan.

“When I first met you…”

“…you wanted stronger magic.”

“I did.”

“What do you want now?”

Aidan didn’t answer immediately.

He watched Malik laugh quietly after Elias nearly dropped another lantern.

He watched Lyra kneel beside a frightened first-year, speaking gently until the child’s trembling eased.

He watched Cassian silently take another witch’s place when exhaustion finally overcame her.

Finally…

“I want them to survive.”

Rowan’s eyes softened.

“Then you’ve already learned everything I could ever teach.”

Aidan blinked.

“That’s…”

“…the last lesson?”

“The Crimson Witch was never meant to become the strongest witch.”

Rowan looked toward the crimson sky.

“The Crimson Witch exists so others can keep living.”

The words settled into Aidan’s heart.

Not as knowledge.

As truth.

FINAL CRIMSON WITCH LESSON

Confirmed.

The Heir no longer seeks power.

The Heir seeks those worth protecting.

Character Evaluation Complete.

Preparing Final Witch Mastery.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message remained.

Waiting.

Not yet complete.

⸻

The ground shook.

Hard enough to send cracks racing across the academy courtyard.

Students cried out.

Faculty immediately reinforced the nearest barriers.

The crimson-black light above the old oak suddenly condensed.

Not upward.

Downward.

Toward the roots.

The Curse had stopped watching.

It had begun acting.
