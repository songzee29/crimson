## Chapter One Hundred Fifty-Three — The Second Door

The First Keeper's Vault remained perfectly still.

The journal rested where Aidan had left it.

The wooden box lay open upon the desk.

Nothing urged him forward.

Nothing demanded another choice.

The Living Legacy had never rushed him.

It simply waited for him to become ready.

The Guardian stood near the entrance, his gaze fixed on the darkness beyond the Vault.

Do you hear it?

Aidan closed his eyes.

At first...

Only silence.

Then...

A deep, rhythmic sound.

Stone grinding against stone.

Ancient gears awakening somewhere beneath the mountain.

The sound lasted only a few seconds before fading once more.

The Guardian nodded.

The Second Door has recognized you.

Recognized?

Not opened.

There is a difference.

The old warrior rested both hands upon the pommel of his sword.

Every Foundation chooses when it is willing to be discovered.

You cannot force it.

Aidan looked toward the darkness.

Then why reveal it now?

The Guardian's answer came without hesitation.

Because history has begun moving faster than before.

---

The two left the Vault together.

As the hidden doorway slowly sealed itself behind them, Aidan noticed something new.

The eastern wall of the Sanctuary had changed.

Where there had once been smooth crimson stone...

There was now a map.

Not painted.

Carved directly into the mountain itself.

It stretched across the entire wall.

Mountains.

Forests.

Rivers.

Ancient roads.

Scattered across the map were seven crimson circles.

Six remained dark.

One glowed faintly.

Aidan stepped closer.

What is this?

The Guardian's expression grew solemn.

The Seven Foundations.

The places where history was preserved when the old world began to fall.

He pointed toward the single glowing circle.

You have entered the First Keeper's Vault.

The other six remain asleep.

Aidan's eyes traveled across the map.

One crimson circle rested beneath a mountain.

Another beneath a forest.

Another far beyond the sea.

The final circle...

Had been carved beyond the edge of the known world.

Almost as if the map itself could no longer continue.

Who built all of this?

The Guardian looked at the map with quiet respect.

Someone who believed that civilizations survive...

Only if their wisdom survives first.

He said nothing more.

Aidan no longer asked.

Some answers deserved patience.

---

Far away...

Within Blackwood Library...

The old bookseller quietly removed a weathered scroll from the highest shelf.

Its seal had remained untouched for generations.

He gently broke the crimson wax.

The parchment inside contained only one sentence.

**When the First Foundation awakens... prepare the Pilgrim Roads.**

The old man smiled sadly.

So...

It truly is time.

He rolled the scroll closed once more before looking toward the rain outside.

Visitors will be coming again.

Not all of them as friends.

---

Across the ocean...

The Crimson Council assembled without summons.

The man wearing the crimson ring stood before the ancient stone table.

No reports had arrived.

None were necessary.

Every member had felt it.

The Seven Foundations have begun responding.

A younger council member frowned.

The Heir has only entered one Vault.

Why would the others awaken?

The oldest member slowly answered.

Because the Foundations were never waiting for a person.

They were waiting...

For the beginning of an age.

Silence followed.

Every council member understood the difference.

One Foundation opening could be dismissed as chance.

Two...

Was history.

Seven...

Would change the world.

---

Night settled over the Darkheart estate.

Aidan stood beneath the ancient oak outside his home.

The same raven that had led him to Ashfall Abbey landed upon one of the branches overhead.

This time...

It carried nothing.

It simply watched him.

Then spread its wings and flew toward the eastern horizon.

Aidan followed its path until it disappeared beyond the trees.

His crimson-and-gold eyes lingered on the darkness.

For the first time since opening the Crimson Book...

He felt the road stretching far beyond his city.

Beyond the Sanctuary.

Beyond House Darkheart.

The Legacy was no longer preparing him to uncover hidden rooms beneath his home.

It was preparing him to leave it.

Deep within the Living Legacy...

A final record quietly inscribed itself before the night ended.

LEGACY OBSERVED

The First Foundation has been entered.

The Pilgrim Roads stir.

The Heir's journey will soon extend beyond the House.

History does not remain within walls.

It walks the world.

The record faded into the endless archive.

Outside...

The wind carried the scent of distant rain from lands Aidan had never seen.

Somewhere beyond that horizon...

The Second Foundation waited.

Not hidden.

Waiting for the day the Heir would arrive... and discover why every road in history had, from the very beginning, been quietly leading toward the Crimson Civilization.
