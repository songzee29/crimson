## 2. Story movement visible across this run

This sequence starts by moving Aidan toward the hidden sanctuary, the Wall of Names, the Watcher, and the first Legacy Quest. It then expands into the hunter/Veil conflict, the warehouse beneath Dauphine Street, the trials and rewards, a wider hunt through the Quarter and bayou, and the introduction of Friend #3 and later trial structures.

The sequence is unusually compressed: most of Chapters 69–180 are delivered on June 27 in multi-chapter batches. The fast numbering is part of the source history; it does not authorize us to invent bridging chapters or assume every batch belongs to the current canon track.
