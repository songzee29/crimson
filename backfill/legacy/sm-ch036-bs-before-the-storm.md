## Chapter Thirty-Six — Before the Storm

The next morning arrived wrapped in silence.

At exactly five o'clock, Aidan stepped onto the academy's empty training field.

The sun had not yet risen.

Morning mist drifted across the grass.

The entire campus seemed asleep.

Except for one person.

Malik was already there.

He stood in the center of the field with his hands in his pockets, watching the horizon.

You're late.

Aidan looked at his phone.

It's four fifty-nine.

Malik smiled.

Exactly.

If you're on time...

You're already late.

Aidan laughed for the first time in days.

Maybe this training won't be so bad.

Malik tossed him a wooden practice sword.

Let's find out.

Aidan caught it.

Its weight reminded him of the practice staff inside the Sanctuary.

Simple.

Balanced.

Honest.

No magic.

No shortcuts.

Only effort.

Malik slowly raised his own practice sword.

One rule.

We're not here to win.

We're here to improve.

Aidan nodded.

The Guardian would have approved.

The two young men bowed respectfully before taking their stances.

Then...

They moved.

## Chapter Thirty-Seven — Brothers Forged Through Effort

Unlike the Guardian...

Malik wasn't impossible to read.

He fought with confidence.

Athleticism.

Natural talent.

His strikes came quickly, but every attack carried just enough hesitation for Aidan to recognize the pattern.

Block.

Step.

Turn.

Breathe.

Again.

Again.

Again.

The lessons from the Sanctuary blended with the real world.

After nearly an hour, both of them collapsed onto the grass, breathing heavily.

Malik stared at the brightening sky.

You're weird.

Aidan laughed.

That's not exactly a compliment.

It is from me.

Most people only care about getting stronger.

You...

You care about getting better.

There's a difference.

Aidan remained quiet.

Malik sat up.

Can I ask you something?

Sure.

Who taught you?

Aidan looked toward the sunrise.

An old warrior.

Malik grinned.

He sounds terrifying.

He is.

The answer was more truthful than Malik realized.

For several minutes, neither of them spoke.

They simply watched the sunrise together.

Sometimes...

The beginning of brotherhood looked exactly like this.

## Chapter Thirty-Eight — The Girl Who Asked Too Many Questions

By lunchtime, the academy buzzed with conversation.

Rumors about yesterday's fight had spread across campus.

Some claimed Aidan knew military combat.

Others insisted he had trained overseas.

One student even swore he had knocked three people unconscious with one punch.

The story became more ridiculous with every retelling.

Aidan ignored all of it.

He carried his lunch outside and sat beneath the oak tree.

A familiar voice interrupted the silence.

Mind if I sit here?

He looked up.

Lyra.

Of course.

She sat beside him, balancing a tray across her knees.

So...

You're famous now.

Aidan groaned.

Please don't remind me.

She laughed.

Too late.

Everyone's talking about the mysterious guy who beat up three seniors.

I didn't beat them up.

I stopped them.

There's a difference.

Lyra studied him for a moment.

Most people would have bragged.

You sound almost embarrassed.

I don't like fighting.

Then why'd you do it?

Aidan answered without thinking.

Because nobody else did.

Lyra smiled.

A genuine smile.

Good answer.

For a moment, neither of them spoke.

The breeze carried crimson leaves across the courtyard.

Lyra caught one before it reached the ground.

Funny.

She held the leaf toward the sunlight.

Why does it feel like we've met before?

Aidan felt the crimson mark beneath his skin pulse once.

I was wondering the same thing.

Neither of them noticed the ancient crimson symbol briefly reflecting across the surface of the leaf before disappearing.

## Chapter Thirty-Nine — The Hidden Library

That evening, Aidan returned beneath the estate.

Instead of entering the Sanctuary...

He began searching the forgotten room itself.

Now that he knew what existed beyond it...

He noticed details he had missed before.

One bookshelf looked slightly different.

Its wood was darker.

The dust covering it was thinner.

Curious, he pressed against it.

Click.

The shelf slowly rotated.

A narrow passage revealed itself.

Beyond it waited a small circular library.

Unlike the Sanctuary...

Everything here was physical.

Ancient journals.

Maps.

Handwritten notes.

Weapons.

Portraits whose faces had been carefully covered by black cloth.

At the center rested a stone table.

Upon it lay a single sealed letter.

Its envelope carried one sentence.

To My Heir.

Aidan's breathing stopped.

His hands trembled as he reached toward it.

Just before his fingers touched the envelope...

Restricted Founder Record.

Authorization Incomplete.

Current Character Evaluation Insufficient.

Continue Choosing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The letter dissolved into crimson light before his eyes.

Aidan stared at the empty table.

Seriously?

## Chapter Forty — The Beginning of the Hunt

Far beyond the city...

Deep within a mountain hidden from every map...

A council chamber carved from black stone slowly filled with hooded figures.

One by one they entered without speaking.

Ten seats.

Ten ancient symbols carved into the floor.

The final chair remained empty.

The hooded man from the cathedral stepped forward.

The Bell has rung.

A woman across the chamber nodded.

We felt it.

Another voice echoed through the darkness.

Is the Heir confirmed?

He is.

Silence followed.

The oldest member of the council slowly rose.

Then history has entered its next chapter.

Do we protect him?

Or test him?

The hooded man answered without hesitation.

Neither.

We observe.

If the Founder chose him...

Then the Legacy itself will decide whether he deserves to stand.

The chamber doors suddenly slammed shut.

A deep crimson flame ignited in the center of the room.

Within the fire appeared a single image.

Aidan Darkheart.

Every member of the council silently stared at the young man whose life had only begun to change.

None of them realized...

They weren't the only ones watching.

Hidden beyond the reach of the council...

Beyond the Sanctuary...

Beyond even the Living Legacy...

A pair of ancient crimson eyes slowly opened inside an endless darkness.

Unlike every force that had awakened so far...

These eyes carried no warmth.

No curiosity.

Only hunger.

The age of preparation was ending.

The age of conflict was quietly drawing near.
