The staircase finally opened into an enormous underground chamber.

None of them spoke.

They couldn't.

The room stole every word.

Stone pillars reached nearly a hundred feet into darkness.

Ancient banners, faded by centuries, hung motionless between them.

At the center...

Dozens of shattered crowns rested upon broken pedestals.

Gold.

Silver.

Black iron.

Each one cracked.

Each one abandoned.

Lyra stepped closer.

"What is this place?"

The System translated ancient writing carved beneath the first pedestal.

---

**Here rests the pride of those who believed power alone made them kings.**

---

Aidan slowly walked among them.

Each crown felt...

wrong.

Heavy.

Cold.

Like they carried regret.

Malik picked one up.

Immediately—

A deafening scream exploded inside his head.

He dropped the crown instantly.

"What the hell!"

The scream stopped.

The crown cracked in half.

Aidan rushed over.

"You okay?"

Malik nodded shakily.

"It..."

He couldn't find the words.

"...It hated me."

Silence.

Then the System spoke again.

---

**Do not wear a crown you did not earn.**

---

Aidan stared at the broken crowns.

The Sanctuary had shown him an empty throne.

Now this place showed him broken kings.

He was beginning to understand.

Power wasn't enough.

Never had been.
