Crimson Witch Authority Deepened.

The crimson light faded.

For the first time…

Aidan did not wonder what ability might come next.

He wondered whether he would be worthy of it.

⸻

Before dawn…

Master Rowan knocked softly on Aidan’s dormitory door.

“No lessons today.”

Aidan blinked.

“No?”

“We have work.”

⸻

Within minutes…

The two stood once again before the Root Chamber.

Headmaster Aldren.

Professor Helena.

Mr. Bennett.

All were already there.

The hidden stone passage beyond the first chamber now stood partially open.

Not wide enough to enter.

Wide enough to reveal ancient mechanisms buried within the mountain itself.

Four empty key sockets surrounded the doorway.

Only one connected to the cracked bronze key.

Professor Helena quietly looked toward Aidan.

“The chamber hasn’t changed since yesterday.”

“It changed once.”

Mr. Bennett answered.

“When you used that Law.”

Every eye settled upon him.

⸻

Master Rowan stepped beside the pedestal.

“The Founder built this place for a reason.”

“He expected an heir.”

“He expected guardians.”

“He expected choices.”

He looked directly at Aidan.

“So today…”

“You won’t follow my instructions.”

Aidan frowned.

“I won’t?”

“No.”

“You’ll make the decision.”

Silence filled the chamber.

The weight of those words settled over everyone present.

Rowan stepped back.

“The Legacy recognized you.”

“The Foundation recognized you.”

“So tell us…”

“What should we do?”

For several long moments…

No one spoke.

Aidan looked toward the cracked key.

Toward the open passage.

Toward the roots.

Then…

He remembered Rowan’s lesson.

The river.

Not the ripples.

He looked toward Headmaster Aldren.

“We don’t go farther.”

No one reacted.

He continued.

“Not yet.”

Professor Helena folded her arms.

“Why?”

“Because…”

He looked around the chamber.

“…we still don’t understand what we’re protecting.”

“If we rush forward because we’re afraid…”

“…then fear makes the decision.”

Master Rowan smiled.

Barely.

“But if we prepare first…”

Aidan continued.

“…then when we move…”

“We choose.”

Silence.

Long.

Meaningful.

Headmaster Aldren slowly nodded.

“Agreed.”

Mr. Bennett smiled openly.

“I was hoping you’d say that.”

Professor Helena looked toward Rowan.

“You knew.”

The old traveler answered quietly.

“I hoped.”

⸻

Suddenly…

The ancient doorway responded.

Not by opening.

By changing.

The runes surrounding the first key socket ignited with deep crimson light.

Then…

A single sentence appeared above the stone.

THE FIRST KEEPER CHOSE WELL.

The chamber recognized the decision.

Not the power.

The choice.

The Crimson Legacy appeared one final time.

For the first time…

The Legacy had not only observed.

It had entrusted Aidan with the next step.

And above them…

Blackwood Academy waited.

No longer watching the mountain.

Preparing to move before it was too late.
