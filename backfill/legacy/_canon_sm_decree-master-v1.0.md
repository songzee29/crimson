# 📚 THE ARCHIVE

Every idea created for the Crimson Legacy shall exist in one of four categories.

## 🔒 CANON

Official law of the Crimson Universe.

---

## 🟡 RESERVED

Approved for future development but not yet active within the story.

---

## 🟠 REPLACED

Former canon officially superseded by a newer Decree.

The original remains preserved for historical reference.

---

## 🔴 REJECTED

Ideas intentionally removed from active canon.

They remain archived as part of the creative history of the Crimson Legacy.

Nothing is permanently deleted.
