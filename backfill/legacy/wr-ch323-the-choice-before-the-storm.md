Courage could wear many faces.

Night settled gently across Blackwood Academy.

The old oak stood motionless beneath a sky filled with stars.

No birds rested within its branches.

No breeze disturbed its leaves.

The silence had become familiar.

Not comforting.

Merely honest.

⸻

Master Rowan waited beneath the tree.

Not because he had summoned Aidan.

Because he knew Aidan would come.

Sure enough…

Footsteps crossed the grass.

“You wanted to see me?”

The old traveler shook his head.

“No.”

“I thought you might want to ask something.”

Aidan smiled.

“You know me too well.”

“I’ve had good teachers.”

The answer made them both laugh quietly.

After a long silence…

Aidan finally asked,

“Are you afraid?”

Rowan did not answer immediately.

Instead…

He looked toward the mountain rising beyond the academy.

“Yes.”

The honesty surprised Aidan.

“I thought…”

“…that you weren’t.”

“I’ve been afraid many times.”

“Then how do you stay calm?”

The old traveler bent down and picked up a fallen acorn.

He placed it in Aidan’s hand.

“What does this become?”

“An oak.”

“When?”

“Years from now.”

Rowan nodded.

“Does it become an oak tomorrow because it fears winter?”

“No.”

“It becomes one…”

“…because every day it chooses to keep growing.”

The old man gently closed Aidan’s fingers around the acorn.

“Courage isn’t the absence of fear.”

“It is refusing to let fear decide what kind of person grows from it.”

The words settled into Aidan’s heart.

Not as another lesson.

As something he knew he would carry for the rest of his life.

⸻

They remained beneath the old oak as the moon slowly climbed higher.

Neither felt the need to fill the silence.

Eventually…

Footsteps approached.

Lyra carried a blanket over one arm.

Cassian balanced a tray with six steaming mugs.

Malik followed carrying fresh bread.

Elias held a lantern.

Cassian stopped.

“…Well.”

“I guess none of us wanted to be alone tonight.”

Master Rowan looked at the five young people standing together.

Then quietly accepted one of the mugs.

“So…”

Cassian looked around the circle.

“…is this officially our spot now?”

Lyra smiled.

“I think it always has been.”

No one disagreed.

For the next hour…

No one spoke about the mountain.

Or the wards.

Or the strange dreams.

They talked about classes.

Bad cooking.

Future travels.

Ridiculous academy rumors.

The ordinary things.

The precious things.

Master Rowan listened more than he spoke.

Watching.

Remembering.

Knowing exactly why the Founder had treasured evenings like this.

⸻

Far beneath them…

The mountain trembled.

Not enough for anyone above to notice.

The awakened presence reached another ancient threshold.

Stone shifted.

A single forgotten ward extinguished itself.

Then another.

Then stopped.

Not broken.

Yielding.

Waiting.

High in the Headmaster’s Tower…

A small crystal set upon Aldren’s desk dimmed slightly.

He looked up from the report he had been reading.

Across the room…

Professor Helena slowly met his eyes.

Neither spoke.

Both understood.

The time for preparation was ending.

The time for endurance…

Had quietly begun.
