The white world slowly cracked.

Like glass.

Darkness spread through the fractures.

Then...

Reality returned.

A massive circular chamber stretched beyond sight.

At its center...

Four colossal thrones.

Each carved from a different substance.

One of obsidian.

One of silver crystal.

One of crimson stone.

One...

Empty.

Above each throne floated a symbol.

A crescent moon.

A crimson crown.

A silver eye.

Above the empty throne...

Nothing.

---

The chamber was silent.

Until...

One figure stood.

Tall.

Dressed entirely in black.

No crown.

No weapon.

Only Authority.

His voice was calm.

"You crossed the road."

Aidan answered.

"...Yes."

"You defeated Valerius."

"...Yes."

"You survived yourself."

"...Yes."

The figure smiled.

"...Good."
