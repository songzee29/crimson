The academy gates closed quietly behind them.

No ceremony.

No farewell.

Only the sound of ten pairs of footsteps disappearing into the old forest.

Master Rowan led without speaking.

Professor Helena remained near the front, her staff lightly brushing the earth every few steps. Whenever its silver runes dimmed, she subtly altered their path.

Mr. Bennett walked last.

He never looked behind them.

Only at the ground.

Reading trails invisible to everyone else.

Hours passed beneath towering pines.

The deeper they traveled, the quieter the forest became.

Birdsong disappeared first.

Then insects.

Eventually…

Even the wind stopped.

Malik frowned.

“I don’t like this.”

Elias looked around.

“It’s too quiet.”

“It isn’t quiet.”

Rowan answered.

“It’s listening.”

No one laughed.

⸻

Aidan slowed.

The Crimson Legacy had been right.

Foundation Awareness wasn’t another spell.

It was another sense.

The forest no longer looked like trees.

Every trunk carried faint threads of pale blue magic flowing beneath its bark.

Roots connected to roots.

Stone connected to water.

Everything…

Connected.

Except…

Ahead.

Something had severed the pattern.

Like a tear in woven cloth.

He quietly raised a hand.

“Stop.”

Everyone froze instantly.

Professor Helena looked toward him.

“What is it?”

Aidan closed his eyes.

“The Foundation…”

He inhaled slowly.

“…doesn’t end.”

“It breaks.”

Master Rowan’s expression brightened.

“Show us.”

Aidan walked several careful steps forward.

The magical threads became easier to see.

One stream flickered violently before disappearing into a moss-covered standing stone.

Half the stone had collapsed.

Ancient Witch runes covered its surface.

Nearly all of them had cracked.

A slow pulse escaped through the fractures.

Then faded.

Professor Helena immediately knelt beside it.

“Foundation Marker.”

She gently brushed away centuries of moss.

“I’ve only seen sketches.”

Mr. Bennett nodded.

“Every Foundation village placed these along its borders.”

“They spread magical pressure.”

“They warned the land.”

Cassian frowned.

“This one’s dying.”

“No.”

Aidan answered quietly.

“It’s trying.”

Everyone looked toward him.

“It keeps rebuilding…”

“…and something keeps tearing it apart.”

Master Rowan smiled.

“Now you’re seeing causes.”

“Not symptoms.”

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOUNDATION AWARENESS

Application Recognized.

You distinguished damage…

From active collapse.

Progress Recorded.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message disappeared almost immediately.

No reward.

Only recognition.

Aidan smiled slightly.

He understood now.

Observation came before power.

⸻

Professor Helena gently placed both hands upon the broken stone.

Golden Witch runes spread across its surface.

Nothing happened.

She frowned.

“It won’t stabilize.”

Mr. Bennett looked toward Aidan.

“Try.”

Cassian blinked.

“That’s it?”

“Just…”

“Try?”

Mr. Bennett chuckled.

“The Founder didn’t build this place for experienced witches.”

“He built it for Keepers.”

Aidan stepped forward.

He placed one hand against the fractured marker.

It felt…

Cold.

Not physically.

Lonely.

Almost abandoned.

The cracks widened another fraction.

Instinct answered before thought.

“Hold.”

The Crimson Law left his lips almost as a whisper.

The response was immediate.

The spreading fracture stopped.

Not repaired.

Stopped.

The crumbling edge became perfectly still.

The blue magical threads surrounding the marker steadied.

One by one…

Several faded runes reignited.

Softly.

Professor Helena slowly removed her hands.

She hadn’t strengthened it.

She had been overwhelming it.

Aidan hadn’t added power.

He had given it time.

Rowan’s eyes closed with quiet satisfaction.

“The First Law…”

“…is beginning to understand itself.”

⸻

A notification shimmered before Aidan.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON LAW

Hold

Mastery Increased

Current Understanding:

Structural stabilization.

Magical interruption.

Controlled preservation.

New Insight Earned:

Holding is not resisting change.

Holding creates time…

For the correct change.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message dissolved.

No spell.

No new Law.

Understanding.

⸻

As they continued deeper into the forest…

The magical threads became weaker.

More broken.

The air smelled faintly of wet stone.

And old ash.

Finally…

The trees ended.

Grey Hollow stood before them.

Or rather…

What remained of it.

Stone cottages leaned at impossible angles.

Ancient wells sat undisturbed in the middle of abandoned streets.

Every doorway bore faded Witch sigils carved by careful hands centuries ago.

Not a single building had collapsed completely.

It was as though the village had simply…

Stopped living.

Lyra quietly whispered,

“It feels like everyone just…”

“…walked away.”

“No.”

Mr. Bennett answered softly.

“They hoped they’d return.”

Silence answered him.

Because none of them had.
