The Guardian walked to an ancient mural hidden behind the throne.

He brushed centuries of dust away.

A painting appeared.

Not of Zee.

Not of Rez.

Someone else.

A king wearing a crown unlike any seen before.

Standing behind him...

A silent figure with closed eyes.

The Guardian spoke softly.

"Before House Darkheart became a dynasty..."

"It was only a family."

He pointed toward the king.

"That..."

"...was the First Crimson King."

Then he pointed toward the silent figure.

"And behind him..."

"...stood the first Crimson Assassin."

Lyra whispered,

"So... it's happened before?"

The Guardian slowly shook his head.

"No."

"This is the problem."

Everyone looked confused.

"The Crimson Assassin has existed before..."

"But never..."

He looked directly at Aidan.

"...without its owner awake."

Silence.

"That..."

"...has never happened."
