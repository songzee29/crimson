Thousands of miles away...

Valerius sat quietly upon his throne.

A goblet of blood rested in his hand.

He smiled.

Then...

His smile widened.

He looked toward New Orleans.

"...He's coming."

Behind him...

Ancient Blood Sovereigns slowly stood.

One asked,

"Should we prepare for war?"

Valerius shook his head.

"No."

"For judgment."

Another frowned.

"You'll kill him?"

Valerius laughed.

"If I can."

The room fell silent.

Because every Sovereign knew...

Valerius had never said those words about anyone.

Ever.
