Cassian reached the royal wing.

His footsteps finally stopped.

Before him...

Two hallways stretched into the darkness.

One...

Led toward the Crimson Throne.

Toward Aidan.

The other...

Led toward the Queen's chambers.

Toward Lyra.

Cassian stared at both.

His breathing became uneven.

His hand tightened around the hilt of his sword.

He remembered Aidan's words.

> *"Walk away."*

He remembered Black Hollow.

His home.

His ashes.

His ring.

He closed his eyes.

"...Why..."

"...did you leave me alive..."

"...if this was always how it ended?"
