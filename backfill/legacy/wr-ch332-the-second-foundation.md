The crimson window dissolved into countless sparks.

Silence reclaimed the Root Chamber.

No one moved.

The ancient doorway no longer felt dormant.

It felt…

Awake.

Aidan continued staring at the single crimson sentence burning above the first key socket.

THE FIRST KEEPER CHOSE WELL.

It wasn’t congratulating him.

It was recording him.

Master Rowan stepped beside the stone.

“You understand now.”

Aidan slowly nodded.

“The Foundations aren’t doors.”

“No.”

Rowan rested a weathered hand against the ancient wall.

“They’re judgments.”

Professor Helena walked closer to the four empty sockets surrounding the doorway.

“Four Foundations.”

“Four Keepers.”

“Four decisions.”

She looked back toward Aidan.

“The first has accepted you.”

Mr. Bennett quietly added,

“Which means the remaining three have begun waiting.”

A chill spread through the chamber.

Waiting.

Not sleeping.

Waiting.

A low vibration rolled beneath their feet.

Not violent.

Steady.

Almost…

Like breathing.

Every root covering the walls pulsed once with faint crimson light.

Then another.

Aidan closed his eyes.

His Witch Perception had changed.

He could feel it now.

The mountain wasn’t stone.

It was woven together by countless streams of ancient magic.

Some burned bright.

Others flickered weakly.

One…

Far away…

Was dying.

His eyes snapped open.

“There.”

Everyone turned toward him.

Professor Helena frowned.

“What did you feel?”

He pointed northwest without hesitation.

“Something’s wrong.”

“The magic there…”

He searched for the words.

“…it’s unraveling.”

Master Rowan smiled.

“Good.”

“You didn’t see it.”

“You sensed it.”

Foundation Sight

The message vanished.

Aidan frowned.

“Foundation Sight?”

Rowan’s expression brightened.

“The Legacy is becoming more generous.”

“No.”

Mr. Bennett quietly corrected.

“It’s becoming more demanding.”

Headmaster Aldren unfolded an old map across the stone pedestal.

It wasn’t a map of Blackwood Academy.

It was older.

Much older.

The school rested at the center.

Around it…

Four crimson circles had been carefully drawn centuries ago.

One glowed brightly.

The First Foundation.

Another flickered.

The western mark.

Professor Helena pointed toward it.

“Grey Hollow.”

Cassian frowned.

“I’ve never heard of it.”

“Almost no one has.”

Mr. Bennett answered.

“It disappeared from modern maps decades ago.”

“People stopped going.”

“Eventually…”

“They stopped remembering.”

Aidan’s stomach tightened.

“Why?”

No one answered immediately.

Finally…

Master Rowan spoke.

“Because witches abandoned it.”

⸻

The chamber fell silent again.

“Willingly?”

Aidan asked.

Rowan looked toward the old map.

“No.”

“They fled.”

Far away…

Beyond Blackwood.

Beyond the forests.

Beyond the roads anyone still traveled…

Mist rolled through the abandoned streets of Grey Hollow.

Broken homes leaned beneath centuries of neglect.

Ancient protective sigils carved into doorframes had almost completely faded.

One final barrier surrounding the forgotten village cracked.

A thin line of crimson light escaped into the sky.

Deep beneath the village…

Something opened its eyes.

Not with rage.

With hunger.

And the Witch Curse smiled.
