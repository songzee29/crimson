The Valley of Ash became silent.

The glowing drop of **True Crimson Blood** floated between Aidan and Cassian.

It pulsed like a living heart.

Every pulse caused the ash beneath their feet to tremble.

Cassian stared at it.

"...What are you planning?"

Aidan answered calmly.

"Something no Founder has ever attempted."

---

Cassian tried to move.

Nothing.

The Crimson Law still held him in place.

He laughed bitterly.

"So this is how it ends."

Aidan slowly shook his head.

"No."

"This is how it begins."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez stepped forward.

His expression was unlike anything Aidan had ever seen.

Concern.

Curiosity.

Hope.

All at once.

**You're about to rewrite your own decree.**

Aidan nodded.

*Not rewrite it.*

*Fulfill it...*

*Then end it.*

---

The wolf lifted its massive head.

**The hunt has reached its end.**

The vampire watched the glowing blood.

**Now the Bloodline decides.**

---

Aidan raised his hand.

The drop of True Crimson Blood drifted closer to Cassian.

It hovered just inches from his lips.

Cassian's breathing became uneven.

"What is that supposed to do?"

Aidan looked him directly in the eyes.

"You've lived for centuries carrying my judgment."

"You've carried guilt."

"You've carried hatred."

"You've carried fear."

He took another step.

"And I carried it with you."

---

The wind vanished.

Not a single grain of ash moved.

The world itself seemed to wait.

---

Aidan extended two fingers.

A Crimson Law formed in the air.

Not a spell.

Not an attack.

A law.

Its crimson symbols rotated around Cassian before dissolving into his body.

Immediately...

Cassian stopped struggling.

Not because he was weaker.

Because he no longer wished to resist.

His body relaxed.

His breathing steadied.

His eyes remained fixed on Aidan.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Living Blueprint**

**Crimson Law Recorded**

### **King's Command**

**Purpose**

The King's will becomes absolute over those bound by his Crimson Blood.

---

Rez's eyes widened.

He had never seen the Living Blueprint record a Law like this before.

---

Cassian frowned.

"...What did you do?"

Aidan answered honestly.

"I removed your fear."

Cassian blinked.

"My... fear?"

"I don't want you kneeling because you're terrified."

"I want you standing because you finally understand."

---

The glowing blood floated closer.

Only an inch remained.

Cassian looked at it.

Then back at Aidan.

"If I accept that..."

"...I'll never be the same."

Aidan nodded.

"I know."

---

Silence.

---

Cassian slowly closed his eyes.

A long breath escaped him.

"I've spent centuries trying to outrun your judgment."

He laughed softly.

"I never realized..."

"...I was also running from myself."

He opened his eyes again.

There was no anger left.

Only exhaustion.

"If this truly ends it..."

"...then I won't run anymore."

---

Aidan looked at him for several long moments.

Then...

He spoke.

Not loudly.

Not angrily.

With the authority of the Crimson King.

**By the King's Decree...**

**The Judgment Ends Here.**

The True Crimson Blood drifted toward Cassian's lips.

The moment it touched them...

The entire valley erupted.

A pillar of crimson light shot into the heavens.

The earth shook violently.

The sky split with crimson lightning.

Far away...

Every member of the Crimson Bloodline froze.

Malik looked toward the northern sky.

Kael stopped mid-training.

Mara's Crimson Laws vanished.

Lyra stood from her seat so suddenly her chair fell backward.

She whispered only one name.

"...Aidan."

---

Back in the Valley of Ash...

Cassian's body arched as the True Crimson Blood entered him.

Crimson veins spread beneath his skin.

His old vampire aura shattered.

Something new...

Something older...

Something born directly from the Founder's Blood...

Began taking its place.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**True Crimson Blood Accepted**

**Bloodline Evolution Beginning**

---

The notification paused.

As though even the Legacy was witnessing something unprecedented.

The Fourth Judgment...

Was no longer following the old path.

Aidan Darkheart had chosen to answer the curse...

Not with another death.

But with a new decree that would change the destiny of the Crimson Bloodline forever.
