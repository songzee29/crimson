That night...

Cassian woke suddenly.

His room was silent.

No attack.

No danger.

Just...

A dream.

He couldn't remember most of it.

Only one sentence.

> **"...You shall never know peace..."**

He sat on the edge of his bed.

His partner stirred.

"Another nightmare?"

Cassian lied.

"...Just couldn't sleep."

He walked outside.

The moon hung over Black Hollow.

Everything was beautiful.

Everything was quiet.

Yet...

Something inside him refused to rest.

The curse wasn't hurting his body.

It was hollowing out his soul.

Little by little.
