## Chapter One Hundred — The Whisper of the Abyss

The Law of Protection no longer drifted among them.

Only clarity.

Close your eyes.

What do you hear?

Nothing.

Not his own.

I can hear them.

People do.

## Chapter One Hundred One — The First Lesson of Authority

The Guardian led Aidan from the Hall of Laws toward a quiet courtyard hidden within the Sanctuary.

Unlike every place Aidan had visited before...

This courtyard contained life.

Flowers bloomed beneath ancient trees.

Birds sang from crimson branches.

A gentle breeze carried the scent of fresh rain.

At the center stood a single stone candle.

It wasn't lit.

The Guardian stopped beside it.

Light the candle.

Aidan frowned.

With what?

Your Authority.

I don't know how.

Good.

The Guardian smiled.

That's the lesson.

Authority does not answer desire.

It answers understanding.

He stepped back.

Why should that candle burn?

Aidan stared at it.

Because...

You told me to?

Nothing happened.

The Guardian folded his arms.

Try again.

Aidan remained silent.

Minutes passed.

Then he noticed something.

The courtyard had grown darker.

The flowers surrounding the candle had begun closing.

Without light...

The entire garden would eventually wither.

He slowly reached toward the candle.

Not because I was told.

Because everything around it deserves the light.

The crimson warmth inside his chest stirred.

One word escaped his lips.

Protect.

A tiny crimson flame appeared atop the candle.

No explosion.

No thunder.

Only a quiet flame dancing peacefully in the breeze.

The Guardian smiled.

There.

Now...

You understand.

The flowers that had begun closing were opening again.

The warmth had spread across the courtyard.

Measure them by how many lives quietly become better because of them.
