## Chapter Forty-One — The Crimson Council

The chamber remained silent.

Ten hooded figures sat around the circular table carved from black stone. Crimson fire burned in the center, its flames rising without heat as Aidan's image slowly turned above it.

No one spoke for several moments.

Each member studied him.

Not his appearance.

His posture.

His eyes.

His choices.

Finally, the oldest among them broke the silence.

He protected someone weaker than himself.

Another nodded.

Without revealing what he is.

A third voice answered from the opposite side of the chamber.

Or perhaps...

Without knowing what he is.

Silence returned.

The hooded man who had watched Aidan from the cathedral slowly folded his arms.

That is precisely why the Founder chose him.

Not because he possesses power.

Because he does not yet understand it.

One member leaned forward.

Should we contact him?

No.

Should we reveal ourselves?

No.

Should we intervene if he is attacked?

The hooded man closed his eyes.

Only if the Legacy itself fails.

The room grew quiet once more.

Everyone understood what that meant.

The Legacy had never failed.
