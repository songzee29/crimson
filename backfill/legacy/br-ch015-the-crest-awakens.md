Three nights later...

Operation: Rise of the King resumed.

Another cursed territory.

Another ancient evil.

This one...

A forgotten cathedral swallowed by vines.

Inside...

A creature of living shadow towered over the Circle.

Without hesitation...

Aidan stepped forward.

His hands rose.

The familiar motions of the Witch Authority.

His fingers drew invisible symbols through the air.

One word.

"Bind."

Crimson Laws erupted beneath the cathedral.

The shadow screamed.

Then...

Something happened that had never happened before.

Behind Aidan...

A massive crimson crest appeared.

The Crown.

The Eye.

The Wolf.

The Thorned Roots.

It existed for less than a heartbeat.

Then disappeared.

The creature froze.

Not because of the Law.

Because it recognized the crest.

It looked at Aidan...

And for the first time in centuries...

A monster felt fear.

It whispered with its dying breath,

"...Darkheart..."

Before dissolving into ash.

---

The Guardian had seen it.

His hands trembled.

He whispered to himself,

"So..."

"...the House has accepted him."
