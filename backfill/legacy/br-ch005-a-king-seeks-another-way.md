That night...

The two kings sat alone before a small fire outside the village.

No guards.

No council.

No audience.

Just two men carrying the weight of old choices.

Cassian stared into the flames.

"...Tell me the truth."

Aidan looked at him.

"...Do you want me to suffer?"

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"...No."

"I wanted you to understand."

Silence.

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"I do."

"I know what it feels like to wake up every day expecting to lose everything."

Another long silence passed.

Then Aidan said something no one expected.

> **"If there's another way to fulfill the decree..."**

> **"...I'll find it."**

The second heartbeat thundered inside his soul.

**THUMP.**

The Wolf lifted its head.

It did not object.

The Crimson Legacy appeared beside the fire.

Its pages turned on their own.

A new line slowly wrote itself.

---

> **Observation**

---

> **A king who writes a decree...**

> **...must also bear its consequences.**

---

The page closed.

High above them...

Hidden on a distant ridge...

A cloaked figure watched the two kings sharing a fire instead of a battlefield.

The figure smiled.

Then whispered into the wind...

> **"Good..."**

> **"Now the real test begins."**

Because Judgment Two was never about whether Aidan could destroy Cassian.

It was about whether the Crimson King could carry the burden of his own decree...

Without losing the king he had become.
