Far beyond House Darkheart...

Beyond Black Hollow...

Beyond even the lands ruled by the Veil...

A solitary figure stood atop the ruins of an ancient tower.

Its face remained hidden beneath a hood.

Below...

Entire valleys stretched into darkness.

The figure looked toward House Darkheart.

Then toward Black Hollow.

Finally...

Toward the distant cathedral of the Veil.

Three kingdoms.

Three powers.

Three futures.

A quiet voice escaped beneath the hood.

"...They're all preparing..."

"...for the wrong war."

The figure slowly turned away.

Hidden beneath the cloak...

A strange mark glowed faintly.

Not the crest of House Darkheart.

Not the Veil's seal.

Not the symbol of the Monarchs.

Something older.

Something not yet revealed.

As the hooded stranger disappeared into the night...

The Crimson Legacy quietly turned a page on its own.

No message appeared.

Only one blank line...

Waiting for the next chapter of history to be written.
