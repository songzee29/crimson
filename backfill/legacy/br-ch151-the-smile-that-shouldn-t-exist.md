Both men were thrown backward.

Cassian landed hard.

He rolled once.

Twice.

Then stood.

His healing slowed.

For the first time...

His regeneration wasn't instant.

He had burned through too much.

He smiled anyway.

Across the battlefield...

Aidan hadn't moved.

Cassian stared.

Then laughed.

"...I finally won."

He began walking toward the motionless king.

Slowly.

Confidently.

Ready to finish it.

But as he got closer...

He stopped.

Aidan...

Was smiling.

Not a wide smile.

Not a happy one.

Just...

A faint smile.

Cassian frowned.

"...What's so funny?"

Aidan slowly opened one eye.

"...You're healing slower."

Silence.

"...That means..."

He coughed blood.

"...I can still kill you."

Cassian's smile vanished.

For the first time in this battle...

He felt something unfamiliar.

Not fear.

Concern.

Because the man lying in the mud...

With almost nothing left...

Still believed he could win.

And somehow...

Cassian wasn't sure he was wrong.
