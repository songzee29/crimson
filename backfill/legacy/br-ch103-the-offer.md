Night.

The cathedral.

The crimson vial floated between Azrath and Cassian.

This time...

Azrath didn't hand it over.

He placed it back on the altar.

Then stepped away.

"I won't ask again."

Cassian looked confused.

"What?"

"If you want it..."

"...take it."

Silence.

"No bargains."

"No promises."

"No commands."

"Only choice."

Cassian stared at the vial.

His breathing slowed.

Azrath turned toward the doorway.

"And remember..."

"...the moment you drink..."

"...there is no returning to the man you are today."

He walked away.

Leaving Cassian...

Alone.
