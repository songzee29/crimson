That night...

Cassian stood alone in the empty village square.

The wind carried silence through abandoned homes.

He looked at the Darkheart Brand on his arm.

For years...

He had blamed Aidan for everything.

Now...

He wasn't so sure.

The curse hadn't taken these people.

It hadn't attacked Black Hollow.

It had simply forced him to confront something he had never admitted.

He couldn't save everyone.

The realization crushed him.

For the first time...

Cassian questioned whether he had ever deserved to lead anyone.

High above the village...

Unseen by every vampire below...

A shadow watched from the cliffs.

Its eyes glowed briefly beneath its hood.

Then it vanished into the darkness.

The game had begun.

And none of the players yet understood who had made the first move.
