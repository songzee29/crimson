“We answer him.”

The words spoken by the Seven disappeared into the wind.

Far beneath Blackwood Academy…

The hidden seam in the Root Chamber slowly opened.

Not all at once.

Stone slid against stone with a deep grinding sound that echoed through the chamber.

Dust poured from the ceiling.

The roots above tightened around the ancient pillars.

The academy trembled again.

More violently than before.

⸻

Above ground…

Students staggered as the courtyard shook beneath their feet.

The old oak groaned.

A massive branch cracked.

Before it could fall toward a group of frightened first-years—

Aidan reacted.

Not because he thought.

Because he chose.

He threw himself forward.

Both hands rose instinctively.

Golden Crimson Witch sigils burst into existence.

The familiar incantation reached his lips—

But stopped.

Something inside him changed.

The Crimson Legacy erupted before his eyes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LEGACY MILESTONE ACHIEVED

The First Foundation Protected.

Judgment:

The Heir chose restraint over power.

The Heir chose trust over possession.

The Heir chose protection over ambition.

Recognition Accepted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crimson light expanded.

Every rune surrounding Aidan’s hands changed.

The intricate circles simplified.

Condensed.

Focused.

As though centuries of witchcraft had been distilled into something purer.

Another message appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON WITCH PROGRESSION

Authority Expanded.

The Crimson Witch acknowledges its bearer.

First Crimson Witch Law Granted.

LAW:

Hold

Purpose:

Command magical force to remain.

Applications:

Barrier stabilization.

Object restraint.

Structural reinforcement.

Magical anchoring.

This Law requires Will…

Not volume.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The messages vanished.

Time resumed.

The branch fell.

Aidan’s hand extended.

One word escaped his lips.

“Hold.”

The world answered.

There was no explosion.

No dramatic pillar of light.

The falling branch…

Stopped.

Not completely.

Its momentum simply…

Ended.

It hung in the air.

The cracking wood ceased.

The splintering bark froze.

Every leaf became perfectly still.

Students stared.

Professor Helena’s eyes widened.

Master Rowan closed his own.

The branch remained suspended just long enough for the first-years beneath it to run clear.

Aidan slowly lowered his hand.

The branch gently settled harmlessly onto the grass.

Silence swallowed the courtyard.

Cassian reached him first.

“…You didn’t cast.”

Aidan looked at his own hand.

“I know.”

⸻

Within the Root Chamber…

The tremors finally ceased.

Headmaster Aldren slowly turned toward Rowan.

“Did you feel that?”

The old traveler smiled.

“I did.”

“The Foundation answered him.”

“No.”

Rowan quietly corrected.

“The Witch did.”

⸻

That evening…

The academy did not return to normal.

Students whispered about what they had witnessed.

No one exaggerated.

No one needed to.

They had all seen it.

One word.

One gesture.

One impossible moment.

⸻

Professor Helena found Aidan beneath the old oak after sunset.

“You’ve learned something new.”

“I think so.”

“You think?”

“I don’t understand it yet.”

She nodded approvingly.

“Good.”

He looked surprised.

“You expected me to?”

“No.”

She smiled.

“The dangerous witches are the ones who believe they understand power immediately.”

She looked toward the old branch resting safely beside the tree.

“You saved lives.”

“That matters.”

“The rest…”

She looked back at him.

“…can wait.”

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOUNDER OBSERVATION

Power that exists…

To protect…

Will always grow deeper…

Than power that exists…

To dominate.

Progress Recorded.

Crimson Witch Authority Deepened.
