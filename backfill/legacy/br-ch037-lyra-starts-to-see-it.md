Back underground, Lyra began doing something she didn’t realize she was doing at first.

Testing him.

Small things.

Subtle things.

“Do you remember what you wanted before all this?” she asked casually while walking.

Aidan thought.

“No.”

“Do you remember your family?”

“I don’t know if I had one.”

“Do you feel like something is missing?”

Aidan stopped walking.

That question created a pause longer than all the others.

He looked down at his chest.

“I feel… like there should be pressure there,” he said quietly.

“But there isn’t.”

Lyra froze.

That was it.

That was the piece.

Not sadness.

Not grief.

Not even memory.

**Weight.**

Something inside Aidan used to tell him what mattered enough to hurt.

And now it was gone.
