As the company prepared to leave the Hall of Names...

Cassian stopped walking.

He looked back toward the Ash King's empty throne.

His chest tightened.

The Darkheart Brand beneath his sleeve burned...

For the first time in weeks.

Not with unbearable pain.

With a single pulse.

He clenched his fist.

"...Why now?"

No one answered.

Far away...

Beyond the fortress...

A raven landed upon a dead tree.

It carried a single black feather in its beak.

Then it took flight...

Toward Black Hollow.

Toward the home Cassian had rebuilt.

Toward the peace he had struggled for.

Inside the darkness...

The Ash King watched the raven disappear.

He spoke only one sentence.

> **"Every decree..."**

> **"...collects its price."**

The wind carried those words across the mountains.

Unheard by Aidan.

Unheard by Lyra.

But not unheard...

By fate.

The Second Judgment had not reached its end.

It had merely taken its next step.

And somewhere beyond the horizon...

The road toward House Darkheart...

Was beginning to darken.
