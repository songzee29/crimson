Flying should have been impossible.

Aidan had never learned.

Never practiced.

Never even possessed wings until minutes ago.

His Dragon instinct did not care.

The first beat nearly sent him spinning.

The second corrected.

By the third…

The sky belonged to him.

Cold wind tore across his face.

His wings adjusted without conscious thought.

Muscles that had not existed minutes earlier flexed with terrifying precision.

Every beat carried him faster.

Too fast.

Below him, forests became streaks of black and green.

Roads disappeared beneath the night.

But Aidan barely saw any of it.

He saw blood.

Not literally.

He sensed it.

Thousands of heartbeats spread across the land.

Humans.

Animals.

Supernatural creatures.

Every one of them called to the starvation consuming him.

Aidan clenched his jaw until blood filled his mouth.

“No.”

The Vampire demanded feeding.

The Dragon demanded strength.

The Wolf demanded survival.

The Siren heard every heartbeat as a song.

The Phoenix burned hotter every second he resisted.

Aidan’s True Crimson Blood consumed whatever reserves remained.

His body had transformed instantly.

Now biology demanded payment.

Aidan dropped lower.

He could smell Cassian’s coven before he saw it.

Vampire blood.

Concentrated.

Powerful.

Enough.

The realization horrified the part of him still thinking clearly.

He knew those people.

Not all by name.

But enough.

They were Cassian’s.

His coven.

His responsibility.

His family.

Aidan tried to turn.

His right wing shifted.

His body refused the direction.

The starvation clenched around his organs.

His vision went black for half a second.

When it returned…

He was descending.

Fast.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Behind him, Lyra ran.

Not as a woman.

As a wolf.

Her first true sprint in her new form tore through the forest.

Crimson fur streaked between trees.

Silver-white runes burned across her body.

She did not know the limits of what she had become.

She didn’t care.

Aidan was ahead.

She followed.

Rowan and the others came farther behind.

Cassian could barely walk.

Still, he forced himself forward.

Every movement reopened something Aidan had broken.

His Curse Mark burned beneath his clothes.

He knew where Aidan was going.

That knowledge hurt worse than his ribs.

“My coven…”

Malik caught him before he fell.

Cassian shoved him away.

“Don’t.”

“You can’t even stand.”

“I have to get there.”

Malik stared at him.

“Why?”

Cassian looked toward the crimson streak crossing the distant sky.

“Because they’re about to pay for something I did.”

He started moving again.

This time…

No one stopped him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan hit the ground inside the coven territory like a falling weapon.

Stone erupted around him.

Vampires rushed from the surrounding structures.

Weapons drawn.

Magic rising.

Then they recognized him.

“My Prince?”

Aidan stood inside the crater.

His wings slowly opened.

Every vampire froze.

Someone whispered,

“What happened to him?”

Aidan couldn’t answer.

There were too many heartbeats.

Thirty.

Forty.

More inside.

He heard blood rushing through every throat.

Every wrist.

Every chest.

His fangs ached.

One vampire stepped cautiously forward.

“Aidan?”

“Stay back.”

The vampire stopped.

Aidan’s claws curled.

“All of you.”

His breathing became ragged.

“Get away from me.”

Confusion spread through the coven.

Then one of them smelled his hunger.

Fear changed the air.

That was the worst possible thing.

The Wolf recognized prey movement.

The Vampire recognized panic.

The Dragon recognized territory filled with weaker bodies.

The Siren heard their fear sharpen their heartbeats.

The Crimson Phoenix demanded more fuel.

Aidan squeezed his eyes shut.

“Go.”

Nobody moved.

Someone farther back said,

“Where is Cassian?”

Aidan opened his eyes.

That question destroyed what remained of his control.

Cassian.

Lyra’s blade wound.

Lyra dying.

Cassian’s blood.

His coven.

His people.

His responsibility.

Aidan’s grief and starvation collided.

The nearest vampire saw his expression.

He turned to run.

Aidan caught him before his second step.

His fangs entered the man’s throat.

The first mouthful of blood hit Aidan’s system like an explosion.

Every newly awakened inheritance responded.

The Dragon wings spread.

The scales brightened.

Crimson fire raced along his veins.

The Siren inside him drank the heartbeat as much as the Vampire drank blood.

The Wolf demanded more.

Aidan drained until the body went limp.

He released it.

Silence.

Everyone stared.

Aidan stared too.

At the body.

At the blood on his mouth.

He knew what he had done.

And he was still starving.

“Run.”

This time…

They did.

Aidan’s pupils narrowed.

And instinct took over.
