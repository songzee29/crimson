The Circle split into teams.

Aidan took the northern villages.

Lyra headed west.

Malik and Sienna rode south.

The Guardian remained behind to protect New Orleans.

For three days...

No one slept.

Everywhere they arrived...

The same thing.

Empty towns.

No bodies.

No battle.

Only one sentence carved into stone.

> **THE KING WAS TOO LATE.**

Aidan ripped one of the stones from the ground.

His breathing grew heavier.

"This isn't about killing."

The Guardian's voice echoed through his communicator.

"No."

"It's about making the survivors ask..."

> **'Where was our king?'**
