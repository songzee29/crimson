The cathedral collapsed around them.

Stone rained from the ceiling.

Dust swallowed the moonlight.

The seven remaining Veil Sentinels spread into a perfect circle.

No panic.

No hesitation.

Ancient warriors created for one purpose.

To kill monsters.

---

Aidan stood in the center.

His black coat fluttered behind him.

His crimson eyes calmly counted them.

"...Seven."

The oldest Sentinel smiled.

"You should fear us."

Aidan looked at him for several long seconds.

Then...

He smiled.

It wasn't warm.

It wasn't cruel.

It was the smile of someone who had just found something interesting.

"...No."

He disappeared.
