Night had fallen over House Darkheart.

The kingdom celebrated below.

Music echoed through the streets.

Children laughed.

For the first time since Lyra's return...

There was hope.

Yet Aidan walked alone.

His destination was the Crimson Archive.

---

The ancient doors opened before him.

He entered without a word.

The massive chamber recognized its King.

Torches ignited with crimson fire.

At the center...

The Crimson Legacy rested upon its pedestal.

Aidan placed his hand upon its cover.

The book slowly opened.

Only he could see the crimson pages.

Only he could read the words.

---

> **CRIMSON LEGACY**

> **Living Archive**

---

The pages turned.

Not to reward him.

Not to grant him power.

To teach him.

Another page revealed itself.

---

## **Authority Blueprint**

**The Crimson Legacy is not your power.**

**You are.**

---

Aidan read silently.

The next page appeared.

---

> **The Crimson Legacy preserves the complete potential of House Darkheart.**

> **It records what the Crimson King has achieved.**

> **It reveals what the Crimson King is capable of achieving.**

> **It cannot command the Crimson King.**

> **It cannot create power.**

> **It cannot awaken power.**

> **Only the Crimson King may do so.**

---

Aidan smiled.

"So..."

"...I've been looking at this the wrong way."

Behind him, Rez folded his arms.

"I was wondering how long it would take you."

Aidan looked over his shoulder.

"You knew?"

Rez nodded.

"I've always told you..."

"The Legacy is a guide."

"It shows the road."

"You still have to walk it."

---

The pages turned again.

---

## **Living Blueprint**

Every Crimson Law.

Every Blood Art.

Every Authority Evolution.

Every Ascension.

Every future technique.

Exists within the Legacy...

As knowledge.

Not as power.

---

Aidan continued reading.

---

> **A Blueprint does not build a kingdom.**

> **A king does.**

---

He closed his eyes.

Everything finally made sense.

When he first inherited the Legacy...

He needed it to understand his abilities.

To organize them.

To study them.

To survive.

But every battle...

Every victory...

Every sacrifice...

Had slowly changed him.

The more he evolved...

The less he needed to ask.

The more naturally Crimson answered his will.

---

The Legacy continued.

---

## **Current Development**

**Early Stage**

The Crimson King consciously uses the Legacy to understand and activate newly learned abilities.

---

**Intermediate Stage**

The Crimson King understands his abilities before consulting the Legacy.

The Legacy confirms and records what has already been accomplished.

---

**Ascended Stage**

The Crimson King commands Crimson directly through his own will.

The Legacy immediately records the event for House Darkheart.

---

Aidan slowly laughed.

"So eventually..."

"...I won't need to look at these pages before every battle."

The Legacy answered with a single line.

---

> **Correct.**

---

> **One day...**

> **You will already know.**

---

The room fell silent.

Aidan looked down at his own hands.

He remembered when he needed quests to survive.

When every new ability felt impossible.

Now...

Crimson felt like breathing.

Natural.

Alive.

Part of him.

Not because the Legacy had changed.

Because he had.

Rez stepped beside him.

"You've outgrown needing permission."

Aidan nodded.

"I think..."

"...I'm finally beginning to understand what being the Crimson King really means."

The Living Archive quietly closed.

It had fulfilled its purpose.

Not by making Aidan stronger.

But by reminding him...

That the greatest power in House Darkheart had never been the book.

It had always been...

The King who chose to write his own Legacy.
