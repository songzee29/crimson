As they walked deeper into the sanctuary, the walls began changing.

Ancient carvings covered every stone.

Battles.

Coronations.

Creatures unlike anything Aidan had ever seen.

Some looked like vampires.

Some resembled wolves.

Some radiated overwhelming witchcraft.

But others...

Didn't belong to any species he recognized.

Lyra traced one carving with her fingertips.

"I've never seen symbols like these."

Aidan stopped beside another mural.

It showed a man wearing a crimson crown.

Behind him stood countless supernatural beings.

None were kneeling.

They stood willingly.

Together.

Not subjects.

Allies.

Below the carving was an inscription.

The language should have been impossible to read.

Yet the moment Aidan looked at it—

the System translated it.

---

**"A king who rules through fear builds a kingdom of silence."**

**"A king who carries the curses of his people builds a legacy."**

---

His chest tightened.

Something about them felt...

personal.

Almost like advice.

Almost like a warning.
