Hours later...

The Guardian found Aidan sitting alone.

"You smelled it."

Aidan looked up sharply.

"...What?"

The Guardian sighed.

"I hoped..."

"...I was wrong."

Silence.

"What do you know?"

The Guardian looked toward the Mississippi.

"I've watched three Crimson Kings."

"...You're the fourth."

Aidan froze.

"You knew them?"

"I buried them."

Those words hung in the air.

Then the Guardian continued.

"They all said the same thing."

"What?"

"They all smelled blood..."

"...before the Vampire Authority awakened."

Silence.

"So..."

"...it's beginning."

The Guardian closed his eyes.

"I think..."

"...it already has."
