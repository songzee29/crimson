Far above House Darkheart...

Upon the highest tower...

A lone figure stood.

Silent.

Motionless.

The **Crimson Assassin**.

The body prepared long ago as Rez's vessel.

Though Rez himself now walked beside Aidan, the Crimson Assassin still carried out its purpose.

Watching.

Protecting.

Without speaking a single word.

A flock of ravens suddenly swept toward the castle.

Before any reached the walls—

The Crimson Assassin moved.

One flash.

Then another.

Every raven fell before crossing the kingdom's boundary.

The Assassin looked toward the northern mountains.

Its silver-crimson eyes narrowed.

Something...

Was coming.
