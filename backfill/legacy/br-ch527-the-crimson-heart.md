Inside the arena...

Aidan suddenly felt it.

Warmth.

Not from power.

From his people.

He looked toward the barrier.

Every member of his circle stood there.

Not trying to rescue him.

Believing in him.

The Wolf smiled.

> **"...Do you feel it?"**

Aidan nodded.

"...Yes."

> **"...That..."**

> **"...is why your pack is different."**

Another crack spread gently across the Sacred Seal.

Not from battle.

Not from pain.

From trust.

The Wolf took one more step.

Now...

It stood directly beside the barrier instead of behind it.

Closer than it had ever been.

The Ash King noticed.

His ancient eyes narrowed.

> **"Interesting..."**

> **"Your greatest strength..."**

He looked past Aidan to the people standing beyond the arena.

> **"...has never been your blood."**

Aidan rose to his feet.

His crimson-and-gold eyes burned brighter than before.

He answered without hesitation.

> **"No."**

He looked toward Lyra.

Then Rez.

Then every member of his circle.

> **"My greatest strength..."**

A faint smile crossed his face.

> **"...is that I never have to stand alone."**

The Ash King said nothing.

For the first time since their meeting...

He simply watched.

Studying the Crimson King.

Trying to understand...

How a kingdom built on loyalty could become stronger than one built on fear and loss.

The duel...

Had entered a new phase.
