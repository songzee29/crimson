One journal...

Bound in cracked crimson leather...

Caught Aidan's attention.

Its title had nearly faded away.

**"The War Before Kings."**

He opened it.

Most pages had rotted.

Only one remained intact.

At the center...

A symbol unlike anything he'd ever seen.

Four interlocking circles.

One crimson.

One silver.

One black.

One empty.

Beneath it...

A single sentence.

> **"When the Fourth Throne rises... the world will belong to neither witch, vampire, nor wolf."**

Aidan's eyes narrowed.

There it was again.

The same mystery Azrath had spoken of.

Not an answer.

Just another clue.

He closed the journal.

The Legacy quietly recorded it.
