Commander Cassian intercepted a Veil convoy transporting an ancient prisoner.

The prisoner's wrists were bound in silver.

His eyes remained closed.

The report simply identified him as:

> **Specimen 001**

The oldest vampire ever captured.

The prisoner suddenly smiled.

Without opening his eyes.

"...You're the one..."

Cassian frowned.

"The one what?"

The vampire inhaled deeply.

"...Who smells like the King's grief."

Cassian's blood ran cold.

"I don't know what you're talking about."

The prisoner chuckled.

"You will."

Then...

He whispered something almost too quietly to hear.

> **"Tell the Crimson King..."**

A pause.

Then the oldest vampire opened one crimson eye.

> **"...his blood remembers us."**

---

Cassian immediately drew his sword.

But the prisoner simply closed his eye again.

As if he'd already said everything that mattered.

---

The chapter ended with Aidan asleep beside Lyra, finally at peace for the first time in years.

Miles away...

The oldest vampire smiled in chains.

Because he knew something Aidan did not.

The Vampire Authority wasn't waiting for Aidan to find it.

It had already begun searching for **him**.
