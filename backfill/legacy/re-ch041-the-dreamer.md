That night...

Cassian dreamed.

He stood inside a throne room he had never seen.

Black stone.

Crimson banners.

An empty throne.

Someone sat upon the steps below it.

Not on the throne itself.

Waiting.

Their face hidden.

Their voice calm.

> **"Tell me..."**

Cassian narrowed his eyes.

"...Who are you?"

The figure ignored the question.

Instead...

It asked one of its own.

> **"Do you know why kings fear shadows?"**

Cassian said nothing.

The figure slowly stood.

Still hidden.

> **"Because shadows..."**

A long pause.

> **"...are born from light."**

Cassian woke with a start.

His heart raced.

The room was empty.

Yet...

He could still hear footsteps fading into the darkness.
