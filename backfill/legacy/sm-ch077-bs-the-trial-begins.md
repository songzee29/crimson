## Chapter Seventy-Seven — The Trial Begins

Friday arrived with an energy unlike any other morning.

Students gathered outside the academy long before the first bell.

Some stretched nervously.

Others reviewed notes.

A few stood confidently with folded arms, convinced they had already won.

Banners bearing the academy crest lined the courtyard.

WELCOME, CANDIDATES.

THE LEADERSHIP TRIALS BEGIN TODAY.

Aidan adjusted the strap of his backpack.

Malik appeared beside him carrying two bottles of water.

Breakfast?

Skipped it.

Malik sighed dramatically.

That's illegal.

You can't conquer the world on an empty stomach.

I'm not trying to conquer the world.

Not yet.

The two laughed together.

Several students turned toward them.

Over the past few weeks, people had begun noticing something different about Aidan.

Not because he demanded attention.

Because he never seemed interested in receiving it.

Character.

Service.
