Miles away...

Inside the Veil Citadel...

The young captain stood before twelve elders.

No sword.

No armor.

Only silence.

One elder slammed a report onto the stone table.

"You disobeyed direct orders."

Another stood.

"You allowed the Darkheart heir to escape."

A third stepped forward.

"Explain."

The captain looked at each of them.

Completely calm.

"If the Council wanted him dead..."

"...you should have sent someone capable."

The chamber became silent.

One elder clenched his fists.

"You think you're stronger than the Council?"

"No."

"I think you underestimate him."

Another elder laughed.

"The heir is barely surviving."

The captain finally looked toward him.

"...Exactly."

"He's surviving."

"Imagine what happens..."

"...when he starts winning."

Nobody answered.

Because deep down...

Every elder had begun asking themselves the same question.
