Elias still stood inside his own illusion.

The burning house.

The little boy.

His younger brother.

Both calling for help.

Tears filled his eyes.

"I can't..."

"...choose."

The illusion smiled.

> **"Then someone dies."**

Silence.

Then...

Elias slowly wiped away his tears.

"No."

He stepped toward the fire.

"If I can't save both..."

"...then I'll die trying."

The illusion froze.

The world cracked.

Glass shattered around him.

The cathedral returned.

The Judge looked directly at Elias.

Then...

For the first time...

The ancient warrior spoke to someone besides Aidan.

> **"A man..."**

> **"...without Crimson blood."**

Another pause.

> **"...yet carrying the heart of a king."**

The Guardian lowered his head.

He had never seen that happen before.

Never.
