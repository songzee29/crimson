The priest disappeared into the monastery.

Moments later...

He returned carrying a book wrapped in black leather.

Unlike the Crimson Legacy...

This book radiated no power.

Only age.

He handed it to Aidan.

"I've protected this for seventy years."

Aidan carefully opened it.

The first page contained only one sentence.

---

> **"A vampire is not born the day he dies."**

---

Another page.

---

> **"He is born the day he begins losing reasons to remain human."**

---

Aidan slowly closed the book.

His chest tightened.

He whispered,

"...Then why do I feel it now?"

The priest answered.

"Because..."

"...your blood remembers before your body does."
