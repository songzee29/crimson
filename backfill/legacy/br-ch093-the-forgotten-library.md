Beyond the trial chamber lay another corridor.

This one wasn't filled with weapons.

It was filled with books.

Thousands of them.

Shelves stretched farther than the eye could see.

Ancient scrolls rested inside glass cases.

Maps covered entire walls.

Some books were so old they had turned crimson instead of brown.

Malik's eyes widened.

"...Okay."

"This is officially the coolest place I've ever accidentally bought."

Lyra ignored him.

She slowly approached one of the shelves.

"So much knowledge..."

Aidan reached for a nearby book.

Immediately—

The System interrupted.

---

> **NOTICE**

> **Knowledge is another form of power.**

> **Choose carefully.**

---

He paused.

There were hundreds of books.

Thousands.

He couldn't read all of them.

So...

He stopped looking for the biggest one.

Instead...

He chose the thinnest.

A worn journal bound in crimson leather.

The title simply read:

**Field Notes**

Nothing else.

The moment he opened it...

Words rewrote themselves into modern English.

---

*"Power is loud."*

*"Wisdom whispers."*

*"The strongest witches never chased bigger spells..."*

*"They mastered the ones they already possessed."*

---

Aidan slowly closed the journal.

That felt...

Important.

Not because it made him stronger.

Because it changed the way he thought.

---

The System quietly updated.

---

> **Hidden Quest Complete**

> **The Student Who Listened**

> **Reward Granted**

---

No ability appeared.

Instead...

Aidan felt something inside him settle.

Like the Crimson Legacy had acknowledged his choice.
