Rain fell softly over New Orleans.

Aidan sat alone on the roof of his apartment building.

The city stretched before him.

Music drifted from Bourbon Street.

The Mississippi shimmered beneath the moonlight.

Normally...

He would have enjoyed the view.

Tonight...

His thoughts were somewhere else.

The beast.

The cemetery.

The old man.

The Guardian accidentally calling Zee...

"Your father."

The words refused to leave him.

His right leg began bouncing unconsciously.

Faster.

Faster.

He didn't even realize he was doing it.

The System quietly appeared.

"You always come up here?"

Lyra's voice broke the silence.

She climbed onto the rooftop carrying two cups of coffee.

"I figured you'd be here."

Aidan accepted one.

"You know me too well."

She smiled.

"I know when you're pretending to be okay."

Neither spoke for a while.

The city did the talking for them.

Finally...

Lyra asked quietly,

"What scares you the most?"

Aidan answered without thinking.

"...Losing control."

She looked at him.

"Of your powers?"

He stared into the city lights.

"No."

"...Of myself."

Lyra didn't reply.

She simply sat beside him.

Sometimes...

Silence was the best answer.
