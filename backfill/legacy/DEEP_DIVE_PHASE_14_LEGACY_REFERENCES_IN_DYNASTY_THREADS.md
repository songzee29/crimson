## 2. Key cross-project decisions in source order

### July 27 — Dynasty is the prequel; Legacy's foundation is Zee's history
In the Dynasty Writers' Room branch, Zee explains that the Legacy work changed Zee's surname to **Darkheart** and helped reveal that Dynasty should lay the groundwork for what Aidan later encounters. The author wants readers to finish Zee's story and then recognize the history behind the decrees, bloodlines, crowns, and ancient laws Aidan inherits.

This makes Dynasty and Legacy related but distinct:
- Dynasty tells Zee's story in the Age of Kingdoms, thousands of years before Legacy.
- Legacy tells Aidan's story and must not become “another Zee.”
- Dynasty can establish the historical foundations that Legacy later inherits, but the books keep separate manuscripts and canon references.

### July 27 — Aidan is connected to Zee, but his story must remain his own
The same branch discusses whether Aidan should eclipse Zee in historical importance or stand as a different kind of king. The author frames the two journeys as emotionally different: Zee's story is about a world treating him as a monster, while Aidan's story inherits the consequences of the history Zee leaves behind.

Archive implication: preserve these as thematic cross-project decisions, not as permission to copy Dynasty scenes into Legacy or make Aidan's powers identical to Zee's.

### July 27 — one direct bridge, not a shared protagonist
Zee says the connection should be linked **one time and one time only**, while still deciding Aidan's birth order at that point. This is an early form of the later rule that Aidan is the hidden heir/son and the direct bridge between the two story generations.

Later Legacy rulings identify Aidan as Zee's oldest son and Queen Nymeris Thalryn's son. The earlier uncertainty over birth order must remain in the historical record; it is resolved by the October 12 current family canon.

### July 27 — Rez and Ner enter as children, not legendary figures
The Dynasty discussion says Rez and Ner should be introduced as children rather than legendary figures. This is a Dynasty staging decision that supports the later Legacy relationships, but it must not be used to rewrite Legacy's existing history or make Rez/Ner Zee's sons.

Current family lock from the October 12 Legacy record: Rez is not Zee's son; Ner Azrion is Zee's partner, not his son. Zee has three sons: Aidan Seyrion Darkheart, Zayrlen Keal Darkheart, and Azren Neyron Darkheart.

### August 4 — timeline separation
The author states that the world of Legacy is thousands of years after Dynasty and that House Darkheart is a kingdom of witches in Zee's world. This is a cross-project time boundary, not a request to merge the books' chapter sequences.

### August 15–16 — Dragon blood and lessons learned from Legacy
Dynasty messages revisit how Dragon nature enters Zee's history and how to handle pacing and chapter continuity. These are Dynasty-specific revisions, but they exist partly because the author wants to avoid repeating the Legacy workflow problems: source documents must be locked before writing, and a continuity correction must not force a whole-book restart.

For Legacy, the reusable workflow lesson is:
- preserve completed chapters;
- use a correction patch rather than restarting unless Zee explicitly orders a restart;
- distinguish locked canon, proposals, and completed prose;
- do not create multiple competing “master” copies of the Codex.

## 3. Cross-project rules that should be linked, not duplicated

1. **Order of work:** the October 8 decision is to finish Crimson Dynasty first, then return to Legacy. Legacy remains paused until that decision is amended.
2. **Separate protagonist/story:** Zee is the Dynasty protagonist; Aidan is the Legacy protagonist. Legacy must not become another Zee origin story.
3. **Shared history, separate manuscripts:** Dynasty is thousands of years earlier and supplies history that Aidan later inherits.
4. **Aidan's parentage:** current Legacy lock says Aidan Seyrion Darkheart is Zee's oldest son and Queen Nymeris Thalryn is his mother. Cassian is not Aidan's parent.
5. **Zee's other sons:** Zayrlen Keal and Azren Neyron belong to the later family canon; they are not Legacy's protagonists and must not be confused with Aidan.
6. **Rez and Ner:** Rez is Zee's close/childhood friend and Aidan's uncle/protector, not Zee's son. Ner Azrion is Zee's partner, not Zee's son.
7. **House name:** House Darkheart is current canon. The historical House Drakhar belongs to the *Forgotten Blood* alternate Legacy track and must not be retroactively substituted into the Dynasty timeline.
8. **Canon ownership:** each series has its own bible/decree. A shared fact can be cross-linked, but the two master decrees should not be merged into one document.
