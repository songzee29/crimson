High above New Orleans...

The Smiling Man stood upon the cathedral roof once more.

He looked toward Jackson Square.

Then...

He smiled wider than ever before.

"So..."

"...you refused."

He chuckled quietly.

"Good."

A soft voice came from the darkness behind him.

"Does that change anything?"

The Smiling Man moved another piece on his invisible chessboard.

"No."

"It changes everything."

He looked toward the moon.

"He's becoming exactly who Zee prayed he would become."

The unseen figure hesitated.

"Isn't that bad for us?"

The Smiling Man laughed.

"No."

"It's much worse."

The figure frowned.

"Why?"

The Smiling Man's smile slowly faded.

Because for the first time...

His voice carried genuine respect.

> **"Because broken kings..."**

He looked toward the sleeping city.

> **"...are the hardest to defeat."**

Miles below, Aidan emerged from beneath Jackson Square into the cool night air.

The city lights reflected on the Mississippi.

The sounds of New Orleans drifted through the streets.

He took a slow breath.

For the first time since Elias' death...

He didn't feel lighter.

He felt steadier.

He looked at his hands.

The same hands that had cast the Witch Laws.

The same hands that had carried his brother.

The same hands that had refused the throne.

Quietly, he formed the sign of **Authority**.

No enemy.

No spell.

Just a promise to himself.

Then he whispered one word into the night:

> **"Forward."**

The Witch Authority answered—not with power, but with calm.

And somewhere deep inside the Crimson Legacy, the Journal wrote:

> **"The greatest kings are not the ones who never look back..."**

> **"...but the ones who choose to keep walking anyway."**

The page turned.

The legend of the Crimson King had only just begun.
