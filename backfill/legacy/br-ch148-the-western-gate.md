Morning arrived quietly.

The golden sun climbed over House Darkheart.

The kingdom woke to another peaceful day.

Farmers entered the fields.

Students hurried toward the Crimson Academy.

The Royal Guards opened the western gate.

Everything...

Seemed normal.

---

High atop the castle...

Aidan still stood where he had been the night before.

He hadn't slept.

Rez walked onto the tower carrying two cups of coffee.

He handed one to Aidan.

"You've been staring at the same road for twelve hours."

Aidan accepted it.

"Thirteen."

Rez laughed.

"I walked into that one."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

**They're closer today.**

Aidan nodded.

*I know.*

---

Suddenly...

Kael's voice echoed from below.

"My King!"

Aidan and Rez looked down.

Kael was already running toward the tower.

His breathing was steady.

But urgency filled his face.

"They're here."

---

Without another word...

Aidan and Rez disappeared from the tower.

---

The western gate.

Royal Guards stood ready.

Not with weapons drawn...

But alert.

Malik.

Darius.

Mara.

Lyra.

Everyone had already gathered.

The massive gates slowly opened.

Two travelers stood beyond them.

Exactly as Aidan had sensed.

One wore a weathered gray cloak.

The other...

A long black coat with a hood hiding their face.

Neither carried visible weapons.

Neither showed fear.

Neither bowed.

---

Silence stretched between both sides.

The older traveler smiled first.

"So..."

"The Founder really did come himself."

Aidan studied them.

"I usually greet guests personally."

The old traveler laughed.

"I like him already."

---

The hooded figure never moved.

They simply watched Aidan.

Watching...

Like they were trying to solve a puzzle.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

*Rez.*

**I know.**

*Which one?*

**The hooded one.**

**That's the Crimson signature you followed.**

---

**Ding.**

Aidan looked directly at the hooded traveler.

"You've been looking for me."

A calm voice answered from beneath the hood.

"No."

A pause.

"I've been waiting for you."

---

Malik quietly leaned toward Darius.

"I don't know why..."

"...but I feel like something important is about to happen."

Darius nodded.

"Me too."

---

The hooded traveler slowly removed their gloves.

Their hands were scarred.

Old scars.

Not from battle.

From age.

Then...

The hood finally came down.

Everyone stared.

The traveler looked...

Young.

No older than Aidan.

Yet their silver eyes carried centuries of experience.

Rez froze.

Not because he recognized the face.

Because he recognized the feeling.

Inside Aidan's mind...

**...I've felt that presence before...**

Aidan remained calm.

*Memory?*

**No...**

**Respect.**

---

The traveler smiled faintly.

"So..."

"The Crimson Assassin still lives."

Rez stepped forward.

"You know me?"

"I know of you."

"You've changed."

Rez folded his arms.

"So have you."

The traveler laughed.

"I suppose we both have."

---

Lyra quietly stepped beside Aidan.

"Friend?"

Aidan answered honestly.

"I don't know."

---

The traveler finally looked directly at Aidan.

"I didn't come to fight."

"I came to ask a question."

Aidan nodded.

"Ask."

The traveler smiled.

"If another Founder appeared tomorrow..."

"...would you call them family..."

"...or your enemy?"

The courtyard became silent.

Even the wind stopped.

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez spoke one quiet sentence.

**Be careful...**

**That's not curiosity.**

**They're measuring your heart.**

Aidan looked into the stranger's silver eyes.

Then smiled.

"My answer depends."

The traveler tilted their head.

"On what?"

Aidan's voice remained calm.

"Whether they came to build..."

"...or to destroy."

For the first time...

The traveler genuinely smiled.

"A good answer."

Then...

Without warning...

They held out their hand.

Not to attack.

To shake Aidan's.

"My name..."

"...is Orion."

The Crimson King looked at the offered hand.

Then at Rez.

Rez gave the slightest nod.

Aidan reached forward.

The moment their hands met—

**Ding.**
