That night...

The warehouse felt too big.

One chair remained empty.

No one sat in it.

No one moved it.

No one touched it.

Malik quietly cooked dinner.

He burned it.

Nobody laughed.

Lyra stared at the empty chair while pretending to read.

Sienna cleaned weapons that were already clean.

Aidan climbed onto the roof.

Alone.

His right leg didn't shake anymore.

It simply hung over the edge.

Still.

The stillness scared him more than the shaking ever had.

The System finally appeared.

Not broken.

Not glitching.

Changed.

Aidan stared.

"...Survived?"

He laughed.

A hollow, broken sound.

"No."

He whispered into the night.

"I didn't survive."

The System remained silent.

No argument.

No correction.

Just another line.

---

**Your heart disagrees.**

For the first time...

The System didn't feel like a game.

It felt like an ancient witness.

Watching.

Recording.

Learning.

Aidan closed his eyes.

Below him, New Orleans slowly came back to life.

Music returned to Bourbon Street.

The river flowed.

People laughed.

The world continued.

Even though his had stopped.

Far away...

Commander Cassian stood alone at his brother's grave marker, hidden in the darkness where no one could see him.

He placed a single white lily on the fresh earth.

Then quietly said the words he had never been brave enough to speak while Elias was alive.

> "I'm sorry... big brother."

He remained there until dawn.

Not as a commander.

Not as Aidan's rival.

Just a younger brother mourning the man he had lost.

And somewhere deep within the Crimson Legacy, beyond the Witch Authority, beyond the cracked Vampire Seal, beyond the sleeping vessel...

Rez slept on.

Unaware that the world had changed forever.

But the Soul Anchor pulsed once.

Slowly.

Patiently.

Waiting for a century to pass before one final promise could be fulfilled.
