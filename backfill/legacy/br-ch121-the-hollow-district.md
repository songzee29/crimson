Morning brought heavy fog.

Entire streets had been abandoned.

Windows were boarded shut.

Old newspapers rolled through empty intersections.

The silence felt...

Wrong.

Aidan activated Curse Sense.

Nothing.

No curses.

No lingering magic.

Nothing at all.

Which somehow frightened him even more.

Lyra whispered,

"Too quiet."

Malik nervously adjusted the backpack carrying supplies.

"I hate when she's right."

Sienna stopped walking.

"Wait."

Everyone froze.

Footsteps.

Not theirs.

Slow.

Dragging.

Coming from inside an abandoned hotel.

Aidan raised one hand.

"Stay behind me."

The others didn't argue.

---

The hotel door creaked open by itself.

Darkness waited inside.

Then...

A little boy walked out.

No older than seven.

Barefoot.

Wearing pajamas.

He looked directly at Aidan.

"...Help..."

Before anyone could move—

Something pulled him backward into the darkness.

His scream echoed through the empty streets.

---

Aidan didn't hesitate.

He ran.
