Nobody reached for a weapon.

There was something peaceful about her.

Ancient.

Wise.

She looked at Lyra.

Then Malik.

Then Sienna.

Then Elias.

Finally...

She stopped in front of Aidan.

"You have his eyes."

Aidan sighed.

"Everybody keeps saying that."

She laughed.

"I imagine they do."

He looked around the endless library.

"What is this place?"

She slowly turned.

Running her fingers across the ancient shelves.

"This..."

"...is where history comes when the world forgets it."

Aidan frowned.

"I don't understand."

"You will."

She looked back toward him.

"But not today."

She held out an old leather journal.

"This belongs to you."

Aidan accepted it carefully.

There was no title.

Only a crimson crest pressed into the cover.

He opened the first page.

Blank.

The second page.

Blank.

Every page...

Blank.

He looked up.

"It's empty."

The Librarian smiled.

"No."

"It hasn't been written yet."
