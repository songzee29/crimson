Rez had barely spoken since Orion arrived.

His fingers still rested on the little wooden wolf.

His thumb traced the worn edges.

Then...

Another memory returned.

---

Rain.

Cold.

A campfire.

A younger Rez sat sharpening a blade.

Across the fire...

Orion.

Younger.

Laughing.

"You'll wear that blade out."

Rez looked up.

"Then I'll sharpen another."

Orion tossed him a loaf of bread.

"You always prepare for tomorrow."

Rez caught it.

"Someone has to."

Orion smiled.

"That's why they trust you."

The memory broke apart.

---

Rez inhaled sharply.

He looked across the council table.

"...We've shared a campfire."

Orion smiled warmly.

"Many."

Rez shook his head.

"I still don't remember when."

"You will."
