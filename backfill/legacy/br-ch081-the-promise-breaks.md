Back in New Orleans...

Another messenger arrived.

Then another.

Then another.

Five villages.

Seven villages.

Twelve.

All crying for help.

All at once.

The command room fell silent.

There weren't enough people.

There wasn't enough time.

Malik slammed his fist onto the table.

"We can't be everywhere!"

Aidan stared at the map.

Every location was hundreds of miles apart.

Impossible.

Someone had planned this.

Someone wanted exactly this moment.

Lyra quietly asked,

"...What do we do?"

Aidan closed his eyes.

He remembered his promise.

> **If you call for House Darkheart...**
>
> **...I will come.**

His hands trembled.

Because for the first time...

He couldn't.
