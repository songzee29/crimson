As they entered the village...

People stared.

Not with hatred.

Not with relief.

Confusion.

Cassian emerged from the center of Black Hollow.

For several seconds...

Neither man spoke.

The last time they had stood this close...

History itself had judged them.

Cassian looked exhausted.

His beard had grown.

Dark circles surrounded his eyes.

The once-proud leader looked like a man who hadn't truly rested in months.

His eyes shifted to Lyra.

Then Rez.

Finally...

Back to Aidan.

"...You came."

Aidan nodded.

"...Your people are disappearing."

Cassian let out a bitter laugh.

"I figured you'd say something about the curse."

Aidan answered calmly.

"...This isn't the curse."

Cassian looked up.

For the first time...

He believed him.
