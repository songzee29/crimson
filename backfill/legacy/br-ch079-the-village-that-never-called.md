A week later...

The impossible happened.

An entire village disappeared.

Not burned.

Not slaughtered.

Not cursed.

Gone.

Homes remained.

Food still sat on tables.

Children's toys rested in the streets.

But every person...

Had vanished.

No magic.

No blood.

No souls.

As though the village had never existed.
