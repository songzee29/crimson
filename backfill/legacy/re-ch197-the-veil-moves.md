Far beneath the Veil Citadel...

The Council had finally gathered.

Twelve elders.

One empty throne.

The captain stood before them.

"The Second Seal has awakened."

One elder nodded.

"Expected."

"The Heir grows."

"Expected."

Another elder stepped forward.

"The Circle grows stronger."

Silence.

Then...

The oldest elder spoke.

"Then..."

"...break the Circle."

The chamber became still.

The captain slowly lifted his head.

"What are your orders?"

> **"Do not target the Heir."**

Another elder continued.

> **"Target everyone around him."**

Another.

> **"Destroy his foundation."**

The oldest elder finished.

> **"...and let grief complete what our blades cannot."**

The captain closed his eyes.

He understood immediately.
