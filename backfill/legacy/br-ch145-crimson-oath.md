Aidan sprinted through the swamp.

The Hollow King noticed.

Too late.

It abandoned everyone else.

Charging straight toward him.

The ground shook beneath every step.

Aidan reached the altar.

Ancient runes covered every inch.

One sentence stood out.

The System translated it.

Aidan stared.

"What..."

The Hollow King roared behind him.

No more time.

He placed his hand on the altar.

Nothing happened.

He frowned.

Then...

Without hesitation...

He drew one of Lyra's small daggers across his palm.

Blood splashed across the stone.

The altar awakened.

The entire swamp shook violently.

The Hollow King screamed.

Not in anger.

In fear.

For the first time...

It knew it could die.
