Night fell.

Neither of them remembered seeing the sun set.

One moment it was afternoon.

The next—

darkness.

Aidan frowned.

"That's weird."

"What is?"

"The time."

Lyra froze.

Because she had noticed it too.

Hours were disappearing.

Not memory loss.

Something stranger.

The city itself seemed broken here.

Twisted.

Disconnected.

As if reality didn't flow normally.

The further they walked—

the less familiar the world became.

Road signs stopped making sense.

Street names changed every time they looked away.

Buildings appeared on one side of the street—

then appeared somewhere else moments later.

The System finally spoke again.

---

> WARNING

> LOCATION NOT FOUND

> LOCATION SHOULD NOT EXIST

---

Aidan stopped walking.

For the first time since opening the Crimson Book—

the System sounded almost concerned.

Lyra moved closer.

"What now?"

Aidan stared at the message.

Then another notification appeared.

---

> HISTORICAL RECORD FAILURE

> NO DATA AVAILABLE

---

The pull in his chest intensified.

Not pain.

Recognition.

Something nearby knew him.
