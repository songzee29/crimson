Seraphine never attacked.

Instead...

She walked circles around Aidan.

Hands behind her back.

Studying him.

"So..."

She looked directly into his crimson eyes.

"You enjoy killing."

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"...Yes."

"You enjoy hunting."

"...Yes."

"You feel nothing."

"...Correct."

She smiled.

"Excellent."

Without warning...

She snapped her fingers.

The mountain exploded.

Hundreds...

No...

Thousands...

Of vampire knights climbed from beneath the snow.

Every one wearing ancient black armor.

Every one carrying blood-forged weapons.

Every one kneeling before their queen.

---

She pointed toward Aidan.

> **"Survive."**

Then she sat upon an ice throne.

Crossed one leg over the other.

And watched.
