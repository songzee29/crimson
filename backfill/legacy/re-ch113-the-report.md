Morning broke over New Orleans.

The city had begun healing again.

Markets reopened.

Children laughed.

Musicians filled the French Quarter with life.

For a moment...

It almost felt like the darkness had passed.

---

Inside the command hall...

Aidan stood over dozens of mission reports.

One folder remained unopened.

Black.

No insignia.

Only one name.

**Cassian.**

He stared at it for several seconds.

Then opened it.

---

"Spotted near the abandoned cathedral."

"Unknown individual seen with him."

"No civilian casualties."

"No confirmed attacks."

"No further movement."

---

Lyra walked in carrying fresh coffee.

She noticed the report.

Her smile disappeared.

"...You're still having him watched?"

Aidan never looked up.

"Yes."

Lyra folded her arms.

"Why?"

A long silence.

"...Because people like Cassian don't stop."

---

Malik walked in behind her.

He glanced at the report and groaned.

"Man..."

"Why are we keeping tabs on somebody you don't even like?"

The room became quiet.

Aidan slowly closed the file.

Then looked at both of them.

His crimson eyes were cold.

Not angry.

Resolved.

> **"I'm not watching Cassian because I care about him."**

Another pause.

> **"I'm watching him because..."**

He looked toward the cathedral.

> **"...the next time I see him..."**

Silence.

> **"...I'll finish what I started."**

Nobody spoke.

Because nobody had forgotten Elias.

---

The Guardian quietly nodded.

"The First Decree..."

"...hasn't ended."

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"No."

"...It hasn't."
