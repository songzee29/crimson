Hundreds of miles away...

Black Hollow remained peaceful.

Cassian walked through his village.

People greeted him warmly.

Children ran through the streets.

His partner smiled as he returned home.

Everything looked perfect.

Everything felt...

Wrong.

He stopped in the center of the road.

The laughter around him suddenly sounded distant.

The warmth of the sun faded.

His chest tightened.

Not from pain.

From emptiness.

His hand instinctively touched the Darkheart Brand hidden beneath his sleeve.

It wasn't burning.

It was...

Waiting.

Cassian whispered to himself,

"...Why can't I enjoy this anymore?"

No one answered.

The curse didn't speak.

It didn't need to.

It was doing exactly what it had been created to do.

Not destroy his body.

Slowly strip away his peace.

One ordinary day at a time.
