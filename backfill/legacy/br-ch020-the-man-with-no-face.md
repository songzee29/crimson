The following night he returned to Lyra.

---

But someone got there first.

---

The abandoned church stood open.

---

The front doors hung crooked.

---

One candle remained burning inside.

---

Everything else was dark.

---

Aidan immediately knew something was wrong.

---

The pressure behind his eyes intensified.

---

His Witch Sight activated automatically.

---

Crimson cracks spread across reality.

---

Then he saw him.

---

Standing inside the sanctuary.

---

The man from across the street.

---

Black coat.

Tall.

Still.

---

Waiting.

---

This time there was no distance.

---

No rain.

---

No streetlight.

---

Only twenty feet between them.

---

And as Aidan stared—

the man's face disappeared again.

---

A blank void.

---

An absence.

---

Like reality refused to define him.

---

Then the man smiled.

Even without a face.

Aidan somehow knew he was smiling.

---

"Found you."

---

The words echoed unnaturally.

---

Like multiple voices speaking at once.

---

Aidan took a step back.

---

The stranger took one forward.

---

"Interesting."

---

Another step.

---

"Very interesting."

---

The pressure in the room became unbearable.

---

Then a knife flew through the darkness.

---

The stranger caught it effortlessly.

---

Lyra emerged from the shadows.

---

And for the first time—

Aidan saw genuine fear on her face.

---

"Run."
