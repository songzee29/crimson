The crimson light faded.

The courtyard remained alive long after sunset.

Lanterns glowed beside stacked books and covered furniture while instructors quietly finished relocating the last materials from the eastern halls.

For the first time…

The academy looked as though it were preparing for winter.

Even though spring had only begun.

⸻

Far beyond Blackwood Academy…

The Seven gathered once more.

The abandoned chamber remained unchanged.

Seven seats.

Seven symbols.

Only six occupied.

The seventh remained empty.

Waiting.

The Archivist slowly closed another ancient record.

“The academy has begun adapting.”

A broad-shouldered elder folded his arms.

“They’re faster than expected.”

“They always were.”

Another member looked toward the empty chair.

“The Keeper has changed.”

“He has.”

“Because of Rowan?”

“Because of all of them.”

Silence settled.

Finally…

The youngest voice among the Seven spoke.

“Then we’re running out of time.”

No one corrected him.

⸻

The elder who had watched Aidan from the rain-soaked cliffs rose slowly.

“For generations…”

“We observed.”

“We measured.”

“We waited.”

His gaze rested upon the empty chair.

“We can no longer remain only witnesses.”

The room became still.

The Archivist looked toward him.

“What are you proposing?”

He answered without hesitation.

“We stop watching the academy.”

“And begin protecting it.”

Several heads lifted.

One member frowned.

“That changes everything.”

“Yes.”

“It does.”

⸻

Back at Blackwood Academy…

Evening patrols had doubled.

Professor Bennett personally inspected each restricted passage before locking it.

Professor Helena quietly updated every duty roster.

Headmaster Aldren canceled faculty leave until further notice.

No announcement explained why.

The instructors simply remained.

Together.

⸻

Aidan crossed the courtyard carrying the last crate from the old archive.

Cassian intercepted him halfway.

“Give me that.”

“I’ve got it.”

“I know.”

Cassian took one side anyway.

“So now…”

“We’ve got it.”

Aidan smiled.

Without another word…

They carried the weight together.

Lyra waited at the library doors to hold them open.

Malik cleared space on the shelves.

Elias checked every catalog number before each volume disappeared into its new place.

No one stood idle.

No one worked alone.

Master Rowan watched the five of them from the upper balcony.

Headmaster Aldren quietly joined him.

“They’re ready.”

Rowan’s expression remained thoughtful.

“They’re becoming ready.”

“There is a difference.”

Aldren looked toward the mountain.

“Will it be enough?”

The old traveler answered honestly.

“I don’t know.”

His eyes returned to Aidan.

“But whatever happens next…”

“He won’t face it as the boy who first arrived carrying that crimson book.”

Below…

The five friends finished shelving the final volume.

Cassian stretched his aching shoulders.

“I’m officially declaring books heavier than monsters.”

Elias snorted.

“You’ve never fought a monster.”

Cassian grinned.

“Then I’m making an educated guess.”

The others laughed.

Aidan laughed with them.

Longer than usual.

As though some part of him already understood…

How precious that sound had become.

Far beneath Blackwood Academy…

The ancient heartbeat echoed.

The second heartbeat answered.

Between them…

The stone shifted once more.

Not in the eastern halls.

Not beneath the archives.

Directly below…

The old oak.

For the first time since the convergence began…

The roots of the ancient tree trembled.
