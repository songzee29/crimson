The messenger was gone.

The city had returned to its celebration.

But Aidan hadn't moved.

His eyes remained fixed on the empty street where the crimson mist had vanished.

Something about those words...

"...The throne has remained empty long enough."

...wouldn't leave his mind.

---

That evening...

The Circle gathered inside the command hall.

No one spoke.

Finally...

The Guardian broke the silence.

"I've been keeping something from you."

Malik folded his arms.

"That ain't exactly surprising."

A faint smile crossed the old man's face.

"No..."

"...because it wasn't my secret to tell."

He slowly reached beneath his cloak.

Not for a weapon.

For a weathered piece of black stone.

The moment it touched the table...

Every candle in the room dimmed.

Crimson veins spread through the stone.

Ancient.

Beautiful.

Terrifying.

Lyra whispered,

"...What is that?"

The Guardian stared at Aidan.

"It is the oldest surviving seal in existence."

A pause.

"It belonged to a family..."

"...older than every modern kingdom."

---

He turned the stone over.

A crest was carved into it.

A black crown.

A crimson eye.

A wolf beneath it.

Wrapped in thorned crimson roots.

The room suddenly felt colder.

The Guardian's voice became almost reverent.

"...House Darkheart."

Silence.

Aidan blinked.

"...Darkheart?"

The name felt...

Familiar.

As if he'd heard it in a dream he couldn't remember.

---

"No..." Malik whispered.

"So the stories were true..."

Sienna frowned.

"You've heard of them?"

Malik nodded slowly.

"My grandmother used to threaten us with bedtime stories."

He laughed nervously.

"If we lied..."

"...she'd say House Darkheart would remember."

No one laughed.

---

The Guardian looked directly at Aidan.

"You thought the Crimson King was one man."

He gently placed a hand on the crest.

"He wasn't."

"He was the Head of House Darkheart."

"And now..."

His crimson eyes met Aidan's.

"...so are you."

---

The Crimson Legacy appeared before Aidan.

Not with its familiar crimson borders.

This time...

Everything was black.

Ancient runes crawled across the screen.

One line slowly formed.

---

**HEAD OF HOUSE DARKHEART**

**STATUS: ACKNOWLEDGED**

---

No reward.

No fanfare.

Only acknowledgment.

The title vanished.

Aidan said nothing.

But somewhere deep inside...

Something answered.
