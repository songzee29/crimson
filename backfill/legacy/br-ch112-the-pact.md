Night fell again.

Cassian returned to the abandoned cathedral.

Different.

His movements were sharper.

His hearing impossibly precise.

His hunger...

Still there.

But quieter.

Azrath stood waiting.

"You've taken your first step."

Cassian looked at his own hands.

"...I'm stronger."

"Yes."

"I'm faster."

"Yes."

"I can hear everything."

"Yes."

Cassian slowly looked up.

"And this is only the beginning?"

Azrath smiled.

For the first time since his freedom...

It wasn't patient.

It was satisfied.

"No."

He stepped closer.

"This..."

"...isn't even the Vampire Curse."

Cassian's expression changed.

"What?"

Azrath's smile widened.

"What you drank..."

"...was only an invitation."

Silence.

"The true curse..."

He looked toward New Orleans.

Toward Aidan.

"...requires the blood..."

"...of a king."

The wind howled through the cathedral.

Cassian followed Azrath's gaze.

Toward the city.

Toward the Crimson King.

And for the first time...

The thought entered his mind...

Not as a whisper.

Not as manipulation.

But as his own desire.

> **If Aidan dies...**

> **...I become everything he was.**

Azrath said nothing.

He didn't need to.

The seed had become a root.

And it had begun to grow. 👑🩸
