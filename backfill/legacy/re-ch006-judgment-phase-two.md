The night air was still.

The fire between Aidan and Cassian burned quietly.

Neither king reached for a weapon.

The years of hatred...

The years of blood...

Had been replaced by something far more dangerous.

Understanding.

Cassian stared into the flames.

"So..."

"...this is how it begins."

Aidan didn't answer.

He couldn't.

The decree had called him here.

But the man sitting across from him...

Was no longer the same Cassian who had murdered him.

He had rebuilt.

He had changed.

He had found peace.

And now...

The Darkheart Curse demanded its price.

---

Without warning...

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

Its pages opened by themselves.

Not gently.

Not violently.

Inevitably.

Crimson letters slowly formed across the page.

---

> **NOTICE**

---

## **Judgment Phase Two... Active**

---

The fire dimmed.

Time itself seemed to slow.

Another page turned.

New words appeared.

---

## **Soul Link Confirmed**

---

**Crimson Heir**
**Aidan Darkheart**

**Legacy Guardian**
**Rez**

**Status**
**ACTIVE**

Aidan frowned.

"...Soul Link?"

Rez blinked.

For the first time in a long while...

He looked genuinely surprised.

"...I thought you knew."

Aidan looked at him.

"Knew what?"

Rez scratched the back of his neck.

"When your Vampire Authority reached one hundred percent..."

"...the Legacy finished something it started the day you opened the Crimson Book."

He pointed toward his own chest.

"It bound our souls."

Silence.

"That's why I can see what you see."

"That's why I can hear what you hear."

"That's why I always know when your heart changes."

He smiled.

"I've been linked to you ever since."

Aidan stared at him.

"...And you waited until now to tell me?"

Rez shrugged with a grin.

"You never asked."

Lyra couldn't help but laugh.

Even in the middle of Judgment...

Rez somehow found a way to be himself.
