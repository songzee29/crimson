They burst out of the folded corridor into an abandoned upper tunnel.

Fresh air hit them like a shock.

Lyra collapsed against the wall, breathing hard.

Aidan stood a few steps away.

Still.

Silent.

He looked at his hands.

Flexed his fingers.

Like he was testing whether they belonged to him.

Lyra looked up at him.

“Do you remember what you lost?” she asked carefully.

Aidan paused.

His expression tightened slightly.

Then loosened.

Then failed to form a full answer.

“I… don’t know,” he said again.

But this time, it wasn’t confusion.

It was emptiness trying to describe itself.

Lyra closed her eyes briefly.

Because she understood what just happened.

The System didn’t take everything.

It took the *foundation that lets everything else connect.*

A voice suddenly echoed in Aidan’s mind.

Not loud.

Not fully formed.

Just a faint residue of something that wasn’t there anymore.

And somewhere deep inside him—

something that should have been asleep shifted slightly.

Not awake.

Not conscious.

Just aware that something had been removed.

The Veil recorded a single new entry.

> SUBJECT: FRACTURE-HEIR  
> STATUS: INCOMPLETE CORE  
> CLASSIFICATION: UNSTABLE EXISTENCE

And for the first time in a long time…

they stopped calling him a mistake.

And started calling him something else.

A possibility.
