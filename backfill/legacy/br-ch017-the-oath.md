That night...

Unable to sleep...

Aidan walked alone through the sanctuary that had become his refuge in New Orleans.

He looked down at his hands.

The same hands that had saved people.

The same hands still stained in his memory with Elias' blood.

He quietly whispered,

"I don't know what House Darkheart is..."

"...but if I'm really its head..."

He closed his eyes.

"...then I'll build it differently."

No echoes answered.

No vision appeared.

Only the soft wind moving through the empty halls.

For the first time...

Aidan wasn't making a promise to the Legacy.

He was making one to himself.

"I won't rule through fear."

"I won't seek power for power's sake."

"I'll protect this city."

"My family."

"My people."

"And if history remembers House Darkheart..."

A slow breath.

"...it will remember that we chose mercy before blood."

Far away...

Within the deepest chamber of the Crimson Legacy...

The Journal opened by itself.

A single sentence wrote itself across an empty page.

> **"The Head of House Darkheart has made his first oath."**

Then, beneath it, in older ink that looked centuries old, another line slowly appeared—as if the Legacy itself were answering:

> **"Every House writes its own legend."**

The book closed.

In the darkness beyond the page...

A chain snapped.

Not 001's.

Not the Vampire Seal.

A different chain.

One that had quietly bound House Darkheart to its past.

And somewhere beyond the reach of memory...

A father's final gift waited patiently.

Not another power.

Not another weapon.

Just the truth.

The truth Aidan had spent his whole life searching for.

And the day that truth finally reached him...

The boy named Aidan would become more than the Crimson King.

He would finally understand...

What it meant...

To come home.
