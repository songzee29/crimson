We don't open with Aidan.

We open with **Cassian**.

For the first time since his defeat, readers see what he's built.

- His throne.
- His castle.
- His coven.
- His queen.
- His children.
- His people laughing.
- A peaceful kingdom.

For a few pages, readers almost forget he's the antagonist.

They think:

> "He's finally moved on."

Exactly when they begin believing that...

A messenger arrives.

Not panicked.

Respectful.

He kneels before the throne.

> "My King..."

> "The Sanctuary of Truth has acknowledged the Crimson Heir."

Silence.

Cassian's smile disappears.

Not because he hates Aidan.

Because...

**His peace just died.**

The decree has begun.

Not through magic.

Not because Aidan did anything.

Because the truth of Aidan's existence reached Cassian.

The words Aidan spoke as a human begin collecting their debt.

> **"You shall never know peace..."**

Cassian dismisses everyone.

Walks to a balcony.

Looks over the kingdom he built.

And for the first time in years...

He can't enjoy it.

The curse doesn't tell him where Aidan is.

It doesn't command him.

It simply destroys the peace he thought he had found.

### End of the Chapter

> "Prepare the coven."

No speeches.

No rage.

No theatrics.

Just...

> "Prepare the coven."

Readers know exactly what that means.

The king is going to war again.
