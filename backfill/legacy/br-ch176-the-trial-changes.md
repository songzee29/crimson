Without warning...

The Judge slammed his sword into the floor.

The cathedral disappeared.

Everyone froze.

They were no longer underground.

Instead...

Each member of the Crimson Circle stood alone.

Different places.

Different worlds.

Different trials.

---

### Lyra

She stood inside a royal throne room.

A crimson crown rested upon her head.

Servants bowed.

The room chanted.

> **"Queen."**

> **"Queen."**

> **"Queen."**

Then the throne beside her appeared.

Empty.

Blood covered it.

A voice whispered...

> **"Rule alone."**

---

### Malik

He stood back in the warehouse.

Empty.

Silent.

No laughter.

No family.

Just him.

A voice echoed...

> **"Everyone dies around you."**

---

### Sienna

She stood surrounded by Veil hunters.

Each one called her a traitor.

Each one drew a blade.

---

### Elias

He stood in front of a burning house.

A little boy screamed from inside.

Another voice came from the opposite direction.

His younger brother.

Calling his name.

He couldn't save both.

His entire body froze.

"...No..."

---

### Aidan

Darkness.

Nothing else.

Then...

A little boy appeared.

Seven years old.

Curly black hair.

Crying.

Covered in dirt.

Aidan stared.

"...Who are you?"

The little boy looked up.

His crimson eyes met Aidan's.

Then whispered...

> **"...I'm you."**
