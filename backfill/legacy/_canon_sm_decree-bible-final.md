## Decree LI — The Fourth Authority

The Fourth Authority of the Crimson Legacy is the **Crimson Demon Eclipse Authority**.

It does not exist at the beginning of the Crimson Legacy.

It cannot be awakened through sacrifice, death, or curses like the first Three Crimson Authorities.

Instead, it awakens only when Aidan achieves his **First Crimson Ascension**.

The Crimson Demon Eclipse Authority serves as the bridge between the Three Crimson Authorities and the Crimson Ascensions.

Without it, the path beyond the First Crimson Ascension remains forever sealed.
