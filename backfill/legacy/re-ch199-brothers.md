Rain poured over the warehouse.

Power had gone out across half the city.

Everyone sat around candles.

Malik had somehow found marshmallows.

"Indoor campfire."

Lyra raised an eyebrow.

"That's not a campfire."

"It is spiritually."

Elias laughed.

"Everything is spiritual to you."

"It is."

Aidan quietly watched the rain.

Elias sat beside him.

"You've been thinking again."

"...Yeah."

"What now?"

Aidan looked out over the city.

"I'm scared."

Elias didn't laugh.

He didn't make a joke.

He simply asked...

"Of what?"

A long silence followed.

"...That one day..."

"...I won't be able to save everyone."

Elias looked toward the storm.

Then smiled.

"You won't."

Aidan looked at him.

"...What?"

"You won't save everyone."

The words hurt.

Elias continued.

"You'll fail."

"You'll lose people."

"You'll make mistakes."

"But..."

He looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

> **"That doesn't mean you stop trying."**

Silence.

Aidan looked away.

"...How do you know?"

Elias smiled sadly.

"Because I learned that a long time ago."

Then he stood.

"Come on."

"Malik's about to burn the marshmallows."

"They're already burning."

"...Exactly."

Aidan laughed.

And followed him.

Far away...

Commander Cassian stood alone beneath the moon.

He unfolded a mission file.

Inside...

Four photographs.

Lyra.

Malik.

Sienna.

Elias.

He stared at them for a long time.

Then slowly closed the folder.

His voice was barely a whisper.

> "...There has to be another way."

No one answered.

Because there wasn't.

Somewhere in the darkness, the Smiling Man watched from a cathedral rooftop.

He moved one final piece across his invisible chessboard.

A white knight.

Then smiled beneath his porcelain mask.

> **"Check."**

Not checkmate.

Just...

Check.

Because the game had entered its final phase.

The Witch Arc was no longer building toward tragedy.

It had quietly begun counting down to it.
