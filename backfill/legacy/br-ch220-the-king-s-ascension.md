## **KING'S ASCENSION**

---

The moment Aidan accepted it...

His body changed.

---

Outside...

The ground exploded beneath his feet.

A crimson shockwave erupted from his body.

The cathedral walls cracked.

Every remaining stained-glass window shattered.

A hurricane of crimson magic spiraled into the heavens.

---

Lyra was thrown backward.

Malik slammed into a broken pillar.

The Guardian planted his staff into the earth just to remain standing.

"What... is happening?!"

No one answered.

Because no one knew.

---

Aidan dropped to one knee.

His muscles tightened so violently that the sound of tendons stretching echoed through the cathedral.

CRACK!

His spine arched.

Bones shifted beneath his skin.

Not breaking.

Becoming stronger.

His veins glowed crimson before fading into obsidian black.

His fingernails lengthened into razor-like claws...

Then returned to normal.

His fangs extended farther than before...

Then settled.

His eyes...

Changed.

The crimson became deeper.

Almost impossible to look into.

Not because they glowed.

Because they felt endless.

---

The pressure doubled.

Then tripled.

The entire city felt it.

---

Across New Orleans...

Every vampire stopped.

Every witch looked toward the cathedral.

Every werewolf instinctively raised its head to the moon.

Even people with no magic...

Felt their hearts race for reasons they couldn't explain.

---

Cassian collapsed to one knee.

The Darkheart Brand on his forearms ignited.

He screamed.

"What... is this?!"

The brand wasn't hurting him.

It was reacting.

It recognized that the one who had cursed him...

Had just become something greater.

---

Miles away...

Azrath slowly smiled.

Then...

His smile disappeared.

For the first time...

He frowned.

"...No..."

The pressure continued rising.

"...This wasn't supposed to happen."

Even he...

Hadn't expected the Legacy to respond like this.

Outside...

The crimson aura slowly collapsed back into Aidan's body.

Silence returned.

Dust settled.

The moon appeared once more.

Aidan slowly stood.

He looked exactly the same.

Yet...

No one dared speak.

Even the air around him felt heavier.

---

He flexed his fingers once.

The sound alone echoed through the cathedral.

He looked toward the shattered entrance.

Then...

Toward the city.

Thousands of heartbeats.

Thousands of choices.

Thousands of hunts.

A slow smile crossed his face.

Cold.

Confident.

Emotionless.

"...Better."

One word.

Nothing more.

Then...

He vanished.

The sonic boom that followed ripped the cathedral doors from their hinges.
