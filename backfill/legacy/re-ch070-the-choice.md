Azrath placed the small crimson vial on the table.

The dark liquid inside continued moving as though it possessed a heartbeat.

"This..."

"...will make you stronger than you've ever dreamed."

Cassian reached for it.

Azrath placed one finger on the glass.

Stopping him.

"Not yet."

Frustration flashed across Cassian's face.

"You keep saying that."

"Because you still want power."

Silence.

Azrath leaned forward.

"When you stop wanting power..."

"...and start needing it..."

"I'll give it to you."

He stood.

Straightened his jacket.

"And on that day..."

"...you won't ask what it costs."

He began walking away.

Cassian called after him.

"Why are you helping me?"

Azrath stopped at the doorway.

Without looking back...

He answered.

> **"Because every Darkheart deserves an ending..."**

A pause.

> **"...and I intend to write Aidan's."**

He disappeared into the night.

Cassian looked down at the empty chessboard.

For the first time...

He wasn't thinking about defeating Aidan.

He was thinking about becoming someone...

Who could.

And somewhere inside the Sanctuary...

The Crimson Legacy flickered.

A page turned by itself.

A new warning slowly wrote itself across the parchment.

> **HOUSE ALERT**
>
> **AN ANCIENT ADVERSARY HAS RETURNED.**
>
> **JUDGMENT APPROACHES.**

The ink dried.

The book closed.

And outside...

The bells of New Orleans rang.

Not because the hour had come.

Because history...

Had begun repeating itself.
