Cassian finally asked the question that had been growing inside him.

"...Why do you hate House Darkheart?"

Azrath didn't answer immediately.

Instead...

He slowly removed one black glove.

His palm carried a scar.

A burn.

In the shape of the Darkheart Crest.

Cassian stared.

"What happened?"

Azrath looked at the scar for a long moment.

Then quietly said,

"...They judged me."

Silence.

"They decided..."

"...that I should never walk beneath the sun again."

His smile returned.

"But kings..."

"...make mistakes."
