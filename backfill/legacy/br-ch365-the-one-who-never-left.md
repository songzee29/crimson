The night stretched on.

Aidan continued walking toward Black Hollow.

The Crimson Assassin remained three silent steps behind him.

Nothing had changed.

Or so it seemed.

---

Inside the Crimson Legacy...

No quests appeared.

No rewards.

No warnings.

Only silence.

A silence so complete...

That even Aidan noticed it.

"...You're quiet."

The Legacy didn't answer.

Not because it couldn't.

Because...

Something else was happening.

---

Far beyond the battlefield of kings...

Far beyond House Darkheart...

Far beyond the waking world...

A prison floated within endless darkness.

Ancient crimson chains stretched in every direction.

Each chain carried thousands of runes.

Each rune represented a promise.

A sacrifice.

A piece of a soul that had refused to disappear.

At the center...

A man slept.

His hands bound.

His eyes closed.

His breathing steady.

Rez.

---

For years...

Nothing had changed.

No dreams.

No thoughts.

No time.

Only sleep.

---

Then...

**CLINK.**

One chain broke.

Rez didn't move.

---

**CLINK.**

A second chain shattered.

His fingers twitched.

---

**CLINK.**

A third.

A fourth.

A fifth.

The darkness itself began trembling.

Not because someone attacked the prison.

Because something outside...

Was calling him home.
