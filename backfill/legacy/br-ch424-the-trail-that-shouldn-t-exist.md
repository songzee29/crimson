The crimson ash refused to disappear.

Rain fell.

The wind howled.

Animals crossed the clearing.

Yet the ash remained exactly where it had fallen.

Cassian knelt once more.

His fingers hovered over it.

He didn't touch it.

Something deep inside him...

Warned him not to.

His partner stepped beside him.

"Have you ever seen anything like this?"

Cassian answered honestly.

"...No."

Not in all his years.

Not even during the wars.

---

One of the scouts suddenly shouted.

"My Lord!"

Cassian turned.

The scout pointed toward the trees.

"There are footprints."

Everyone rushed forward.

The ground held a single trail.

One set of footprints.

No struggle.

No signs of the missing families.

Just...

One person walking calmly through the forest.

As though they wanted to be followed.
