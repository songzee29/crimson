The ruined battlefield remained frozen in time.

Cassian's sword stood planted between them.

Neither king reached for it.

Minutes passed.

Finally...

Cassian spoke.

"So..."

"...what now?"

Aidan looked across the broken earth.

"When I was human..."

"I thought justice meant making someone hurt exactly as much as I did."

He looked back at Cassian.

"I was wrong."

Cassian's eyes narrowed.

"You burned my home."

"I did."

"You fulfilled your decree."

"I did."

Silence.

"But I won't chase you forever."

The wind carried those words across the battlefield.

Cassian searched Aidan's face.

"You mean that."

"I do."

"The Second Judgment is fulfilled."

Aidan took one step backward.

"What comes next..."

"...isn't my choice."

The words struck Cassian harder than any weapon.

Because he knew exactly what Aidan meant.

The next Judgment...

Would only begin if **he** chose it.
