## Chapter Ninety-Nine — The First Crimson Law

The warmth inside Aidan's chest continued spreading.

It wasn't overwhelming.

It wasn't violent.

It felt...

Alive.

Every heartbeat sent gentle waves of crimson energy through his body before fading back into silence.

He looked down at his hands.

They were exactly the same.

No glowing symbols.

No crimson flames.

No impossible display of power.

Only his own hands.

The same hands that had helped a frightened freshman gather his lunch.

The same hands that had carried groceries for an elderly woman.

The same hands that had reached toward the Crimson Book.

The Guardian noticed the uncertainty in his eyes.

Disappointed?

Aidan smiled awkwardly.

Maybe a little.

I thought...

I don't know.

I expected something bigger.

The Guardian slowly walked toward the center of the Chamber of Threads.

Most people do.

He rested his repaired sword against one shoulder.

That is why most people never become worthy of Authority.

They mistake spectacle for significance.

He turned toward Aidan.

Tell me.

When you planted a seed...

Did the tree appear the next morning?

Of course not.

Exactly.

The Guardian pointed toward Aidan's chest.

The Authority isn't your power.

It is your root.

Everything you become will grow from what was planted today.

He began walking once more.

Come.

There is one final lesson before you return home.

---

The Guardian led Aidan beyond the Chamber of Threads.

Massive crimson doors silently opened as they approached.

Beyond them lay an enormous circular chamber unlike any Aidan had seen before.

Thousands of glowing words floated through the air.

Some burned brightly.

Others flickered as though waiting to awaken.

None were connected to walls.

They simply existed.

As if language itself had taken physical form.

Aidan slowly stepped inside.

What is this place?

The Hall of Laws.

His voice echoed throughout the chamber.

Every Crimson Witch...

Every Founder...

Every Guardian...

Has stood where you stand now.

Aidan looked around in amazement.

Each floating word carried an indescribable weight.

Protect.

Heal.

Silence.

Barrier.

Truth.

Memory.

Hope.

Mercy.

Justice.

Balance.

Hundreds more drifted across the endless chamber.

Some words were so ancient they no longer resembled any language spoken in the modern world.

The Guardian spoke quietly.

A Witch does not command magic.

A Witch commands Law.

Magic obeys.

The words settled heavily within Aidan.

Law...

Not spells.

Not incantations.

Law.

The Guardian nodded toward the floating words.

Every Law carries a cost.

Every cost teaches restraint.

Without cost...

Authority becomes tyranny.

Without restraint...

Power becomes corruption.

He stepped aside.

Today...

The Hall will decide which Law recognizes you first.

Not the strongest.

The first.

Every floating word suddenly stopped moving.

Silence consumed the chamber.

One by one...

The ancient Laws dimmed.

Thousands became hundreds.

Hundreds became dozens.

Dozens became ten.

Then...

Only three remained.

Protect.

Truth.

Mercy.

The three words circled Aidan slowly.

The Guardian watched without speaking.

Even he did not know which would answer.

Protect shone with steady crimson light.

Truth burned like polished gold.

Mercy glowed softly with silver warmth.

Aidan closed his eyes.

He remembered every lesson.

The Guardian standing before the monster.

The old bookseller refusing to reveal knowledge before its proper time.

Malik offering friendship without expecting anything in return.

Lyra asking him not to disappear.

Kael smiling because someone had finally noticed him.

He understood.

Power had never been about proving himself.

It had always been about protecting others.

His eyes opened.

Protect.

The moment he whispered the word...

The Hall erupted.

The crimson word shot toward him like a falling star.

It entered his chest.

A pulse of ancient Authority spread through the Sanctuary.

Every floating Law bowed.

Not to Aidan.

To the choice he had made.

The Guardian slowly lowered his head.

The Hall has spoken.

A crimson notification appeared before Aidan.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FIRST CRIMSON LAW ACQUIRED

LAW OF PROTECTION

Classification:

Foundational Law

Current Rank:

Dormant

Activation Requirements:

Understanding before Action

Selflessness before Power

Cost:

The greater the protection...

The greater the burden carried by its bearer.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification slowly faded.

Aidan remained silent.

The Guardian smiled proudly.

Congratulations...

You did not choose the Law.

The Law...

Chose you.

Far beyond the Living Legacy...

Deep beneath the earth...

One of the ancient chains trembled.

A massive crimson eye slowly opened within the darkness.

Its owner smiled.

Not because the Law had awakened.

Because it recognized which Law had awakened.

The Protector...

Has returned.

And for the first time in countless ages...

Something imprisoned in the abyss whispered a forgotten name that the world itself had erased from history.
