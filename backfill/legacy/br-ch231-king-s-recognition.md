The Blood Sovereign bowed his head.

Not deeply.

Just enough.

A gesture reserved only for another king.

"...Very well."

"You have earned..."

"...my name."

The earth shook beneath his feet.

"I..."

"...am..."

"...First of the Blood Sovereigns."

As though history remembered it.
