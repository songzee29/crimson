The endless guardians adapted.

Some fought with blades.

Others with claws.

Others cast ancient blood techniques that turned the chamber itself into a weapon.

Crimson spikes erupted from the floor.

Walls shifted.

Ceilings collapsed.

Nothing remained still.

Aidan no longer reacted.

He anticipated.

Predator’s Reflexes guided every movement.

Crimson Dash became impossible for ordinary eyes to follow.

Each successful exchange carried him another few feet closer to the throne.

Stone shattered beneath his feet.

Blood Armor fractured.

Reformed.

Fractured again.

His blood reserves steadily declined despite the ancient Offering.

The warning vanished.

Aidan never slowed.

One guardian seized his arm.

Another drove a spear toward his heart.

His body twisted instinctively.

The spear pierced the first guardian instead.

Before the second could recover—

His claw tore through both constructs at once.

They dissolved into crimson mist.

The throne stood only twenty steps away.

The remaining guardians stopped.

Not defeated.

Waiting.

The chamber itself was changing.

Every crimson statue slowly bowed once more.

A path opened directly to the throne.

For the first time…

The Ancient Gates offered no attack.

Only a choice.
