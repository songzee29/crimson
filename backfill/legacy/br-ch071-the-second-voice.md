The city slept peacefully.

Rain tapped softly against cathedral windows.

Inside his apartment...

Cassian sat alone.

The crimson vial rested on the table before him.

He hadn't touched it.

"...Why me..."

Azrath's voice.

Not from outside.

Not inside his head.

Everywhere.

Cassian closed his eyes.

"...Then what should I ask?"
