Their first class passed uneventfully.

History.

Ironically.

Professor Helena stood before the classroom with an unusually serious expression.

Normally, she filled her lessons with stories and debates.

Today...

She closed every curtain.

History is not merely the study of the past.

It is the study of decisions.

One decision...

Can shape generations.

She slowly placed an ancient wooden chest on her desk.

Dust covered its iron bands.

The academy crest had faded with time.

This chest has remained sealed beneath Blackwood Academy for over two hundred years.

No student has ever seen its contents.

A murmur spread through the room.

Professor Helena continued.

Yesterday morning...

The seal broke on its own.

Silence.

Every student leaned forward.

She carefully lifted the lid.

Inside rested dozens of weathered journals.

Letters.

Maps.

And one folded piece of parchment.

She unfolded it with extraordinary care.

The handwriting had faded but remained readable.

She read aloud.

If this archive is ever opened...

Then history has begun repeating itself.

Do not seek the strongest student.

Seek the one others willingly follow.

He immediately looked away.

Professor Helena noticed.

She said nothing.

Instead...

Your first assignment is simple.

Study these records.

Find patterns everyone else overlooks.

History rarely repeats itself exactly.

It whispers first.

Only later...

Does it scream.
