The wave of crimson-black magic swept across Blackwood Academy.

Golden barriers shattered.

Windows exploded.

Ancient runes carved into the stone walls fractured under the weight of the Curse’s awakening.

Students cried out.

Teachers stumbled.

The old oak continued splitting apart.

Its roots burst through the courtyard like black veins spreading beneath living flesh.

The Witch Curse had stopped whispering.

Now…

It acted.

Professor Helena slammed her staff into the earth.

“Every barrier!”

Golden circles erupted around the remaining students.

Faculty witches poured everything they had into holding the academy together.

One by one…

Several collapsed from exhaustion.

The barriers remained.

Barely.

⸻

Aidan walked forward.

Not running.

Not hesitating.

The Curse no longer hid itself.

Every step toward the old oak made the pressure heavier.

The voices returned.

Not pleading.

Condemning.

You cannot save them.

You were never enough.

You will watch them fall.

He kept walking.

“Stand.”

The single Law echoed through the academy.

Not loudly.

Certainly.

Golden-crimson light spread beneath his feet.

The broken courtyard stopped collapsing around him.

Behind him…

Dozens of exhausted witches found their footing again.

Not because their strength returned.

Because their resolve did.

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LAW

Stand

Immediate Application Confirmed.

The strength of one unwavering heart…

May steady many.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message disappeared.

Its purpose fulfilled.

⸻

The old oak split completely open.

There was no monster waiting inside.

No hidden body.

Only an immense sphere of crimson-black magic suspended between the broken halves of the tree.

It pulsed like a wounded heart.

Every pulse sent another wave of despair across the academy.

Lyra staggered.

For a moment…

She saw herself standing alone.

Aidan gone.

Everyone gone.

She squeezed her eyes shut.

Then remembered his hand in Grey Hollow.

She smiled through tears.

“No.”

The vision shattered.

⸻

Malik dropped to one knee.

He saw every person he had ever failed.

Every regret.

Every mistake.

A heavy voice whispered,

You always arrive too late.

His fists clenched.

Then another memory answered.

Aidan running beneath the falling branch.

Cassian reaching for him.

Elias refusing to let him walk away.

He stood.

“I’m here now.”

The illusion broke.

⸻

Elias watched his brother disappear into darkness again.

This time…

He didn’t chase him.

“I’ll carry you…”

“…by living.”

The shadow dissolved.

⸻

Cassian trembled as every life he had taken surrounded him.

Every face.

Every accusation.

He closed his eyes.

Then remembered one simple sentence.

I chose.

Aidan hadn’t judged him.

He had chosen him.

Cassian lifted his head.

“I choose too.”

The accusations became light.

⸻

The crimson sphere violently contracted.

For the first time…

It weakened.

Not from magic.

From people refusing it.

Master Rowan quietly laughed.

“The Founder…”

“…you impossible old fool.”

Professor Helena looked toward him.

“What?”

“He understood.”

“What?”

“The Curse was never defeated by stronger magic.”

He watched the five young witches standing together beneath the broken oak.

“It was always defeated…”

“…by stronger hearts.”
