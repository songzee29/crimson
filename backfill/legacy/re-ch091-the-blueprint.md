The morning after the Queen's return...

House Darkheart slowly began healing.

The broken walls were repaired.

The blood was washed from the throne room.

The kingdom celebrated.

But Aidan...

Could not celebrate yet.

He stood alone on the highest balcony, looking over the kingdom he had sworn to protect.

Lyra quietly stepped beside him.

"You've been thinking all morning."

Aidan smiled.

"I always am."

She leaned against him.

"About Cassian?"

He shook his head.

"...About what comes next."

Before Lyra could answer—

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

### **Living Blueprint Updated**

**New Crimson Blood Art Available**

**Requirements Met**

Aidan smiled slightly.

"So..."

"It's starting."

Lyra noticed the change in his expression.

"The Legacy?"

Aidan nodded.

"It finally has something new to teach me."

---

The Crimson Legacy continued updating.

---

## **Blood Art Blueprint**

### **Crimson Pulse**

Classification: Blood Art

Difficulty: Beginner

Status: Available

Description:

Release a wave of Crimson power through the bloodstream to strengthen allies connected to the Crimson Bloodline.

Blueprint Status:

Ready for Practice.

---

Aidan quietly read every detail.

The Legacy never told him to use it.

It simply explained the principle.

The flow of Crimson Blood.

The rhythm.

The balance.

The cost.

Everything he needed to understand.

Nothing more.

---

Rez walked onto the balcony.

"So..."

"What did you get?"

Aidan grinned.

"A blueprint."

Rez laughed.

"There it is."

"The Legacy is finally treating you like a king."

Aidan looked over.

"What do you mean?"

Rez folded his arms.

"When you first awakened..."

"It held your hand."

"It taught you how to survive."

He looked toward the horizon.

"Now..."

"It assumes you're capable of learning."

"It doesn't hand you abilities anymore."

"It shows you the path."

"You decide whether to walk it."

---

Aidan looked back at the notification.

He slowly raised one hand.

No System command.

No activation.

Only concentration.

He remembered every principle shown in the blueprint.

His Crimson Blood answered.

A faint crimson wave spread across his arm.

Then outward.

The flowers growing beneath the balcony straightened.

The nearby Crimson Guards suddenly felt lighter.

Their fatigue disappeared.

No one understood why.

Aidan lowered his hand.

The technique vanished.

He smiled.

"It worked."

**Ding.**

**Crimson Pulse**

**Learned**

**Recorded**

Rez smiled proudly.

"See?"

"It never gave you the Blood Art."

"You learned it."

Aidan nodded.

"And now..."

"...it's mine."

The breeze carried the Crimson King's words across House Darkheart.

This was only the beginning.

Every battle...

Every discovery...

Every evolution...

Would reveal another blueprint.

Not to make him stronger.

But to show him what the Crimson Bloodline was capable of becoming.

And Aidan Darkheart...

Would decide which of those possibilities became reality.
