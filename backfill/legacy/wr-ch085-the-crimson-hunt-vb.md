The road south disappeared beneath endless darkness.

Aidan did not feel the cold.

He did not feel fatigue.

He no longer counted breaths.

Only heartbeats.

Thousands of them.

Every living creature surrounding the road announced itself through rhythm alone.

A frightened rabbit beneath tangled roots.

A sleeping horse inside a nearby stable.

A family resting in a farmhouse beyond the fields.

Each pulse struck his senses with impossible clarity.

His throat burned.

The pain no longer resembled thirst.

It felt like molten iron being forced through his veins.

Every step became harder than the last.

Not because his body weakened.

Because every heartbeat demanded he stop walking.

And feed.

The notifications vanished.

The hunger did not.

…

Movement caught his eye.

A young stag stepped cautiously from the trees.

Its heartbeat pounded against the silence.

His body reacted before thought.

The distance disappeared beneath a blur of impossible speed.

His hand struck the animal’s shoulder.

The stag flew several yards before crashing through a fallen log.

Aidan stared.

He had not intended to use that much force.

The animal staggered upright, frightened but alive, before vanishing into the forest.

His eyes remained fixed on the shattered log.

His own strength exceeded his expectations.

The realization barely mattered.

The scent of living blood still consumed every instinct.

The new instincts settled naturally into his body.

Not learned.

Remembered.

Immediately…

The world became louder.

Hundreds of overlapping heartbeats separated themselves into distinct rhythms.

The smallest wound carried across impossible distances.

Every scent became identifiable.

Human.

Animal.

Old blood.

Fresh blood.

Fear.

Pain.

Life.

His throat tightened again.

Animal blood called to him.

Yet something inside rejected it before he consciously understood why.

It smelled alive.

It did not smell…

Enough.

The Crimson Vampire kept walking.

South.

The burning followed.
