Valerius disappeared.

Aidan reacted instantly.

Too late.

A fist struck his chest.

CRAAAAACK!!

His sternum shattered.

A second strike crushed his ribs.

A third sent him crashing through the crimson arena.

Stone exploded beneath him.

The entire valley shook.

Ancient vampires leaned forward.

They had seen this before.

Kings rose.

Kings fell.

Blood decided everything.

---

Aidan slowly stood.

Blood streamed from his mouth.

His regeneration struggled.

Royal Regeneration repaired one wound...

As another opened.

His breathing became heavier.

His body...

Was reaching its limit.

---

Inside his mind...

One quiet notification appeared.

---

> **Royal Blood Reservoir**

---

> **21% Remaining**

---

No warning.

No panic.

Only information.

---

Aidan looked up.

His crimson eyes met Valerius'.

Then...

He smiled.

Not because he was winning.

Because...

He finally understood.

---

"...This..."

He wiped the blood from his mouth.

"...is what being king feels like."

---

Valerius laughed.

Not loudly.

Proudly.

"...Exactly."
