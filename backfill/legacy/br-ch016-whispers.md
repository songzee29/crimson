Something changed.

An ancient vampire sitting beneath Rome suddenly opened his eyes.

"...Impossible."

"...A Darkheart?"

"So..."

"...the blood still lives."

Far beneath the Veil...

001 slowly opened his eyes.

For the first time...

He smiled.

Not with joy.

Recognition.

"...At last."

The chains around his wrists groaned.

One tiny crack spread across black steel.

He whispered into the darkness,

"I wondered..."

"...which Darkheart would come for me."

His smile widened.

"I never imagined..."

"...it would be the son."

The crack grew.

Almost too small to notice.

But it was there.

The first crack...

In a prison that had held for centuries.
