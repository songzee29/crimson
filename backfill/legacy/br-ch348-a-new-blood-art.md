The first Dusk Eater attacked.

Its claws carved through stone.

Aidan stepped aside.

Too easy.

The second came from behind.

The third from above.

They hunted as one.

Good.

Aidan wanted pressure.

He wanted something that forced him to evolve.

He stretched out one hand.

Blood from the fallen creature floated into the air.

Not enough.

He compressed it.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Until the blood became...

A tiny crimson sphere.

Smaller than a marble.

Denser than steel.

Aidan looked at it thoughtfully.

"...Interesting."

He flicked his finger.

The sphere vanished.

Half a second later—

BOOOOOOM!!

A mountain-sized boulder behind the Dusk Eaters exploded into crimson dust.

Even the Crimson Assassin paused.

The Legacy immediately recorded the technique.
