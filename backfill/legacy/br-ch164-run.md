The hunt began before sunrise.

Mist rolled through the ancient forest west of House Darkheart.

Not a single bird sang.

The woods themselves seemed to know...

Something greater than any predator had entered.

Aidan walked at the front of the group.

Rez moved silently through the trees beside him.

Kael and Darius guarded the flanks.

Malik was already scanning for tracks.

Mara observed every strange rune carved into the old trees.

Lyra walked beside Aidan without saying a word.

She knew something was different.

---

Hours passed.

They tracked claw marks across shattered stone.

A massive beast had torn through the forest days earlier.

Trees had been uprooted.

Bones littered the ground.

Whatever they were following...

Was enormous.

Malik crouched beside one of the tracks.

"...That's no bear."

Kael measured it with his forearm.

"It would crush a horse."

Darius smiled.

"Good."

"I was hoping this trip wouldn't be boring."

Rez looked at the torn earth.

"It isn't hunting for food."

Everyone looked at him.

"It's hunting for territory."

Aidan quietly nodded.

*Just like the wolf said.*

---

They continued deeper.

The air became colder.

The trees thicker.

Sunlight barely reached the forest floor.

Then...

Aidan stopped.

Everyone behind him stopped as well.

Lyra looked at him.

"What is it?"

Aidan slowly closed his eyes.

The wolf stirred.

Not with hunger.

With excitement.

Inside his mind...

The great crimson wolf stood at the edge of the endless forest.

Its tail swayed once.

Its ears lifted.

Then it looked directly at Aidan.

**Enough walking.**

**Let me run.**

Aidan smiled faintly.

*You want to stretch your legs?*

The wolf let out a deep rumbling sound that almost sounded amused.

**You've carried my instincts...**

**Now carry my form.**

The vampire leaned against a crimson tree, watching with a faint smile.

**You've accepted us...**

**Now trust us.**

Aidan opened his eyes.

He looked toward Rez.

"I think..."

"...it's time."

Rez studied his face.

"You've decided."

Aidan nodded once.

"I don't want to keep the wolf locked inside me."

"I want him to breathe."

Silence settled over the group.

Malik frowned.

"...What are you talking about?"

Lyra's eyes widened.

She understood before anyone else.

She stepped closer.

"You're going to shift."

Aidan met her gaze.

"For the first time..."

"...by choice."

The forest became completely still.

Rez smiled.

"I've waited a long time to see this."

Kael folded his arms.

"I thought your wolf was just another form."

Rez shook his head.

"No."

"This isn't about power."

"This is about acceptance."

Aidan stepped into a clearing.

Morning light broke through the trees above him.

He took a slow breath.

He didn't force the change.

He didn't command it.

He simply...

Let go.

The Crimson Blood flowed naturally.

His bones shifted.

Muscles expanded.

His senses exploded outward.

The sound of leaves.

The scent of rain miles away.

The heartbeat of every member of his family.

None of it hurt.

None of it frightened him.

For the first time...

The transformation felt...

Right.

A crimson aura spiraled gently around him.

The air pulsed.

Then...

A massive crimson wolf stepped forward where Aidan had been standing.

Its coat was black as midnight, but every strand shimmered with deep crimson light beneath the surface, as if liquid blood flowed through its fur.

Its eyes burned with the same crimson gaze Aidan carried as a man.

Ancient.

Calm.

Royal.

The wolf stood taller than any ordinary beast.

Power radiated from it without a single growl.

It wasn't merely a predator.

It looked like a king wearing the form of a wolf.

No one spoke.

Malik's jaw dropped.

"...He's beautiful."

Kael slowly lowered his sword.

"I've never..."

"...seen anything like that."

Mara whispered almost to herself.

"The Crimson Wolf..."

Lyra smiled.

Not because of the power.

Because she could still recognize Aidan.

The wolf walked toward her.

He lowered his massive head.

Lyra gently rested her hand against his forehead.

"You finally got your wish..."

The wolf closed his eyes for a brief moment.

His tail gave one slow sweep behind him.

Inside the Soul Link, Rez laughed.

**He's happy.**

Even in wolf form...

Aidan's amused reply reached him.

*I know.*

Rez folded his arms with a grin.

**Go on then...**

**Run.**

The crimson wolf looked toward the endless forest.

His ears lifted.

The wind carried thousands of scents.

Freedom.

The hunt.

The wild.

Without another sound...

He exploded forward.

The earth cracked beneath his paws.

Leaves swirled into the air as the Crimson Wolf disappeared between the ancient trees.

Not fleeing.

Not chasing blood.

Simply running.

For the first time in his life...

The wolf wasn't imprisoned inside Aidan.

The wolf was free.

And somewhere deep in the forest...

A monstrous roar answered.

The hunt...

Had finally begun.
