Deep beneath the ancient cathedral...

Caelum stood before a wall covered with crimson strings connecting names, kingdoms, and bloodlines.

One hunter entered hurriedly.

"Master."

"We've confirmed movement."

"Who?"

"The Crimson King."

Caelum nodded.

"And?"

"He hasn't marched."

"He hasn't attacked."

"He hasn't responded to Cassian."

Caelum smiled.

"Good."

The hunter frowned.

"You expected this?"

"I expected..."

Caelum looked toward the House Darkheart crest pinned to the wall.

"...a true king."

"He understands that rushing toward every battle is how kingdoms die."

He quietly cut one crimson string from the map.

"Continue observing."

"The storm isn't here yet."
