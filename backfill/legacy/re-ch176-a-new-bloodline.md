The Valley of Ash was quiet.

The crimson pillar faded.

The sky slowly returned to normal.

Only one thing had changed.

Everything.

---

Cassian stood motionless.

His breathing had steadied.

His heartbeat...

No longer resembled that of the vampire he had been for centuries.

It carried the rhythm of the Crimson Bloodline.

He could feel it.

Every heartbeat echoed with impossible clarity.

Every breath carried the scent of the world around him.

The wind.

The ash.

The earth.

Even Aidan.

---

He slowly looked down at his own hands.

Crimson veins pulsed beneath his skin before disappearing.

"...I'm..."

He couldn't finish the sentence.

He didn't know what he had become.

---

Aidan stepped closer.

"You aren't the man you were."

Cassian nodded slowly.

"I can feel that."

"You aren't my enemy anymore."

Cassian closed his eyes.

"My mind remembers every reason I should hate you."

He placed a hand over his chest.

"...but my blood..."

"It won't let me."

---

Rez approached them.

The Crimson Assassin studied Cassian carefully.

Not as an enemy.

As family.

After a long silence...

Rez smiled.

"Welcome to House Darkheart."

Cassian looked at him in disbelief.

"After everything I've done..."

"...you're welcoming me?"

Rez answered without hesitation.

"I didn't say I'd let you forget."

A faint grin crossed his face.

"But I also won't deny what the Founder has chosen."

---

Cassian lowered his head.

For the first time in centuries...

The weight on his shoulders felt lighter.

Not gone.

Just...

Different.

Cassian frowned.

"Loyal..."

He laughed softly.

"I never thought that word would describe me."

---

Aidan folded his arms.

"It doesn't erase your past."

"It gives you the chance to build a different future."

---

Silence settled over the valley.

Then...

Cassian did something neither Aidan nor Rez expected.

He dropped to one knee.

Not because his blood compelled him.

Because he chose to.

He bowed his head.

"My King..."

His voice was steady.

"I cannot undo what I took from you."

"I cannot return the years."

"I cannot erase Lyra's death."

He looked up.

"But if there is even the smallest chance..."

"...to spend the rest of my immortal life protecting what I once tried to destroy..."

"...then let that be my judgment."

The Valley of Ash fell silent.

Aidan looked at Cassian for a long moment.

Then...

He extended his hand.

Cassian looked at it.

Confused.

Aidan smiled.

"No more kneeling."

"We build from here."

Cassian slowly accepted the hand.

Aidan pulled him to his feet.

Not as a defeated enemy.

As the newest member of the Crimson Bloodline.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf watched quietly.

**The pack grows.**

The vampire nodded.

**Not by conquest...**

**By choice.**

Aidan smiled to himself.

*Exactly.*

---

Far beyond the Valley of Ash...

The change rippled through the world.

Ancient covens stopped their rituals.

Old vampire lords suddenly felt an unfamiliar pressure in their blood.

Werewolf elders lifted their heads toward the northern sky.

Something impossible had happened.

A Founder had not merely created another descendant.

He had transformed an ancient enemy into part of his own Bloodline.

The old rules no longer applied.

---

Back at House Darkheart...

Lyra stood on the highest balcony.

A warm breeze crossed her face.

Her wolf stirred peacefully.

She smiled without knowing why.

Then she whispered into the evening wind.

"Come home."

Miles away...

Aidan looked toward the horizon.

He smiled.

"I think it's time."

Together...

Aidan.

Rez.

And Cassian...

Turned toward House Darkheart.

The Fourth Judgment had not ended with another grave.

It had ended with a new beginning.

And somewhere deep within the Crimson Legacy...

A final notification quietly appeared.
