## Chapter Two Hundred Four — Fractures

By the next morning, the cracked bell tower had become the only topic anyone discussed.

Students gathered behind safety barriers while academy workers inspected the damage.

No one understood how a structure that had survived centuries could suddenly fracture overnight.

Headmaster Aldren stood silently with Professor Helena.

Neither spoke until the workers stepped away.

It's spreading.

Helena whispered.

Aldren nodded.

Stone doesn't fail without reason.

His eyes drifted toward Aidan.

Neither do people.

---

Aidan and his friends watched from across the courtyard.

Malik folded his arms.

Looks like they're closing half the academy.

Darius studied the tower.

No...

They're hiding something.

Lyra quietly sketched the fracture.

The crack isn't random.

Everyone looked at her.

She held up her drawing.

See?

It follows the old foundation stones.

Almost...

Like it already knew where to break.

Cassian stared at the sketch longer than anyone else.

His expression grew distant.

Aidan noticed.

You alright?

Cassian forced a smile.

Just thinking.

But inside...

Something about the fracture felt painfully familiar.

Your research groups have been selected.

Each group will investigate one historical site surrounding Blackwood.

This is your first official assignment for the Historical Field Program.

She began handing out envelopes.

When Aidan's group received theirs, Elias immediately broke the seal.

His smile slowly disappeared.

That's...

Impossible.

Aidan looked over his shoulder.

The destination read:

**Ashfall Cemetery**

A place abandoned for more than a century.

According to academy records...

No one had been permitted to enter since the Great Burning.

Professor Helena looked directly at Aidan's group.

You leave tomorrow morning.

Travel together.

Return together.

Ask questions.

Observe everything.

Touch nothing...

Unless history invites you to.

Those final words caught Aidan's attention.

They sounded less like an academy rule...

And more like something a Keeper would say.

No.

You're terrified.

Is it really that obvious?

To everyone?

No.

Just to me.

Silence settled between them.

Then Elias leaned forward.

Promise me something.

Depends.

If something happens...

Don't shut everyone out.

Aidan frowned.

Nothing's going to happen.

Elias looked toward the darkening sky.

I know.

But promise me anyway.

I promise.

## Chapter Two Hundred Seven — A Name in Stone

The following morning, the group arrived at Ashfall Cemetery just after sunrise.

The iron gates stood open.

Not broken.

Waiting.

Rows of ancient headstones stretched across a mist-covered hillside.

Many names had been worn away by time.

Others had been deliberately chipped from the stone.

Lyra knelt beside one weathered marker.

Someone erased these.

Not nature.

Aidan slowly walked farther into the cemetery.

Near the oldest section...

He stopped.

One grave remained perfectly preserved.

Its inscription read:

**Here rests a Keeper of History.**

No name.

No dates.

Only purpose.

Elias stepped beside him.

Looks familiar.

Aidan nodded.

Just like the Founder.

Purpose before identity.

As he reached toward the stone...

A soft breeze swept across the cemetery.

The old oak standing at its center released a single crimson leaf.

It drifted gently onto the grave.

Nothing else happened.

Yet every member of the group instinctively fell silent.

Some places...

Remembered respect.

Hidden beyond the cemetery wall...

A lone figure observed the students through the morning mist.

Not the Hunter.

Not the man dressed in white.

Someone new.

A woman wrapped in a dark traveling cloak.

A silver raven rested calmly upon her shoulder.

She watched Aidan's group carefully.

Her gaze lingered on Elias.

Then on Cassian.

Finally...

On Aidan.

She reached into her coat and unfolded a weathered sketch.

The drawing depicted a much younger Aidan standing beside another child.

Elias.

She compared the sketch to the young man standing in the cemetery.

Then quietly smiled.

So...

History has reunited the brothers.

Good.

They'll need each other.

Because before the leaves change color...

One friendship will shape the future.

And another...

Will begin its long road toward tragedy.

Without another sound, she disappeared into the forest.

Unseen.

Unheard.

Waiting for history to make its next move.
