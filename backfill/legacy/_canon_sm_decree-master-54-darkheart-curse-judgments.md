# 📜 THE CRIMSON MASTER DECREE

## 🔒 Decree 54 — The Darkheart Curse: The Five Judgments

### Purpose

To establish the Final Judgment of House Darkheart, the laws governing the Darkheart Curse, and the five immutable phases through which its sentence is fulfilled.

---

## The Final Judgment of House Darkheart

The final decree spoken by **Aidan Darkheart** before the end of his human life became an **Authority-Born Curse** known throughout history as the **Darkheart Curse**.

The decree states:

> **"By the Final Decree... of House Darkheart...**
>
> **You shall never know peace...**
>
> **For your soul shall never outrun my judgment."**

From that moment forward, Cassian's soul became eternally bound to the judgment of House Darkheart.

---

## Law I — The Eternal Link

The Darkheart Curse is not a tracking ability.

It is not a locator.

It is not a command over Cassian's will.

Instead, it creates an eternal bond between Aidan Darkheart and Cassian.

Their destinies remain forever connected until the judgment has run its full course.

---

## Law II — Peace Shall Never Last

Cassian may build kingdoms.

He may build covens.

He may build families.

He may find love.

He may discover happiness.

But he shall never keep it.

Whenever Cassian truly finds peace, the judgment of House Darkheart will inevitably return and take that peace away.

---

## Law III — The Five Judgment Phases

The Darkheart Curse unfolds through five predetermined phases.

### Phase 1 — Fear

Cassian realizes he never escaped Aidan's judgment.

The curse begins by destroying certainty and replacing it with fear.

---

### Phase 2 — Loss

After Cassian rebuilds his life and finds peace again, Aidan destroys that peace by taking from him what he loves most—whether by destroying his coven or taking from him someone he holds dear—forcing Cassian to begin again.

---

### Phase 3 — Revenge

Unable to endure the judgment any longer, Cassian chooses revenge.

He launches his attack against Aidan.

During this phase, Cassian kills Lyra, forever changing Aidan.

---

### Phase 4 — Reflection

Broken by Lyra's death, Aidan crosses a line of his own.

He hurts Cassian's child.

For the first time, Cassian sees Aidan becoming someone shaped by unbearable loss.

---

### Phase 5 — Acceptance

Years pass.

Cassian rebuilds.

Aidan tears his peace away.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Until Cassian finally reaches the end of the sentence and begs Aidan for death.

Only then does he fully understand the weight of the judgment placed upon him.

---

## Law IV — The Philosophy of Judgment

The Five Judgment Phases are intentional.

They are not the philosophy of Zee.

They are the philosophy of Aidan Darkheart.

The Crimson Legacy never sought another Zee.

It sought the first Aidan.

Therefore, Aidan's judgment shall remain his own, and it shall never be rewritten to follow the path the Founder would have taken.

---

## Foundational Principle

A Darkheart's word does not fade with time.

A Darkheart's judgment does not require pursuit.

Once decreed, history itself bends until the sentence is fulfilled.
