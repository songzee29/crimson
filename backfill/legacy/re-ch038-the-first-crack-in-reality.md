That night, they rested inside an abandoned subway room.

Lyra couldn’t sleep.

Aidan sat across from her, staring at the wall.

Not blankly.

Observantly.

Like he was reading something no one else could see.

“Aidan,” she whispered.

He turned slightly.

“Yes.”

“I need you to try something.”

“What?”

“Tell me what you feel right now.”

A long pause.

Then:

“I feel like I’m here.”

Lyra shook her head.

“That’s not enough.”

Aidan tilted his head slightly.

“I don’t know what else there is.”

And that was when it happened.

A flicker in the air.

Reality briefly warped.

A crack of Witch perception opened involuntarily across Aidan’s vision.

The wall in front of him briefly split into layers—

And behind it—

he saw something impossible.

A silhouette.

A woman’s shape.

Holding a child.

Then gone.

Aidan stood up suddenly.

“What was that?”

Lyra immediately stood too.

“What did you see?”

“I don’t know,” he said quickly. “It felt… important.”

Lyra stepped closer.

“Describe it.”

Aidan tried.

But stopped.

Because for the first time since the extraction—

he felt something he couldn’t name.

A pressure.

Not emotion.

Not memory.

A direction.

Like something inside him was pointing at a missing piece.

Lyra noticed the shift immediately.

“Aidan…?”

He looked at her.

And for the first time since losing the core memory—

something behind his eyes changed slightly.

Not returning.

Not healing.

Just **reactivating.**

And far below reality, deep inside the System layer—

a dormant fragment responded.

Not fully awake.

But no longer silent.
