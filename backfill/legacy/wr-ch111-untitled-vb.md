The eyes did not move.

They simply watched.

The chamber fell completely silent.

Even the grinding stone door stopped.

Cassian raised both swords.

Lyra shifted into a lower stance.

Malik’s runes flared to life.

Only Aidan continued walking.

Step.

Step.

Step.

The crimson eyes never blinked.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The darkness slowly withdrew.

Not disappearing.

Receding.

It revealed a solitary figure standing beyond the threshold.

Ancient black armor covered its body from head to toe.

A long crimson cloak hung unmoving despite the cold wind flowing through the chamber.

Its face remained hidden behind a featureless helm.

No weapon rested in its hands.

Yet its presence alone weighed upon the room.

Draco stopped breathing for a moment.

“…Impossible.”

Rowan looked toward him.

“You know it?”

“No.”

Draco answered without taking his eyes off the armored figure.

“But every record I ever found ended before this.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The armored guardian took one deliberate step forward.

The sound echoed like a hammer striking stone.

Its voice followed.

Deep.

Ancient.

Measured.

“Heir.”

Only that single word.

Nothing more.

Aidan stopped several paces away.

“I am here.”

The figure tilted its head almost imperceptibly.

“You survived.”

“I did.”

Another long silence followed.

Then—

“You are not complete.”

No one spoke.

Cassian’s grip tightened.

The statement lingered heavily in the chamber.

Not an accusation.

An observation.

Aidan’s expression remained unchanged.

“I know.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Behind them, the ancient stone door began sliding shut.

Malik spun toward it.

“It’s closing!”

Elias rushed forward.

His hands pressed against the moving stone.

It did not slow.

Lyra joined him.

Together they barely delayed it.

Rowan shouted, “Everyone inside!”

Without hesitation, the group crossed the threshold.

The massive door sealed behind them with a deafening boom.

The sound echoed into silence.

There was no handle on this side either.

Only smooth stone.

They were committed now.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The armored figure slowly raised one hand.

Not to attack.

To point.

Beyond it, a vast corridor stretched into darkness.

Ancient braziers burst into crimson flame one by one, revealing rows of towering statues disappearing into the distance.

The figure spoke again.

“The path remains.”

It lowered its hand.

“Walk.”

No threat.

No command shouted in anger.

Just a simple instruction.

Aidan walked first.

The others exchanged wary glances before following.

As they disappeared into the corridor, the armored guardian remained where it stood.

Watching.

Waiting.

As if countless others had once taken this same path…

And very few had ever returned.
