At the center of the city...

Aidan was waiting.

Alone.

No guards.

No weapons drawn.

No army.

Only the Crimson King.

Cassian stopped.

"So..."

"...you came alone."

Aidan nodded.

"I told you."

"I wouldn't let innocent people pay for my decree."

Silence stretched between them.

The marketplace slowly emptied.

Not because anyone ordered it.

Because everyone could feel...

Something important was happening.

"You fulfilled the Second Judgment."

"You took my peace."

"...Yes."

"You could have killed everyone I loved."

"I never would."

"...That's what makes this so difficult."
