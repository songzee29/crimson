The abandoned cathedral.

Azrath stood before dozens of cracked mirrors.

Each reflected a different version of Cassian.

One smiling.

One bleeding.

One wearing a crown.

One kneeling before Aidan.

Cassian frowned.

"What is this?"

Azrath answered calmly.

"You."

Cassian scoffed.

"I don't look like that."

Azrath smiled.

"No."

"...You could."

He waved his hand.

The reflections changed.

Now every mirror showed Aidan.

People cheering.

Children smiling.

The Crimson Crown.

The Darkheart Crest.

Cassian couldn't look away.

Azrath stepped beside him.

"They don't love him..."

"They love..."

"...what he represents."

Cassian clenched his fists.

"And?"

Azrath leaned closer.

"If you become greater than the symbol..."

"...they'll follow you instead."
