# Chapter Twenty-Six: The Ones Who Refused to Leave

Darkness.

Cold.

Stone.

Aidan floated somewhere between consciousness and oblivion.

Pain had become distant.

The crushing weight in his chest barely registered anymore.

His body felt impossibly heavy.

Then...

"Aidan!"

The voice reached him first.

Lyra.

Faint.

Desperate.

Stone cracked somewhere above.

Another voice followed.

"Again!"

Malik.

A heavy impact shook the tunnel.

Cassian.

"They're alive..."

Elias's strained voice echoed through fractured stone.

"I can hear him breathing."

Aidan tried to answer.

Nothing came.

Only blood filled his mouth.

Above him, Rowan rested one weathered hand against the collapsed passage.

His eyes closed.

"He isn't gone."

Professor Helena looked toward the exhausted group.

"We've been digging for six hours."

"Then we dig seven."

"Rowan..."

"He comes home."

No one argued.

Not one person suggested retreat.

Not one person questioned whether it was already too late.

They simply kept digging.

...

Far below...

Aidan slowly opened one eye.

Darkness.

Only darkness.

His hearing reached farther than his sight.

Stone shifting.

Water dripping.

A distant heartbeat.

No...

Several.

Not his friends.

The hunters hadn't truly left.

They were still there.

Watching.

Waiting to see if Blackwood would abandon him.

"They're testing us..."

His voice barely escaped.

He tried to move.

His ribs screamed.

Fresh blood soaked through torn bandages.

His left arm refused to answer.

Every breath felt borrowed.

Still...

He crawled.

One painful movement at a time.

Not toward escape.

Toward the faint sound of Rowan's voice echoing through solid rock.

...

The Observer watched from another tunnel.

It didn't attack.

Instead...

It quietly carved another symbol into the wall.

This one different from the others.

Two figures.

Separated.

Then joined again.

It turned.

The Commander waited silently deeper within the tunnels.

The Observer lowered its head.

The Commander understood.

Blackwood had not abandoned its Crimson Witch.

The academy had learned something.

So had the hunters.

...

Hours passed.

The stone finally gave way.

Light pierced the darkness.

"Aidan!"

Lyra dropped to her knees beside him before anyone else could reach him.

She didn't care about the blood.

Didn't care about the collapsing tunnel.

She wrapped both arms around him anyway.

"We found you."

Aidan managed the faintest smile.

"I knew..."

His voice cracked.

"...you would."

Behind them...

Malik and Cassian carefully lifted the fallen stone.

Elias leaned heavily against his crutch despite Helena repeatedly telling him to sit down.

Rowan looked at Aidan.

No relief crossed his face.

Only quiet resolve.

"Let's go home."

...

The climb back nearly killed Aidan.

Not because the tunnels fought him.

Because his body had finally reached its limit.

Halfway to the surface...

His legs gave out.

Without a word...

Cassian stepped beneath one arm.

Malik took the other.

Neither asked permission.

Neither treated it like charity.

Family carried family.

That was all.

# Chapter Twenty-Seven: The Quiet Question

Blackwood Academy stood.

Barely.

The western wall remained broken.

The eastern shelters still bore fresh scars.

Students worked in silence.

Even laughter had become rare.

The hunt had changed everyone.

...

Professor Helena spent nearly an hour stitching Aidan's newest wounds.

When she finished...

She remained standing beside his bed.

"You should be dead."

Aidan gave a tired smile.

"I've heard that before."

"I'm serious."

"So am I."

Helena looked down at the bloodstained bandages filling the waste basket.

"I don't understand your body anymore."

Neither did he.

...

That evening Rowan gathered only the inner circle.

No students.

No instructors.

Only Rowan.

Helena.

Lyra.

Cassian.

Malik.

Elias.

And Aidan.

"We've confirmed something."

Rowan placed several maps across the table.

"They knew every patrol."

Cassian nodded slowly.

"They attacked where we were weakest."

"They knew when we'd split."

Elias quietly added,

"And they knew we'd rescue Aidan."

Silence.

The words from the cavern returned uninvited.

*You have your traitor to thank for this.*

Aidan kept his expression unchanged.

His eyes drifted across the room.

Rowan.

Impossible.

Helena.

No.

Lyra.

Never.

Cassian...

Malik...

Elias...

No.

His chest tightened.

He hated himself for even allowing the thought to exist.

The hunter wanted this.

He knew it.

Yet...

Questions refused to disappear.

How had they always arrived first?

How had they always known?

"Aidan?"

Rowan's voice pulled him back.

"You've been quiet."

"I'm thinking."

"What about?"

He almost told them.

Almost repeated the hunter's words.

Instead...

He looked down at the maps.

"Our patrol routes."

Everyone waited.

"I think..."

He chose each word carefully.

"...from now on we should share information only when it's necessary."

Cassian frowned.

"You think they're predicting us?"

"Maybe."

It wasn't a lie.

Just not the whole truth.

Rowan studied him for a long moment before nodding.

"A precaution isn't unreasonable."

The meeting continued.

No one noticed Aidan quietly memorizing every face around the table.

Not with suspicion.

Not yet.

With uncertainty.

The doubt hurt more than any wound the hunters had given him.

...

Later that night...

Unable to sleep once again...

Aidan stood outside beneath the old oak.

The wind carried the familiar scent of rain.

Far away...

He heard the changing of the academy guard.

A few students whispering.

Malik laughing weakly at something Elias had said.

Life.

Still continuing.

His eyes drifted toward the dark forest beyond Blackwood's walls.

The hunters had left him alive.

On purpose.

Not to spare him.

To wound something far deeper than his body.

His trust.

The second heartbeat answered quietly beneath his own.

Steady.

Patient.

Waiting.

For the first time...

Aidan didn't feel completely alone beneath the night sky.

And that realization frightened him more than the darkness ever had.
