Cassian slowly stood.

His sword trembled.

Not because of fear.

Because of pressure.

He stared at Aidan.

Then...

He looked at Elias.

The blood.

The body.

His older brother.

Gone.

His breathing became uneven.

"...Brother..."

His voice cracked.

Just once.

The Veil commander fell to one knee.

He reached toward Elias...

But stopped halfway.

His hand trembled.

He had spent years convincing himself duty came first.

Yet now...

Duty had left him kneeling before the brother he could never apologize to.

For the first time in his life...

Cassian hated the Veil.

Not because of their mission.

Because he had obeyed it.
