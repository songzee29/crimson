Far away...

Black Hollow.

Cassian stood in the crimson ash once more.

The hooded figure appeared between the trees.

This time...

It did not disappear.

Cassian drew his sword.

"Who are you?"

The figure tilted its head.

Its voice sounded neither male nor female.

Only ancient.

> "You seek the missing."

Cassian tightened his grip.

"Answer me."

The figure ignored the demand.

Instead it looked directly into Cassian's eyes.

> "You believe your greatest enemy is Aidan Darkheart."

A pause.

Then...

> **"You are wrong."**

Before Cassian could move...

The figure dissolved into crimson ash.

Only four words remained, carried on the wind.

> **"Wake the sleeping wolf."**

Cassian's eyes widened.

"...Wolf?"

He had no idea what those words meant.

But somewhere beyond the mountains...

The second heartbeat echoed once more.

**THUMP.**

And this time...

Aidan heard it too.
