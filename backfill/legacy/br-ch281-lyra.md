Another year passed.

Late one evening...

Aidan and Lyra stood on a rooftop overlooking New Orleans.

Neither spoke.

Neither needed to.

Finally...

Lyra laughed.

"You still overthink."

"I do not."

"You definitely do."

She gently bumped his shoulder.

Just like Elias used to.

For a split second...

Aidan froze.

Then smiled.

Not because it didn't hurt.

Because someone else had learned how to reach him.

Their hands brushed.

Neither pulled away.
