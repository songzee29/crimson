They reached an abandoned service junction.

Old signage flickered weakly above them.

Lyra finally stopped pretending she was okay.

She grabbed Aidan’s shoulders.

“Look at me.”

He did.

Directly.

Calmly.

Too calmly.

“What is your name?” she asked.

“Aidan.”

“Do you know who I am?”

He paused.

“Yes.”

“That’s not an answer,” she said sharply. “Say it properly.”

He tried.

“I… think you are Lyra.”

“Think?”

“I don’t feel uncertainty about it,” he corrected.

Lyra’s grip tightened.

“You don’t feel anything about it?”

Aidan shook his head slightly.

“No.”

That word landed harder than she expected.

Lyra stepped back slowly.

Because now she understood the real problem.

The System didn’t erase *him.*

It erased the part of him that made other people *matter in a specific way.*

And that meant—

Aidan could still care.

But he wouldn’t know *why he should.*
