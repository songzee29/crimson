Six figures stepped from the shadows.

Black combat uniforms.

Silver masks.

A spear.

Chains.

A bow.

A young man with a sheathed sword.

He couldn't have been much older than Aidan.

Almost lazy.

But his eyes...

Never left Aidan.

One of the Veil hunters stepped forward.

"By order of the Veil Council..."

And the mysterious swordsman.
