That night...

Aidan dreamed.

Not of battle.

Not of Elias.

Not of blood.

He stood before enormous black gates.

Carved into them...

The crest of House Darkheart.

The gates slowly opened.

Inside stood a castle unlike anything he had ever imagined.

Dark stone.

Endless towers.

Crimson banners hanging motionless despite the wind.

An empty throne.

Waiting.

No one sat upon it.

Aidan slowly stepped forward.

Then stopped.

Someone was standing beside the throne.

Not Zee.

Not the Guardian.

A woman.

Her face hidden beneath a crimson veil.

She never looked at him.

She only spoke.

Her voice sounded ancient.

Almost like history itself.

> "A House is not remembered..."

A long silence.

> "...because it was powerful."

She gently rested one hand upon the throne.

> "It is remembered..."

Another pause.

> "...because of the judgments it leaves behind."

Aidan took another step.

"Who are you?"

The woman slowly turned.

Before he could see her face...

The dream shattered.

---

He woke suddenly.

His heartbeat raced.

Outside...

Rain poured across New Orleans.

The Crimson Legacy quietly materialized.

No quest.

No warning.

Only a single line.

> **House Record Updated.**

Then...

For the first time since Aidan opened the Crimson Book...

A second notification appeared beneath it.

It wasn't from the System.

It wasn't from the Legacy.

It looked older than both.

The letters seemed carved into stone instead of written in light.

> **The First Decree has been remembered.**

No explanation followed.

Just those six words.

Far beneath the Veil...

001 opened his eyes.

He smiled toward the ceiling.

"...Good."

"...Now history has begun remembering itself."

The first crack across his prison seal widened.

Almost invisible.

Almost.

But enough to let a single drop of ancient crimson blood escape onto the stone floor.

The moment it landed...

Every torch in the prison went out.

Darkness swallowed everything.

And somewhere, deep inside the silence...

Something laughed.
