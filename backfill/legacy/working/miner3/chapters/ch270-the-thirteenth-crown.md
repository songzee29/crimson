The memory began fading.

The Hall slowly returned.

The throne stood exactly where it had been.

Empty.

Waiting.

The System quietly appeared.

---

**Royal Trial**

---

No enemies.

No timer.

No battle.

Only one question.

---

> **"Will you sit?"**

---

Aidan looked at the throne.

Then...

At the twelve empty seats surrounding it.

He smiled.

A tired...

Honest...

Human smile.

Then quietly answered:

"...No."

Silence.

"I haven't earned it."

The Hall shook.

Not with disappointment.

Approval.

The throne slowly lowered itself into the floor.

Disappearing.

The room acknowledged his answer.

The System glowed brighter than ever before.

---

**Royal Trial**

**Completed.**

---

**Reason...**

> **The King refused the Crown.**

---

The Legacy didn't reward him with a spell.

Or an ability.

Instead...

The Journal opened.

A new page wrote itself.

---

> **Some men chase thrones.**

Another line.

> **Some inherit them.**

A final sentence appeared.

> **The greatest kings are the ones willing to leave them empty until they are worthy.**

The page closed.

As Aidan turned to leave, something caught his eye.

Behind the vanished throne...

Hidden within the stone...

Was another sealed doorway.

Unlike everything else in the Hall, it bore no crown.

Only a single crimson handprint.

Above it, carved in ancient script, were seven words:

> **"Only when the Vampire weeps may this door open."**

Aidan frowned.

"...Vampire?"

The ring on his thumb suddenly turned cold.

The cracked seal deep within the Crimson Legacy pulsed once.

Just once.

Then fell silent.

The Hall of Kings offered no explanation.

Only another mystery.

And somewhere, deep within the darkness beyond that sealed door...

Something answered the pulse with a single, slow heartbeat.

**...Thump.**
