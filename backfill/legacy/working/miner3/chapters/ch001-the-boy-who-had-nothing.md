Aidan Vale was seventeen years old.

Hungry.

Poor.

Alone.

The war had taken everything.

His mother died when he was eight.

His father disappeared years later while searching for food beyond the walls of their settlement.

Nobody came back from beyond the walls.

Nobody.

Aidan survived by stealing.

Not because he wanted to.

Because he had to.

Every day was a fight.

Every meal was earned.

Every night he slept with one eye open.

The world wasn't kind anymore.

It was cruel.

And cruelty rewarded strength.

Something Aidan didn't have.

He wasn't the fastest.

He wasn't the strongest.

He wasn't magical.

He wasn't special.

At least that's what he believed.

Until the day he found the book.

---

While scavenging beneath the flooded ruins of old New Orleans, Aidan discovered a sealed chamber hidden beneath a collapsed cathedral.

The room should not have existed.

The stone walls looked untouched by time.

Ancient crimson symbols glowed across the floor.

And resting upon a black throne sat a single object.

A book.

The moment Aidan touched it—

A razor-sharp pain shot through his hand.

Blood dripped onto the cover.

The symbols exploded with crimson light.

The entire room shook.

And for the first time in centuries...

The book opened.

---

### SYSTEM ACTIVATED

**WELCOME, HEIR OF THE CRIMSON BLOODLINE**

Aidan froze.

Words floated in front of his eyes.

Glowing red.

Impossible.

"Uh..."

The screen changed.

### ERROR

### USER APPEARS TERRIFIED

### ATTEMPTING SOCIAL INTERACTION

A voice suddenly echoed inside his head.

"Well... this is awkward."

Aidan nearly screamed.

"What was that?!"

The voice sighed dramatically.

"Wonderful. Three hundred years trapped inside a magical book and the first person I meet is a panicking teenager."

"WHO ARE YOU?!"

"My name is Rez."

Silence.

Then:

"I'm technically dead."

Pause.

"Mostly."

Another pause.

"Actually that's a long story."

Aidan stared into space.

"I've lost my mind."

"Nope."

"You aren't real."

"I'm very real."

"You're a hallucination."

"And you're ugly."

"What?!"

"See? Hallucinations don't insult people that accurately."

---

For the first time in years...

Aidan laughed.

Just a little.

And deep inside the book...

Rez smiled.

Because after three hundred years of darkness...

The Legacy had finally begun.
