Outside...

The fire suddenly stopped spreading.

The entire street became silent.

Too silent.

Aidan felt it before he saw it.

Someone had arrived.

Slow.

Measured footsteps echoed through the smoke.

Cassian.

His sword remained sheathed.

His eyes met Elias's.

For the first time in years...

Neither brother spoke.

The world seemed to disappear around them.

Just two brothers.

Standing on opposite sides of a promise.

Elias smiled first.

"...You got taller."

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"So did you."

His mind raced.

"...You know each other?"

Cassian finally stepped closer.

"I came to take the Heir."

"...Then you'll have to go through me."

For the first time...

Not anger.

Not hatred.

"I was hoping..."

"...you wouldn't say that."

Then twice.

Then began to flicker.

Not because the curse had broken...

But because destiny had finally arrived.

The next decision...

Would change every life on that battlefield forever.
