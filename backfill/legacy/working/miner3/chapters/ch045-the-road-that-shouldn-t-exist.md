Aidan couldn't explain why he turned left.

There was no sign.

No path.

No System prompt floating in his vision.

Nothing.

Yet the moment the tunnel split into two directions, his body moved before his mind decided.

Left.

Lyra stopped walking.

"Aidan."

He kept moving.

"Aidan."

This time he looked back.

"What?"

"Why are we going this way?"

He opened his mouth.

Then closed it.

Because he genuinely didn't know.

The answer wasn't in his head.

It wasn't a thought.

It was a feeling.

A pressure.

A pull.

Like gravity.

"I don't know," he admitted.

Lyra sighed.

"You keep saying that."

Aidan looked ahead again.

The darkness waiting down the tunnel somehow felt familiar.

Not safe.

Not welcoming.

Just...

right.

"If I go the other way," he said quietly, "something feels wrong."

Lyra's expression tightened.

Those words again.

Something feels wrong.

Not logic.

Not memory.

Instinct.

And that frightened her more than any Veil hunter.

---

The System suddenly flickered.

Not smoothly.

Like it was struggling.

---

> PATHWAY ANALYSIS FAILED

> DESTINATION UNKNOWN

> RECOMMENDED ACTION: STOP

---

Aidan froze.

The System had never done that before.

Ever.

Normally it always knew something.

Even when information was limited.

Even when danger was unknown.

But now—

it sounded uncertain.

Lyra saw the message reflected in his eyes.

"What happened?"

Aidan swallowed.

"The System wants me to stop."

"Then maybe we should."

The pull immediately tightened inside his chest.

Hard enough to hurt.

Aidan clenched his jaw.

"No."

The answer came instantly.

Without hesitation.

Without thought.

No.

---

The System remained silent.

Watching.

Learning.
