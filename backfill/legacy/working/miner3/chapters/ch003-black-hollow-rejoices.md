At that same moment...

Black Hollow celebrated.

Music filled the streets.

Children chased one another through the marketplace.

The scars of war had finally begun to fade.

Cassian stood before his people.

They cheered his name.

Not because he had conquered kingdoms.

Because he had rebuilt one.

His partner stepped beside him and whispered,

"You did it."

Cassian smiled.

"...No."

"We did."

For the first time in centuries...

His heart felt light.

The nightmares had stopped.

The guilt had quieted.

He believed...

He could finally become something more than the man who had lived through endless violence.

---

Then...

The Darkheart Brand burned.

Not painfully.

Coldly.

Cassian froze.

The cheering around him suddenly sounded distant.

The world became strangely quiet.

The laughter...

Faded.

The music...

Stopped.

Not because it truly had.

Because the decree had his attention.

A voice echoed in the deepest part of his soul.

The same voice he had heard the day Aidan died.

> **"Peace acknowledged."**

A pause.

> **"Judgment continues."**

The Brand stopped burning.

Cassian's smile disappeared.
