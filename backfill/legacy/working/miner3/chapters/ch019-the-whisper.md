A cold wind crossed the ruins.

Cassian looked up.

A hooded figure stood where the doorway of his home had once been.

Still.

Silent.

Cassian rose immediately.

"Who are you?"

The figure didn't move.

Instead...

It spoke softly.

> **"You forgave him..."**

Cassian frowned.

"...No."

> **"Not yet."**

The figure tilted its head.

> **"But you will."**

Silence.

> **"Unless..."**

Cassian's eyes narrowed.

"Unless what?"

The figure's voice became almost impossible to hear.

> **"...you remember..."**

> **"...what he still has..."**

The hooded figure slowly turned.

Its gaze drifted toward the south.

Toward House Darkheart.

Toward the Crimson Castle.

Then...

One name escaped its lips.

> **"...Lyra."**

The figure dissolved into drifting ash.

Gone.

Cassian stood frozen.

The name echoed through his mind.

Lyra.

She was the center of Aidan's peace.

His home.

His future.

His queen.

Cassian immediately pushed the thought away.

"...No."

"I won't become that man."

But the seed had been planted.

Not by the decree.

By choice.
