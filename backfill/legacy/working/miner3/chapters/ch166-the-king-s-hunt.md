The Crimson Pack tore through the ancient wilderness.

Trees became crimson streaks.

The earth shook beneath five wolves running as one.

At the front...

Aidan.

The Crimson Wolf.

His entire coat blazed crimson beneath the morning light, as though every strand had been forged from living blood. His eyes burned with crimson and molten gold, watching every movement within the forest.

He wasn't chasing.

He was leading.

---

Behind him...

Lyra matched his pace effortlessly.

The First Crimson Runefang moved with graceful precision, her senses flowing through the forest like a river.

To Aidan's right...

Malik bounded over fallen trees with youthful excitement.

To his left...

Kael moved with calculated precision, reading every trail before his paws touched the earth.

Darius stayed slightly behind, his sheer size breaking through brush and dead timber that stood in the pack's way.

For the first time...

The Crimson Pack hunted together.

---

Miles passed in silence.

Not because they couldn't communicate.

Because they didn't need words.

Every movement.

Every glance.

Every shift of Aidan's ears.

The pack understood.

---

Suddenly...

Aidan stopped.

The entire pack stopped with him.

Not a second later.

Instantly.

The forest became still.

Aidan lifted his nose.

The wind changed.

Blood.

Not fresh.

Old.

Mixed with something else.

Decay.

Fear.

Death.

---

Inside his mind...

The wolf spoke.

**Predator.**

The vampire answered.

**A powerful one.**

Aidan lowered his head.

*Together.*

Both voices became silent.

---

Behind them...

Rez and Mara arrived.

Rez knelt beside the torn earth.

His fingers brushed a deep claw mark carved into solid stone.

His expression hardened.

"I know this."

Mara looked down.

"What made it?"

Rez stood slowly.

"Not what..."

"...who."

---

The forest erupted.

BOOM!!

An enormous creature burst through the trees.

Nearly fifteen feet tall.

Its body was covered in twisted crimson scars.

Its claws looked like sharpened bone.

Golden eyes locked onto the Crimson Pack.

It wasn't a wolf.

It wasn't a bear.

It looked like something nature had abandoned centuries ago.

Malik instinctively growled.

Darius pawed at the earth.

Kael circled to the left.

Lyra remained beside Aidan.

The formation happened naturally.

No commands.

No hesitation.

A pack.

---

The beast roared.

The sound echoed through the valley.

Instead of fear...

Aidan stepped forward.

The Crimson Wolf stood directly in front of the monster.

His crimson and gold eyes never left it.

He let out one low growl.

Not loud.

Not wild.

A warning.

The creature hesitated.

For the first time...

It realized...

It wasn't hunting.

It had become the hunted.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The vampire smiled.

**It's afraid.**

The wolf lowered its head.

**Good.**

Aidan's instincts merged with both.

Not consumed by them.

Guided by them.

He didn't feel bloodlust.

He felt purpose.

This creature had slaughtered villages.

Destroyed forests.

Left nothing alive in its path.

Tonight...

The hunt ended.

---

The monster charged.

The ground exploded beneath its feet.

It crossed the clearing in seconds.

Aidan didn't move.

He waited.

One heartbeat.

Two.

Three.

Then—

The Crimson Wolf disappeared.

Not because he was invisible.

Because he was faster.

The beast crashed through empty space.

A crimson blur flashed beneath its body.

Aidan's claws carved four deep crimson lines across its side.

The monster roared in pain.

Before it could recover—

Malik struck its shoulder.

Kael slashed across its back.

Darius hit it head-on with enough force to send the massive creature crashing through three ancient trees.

Lyra leapt gracefully, her claws cutting cleanly across its neck before landing beside Aidan once more.

The pack never fought as individuals.

They moved as one.

Every attack flowed into the next.

Every opening created another opportunity.

From the ridge above...

Rez watched with a proud smile.

"They've stopped thinking like five fighters."

Mara nodded.

"They're thinking like one pack."

---

The creature staggered to its feet.

Its golden eyes now held something new.

Fear.

It looked at Aidan.

Then at the wolves surrounding it.

Then...

It turned to flee.

The Crimson Wolf stood directly in its path.

His crimson coat shimmered beneath the light filtering through the trees.

His red and gold eyes never blinked.

The beast stopped.

It understood.

There was no escape.

Aidan took one slow step forward.

Then another.

The entire pack followed.

Five wolves.

One King.

One Hunt.

And the monster that had terrorized these lands for years finally understood...

The wilderness had found a new apex predator.
