"My King."

Aidan turned.

"The eastern villages have completed their reconstruction."

"The southern covens have sworn loyalty."

The reports continued.

Not battles.

Not emergencies.

Progress.

Aidan listened to every report.

Not because he had to.

Because every village...

Every family...

Every citizen...

Was now his responsibility.

"You've become a ruler."

"I'm still learning."

"A king who believes he's finished learning..."
