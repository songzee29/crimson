The warehouse door groaned open.

Dust floated through beams of afternoon light.

Rows of abandoned crates stretched into darkness.

Water dripped steadily somewhere inside.

Malik whispered,

"...Okay."

"Maybe this wasn't my best purchase."

Lyra looked around carefully.

"It's too quiet."

Aidan nodded.

"Stay behind me."

The words surprised even him.

He hadn't meant to say them.

It just...

felt natural.

Three steps inside—

CRACK.

One of the floorboards snapped beneath Malik's foot.

The entire warehouse trembled.

Then—

Every crate slammed open at once.

Not by wind.

By force.

Black smoke poured from dozens of shattered boxes, twisting into shadowy figures with glowing crimson eyes.

Lyra took a step back.

"What are those?"

Aidan's Witch Authority reacted instinctively.

Dark crimson symbols spread briefly across his forearms.

The System flashed.

---

> **Hostile Cursed Entities Detected**
>
> **Recommendation: Survive.**

---

No instructions.

No glowing weak points.

Just one word.

**Survive.**

The creatures screamed.

Then charged.

Aidan thrust out his hand on instinct.

Crimson energy erupted across the floor like thorn-covered vines.

One creature slammed into the curse and exploded into black ash.

Aidan stared.

"...I did that?"

Another creature lunged.

Lyra shoved him aside.

"Aidan!"

Too slow.

Claws raked across his shoulder.

Pain exploded through his body.

The smell of blood filled the warehouse.

Every creature froze.

They all turned toward him at once.

Not with anger.

With hunger.

The System reacted instantly.

---

> **Blood Detected**
>
> **Curse Activity Rising**

---

Aidan's heart pounded.

For a split second...

He wanted to destroy everything in the room.

The feeling vanished almost as quickly as it came.

"What... was that?"

He didn't have time to think.

Another creature attacked.

This time Aidan moved without hesitation.

A crimson seal formed beneath his feet.

He disappeared.

Reappearing several yards away in a blur of cursed light.

He blinked.

"I... moved?"

The System answered.

---

> **New Technique Manifested**
>
> **Hex Step**

> **Status: Unstable**

---

Aidan smiled despite the danger.

"I'm getting stronger."

But across the warehouse...

Hidden in the rafters...

A pair of silver eyes watched.

Watching not Aidan's power—

But the darkness flickering behind it.

A quiet voice whispered into the shadows:

"So the first Authority is finally beginning to bloom..."

The figure disappeared before anyone noticed.

And somewhere beneath the warehouse floor...

An ancient seal cracked.

Just a little.

As if something buried long ago had heard the Crimson Bloodline return.
