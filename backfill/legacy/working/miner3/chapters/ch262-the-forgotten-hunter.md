Above the chamber...

The attacker landed lightly on the broken stone.

Wrapped in silver armor etched with ancient runes.

His face hidden behind a cracked mask.

He looked down at Aidan.

Then...

At the Crimson Assassin.

His voice was cold.

"So..."

"The stories are true."

"The Crimson Legacy has begun rebuilding itself."

Aidan said nothing.

The stranger slowly drew a blade forged from black silver.

"I am Caelum..."

"...First Hunter of the Veil Dominion."

His gaze remained fixed on the Assassin.

"We destroyed every Hybrid Vessel centuries ago."

"...That one should not exist."

The Crimson Assassin stood.

Still silent.

Still with closed eyes.

One step.

Then another.

It placed itself between Aidan...

And Caelum.

Aidan's eyes lingered on the notification for a fraction of a second.

Then he looked back at Caelum.

For the first time in days...

He spoke more than one sentence.

"...You chose the wrong night."

His Witch Authority stirred beneath his skin.

His Vampire Authority flooded his veins.

Behind him...

The Crimson Assassin shifted into a fighting stance.

It still hadn't spoken.

It never would.

Not until its true owner...

Finally awakened.

---

Far away...

Inside the prison of the Crimson Legacy...

A sleeping consciousness stirred.

Not awake.

Not aware.

Just enough to whisper one word...

Too faint for anyone but the Legacy itself to hear.

> **"...Aidan..."**

The voice faded.

The chains remained.

Rez continued sleeping.

But for the first time...

The distance between him...

And his body...

Had begun to close.
