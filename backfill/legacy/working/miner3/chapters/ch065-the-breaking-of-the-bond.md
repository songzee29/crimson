No one moved.

No one dared to speak.

Aidan held Lyra against his chest.

His hands.

His heart.

"No..."

"You promised me forever."

"...Forever..."

"...isn't measured..."

"...by time..."

"...It's measured..."

"...by love..."

"No."

"We still have QuinZ..."

"We still have Zuko..."

"They'll still come..."

"...because you'll never stop loving me..."

Not toward his heart.

Toward the place...

Where she had secretly bound the Wolf so long ago.

Her fingertips rested there.

"Aidan..."

"...there's something..."
