House Darkheart.

Rez stood in the royal training grounds before sunrise.

His crimson sword rested in both hands.

For hours...

He practiced alone.

Every movement was precise.

Controlled.

Patient.

When he finally lowered the blade...

A familiar voice came from behind.

"...You're rusty."

Rez laughed without turning.

"I was asleep for centuries."

Aidan stepped into the training circle.

"Excuses."

Rez smiled.

"So..."

"You want to remind me how much stronger you've become?"

Aidan simply raised one hand.

"...Begin."

The two disappeared.

Steel rang through the castle.

Guards stopped training just to watch.

Neither man fought to win.

They fought to remember each other.

Every strike rebuilt trust.

Every parry restored timing.

When it ended...

Both lowered their swords at the same moment.

Rez smiled.

"...You're faster."

Aidan answered,

"...You're smarter."

Neither considered it a compliment.

It was simply truth.
