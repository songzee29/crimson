Aidan walked into the bathroom.

Turned on the sink.

Splashed cold water onto his face.

He looked into the mirror.

Nothing unusual.

Just himself.

He exhaled.

Then...

The reflection smiled.

Aidan hadn't.

His heart stopped.

The reflection slowly tilted its head.

Its eyes...

Were crimson.

Not glowing.

Hungry.

It raised one finger.

Touched the glass from the inside.

Then silently mouthed two words.

> **"Not yet."**

The mirror shattered.

Thousands of pieces exploded across the room.

Lyra rushed from the hallway.

"Aidan!"

She found him standing barefoot among broken glass.

Blood dripped from his hand where the shards had cut him.

He stared at the floor.

"...It smiled."

Lyra looked at the broken mirror.

There was nothing there.

She gently took his bleeding hand.

"It's okay."

Aidan whispered,

"...No..."

"...it's not."
