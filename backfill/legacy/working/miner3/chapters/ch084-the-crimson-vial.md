Cassian returned to the abandoned cathedral.

The vial still rested on the altar.

Waiting.

He picked it up.

The blood inside reacted immediately.

It rose toward him.

Almost...

Recognizing him.

Azrath's voice echoed from the darkness.

"You came back."

Cassian didn't look up.

"I haven't taken it."

"No."

Azrath smiled.

"But now you're holding it."

Silence.

Cassian stared into the moving crimson liquid.

"...What is it?"

Azrath stepped from the shadows.

For the first time...

His smile disappeared.

"It's freedom."

Cassian laughed bitterly.

"It looks like blood."

Azrath nodded.

"It always does."
