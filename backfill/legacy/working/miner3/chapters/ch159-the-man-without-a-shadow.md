The chase lasted nearly an hour.

Through Bourbon Street.

Past musicians.

Past tourists.

Across rooftops.

Through abandoned alleyways.

The stranger never ran.

He simply walked.

Every time Aidan got close...

He somehow became farther away.

Until finally...

The man stopped beneath the oldest oak tree in City Park.

Without turning around he spoke.

"You've gotten faster."

Aidan frowned.

"You know me?"

"No."

The man smiled.

"I know your blood."

He slowly turned.

His face...

Didn't exist.

No eyes.

No mouth.

Only smooth pale skin.

Yet somehow...

He was smiling.

"I've waited a long time..."
