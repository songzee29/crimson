Three weeks passed.

Operation: Rise of the King continued exactly as planned.

Another cursed district fell.

Another family returned home.

Another forgotten church rang its bells for the first time in decades.

The newspapers all carried the same headline.

> **THE CRIMSON KING SAVES ANOTHER DISTRICT**

Children began drawing the Darkheart Crest on school walls.

Merchants hung crimson banners above their shops.

Hope...

Was spreading.

---

Cassian hated it.

He stood on a rooftop overlooking the celebration.

Every cheer below...

Felt like another reminder.

Not of Aidan's strength.

Of his own failure.

His hand unconsciously tightened around the hilt of his sword.

"...How long..."

"...before they stop saying his name?"

No answer came.

Only silence.

---

Then...

A whisper.

So quiet it was almost impossible to hear.

> **"Never."**

No one.

His jaw tightened.

"...Who's there?"

Nothing.

Only the wind.

He shook his head.

"...I'm losing my mind."

He walked away.

Behind him...

A single raven watched from the cathedral roof.

Its crimson eyes never blinked.
