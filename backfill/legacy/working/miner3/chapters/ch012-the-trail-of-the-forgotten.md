Aidan woke up exhausted.

Not the kind of tired that sleep fixed.

The kind that settled deep inside your bones.

The kind that came from carrying questions you weren't supposed to have.

---

The man across the street haunted him.

The smile.

The missing face.

The way the System had acknowledged him without explaining anything.

---

And then there was Lyra.

A girl who didn't exist.

A girl who somehow existed enough to leave messages.

A girl who now seemed to be looking for him too.

---

None of it made sense.

Which meant it was probably important.

By noon, Aidan found himself back in the tunnels.

Again.

The deeper he went, the more comfortable the darkness felt.

That realization bothered him.

---

Water dripped from cracked ceilings.

Rust-covered beams groaned overhead.

The city above felt impossibly far away.

Aidan pulled out his notebook.
