**

Near midnight...

Aidan rose from the throne.

He walked through silent halls.

Every mirror he passed...

Reflected someone else.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

He stopped before one ancient mirror.

Looked into it.

For a long time.

Then quietly asked...

"...Who..."

Silence.

"...am I?"

The reflection smiled.

He didn't.

The smile disappeared.

The mirror cracked.

From the inside.

A single crimson line spread across the glass.

Then another.

Then another.

Until the entire mirror shattered without Aidan touching it.

He simply kept walking.

Not looking back.

Because the man searching for himself...

No longer existed.
