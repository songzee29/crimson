Near midnight...

Aidan returned to St. Louis Cathedral.

Not because he remembered it.

Because something inside him...

Kept bringing him back.

He walked through the empty sanctuary.

Rows of abandoned pews stretched into darkness.

Candles had long burned out.

Moonlight poured through stained glass.

He stopped before the altar.

Silence.

For the first time since his death...

He didn't move.

Minutes passed.

Then...

His hand slowly reached toward the old wooden altar.

The moment his fingers touched it...

A memory flashed.

Just one.

Elias laughing.

Gone.

Then another.

Lyra smiling.

Gone.

Then another.

A younger Aidan standing beside Zee.

Gone.

The memories shattered like glass before he could hold onto them.

A low growl escaped his throat.

Not anger.

Frustration.

The hunger hated memories.

They slowed the hunt.

With one effortless motion...

Aidan drove his fist into the altar.

The ancient stone exploded into dust.

The cathedral shook.

The stained-glass windows burst outward.

The bell tower rang once.

A deep...

Lonely sound.

Echoing across New Orleans.

---

Far away...

Lyra's head snapped toward the city.

She knew that bell.

"...Aidan..."

Without waiting for permission...

She ran.

Malik chased after her.

The Guardian sighed.

Then followed.

Not because he believed they could stop him.

Because...

Some bonds are stronger than fear.

And somewhere in the darkness...

Azrath smiled.

The first stage of his plan had ended.

The second...

Was about to begin.

He whispered into the night...

> **"Run to him..."**

> **"Let's see how much of Aidan is truly gone."**

The moon disappeared behind crimson clouds.

And beneath the broken cathedral...

Something ancient answered.

Not with words.

But with a pulse.

One that had waited centuries...

For the Crimson King to become exactly what he was now.
