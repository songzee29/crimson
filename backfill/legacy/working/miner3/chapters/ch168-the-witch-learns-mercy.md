The Mirror Witch screamed.

Every reflection merged into one giant mirror suspended above the park.

Inside it...

Hundreds of missing children.

Crying.

Trapped.

Lyra looked horrified.

"They're alive..."

The Witch laughed.

"They're my power."

Aidan looked at the System.

For the first time...

There was no objective.

No recommendation.

Nothing.

He understood.

The Legacy wasn't going to solve this.

He had to.

He closed his eyes.

Not reaching for power.

Listening.

Feeling.

The Witch Authority stirred.

Not violently.

Quietly.

He remembered what the Guardian had taught him.

> **"A Witch understands the curse before breaking it."**

He opened his eyes.

The answer wasn't the Mirror Witch.

It was the mirror itself.

Every child was connected through one enormous curse thread.

If he shattered the mirror...

They would die.

If he broke the thread...

The mirror would collapse on its own.

He smiled.

"I got you."

Crimson Thread spread from his fingertips.

One thread became ten.

Ten became one hundred.

Then one thousand.

Every curse thread became visible only to Aidan.

One by one...

He began cutting them.

Not with force.

With precision.

The Mirror Witch's smile slowly disappeared.

"What..."

"...what are you doing?"

The last thread snapped.

The giant mirror exploded into harmless crimson light.

Children fell safely into Lyra's waiting barrier.

The Mirror Witch let out one final scream before dissolving into dust.

Silence.

Then...

Children crying.

Parents arriving.

Hugs.

Relief.

Life.

The amusement park felt lighter.

As if decades of suffering had finally been released.

The System quietly appeared.

---

**《TRIAL TWO COMPLETE》**

**Children Saved: 37**

**Civilian Casualties: 0**

---

Then...

Another message.

One that made Aidan stop breathing for just a moment.

---

**Witch Authority Synchronization**

**31%**

---

Thirty-one percent.

He was getting closer.

Much closer.

He didn't know it.

But somewhere deep inside the Witch Authority...

Ancient seals had already begun cracking.

Not enough to awaken the Curse.

Not yet.

But enough that the Legacy had begun preparing itself.

Preparing...

For the day it would no longer ask Aidan to survive monsters.

It would ask him to survive **loss.**
