The journey lasted three days.

No armies marched.

No banners flew.

Only three figures crossed the northern frontier.

Aidan.

Rez.

Lyra.

The Founder.

The Legacy Guardian.

The First Crimson Runefang.

None of them spoke much.

There was nothing left to say.

---

The Valley of Ash earned its name honestly.

The earth was gray.

Ancient trees stood like charred skeletons against the horizon.

Nothing grew there.

Nothing sang.

Even the wind seemed reluctant to pass through.

It was a place where old wars refused to die.

---

Cassian stood exactly where the messenger had described.

His sword remained planted in the ground before him.

His hands rested upon its pommel.

His eyes never left the approaching trio.

When Aidan stopped twenty feet away...

Cassian smiled.

A tired smile.

"You came."

Aidan answered simply.

"You asked."

---

Rez remained silent.

Lyra watched Cassian carefully.

Her crimson eyes carried no hatred.

Only caution.

Cassian noticed.

He laughed quietly.

"I expected you to look at me with more anger."

Lyra finally spoke.

"I died once."

A long silence followed.

"I'm not interested in dying twice."

Cassian lowered his head.

"...Fair enough."

---

The wind swept through the valley.

Ash drifted between them like gray snow.

---

Cassian looked back at Aidan.

"I know why you're here."

Aidan folded his arms.

"Do you?"

"You've come to fulfill the Fourth Judgment."

Aidan didn't answer.

---

Instead...

He took one slow step forward.

"No."

Cassian frowned.

"No?"

"I came because I'm tired."

The words echoed across the empty valley.

"Tired of death."

"Tired of revenge."

"Tired of watching two immortal men drag centuries behind them like chains."

---

Cassian stared at him.

Unable to speak.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez remained completely still.

He knew these words had never been spoken before.

---

Aidan continued.

"I've spent centuries believing my decree could only end one way."

"I was wrong."

---

The notification faded.

Not because the Legacy activated anything.

Because it had recognized what Aidan was about to do.

---

Cassian slowly removed his hands from the sword.

"What are you saying?"

Aidan's crimson eyes never left his.

"I'm saying..."

"...the curse ends today."

The valley became silent.

Even the wind disappeared.

Cassian laughed bitterly.

"You can't end it."

"I wrote it."

"You also wrote that it would continue."

Aidan nodded.

"I did."

"And today..."

"...I'm writing something greater."

---

Rez finally smiled.

Very faintly.

**Now you're thinking like a Founder.**

---

Cassian took a cautious step backward.

"I don't understand."

"You will."

Aidan extended one hand.

Not in friendship.

Not in peace.

In authority.

His voice remained calm.

Firm.

Unshakable.

"Cassian."

"Look at me."

Cassian's eyes instinctively met Aidan's crimson gaze.

For one brief moment...

Neither man blinked.

The Vampire Authority flowed naturally through Aidan.

Not because the Legacy activated it.

Because he chose to use it.

His will became absolute.

The command left his lips like law.

"Be still."

The world seemed to freeze.

Cassian tried to move.

His body refused.

Not from weakness.

Not from fear.

His own instincts recognized the command of the Founder.

His arms remained at his sides.

His legs would not answer him.

Only his eyes moved.

Confusion filled them.

"What..."

"...have you done?"

Aidan slowly walked forward.

Each step echoed across the Valley of Ash.

"I could kill you."

Another step.

"I could continue the curse."

Another.

"I could make you suffer for another thousand years."

He stopped directly in front of Cassian.

"But none of those things would break the cycle."

---

Lyra watched without speaking.

She understood.

For the first time...

Aidan wasn't acting from grief.

He was acting as the Crimson King.

---

Aidan raised one hand.

A single drop of **True Crimson Blood** formed at the tip of his finger.

It floated between them.

Brilliant.

Alive.

Ancient.

The valley itself seemed to bow before it.

Cassian's eyes widened.

"No..."

His voice trembled.

"You wouldn't..."

Aidan looked directly into his eyes.

"I already decided."

The drop drifted closer.

Cassian fought with everything he had.

His body never moved.

The Founder's command held.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf watched silently.

The vampire smiled once.

Neither spoke.

This decision belonged to the King alone.

---

Aidan placed his finger gently against Cassian's lips.

"Open."

Cassian's jaw resisted.

Then slowly obeyed.

One drop of True Crimson Blood fell onto his tongue.

The valley exploded with Crimson light.

BOOOOOOOM!!

Cassian's scream shook the mountains.

Ancient Crimson Blood surged through every vein in his body.

His old power shattered.

His immortality twisted.

His blood...

Began changing forever.
