Days later...

During a Veil raid on an abandoned monastery...

Cassian searched through the ruins alone.

Most of the books had rotted away.

One survived.

Barely.

Its cover crumbled the moment he touched it.

Inside...

Every page was blank.

Except one.

In faded black ink...

A single sentence remained.

> **"Power answers only to..."**

The bottom half of the page had burned away.

Only six letters survived.

**AZRATH**

Cassian frowned.

"...Azrath?"

Nothing happened.

No magic.

No light.

Just...

A strange feeling.

As if the name wanted to be remembered.

He quietly folded the page.

Slipped it into his coat.

Then walked away.

---

Deep beneath the earth...

A crack spread across an ancient crimson seal.

Not enough to free anything.

Not yet.

But enough...

For someone inside to smile.
