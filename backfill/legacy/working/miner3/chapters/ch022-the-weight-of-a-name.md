That evening...

Aidan walked through Jackson Square wearing ordinary clothes.

No crown.

No guards.

Just another young man trying to breathe.

A little girl ran up to him.

"Mister!"

He smiled.

"Hey."

She held out a drawing.

It showed Aidan with an oversized crimson crown.

Beside him...

People of every race and species stood together.

Above them she had written, in crooked handwriting:

**The Crimson King Saves Everyone.**

Aidan laughed softly.

"You made this?"

She nodded proudly.

"My mommy says you're the reason I don't have to be scared anymore."

His smile faded.

Not because he was sad.

Because the responsibility suddenly felt heavier than ever.

He knelt to her height.

"I'll do my best."

The girl hugged him.

Then ran back to her mother.

Aidan stood there holding the drawing.

His hands trembled.

Not from fear.

From pressure.

Behind him...

The Guardian watched silently.

He finally understood what Zee had once tried to explain.

Power wasn't the heaviest thing a king carried.

Hope was.
