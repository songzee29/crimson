Aidan stood alone on the cathedral roof.

The city lights reflected across the Mississippi.

He rested both hands on the stone railing.

For a moment...

Everything was peaceful.

Then...

He smelled it.

Blood.

Fresh.

Close.

His heartbeat quickened.

His eyes instinctively searched the streets below.

A man had scraped his arm while unloading supplies.

Aidan stared.

Too long.

The Witch Authority ignited across his hands without him calling it.

Trying to steady him.

Trying to remind him who he was.

"...Not today."

Almost.

"You coming inside?"

"Yeah."

Neither of them noticed...

A rune that had never existed before.

> **"...but hunger has learned to wait."**

And somewhere in the darkness...
