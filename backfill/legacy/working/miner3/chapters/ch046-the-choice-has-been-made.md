The battlefield fell silent.

Cassian watched Aidan disappear beyond the trees.

He could still hear his final words.

> *"What comes next... is your choice."*

The wind carried them across the broken ground.

Cassian looked down at the ring resting in his palm.

His home...

His peace...

His future...

Reduced to a single piece of metal.

He closed his hand until the ring dug into his skin.

"...You wanted me to understand your pain."

His voice cracked.

"...Now I do."

A long silence followed.

Then he whispered the words that changed everything.

> "...And I hate you for it."

There was no magic forcing him.

No voice in his head.

No curse taking control.

Only a man...

Choosing.

Cassian turned away from the battlefield.

Not toward Black Hollow.

Toward House Darkheart.
