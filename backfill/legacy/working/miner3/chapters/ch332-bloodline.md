That night...

Aidan stood before the Mississippi River.

Watching the current.

Lyra quietly joined him.

She didn't speak.

She simply slipped her hand into his.

Aidan smiled.

Then suddenly froze.

For one impossible second...

He heard her heartbeat.

Not just heard it.

Felt it.

Every beat.

Every pulse.

Every drop of blood moving through her body.

It was...

Beautiful.

His throat became dry.

His breathing changed.

The Witch Authority exploded across his hands.

Crimson runes wrapped around his wrists.

Trying to suppress something.

Aidan immediately let go of Lyra's hand.

She looked confused.

"...What happened?"

He stepped backward.

"...Nothing."

He lied again.

Because how could he tell her...

That for one horrifying moment...

The person he loved most...

Had smelled like the sweetest thing in the world?
