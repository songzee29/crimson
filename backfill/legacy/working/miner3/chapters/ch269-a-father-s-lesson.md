"I left this place..."

"...because kings should never worship crowns."

He looked directly at Aidan.

"They should serve people."

The twelve kings behind him nodded silently.

Zee continued.

"Every Crimson King who sat here..."

"...failed."

Aidan blinked.

"...Every one?"

"Every one."

"Even you?"

Zee smiled sadly.

"...Especially me."

Silence.

"You know why?"

Aidan shook his head.

"Because..."

"...we believed strength made us kings."

"It doesn't."

"Loss doesn't make you a king."

"Power doesn't make you a king."

"You became one..."

"...the moment you carried Elias home."

"...I couldn't save him."

"I know."

"I failed."

"I know."

"I wasn't enough."

Zee stepped closer.

His hand almost reached Aidan's shoulder.

Almost.

"If you had saved everyone..."
