Morning came.

Aidan stood before the gates of House Darkheart once again.

Lyra approached him.

"You're leaving."

"...Yes."

"For the Monarch?"

"...Yes."

She looked at him for several seconds.

Then quietly nodded.

"Come back."

Aidan didn't promise.

Kings couldn't promise survival.

Instead...

He answered honestly.

"...I'll return if I'm able."

For Aidan...

That was enough.

The gates slowly opened.

Three steps behind him...

The Crimson Assassin took its place.

The Guardian watched them disappear down the old road.

His voice barely rose above a whisper.

"The Blood Sovereigns tested his strength..."

He looked toward the eastern horizon.

"...Now the Monarchs will test his soul."

The wind carried the words away.

Ahead...

The road stretched into lands that even Zee had rarely entered.

Lands where forgotten Authorities ruled.

Lands where crowns meant nothing...

Unless the one wearing them could survive.

And somewhere beyond that horizon...

A throne sat empty.

Waiting.

Not for a hero.

Not for a savior.

For a king willing to claim it.
