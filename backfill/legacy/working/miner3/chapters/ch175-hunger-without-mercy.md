Inside the darkness...

Aidan opened his eyes.

Crimson.

Not glowing.

Burning.

He still couldn't remember.

He didn't know Lyra.

He didn't know Malik.

He didn't know Elias.

He didn't even know his own name.

Only...

Hunger.

A hunger unlike anything a human mind could imagine.

It wasn't a desire.

It was an instinct older than thought itself.

His lips parted.

One word escaped.

Barely audible.

> **"...Blood..."**

---

Outside...

Aidan's lips moved.

The same word.

So quietly...

Lyra almost thought she imagined it.

She froze.

Her eyes widened.

"...Aidan...?"

No answer.

---

His chest...

Still did not rise.

His heart...

Still did not beat.

---

Then...

His eyes opened.

Deep crimson.

Empty.

Cold.

Ancient.

He stared directly into the sky.

Not blinking.

Not breathing.

Just...

Staring.

---

Lyra gasped.

"Aidan..."

She reached toward his face.

The Guardian's voice exploded across the swamp.

> **"DON'T TOUCH HIM!!"**

Everyone froze.

The old man's expression had changed.

Not fear.

Recognition.

As if he'd seen this once before.

Long ago.

He whispered...

"...The curse..."

"...has claimed him."

---

Aidan slowly sat up.

Every movement unnaturally smooth.

No pain.

No hesitation.

The hole through his heart...

Closed.

Not rapidly.

Silently.

As though it had never existed.

He looked around.

His gaze passed over Lyra.

Over Malik.

Over the Guardian.

He recognized...

No one.

Then...

His eyes stopped on Cassian.

The smell.

The blood.

The heartbeat.

The vampire blood flowing through Cassian's veins.

Everything inside Aidan screamed.

**FEED.**

Cassian felt it instantly.

Every instinct he possessed as a vampire told him the same thing.

Run.

For the first time since becoming immortal...

Cassian felt true fear.

Because the thing looking back at him...

Wasn't the exhausted king he'd fought for days.

It wasn't the grieving brother.

It wasn't Aidan.

It was...

A newborn apex predator.

And it had just chosen its prey.

**End of Chapter 175.**

Brother...

I stopped **right here** on purpose.

I don't want Aidan's first act as a vampire to be a speech.

Or a conversation.

Or even recognition.

I want his **first instinct** to be exactly what we've been building toward:

**Hunger.**

Not evil.

Not cruelty.

Not revenge.

Just pure, overwhelming bloodlust.

That way, when he beats Cassian within an inch of his existence in the next chapters, readers understand it isn't because Aidan consciously chose revenge in that instant.

It's because the Vampire Curse has awakened before his humanity has any chance to fight back.

And **that** is the beginning of the true Vampire Arc. 👑🩸
