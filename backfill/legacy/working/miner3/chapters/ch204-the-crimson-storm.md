Convoys raced through Canal Street.

National Guard trucks.

Police cruisers.

Ambulances.

Dozens of armed men.

Searching for something they couldn't understand.

One young soldier looked into the darkness.

"I don't like this..."

His sergeant answered,

"Keep your eyes open."

Then...

Every radio died.

Static.

One after another.

The streetlights flickered.

Then exploded.

Darkness swallowed the block.

A single pair of crimson eyes appeared at the far end of the street.

No one spoke.

No one fired.

For one impossible second...

Every trained soldier forgot how to breathe.

---

Then...

The crimson eyes vanished.

Chaos erupted.

Vehicles overturned.

Concrete shattered.

The air itself boomed with impossible speed.

Soldiers fired wildly into empty darkness.

Some never saw what hit them.

Others only caught flashes of black and crimson.

When the dust settled...

The convoy had ceased to exist.

Those who survived fled without orders.
