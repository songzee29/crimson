Later that night...

The warehouse was quiet.

Everyone else had gone to sleep.

Only Aidan and Cyrus remained awake.

Cyrus stared at an old photograph.

It showed a much younger Zee.

Laughing.

Covered in mud.

Standing beside soldiers around a campfire.

Not a throne.

Not a crown.

Just friends.

Aidan had never seen that version of him.

"You really knew him."

Cyrus smiled.

"He wasn't always a king."

"What was he like?"

Cyrus laughed.

"He cheated at cards."

Aidan blinked.

"...What?"

"He absolutely cheated."

"He denied it every single time."

Aidan couldn't help smiling.

Cyrus continued.

"He snored."

"He couldn't cook."

"He'd volunteer for the dangerous missions before anyone else could."

His smile slowly faded.

"But..."

"...if one of us got hurt..."

"...he blamed himself."

Silence settled over the room.

Aidan looked at the old picture.

For the first time...

Zee didn't feel untouchable.

He felt...

Like a man.

---

Cyrus carefully folded the photograph.

Then looked Aidan directly in the eyes.

"I didn't come here just to ask for help."

"I came because..."

He reached into his jacket.

Pulling out an old crimson coin engraved with the Darkheart crest.

"...Zee told me..."

"...if the day ever came that his heir appeared..."

"...I was to stand beside him."

He placed the coin into Aidan's hand.

"I made your father a promise."

"And I don't break promises."

---

The System quietly appeared.

---

**《LEGACY ACKNOWLEDGED》**

**A Crimson Oath Has Been Fulfilled.**

---

No reward.

No new power.

Just another piece of Zee's legacy returning to the heir.

---

Outside the warehouse...

Hidden on a nearby rooftop...

The young swordsman watched the lights through the rain.

He had heard every word.

He quietly turned away.

"...So."

"The last of Zee's old guard has finally chosen a side."

He pulled his hood over his head.

Rain ran down the edge of his blade.

"...Good."

"The game is finally beginning."

And for the first time...

A smile crossed his face.

Not one of hatred.

One of anticipation.

Because he knew something Aidan didn't.

The Veil Council had just voted.

The order had been given.

The heir would no longer be observed.

He would be hunted.
