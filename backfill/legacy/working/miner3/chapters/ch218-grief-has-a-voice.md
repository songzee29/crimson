He still held Elias.

Blood dripped through trembling fingers.

No one could see his eyes.

"Aidan..."

"...Brother?"

Nothing.

Not attacking anyone.

Escaping.

So quietly...

Not loud.

Not angry.

Because this wasn't rage.
