The monstrous beast backed away.

Its golden eyes darted from one Crimson Wolf to the next.

There was nowhere to run.

Nowhere to hide.

The Crimson Pack had completely surrounded it.

---

The forest fell silent.

No birds.

No insects.

Even the wind seemed to stop.

Every predator hidden within the wilderness understood one thing.

A new Alpha ruled these woods.

---

The beast lunged.

Not toward Aidan.

Toward Malik.

It sensed the youngest hunter.

The easiest target.

It was wrong.

Before Malik even reacted—

A crimson blur intercepted the attack.

BOOM!!

Aidan slammed into the creature with enough force to send both of them crashing through a wall of ancient trees.

The ground split beneath them.

Dust filled the air.

---

When the dust settled...

The Crimson Wolf stood over the creature.

His entire crimson coat shimmered beneath the morning light.

His crimson and gold eyes burned with calm authority.

Not rage.

Judgment.

---

The creature tried to stand.

Aidan placed one massive paw across its chest.

The beast froze.

It couldn't move.

Not because of strength alone.

Because instinct itself recognized the King standing above it.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The vampire spoke first.

**It's over.**

The wolf answered.

**Finish the hunt.**

---

Aidan looked into the creature's eyes.

He saw fear.

Pain.

Instinct.

Not evil.

Just a predator that had forgotten balance.

---

The beast suddenly lashed out one final time.

Its claws tore across Aidan's shoulder.

Crimson blood splashed onto the forest floor.

The scent filled the air.

Every member of the Crimson Pack froze.

---

Malik's breathing deepened.

Darius lowered his head.

Kael's pupils narrowed.

Lyra instinctively stepped forward.

The scent of Founder's Blood awakened something ancient inside every descendant carrying the Vampire Authority.

Not madness.

Need.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The vampire inhaled deeply.

**Founder Blood...**

**The oldest call of the Bloodline.**

The wolf remained calm.

**The hunt is complete.**

---

Aidan didn't flinch.

He simply looked back at his pack.

One by one...

Their eyes met his.

They were waiting.

Not for permission to attack.

For permission to feed.

---

Rez stepped into the clearing.

He looked at Aidan.

Then at the wounded beast.

He nodded once.

"The hunt has ended."

"The pack has earned its meal."

---

Aidan slowly lifted his paw from the creature's chest.

The beast was too wounded to escape.

Its breathing became slower.

Weaker.

The Crimson Wolf threw back his head.

A single howl rolled through the valley.

Not a call to battle.

A call to feast.

---

Malik moved first.

Not wildly.

Not savagely.

With respect.

The Blood Fangclaw lowered his head and fed.

Darius followed.

Then Aidan.

The Founder took only what he needed before stepping back.

His hunger faded almost immediately.

He looked toward Lyra.

As the First Crimson Runefang, her wolf did not share the Vampire Authority. She remained beside Aidan, watchful, while Kael—also a Runefang—stood with her, both guarding the clearing as the Blood Fangclaws fed. Mara, the Crimson Hexborn, approached afterward in her human form, taking only enough to satisfy the vampire side of her Crimson Blood before stepping back.

When it was over...

No one had lost control.

No one had become a monster.

The forest remained untouched except for the fallen predator.

Aidan looked around at his family.

This...

Was how the Crimson Bloodline survived.

Not by denying its nature.

By mastering it.

---

The Crimson Wolf lifted his head once more.

His crimson and gold eyes swept across the trees.

The hunger was gone.

In its place...

Peace.

Inside his mind, the wolf spoke quietly.

**Now you understand.**

The vampire answered.

**Hunger is not our enemy.**

**Forgetting who we are... is.**

Aidan smiled.

*Then we'll never forget.*

The entire Crimson Pack turned toward House Darkheart.

The hunt had ended.

But something deep within the forest...

Had watched every moment.

And it had learned something dangerous.

The Founder no longer feared the darkness within him.

He had made it part of his crown.

Far beyond the trees...

A shadow slowly disappeared into the mist.

It carried only one message.

**The Crimson King has embraced his beast.**
