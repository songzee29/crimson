The swamp fell silent.

Neither young man reached for a weapon.

The swordsman slowly approached.

Stopping only a few feet away.

"You've improved."

Aidan blinked.

"We've never met."

"No."

"But I've watched."

He extended one hand.

"My name..."

A small smile crossed his face.

"...doesn't matter yet."

Aidan ignored the hand.

"You work for the Veil."

"I work with the Veil."

"That's not the same thing."

"It isn't."

Lyra quietly stepped closer.

She didn't trust him.

Neither did Malik.

The swordsman noticed.

"They're loyal."

He looked back at Aidan.

"Protect them."

Then he turned to leave.

Aidan spoke before he could disappear.

"Why stop your own people?"

The swordsman didn't look back.

"Because..."

"...I don't want someone else deciding how our story begins."

Then he vanished into the trees.

Malik stood there for a long moment.

"...That dude was weird."

Lyra quietly replied...

"No."

"...He was dangerous."
