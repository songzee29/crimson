That night...

Aidan finally returned to New Orleans.

Exhausted.

Covered in dust.

Unable to save everyone.

The little girl from Jackson Square ran toward him.

"Mister King!"

He forced a smile.

"Hey..."

She looked up at him.

"...My aunt was in one of those villages."

Aidan froze.

She wasn't crying.

She genuinely believed he had saved her.

"Did you find her?"

Silence.

Aidan couldn't answer.

The girl slowly lowered her head.

"...Oh."

She walked away.

Not angry.

Just...

Disappointed.

Aidan stood completely still.

That hurt more than any wound he'd ever suffered.

Azrath noticed.

The hook had set.

He only needed to reel it in.
