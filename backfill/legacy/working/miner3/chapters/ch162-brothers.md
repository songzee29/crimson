A week later...

Another emergency.

Another burning building.

Another impossible rescue.

Except this time...

Aidan wasn't alone.

A little girl was trapped on the third floor.

The staircase collapsed.

Smoke filled every room.

Aidan prepared to jump.

Elias grabbed his shoulder.

"You take the window."

"I'll take the stairs."

"The stairs are gone."

Elias smiled.

"So are my good decisions."

Before Aidan could stop him...

Elias charged straight into the smoke.

Aidan cursed under his breath.

"Idiot..."

He leaped through the third-floor window.

Inside...

The little girl was unconscious.

He picked her up.

The floor cracked.

Then—

A hand burst through the smoke.

Elias.

Covered in burns.

Coughing.

Still smiling.

"Told you..."

"...I'd find another way up."

The two of them escaped seconds before the building collapsed.

Outside...

The little girl hugged both of them.

Her mother collapsed in tears.

Elias quietly stepped away from the crowd.

Aidan caught up with him.

"You could've died."

Elias shrugged.

"So could you."

Silence.

Then Elias looked at him.

"You know..."

"My little brother used to make that same face."

"What face?"

"The one that says..."

> **'If I care about you... I'm going to worry about you forever.'**

Aidan laughed.

A real laugh.

"You talk too much."

Elias grinned.

"So I've been told."

They bumped fists.

A simple gesture.

But in that moment...

Neither of them realized they had just become brothers.

Far away...

The Veil captain read another report.

He stopped on one name.

**Elias.**

He quietly closed the file.

For the first time...

His expression changed.

Not anger.

Not hatred.

Recognition.

He whispered to himself...

> "So..."

> "...they finally met."

Then he burned the report before anyone else could read it.

Because somewhere deep inside...

Even he knew...

The day would come when duty would demand a choice neither of them wanted to make.

And that choice would echo through the rest of **Crimson Legacy**.
