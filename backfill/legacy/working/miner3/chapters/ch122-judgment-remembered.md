The two men finally saw each other.

Across an open field.

Separated by rain.

Neither moved.

The silence between them felt heavier than any battlefield.

Cassian broke it first.

"You look tired."

Aidan didn't answer.

His eyes slowly drifted downward.

To Cassian's hands.

To the veins beneath his skin.

To the unnatural stillness in his chest.

No heartbeat.

Nothing.

Aidan understood immediately.

His jaw clenched.

"...You."

Another step.

"...You became one."

Cassian smiled wider.

"I became stronger."

Aidan's eyes darkened.

"No."

His voice became colder than the rain.

"...You escaped."

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

"The man I judged..."

Aidan slowly raised one crimson-lit hand.

"...is dead."

The Witch Authority spread across the flooded ground.

Crimson circles bloomed beneath their feet.

"...What's standing in front of me..."

Aidan's voice dropped into something almost inhuman.

"...is a corpse pretending to be Cassian."
