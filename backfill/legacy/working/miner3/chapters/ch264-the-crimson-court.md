That night...

Aidan couldn't sleep.

The ring felt unusually warm.

He walked outside.

The city lights reflected across the Mississippi River.

Then...

The Witch Authority reacted.

Not violently.

Calling him.

The ring glowed once.

A single crimson thread appeared before him.

Leading into the darkness.

The System quietly responded.

---

**《ROYAL SUMMONS》**

---

Destination...

Unknown.

---

No quest.

No reward.

Only...

One sentence.

---

> **"A king's throne is never found..."**

> **"...it remembers its ruler."**

The crimson thread stretched toward the oldest part of New Orleans...

Far beneath the city...

Toward a place erased from every map.

Aidan looked at the thread.

Then back toward the warehouse.

He could wake everyone.

He could wait until morning.

Instead...

He smiled faintly.

The first genuine smile since Elias' funeral.

Not because he was happy.

Because he finally felt something he hadn't felt in a long time.

Curiosity.

He whispered into the night:

> "Dad..."

> "...what did you leave for me now?"

With one hand resting on the ancient ring and the other ready to shape the familiar signs of the Witch Authority if danger came, Aidan stepped into the darkness.

Behind him, the Journal opened one last time before the page turned.

Ink slowly formed.

> **"Every king inherits a crown."**

Another line appeared.

> **"Only a legend discovers why it was hidden."**

The page closed.

Somewhere beneath New Orleans...

Stone doors that had not opened in over a thousand years slowly began to move.

Something...

Or someone...

Was expecting the Crimson King.
