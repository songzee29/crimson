The sanctuary fell silent again.

Not because nothing remained.

Because it had already said too much.

Aidan stayed on one knee, one hand pressed against the cold stone floor beneath him.

His breathing refused to steady.

His chest still ached from emotions he couldn't explain.

He had never met the man.

He had never heard that voice before.

Yet hearing it break...

felt like losing someone.

Lyra slowly approached.

"Aidan..."

He didn't answer.

She knelt beside him.

"What did you see?"

Aidan stared ahead.

"...a king."

Lyra waited.

"He wasn't sitting on a throne."

Aidan swallowed.

"He was crying."

The words echoed through the sanctuary.

For some reason...

they felt wrong.

Kings weren't supposed to cry.

At least...

that was what Aidan had always believed.

But this one had.

Not because he was weak.

Because he was a father.

Although Aidan still didn't understand why that mattered.
