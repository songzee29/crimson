Beyond the bridge...

The mountains opened into a valley unlike anything Aidan had ever seen.

Thousands...

No...

Tens of thousands...

Of ancient vampires stood in complete silence.

No one moved.

No one spoke.

At the center of the valley...

A massive circular arena carved from crimson stone.

Above it...

Seven floating thrones.

Six occupied.

One empty.

Valerius sat upon the central throne.

Seraphine sat to his right.

The remaining Sovereigns watched in silence.

As Aidan entered...

Every eye turned toward him.

The pressure became suffocating.

Yet...

He never slowed his pace.

Three steps behind...

The Crimson Assassin entered the arena as well.

The moment the ancient Sovereigns saw the silent figure...

Several stood immediately.

One whispered in disbelief...

"...The Crimson Assassin..."

Another slowly tightened his grip on his throne.

"...House Darkheart truly has returned."

Valerius smiled.

Not at Aidan.

At destiny itself.

Then his voice echoed across the valley.

> **"Welcome... King Aidan Darkheart."**

> **"Today..."**

> **"The Blood Sovereigns will witness whether your crown..."**

> **"...is worthy of history."**

The valley fell completely silent.

The Royal Challenge...

Was about to begin.
