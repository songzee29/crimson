The next afternoon...

The Circle investigated another vampire nest.

Routine.

Simple.

Until...

One young vampire lunged toward Lyra from behind.

Aidan reacted instantly.

His hands formed the sign of **Chains**.

His mouth opened to speak.

But...

No word came out.

Instead...

His body moved on instinct.

He caught the vampire by the throat.

The room became silent.

The vampire stopped struggling.

Not because Aidan squeezed harder.

Because...

The vampire smiled.

Then whispered,

> **"...Father..."**

Aidan's grip loosened.

"...What?"

The vampire laughed.

Not mockingly.

Joyfully.

"As expected..."

"...you don't remember."

Then...

Without warning...

He bit through his own tongue.

Collapsed.

And died.

The room stood frozen.

Lyra slowly looked at Aidan.

"...What did he mean?"

Aidan stared at the lifeless vampire.

"I..."

"...don't know."

But somewhere deep inside...

Something answered.

Not a voice.

A feeling.

A strange ache.

Like hearing a song he somehow already knew.
