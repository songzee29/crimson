The road was quiet.

Only the sound of boots against ancient stone echoed through the mountains.

Aidan walked ahead.

Three steps behind him...

No one followed.

Rez frowned.

"...I don't have to stay three steps behind anymore..."

Aidan stopped.

Without turning around, he answered.

"...You never had to."

Silence.

Rez chuckled.

"...Guess old habits die hard."

For the first time in centuries...

The Crimson Assassin's formation was broken.

Rez walked beside Aidan.

Not behind him.

Beside him.

As he always had before everything was lost.

---

For several miles...

Neither spoke.

Not because there was nothing to say.

Because too much time had passed.

Finally...

Rez broke the silence.

"...How long?"

Aidan answered honestly.

"...Long enough."

"...Zee?"

Aidan lowered his eyes.

"...Gone."

Rez closed his own.

He already knew the answer.

He had simply hoped history had chosen differently.

"...And Elias?"

Aidan didn't answer.

He didn't have to.

Rez understood.

One by one...

The names he wanted to ask about disappeared before he could speak them.

The old world...

Really was gone.
