Hours passed.

The deeper they traveled...

The stranger the forest became.

Trees reached hundreds of feet into the sky.

Their bark shimmered with silver veins.

The wind carried whispers.

Not voices.

Memories.

Aidan ignored them.

They weren't real.

Only echoes left behind by forgotten Authorities.

Suddenly...

The Crimson Assassin stopped.

Its head tilted slightly.

Its closed eyes remained shut.

Its hand slowly wrapped around the hilt of its sword.

Aidan stopped walking.

"...Three."

He didn't need the System.

He had already counted the heartbeats.

Three.

Watching from above.

---

The branches exploded.

Three figures descended without making a sound.

Not vampires.

Not witches.

Not wolves.

Each wore bone-white armor carved with four interlocking circles.

One stepped forward.

"You crossed the border."

Aidan answered calmly.

"...Yes."

"State your purpose."

"...To continue walking."

Silence.

The three figures exchanged glances.

Then...

The leader smiled.

"...Good answer."

They stepped aside.

"The path belongs to those who don't ask permission."

Without another word...

They vanished.
