Aidan walked forward until only a few feet separated them.

Caelum held out his hand.

"For the sake of the world."

Long silence.

Then Aidan spoke.

"...No."

The Veil hunters immediately tensed.

Caelum remained calm.

"...Why?"

Aidan looked past him.

Toward the forest.

Toward the horizon.

Toward every kingdom depending on someone to stand.

Then he answered.

"I'll fight beside anyone..."

"...who stands to protect innocent lives."

His crimson eyes met Caelum's.

"But I won't place House Darkheart beneath another banner."

Silence.

"You don't need my loyalty."

"You need my cooperation."

Caelum slowly lowered his hand.

A small smile crossed his face.

"...That..."

"...sounds exactly like a king."

He turned toward his hunters.

"We move separately."

"But toward the same enemy."

The Veil disappeared into the forest as quickly as they had arrived.
