Aidan reached toward it.

The moment his fingertips touched the metal—

The room vanished.

Not into stars.

Into memories.

Thousands.

Kings laughing.

Kings crying.

Kings burying children.

Kings holding dying friends.

Kings celebrating victories.

Kings questioning themselves.

None of them were perfect.

None of them were fearless.

None of them were remembered because they never failed.

They were remembered...

Because they kept standing.

Aidan suddenly understood why the crown felt so heavy.

It wasn't made of silver.

It carried every life that had ever depended on it.

He immediately withdrew his hand.

Breathing hard.

"...I'm not ready."

The memories stopped.

The crown became still.
