Aidan spent three hours searching.

Three hours of dead ends.

Every database he could access.

Every school archive.

Every public record.

Nothing.

No Lyra.

No student history.

No disciplinary reports.

No transfer paperwork.

No attendance records.

Nothing.

It was as if someone had taken a person and cut her out of reality with surgical precision.

---

That should have been impossible.

Yet impossible things had stopped meaning much to Aidan lately.

---

The clock on his bedroom wall read 2:17 A.M.

His laptop glowed in the darkness.

Search results.

Empty folders.

Dead links.

A growing headache.

Frustrated.

Exhausted.

Confused.

Empty.

Silent.

Longer.

Darker.

Older.
