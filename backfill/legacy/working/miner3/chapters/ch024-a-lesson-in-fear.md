Hours passed.

Neither of them slept.

---

Around dawn, Lyra finally stood.

---

"We need to move."

---

"Already?"

---

She gave him a look.

---

"The scout found us once."

---

Aidan understood immediately.

---

Right.

---

Running.

---

That seemed to be his life now.

---

They moved through abandoned service tunnels beneath the city.

The deeper they traveled, the stranger things became.

---

Aidan noticed symbols carved into walls.

Ancient markings.

Burned circles.

Old warnings.

---

Some were fresh.

Some looked centuries old.

---

Every now and then Lyra would stop.

Examine something.

Then silently change directions.

---

Eventually Aidan couldn't take it anymore.

---

"What are you looking for?"

---

"Signs."

---

"Signs of what?"

---

She didn't answer immediately.

---

When she finally spoke...

her voice had become quieter.

---

"Things stronger than us."

---

That answer didn't make him feel better.

---

Not even a little.

---

Several hours later they reached an old underground marketplace.

Or at least it had once been one.

---

Now it was abandoned.

Broken stalls lined the tunnels.

Ancient lanterns hung from rusted chains.

Empty tables sat untouched beneath layers of dust.

---

"What is this place?"

---

Lyra slowed.

---

"It used to be safe."

---

The way she said it made Aidan uncomfortable.

---

Used to be.

---

Past tense.

---

the System suddenly appeared.

---

> WARNING

Aidan froze.

---

The text continued.

---

> HOSTILE AUTHORITY DETECTED

---

The words vanished.

"What happened?"

Lyra asked immediately.

---

Aidan looked at her.

---

"The System warned me."

The color drained from her face.

---

"Run."

Again.

Always run.

Aidan was getting tired of running.

Unfortunately...

the darkness ahead had other plans.

Then another.

Then another.

Three strangers.

---

All wearing gray masks.

---

All carrying strange black blades covered in symbols.

---

And all staring directly at Aidan.

---

Almost curiously.

Then spoke.

---

"The heir."

"You were easier to find than expected."
