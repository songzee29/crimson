Miles away...

Cassian returned to the abandoned cathedral.

The place where everything changed.

The place where he drank the blood.

The place where Azrath found him.

The altar still stood.

Cracked.

Weathered.

Forgotten.

Cassian rested one hand against it.

His Darkheart Brand burned faintly beneath his glove.

"...One day..."

"...I'll rip that crest off my skin."

Azrath's voice answered from the darkness.

"No."

Cassian didn't turn around.

"You'll learn to fear it."

---

Azrath stepped into the moonlight.

His smile was calm.

Patient.

"The mark isn't your greatest punishment."

Cassian frowned.

"Then what is?"

Azrath looked toward New Orleans.

"The fact..."

"...that you're still thinking about him."

---

Cassian's fist slammed into the altar.

Stone shattered.

"I don't think about him."

Azrath simply smiled.

"You just said his name..."

"...three times."

Cassian froze.

Azrath walked away.

He didn't need another word.

The silence was enough.
