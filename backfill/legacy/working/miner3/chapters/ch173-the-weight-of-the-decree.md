Aidan remained still.

Cassian stood with his sword lowered at his side.

Neither rushed forward.

This wasn't a battle born from hatred.

It was the continuation of a sentence spoken centuries ago.

---

Only the wind spoke.

Cassian looked toward Elena's grave one last time before returning his gaze to Aidan.

"So..."

"...is this where you take everything from me again?"

Aidan's expression never changed.

"The decree doesn't force your choices."

"It only follows them."

Cassian laughed bitterly.

"I've heard those words before."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez watched in silence.

He knew this moment wasn't about strength.

It was about fate.

**He's broken.**

Aidan answered quietly.

*Not yet.*

---

Cassian planted the tip of his sword into the frozen ground.

"I spent centuries running from you."

"I crossed kingdoms."

"I buried my name."

"I built another life."

He looked back at the grave.

"I loved again."

His voice cracked.

"And for the first time..."

"...I believed the curse had ended."

Snow settled across his shoulders.

"But then you came."

---

Aidan took a single step forward.

"I didn't come because I wanted to."

"I came because the Judgment reached you."

Cassian closed his eyes.

"I know."

---

For several long moments...

Neither man spoke.

Finally, Cassian asked the question he had carried for centuries.

"Do you still hate me?"

The wind carried the words away.

Aidan answered honestly.

"No."

Cassian slowly opened his eyes.

"No?"

"No."

"Then why continue this?"

Aidan looked toward the crimson sky.

"Because hatred died a long time ago."

"My decree didn't."

---

The silence that followed was heavier than any battle.

He couldn't see the Legacy.

But he could see something had changed.

"You've become colder."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

"I've become responsible for the words I spoke."

---

Behind Aidan...

The snow shifted.

Rez stepped from the shadows.

His crimson coat moved gently in the wind.

Cassian looked at him and immediately recognized him.

"The Crimson Assassin..."

Rez met his gaze.

"It's been a long time."

Cassian gave a tired smile.

"I thought you died."

"I've been accused of worse."

For the first time...

A faint smile crossed Cassian's face.

It disappeared just as quickly.

---

Rez looked at Elena's grave.

"You truly loved her."

Cassian nodded.

"More than my own life."

"And she knew who you were?"

"Everything."

"Even Aidan?"

Cassian nodded again.

"She told me to stop running."

His voice became almost a whisper.

"I should have listened."

---

Aidan looked down at the crimson rose resting on the grave.

Then he looked back at Cassian.

"Leave this place."

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

"Go."

"I won't fight you here."

Confusion spread across Cassian's face.

"This is Judgment."

"It is."

"Then why spare me?"

Aidan's crimson eyes hardened.

"Because she has nothing to do with this."

He nodded toward Elena's grave.

"I won't stain her resting place with our curse."

Cassian stood speechless.

He had expected fury.

He had expected violence.

He had expected the monster from centuries ago.

Instead...

The man standing before him honored the dead.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The vampire remained silent.

The wolf lowered its head.

Neither objected.

For the first time...

Both Authorities accepted the King's decision.

---

Cassian slowly pulled his sword from the frozen ground.

"You've changed."

Aidan answered quietly.

"So have you."

A cold wind swept through the cemetery.

The crimson rose on Elena's grave remained untouched.

Aidan turned away.

"Go bury your dead in peace."

"When the time comes..."

"...I'll find you again."

Cassian watched Aidan walk into the falling snow.

He understood.

The Third Judgment had begun.

But this first meeting...

Was not its conclusion.

It was its warning.

Behind Aidan, Rez followed in silence.

Inside the storm, his voice reached Aidan through the Soul Link.

**You know he'll come after Lyra.**

Aidan didn't stop walking.

*I know.*

**Then why let him live?**

Aidan looked into the blizzard ahead.

*Because Judgment isn't vengeance.*

*It's choice.*

*And Cassian has just been given one.*

The snow swallowed them both.

Far behind...

Cassian stood alone before Elena's grave.

His grip tightened around the hilt of his sword.

For the first time in centuries...

The thought entered his mind.

If the only way to end the Darkheart Curse...

Was to make Aidan suffer as he had suffered...

Then perhaps...

The Queen of House Darkheart...

Would become the next target.
