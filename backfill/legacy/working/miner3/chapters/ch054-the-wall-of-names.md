As they prepared to leave the central chamber, something caught Lyra's eye.

A wall.

Hidden behind one of the massive stone pillars.

Thousands of names.

Carved into crimson stone.

Some fresh.

Most ancient.

Every name had a single line beneath it.

Not titles.

Not dates.

One sentence.

One sacrifice.

Lyra slowly stepped closer.

"What is this..."

Aidan joined her.

The first name read:

**Elias Thorn**

*"He chose the kingdom over himself."*

The next.

**Selene Voss**

*"She carried a curse so her children wouldn't."*

Another.

**Marcus Vale**

*"He never returned."*

The further they looked...

the darker the sacrifices became.

No victories.

No celebrations.

Only costs.

Every person remembered here...

had given something away.

Finally...

Aidan's eyes reached an empty space.

No name.

Just smooth stone.

Waiting.

Then—

the stone began writing itself.

Very slowly.

Letter by letter.

Lyra's breath caught.

"Aidan..."

He watched as the first word appeared.

**AIDAN**

Then it stopped.

Nothing else.

Just his name.

Waiting.

As if the sanctuary itself didn't yet know how his story would end.
