For hours they talked.

Or rather—

Aidan asked questions.

Lyra decided which ones deserved answers.

The first truth shattered everything.

Erasure wasn't death.

It was worse.

When someone was erased:

The world forgot them.

Records vanished.

Photos changed.

History rewrote itself.

Friends forgot.

Family forgot.

Everyone forgot.

Except those protected by special authority.

Special curses.

Or special bloodlines.

Like Aidan's.

Then came the second truth.

The people responsible for erasures weren't monsters.

They were humans.

An organization.

Ancient.

Powerful.

Hidden.

Their purpose?

To remove dangerous things from reality.

Dangerous people.

Dangerous bloodlines.

Dangerous secrets.

Dangerous legacies.

Including Zee's.

Aidan sat silently.

Trying to process everything.

Then Lyra delivered the final blow.

The thing that changed everything.

She looked directly at him and said:

"Three weeks ago, before you opened that book, nobody knew you existed."

Aidan frowned.

"What?"

Lyra's expression darkened.

"You weren't hidden."

She swallowed.

"You were erased."

Silence filled the room.

Aidan's blood ran cold.

Because for the first time since the Crimson Book chose him...

he realized the question was never:

> Why did the System choose me?

The real question was:

> Who erased me... and why did Zee's legacy bring me back?

And somewhere deep inside the Bloodbound Verdict System...

something ancient stirred slightly harder than before.

Not awake.

Not yet.

But listening.
