The fire had long since died.

Only smoke curled from the ruins of Cassian's home.

The villagers stood at a respectful distance.

No one spoke.

No one blamed.

Because everyone knew.

This wasn't an accident.

This was Judgment.

---

Cassian walked slowly through the ashes.

Every step crunched beneath his boots.

His hand reached down.

He picked up a half-burned wooden carving.

It was the first gift his partner had ever made for him.

It crumbled into ash.

His fingers tightened.

His breathing became heavier.

---

His partner quietly knelt beside him.

"We're alive."

Cassian nodded.

"...We are."

"We can rebuild."

He looked across the blackened ruins.

"...Again."

But this time...

There was no hope in his voice.

Only exhaustion.
