The Sanctuary had gone quiet.

The echoes faded.

The crimson crystals dimmed until they looked like ordinary stone once more.

Aidan stood motionless.

The Founder's Warning still lingered inside his mind.

> **Do not mistake my path for yours.**
>
> **If you become me...**
>
> **The Crimson Legacy has failed.**

For the first time since touching the Crimson Book...

Aidan wasn't thinking about power.

He was thinking about Zee.

Not the legend.

The man.

A father.

A king who had apparently wanted something better for the son he was forced to hide.

---

The System interrupted his thoughts.

Unlike every notification before...

This one unfolded slowly.

Almost respectfully.

The crimson borders were replaced by ancient black runes burning with deep red light.

The interface itself seemed... older.

---

**《CRIMSON LEGACY》**

**Legacy Echo Accepted**

**Evaluating Current Heir...**

---

A loading symbol appeared.

Not a spinning circle.

A crimson crown slowly forming itself one crack at a time.

The Guardian immediately noticed Aidan's expression change.

"...It's speaking to you again."

Aidan nodded.

"It feels..."

He searched for the right word.

"...different."

The Guardian smiled.

"It should."

"You are no longer speaking to the System."

Aidan looked up.

"What?"

The old man pointed toward the Sanctuary floor.

"You're speaking to the Legacy."

---

The crown finished forming.

Then shattered.

A single message appeared.

---

> **NEW LEGACY QUEST**

## **Become someone the Founder could never be.**

---

Aidan frowned.

"...That's the quest?"

Nothing else appeared.

No directions.

No reward.

No objective.

Just one impossible sentence.

---

The Guardian laughed quietly.

"What is it?"

Aidan repeated the quest aloud.

The old man closed his eyes.

"...Of course."

Lyra looked between them.

"What does that even mean?"

The Guardian answered before Aidan could.

"It means the Legacy doesn't want another Zee."

"It wants the first Aidan."
