High above House Darkheart...

The Crimson Assassin stood motionless atop the highest tower.

Its silver-crimson eyes scanned the darkness.

Then...

Its head turned.

North.

Something moved.

Without hesitation...

The Crimson Assassin disappeared.

Not with magic.

With impossible speed.

Across rooftops.

Over walls.

Into the surrounding forest.

Miles from the castle...

It stopped.

The air smelled of ash.

The ground carried only one footprint.

The Assassin knelt.

Touched it.

Its eyes flashed crimson.

For the first time...

The silent guardian sensed another presence.

Not the Ash King.

Not the Veil.

Someone...

Watching both.

The Crimson Assassin vanished again.

Returning home with a warning no words could express.
