## 🔒 PERMANENT RULE — REZ, THE WITCH HYBRID, AND THE SOUL ANCHOR

This is now canon for **Crimson Legacy**.

### During the Witch Arc

- Rez remains completely asleep inside the Crimson Legacy.
- Nobody can see or hear Rez.
- Aidan does not know Rez exists.
- The System continues acting as Aidan's only guide.

**Nothing changes.**

### During the Witch Curse

When Elias dies...

- The Witch Curse breaks.
- Aidan completely loses emotional control.
- The Witch Authority erupts.
- The System glitches exactly like it did when Aidan first opened the Crimson Book.
- The Vampire Authority is accidentally cracked—but **not unlocked**.
- The collision of the Witch Authority and the cracked Vampire Authority accidentally creates an **empty Witch Hybrid vessel**.

This is an accident.

Not a spell.

Not a choice.

Not an ability.

It is the first time the Crimson Legacy itself encounters something outside its own rules.

---

### The Soul Anchor

Instead of waking Rez...

The Crimson Legacy permanently creates a **Soul Anchor**.

That Soul Anchor links:

- Aidan
- Rez's sleeping soul
- The empty Witch Hybrid vessel

The connection exists...

But nothing awakens.

---

### Vampire Arc (100 Years Later)

When Aidan dies and awakens as a vampire...

- His humanity switch is automatically OFF.
- Rez is **still asleep**.
- The Witch Hybrid vessel is **still asleep**.
- Aidan spends roughly a century alone, consumed by grief, bloodlust, and the struggle to control unimaginable power.

No one is there to stop him.

No one is there to guide him.

That darkness has to be earned.

Only after that long period...

When the story reaches the moment we've been building toward...

The Witch Hybrid vessel awakens.

At that exact same moment...

Rez awakens.

### Permanent Mental Link

Even after Rez has a physical body...

The Soul Anchor remains forever.

That means:

- Rez can speak inside Aidan's mind anytime.
- Aidan can answer mentally without speaking aloud.
- Nobody else hears those conversations.
- Rez can still physically interact with Lyra, Malik, Sienna, and the rest of the world.

We keep the unique relationship that made Rez special while allowing him to eventually exist in the physical world.

**Elias is never replaced.**

Rez does **not** become "the new Elias."

Those are two completely different relationships.

- Elias is Aidan's brother by choice during his human years.
- Rez is Aidan's brother through the Crimson Legacy and Zee's past.

One cannot replace the other.

That keeps Elias' sacrifice meaningful forever.
