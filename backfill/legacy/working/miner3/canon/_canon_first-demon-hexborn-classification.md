Because Cassian is being transformed by **Aidan's True Crimson Blood** during the breaking of the Darkheart Curse, his blood doesn't simply recreate an existing race—it **evolves**.

So the classification would be:

## 👑📜 Crimson Bloodline Classification

**Cassian**
- **Race:** **First Crimson Demon Hexborn**
- **Authorities:**
  - 🧙🏽 Crimson Witch Authority
  - 🩸 Crimson Vampire Authority
- **Evolution:** Demon
- **Founder:** Aidan Darkheart

That makes him fundamentally different from a standard **Crimson Hexborn**. He's the **first Demon Hexborn**, born directly from the Founder's True Crimson Blood at the moment the curse is broken.

It also creates a nice progression:

- 🧙🏽🩸 **Crimson Hexborn** → Witch + Vampire
- 🧙🏽🐺 **Crimson Runefang** → Witch + Wolf
- 🩸🐺 **Crimson Blood Fangclaw** → Vampire + Wolf
- 👑🧙🏽🩸🐺 **Crimson Trinity** → Witch + Vampire + Wolf
- 😈🧙🏽🩸 **Crimson Demon Hexborn** → Demon evolution of the Witch + Vampire bloodline

That gives Cassian his own unique place in the Crimson Bloodline instead of making him another Blood Fangclaw, while also marking him as the first person to undergo this evolution through Aidan's True Crimson Blood. It makes his transformation feel like a historic event rather than a standard turning.
