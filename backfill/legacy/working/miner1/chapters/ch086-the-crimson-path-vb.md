Night welcomed him.

The darkness no longer hid the world.

It revealed it.

Aidan crossed abandoned roads, silent fields, and forgotten forests with unnatural speed.

More than once…

He reached the place he intended to go before realizing he had already moved.

His body hunted efficiency without instruction.

A weathered stone marker stood beside the road.

He brushed it while passing.

The entire marker shattered into fragments.

He never looked back.

Miles later…

The scent reached him.

Fresh blood.

Human.

Close.

His body stopped before his mind caught up.

A roadside inn rested beyond the trees.

Warm light escaped through narrow windows.

Inside…

Living hearts filled the building.

One child coughed weakly.

Two merchants argued over cards.

An elderly traveler slept beside the fire.

A stable hand wrapped a fresh cut across his palm.

That single wound overwhelmed everything else.

The scent pierced straight through the fire consuming Aidan’s throat.

His fangs descended.

His vision narrowed.

Every heartbeat inside the inn became perfectly distinct.

His muscles tightened.

One step carried him into the shadows overlooking the building.

Then another.

The hunger no longer asked.

It demanded.

He did not notice.

He only heard the blood moving beneath living skin.

The ability settled into his body as naturally as breathing once had.

No practice.

No instruction.

Only instinct.

He shifted his weight.

The ground cracked beneath one foot.

In the next instant he stood on the roof of the inn.

He had not decided to move.

His body had already done it.

Below him…

The stable hand tightened the bandage around his bleeding palm.

The scent struck Aidan harder than before.

His throat felt as though it were tearing itself apart.

Animal blood would not answer it.

Distance would not answer it.

Willpower would not answer it.

Only one thing would.

The Crimson Vampire stared through the rain-streaked roof toward the living hearts below.

For one endless moment…

He remained perfectly still.

Then he turned his crimson gaze south.

The ancient sealed doors still called to him.

The hunger called louder.

Without another sound, Aidan disappeared into the darkness, racing toward New Orleans while the First Hunger remained unresolved, growing more violent with every passing mile.
