The thread of crimson-black light rising from beneath the old oak refused to disperse.

Instead…

It climbed.

Slowly.

Like smoke remembering where it belonged.

Every protective ward surrounding Blackwood Academy vibrated in response.

Golden barriers shimmered across rooftops.

Ancient runes carved into the academy walls ignited one after another.

The entire campus had become a fortress.

Yet the Curse had entered anyway.

Not by breaking the barriers.

By arriving before they understood what they were defending against.

Master Rowan never took his eyes off the crimson thread.

“It isn’t testing our defenses.”

Professor Helena frowned.

“Then what is it doing?”

“It is learning our hearts.”

Silence answered him.

⸻

Students had already been evacuated into the main buildings.

Faculty members reinforced every ward they knew.

No one panicked.

But everyone moved faster than before.

Headmaster Aldren stood in the center of the courtyard.

“Seal the eastern dormitory.”

“Move everyone into the central halls.”

“No one remains alone.”

Teachers immediately obeyed.

Aidan noticed the order.

No one remains alone.

The Curse smiled.

He felt it.

Not as laughter.

As satisfaction.

⸻

Without warning…

Malik stopped walking.

He stared toward the old gymnasium.

“…Dad?”

Everyone turned.

There was no one standing there.

Only shadows beneath the stone archway.

Malik took one step forward.

“I heard him.”

Professor Helena immediately caught his arm.

“There isn’t anyone there.”

“He called my name.”

“No.”

Rowan’s voice cut cleanly through the courtyard.

“The Curse did.”

Malik’s breathing became uneven.

“He sounded…”

“…exactly like him.”

The shadow beneath the archway quietly disappeared.

Aidan’s chest tightened.

It wasn’t attacking with power.

It was attacking with memory.

⸻

Minutes later…

Cassian froze.

He turned toward the academy cemetery.

His expression lost all color.

“No…”

His voice barely escaped.

“She’s alive…”

Lyra immediately looked toward him.

“Cassian?”

He took another step.

“I know that voice.”

Aidan moved in front of him.

“Cassian.”

The older boy never looked away.

“They’re calling me.”

Aidan placed one hand against Cassian’s shoulder.

“Hold.”

The Law spread gently through him.

Not controlling Cassian.

Stabilizing him.

Cassian’s breathing steadied.

His eyes slowly focused again.

The distant voice vanished.

He closed his eyes.

“…Thank you.”

Aidan quietly nodded.

The Law had worked.

Not because it erased pain.

Because it gave someone enough time to choose.

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

CRIMSON LAW

Hold

Practical Application Confirmed

The greatest preservation…

Is another person’s freedom to choose.

Mastery Increased.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message faded.

⸻

That evening…

Rowan gathered Aidan, Lyra, Cassian, Malik, and Elias inside the old library.

Not for another lecture.

For honesty.

Candles burned quietly between them.

Outside…

The crimson-black thread continued rising beyond the old oak.

“I have one lesson left.”

Rowan’s voice remained calm.

“Only one.”

No one interrupted him.

“The Curse cannot create.”

“It only twists.”

He looked toward each of them.

“It cannot invent doubt.”

“It finds doubt already living inside you.”

“It cannot invent grief.”

“It finds grief.”

“It cannot invent fear.”

“It finds fear.”

He finally looked at Aidan.

“So tell me…”

“What defeats it?”

Aidan thought for several long moments.

Then slowly answered.

“Truth.”

Rowan smiled.

“No.”

Aidan frowned.

“Love.”

The room became completely still.

“When people love one another enough…”

“…they tell the truth.”

“They forgive.”

“They stay.”

“They choose each other.”

“The Curse cannot survive where hearts refuse to abandon one another.”

No one spoke.

Not because they disagreed.

Because every one of them suddenly understood why the Curse had begun with relationships instead of battle.
