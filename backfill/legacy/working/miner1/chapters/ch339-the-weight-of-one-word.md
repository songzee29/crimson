Its light spread beyond the chamber.

Foundation Threads surged through the surrounding walls.

One after another…

Dark passages awakened.

Ancient doors quietly unlocked.

The mountain itself seemed lighter.

Then…

The crystal flickered again.

Harder this time.

Its light briefly turned crimson.

Every Foundation Thread trembled.

Aidan felt it instantly.

Not here.

Far deeper.

Something pressed against the Foundation from below.

Not attacking.

Waiting.

The Witch Curse had moved again.

Only a little.

Enough.

His chest tightened.

Aidan whispered.

“They’re still here.”

Hold stopped collapse.

This was different.

This wasn’t breaking.

It was drifting apart.

He knelt beside the fractured circle.

Closed his eyes.

Foundation Awareness.

Foundation Thread.

The countless streams revealed themselves again.

None were broken.

Only moving away from one another.

Like people who had forgotten how to stand together.

Aidan slowly rested both hands upon the ancient stone.

He didn’t force magic into it.

He simply listened.

The answer came naturally.

One word.

Quiet.

Certain.

“Bind.”

Golden-crimson light flowed across the fractured circle.

Unlike Hold…

Nothing stopped.

Instead…

Separated lines gently drew toward one another.

Not permanently.

Not completely.

Just enough.

Enough to remember their connection.

The fractured circle stabilized.

The crystal flared brighter than at any point since they had entered.

The entire chamber filled with warm light.

Every Foundation Thread strengthened.

Even the abandoned village far above seemed to breathe.

Master Rowan slowly lowered his head.

“The Second Law.”
