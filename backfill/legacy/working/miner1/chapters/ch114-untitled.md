The city was already changing.

As Aidan and the others emerged from beneath the ruins of the Ancient Gates, the streets of the French Quarter no longer felt merely tense.

They felt hunted.

Police sirens echoed in every direction.

Several buildings stood abandoned.

Storefront windows were shattered.

The scent of fresh blood drifted through the humid night.

Draco knelt beside one of the bodies left in a narrow alley.

There were no wounds beyond two clean punctures in the victim’s neck.

“No struggle,” he said quietly.

“No hesitation.”

“They trusted whoever did this.”

Rowan’s expression darkened.

“Compulsion.”

Aidan crouched beside the body.

He didn’t examine the victim.

He inhaled.

One slow breath.

“The scent is recent.”

“Less than an hour.”

Cassian looked at him.

“You can track them?”

“I already am.”

Without waiting for the others, Aidan began walking.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

They found the nest beneath an abandoned hotel overlooking the river.

The doors had already been forced open.

Inside…

The massacre had begun.

Newly turned vampires fed without restraint.

Some were little more than starving animals.

Others fought each other over corpses.

The moment they sensed Aidan…

Every head turned.

Silence filled the room.

One by one…

The feral vampires backed away.

None attacked.

None snarled.

They lowered their heads.

Recognition spread through the room.

Not of a king.

Of something higher in the food chain.

Cassian saw it.

“So they can feel what you are.”

Aidan didn’t answer.

He simply walked through them.

Not one dared touch him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A frightened teenage girl stumbled from behind a collapsed wall.

She couldn’t have been more than sixteen.

Fresh blood covered her mouth.

Her eyes overflowed with terror.

“Please…”

She looked at Aidan.

“I don’t want to hurt anyone.”

The hunger inside him answered before he could think.

Her heartbeat.

Her blood.

The scent filled the room.

For the first time since entering the hotel…

His control slipped.

One step.

Then another.

Cassian immediately moved between them.

“Aidan.”

Crimson-gold eyes met his.

Cold.

Predatory.

“Move.”

“No.”

“If you stop me…”

A faint smile touched Aidan’s lips.

“…I’ll move you.”

The room froze.

Every vampire watched.

Cassian slowly lowered his swords.

“If you’re going to kill someone…”

His voice shook despite his resolve.

“…then start with me.”

A long silence followed.

The predator stared at him.

Seconds stretched.

Then…

Aidan turned away.

“She’s not the target.”

He walked deeper into the hotel.

The teenage vampire collapsed to her knees, sobbing.

Cassian remained where he stood.

He had reached him.

Not emotionally.

Only enough to redirect the hunt.

It was the smallest victory imaginable.

But it was still a victory.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco watched Aidan disappear into the darkness.

“He didn’t spare her because he cared.”

Rowan nodded.

“I know.”

“He spared her because Cassian changed the calculation.”

Neither of them liked the answer.

Yet it proved one thing.

The monster could still be influenced.

Even if it no longer understood why.
