From respect.

People turned toward the riverbank.

An elderly woman struggled to lift a wagon trapped in thick mud after one of its wheels had broken.

Without hesitation…

No one waited to be asked.

Farmers.

Craftsmen.

Guards.

No ruler stands above the House.
