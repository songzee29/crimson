Morning sunlight broke across Blackwood Academy.

Yet the campus felt different.

Students spoke in quieter voices.

Teachers watched the surrounding forest more often than their lesson plans.

Everyone felt the mountain.

Even if they couldn’t explain why.

⸻

Inside the Headmaster’s office…

Aidan, Lyra, Cassian, Malik, Elias, Darius, Kael, Professor Helena, Master Rowan, and Headmaster Aldren stood around the ancient map.

No one treated this like another school expedition.

This was a Foundation mission.

Rowan rested both palms on the table.

“Listen carefully.”

He looked at each of them.

“The Second Foundation is not your enemy.”

“The thing feeding upon it is.”

Cassian folded his arms.

“So we destroy it.”

“No.”

Rowan answered immediately.

Everyone looked surprised.

“You’ve all become too comfortable looking for battles.”

He turned toward Aidan.

“What is your objective?”

Aidan looked at the map.

“Understand why it’s failing.”

“Exactly.”

“The Legacy already told you.”

“If you ignore the lesson…”

“…the Witch will stop teaching.”

⸻

The room became quiet.

The weight of the words settled over everyone.

Aidan finally understood.

Every previous quest had rewarded action.

These quests rewarded judgment.

⸻

Professor Helena opened an old leather case.

Inside rested several black-wood staffs carved with silver runes.

She handed one to each member of the group.

“These are Academy Focus Staves.”

“They aren’t weapons.”

“They help stabilize your magic if the local Foundation begins collapsing.”

Lyra carefully examined hers.

“It feels…”

“…alive.”

Professor Helena smiled.

“Good.”

“It should.”

⸻

Mr. Bennett entered carrying a weathered satchel.

He placed it before Aidan.

“Field journals.”

“Blank?”

Aidan asked.

“For now.”

Mr. Bennett nodded.

“The Founder believed every Keeper should leave records for the next one.”

Aidan slowly accepted the satchel.

Not another reward.

Another responsibility.

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared one final time before they departed.

The message faded.

Aidan looked toward the academy gates.

The old oak swayed gently in the morning breeze.

Its crimson leaves drifted around the group as they began walking.

Not toward another classroom.

Not toward another lesson.

Toward the first forgotten place where the Witch Arc’s final answers had begun waiting centuries before.

Far beneath Grey Hollow…

The unseen presence slowly smiled.

This time…

The Heir was coming to it.
