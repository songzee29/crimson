They entered Grey Hollow together.

No one separated.

No one spoke louder than necessary.

Every footstep echoed farther than it should have.

The village seemed to collect sound…

Then return it several seconds later.

Elias glanced over his shoulder.

“Did anyone…”

“…hear that?”

Darius nodded.

“Our footsteps.”

“They’re repeating.”

Professor Helena looked around the empty street.

“Residual magic.”

“The Foundation remembers movement.”

“It doesn’t know the village is empty.”

⸻

Lyra stopped before the first cottage.

The carved protection sigils surrounding its doorway had almost completely faded.

She carefully traced one with her fingertips.

“This was beautiful.”

“The craftsmanship…”

She looked toward Rowan.

“…I’ve never seen runes woven like this.”

Rowan stepped beside her.

“Because modern witches simplified them.”

He gently brushed dust away from another carving.

“The old Foundations believed every home deserved protection.”

“They didn’t separate ordinary life from magic.”

“They lived together.”

Aidan quietly watched.

Another lesson.

The Crimson Witch wasn’t merely battle.

It was civilization.

⸻

Malik’s voice interrupted the silence.

“Movement.”

Everyone turned.

Nothing stood in the street.

Yet loose leaves slowly drifted across the stone road.

Against the direction they should have moved.

Cassian instinctively stepped in front of Lyra.

Kael raised his staff.

Professor Helena simply watched.

“Don’t attack.”

The leaves circled once.

Twice.

Then settled around an old stone well.

Aidan walked toward it.

Foundation Awareness pulsed again.

Not danger.

Direction.

“This way.”

⸻

The ancient well reached deeper than anyone expected.

No water reflected below.

Only darkness.

Mr. Bennett carefully lowered a lantern.

Thirty feet.

Forty.

Fifty.

No bottom.

Malik frowned.

“That’s impossible.”

“It isn’t a well.”

Rowan answered.

“It’s ventilation.”

“For something beneath the village.”

Aidan looked toward the surrounding streets.

The magical threads converged here.

Every single one.

“The Second Foundation…”

“…is underneath us.”

Professor Helena nodded.

“I believe you’re right.”

“But not here.”

She tapped the stone rim.

“This is only where it breathes.”

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

GREY HOLLOW

Village Layout Understood.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message faded.

Aidan looked down into the endless darkness once more.

Then…

A faint sound rose from below.

Not a growl.

Not a scream.

Children…

Laughing.

Soft.

Happy.

For only a heartbeat.

Then silence.

Every face around the well changed.

Mr. Bennett slowly closed his eyes.

“They’re memories.”

“The Foundation…”

“…is remembering the people it once protected.”

Even Rowan lowered his head.

“No.”

He whispered.

“It isn’t remembering.”

“It is trying…”

“…not to forget.”

No one spoke after that.

Together…

They stood around the silent well.

Not facing an enemy.

Facing the slow death of something that had once devoted its existence to protecting others.

And beneath their feet…

The Second Foundation waited.

Not for warriors.

For Keepers.
