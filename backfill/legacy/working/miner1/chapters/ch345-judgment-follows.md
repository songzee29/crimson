The academy gates closed behind them.

The sound echoed across the entire campus.

Students watched from classroom windows.

Teachers quietly gathered along the stone walkways.

No panic.

Only unease.

Headmaster Aldren led the expedition directly toward the central courtyard.

The old oak stood waiting.

Its crimson leaves no longer drifted peacefully.

Every branch strained against an unseen wind.

Aidan stopped beneath it.

The ache behind his eyes returned instantly.

Stronger.

The whisper no longer sounded distant.

It surrounded him.

Keeper…

Heir…

Witch…

One voice became many.

Hundreds.

Thousands.

Not screaming.

Calling.

The old oak shuddered violently.

Every protective rune carved into its trunk ignited.

Then…

One went dark.

A second later…

Another.

Professor Helena immediately raised her staff.

“The wards are failing!”

Faculty witches rushed into position around the courtyard.

Golden barriers spread between the buildings.

Students were calmly ushered indoors.

No chaos.

Everyone had rehearsed for disasters.

Just not this one.

Master Rowan stepped beside Aidan.

“Do not fight it.”

Aidan’s eyes remained fixed on the tree.

“It wants me.”

“Yes.”

“It followed me.”

“Yes.”

“Why?”

Rowan looked toward the fading runes.

“Because…”

“…you’ve become the only thing standing between it and everyone else.”

⸻

The whisper became clearer.

Not words.

Intent.

Hatred.

Loneliness.

Grief.

They crashed against Aidan’s mind like waves.

His knees nearly buckled.

Cassian caught him again.

“I’m here.”

Malik stepped closer.

“So am I.”

Elias planted his staff into the earth.

“You don’t carry this alone.”

Lyra reached for Aidan’s hand.

He gripped it tightly.

Immediately…

Circle Weaving responded.

Thin crimson-gold threads appeared between them.

Not because Aidan summoned them.

Because they trusted one another.

The pressure eased.

Only slightly.

Enough.

Master Rowan nodded.

“Remember this feeling.”

Aidan looked at him.

“Why?”

“Because when the Curse finally breaks…”

He paused.

“…it will try to convince you that you stand alone.”

He looked at each of the others.

“It will lie.”

The message dissolved.

The whisper vanished with it.

For one brief moment…

Complete silence returned to Blackwood Academy.

Then…

A long crack split the earth beneath the old oak.

Not deep.

Not wide.

Just enough for a single thread of crimson-black light to escape into the evening air.

Every witch in the courtyard felt it.

No one mistook it for ordinary magic.

The Curse was no longer approaching.

It had arrived.
