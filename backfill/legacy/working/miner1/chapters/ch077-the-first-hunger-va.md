The darkness shattered.

Aidan’s eyes opened.

Crimson.

Brilliant.

Ancient.

His body arched violently from the stone bier.

Air rushed into lungs that no longer needed it.

Every muscle tightened at once.

His broken ribs settled into place with sickening cracks.

Torn muscle pulled itself together.

His hearing exploded outward.

Hundreds of heartbeats.

Dozens of breaths.

Blood.

Everywhere.

The scent struck him like a physical blow.

His throat burned.

No—

Not burned.

Hungered.

The feeling was unlike anything he had ever known.

It demanded.

Consumed.

Ruled.

He stood in a single motion.

Too fast.

The stone beneath his feet fractured.

Cassian staggered backward.

“Aidan…”

Those crimson eyes found him.

Recognition came instantly.

Not friendship.

Not relief.

Memory.

Cassian.

The one who had ended him.

Something cold settled where grief should have been.

Aidan moved.

Cassian barely raised his sword before Aidan reached him.

The strike threw Cassian across the Great Hall.

Stone pillars cracked as he crashed through them.

The doors burst open.

Rowan, Lyra, Malik, Elias, and Helena turned at the sound.

They froze.

The figure standing in the darkness looked like Aidan.

Until he smiled.

It held no warmth.

No comfort.

Only hunger.

“Aidan!”

Lyra took one step forward.

He never looked at her.

His gaze remained fixed on Cassian.

Cassian forced himself upright.

Blood ran from a cut across his forehead.

One drop slid down his cheek.

Aidan’s pupils narrowed.

The scent reached him.

Everything else disappeared.

He crossed half the hall before Cassian could recover.

One hand closed around Cassian’s throat.

The stone floor shattered beneath them.

Cassian struggled for air.

Aidan leaned closer.

His lips parted.

Fangs descended.

Long.

White.

Perfect.

The hunger screamed.

Drink.

Finish it.

Now.

Then—

Another scent.

Fresh.

Closer.

Different.

Not Cassian.

Not anyone he knew.

A pulse.

Fast.

Terrified.

Somewhere beyond the Great Hall.

Outside.

An injured academy porter had fallen while running toward the hall, cutting his arm badly against broken stone.

The blood reached Aidan before the sound of the man’s heartbeat did.

The world stopped.

Cassian vanished from his thoughts.

The hunger chose.

Aidan released him instantly.

Before anyone could react—

He was gone.

The Great Hall doors exploded from their hinges as he passed through them.

Wind followed in his wake.

Silence lingered for only a heartbeat.

Then Helena whispered,

“…he smelled blood.”

No one answered.

Because they all knew.

Cassian struggled to his feet, staring toward the ruined doorway.

The fury in Aidan’s eyes had vanished the instant another source of blood appeared.

It hadn’t been mercy.

It had been something worse.

Something stronger than revenge.

Outside…

A scream echoed through the academy grounds.

Rowan’s expression hardened.

“Move!”

The entire inner circle ran.

Not to save Aidan.

To reach whoever he had found first.
