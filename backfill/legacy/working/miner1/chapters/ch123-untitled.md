No one spoke.

Lucien’s ashes drifted across the black stone before disappearing into ancient cracks beneath the cavern floor.

The silence felt heavier than the battle.

Aidan turned away first.

Lucien was dead.

The hunt was over.

There was nothing left to do.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Above the cavern…

The remaining elite vampires felt it immediately.

Their commander was gone.

Some dropped their weapons.

Others fled into the night.

A handful continued fighting.

Not for victory.

For revenge.

Cassian disarmed one with a single strike.

“Enough.”

The vampire froze.

Confusion crossed his face.

“The Commander…”

Cassian pointed toward the shattered floor.

“…is dead.”

The words spread faster than any blade.

Within moments…

Every surviving member of Lucien’s organization disappeared into the streets of New Orleans.

The command structure that had ruled from the shadows for decades collapsed in a single night.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco descended into the cavern with the others.

No one rushed toward Aidan.

No one embraced him.

The distance between them felt greater than the depth of the collapse.

Aidan walked past them.

His shoulder brushed Cassian’s.

He didn’t acknowledge anyone.

Not because he hated them.

Because, to him, there was nothing to acknowledge.

The mission had ended.

Everything else was irrelevant.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Rowan looked around the cavern.

Ancient carvings covered the walls.

Most had already crumbled.

Only one inscription remained intact.

Draco brushed centuries of dust away.

“It isn’t another prophecy.”

“What is it?” Malik asked.

Draco read the final surviving line aloud.

“The Heir shall inherit the Gates.

The Heir shall choose the Blood.

The final inheritance shall never be given.

It shall be taken.”

The stone cracked.

The inscription collapsed into dust.

No copies remained.

The Ancient Gates had surrendered everything they were meant to leave behind.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Outside…

Dawn crept over New Orleans.

For the first time in years…

The city no longer belonged to Lucien Vale.

Police entered abandoned safe houses.

Compelled victims slowly awakened.

Hidden vampire nests stood empty.

The war for the city had ended.

But peace did not arrive with it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Mississippi flowed quietly beneath the morning light.

As Aidan crossed the old bridge leading away from the French Quarter…

The current briefly separated around the reflection beneath his feet before joining together downstream.

The river continued as though nothing had happened.

No one noticed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian finally spoke.

“Where are you going?”

Aidan never slowed.

“To the next hunt.”

“There isn’t one.”

“There will be.”

“You don’t have to leave today.”

Aidan stopped.

For a heartbeat…

Hope returned to Cassian’s face.

Then Aidan answered.

“Standing still accomplishes nothing.”

He walked away.

Cassian made no attempt to follow.

For the first time…

He let him go.
