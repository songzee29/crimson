Every head turned.

The Hall of Laws stood empty.

The corridor beyond remained silent.

Nothing approached.

Nothing attacked.

Yet the pressure filled every corner of the Foundation.

Not crushing.

Watching.

The warmth disappeared completely.

One lantern after another extinguished itself.

Until only the Foundation crystal illuminated the chamber.

Mr. Bennett whispered,

“It crossed.”

Professor Helena’s expression tightened.

“No…”

“It found another way.”

⸻

The ancient voice spoke one last time.

Barely audible.

Keepers…

Do not fight…

The crystal flickered.

Its hunger…

Is not for stone…

The final words echoed through the chamber.

It chooses hearts.

The light vanished.

The Foundation Crystal became perfectly still.

Its purpose fulfilled.

⸻

A shadow flowed across the floor.

Not from a creature.

Not from darkness.

From absence.

Where the golden Foundation Threads had once shone brightest…

One slowly dimmed.

Then another.

Not severed.

Touched.

Aidan instinctively raised his hand.

“Hold.”

The Law spread through the nearest thread.

Its fading stopped immediately.

But only that one.

The others continued dimming.

His jaw tightened.

Hold could preserve.

It couldn’t protect all of them alone.

Lyra immediately understood.

“Circle!”

Everyone resumed their places without waiting for orders.

Circle Weaving ignited again.

Golden light surged through the remaining threads.

The fading slowed.

Cassian clenched his staff.

“It’s testing us.”

Rowan answered quietly,

“No.”

“It is learning.”

⸻

The shadow withdrew.

Not defeated.

Satisfied.

Like a predator confirming the strength of a wall before searching for its weakest stone.

Foundation Awareness expanded once more.

Aidan followed the retreating current.

His heart sank.

“It isn’t trying to destroy the Foundation anymore.”

Professor Helena looked at him.

“What is it doing?”

Aidan stared toward the distant ceiling, where Grey Hollow rested above them.

“It’s leaving.”

Silence.

Master Rowan closed his eyes.

“Of course.”

Mr. Bennett slowly removed his hat.

“The Foundations were never its destination.”

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOUNDATION MISSION

Second Foundation Secured.

Status:

Stable.

The external pressure has withdrawn.

Reason:

The Witch Curse has chosen its next path.

New Objective:

Return to Blackwood Academy.

Protect the living.

The Witch Arc has entered its Final Judgment.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crimson window dissolved.

No one celebrated.

No one smiled.

They had saved Grey Hollow.

But in doing so…

They had forced the Witch Curse to abandon the dying Foundation.

Now it was free to pursue what it had wanted from the beginning.

The Heir.

Aidan looked once toward the silent Foundation Crystal.

Then toward the staircase leading back to the surface.

“The Foundation kept its promise.”

His voice was calm.

“We keep ours.”

Without another word…

The Keepers began the ascent.

And behind them, for the first time in centuries, the Second Foundation remained standing—not because its burden had disappeared, but because someone new had accepted the duty to carry it forward.
