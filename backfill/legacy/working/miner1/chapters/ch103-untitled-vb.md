The rain never stopped.

It drummed against the pavement between the two groups.

No one lowered their guard.

Cassian took half a step forward.

“So you’ve been following us.”

Draco’s expression remained calm.

“I’ve been following the trail.”

“And now you found it,” Malik said.

“I found him.”

His eyes never left Aidan.

The silence stretched.

Rowan studied the stranger carefully.

Unlike the creatures they hunted…

This man breathed normally.

His heartbeat was steady.

His posture carried no unnecessary tension.

He looked like someone who had survived impossible battles for so long that danger had become ordinary.

“You’re not surprised by what you see,” Rowan observed.

“No.”

“You knew about vampires?”

“I’ve killed them.”

“Witches?”

“I’ve buried them.”

“Wolves?”

“I’ve hunted them.”

Cassian frowned.

“You expect us to believe that?”

“I don’t care what you believe.”

His answer carried no arrogance.

Only certainty.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan continued watching him.

“You know my name.”

“Everyone worth hunting knows your name.”

Cassian immediately drew one of his swords.

“You should rethink that sentence.”

Draco ignored him.

“The Crimson Heir.”

“The First Crimson Witch.”

“The First Crimson Vampire.”

“The Butcher of the Ancient Gates.”

“The Hunter who never stops.”

“You’ve earned many titles.”

Aidan’s face never changed.

“I don’t collect titles.”

“I know.”

“You collect victories.”

The words lingered in the air.

Lyra noticed something unsettling.

Every title Draco had spoken…

None had been public.

Not one.

Her eyes narrowed.

“How do you know about the Ancient Gates?”

Draco finally looked toward her.

“I know enough.”

“Answer the question.”

“I wasn’t speaking to you.”

Cassian moved.

His blade stopped less than an inch from Draco’s throat.

“You are now.”

No one saw Draco move.

One moment…

A sword rested against his neck.

The next…

Cassian’s weapon pointed harmlessly at empty air.

Draco stood three meters away.

His coat settled naturally as though he had never moved.

Cassian’s eyes narrowed.

“I hate people who do that.”

“So did the last man who tried to kill me.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan raised one hand.

Cassian immediately lowered his weapon.

No words were needed.

That alone answered one of Draco’s silent questions.

They trusted Aidan’s judgment absolutely.

“You’re fast,” Aidan said.

“I’m alive.”

“That’s not the same thing.”

“No.”

“It isn’t.”

For the first time…

A faint smile crossed Draco’s face.

“You really have changed.”

Aidan’s eyes sharpened.

“We’ve met.”

“No.”

“But I’ve watched.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON LEGACY

Historical Observation

External Variable Detected

Archive Updated

Observation Continues

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification vanished almost instantly.

Aidan frowned.

Again.

The Legacy refused to explain itself.

It simply recorded.

Watched.

Waited.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco reached into his coat.

Every weapon in the group shifted toward him.

He stopped immediately.

“Relax.”

“I’ve survived this long by assuming nobody relaxes.”

Slowly…

He removed a weathered leather journal.

Its cover was scarred.

Burned.

Nearly black with age.

He tossed it toward Aidan.

Aidan caught it effortlessly.

The leather felt strangely cold.

Older than it should have been.

He opened the first page.

Most of the writing had faded.

Only one sentence remained perfectly clear.

They are not fleeing from us.

They are fleeing from what sleeps beneath the earth.

Aidan turned another page.

Blank.

Another.

Blank.

Every remaining page…

Empty.

Malik looked over his shoulder.

“That’s it?”

Draco nodded.

“The rest disappeared.”

“How?”

“I don’t know.”

“You expect us to believe that?”

“I stopped caring what people believe a long time ago.”

Rowan studied the journal.

“It feels enchanted.”

“It is.”

“By who?”

“I’ve spent thirty-two years trying to answer that.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lightning split the sky.

For an instant…

Every shadow disappeared.

Then darkness returned.

Aidan closed the journal.

“You crossed half the world to give me a book with one sentence.”

“No.”

Draco looked toward the river.

“I crossed half the world because something changed.”

His eyes returned to Aidan.

“For years…”

“The creatures ran.”

“They hid.”

“They avoided cities.”

“They avoided you.”

He paused.

“They’ve stopped running.”

Nobody spoke.

“The ones you’ve been killing…”

“They’re no longer escaping.”

“They’re gathering.”

The words settled heavily over the group.

Aidan remained perfectly still.

“Where?”

Draco answered without hesitation.

“Everywhere.”

A distant explosion echoed somewhere across New Orleans.

Not close.

Not far.

A deep crimson glow briefly illuminated the clouds before fading into the storm.

Every head turned toward the horizon.

The cleanup crews froze.

Police sirens suddenly changed direction.

Then—

A sharp chime echoed inside Aidan’s mind.

Not from the Crimson System.

Not from the Crimson Legacy.

Different.

Older.

A single line appeared before him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

UNKNOWN SOURCE

The Hunt Has Begun.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message vanished before he could react.

Aidan’s crimson-gold eyes fixed on the distant red glow.

Without another word…

He started walking toward it.

The others followed.

Even Draco.

Whatever had happened across the city…

None of them believed it was a coincidence.

And for the first time in years…

Aidan had the unmistakable feeling that he was no longer the one doing the hunting.
