For one terrible heartbeat…

No one moved.

Dust rolled through the gorge, swallowing everything beyond the collapsed mountainside.

Then Lyra screamed.

“Aidan!”

She lunged toward the wall of broken stone.

Cassian caught her before loose rock buried them both.

“It’s still falling!”

“Let me go!”

She fought against him with every ounce of strength she had left.

“He’ll be trapped!”

“I know!”

His voice cracked.

“I know.”

…

Rowan reached the collapse seconds later.

His eyes swept across the shattered cliff.

No opening.

No path through.

Only thousands of tons of broken mountain.

“Dig.”

The order came without hesitation.

Nobody questioned whether it was possible.

Malik drove his shovel into the rubble.

Students who had escaped the gorge returned without being asked.

Parents handed frightened children to strangers and joined the rescue.

The people Aidan had saved refused to leave him behind.

…

Far beneath the collapse…

Aidan slowly opened one eye.

Darkness.

Again.

His first thought wasn’t fear.

It was disappointment.

Not again.

He tried to breathe.

Something heavy pressed across his legs.

His left shoulder screamed.

His ribs felt as though they had finally shattered completely.

He closed his eyes.

The second heartbeat continued beneath his own.

Steady.

Patient.

Waiting.

He listened.

Nothing.

Not yet.

Then…

Very faintly…

Stone.

Moving.

They were digging.

Of course they were.

He leaned his head against cold rock.

“They never quit…”

The words barely escaped his lips.

…

Hours passed.

He drifted in and out of consciousness.

Sometimes he heard Lyra shouting.

Sometimes Cassian.

Sometimes only the mountain settling around him.

More than once he reached for his staff.

His fingers no longer had the strength to close around it.

Blood had soaked through every remaining bandage.

His breathing became shallower.

Not because he wanted to stop.

Because his body had less and less left to give.

…

Above…

Helena grabbed Rowan’s arm.

“He can’t survive another full collapse.”

Rowan never stopped clearing stone.

“Then we reach him first.”

“We’ve been digging for six hours.”

“Then we dig seven.”

Cassian said nothing.

He simply lifted another slab with his good arm until the muscles trembled violently.

Malik’s damaged leg gave out beneath him.

He stood again.

Lyra’s hands bled openly.

She never noticed.

No one left.

Not one.

…

Late into the night…

A faint knock echoed from somewhere beneath the mountain.

Everyone froze.

Cassian dropped to one knee.

“Aidan!”

Silence.

Then…

Another weak knock.

Further away than before.

The mountain had shifted.

“He moved,” Elias whispered.

“No.”

Rowan looked toward the unstable rock overhead.

“The mountain moved.”

Hope flickered.

Only for an instant.

The ceiling groaned.

A fresh shower of stone crashed into the rescue tunnel.

Everyone was forced backward.

Another path sealed itself.

Another hour was lost.

Cassian slammed his fist into the rubble.

For the first time in years…

He looked utterly helpless.
