The corridor ended far sooner than anyone expected.

There were no branching passages.

No hidden chambers.

No waiting army.

Only one destination.

An immense circular hall carved from black stone.

At its center stood a solitary pedestal.

Upon it rested a crimson key fashioned from crystal unlike any material Aidan had ever seen.

The moment he stepped into the chamber—

The second heartbeat struck.

The crystal key answered.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Ancient Gates Inheritance

Final Heir Authentication

Status:

In Progress…

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification disappeared.

No reward.

No explanation.

Only another step forward.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The armored guardian stopped at the entrance.

It did not follow.

Its duty ended here.

Draco slowly exhaled.

“So this is why the Guardians existed.”

Rowan looked around the chamber.

“They weren’t protecting the key.”

“They were judging the one who reached it.”

Aidan walked toward the pedestal.

No hesitation.

No excitement.

Only purpose.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Halfway across the chamber—

His hunger exploded.

It came without warning.

His throat burned.

Every heartbeat in the room became deafening.

Cassian.

Lyra.

Malik.

Elias.

Rowan.

Draco.

Six living heartbeats.

Six sources of warm blood.

The predator awakened instantly.

Aidan stopped walking.

His breathing slowed.

Not to calm himself.

To calculate.

Cassian recognized the look immediately.

“Aidan.”

No response.

Lyra quietly moved between Rowan and Aidan without making it obvious.

Malik lowered his hands.

No spell.

Only caution.

The room had become more dangerous than any battlefield.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

“I know that look.”

Cassian’s voice remained steady.

“You don’t have to fight this alone.”

Silence.

“We’ve survived worse together.”

Aidan finally spoke.

“You should all leave.”

“No.”

“You are impairing my judgment.”

Cassian took another step.

“Then let us help.”

“I didn’t ask for help.”

“You never do.”

For the briefest instant…

Aidan’s eyes rested on Cassian.

Something almost familiar surfaced.

Gone before anyone could reach it.

He looked away.

“I won’t repeat myself.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco quietly stepped beside Rowan.

“He isn’t threatening us.”

“No.”

“He’s calculating the probability that he loses control.”

Rowan’s grip tightened around his staff.

“And?”

“He doesn’t like uncertainty.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra took one careful step closer.

Not as a warrior.

As the woman who had spent years fighting beside him.

“You once told me strength wasn’t carrying everything alone.”

Aidan didn’t look at her.

“That man no longer exists.”

The words landed harder than any wound.

Lyra closed her eyes.

Only for a moment.

Then she stepped back.

Not because she believed him.

Because pushing further now would only make the predator stronger.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Far above…

Rainwater flowed through ancient streets into the Mississippi River.

As the current passed beneath the forgotten foundations of the Ancient Gates…

It divided silently around the stone directly above Aidan’s path before joining together once more.

No one saw it.

The river continued flowing toward the sea.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The hunger gradually stabilized.

Not because it faded.

Because Aidan buried it beneath absolute control.

Without another word, he reached the pedestal.

The instant his fingers touched the crimson key—

The entire Ancient Gates shook.

Ancient runes blazed across the floor.

The key dissolved into crimson light.

It flowed into Aidan’s hand.

Not as power.

As recognition.

Final Seal Released

The Ancient Gates had nothing left to test.

Deep beneath the chamber…

Something older than the Guardians unlocked.

Not awakening.

Opening.

The inheritance had never been the end of the Ancient Gates.

It had been the key to releasing whatever waited beyond the Final Seal.
