The map led them behind the Keeper’s cottage.

At first…

Nothing appeared unusual.

Only a neglected herb garden enclosed by a low stone wall.

Dead vines wrapped around weathered wooden stakes.

Medicinal herbs had long since withered away.

Lyra slowly walked among the empty beds.

She knelt beside one.

“This wasn’t abandoned.”

She brushed loose earth aside.

“It was harvested.”

Mr. Bennett nodded.

“Everything useful was taken.”

“The Keeper made sure nothing was wasted.”

Cassian looked toward the surrounding hills.

“They planned to leave.”

“They planned to survive.”

Rowan quietly answered,

“They planned to return.”

Aidan continued studying the ground.

Foundation Awareness pulled him toward the oldest tree in the garden.

Unlike the others…

Its roots grew in perfect circles.

He rested his hand against the bark.

The magical current immediately strengthened.

“There.”

Professor Helena joined him.

The tree wasn’t merely growing.

It had been shaped.

Ancient Witch runes spiraled naturally through the trunk itself.

“Living magic.”

She smiled faintly.

“I’ve only read about this.”

⸻

Rowan stepped forward.

“Keepers never hid important things beneath stone.”

“They hid them beneath life.”

Aidan understood.

He crouched beside one exposed root.

It had split apart centuries ago, revealing a narrow groove carved beneath it.

A single Witch symbol.

Not for opening.

For permission.

He looked toward Rowan.

“I don’t think it wants power.”

“What does it want?”

Aidan smiled.

“A Keeper.”

He placed his palm over the symbol.

No incantation.

No surge of magic.

Only a calm voice.

“We’re here to help.”

Nothing happened.

Then…

The tree slowly shuddered.

Its roots began withdrawing from the earth.

Stone shifted beneath the garden.

A circular stairway descended into darkness.

Warm air drifted upward.

Unlike the cold well.

This carried the scent of old cedar…

Burning candles…

And faint traces of lavender.

Lyra’s eyes widened.

“It still feels…”

“…lived in.”

Professor Helena lowered her staff.

“The Foundation accepted your answer.”

“No spell.”

“No Law.”

“Only intent.”

Golden-crimson threads immediately appeared within Aidan’s vision.

Unlike Foundation Awareness…

These were deliberate.

One brilliant thread wound gently down the ancient staircase.

Another branched into sealed chambers hidden beneath Grey Hollow.

Several weaker threads had already faded into darkness.

Aidan took a slow breath.

“This way.”

Everyone instinctively fell in behind him.

Not because Rowan ordered it.

Not because Helena suggested it.

Because for the first time…

The Keeper’s Path itself was following the Heir.

And deep below…

The Second Foundation stirred.

Not in fear.

Not in anger.

But with the quiet hope that, after generations of waiting…

Someone had finally come who understood the difference between possessing power…

And protecting it.
