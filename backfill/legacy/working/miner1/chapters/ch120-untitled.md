The cathedral exploded into motion.

Lucien vanished from the altar.

Aidan met him in the center aisle.

Their collision shattered rows of wooden pews.

Neither gave ground.

Lucien smiled as he struck again.

“Faster.”

Aidan answered with a brutal elbow that fractured Lucien’s jaw.

The vampire healed before he landed.

“Better.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Around them, the Inner Circle fought elite vampires emerging from hidden passages beneath the cathedral.

Unlike before…

These vampires weren’t trying to kill everyone.

They isolated them.

Cassian found himself separated from Aidan.

Lyra was forced toward the opposite side of the sanctuary.

Malik and Rowan fought together while protecting civilians hiding in the lower chapel.

The enemy had studied them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lucien drove Aidan through a stone column.

“You know why I wanted to meet you?”

Aidan rose from the rubble.

“No.”

“Because every report said the Crimson Heir still loved people.”

Lucien struck again.

“But that’s gone now.”

Another exchange.

Stone burst apart.

“You’ve finally become dangerous.”

Aidan caught Lucien’s wrist.

“You mistake control for agreement.”

With a violent twist, he snapped the arm.

Lucien only smiled.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Across the sanctuary, Elias intercepted a vampire rushing toward Aidan’s blind side.

The strike meant for Aidan pierced Elias’s shoulder instead.

Blood splashed across the floor.

The scent reached Aidan instantly.

Everything stopped.

His eyes shifted.

Not toward Lucien.

Toward Elias.

The hunger surged harder than ever before.

Lucien saw it.

“There it is.”

“The real battle.”

He stepped back instead of attacking.

Watching.

Waiting.

Testing.

Cassian shouted from across the cathedral.

“Aidan!”

Elias, pale but still standing, met Aidan’s gaze.

He didn’t plead.

He didn’t beg.

He simply said, “Finish the fight.”

For a fraction of a heartbeat…

The predator hesitated.

Then Aidan turned away from Elias and lunged back at Lucien with overwhelming force.

The cathedral shook under the impact.

Cassian stared in disbelief.

He hadn’t chosen Elias.

He hadn’t chosen the hunger.

He had chosen the mission.

It wasn’t the decision the old Aidan would have made.

But it wasn’t the one the monster should have made either.

For the first time in a long time…

None of them knew what that meant.
