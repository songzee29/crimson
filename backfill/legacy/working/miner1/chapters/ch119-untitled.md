The vampires did not flee.

They guided him.

Aidan crossed the rooftops overlooking the Mississippi with relentless speed.

The Inner Circle stayed close behind.

None of them questioned where he was going.

They knew.

The enemy wanted to be found.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

“They’re controlling the pace,” Draco said as he landed beside Rowan.

“They’ve been leaving just enough evidence for us to follow.”

“You’ve seen this before.”

Draco nodded.

“Thirty-two years ago.”

Cassian glanced toward him.

“You were here.”

“I arrived too late.”

Draco’s voice remained calm, but something older lingered beneath it.

“I found an entire district emptied overnight.”

“No bodies.”

“No survivors.”

“Only blood.”

“And one message.”

“What did it say?” Elias asked.

Draco looked toward Aidan.

“‘The Heir never came.’”

Silence settled over the group.

“You’ve been searching for him ever since,” Rowan realized.

Draco nodded once.

“I wasn’t looking for a weapon.”

“I was looking for the only person who could finish what started here.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ahead, Aidan stopped.

Below them stood an abandoned cathedral overlooking the river.

Its stained-glass windows had long since shattered.

Every entrance stood open.

No guards.

No traps.

Only invitation.

“They want us inside,” Malik said.

Aidan stepped forward.

“Then we stop making them wait.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

As they crossed the cracked courtyard, water from the Mississippi washed gently against the old stone embankment below.

For only a heartbeat…

The current curved around the cathedral’s reflection before continuing downstream.

No one noticed.

The doors opened on their own.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Inside…

Hundreds of candles burned.

No vampires waited.

Only one man.

He stood before the old altar dressed in a black suit untouched by dust.

His hair had turned silver with age.

His crimson eyes never left Aidan.

“So…”

“The Crimson Heir finally arrives.”

Aidan studied him.

“You’ve been watching me.”

“For years.”

“You’ve killed thousands.”

The man smiled faintly.

“I’ve ruled thousands.”

“My name is Lucien Vale.”

“I built this city’s hidden covens while the Ancient Gates slept.”

He spread his hands.

“I have no interest in your throne.”

“I have interest in what you became.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian stepped forward.

“You murdered innocent people.”

Lucien didn’t even look at him.

“I sacrificed pieces.”

“I protect the whole.”

Aidan answered before anyone else could.

“Logical.”

The Inner Circle looked toward him.

Lucien smiled wider.

“You understand.”

“No.”

Aidan’s voice remained flat.

“I understand how you think.”

“And?”

“I’ve already calculated your death.”

For the first time…

Lucien laughed.

“Excellent.”

“I was hoping the monster was real.”
