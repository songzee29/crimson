The infirmary disappeared.

Not physically.

Everything beyond the Crimson System seemed to fade into silence.

Aidan stared at the glowing words suspended before him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

SURVIVAL FOUNDATION ACCEPTED

Foundational Adaptations Granted

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification remained.

Longer than any notification Aidan had ever received.

Then…

One by one…

New lines appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Adaptations Accepted

• Enhanced Battle Instinct

• Crimson Vitality I

• Night Perception

• Predator’s Focus

• Enhanced Hearing

• Enhanced Smell

• Crimson Endurance

• Iron Stomach

• Pain Resistance

• Accelerated Reflexes

• Survival Adaptation I

• Blood Sense

• Threat Assessment

• Silent Movement

• Rapid Recovery I

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan read every line twice.

None of them explained themselves.

None of them felt like magic.

They simply…

Made sense.

The final notification appeared beneath them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Notice

These adaptations strengthen the body.

Their true purpose remains unknown.

Future adaptations will be earned through survival.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The screen dissolved.

No surge of power swept through him.

No dramatic transformation shook the room.

Only silence.

…

Aidan slowly stood.

His ribs still hurt.

His shoulder still bled beneath fresh bandages.

The scars across his back still pulled painfully whenever he moved.

Nothing had been erased.

Nothing had been healed.

Yet…

Something felt subtly different.

He crossed the infirmary floor.

Without thinking.

His footsteps made almost no sound.

He stopped.

Looked down.

Then deliberately walked again.

Still…

Silent.

He frowned.

“I wasn’t trying…”

A cool breeze drifted through the open window.

It carried dozens of familiar scents.

Fresh rain.

Healing herbs.

Old books from the nearby archive.

Warm bread from the kitchens.

Lamp oil.

Stone dust.

And…

Blood.

Very faint.

Someone had scraped a knuckle outside.

He turned toward the window before he consciously realized why.

His eyes narrowed.

No.

Don’t.

He closed the window.

The scent weakened.

His heartbeat gradually settled.

…

The academy had long since fallen asleep.

Unable to rest, Aidan left the infirmary.

The corridors stretched ahead beneath dim lantern light.

Normally the shadows gathered between pillars would have been nearly black.

Tonight…

He saw them.

Not perfectly.

Not as though it were day.

But enough.

The edges of broken stone.

The worn grain of ancient wood.

The faint outline of old carvings hidden in darkness.

He slowed.

“How…”

No answer came.

…

Outside…

Night blanketed Blackwood Academy.

Clouds covered the moon.

Students on late patrol carried lanterns across the walls.

Before Aidan even reached the courtyard…

He heard them.

Two guards talking quietly atop the eastern tower.

Another pair crossing the western wall.

Someone yawning near the kitchens.

The scrape of armor.

Boots against stone.

A mouse rustling beneath fallen leaves.

Every sound arrived naturally.

Not overwhelming.

Simply…

Present.

He instinctively separated each one without effort.

His brow furrowed.

“I’ve never heard this far before.”

…

The old oak stood motionless beneath the night sky.

Aidan rested one hand against its scarred trunk.

The tree felt alive.

Its slow pulse traveled through ancient roots buried deep beneath Blackwood.

Then…

His body tensed.

Not fear.

Instinct.

His eyes shifted toward the eastern cliff.

Nothing moved.

Nothing visible.

Still…

He remained staring.

A heartbeat later…

A section of loose rock collapsed exactly where he’d been looking.

The sound echoed across the valley.

He hadn’t predicted it.

He had reacted to it…

Before it happened.

“Aidan.”

Rowan’s voice drifted from behind him.

The old master approached quietly.

“You heard it.”

Aidan looked over.

“Heard what?”

“The cliff.”

“You did too?”

Rowan shook his head.

“I heard it after it fell.”

Silence settled between them.

Finally Rowan asked,

“How did you know?”

Aidan searched for an answer.

“I don’t.”

It was the truth.

“I just…”

He looked back toward the broken cliff.

“…felt something was wrong.”

Rowan studied him for a long moment.

“Then trust it.”

Aidan blinked.

“Without understanding it?”

Rowan smiled faintly.

“You trusted your first spell before you understood it.”

He placed a hand on Aidan’s shoulder.

“Some lessons begin in the body long before they reach the mind.”

The old master walked away.

Leaving Aidan alone beneath the oak.

Far below…

Deep beneath Blackwood Academy…

Seven distant heartbeats echoed through the mountain.

Slow.

Heavy.

Climbing.

Aidan heard every one of them.

And without realizing it…

He had already begun counting the distance between them and everyone he wanted to protect.
