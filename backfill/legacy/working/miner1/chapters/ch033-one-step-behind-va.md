The northern watchtower stood intact.

That was the first thing that felt wrong.

No smoke.

No blood.

No signs of battle.

Only silence.

Aidan slowly climbed the wooden stairs.

Each step sent pain through his ribs.

The wound across his side pulled against fresh stitches.

He ignored both.

At the top…

The guards remained alive.

Sleeping.

Every one of them.

Helena examined the nearest.

“They aren’t injured.”

“What happened?” Malik asked.

Aidan knelt.

He smelled bitter herbs.

Not enough to notice before.

Enough now.

“They were drugged.”

Elias looked surprised.

“The hunters didn’t kill them?”

“No.”

Rowan’s expression hardened.

“They wanted the tower.”

The realization came too late.

Aidan’s hearing caught distant movement.

South.

Fast.

Not one runner.

Many.

He turned sharply.

“They’re attacking Blackwood.”

Everyone sprinted.

The hunters had never intended to take the tower.

Only to pull the academy’s strongest defenders away.

By the time Blackwood came into view…

Smoke already rose above the eastern training grounds.

Students fought beside instructors.

Barrier circles flickered.

The hunters were already retreating.

Again.

Another lesson learned.

Another wound left behind.

They had struck the academy…

While its protectors chased shadows.

…

The eastern training grounds lay in ruins.

No students had died.

But three instructors had fallen protecting them.

Helena silently knelt beside the wounded.

Her hands never stopped moving.

Malik carried broken stone from collapsed buildings despite his worsening limp.

Cassian ignored the pain in his damaged arm while helping evacuate apprentices.

Lyra organized frightened first-years into healing circles.

Everyone kept moving.

Only Aidan stood still.

His eyes drifted across the battlefield.

The hunters hadn’t stayed long enough to win.

Only long enough to prove they could reach Blackwood whenever they chose.

His fists slowly tightened.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

The scent of blood surrounded him.

Not one source.

Dozens.

He closed his eyes.

Focused on breathing.

One breath.

Then another.

The hunger eased.

Barely.

Lyra quietly stepped beside him.

“You found them too late.”

He nodded.

“They’re always one step ahead.”

She looked toward the broken training grounds.

“No.”

Her voice remained gentle.

“They’re making you believe that.”

He wanted to believe her.

He truly did.

But somewhere deep inside…

The hunter’s whisper returned.

You have your traitor to thank for this.

He immediately pushed the thought away.

Not Lyra.

Not Rowan.

Not Cassian.

Not Malik.

Not Elias.

Never.

Still…

He found himself looking over the ruined academy with different eyes.

Not searching for enemies.

Searching for answers.

And while Blackwood rebuilt once more…

Far beyond the academy…

The Commander quietly erased the thirty-third mark from a weathered stone.

In its place…

It carved a different symbol.

A single circle.

Closing.

The hunt was no longer tightening around Blackwood.

It was tightening around Aidan himself.
