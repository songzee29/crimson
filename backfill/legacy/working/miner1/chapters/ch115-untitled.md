The basement lay beneath decades of abandoned brick and rusted steel.

The smell hit them before they reached the bottom.

Blood.

Hundreds of gallons.

Ancient.

Fresh.

Layered together.

The chamber had once been a wine cellar.

Now crimson symbols covered every wall.

Not Witch runes.

Not markings from the Ancient Gates.

Vampire sigils.

Draco stopped immediately.

“I’ve seen these.”

Everyone turned toward him.

“They were drawn by the first vampires who rejected the Ancient Gates.”

Cassian frowned.

“You never mentioned that.”

“I didn’t know they still existed.”

He looked around the chamber.

“I do now.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The symbols suddenly ignited.

One after another.

A voice echoed through the cellar.

Calm.

Ancient.

Patient.

“So…”

“The Heir finally leaves his inheritance.”

No body appeared.

Only the voice.

Aidan’s expression never changed.

“You’ve been following me.”

“For years.”

“What do you want?”

A quiet laugh echoed through the cellar.

“You.”

Silence.

“Not your blood.”

“Not your throne.”

“You.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crimson symbols exploded.

The entire cellar shook.

Hidden passages opened in every direction.

Dozens of vampires poured into the room.

Not feral.

Organized.

Disciplined.

Their eyes remained fixed on Aidan.

Not the others.

Only him.

The ancient voice spoke once more.

“You ended the Ancient Gates.”

“Now end me.”

The attack began.

And for the first time since arriving in New Orleans…

Aidan smiled.

Not with joy.

With anticipation.

The predator had finally found prey worthy of hunting.
