With precision.

“Break the formation.”

Her silver form became a blur as she crashed into the flank of two advancing Guardians.

One stumbled.

The formation shifted.

Only slightly.

But it was enough.

“Elias.”

A heartbeat later, he emerged behind the disrupted Guardian.

Cracks spread through its body.

Lyra struck from the opposite side.

A pulsing crimson light.

The Guardian froze.

Its body trembled.

Then…
