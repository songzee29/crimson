The first scream reached Cassian before the coven came into view.

He stopped.

Just once.

Malik saw his face.

Cassian knew the voice.

He whispered a name.

Then ran.

Broken knee.

Cracked ribs.

Dislocated shoulder.

None of it mattered.

His coven was screaming.

He forced his body forward.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Inside the compound…

Aidan hunted.

Not wildly.

That would have been easier to forgive.

He was precise.

One vampire attempted to flee across the roof.

Aidan’s wings carried him there instantly.

He seized the vampire in midair and drove him into the ground.

Fangs.

Blood.

Empty.

Another attacked from behind.

Dragon scales hardened across Aidan’s back before the blade landed.

Steel snapped.

Aidan turned.

His claws tore through the attacker’s chest.

The Wolf tracked another heartbeat beneath the building.

The Witch found the hidden space.

Aidan ripped through the floor.

The Siren heard pleading before words reached his ears.

The Phoenix burned through a barricade.

Then came blood.

More.

More.

More.

Every feeding strengthened him.

Every feeding stabilized the violent changes inside his body.

The pain diminished.

The hunger did not.

Not quickly enough.

Aidan moved through Cassian’s coven like a catastrophe wearing a human face.

Until Lyra arrived.

She shifted mid-stride.

Crimson wolf became woman.

Rowan’s cloak was long gone, but her new Crimson Blood answered instinctively, living runic magic forming enough covering across her body as she ran into the courtyard.

“AIDAN!”

He stopped over another vampire.

His fangs were already at her throat.

Lyra saw his eyes.

Saw recognition.

Saw starvation.

Saw shame.

All at once.

“Put her down.”

Aidan’s jaw tightened.

“I can’t.”

“Yes, you can.”

“You don’t understand.”

“I died today.”

Her voice cracked across the courtyard.

“I think we’re past pretending either of us understands what normal feels like.”

The vampire beneath Aidan whimpered.

That heartbeat hit him again.

Aidan snarled.

Lyra stepped closer.

“Look at me.”

“Don’t.”

“Aidan.”

“I said don’t!”

Crimson fire erupted around him.

Lyra did not move.

The fire bent around her exactly as it had on the mountain.

Aidan stared.

Lyra kept coming.

One step.

Another.

Their Crimson bond ignited.

Her heartbeat rose above all the others.

Not as prey.

As Lyra.

The woman he had lost.

The woman he had brought back.

The first life his True Crimson Blood had transformed.

Aidan released the vampire.

She scrambled away.

Aidan staggered backward.

His wings dragged across the stone.

“I killed them.”

Lyra looked around.

Bodies covered the coven grounds.

Some moved weakly.

Many did not.

“Yes.”

“I couldn’t stop.”

“You stopped now.”

“Because you came.”

“Then remember that.”

Aidan stared at her.

Lyra’s eyes filled with pain.

“Don’t ask me to tell you this didn’t happen.”

He looked down.

“I wasn’t going to.”

“Good.”

Because neither of them could afford lies anymore.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A heartbeat sounded behind them.

Then another.

Cassian entered the courtyard.

He stopped.

His entire body became still.

One of the dead lay near the entrance.

Cassian recognized him immediately.

Then another.

And another.

His face emptied.

“No.”

He limped forward.

“No…”

Aidan turned.

Blood covered his mouth.

His hands.

His clothes.

Cassian looked at him.

Then at the bodies.

Understanding came slowly.

Cruelly.

“You…”

Aidan said nothing.

Cassian stumbled toward one of his coven members and dropped beside her.

His good hand pressed against her throat.

Nothing.

He moved to the next.

Nothing.

Another.

A faint pulse.

“Rowan!”

Rowan had just reached the courtyard with Elias, Malik, and Draco.

He moved immediately.

Cassian crawled from body to body.

Names poured from his mouth.

Some answered.

Most didn’t.

The Curse Mark across his chest began to burn.

Cassian screamed and tore at his shirt.

The broken-heart mark glowed black-crimson.

Aidan watched him.

There was no satisfaction.

No revenge.

Only horror.

Cassian looked toward him.

“Why?”

Aidan’s voice came rough.

“I was starving.”

Cassian laughed.

It was the ugliest sound Aidan had ever heard.

“Starving?”

His eyes filled.

“That’s what I get?”

Aidan absorbed the words.

“No.”

Cassian stood despite everything broken inside him.

“These people didn’t kill Lyra.”

“I know.”

“They didn’t put a blade through her.”

“I know.”

“They didn’t curse you.”

“I know.”

“They were mine!”

His voice shattered.

Aidan’s wings lowered.

Cassian pointed at the dead.

“That one kept this coven alive when I couldn’t.”

Another.

“She buried her brother last year.”

Another.

“He had a daughter.”

Aidan closed his eyes.

“Stop.”

“No.”

Cassian limped closer.

“You made me look at Lyra.”

Aidan opened his eyes.

Cassian was crying now.

“So look.”

Aidan did.

Every body.

Every survivor.

Every pool of blood.

Every consequence.

He forced himself to see all of it.

Cassian stopped several feet away.

“You wanted me alive to remember what I did.”

Aidan’s voice was barely audible.

“Yes.”

Cassian gestured around them.

“Then you remember this.”

Silence.

Aidan nodded.

“I will.”

The answer took some of the rage out of Cassian.

Not the grief.

Never that.

Lyra came beside Aidan.

She did not defend him.

She did not excuse him.

Her hand found his anyway.

Cassian noticed.

That hurt too.

But he said nothing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Rowan moved through the survivors.

Elias joined him.

Malik carried the wounded.

Draco searched the structures for anyone still alive.

The damage could not be undone.

Not even True Crimson Blood changed that.

Aidan had already learned what happened when he refused to accept death.

He would not turn a massacre into an experiment.

Cassian knelt in the center of what remained of his coven.

For several minutes…

He simply breathed.

Then one wounded vampire grabbed his wrist.

“Cassian…”

He looked down.

The vampire’s eyes were terrified.

“Don’t leave us.”

Cassian’s face broke.

He clasped the vampire’s hand.

“I won’t.”

The Curse Mark burned.

Cassian winced.

But this time he did not scream.

He looked across the destroyed compound.

At the living.

At the dead.

At what remained.

Then he made his decision.

“We rebuild.”

Aidan watched him.

Cassian’s voice grew stronger.

“Everyone who survived gets treated first.”

He looked at Malik.

“Help me move them.”

Malik nodded.

Cassian looked toward Rowan.

“Tell me who can be saved.”

Rowan answered immediately.

Cassian began working.

Not because anyone forgave him.

Not because the Curse was finished.

Not because Aidan had forgiven what happened to Lyra.

Cassian worked because destruction had finally left something in his hands that could not be solved by sacrifice.

Only responsibility.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Crimson System appeared before Aidan.

For once…

It was stable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Emergency Evolution:

Stabilized

Dormant Inheritances:

DRAGON — AWAKENED

SIREN — AWAKENED

CRIMSON PHOENIX — AWAKENED

Original Authorities:

WITCH

VAMPIRE

WOLF

Status:

Intact

True Crimson Blood:

Biological expansion confirmed

Evolutionary Hunger:

Stabilized

Major Consequence Recorded:

CASSIAN’S COVEN DRAINED

Casualties:

Permanent

Survivors:

Present

Responsibility:

Unresolved

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

No reward appeared.

Aidan was grateful.

A reward would have felt obscene.

The System vanished.

Aidan looked at his wings.

At the scales across his hands.

At the blood beneath his claws.

Power had arrived instantly.

Understanding had not.

And the price had been paid by people who never chose to participate.

Lyra squeezed his hand.

“What now?”

Aidan looked toward Cassian.

Cassian was lifting one of his surviving coven members with his one usable arm.

Their eyes met.

There was hatred there.

Grief.

History.

A brotherhood neither could completely erase and neither had any right to restore.

Aidan answered quietly.

“We live with what we did.”

Cassian heard him.

He looked around at his ruined coven.

Then back at Aidan.

“And I rebuild what you destroyed.”

Aidan didn’t argue.

“Yes.”

Cassian turned away.

The Curse Mark remained burned into his chest.

Lyra remained alive beside Aidan.

The First Crimson Runefang.

Aidan stood beneath the night with Dragon horns rising from his head, crimson-black scales across his skin, and real Dragon wings folded behind him.

Somewhere inside his blood…

The Siren waited for deep water.

Crimson Phoenix fire smoldered beneath his heartbeat.

Witch.

Vampire.

Wolf.

Dragon.

Siren.

Crimson Phoenix.

Not six Authorities.

One impossible inheritance carried by one man.

And around him lay proof that becoming more powerful had never meant becoming free of consequence.

Cassian’s coven was broken.

Cassian was alive.

And rebuilding it had just become another burden neither blood nor magic could carry for him.
