For the first time since the convergence began…

The roots of the ancient tree trembled.

It lasted less than a heartbeat.

The old oak became still once more.

No student noticed.

No passing instructor looked twice.

Only Aidan.

Only Lyra.

Only Master Rowan.

They turned toward the tree at the same instant.

The silence between them was enough.

Something had changed.

⸻

The next academy bell never rang.

The rope swung.

The clapper moved.

No sound came.

Students looked upward.

Confused murmurs spread across the courtyard.

Professor Helena was already moving.

“Everyone remain where you are.”

Her calm voice carried farther than any bell.

Headmaster Aldren crossed the courtyard with Mr. Bennett at his side.

Neither looked toward the tower.

Both looked toward the old oak.

⸻

The ground beneath the tree shifted.

Not violently.

The earth gently settled inward.

Just enough for one of the massive roots to emerge from beneath the soil.

Ancient.

Crimson veins pulsed faintly beneath its bark before fading again.

Students slowly backed away.

No one screamed.

The academy itself seemed to be holding its breath.

Master Rowan reached the tree first.

He rested one hand upon the exposed root.

His expression changed immediately.

“Aldren.”

The Headmaster joined him.

“It opened.”

“What opened?”

“The old passage.”

⸻

Within minutes the courtyard was cleared.

Not by force.

By trust.

Professor Helena redirected every student toward the western halls.

“Afternoon lessons are relocated.”

“There is nothing to worry about.”

“Follow your instructors.”

No panic spread.

Because the teachers never allowed panic to become the loudest voice.

⸻

Aidan watched from the edge of the courtyard.

Cassian stepped beside him.

“They’re hiding something.”

“They are.”

“So what do we do?”

Before Aidan could answer…

Master Rowan turned.

His eyes found Aidan immediately.

“You.”

The old traveler’s voice was calm.

“Come with me.”

Cassian took one step forward.

“So am I.”

Professor Bennett gently placed a hand on his shoulder.

“Not this time.”

Cassian’s jaw tightened.

“I’m not letting him walk in there alone.”

“He won’t.”

Bennett answered.

“I’ll be beside him.”

Cassian looked toward Aidan.

For several long moments.

Then finally nodded.

“Come back.”

It wasn’t dramatic.

It wasn’t heroic.

It sounded exactly like a brother speaking to family.

Aidan smiled.

“I will.”

⸻

The exposed root concealed a narrow stone stairway descending beneath the academy.

Not newly formed.

Revealed.

Lanterns were lit.

Headmaster Aldren led the way.

Professor Helena followed.

Mr. Bennett remained behind Aidan.

Master Rowan walked beside him.

The air grew cooler with every step.

The walls looked strangely familiar.

Not because Aidan remembered them.

Because the stone resembled the ancient corridors beneath the Sanctuary.

No one spoke.

At the bottom of the stairs…

They found a circular chamber.

Its ceiling disappeared into twisting roots from the old oak above.

The roots intertwined with ancient stone pillars covered in weathered runes.

At the center stood a single pedestal.

Empty.

Except for one object.

A cracked bronze key.

Master Rowan stopped walking.

His face became unreadable.

“I remember this room.”

Headmaster Aldren looked toward him.

“You’ve been here?”

“No.”

The old traveler quietly shook his head.

“The Founder told me about it.”

He stepped closer to the pedestal.

“The Root Chamber.”

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOUNDER RECORD UNSEALED

Location Confirmed:

The Root Chamber

Status:

First Foundation Node

Authority:

Recognition Granted

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crimson light faded.

A second message appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LEGACY OBJECTIVE

Restore the First Foundation.

The key must not be removed.

The chamber must first be understood.

Failure will awaken what the Foundation was built to restrain.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

No reward followed.

Only silence.

⸻

Headmaster Aldren slowly exhaled.

“So that’s why the academy shifted.”

Master Rowan nodded.

“The roots weren’t breaking.”

“They were leading us here.”

Aidan stepped toward the pedestal.

Then stopped.

Rowan noticed immediately.

“What is it?”

“The Legacy.”

“What did it tell you?”

Aidan looked toward the cracked key.

“It warned me not to touch it.”

No one questioned him.

Not anymore.
