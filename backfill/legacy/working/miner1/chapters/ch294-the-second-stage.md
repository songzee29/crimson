Rain drifted across the mountain peaks in long silver curtains.

Neither of the two figures moved.

The elder witch stood with his hands folded behind his back, his weathered robes stirring gently in the cold wind while the younger figure beside him watched the distant horizon where Blackwood Academy disappeared beneath clouds.

For several moments…

Only silence existed.

Finally, the younger figure spoke.

“Will he survive it?”

The elder did not answer immediately.

His ancient eyes remained fixed upon the unseen academy.

“I do not know.”

The admission surprised the younger witch.

“You’ve always known.”

“No.”

The elder smiled faintly.

“I’ve only known the Founder.”

His gaze hardened.

“Aidan is becoming someone else.”

Lightning silently illuminated the distant mountains.

“The Legacy has noticed.”

“So have we.”

The younger witch lowered his voice.

“And the others?”

“They’re moving.”

⸻

Miles away…

Within the forgotten chamber beneath the ruined cathedral…

The remaining members of the Seven gathered once more.

This time…

Only six chairs were occupied.

The seventh remained empty.

Not abandoned.

Waiting.

The woman known as the Archivist slowly closed the ancient journal resting before her.

“The Foundation has accepted him.”

A broad-shouldered man wearing weathered crimson armor folded his arms.

“He refused the shortcut.”

“He refused power.”

“He refused certainty.”

Another elder quietly nodded.

“He chose patience.”

Silence settled across the chamber.

Then…

The empty chair creaked.

Every member immediately turned.

No one occupied it.

Yet it had moved.

Only slightly.

As though someone unseen had leaned forward.

No one spoke.

No one breathed.

The Archivist carefully lowered her eyes.

“The Keeper is listening.”

The room instantly became colder.

⸻

Blackwood Academy…

Evening settled peacefully across the restored campus.

Students filled the courtyards once again.

Workers repaired shattered windows.

Teachers laughed together after another exhausting day.

Life continued.

Exactly as the Founder intended.

Ordinary.

Aidan walked beside Malik, Elias, Cassian and Lyra toward the western gardens where the academy overlooked the valley below.

Malik stretched both arms above his head.

“I swear Headmaster Aldren enjoys making reconstruction everyone’s responsibility.”

Cassian laughed.

“He does.”

“And he’s right.”

Elias looked toward the damaged bell tower.

“Still…”

“I’m glad it’s standing.”

Lyra quietly glanced toward Aidan.

“So am I.”

Only Aidan understood the weight behind those words.

The tower still stood.

Because they had chosen restraint.

Not ambition.

The lesson lingered inside him.

⸻

The western gardens had changed since the battle.

Flowers once buried beneath shattered stone had begun blooming again.

Young trees bent gently beneath the evening breeze.

Several first-year students sat beneath them studying while others laughed nearby.

Peace.

Simple.

Fragile.

Beautiful.

Aidan found himself smiling.

The Crimson System quietly appeared before him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOUNDER OBSERVATION

Strength protects peace.

Character preserves it.

Never confuse the two.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message faded almost immediately.

No reward.

No percentage.

No quest.

Just another lesson.

He appreciated those the most.

⸻

Suddenly…

Every bird in the garden became silent.

The breeze stopped.

The warmth disappeared.

Aidan halted.

Malik noticed first.

“What is it?”

Before Aidan could answer…

Professor Helena appeared from the academy doors.

She wasn’t running.

She wasn’t shouting.

Yet the expression on her face caused every teacher nearby to stand.

Headmaster Aldren emerged seconds later.

Behind him walked Mr. Bennett.

All three looked toward the western gate.

Not the mountain.

Not the bell tower.

The road.

Someone had arrived.

The great academy gates slowly opened.

An elderly traveler stepped inside carrying nothing but a walking staff and a worn leather satchel.

Simple gray robes.

Weathered boots.

Silver hair.

He looked no different from an ordinary old man who had spent his life wandering from village to village.

Students barely noticed him.

Several even continued walking past.

Yet every professor stopped speaking.

Every instructor quietly stood.

Headmaster Aldren descended the academy steps himself.

Not as Headmaster.

As a man greeting someone worthy of respect.

The elderly traveler smiled warmly.

“It has been many years, Aldren.”

The Headmaster bowed his head.

“Too many, Master Rowan.”

Aidan felt something stir beneath the crimson mark on his hand.

Not danger.

Recognition.

The System appeared again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

IDENTITY CONFIRMED

Master Rowan

Legacy Classification:

The Wandering Teacher

Founder Record:

Accessible

Character Evaluation:

Incomplete

Recommendation:

Observe.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan’s breathing slowed.

Another teacher.

Another piece of the Legacy.

Not a warrior.

Not a guardian.

A teacher.

Master Rowan’s gentle eyes slowly drifted across the courtyard.

Past the instructors.

Past the students.

Until they settled upon Aidan.

The old man smiled.

Not because he had found the Crimson Heir.

But because…

He had found a young man standing quietly among his friends instead of apart from them.

Master Rowan gave the smallest approving nod before looking back toward Headmaster Aldren.

“Good.”

“He isn’t alone.”

Far beyond the academy…

The elder witch standing upon the rain-soaked cliff quietly closed his eyes.

“So…”

“The Teacher has arrived.”

The younger witch beside him frowned.

“Will he train the Keeper?”

The elder slowly shook his head.

“No.”

His ancient voice carried absolute certainty.

“The Founder already left him teachers.”

“This old man has come for something far more difficult.”

The younger witch looked toward him.

“What could be harder than preparing the Keeper for war?”

The elder’s answer disappeared into the evening wind.

“Preparing him…”

“…to remain human after he survives it.”
