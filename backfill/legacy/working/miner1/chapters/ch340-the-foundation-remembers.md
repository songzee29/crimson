The warm light surrounding the Second Foundation settled into a steady pulse.

For the first time since they had entered Grey Hollow…

Nothing felt broken.

The chamber breathed.

Not with lungs.

With purpose.

Every Foundation Thread Aidan could see now flowed cleanly through the immense underground structure, weaving through pillars, walls, and chambers older than any kingdom still standing.

Then…

The central crystal rose from its pedestal.

No magic forced it upward.

It simply…

Remembered.

Everyone instinctively stepped back.

Except Rowan.

He smiled.

“It has judged us.”

The crystal slowly rotated.

The golden light inside deepened until countless images appeared within its core.

Families.

Children.

Keepers.

Entire generations living beneath Grey Hollow.

Not hiding.

Thriving.

The ancient voice echoed through every stone.

Remember.

The images changed.

The Keepers were not soldiers.

They were teachers.

Builders.

Healers.

Judges.

Farmers.

They argued.

They celebrated.

They mourned.

Every important decision happened within this chamber.

Never alone.

Always together.

Professor Helena quietly wiped away a tear.

“This…”

“…this is what we lost.”

“No.”

Mr. Bennett whispered.

“This is what survived.”

The vision shifted again.

The Foundation Keepers stood around the crystal exactly as Aidan’s group now stood.

One by one…

They placed their hands upon it.

Not to receive power.

To leave something behind.

Knowledge.

Experience.

Memory.

Aidan suddenly understood.

“The Foundation…”

“…isn’t protecting magic.”

Master Rowan looked at him.

“What is it protecting?”

He watched another Keeper gently teaching a child to draw her first rune.

“Civilization.”

The chamber answered.

Accepted.

The crystal brightened.

The chamber trembled.

This time…

Not from instability.

From release.

The central crystal slowly lowered.

Ancient stone surrounding the room folded away like petals opening toward the sun.

A hidden hall stretched beyond.

Unlike every corridor before it…

This one had no decorations.

Only words carved into both walls.

Every sentence was a Law.

Not magical.

Moral.

Power without restraint becomes tyranny.

Knowledge without compassion becomes cruelty.

Protection without sacrifice becomes vanity.

Lyra walked slowly between them.

“They wrote their failures.”

Professor Helena nodded.

“So no generation would repeat them.”

Cassian quietly stopped before another inscription.

He read it aloud.

“Judge actions before judging people.”

His voice grew softer.

“I needed to hear that.”

Aidan glanced toward him.

Neither spoke.

Neither needed to.

⸻

Halfway down the hall…

Foundation Awareness screamed.

Not in fear.

Warning.

Every golden thread abruptly shivered.

The warmth vanished.

Cold flooded the chamber.

One by one…

The ancient Laws carved into the walls darkened.

Black veins spread beneath the stone.

Not fast.

Steady.

Purposeful.

Master Rowan’s smile disappeared.

“So.”

He whispered.

“It has finally reached us.”
