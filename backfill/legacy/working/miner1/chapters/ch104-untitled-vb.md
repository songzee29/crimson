The streets of New Orleans blurred beneath the rain.

No one spoke.

There was no need.

The crimson glow pulsed once more against the clouds before disappearing behind the old buildings of the French Quarter.

Aidan reached the next rooftop first.

His boots landed without a sound.

Behind him, Lyra’s wolf leaped effortlessly across the gap.

Cassian followed close behind.

Malik and Elias arrived seconds later while Rowan remained on the streets below with the cleanup teams, directing them toward the growing disturbance.

Draco never seemed to hurry.

Yet somehow…

He remained beside Aidan every step of the way.

Aidan noticed.

He said nothing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The city felt wrong.

Not because of the rain.

Not because of the lingering scent of blood.

Everything was…

Quiet.

Too quiet.

Normally, Aidan could hear thousands of heartbeats flowing through the streets.

Laughter.

Music.

Tourists wandering Bourbon Street.

Tonight…

Entire blocks had fallen silent.

He stopped.

Cassian nearly walked into him.

“What is it?”

Aidan slowly turned his head.

“There.”

He pointed toward a narrow alley between two ancient brick buildings.

No movement.

Nothing visible.

Only darkness.

Draco spoke first.

“You hear it.”

It wasn’t a question.

Aidan nodded once.

“One heartbeat.”

“Only one?”

“No.”

Aidan’s crimson-gold eyes narrowed.

“One living heartbeat.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra shifted first.

Silver fur rippled across her body.

Within seconds, the massive Crimson Runefang stood beside Aidan.

Her ears flattened.

“I don’t smell blood.”

Malik frowned.

“I don’t sense magic either.”

Elias loosened one of the crimson blades on his back.

“Then why does this place feel like a graveyard?”

No one answered.

Because every one of them felt it.

The alley wasn’t empty.

It was…

Waiting.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan stepped forward.

One pace.

Then another.

The rain no longer reached the ground inside the alley.

Each droplet vanished halfway down.

As if something unseen swallowed it.

Ancient brick walls stretched impossibly farther than they should have.

Faint, worn carvings—barely visible beneath centuries of decay—lined the stone, their patterns echoing symbols Aidan had seen before within the Ancient Gates.

The deeper they walked…

The farther away the sounds of the city became.

Until…

Nothing remained.

Not wind.

Not rain.

Not heartbeats.

Except one.

Steady.

Patient.

Ahead.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian glanced behind him.

“The entrance…”

The street they had just left…

Was gone.

Only darkness remained.

Malik instinctively raised both hands.

Golden runes ignited around his fingers.

Nothing happened.

His magic simply…

Disappeared.

“What?”

He tried again.

Nothing.

“It’s eating my spells.”

Rowan’s voice echoed from the entrance they could no longer see.

“Everyone stay together!”

But even his voice sounded impossibly distant.

As though it came from another world.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco finally stopped walking.

“We’re here.”

Aidan looked ahead.

The alley ended.

Not with another street.

Not with a wall.

With a courtyard.

Ancient.

Circular.

Untouched by time.

Not separate—but connected.

An outer sanctuary.

A forgotten approach to something far older.

A stone fountain stood at its center.

Dry.

Cracked.

Covered in crimson vines.

Faint sigils—matching those etched deep within the Ancient Gates—ran along its base, worn but unmistakable.

Around it stood twelve weathered statues.

Every one of them faced inward.

Every one of them knelt.

Not in worship.

In fear.

Cassian’s hand tightened around his sword.

“I don’t like this.”

“No,” Draco replied quietly.

“You shouldn’t.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan’s second heartbeat struck his chest.

Once.

The dry fountain answered.

A faint crimson glow spread through the ancient stone.

The vines began to move.

Not like plants.

Like veins.

The crimson light climbed the fountain before flowing toward each kneeling statue.

One after another…

Stone eyes slowly opened.

Not glowing.

Watching.

Ancient.

Empty.

Yet somehow…

Aware.

Recognition flickered within them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON LEGACY

Historical Observation

Ancient Gates Peripheral Structure Identified

Outer Sanctuary Confirmed

Guardian Court Recognized

Record Cross-Referenced

Archive Expanded

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message vanished.

No explanation.

Only another record.

Aidan barely reacted anymore.

The Legacy continued doing what it always had.

Watching history unfold.

Never interfering.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A sharp crack echoed through the courtyard.

One statue moved.

Stone dust fell from its shoulders.

Then another.

Then a third.

Lyra lowered herself into a fighting stance.

Cassian drew both swords.

Malik finally forced a single golden rune into existence.

Elias silently positioned himself beside Rowan.

Only Draco remained still.

Almost…

Expectant.

Aidan looked at him.

“You knew this place existed.”

Draco answered without taking his eyes off the statues.

“I knew there were more layers to the Gates.”

“You’ve been searching for this.”

“For decades.”

Aidan’s gaze hardened.

“And you brought us here.”

“No.”

Draco’s voice remained calm.

“I followed you.”

Before Aidan could respond—

The first statue stood.

Ancient stone cracked across its body.

A rusted sword slid free from its side.

Then another statue rose.

And another.

Twelve forgotten guardians slowly lifted their heads together.

Ancient Gate Guardians.

Awakened.

The courtyard trembled.

The dry fountain suddenly filled with dark crimson liquid.

It wasn’t water.

Aidan knew the scent instantly.

Blood.

Very old blood.

The surface rippled once.

Then a single voice echoed from somewhere beneath the stone.

From deep within the structure tied to the Gates themselves.

“So…”

The whisper was ancient.

Patient.

Almost mechanical in its purpose.

“…the Heir returns.”

Every statue turned toward Aidan at the exact same moment.
