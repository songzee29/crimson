Blackwood Academy stood.

Barely.

The western wall remained broken.

The eastern shelters still bore fresh scars.

Students worked in silence.

Even laughter had become rare.

The hunt had changed everyone.

…

Professor Helena spent nearly an hour stitching Aidan’s newest wounds.

When she finished…

She remained standing beside his bed.

“You should be dead.”

Aidan gave a tired smile.

“I’ve heard that before.”

“I’m serious.”

“So am I.”

Helena looked down at the bloodstained bandages filling the waste basket.

“I don’t understand your body anymore.”

Neither did he.

…

That evening Rowan gathered only the inner circle.

No students.

No instructors.

Only Rowan.

Helena.

Lyra.

Cassian.

Malik.

Elias.

And Aidan.

“We’ve confirmed something.”

Rowan placed several maps across the table.

“They knew every patrol.”

Cassian nodded slowly.

“They attacked where we were weakest.”

“They knew when we’d split.”

Elias quietly added,

“And they knew we’d rescue Aidan.”

Silence.

The words from the cavern returned uninvited.

You have your traitor to thank for this.

Aidan kept his expression unchanged.

His eyes drifted across the room.

Rowan.

Impossible.

Helena.

No.

Lyra.

Never.

Cassian…

Malik…

Elias…

No.

His chest tightened.

He hated himself for even allowing the thought to exist.

The hunter wanted this.

He knew it.

Yet…

Questions refused to disappear.

How had they always arrived first?

How had they always known?

“Aidan?”

Rowan’s voice pulled him back.

“You’ve been quiet.”

“I’m thinking.”

“What about?”

He almost told them.

Almost repeated the hunter’s words.

Instead…

He looked down at the maps.

“Our patrol routes.”

Everyone waited.

“I think…”

He chose each word carefully.

“…from now on we should share information only when it’s necessary.”

Cassian frowned.

“You think they’re predicting us?”

“Maybe.”

It wasn’t a lie.

Just not the whole truth.

Rowan studied him for a long moment before nodding.

“A precaution isn’t unreasonable.”

The meeting continued.

No one noticed Aidan quietly memorizing every face around the table.

Not with suspicion.

Not yet.

With uncertainty.

The doubt hurt more than any wound the hunters had given him.

…

Later that night…

Unable to sleep once again…

Aidan stood outside beneath the old oak.

The wind carried the familiar scent of rain.

Far away…

He heard the changing of the academy guard.

A few students whispering.

Malik laughing weakly at something Elias had said.

Life.

Still continuing.

His eyes drifted toward the dark forest beyond Blackwood’s walls.

The hunters had left him alive.

On purpose.

Not to spare him.

To wound something far deeper than his body.

His trust.

The second heartbeat answered quietly beneath his own.

Steady.

Patient.

Waiting.

For the first time…

Aidan didn’t feel completely alone beneath the night sky.

And that realization frightened him more than the darkness ever had.
