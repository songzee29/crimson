The road south stretched beneath a moonless sky.

Aidan did not tire.

He did not slow.

He no longer breathed unless instinct demanded the illusion.

The hunger remained.

Never satisfied.

Only quieter.

For now.

Every few miles another heartbeat drifted into his awareness.

Travelers sleeping inside wagons.

Farmers hiding behind locked doors.

Children dreaming beside dying hearths.

Every pulse called to him.

Every scent tempted him.

He never stopped walking.

The objective lay farther south.

His body knew it.

The notifications disappeared.

His throat burned hotter.

The System had not removed the hunger.

It had challenged him to survive it.

…

A deer burst through the undergrowth.

Its heart hammered wildly.

Aidan’s body moved before thought.

He crossed the distance in an instant.

His hand struck the animal’s shoulder.

The impact threw it across the forest floor.

He stared.

He had not intended to hit it that hard.

The deer struggled to stand.

Its heartbeat thundered through the darkness.

The hunger surged.

He turned away.

The burning became almost unbearable.

Every instinct screamed that he was making a mistake.

He continued south.

The fire inside his throat eased.

Only slightly.

Enough to keep moving.

…

The forest ended.

Open fields replaced ancient trees.

In the distance…

A lonely roadside inn stood beneath the stars.

Aidan heard them before he saw them.

Nine heartbeats.

One coughing child.

One exhausted woman.

Three laughing merchants.

An elderly couple.

Two stable hands.

Nine separate scents.

Nine living souls.

His fangs descended.

The hunger roared.

He vanished into the darkness before he reached the lights.
