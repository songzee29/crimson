No one questioned him.

Not anymore.

The chamber remained silent.

The cracked bronze key rested upon the pedestal as though it had waited centuries for someone to make exactly this choice.

Professor Helena slowly circled the room.

“The runes.”

“They’re not damaged.”

Mr. Bennett examined the floor.

“No signs of forced entry.”

Headmaster Aldren looked toward the key.

“So why is it cracked?”

Master Rowan answered quietly.

“Because it has already been used.”

Silence settled again.

⸻

Aidan continued studying the chamber.

Not searching for danger.

Searching for patterns.

Just as Rowan had taught him.

His eyes followed the roots climbing into the ceiling.

Then the stone.

Then the runes.

Finally…

The empty sockets surrounding the pedestal.

Four of them.

Not one.

The key wasn’t centered.

It belonged to something larger.

He looked toward Rowan.

“This isn’t the Foundation.”

“No.”

The old traveler smiled.

“It’s only the first lock protecting it.”

⸻

The chamber trembled.

A little stronger than before.

Dust drifted from the ceiling.

One lantern extinguished itself.

Then another.

Professor Helena immediately looked upward.

“The roots are moving.”

They weren’t.

The shadows were.

As the remaining lanterns flickered…

The twisting roots cast new patterns across the walls.

The runes no longer appeared random.

Together…

They formed a path.

Leading toward a narrow seam hidden behind one of the pillars.

Mr. Bennett stepped toward it.

“A door.”

“There wasn’t one a moment ago.”

“There was.”

Rowan answered.

“We simply couldn’t see it.”

⸻

The Headmaster turned toward Aidan.

“What does the Legacy say now?”

The crimson light returned.

Not hurriedly.

Patiently.

FOUNDER OBSERVATION

Power often asks to be taken.

Wisdom waits…

To be invited.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message lingered.

Then another followed.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LEGACY OBJECTIVE UPDATED

Do not claim the key.

Open the way.

Trust those beside you.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Aidan looked at the hidden seam.

Then at the cracked key.

Then back toward Rowan.

“I think…”

He said quietly.

“…the chamber isn’t asking for one person.”

“It’s asking for all of us.”

Rowan’s eyes warmed.

“You’ve changed.”

Aidan smiled faintly.

“You told me to look for the river.”

“And what did you find?”

“The Foundation was never protecting one heir.”

He looked around the chamber.

“It was protecting everyone who stood with him.”

No one spoke.

Because no one could improve upon the answer.

⸻

Above them…

The academy shook once.

This time every student felt it.

Books slipped from shelves.

Windows rattled.

The old oak shed hundreds of leaves despite the season.

Cassian was already running toward the courtyard.

Malik beside him.

Lyra and Elias close behind.

Not toward safety.

Toward Aidan.

Because when the academy moved…

Their first thought wasn’t the mountain.

It was him.

Far beyond Blackwood Academy…

The elder among the Seven rose from the rain-soaked cliff.

He looked toward the academy one final time.

“The First Foundation has opened.”

The younger member beside him looked up.

“Then it’s time.”

The elder nodded.

“Yes.”

“For the first time in generations…”

He stepped away from the cliff.

“…we no longer watch the Keeper.”

“We answer him.”
