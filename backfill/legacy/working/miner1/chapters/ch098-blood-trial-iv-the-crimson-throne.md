The widening passage beyond the Ancient Gates swallowed all light.

Aidan entered without hesitation.

The chamber beyond dwarfed everything he had seen before.

An underground kingdom lay hidden beneath New Orleans.

Not a city.

Not a fortress.

A mausoleum.

Countless crimson statues lined an avenue stretching toward a throne carved from black stone.

Every statue faced the throne.

Every statue knelt.

None bore names.

None carried crowns.

Only empty hands.

The throne itself remained vacant.

Its armrests were stained with blood so old it had become part of the stone.

Aidan’s crimson eyes settled upon it.

The second heartbeat thundered.

The throne answered.

A crimson pulse spread through the chamber.

Every kneeling statue slowly raised its head.

Thousands of stone eyes opened simultaneously.

Aidan vanished.

A stone fist shattered the floor where he had stood.

Stone exploded.

The armor held.

His claw pierced through the guardian’s neck.

The construct collapsed into crimson dust.

Immediately another replaced it.

The path became a battlefield.

Every step toward the throne demanded blood.
