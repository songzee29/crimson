The temperature continued falling.

Breath became visible.

Not a single Foundation Thread snapped.

Instead…

They bent.

As though something unimaginably heavy had settled upon them.

The ancient voice returned.

No longer calm.

No longer whole.

Keeper…

Listen…

Images poured into the chamber.

Not memories.

Warnings.

The First Foundation.

Still standing.

The Second Foundation.

Weakening.

Then…

Something deeper.

A sea of crimson darkness pressing upward beneath both.

Not attacking.

Waiting.

Patient.

Hungry.

The Foundation spoke through broken light.

The Curse…

Never sought the Foundations…

Everyone froze.

The next words echoed like thunder.

It seeks…

The Heir.

Silence.

Absolute.

Professor Helena stepped forward.

“What does that mean?”

The Foundation answered.

Every Foundation delayed it.

Every Keeper prepared for this generation.

Every lesson…

Every judgment…

Every Law…

The crystal flared.

Was preparing one Witch…

To survive what comes next.

Aidan’s heartbeat pounded.

Not to defeat it.

To survive it.

Master Rowan slowly closed his eyes.

“So the Founder knew.”

“He always knew.”

None survive alone.

Not binding them.
