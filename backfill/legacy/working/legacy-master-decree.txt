# THE CRIMSON LEGACY BIBLE DECREE

## Supreme Decree

This document is the highest authority of **The Crimson Legacy**.

If the story, future chapters, artwork, character designs, lore, powers, or worldbuilding ever contradict this Bible, **this Bible is correct**.

Nothing overrides these decrees except a future decree intentionally added to this Bible.

---

# DECREE I — THE FOUNDATION

**Crimson Legacy is not the story of another Zee.**

It is the story of Aidan.

Zee created the Legacy.

Aidan decides what the Legacy becomes.

The goal of the Crimson Legacy is not to recreate the Founder.

The goal is to create someone the Founder himself could never become.

---

# DECREE II — THE FOUNDER

Zee is the Founder of the Crimson Legacy.

He was the first being to walk every Crimson Path.

His greatest achievement was never becoming the strongest.

His greatest achievement was creating a future where someone else would not have to become him.

His greatest fear was the day absolute power would erase his humanity.

Because of that fear, he built the Crimson Legacy to challenge every future heir instead of blindly empowering them.

---

# DECREE III — THE TRUE HEIR

Aidan is the hidden son of Zee.

Almost no one knows this truth.

The Crimson Book recognized Aidan because of his blood.

No one else could have awakened the Legacy.

Aidan was hidden before history was rewritten.

Even Aidan does not know the full truth.

The Sanctuary knows.

Rez knows pieces.

The Legacy knows everything.

---

# DECREE IV — THE CRIMSON BOOK

The Crimson Book is not merely a magical artifact.

It is a living inheritance.

It contains the Legacy created by Zee.

Once awakened, it disappears from the physical world and becomes permanently bound to Aidan's mind.

Only Aidan can see the System.

No other person can see its interface.

Others only witness Aidan reacting to something invisible.

---

# DECREE V — THE LEGACY

The Legacy is alive.

It learns from Aidan.

It adapts.

It watches.

It refuses to become predictable.

It never exists to make Aidan overpowered.

It exists to prepare him for impossible choices.

---

# DECREE VI — THE SYSTEM

The System and the Legacy are not the same entity.

The System is the interface.

The Legacy is the intelligence behind it.

The System teaches.

The Legacy judges.

The System gives information.

The Legacy determines whether Aidan is worthy to receive more.

---

# DECREE VII — REZ

Rez is Zee's closest friend.

He is trapped inside the Crimson Legacy.

He cannot fully awaken until the Vampire Curse is broken through Aidan's first true death.

Before awakening, only fragments of Rez exist.

After awakening, Rez becomes Aidan's greatest mentor—not by giving answers, but by forcing Aidan to find them.

Rez is never the main character.

He exists to protect the Legacy, not control it.

---

# DECREE VIII — THE CURSES

Every evolution requires sacrifice.

No evolution is free.

Every blessing carries a curse.

Every new power demands a greater responsibility.

Power without cost does not exist in the Crimson Legacy.

---

# DECREE IX — THE ORDER OF AWAKENING

The awakening order is fixed.

1. Crimson Witch
2. Vampire
3. Wolf
4. Every future Crimson Ascension

This order cannot change unless a future decree officially rewrites it.

---

# DECREE X — THE WITCH

The Witch is Aidan's first awakening.

Magic before domination.

Understanding before destruction.

---

# DECREE XI — THE VAMPIRE

The Vampire Curse begins only after Aidan dies.

# DECREE XII — THE WOLF

The Wolf remains sealed.

Its awakening is tied to failure, guilt, and instinct.

The curse is not earned through victory.

It is born from unbearable loss.

# DECREE XIII — THE SANCTUARY

The Sanctuary exists outside normal history.

It protects Zee's Legacy.

It reveals only the truths Aidan has earned.

Every answer found inside the Sanctuary must create even greater mysteries.

The Sanctuary never reveals everything at once.

---

# DECREE XIV — THE GUARDIAN

The Guardian protects the Sanctuary.

He is not Aidan's mentor.

He is not the hero.

He knows only the truths Zee allowed him to know.

This story is not about becoming stronger.
