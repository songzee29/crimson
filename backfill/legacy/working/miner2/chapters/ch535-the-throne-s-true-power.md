The Ash King extended both hands.

The Hall of Names responded.

One by one...

The glowing names lifted from the pillars.

Not disappearing.

Becoming light.

Millions of tiny lights floated through the air.

The Ash King spoke softly.

> **"They trusted kings..."**

Another light rose.

> **"They trusted heroes..."**

Another.

> **"They trusted history..."**

The lights circled around him.

Then his voice deepened.

> **"...History forgot them."**

The lights transformed into warriors.

Not monsters.

Not ghosts.

The remembered echoes of ancient protectors.

Kings.

Queens.

Knights.

Witches.

Vampires.

Wolf Alphas.

Each carrying the memories of the age they once lived in.

Rez stared in disbelief.

"...This..."

"...is his real army."
