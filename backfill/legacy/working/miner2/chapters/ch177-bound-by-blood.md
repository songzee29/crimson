The crimson light slowly faded.

The Valley of Ash fell silent once more.

Cassian stood where he had transformed.

His breathing had become steady.

His heartbeat...

Stronger than ever before.

He slowly looked at Aidan.

There was no hatred in his eyes.

No burning desire for revenge.

Only disbelief.

He clenched his fists.

"...I can still remember everything."

Aidan nodded.

"I never wanted to erase your past."

Cassian looked down at his hands.

"My memories are still there."

"The guilt..."

"The anger..."

"The regret..."

He slowly looked back up.

"But none of it controls me anymore."

---

Rez stepped forward.

"The True Crimson Blood didn't erase who you were."

"It freed you from what you became."

---

Cassian closed his eyes.

For centuries...

His every decision had been driven by the curse.

Every step.

Every battle.

Every act of revenge.

Now...

For the first time...

His mind was quiet.

---

He opened his eyes.

His gaze settled on Aidan.

Without thinking...

He took one step forward.

Then stopped.

He frowned.

"...This feeling..."

Aidan raised an eyebrow.

"What feeling?"

Cassian placed a hand over his chest.

"When I look at you..."

"...my blood recognizes you."

"I've never experienced anything like it."

---

Aidan answered calmly.

"That's the Founder's Blood."

"It doesn't demand loyalty."

"It becomes part of who you are."

---

Cassian slowly nodded.

"I understand."

He looked across the valley.

"I spent centuries trying to destroy House Darkheart."

A faint smile appeared.

"And now..."

"...I find myself wanting to protect it."

---

The wind carried ash between them.

Rez looked from Cassian to Aidan.

Then smiled.

"I think the Fourth Judgment is finally over."

Aidan looked toward the crimson sky.

"No."

"It's finally fulfilled."

The three men stood together in the Valley of Ash.

Not as enemies.

Not yet as friends.

But no longer bound by the hatred that had defined centuries of their lives.

For the first time...

The future belonged to them, not to the curse.
