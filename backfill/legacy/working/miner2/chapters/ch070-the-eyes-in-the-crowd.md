Across the street...

Someone was watching.

Not a hunter.

Not a civilian.

A woman.

Long silver hair hidden beneath a hood.

She leaned against the wall of an old bookstore.

Her eyes never left Aidan.

She wasn't surprised.

She wasn't afraid.

She looked...

Curious.

The moment Aidan turned toward her—

She was gone.

Lyra frowned.

"What?"

"I thought someone was watching us."

She looked around.

"I don't see anyone."

Neither did Aidan.

But the feeling remained.

Somewhere...

Someone had recognized him.

Not as prey.

Not as a king.

As...

Something else.

Miles away...

Inside an abandoned clock tower...

The silver-haired woman smiled.

"So..."

"The bloodline survived."

She reached into her pocket and unfolded a faded photograph.

Two young men stood together.

One was Zee.

The other had his face burned away.

She gently folded the photograph again.

"I wondered which one..."

"...would leave the stronger legacy."

She disappeared into the shadows.
