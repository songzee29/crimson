The western wind refused to settle.

Aidan remained atop the castle wall.

His crimson eyes searched the endless horizon.

Whatever he had sensed...

It wasn't imagination.

It wasn't a mistake.

---

Rez appeared beside him, resting his forearms against the stone wall.

His crimson coat shifted with the breeze.

For several minutes...

Neither of them spoke.

Inside Aidan's mind...

*You still feel it?*

Rez closed his eyes.

**Barely.**

**Whoever it is...**

**They know how to hide.**

---

Aidan frowned.

*Someone hiding from Blood Sense...*

*That's not normal.*

Rez nodded.

**Nothing about this feels normal.**

The First Generation trained in the courtyard.

Everything looked...

Peaceful.

Yet Aidan couldn't shake the feeling...

That peace was ending.

It wasn't Aidan's.

"Good."

Straight toward House Darkheart.
