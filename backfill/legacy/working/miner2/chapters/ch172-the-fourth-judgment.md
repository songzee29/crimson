The celebration never came.

The Crimson Pack returned to House Darkheart beneath a sky that had grown unnaturally dark.

No one spoke during the journey home.

The hunt had changed them.

Especially...

Aidan.

---

The gates of House Darkheart opened.

The Royal Guards immediately bowed.

"My King."

Aidan acknowledged them with a single nod before continuing toward the castle.

His crimson eyes looked the same.

But everyone who knew him...

Felt the difference.

The Founder no longer fought his nature.

He had accepted it.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf rested peacefully beneath an endless crimson sky.

The vampire stood nearby with folded arms.

Neither demanded anything.

Neither argued.

For the first time...

The Three Authorities existed in harmony.

---

Rez walked beside Aidan.

**You've changed.**

*I know.*

**Do you regret it?**

Aidan looked toward the throne room ahead.

*No.*

*I regret how long I spent fearing what I was.*

---

The massive doors opened.

The Crimson Throne waited.

Aidan stopped in front of it.

He didn't sit.

Something...

Felt wrong.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

### **Darkheart Curse**

**Judgment Phase Four**

**Reflection**

**Active**

---

The notification disappeared.

Aidan didn't react.

He had been expecting it.

---

Rez frowned.

He looked toward Aidan.

**It's beginning.**

Aidan nodded slowly.

*Yes.*

---

Before another word could be spoken—

BOOM!

The throne room doors burst open.

A Royal Messenger stumbled inside.

His armor was cracked.

Blood ran down the side of his face.

He dropped to one knee.

"My King..."

His breathing was ragged.

"We've found him."

Silence filled the throne room.

Aidan didn't ask who.

He already knew.

---

"Cassian."

The messenger nodded.

"Yes, My King."

"He isn't hiding anymore."

"He wants to be found."

---

Aidan slowly turned away from the throne.

His expression never changed.

"Where?"

"The northern frontier."

"Near the Valley of Ash."

---

Rez's eyes narrowed.

**He's making the first move.**

Aidan answered calmly.

*No.*

*He's waiting.*

---

The messenger continued.

"He sent a message."

Aidan extended his hand.

The messenger placed a sealed letter into it.

The seal...

Was broken in half.

The symbol of Cassian's old house had been scratched away.

Only one sentence had been written across the parchment.

**No more running.**

---

The room fell silent.

Aidan folded the letter and slipped it into his coat.

Then he looked toward Rez.

"It's time."

Rez nodded once.

"I'll prepare the horses."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

Rez paused.

"No?"

A slow smile crossed Aidan's face.

"We're done chasing each other across the world."

"He asked for a meeting."

"So..."

"...I'll give him one."

---

Outside...

Thunder rolled across the Crimson Kingdom.

Far beyond House Darkheart...

Cassian stood alone at the center of the Valley of Ash.

No army.

No allies.

No escape plan.

Only a single sword planted in the ground before him.

He looked toward the distant horizon.

He knew Aidan would come.

Not because of hatred.

Because the Fourth Judgment demanded it.

Cassian closed his eyes.

For the first time in centuries...

He whispered a prayer.

Not for victory.

Not for mercy.

But that whatever happened next...

Would finally end the curse that had consumed both of their lives.

Far away...

Aidan stepped onto the castle balcony.

The wind carried the scent of ash.

His crimson eyes looked toward the northern horizon.

Inside his mind...

The wolf stood.

The vampire opened its eyes.

Rez's voice echoed through the Soul Link.

**How do you intend to end this?**

Aidan's answer came without hesitation.

*Not with another Judgment.*

*With a new decree.*

The wind howled over House Darkheart.

For the first time since the Darkheart Curse was spoken...

The Crimson King wasn't riding toward vengeance.

He was riding toward the chance to break the curse forever.
