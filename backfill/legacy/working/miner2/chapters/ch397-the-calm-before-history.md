Morning arrived without blood.

Without war.

Without screams.

House Darkheart awoke to another ordinary day.

For most kingdoms...

That was nothing.

For House Darkheart...

It was a victory.

The castle gates opened as merchants entered the city.

Children laughed through the market streets.

Young witches practiced beneath crimson banners while vampire knights sparred in the training grounds.

Life continued.

Exactly as Aidan had fought to make it.

---

From the highest balcony of the Crimson Castle...

The Crimson King watched it all.

He wore no armor.

No crown.

Only a long black coat embroidered with the crest of House Darkheart.

Behind him...

Rez leaned against one of the marble pillars with a grin.

"You know..."

"You look less terrifying without the crown."

Aidan glanced at him.

"...Do I?"

Rez laughed.

"No."

"Not even a little."

For the first time in centuries...

The sound of laughter echoed through the royal balcony.
