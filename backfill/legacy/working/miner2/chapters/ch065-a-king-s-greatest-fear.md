The Guardian slowly walked toward the empty throne again.

"You've been asking the wrong question."

Aidan looked confused.

"What question?"

"You keep wondering..."

The Guardian rested his hand on the throne.

"...how powerful Zee became."

He shook his head.

"That isn't the important question."

Silence.

"The important question is..."

His crimson eyes met Aidan's.

"...what terrified him."

Aidan blinked.

"Terrified?"

The Guardian nodded.

"I watched that man stand before armies."

"I watched him fight creatures whose names have been erased."

"I watched him survive curses that should have destroyed him."

He paused.

"I never once saw him afraid..."

Until...

"...he became too powerful."

Lyra frowned.

"What do you mean?"

The Guardian sighed.

"There comes a point..."

"...where power stops feeling like a gift."

"It starts feeling like permission."

Aidan listened carefully.

The Guardian continued.

"Zee once told me..."

> **'The day I stop questioning whether I'm doing the right thing...**
>
> **...is the day someone needs to stop me.'**

The words hung in the air.

Heavy.

Because Aidan realized something.

The strongest man in history...

Feared himself more than anyone else.
