Cassian stumbled backward.

His entire body screamed.

Every instinct inside him shouted one command.

**Run.**

He had never run from Aidan.

Not once.

Until now.

He turned.

And fled.

---

Aidan watched.

His head tilted slightly.

The prey...

Was escaping.

Then—

A scream echoed through the swamp.

Not from the battlefield.

From the road beyond the trees.

A family had been trying to flee the destruction.

A father.

A mother.

Their teenage son.

They had hidden for days.

Waiting for the fighting to stop.

Instead...

They had walked straight into it.

---

The father froze when he saw Aidan.

"...S-son..."

He pushed his family behind him.

"It's okay..."

"...we don't want any trouble."

Aidan didn't answer.

He didn't even understand the words.

All he heard...

Was three heartbeats.

Steady.

Warm.

Alive.

---

The Guardian's face turned white.

"...No..."

Lyra immediately realized what Aidan had sensed.

She sprinted.

"AIDAN!!"

Malik followed.

"GET THEM OUT OF HERE!"

---

The family tried to run.

Too late.
