Far beyond New Orleans...

Azrath stood before a massive obsidian mirror.

Inside the mirror...

Ancient faces watched him.

The Monarchs.

One finally spoke.

"You've lost control."

Azrath smiled.

"No."

"I've accelerated destiny."

Another Monarch answered.

"You've created a king."

Azrath laughed softly.

"No..."

"I've created..."

"...the only one capable of surviving what's coming."

The room became silent.

The oldest Monarch leaned forward.

"...Then tell us."

"...What are you afraid of?"

For the first time...

Azrath didn't smile.

His voice dropped to almost a whisper.

> **"...The Fourth Authority."**

Every Monarch in the chamber froze.

One slowly stood.

"...Impossible."

Azrath looked toward the reflection of the Crimson King.

"I hope you're right."
