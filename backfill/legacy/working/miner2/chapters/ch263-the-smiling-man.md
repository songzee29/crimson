Elsewhere...

The Smiling Man stood inside an abandoned opera house.

Candles surrounded him.

At the center of the room rested a massive chessboard.

Unlike before...

The pieces were real.

Each carved from crimson crystal.

He gently moved the Crimson King's piece forward.

Then smiled.

"...You've become visible."

A voice echoed from the darkness.

"Should we move?"

"No."

"The King has finally begun ruling."

"We don't interrupt that."

Another voice asked,

"When?"

The Smiling Man slowly looked toward New Orleans.

"When he believes..."

"...he has finally found peace."

Then...

"We take it."
