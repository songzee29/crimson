One chain fell.

**Clink.**

Another.

**Clink.**

Then another.

None shattered.

None exploded.

They simply...

Released him.

Like servants opening a door for their king.

Azrath looked down at his wrists.

Ancient scars remained where the seals had rested.

He traced one with his finger.

"...House Darkheart."

He almost laughed.

"You always were stubborn."

He took one step.

The prison shook.

Not because he was powerful.

Because...

For the first time in thousands of years...

Someone occupied space that had remained empty.
