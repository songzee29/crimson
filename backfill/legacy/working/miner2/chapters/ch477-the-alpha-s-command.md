Aidan watched everyone fighting.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

Rez.

Lyra.

Cassian.

Every one of them protecting someone else.

The second heartbeat thundered.

**THUMP!**

The Wolf spoke.

Not with rage.

With certainty.

> **"...You already lead them..."**

> **"...Now command them."**

For the first time...

Aidan didn't hesitate.

His voice rolled across the battlefield like thunder.

> **"HOUSE DARKHEART!"**

Every ally instinctively turned toward him.

> **"Hold the line!"**

> **"Protect the innocent!"**

> **"No one fights alone!"**

The words carried more than determination.

They carried Authority.

Not magical domination.

Leadership.

Kael immediately shifted to reinforce Darius.

Mara expanded her protective wards around the civilians.

Rez moved to intercept the largest wave before it reached Lyra.

Even Cassian, after the briefest pause...

Followed Aidan's commands.

Not because he served the Crimson King.

Because...

The plan was right.

The Wolf smiled behind the Sacred Seal.

> **"...That..."**

> **"...is an Alpha."**

The Keeper watched the battlefield.

For the first time...

Its expression changed.

Not fear.

Recognition.

It slowly whispered beneath its broken stone mask.

> **"...So..."**

> **"...House Darkheart has produced another one."**

The ash storm intensified.

The true battle...

Had only just begun.
