The training grounds echoed with the clash of steel.

Kael barked orders as soldiers sparred.

Darius had three recruits on the ground within seconds.

Mara watched from beneath the shade of an old oak tree, correcting a young witch's hand movements.

Malik stood in the center of the yard...

Winning.

Again.

He knocked another soldier onto his back before offering him a hand.

"You're getting better."

The soldier laughed.

"I'm trying not to die."

Malik pulled him to his feet.

"Good goal."

Suddenly...

The entire training yard became quiet.

Aidan had arrived.

No royal announcement.

No guards surrounding him.

Just the Crimson King.

He walked across the field with his hands in his pockets as if he had nowhere else to be.

Kael smiled.

"My King."

Mara bowed her head.

"Your Majesty."

Darius rested his sword across his shoulder.

Malik grinned.

"What brings the King to my training yard?"

Aidan looked around at everyone.

Then asked one simple question.

> **"Who's tired of being weak?"**

Silence.

Then laughter.

Malik folded his arms.

"You're looking at the strongest man here."

Aidan looked him up and down.

"You think so?"

"..."

"...That hurt a little."

The yard erupted with laughter.

Kael crossed his arms.

"If you've got a point..."

"...get to it."

Aidan nodded.

"I will."

He looked at every one of them.

Malik.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

These weren't just warriors.

They were family.

They had fought beside him before he was a king.

Before he was the Crimson Heir.

Before anyone believed he could survive.

They had stayed.

Now...

It was his turn.

Aidan stopped in front of Malik.

> **"You trust me?"**

Malik didn't even think about it.

> **"With my life."**

Aidan nodded once.

> **"Good."**

He extended his arm.

Then...

With one swift motion...

His fangs pierced his own wrist.

Crimson blood flowed down his forearm.

Bright.

Alive.

The entire training yard fell silent.

No one had ever seen blood like it.

It almost seemed to breathe.

Malik stared at it.

"...That's new."

Aidan smirked.

> **"Open your mouth."**

...

Malik blinked.

"...Come again?"

Kael burst out laughing.

Darius leaned on his sword, trying not to choke.

Mara covered her face.

Lyra, who had just walked into the courtyard, immediately knew what Aidan was doing.

She laughed so hard she had to lean against a pillar.

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez completely lost control.

**"You seriously can't explain anything first?"**

Aidan answered calmly.

*"I am explaining."*

**"No..."**

**"You're traumatizing them."**

Aidan ignored him.

His eyes never left Malik.

> **"Do it."**

Malik looked around.

"...Is anybody else concerned about how normal he's acting right now?"

Darius shrugged.

"I've seen worse."

Kael nodded.

"Honestly..."

"...same."

Malik sighed dramatically.

"...Fine."

He stepped closer.

"...If this kills me..."

Aidan answered without missing a beat.

> **"It will."**

...

The entire yard froze.

Malik slowly lowered his head.

"...I'm sorry..."

"...WHAT?"

Even Kael stopped laughing.

Mara looked between the two of them.

"...Aidan..."

"...You probably should've led with that."

Inside his mind...

Rez was laughing so hard he could barely speak.

**"I'm never letting you explain anything again."**

Aidan finally smiled.

Then his expression became serious.

He looked directly into Malik's eyes.

> **"I'm not joking anymore."**

The laughter faded.

Everyone could hear the change in his voice.

> **"Your human life ends today."**

> **"If you drink my blood..."**

> **"...there is no going back."**

The wind passed through the silent training grounds.

No one spoke.

Aidan continued.

> **"You'll become something the world has never seen."**

> **"You'll become part of my bloodline."**

> **"You'll become family by blood..."**

> **"...not just by choice."**

He lowered his arm slightly.

> **"So I'm asking you one last time."**

> **"Do you still trust me?"**

Malik looked at Aidan.

Then at Lyra.

She smiled and gave a small nod.

Malik laughed quietly to himself.

"You know..."

"...I really should ask more questions."

He stepped forward until he stood inches away from Aidan.

Then he smiled.

> **"Nah."**

> **"Let's see what happens."**

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez's laughter finally faded.

His voice became quiet.

**"This..."**

**"...is the beginning of the First Crimson Generation."**

Aidan smiled.

*"Yeah..."*

*"It is."*

He lifted his wrist toward Malik.

The Crimson Blood shimmered in the sunlight.

And the future of House Darkheart...

Was about to change forever.
