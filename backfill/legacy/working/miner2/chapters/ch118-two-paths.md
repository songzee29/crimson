Night.

Aidan stood beneath the crimson moon.

His hands moved through the motions of the Witch Authority.

Practicing.

Perfecting.

Every movement flowed like water.

Every Law became smoother.

More precise.

Meanwhile...

Miles away...

Cassian practiced with nothing but his sword.

Again.

Again.

Again.

His blade cut through stone.

Through steel.

Through trees.

Until his arms bled.

Two young men.

Training beneath the same moon.

One...

Trying to become strong enough to protect.

The other...

Trying to become strong enough to destroy.

Far above them both...

Azrath watched in silence.

Then he whispered the words that sent a chill through the empty night.

> **"The day is coming..."**

A slow smile spread across his face.

> **"...when only one of you will rise from the battlefield."**

The wind carried his voice into the darkness.

Neither Aidan nor Cassian heard it.

But fate did.

And fate...

Had already begun choosing its witness.
