The underground sanctuary was filled with candles.

Old books.

Ancient symbols.

Evidence of someone living off the grid for years.

Aidan searched carefully.

Then he heard movement.

A knife pressed against his throat.

A female voice whispered:

"Tell me why the System chose you."

Aidan froze.

Slowly turned.

And finally saw her.

Lyra.

Not a vision.

Not an echo.

Not a ghost.

Real.

Very real.

And very dangerous.

The girl looked exhausted.

Older than her age.

Like someone who had survived things she shouldn't have survived.

The first thing she said wasn't hello.

Wasn't thank you.

Wasn't who are you.

It was:

"How many times has it spoken to you?"

Aidan blinked.

"The System?"

For the first time, fear appeared in Lyra's eyes.

Because that answer told her everything.
