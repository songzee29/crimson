High above the city...

Inside the bell tower of St. Louis Cathedral...

The silver-haired woman watched Aidan from afar.

Another figure approached.

"You seem impressed."

She nodded.

"He chose mercy."

The newcomer frowned.

"Just like Zee."

She smiled sadly.

"No."

"That's the difference."

The figure looked confused.

"Zee would've broken every curse..."

Then hunted down the person who placed them.

She looked toward Aidan in the distance.

"This boy..."

"...saved the victim first."

For a long moment, she said nothing.

Then quietly whispered:

"He may become something even the Crimson Legacy never expected."

---

Deep beneath New Orleans...

Far below the catacombs...

Far below the Sanctuary...

An ancient relic resting inside a coffin of black stone pulsed once.

Then again.

A single drop of crimson blood rolled across one impossibly sharp fang.

The System appeared in Aidan's mind one last time that night.

Not as a quest.

Not as a warning.

Just one sentence.

---

> **The Crimson Cathedral has begun remembering your name.**

---

Aidan stared at the message until it faded.

He had no idea what the Crimson Cathedral was.

He had no idea why it mattered.

He only knew one thing.

For the first time...

The System wasn't sending him somewhere.

It felt like...

Something ancient had begun searching for **him**.
