Cassian dropped to one knee.

Not because the curse forced him.

Because the weight of it had become unbearable.

He looked up at Aidan.

"...End it."

Silence.

"...Please."

Rez looked between them.

He understood.

This wasn't a battle.

It was a sentence.

Cassian had imagined revenge.

Instead...

He had found judgment.

Aidan's expression never changed.

"You asked me once..."

"...why I couldn't let it go."

Cassian lowered his head.

"I remember."

"You thought time would erase what happened."

Another step.

"It didn't."

Another.

"You thought death would end my words."

Another.

"It didn't."

Aidan stopped within arm's reach.

"My decree has never hunted you."

"It has never followed you."

"It simply waited..."

"...for the day your soul finally understood what peace was."

Only then...

Could it take it away.

---

Cassian closed his eyes.

For the first time...

He understood the cruelty of the decree.

If Aidan had cursed him immediately...

He would have adapted.

Instead...

The curse allowed him to rebuild.

To love.

To hope.

To believe the nightmare had ended.

Only then...

Did it remind him.

Nothing was over.
