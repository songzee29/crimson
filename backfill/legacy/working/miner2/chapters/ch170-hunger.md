Minutes passed.

Then...

Something changed.

Not outside.

Inside.

Inside the body that no longer had a heartbeat.

Deep...

Deep within the silence.

Where no light existed.

Where no sound could reach.

There was...

A single drop.

Not of blood.

Of hunger.

It fell into endless darkness.

Ripples spread forever.

Then...

Another.

Then another.

The darkness moved.

Not quickly.

Patiently.

Like an ancient beast opening one eye after centuries of sleep.

The silence...

Was no longer empty.

It was waiting.

---

Far above...

No one noticed.

Not Lyra.

Not Malik.

Not Cassian.

Not even Azrath.

Because nothing had happened.

Not yet.

---

But somewhere beyond life...

Beyond death...

Beyond the Crimson Legacy itself...

A voice echoed through absolute darkness.

Not loud.

Barely a whisper.

Almost forgotten.

---

> **"...Aidan...?"**

Silence.

---

Again.

Softer this time.

Confused.

---

> **"...Can you hear me...?"**

The darkness answered with only one thing.

An endless...

Unbearable...

Hunger.

---

The voice stopped.

For the first time...

It understood.

Something...

Had gone terribly wrong.
