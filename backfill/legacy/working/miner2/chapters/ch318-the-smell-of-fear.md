That night...

Aidan sat alone on the warehouse roof.

Trying to breathe.

Trying to think.

Lyra quietly joined him.

She leaned against his shoulder.

Neither spoke.

Then...

Aidan frowned.

"What?"

She looked up.

"...You're scared."

"No..."

"You are."

He slowly looked at her.

"...How did you know?"

She smiled weakly.

"I can smell rain before it falls."

She placed a hand over his heart.

"...And I know your heartbeat."

Silence.

Then she whispered the sentence that terrified him.

> **"It's changing."**
