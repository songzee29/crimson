A deafening crash echoed through the warehouse.

The brick wall exploded inward.

Dust filled the room.

A figure walked through it.

Long black coat.

Silver Veil insignia.

A sword unlike anything Aidan had seen before.

It wasn't glowing.

It absorbed light.

The hunter surveyed the room calmly.

"Cursed remnants."

His eyes settled on Aidan.

"So..."

"...you're the boy."

Aidan instinctively stepped in front of Lyra and Malik.

"You know me?"

The hunter ignored the question.

Instead he asked one of his own.

"Did the Sanctuary accept you?"

Silence.

Aidan didn't answer.

The hunter smiled faintly.

"That means yes."

Without warning—

He moved.

Faster than anything Aidan had ever seen.

Steel met crimson.

The impact launched Aidan across the warehouse.

He crashed through two concrete pillars before stopping.

His ears rang.

His vision blurred.

"...What..."

The hunter lowered his sword.

"You've got potential."

A pause.

"But no discipline."

---

The cursed entities suddenly attacked the hunter.

All at once.

The hunter didn't even look at them.

One swing.

Five creatures vanished.

Second swing.

Three more disintegrated.

Third swing.

Silence.

Every remaining curse fled deeper beneath the warehouse.

Malik stared.

"What..."

"...was that?"

The hunter looked back at Aidan.

"You rely on power."

He pointed the sword at him.

"Power without control dies young."

Then...

He simply walked away.

No name.

No explanation.

Gone.

Because...

It was right.
