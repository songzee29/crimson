Two months later...

Representatives from every allied faction gathered inside New Orleans City Hall.

The largest supernatural council in history.

The Guardian addressed the room.

"For centuries..."

"We fought one another."

"Now..."

"We fight for one another."

Applause filled the chamber.

Then...

Every delegate stood.

One after another.

Not for the Guardian.

For Aidan.

The room echoed with respectful bows.

No one had asked them to.

It simply...

Felt right.

Aidan looked uncomfortable.

"You don't have to—"

An elderly witch interrupted him with a smile.

"We know."

"That's exactly why we want to."

Cassian watched quietly.
