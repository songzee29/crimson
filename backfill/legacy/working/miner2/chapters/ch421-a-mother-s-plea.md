Morning.

Black Hollow.

An older woman approached Cassian.

Her hands shook as she held out a small wooden carving.

"My granddaughter made this."

Cassian accepted it.

"She disappeared three nights ago."

Tears rolled down her face.

"I don't care who you have to become..."

"...Bring her home."

Those words struck Cassian harder than any enemy ever had.

For years...

He had believed strength meant winning battles.

Now...

He realized...

Sometimes strength meant looking someone in the eyes...

When you had no answers.
