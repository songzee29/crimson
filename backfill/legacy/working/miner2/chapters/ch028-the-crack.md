Aidan woke before dawn.

He was kneeling on the cold cathedral floor.

The rain had stopped.

Sunlight began creeping through the stained-glass windows.

For a long time...

He didn't move.

Then he quietly whispered,

"I understand."

Not everything.

Not yet.

But enough.

Enough to know House Darkheart wasn't built on power.

It was built on responsibility.

He stood.

Picked up the little girl's drawing from the night before.

Folded it carefully.

Placed it inside his jacket over his heart.

Then walked toward the door.

Outside...

The city was waking.

People smiled when they saw him.

Children waved.

Vendors greeted him.

None of them knew...

How much heavier the crown had become overnight.

---

Far beneath the Veil...

A prison guard made his morning rounds.

He stopped.

Confused.

The chains around 001 looked...

Loose.

Only slightly.

But enough to notice.

He frowned.

"That's impossible."

He reached out to inspect the nearest seal.

The moment his fingers touched it...

A whisper escaped from inside the prison.

Not loud.

Not threatening.

Calm.

Almost amused.

> **"Tell me..."**

The guard froze.

> **"...does the Crimson King still believe..."**

A long pause.

> **"...that everyone can be saved?"**

The guard stumbled backward.

His breathing became frantic.

"Who's there?!"

Silence.

Then...

A laugh.

Soft.

Patient.

Certain.

> **"Good..."**

> **"...leave him believing that."**

The guard ran.

Never once looking back.

Behind him...

Another chain snapped.

Not violently.

Quietly.

Like an old promise...

Finally beginning to break.
