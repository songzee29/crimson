The training grounds behind House Darkheart were quiet.

Morning dew still covered the grass.

Royal Guards sparred in the distance.

Kael barked orders as soldiers moved through formation.

Darius corrected a young recruit's stance.

Life had begun returning to normal.

For everyone...

Except the Crimson King.

---

Aidan stood alone in the center of the field.

His eyes closed.

His breathing slow.

He wasn't trying to become stronger.

He was trying to understand.

Inside his mind...

Rez watched patiently.

**"What did the blueprint teach you?"**

Aidan answered immediately.

*"That Crimson responds to intent before force."*

Rez nodded.

**"Good."**

**"Then don't think about power."**

**"Think about purpose."**

---

Aidan slowly raised both hands.

His fingers moved naturally.

The same way they had when he first awakened as the Crimson Witch.

Only now...

There was something different.

The Witch.

The Vampire.

The Wolf.

Three different instincts.

Working together.

Not merged.

Not yet.

But finally...

Working in harmony.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Living Blueprint**

**Crimson Law Available**

**Purpose Before Power**

---

Aidan smiled.

"So that's today's lesson."

He lowered his stance.

His hands began weaving Crimson Laws through the air.

Each movement was deliberate.

Each symbol formed because he understood its purpose.

Not because he memorized it.

The air around his fingertips shimmered.

Thin crimson lines floated between his hands like strands of light.

Kael noticed.

"My King..."

The soldiers stopped training.

None of them could see the Legacy.

But they could see Aidan practicing.

None of them dared interrupt.

---

Inside his mind...

Rez grinned.

**"Now speak."**

Aidan nodded.

His voice was calm.

"Shield."

The Crimson Law answered.

The strands folded together.

A circular barrier formed around Aidan.

Not massive.

Not overwhelming.

Simple.

Stable.

Purposeful.

Aidan touched the barrier with his fingertips.

It felt alive.

He smiled.

"It worked."

---

The shield suddenly flickered.

Then shattered into crimson particles.

Aidan sighed.

"...Still unstable."

Rez laughed.

**"Exactly."**

**"A real Founder doesn't stop because the first attempt failed."**

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Crimson Law Recorded**

**Shield**

**Current Stability: 14%**

**Recommendation**

**Continue Practical Training**

---

Aidan dismissed the notification.

Instead of frustration...

He felt motivated.

Every percentage represented experience.

Every failure taught him something.

Every lesson became part of his own Legacy.

---

From the edge of the field...

Lyra watched quietly.

She folded her arms and smiled.

Mara walked beside her.

"He looks different."

Lyra nodded.

"He is."

"He isn't chasing power anymore."

Mara watched Aidan begin practicing the Law again.

"What is he chasing?"

Lyra's smile grew.

"The kind of king our children will be proud to inherit from."

---

Aidan tried the Law again.

The symbols became cleaner.

His movements smoother.

His breathing steadier.

This time...

The shield remained.

Not because the Legacy had activated it.

Because Aidan had learned a little more than he knew yesterday.

Inside his mind...

Rez smiled with quiet pride.

**"That's it..."**

**"One lesson at a time."**

The Crimson King's journey had entered a new chapter.

No longer a race to unlock power.

But the lifelong discipline of mastering it.
