Deep beneath an old cathedral...

The leaders of the Veil gathered around a circular stone table.

Maps covered the walls.

Ancient weapons rested inside glass displays.

One chair remained empty.

No one sat in it.

No one dared.

The oldest hunter stepped forward.

"The Sanctuary acknowledged him."

Silence.

Another hunter asked,

"Has he awakened?"

"No."

"Only the first Authority."

A woman in crimson armor crossed her arms.

"Then we eliminate him now."

The old hunter slowly shook his head.

"If killing him were that easy..."

"...Zee would never have hidden him."

The room became deathly still.

Another voice finally spoke from the darkness.

Cold.

Emotionless.

"We tried once."

Every hunter lowered their head.

The unseen speaker continued.

"We will not underestimate the Crimson Bloodline again."

A single file was placed on the stone table.

Across the front, written in faded ink, were the words:

> **PROJECT: SECOND ERASURE**

The old hunter looked at the file for a long moment before speaking.

"This time..."

"...we erase the heir before the world remembers his name."

As rain continued to fall over New Orleans, Aidan stood by his apartment window, unaware that multiple forces had begun moving around him.

The Sanctuary had accepted him.

The Veil had declared him a threat.

The Crimson Legacy had given him his first true gift.

And somewhere in the shadows, a woman carrying a photograph of Zee had whispered words no one else had heard:

> "Let's see what kind of man you become... Aidan."

The game had truly begun—but for Aidan, it still felt like an ordinary morning. And that contrast would make what comes next all the more devastating.
