The cathedral was enormous.

Ancient stained-glass windows somehow glowed beneath the river.

At the center stood a throne carved from crimson crystal.

Someone was already sitting on it.

Not alive.

Not dead.

An armored knight.

Motionless.

A rusted crown rested upon his helmet.

The Guardian immediately bowed his head.

"...One of Zee's Judges."

The Circle froze.

The armored figure slowly stood.

The sound of metal echoed through the cathedral.

He drew a massive crimson greatsword.

Then...

Looked directly at Aidan.

His voice thundered through the hall.

> **"State your claim..."**

> **"...Heir of Darkheart."**

Aidan stepped forward.

"I don't seek a throne."

"I seek answers."

Silence.

The Judge slowly nodded.

Then raised his sword.

> **"Then prove..."**

> **"...that your heart deserves the answers your blood demands."**

The cathedral shook violently.

The battle began.
