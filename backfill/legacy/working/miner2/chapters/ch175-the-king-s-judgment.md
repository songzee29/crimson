The ancient Judge stood unmoving.

His great crimson sword rested against the cathedral floor.

The entire chamber had fallen silent.

Water dripped from the ceiling.

The only sound...

Was the heartbeat beneath the cathedral.

**...THUMP...**

**...THUMP...**

**...THUMP...**

Every beat made the crimson crystals lining the walls pulse with light.

The Guardian's face had gone pale.

"No..."

"...it's waking."

Aidan never looked away from the Judge.

The armored giant spoke again.

> **"Heir..."**

> **"...why do you seek the Crimson Legacy?"**

Aidan answered honestly.

"I don't."

Silence.

The Judge tilted his helmet.

"I never wanted any of this."

"I just wanted answers."

"I wanted to know who my parents were."

"Who Zee was."

"Why everyone keeps trying to kill me."

He tightened his fists.

"But now..."

He looked back toward Lyra...

Toward Malik...

Toward Sienna...

Toward Elias...

"They're my reason."

"I fight because if I don't..."

"...someone else dies."

The cathedral became completely still.

The Judge slowly nodded.

Not approval.

Recognition.
