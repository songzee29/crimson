Aidan walked alone through the French Quarter that evening.

He wanted one peaceful hour.

Nothing more.

Then...

Someone spoke.

"You've built something beautiful."

Aidan turned.

The traveler stood beneath a streetlamp.

Hands folded behind his back.

Still smiling.

"Do I know you?"

"No."

The answer came instantly.

"But I know your father."

Aidan's heart skipped.

"What?"

The man tilted his head.

"King Zee Darkheart."

Silence.

"You have his eyes."

Aidan instinctively raised one hand.

Crimson runes crawled across his fingers.

"Who are you?"

The man ignored the question.

Instead...

He looked around the city.

Humans.

Witches.

Children.

Families.

"So much hope..."

He smiled wider.

"It almost makes me feel guilty."

Aidan's voice hardened.

"You still haven't answered me."

The traveler finally looked him directly in the eyes.

"My name..."

He paused.

"...doesn't matter anymore."

A long silence.

"History only remembers what I became."

The air suddenly became cold.

"So..."

"...call me..."

Another smile.

"...the Smiling Man."

---

The Witch Authority erupted across Aidan's hands.

Every instinct screamed.

Danger.

Not ordinary danger.

The kind that rewrote history.

Yet...

The Smiling Man never prepared to fight.

He simply admired Aidan.

"As expected..."

"...you're nothing like Zee."

Aidan didn't lower his guard.

"What do you want?"

The Smiling Man's smile softened.

"To see."

"What?"

"If the son..."

"...can survive the inheritance..."

He stepped backward into the shadows.

"I'm looking forward..."

"...to meeting you again..."

"...Head of House Darkheart."

He vanished.

No magic circle.

No teleportation.

No sound.

He simply...

Wasn't there anymore.
