The chamber doors slowly opened.

Lyra looked up from her desk.

She wasn't afraid.

She simply stood.

"Cassian."

He stepped inside.

Rain dripped from his coat.

His sword remained sheathed.

Neither spoke for several moments.

Finally...

Lyra broke the silence.

"I knew you'd come."

Cassian's eyes narrowed.

"You knew?"

She nodded.

"The day Aidan fulfilled the Second Judgment..."

"I knew the Third had begun."

Silence.

Lyra looked directly into his eyes.

"You still have a choice."

Cassian laughed bitterly.

"So did Aidan."

"He chose."

"And now..."

"...it's my turn."
