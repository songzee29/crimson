Morning settled over New Orleans with warm sunlight and the sound of jazz drifting through the French Quarter.

For everyone else...

It was another beautiful day.

For Aidan...

Something felt different.

He stood alone before the cathedral windows.

His reflection stared back at him.

For just a heartbeat...

It smiled.

Aidan hadn't.

The reflection returned to normal.

"..."

He rubbed his eyes.

"Guess I'm more tired than I thought."

He walked away.

Behind him...

The reflection remained standing for one second longer.

Then disappeared.
