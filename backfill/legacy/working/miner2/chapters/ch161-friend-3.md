That evening...

The warehouse door opened.

Not dramatically.

Just...

A knock.

Malik answered.

Standing outside was a young man carrying two grocery bags.

"...Uh..."

"I think I have the wrong address."

He looked down at a piece of paper.

"Warehouse Seventeen?"

Malik laughed.

"Nope."

"Warehouse Eighteen."

The young man sighed dramatically.

"I knew I missed a turn."

He started walking away.

Then stopped.

Turned back.

"...Wait."

"You guys are the people that keep blowing up abandoned buildings."

Silence.

Malik scratched the back of his neck.

"...Maybe?"

The young man laughed.

"So it WAS you."

Everyone inside the warehouse started laughing.

Even Aidan.

The stranger smiled.

"My name's..."

## **Elias.**

Twenty-three.

Former paramedic.

Volunteer firefighter.

Former Army combat medic.

Someone who spent his life running toward danger.

Not because he was fearless.

Because someone had to.

He wasn't flashy.

He wasn't mysterious.

He wasn't chosen.

He was...

Good.

---

Lyra immediately liked him.

Malik immediately started joking with him.

Sienna tested him with questions.

He answered every one honestly.

Then...

He noticed Aidan quietly watching everyone instead of joining in.

Elias walked over.

Held out one hand.

"You don't smile much."

Aidan looked at it.

"No."

"I don't."

Elias grinned.

"Good."

"One of us has to balance Malik out."

Malik shouted from across the room.

"I HEARD THAT."

Everyone laughed again.

---

For the first time in a very long time...

The warehouse felt warm.

Not because of magic.

Because of people.
