As they crossed another mountain ridge...

The second heartbeat echoed again.

**THUMP.**

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf approached the Sacred Seal.

Not to break it.

To sit beside it.

It looked toward Aidan.

> **"...I once believed strength..."**

A pause.

> **"...meant standing alone."**

The Wolf watched memories of the battle.

Kael protecting strangers.

Mara shielding children.

Darius refusing to retreat.

Rez trusting Aidan without hesitation.

Lyra risking herself to save him.

Even Cassian choosing to fight beside former enemies.

The great beast slowly smiled.

> **"...Your pack has taught me..."**

> **"...that an Alpha is strongest..."**

> **"...when the pack chooses to stand with him."**

The Wolf lay down peacefully.

For the first time since awakening...

Its eyes closed.

Not in sleep.

In trust.

Outside...

Aidan felt the change.

The tension inside his soul eased.

The Third Authority had stopped resisting.

It had begun...

Believing.

Ahead...

The northern mountains disappeared beneath dark clouds.

The Ash King waited.

The Second Judgment continued.

And somewhere beyond the horizon...

Destiny was already taking its next step toward the day when House Darkheart would face its greatest loss...

And its greatest awakening.
