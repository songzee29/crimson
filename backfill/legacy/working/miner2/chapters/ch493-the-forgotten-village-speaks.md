As the group prepared to leave...

The rescued boy tugged gently on Aidan's sleeve.

"...Mister King?"

Aidan knelt to meet his eyes.

"What is it?"

The boy pointed toward the oldest house in the village.

"My grandmother..."

"...used to tell stories."

"What stories?"

"About..."

He swallowed.

"...a king whose blood could tell the truth."

Everyone exchanged confused looks.

The boy continued.

"She said..."

"...when the world started forgetting..."

"...only the Crimson King could make history remember."

The Wolf stirred.

The Legacy remained silent.

The child had unknowingly repeated a legend older than House Darkheart itself.
