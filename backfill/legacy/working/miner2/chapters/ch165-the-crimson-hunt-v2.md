The forest stretched endlessly before them.

Ancient trees towered over the land like forgotten giants.

Morning mist drifted between the trunks.

Not a single bird sang.

Every creature hidden within the wilderness already knew...

A predator had entered its domain.

---

Aidan stood at the edge of the forest.

His eyes slowly closed.

The wolf inside him stirred.

Not with anger.

Not with bloodlust.

With anticipation.

Inside his mind...

The great Crimson Wolf stepped beside him.

Its crimson eyes reflected the endless forest ahead.

**Do you feel it?**

Aidan smiled.

*Every heartbeat.*

*Every scent.*

*Every trail.*

The wolf lowered its head.

**Then stop making me wait.**

Aidan laughed quietly.

*Impatient.*

**Always.**

---

Rez walked beside him.

He didn't need the Soul Link to understand.

He had seen that look before.

Years ago...

On another Darkheart.

"You've made your decision."

Aidan nodded.

"I have."

He looked back at his family.

Malik.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

Lyra.

"I'll be back."

Malik grinned.

"...You're really going to do it."

Aidan simply smiled.

Then...

He stepped forward.

The Crimson Blood answered.

His body shifted naturally.

No pain.

No struggle.

No loss of control.

His human form dissolved into something older.

Something primal.

Moments later...

The great Crimson Wolf stood where Aidan had been.

His coat was black as midnight, threaded with flowing crimson light beneath the fur.

His crimson eyes swept across the forest.

Calm.

Patient.

Royal.

He wasn't merely a wolf.

He was a king wearing the shape of one.

Lyra smiled.

"There you are."

The wolf walked over and gently lowered his head.

She rested her hand against his forehead.

For one quiet moment...

Neither moved.

Then she stepped back.

"Go."

The Crimson Wolf looked toward the forest.

And ran.

---

His first stride shattered the earth beneath his paws.

His second carried him through the trees.

By the third...

He was nothing more than a crimson blur racing through the wilderness.

He wasn't chasing prey.

He wasn't escaping.

He was simply...

Running.

The wind rushed through his fur.

Every muscle moved effortlessly.

Every leap felt natural.

For the first time in his life...

His wolf wasn't confined inside a human body.

It was alive.

Free.

---

Back at the edge of the forest...

Malik suddenly stopped breathing.

His eyes widened.

"...Do you guys feel that?"

Darius slowly nodded.

"My wolf..."

"...wants to run."

Kael placed a hand against his chest.

"So does mine."

Rez smiled.

"The Founder has begun the hunt."

"The Wolf Authority within your blood is responding."

Malik looked at Kael.

Then at Darius.

A grin spread across all three faces.

Without another word...

Their Crimson Blood answered.

Malik shifted first.

His body transformed into a massive dark wolf streaked with crimson along its shoulders and back.

Power rolled from him naturally.

Darius transformed next.

His wolf was broader.

Heavier.

Built like an unstoppable fortress with crimson eyes burning beneath thick black fur.

Finally...

Kael smiled.

Crimson runes briefly shimmered across his skin before his form shifted.

When the transformation finished...

A powerful wolf stood in his place, faint crimson runes woven through the dark fur around his legs and shoulders.

The three wolves looked toward the forest.

Waiting.

---

Mara folded her arms and watched them.

"I'll admit..."

"I'm a little jealous."

Rez chuckled.

"You shouldn't be."

She looked at him.

"You are a Crimson Hexborn."

"You don't carry the Wolf Authority."

"You carry something they don't."

Mara smiled.

"My mind."

"And your Crimson Laws."

Rez nodded.

"When the wolves flush prey into the open..."

"...you'll be the one waiting."

She laughed.

"So everyone has a role."

"Exactly."

---

Lyra watched the three wolves pacing impatiently.

"They're waiting for permission."

Rez nodded once.

"They're waiting for their Alpha."

As if on cue...

A deep, commanding howl rolled across the mountains.

The Crimson Wolf.

Aidan.

Every wolf instinctively lifted its head.

Malik answered first.

Then Darius.

Then Kael.

Three howls rose together.

The sound echoed through the ancient forest.

Rez smiled.

"The pack has been called."

The three wolves exploded into motion.

They disappeared into the trees, following the scent of their Founder.

Mara watched them vanish before looking at Lyra.

"I guess it's just us."

Lyra smiled.

"We'll keep up."

She glanced toward Rez.

"You coming?"

Rez adjusted the Crimson Assassin's coat and grinned.

"I'll hunt on two legs."

The three of them stepped into the forest.

Not as prey.

Not as predators.

But as the family of the Crimson King.

Deep within the wilderness...

The Crimson Wolf slowed atop a ridge.

His ears twitched.

Behind him...

Three familiar scents were drawing closer.

Malik.

Kael.

Darius.

Aidan turned toward the rising sun.

A slow, satisfied rumble escaped his chest.

His pack...

Had answered the call.

And somewhere beyond the trees...

Another howl answered.

This one...

Did not belong to House Darkheart.
