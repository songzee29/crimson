The Guardian arrived moments later.

Too late.

"What happened?"

Aidan looked toward the empty alley.

"He knew Zee."

The Guardian's face hardened.

"What else?"

"He knew about House Darkheart."

"...and he called himself the Smiling Man."

The Guardian closed his eyes.

"...Then he's finally here."

Aidan frowned.

"You know him?"

The old man nodded once.

"I know **of** him."

Silence.

"He's never attacked a city."

"Never started a war."

"Never conquered a kingdom."

Aidan looked confused.

"Then why is everyone afraid of him?"

The Guardian answered with a voice barely above a whisper.

"Because..."

"...every catastrophe in recorded history..."

"...began the day he smiled."

Far beneath the Veil...

001 suddenly laughed.

For the first time in centuries...

It wasn't directed at his prison.

It was directed...

At the world above.

"...So..."

"...you've finally decided to move."

His crimson eyes slowly opened.

"...Old friend."

The final seal surrounding one of his wrists flickered.

Just once.

Then...

A tiny piece of black iron...

Fell to the prison floor.

**Clink.**

The sound echoed endlessly through the darkness.

And for the first time since Operation: Rise of the King began...

The readers would understand one terrifying truth.

The heroes were no longer moving toward the enemy.

**The enemy had begun moving toward them.**
