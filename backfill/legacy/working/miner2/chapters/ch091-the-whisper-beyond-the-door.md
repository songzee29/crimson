The giant's disappearance caused another doorway to emerge at the far end of the chamber.

Unlike everything before...

This door wasn't crimson.

It was obsidian.

Across its surface were carved three symbols:

A crescent moon.

A wolf.

A crown split in half.

Aidan reached toward it.

Immediately—

The System exploded into warnings.

---

**DO NOT OPEN**

---

Another warning.

---

**FOUNDATION INCOMPLETE**

---

Then another.

---

**CRIMSON ASCENSIONS SEALED**

---

Aidan slowly lowered his hand.

"What..."

"...is behind that?"

The System answered.

Not with an objective.

Not with a quest.

Just a single sentence.

---

**One day...**

**When the Witch, the Vampire, and the Wolf stand together as one...**

**Return.**

---

Silence settled over the chamber.

Aidan looked at the door one last time.

Then turned away.

Not because he wasn't curious.

Because...

For the first time...

He understood something Zee had understood long before him.

**Not every door is meant to be opened the first time you find it.**

And somewhere beyond the obsidian door...

Something ancient smiled.

Because the heir had done what countless others had failed to do.

He had chosen patience over power.

A choice that, unknown to Aidan, would echo all the way to the day the Crimson Ascensions finally awakened.
