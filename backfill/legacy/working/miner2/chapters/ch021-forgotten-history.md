Back in New Orleans...

Aidan spent days inside the oldest libraries he could find.

Searching.

Every book mentioned the Crimson King.

None mentioned House Darkheart.

Every record stopped.

Every page ended abruptly.

Almost as if history itself had been edited.

Frustrated...

He slammed one dusty book shut.

"It doesn't make sense."

Lyra looked up from another stack.

"What?"

"If House Darkheart ruled..."

"...why did everyone forget them?"

Neither noticed...

An old librarian quietly watching from across the room.

He whispered beneath his breath.

"They didn't forget."

"They were made to."

Then he disappeared into the shelves before anyone could question him.
