Deep beneath the Veil Cathedral...

Caelum received a report.

"The Crimson King entered the Ash Dominion."

"And?"

"He returned alive."

Silence.

"And the Ash King?"

"He allowed him to leave."

Caelum slowly closed his eyes.

"...Then it has begun."

One hunter looked confused.

"What has begun?"

Caelum walked toward an ancient mural carved into the cathedral wall.

It depicted three crowns.

One made of bone.

One of crimson.

One of ash.

"The balance..."

"...between kings."

He touched the crimson crown.

"If one rises..."

"...the others must answer."
