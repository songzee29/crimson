The Trial finally ended.

The crimson world collapsed.

Valerius disappeared.

His final words echoed through the fading sky.

> **"Return..."**

> **"...when you are worthy of learning the Second Blood Crown."**

---

Everything went black.

---

Aidan opened his eyes.

He stood once again...

Inside the ruined cathedral.

Only seconds had passed in the real world.

Lyra.

Malik.

The Guardian.

All stood frozen.

None of them understood.

They only knew...

Something had happened.

---

Aidan took one step forward.

BOOM!!

An invisible pressure rolled through the cathedral.

Not an attack.

An existence.

Everyone's knees bent instinctively.

Stone cracked beneath their feet.

Even Malik struggled to stay standing.

Lyra gasped.

"...That pressure..."

The Guardian stared in disbelief.

"...Royal Aura..."

"...He brought something back."

---

Aidan looked around the ruined cathedral.

His crimson eyes rested briefly on Lyra.

No warmth.

No hatred.

Just recognition.

Then he looked toward the night sky.

The hunt wasn't over.

It had only grown larger.

And somewhere across the world...

Kings who had slept for centuries...

Opened their eyes.

Because one truth had now spread through every supernatural kingdom:

**The Crimson King was no longer just a legend.**

**He had been acknowledged by the First Blood Sovereign himself.**

And that meant every ancient ruler who believed they deserved the throne...

Would eventually come.

Not to serve him.

To test him.
