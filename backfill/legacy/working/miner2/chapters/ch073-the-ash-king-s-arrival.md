Without warning...

The castle went cold.

Every torch was extinguished.

The crimson light faded.

Ash began falling through the broken ceiling.

Not from fire.

From somewhere else.

The temperature dropped.

The Wolf immediately stepped in front of Aidan inside his soul.

Its ears flattened.

Its fangs bared.

> **"...He has come."**

The great doors of the royal chamber opened by themselves.

No guard touched them.

No magic forced them.

A figure walked through drifting ash.

The Ash King.

He looked first at Lyra.

Then at Aidan.

Finally...

He smiled.

Not cruelly.

Knowingly.

His ancient voice echoed through the chamber.

> **"So..."**

> **"...you refused to surrender your Queen."**

Aidan slowly stood.

Positioning himself between Lyra and the Ash King.

His Crimson Blood still glowed beneath his skin.

"I did."

The Ash King nodded once.

> **"Then you have chosen."**

Aidan's voice was unwavering.

"I have."

Silence filled the room.

The Ash King looked at Lyra again.

Then back to Aidan.

> **"Do you understand..."**

> **"...what you have created?"**

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"Yes."

"I created hope."

For the first time...

The Ash King did not smile.

He simply watched the Crimson King.

The room trembled as two ancient powers faced one another once again.

But this time...

Aidan did not stand before him as a king with three Authorities.

He stood before him...

As the first being in history to wield **True Crimson Blood**.

And the era of the Crimson Bloodline...

Had finally begun.
