The Keeper raised one hand.

A spear of condensed ash formed instantly.

It wasn't thrown.

It simply...

Appeared.

Directly in front of Lyra.

Time seemed to slow.

Aidan saw it.

Rez saw it.

Cassian saw it.

None of them were close enough.

The spear shot forward.

Aidan disappeared in a crimson blur.

He reached Lyra just in time.

The ash spear tore across his side instead.

A deep wound opened from shoulder to waist.

Dark crimson blood spilled onto the forgotten road.

The battlefield froze.

The Keeper looked at the blood.

Its ancient voice whispered.

> **"...Almost..."**

Aidan staggered...

But remained standing.

Behind the Sacred Seal...

The Wolf's eyes widened.

Not from fear.

From instinct.

The scent of Aidan's blood filled its world.

It lowered into a defensive stance.

Not trying to escape.

Trying...

To protect its king.

The Sacred Seal cracked one more time.

Just enough...

For a low, primal growl to escape into the living world.

Every wolf—living or ash-born—fell silent.

The Alpha...

Had spoken.
