Miles away...

Cassian sat alone in his room.

**A Z R A T H**

Not aloud.

Only in his mind.

Somewhere beyond the reach of mortal hearing...

A voice answered.

Not with words.

With laughter.

Soft.

Patient.

Waiting.

The game had begun.
