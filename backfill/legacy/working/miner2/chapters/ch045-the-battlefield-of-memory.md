The following night...

Aidan stood where it had all begun.

The ruined battlefield.

The place where Human Aidan Darkheart had died.

The wind carried the scent of old blood.

Broken weapons still lay half-buried beneath the earth.

Cassian emerged from the darkness.

He wore no armor.

No crown.

Only the sword that had ended Aidan's human life.

The two men stood exactly where history had split apart.

Neither reached for a weapon.

Cassian broke the silence.

> **"Do you remember your last words as a human?"**

Aidan nodded slowly.

"...Every one of them."

Cassian looked at the ground.

"So do I."

He drew his sword.

Not to attack.

He planted it point-first into the earth between them.

> **"Then let's decide..."**

> **"...whether this curse ends with us..."**

> **"...or destroys everyone we love."**

The wind howled across the battlefield.

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf stood.

The Crimson Legacy remained closed.

For the first time...

It offered no guidance.

This decision belonged only...

To two kings.
