Cassian stood and looked over Black Hollow.

Children were still laughing in the streets below.

Families were returning home.

Lights glowed warmly from every window.

He closed his eyes.

"I spent centuries believing you would come here to destroy everything I built."

Aidan stood beside him.

"I won't."

Cassian looked at him.

"But the decree says—"

"The decree demands judgment."

Aidan's voice was calm.

"It doesn't command me to become a monster."

The Wolf listened from within Aidan's soul.

For the first time...

It did not speak.

Because its king already knew the path.

The Crimson King would fulfill his decree.

But he would do so...

Without abandoning the man he had become.

Far above them...

The crimson moon disappeared behind dark clouds.

Somewhere unseen...

Another player quietly watched.

Waiting.

Because Judgment Phase Two had begun...

But no one yet understood **how** fate intended to collect its debt.
