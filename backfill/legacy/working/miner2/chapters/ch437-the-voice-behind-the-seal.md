Inside Aidan's mind...

The Crimson Legacy did not appear.

Instead...

Darkness.

Endless darkness.

Then...

Footsteps.

Heavy.

Measured.

A silhouette emerged.

Towering.

Broad shouldered.

Its eyes glowed crimson and gold.

It stopped only a few feet away.

Neither spoke.

The creature simply stared at Aidan.

Then placed one massive hand against an invisible crimson wall.

A wall covered in ancient runes.

The Sacred Seal.

The wolf lowered its head.

Not in submission.

Recognition.

Then...

For the first time...

It spoke.

One word.

> **"...Brother."**

The vision shattered.
