The forest did not breathe.

Even after the Shadow Pack disappeared...

Something remained.

Watching.

Waiting.

Judging.

---

Aidan stood in the center of the clearing.

Still in his Crimson Wolf form.

His entire crimson coat shimmered beneath the fading Blood Moon.

His crimson-and-gold eyes swept across the ancient forest.

The danger...

Hadn't left.

---

Lyra slowly approached.

She rubbed her muzzle gently against Aidan's neck.

A silent question.

*Are you alright?*

The Crimson Wolf closed his eyes for only a heartbeat.

*I'm fine.*

But through the Wolf Authority...

She felt the truth.

He wasn't injured.

He was changing.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf stood motionless.

Its crimson eyes looked deeper into the endless forest.

**Something older woke up.**

The vampire nodded.

**I felt it too.**

*What is it?*

Neither answered.

For the first time...

Neither Authority knew.

---

A deep vibration rolled beneath the earth.

Not an earthquake.

A heartbeat.

One...

Massive...

Heartbeat.

**THUMP...**

The entire forest shook.

Leaves fell from trees.

Ancient branches cracked.

Every wolf in the Crimson Pack instinctively lowered their bodies.

Not from fear.

From instinct.

Something...

Far above them on the food chain...

Had awakened.

---

Rez's expression darkened.

"No..."

Mara looked at him.

"You know that sound?"

Rez nodded slowly.

"I prayed..."

"...I'd never hear it again."

---

Another heartbeat.

**THUMP...**

This time...

Blood began flowing from the old claw marks carved into the stones surrounding the clearing.

Not fresh blood.

Ancient blood.

Centuries old.

It seeped from the earth itself.

---

The Crimson Wolf stepped forward.

His nose lowered toward the crimson-stained ground.

The scent...

Was familiar.

Not because he had smelled it before.

Because...

His blood recognized it.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Founder Instinct**

**Blood Memory Activated**

---

No explanation followed.

Only the notification.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Images flashed.

Not memories.

Instincts.

A mountain covered in crimson mist.

Thousands of wolves kneeling.

A throne carved from crimson crystal.

A crown...

Resting upon no one's head.

The vision disappeared.

---

Aidan's breathing deepened.

*What was that?*

The wolf answered quietly.

**A place.**

The vampire added.

**A place every Crimson Alpha eventually finds...**

---

Rez suddenly shouted.

"AIDAN!"

The Crimson Wolf turned.

The ground behind him exploded.

A claw the size of a tree trunk erupted from beneath the earth.

Its skin was covered in ancient crimson scars.

Its claws were longer than spears.

It swung once.

Aidan leapt away just before the impact shattered the clearing.

The forest erupted into chaos.

---

A monstrous arm pulled itself from beneath the ground.

Then another.

Then another.

The earth split open.

An enormous creature slowly rose from the darkness below.

Not a wolf.

Not a beast.

Something older.

Its body looked as though it had been carved from crimson stone and living flesh.

Ancient runes covered its skin.

Its eyes...

Burned with molten gold.

It looked directly at Aidan.

Not at the pack.

Not at Rez.

Only...

The Founder.

---

The creature spoke.

Its voice echoed through the forest like thunder.

**...Crimson...**

Silence.

**...Blood...**

Another step.

The earth caved beneath its weight.

**...Has Returned...**

Malik instinctively moved beside Aidan.

Darius lowered his massive shoulders.

Kael's runes ignited across his wolf form.

Lyra bared her fangs.

The Crimson Pack stood together.

---

The creature ignored all of them.

Its molten-gold eyes never left Aidan.

Then...

To everyone's shock...

It slowly knelt.

The entire forest froze.

Even Rez looked stunned.

The ancient giant lowered its head before the Crimson Wolf.

Not in defeat.

In recognition.

Its ancient voice rumbled one final time.

**Welcome home...**

**...My King.**

The words echoed across the mountains.

Aidan stood motionless.

His crimson-and-gold eyes narrowed.

He had never seen this creature before.

Yet somehow...

Deep in his soul...

It didn't feel like a stranger.

And beneath the ancient forest...

Something even larger...

Shifted in its sleep.
