Cassian stood at the edge of the kingdom.

He could already see the crimson banners flying above the castle.

His feet refused to move.

His heart wanted to turn back.

His anger wanted to continue.

Then...

He heard children laughing from inside the city.

The sound reminded him of Black Hollow.

Of everything he had rebuilt.

Of everything he had lost.

His hand tightened around the burned ring.

The Darkheart Brand burned brighter than ever before.

A voice echoed inside him.

Not magical.

Not commanding.

Simply remembered.

> **"...You shall never know peace..."**

Cassian closed his eyes.

"...I understand now."

He opened them again.

The kindness that had begun returning to his face...

Was gone.

Not replaced by madness.

Replaced by resolve.

He stepped across the border.

The first step...

Toward the Third Judgment.
