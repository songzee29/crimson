Aidan landed before the plantation gates.

The air smelled different.

Ancient.

Heavy.

Royal.

For the first time since his rebirth...

His vampire instincts didn't whisper.

They roared.

This wasn't prey.

This was another king.

The massive iron gates slowly opened by themselves.

No servants.

No guards.

Just darkness.

A voice echoed from inside.

> **"Enter."**

Aidan walked forward without hesitation.

---

The throne room waited beyond.

Candles lit themselves one by one.

At the far end...

The Sovereign remained seated.

He didn't rise.

He simply observed.

"...Interesting."

His crimson eyes studied Aidan.

"...You smell of three destinies."

Aidan answered calmly.

"...And you smell..."

He looked around the room.

"...old."

Silence.

Then...

The Sovereign laughed.

A deep, genuine laugh.

For the first time in centuries...

Someone had spoken to him without fear.
