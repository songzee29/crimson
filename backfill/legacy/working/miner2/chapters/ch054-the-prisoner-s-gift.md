Far away...

Cassian awoke screaming.

His room was freezing.

The burned page lay untouched on the floor.

Except...

The word was gone.

**AZRATH**

Had disappeared.

As if it had never been written.

Cassian frowned.

"...No..."

He searched the room.

Nothing.

The whisper had vanished.

Completely.

For the first time in weeks...

His mind was silent.

He laughed.

"It's over..."

"...I finally got rid of it."

He had no idea...

The voice hadn't disappeared.

It had simply...

Stopped needing him.
