The water exploded.

A gigantic figure emerged from beneath the swamp.

Nearly twenty feet tall.

Its body was woven from roots, bones and black mud.

Human faces protruded from its chest.

Each face whispered the same words.

"Help..."

"Help..."

"Help..."

Lyra covered her ears.

The voices weren't sound.

They were memories.

The Guardian immediately recognized it.

His eyes widened.

"No..."

"It survived."

The creature looked directly at Aidan.

Then smiled.

Every stolen face smiled with it.

The Hollow King spoke.

Its voice sounded like hundreds of people talking at once.

> "Another child of Crimson..."

> "...has finally come home."

---

The battle began.

Immediately.

The Hollow King slammed one massive arm into the swamp.

A tidal wave of black water rushed toward them.

"Aidan!"

He didn't think.

He moved.

Hex Step.

Crimson Thread.

Shackles.

Everything flowed together.

Not perfectly.

Naturally.

He wasn't consciously choosing techniques anymore.

He was surviving.
