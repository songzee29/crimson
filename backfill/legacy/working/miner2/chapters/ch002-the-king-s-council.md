The council chamber slowly filled.

Lyra entered first, carrying a stack of reports.

Kael arrived next, still wearing his training armor.

Mara carried scrolls covered in new rune designs.

Darius brought reports from the Royal Guard.

Even Cassian had accepted the invitation, taking a place near the end of the table.

Not as a subject.

As an ally.

Aidan looked around the room.

"This..."

"...is the future of House Darkheart."

---

Lyra opened the first report.

"Our population has doubled."

Kael spoke next.

"The Crimson Knights now protect every major road."

Mara smiled.

"The Academy welcomed its first class of young witches yesterday."

Darius nodded.

"Crime has dropped across the kingdom."

Rez leaned back in his chair.

"...Sounds almost boring."

Everyone laughed.

---

Then...

A messenger burst through the doors.

"My King!"

The room instantly became serious.

"What is it?"

The messenger struggled to catch his breath.

"Three kingdoms..."

"...have requested an audience."

Silence.

Cassian looked toward Aidan.

"They've never done that before."

The messenger continued.

"They aren't asking for protection..."

"They're asking..."

"...to join House Darkheart."
