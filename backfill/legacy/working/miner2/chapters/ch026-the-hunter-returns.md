Three days.

Cassian never stopped walking.

He crossed rivers.

Mountains.

Forests.

Every step carried him closer to House Darkheart.

He wasn't running.

He wasn't raging.

He was thinking.

And somehow...

That frightened him more.

---

Inside his mind...

A single name echoed.

**Lyra.**

He tried to push it away.

It returned.

Again.

And again.

---

The Darkheart Brand pulsed.

Not with pain.

With memory.

The Final Decree whispered through his soul.

> **"...When peace returns..."**

> **"...judgment follows..."**

Cassian clenched his fists.

"...Enough."

But the decree never answered.

It simply...

Waited.
