Only one chain remained.

It stretched from Azrath's chest into the darkness itself.

Unlike the others...

This one wasn't made of iron.

It was made of words.

Ancient Royal Decrees.

Thousands of them.

Each written by a different ruler of House Darkheart.

Every judgment.

Every sacrifice.

Every promise.

All woven together.

Azrath stared at it.

His smile faded.

"...So."

"...this was yours."

His hand touched the chain.

It burned him.

For the first time...

He felt pain.

Then...

He smiled again.

"...Good."

He closed his eyes.

Somewhere...

Far above...

Cassian whispered one word in his sleep.

> **"...Azrath..."**

The final chain dissolved into ash.
