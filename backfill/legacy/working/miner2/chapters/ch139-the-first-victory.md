"Aidan!"

Lyra shouted as another wave of crimson fire raced toward him.

He didn't move.

Not yet.

The fire came closer.

Closer.

Then—

**Hex Step.**

He vanished.

Reappearing directly above the Living Curse.

Midair.

He extended one hand.

Not toward the flames.

Toward the crystal.

**Crimson Thread.**

One invisible thread connected his hand to the crystal.

The creature roared.

It finally noticed him.

Too late.

Aidan pulled.

The thread tightened.

The crystal cracked.

A second pull.

Another crack.

The Living Curse staggered.

People stared in disbelief.

The Guardian smiled.

"He figured it out."

One final pull—

The crystal shattered.

The flames froze.

Then...

Every crimson fire throughout the Quarter vanished at once.

Silence.

The only sound left was people breathing.

The Living Curse dissolved into glowing crimson ash.

Aidan landed hard on one knee.

Completely exhausted.

His arms shook.

His vision blurred.

But...

He smiled.

Not because he had won.

Because...

Nobody died.
