One of the shadowless children suddenly screamed.

Black smoke erupted from his mouth.

Ancient curse symbols exploded across the cathedral walls.

The Guardian shouted,

"Aidan!"

Aidan stepped forward calmly.

No panic.

No fear.

He lifted both hands.

His fingers moved with elegant precision.

Not rushed.

Not dramatic.

Beautiful.

Every movement completed an ancient witch pattern.

Then...

He spoke one word.

> **"Chains."**

The Fifth Crimson Law answered.

Hundreds of Crimson Chains erupted from glowing runes beneath the cathedral floor.

They wrapped around the curse itself—

Not the child.

The shadow screamed.

Trying to flee.

The chains tightened.

The little boy collapsed safely into Lyra's arms.

She looked at Aidan.

"...You separated them."

Aidan nodded.

"The child wasn't cursed."

"...His shadow was."

---

The Guardian smiled to himself.

Not because of Aidan's strength.

Because of his restraint.

Months ago...

Aidan would've destroyed everything.

Now...

He understood the difference between the victim...

And the curse.
