Three weeks passed.

Nothing happened.

No attacks.

No monsters.

No wars.

House Darkheart flourished.

Black Hollow prospered.

The Veil remained silent.

To everyone else...

The world had become peaceful.

The Crimson Legacy remained quiet.

Not because history had stopped.

Because history was gathering momentum.

---

Inside the throne room...

Aidan sat reviewing reports from every Darkheart territory.

Food supplies.

Training progress.

Trade routes.

New settlements.

The responsibilities of a king never truly ended.

Rez walked in carrying another stack of reports.

He sighed dramatically.

"...I miss when our biggest problem was surviving."

Aidan looked up.

"...Now our biggest problem is paperwork."

Rez laughed.

"I liked monsters better."

Even Aidan allowed himself a small smile.
