The First Generation had retired for the night.

Malik was still arguing with Darius about who had won their last spar.

Kael had returned to the barracks.

Mara disappeared into the library with an armful of books.

Lyra stood on the balcony overlooking House Darkheart.

Watching the lights below.

---

Aidan joined her.

Neither spoke.

Sometimes...

Silence was enough.

---

Rez stood several feet away in his Crimson Assassin body.

His crimson eyes wandered across the kingdom.

The people.

The walls.

The banners.

Something about all of it...

Felt familiar.

Too familiar.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

**...Do you ever feel like you've lived the same day twice?**

Aidan looked toward Rez.

*More often than I'd like.*

Rez chuckled.

**No...**

**I mean centuries apart.**

That answer made Aidan pause.

Rez suddenly stopped walking.

His breathing caught.

His hand rose to his temple.

"...Rez?"

Lyra immediately noticed.

"Aidan?"

"I'm fine."

He wasn't answering Lyra.

He was answering Rez.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

*What's wrong?*

Rez didn't answer.

He couldn't.

---

His body staggered.

The Crimson Assassin dropped to one knee.

The room instantly reacted.

Malik rushed forward.

"Rez!"

Kael reached for his sword.

"What happened?"

Mara immediately began examining the magic surrounding him.

"There isn't a curse..."

"There isn't a spell..."

"I don't understand."

---

Aidan was already beside him.

He placed one hand on Rez's shoulder.

"Rez."

The Crimson Assassin's breathing became uneven.

His crimson eyes lost focus.

Then...

Everything disappeared.

---

Darkness.

---

A battlefield.

Broken banners.

Crimson fire.

A younger Rez stood among countless warriors.

His eyes...

Were still closed.

Someone approached him.

Their face couldn't be seen.

Only their voice.

Calm.

Certain.

Ancient.

> **"One day..."**

> **"...he will awaken."**

The younger Rez lowered his head.

> **"And if I fail?"**

The voice answered without hesitation.

> **"Then stand again."**

Silence.

> **"And if I forget?"**

The answer came like law.

> **"Then remember your promise..."**

> **"...before you remember your name."**

The battlefield vanished.

---

Rez gasped.

His eyes flew open.

He was back inside House Darkheart.

Everyone surrounded him.

Concern filled every face.

He looked at Aidan.

Not with confusion.

Recognition.

His voice was quiet.

"...I remember."

The room became silent.

Aidan knelt beside him.

"What?"

Rez swallowed.

"I don't remember who he was..."

"I don't remember his face..."

"But I remember..."

"...why I stayed."

He slowly looked at his own hands.

"I chose this."

Everyone frowned.

Rez looked directly at Aidan.

"I wasn't trapped."

"I volunteered."

Silence.

"I chose to become your Legacy Guardian."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

*Why?*

Rez smiled sadly.

"I still don't know."

"But..."

"I remember making the promise."

Aidan couldn't stop thinking about the words Rez had remembered.

> **Remember your promise... before you remember your name.**

Who had spoken those words?

Why had Rez willingly become the Legacy Guardian?

And what kind of promise could survive centuries...

Even after memories had faded?

For the first time...

The mystery wasn't just about the Crimson Bloodline.

It was about the man who had stood beside the Crimson King all along.

And somewhere...

Buried beneath centuries of forgotten memories...

The rest of Rez's story...

Was waiting to be remembered.
