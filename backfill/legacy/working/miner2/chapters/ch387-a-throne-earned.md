The massive throne room doors opened.

The Guardian was already waiting.

Lyra stood beside him.

Malik.

The captains.

The elders.

Every member of House Darkheart's council.

For one long moment...

Nobody spoke.

Then Lyra crossed the room.

She didn't bow.

She didn't kneel.

She simply smiled.

"...Welcome home."

Aidan returned the smile.

Small.

Real.

"...I'm home."

The Guardian stepped forward next.

His eyes shifted to Rez.

For the first time in centuries...

The old warrior's composure cracked.

"...Rez..."

Rez chuckled.

"...Still ugly, old man."

The Guardian burst into laughter.

"You were asleep for centuries..."

"...and that's the first thing you say?"

"I had priorities."

The entire throne room laughed.

For just a moment...

House Darkheart wasn't preparing for war.

It was a family welcoming one of its own home.
