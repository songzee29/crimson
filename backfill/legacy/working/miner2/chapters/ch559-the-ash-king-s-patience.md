Deep inside the Ash Dominion...

The Ash King stood before the Hall of Names.

The Keeper approached quietly.

"My King..."

"The Crimson King grows stronger."

"I know."

"The Wolf Authority continues to synchronize."

"I know."

"The Veil is moving."

"I know."

The Keeper hesitated.

"...Should we stop them?"

The Ash King slowly shook his head.

"No."

"Why?"

**Lyra.**

It glowed softly.

Not fading.

Not yet.

The Ash King closed his eyes.
