The Ash King slowly descended the ancient steps.

No weapon rested in his hands.

No hostility.

Only presence.

When he stood before Aidan...

He looked past the Crimson King.

Toward Rez.

"...You survived."

Rez remained silent.

Then to Lyra.

"...You carry a burden."

Lyra instinctively touched Aidan's arm.

Finally...

His eyes settled on Cassian.

"...You are drowning."

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"...You don't know me."

The Ash King answered calmly.

> **"No..."**

> **"...but grief always wears the same face."**

The words struck Cassian harder than any blow.
