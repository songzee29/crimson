The Librarian was waiting.

As if she already knew.

She smiled when Aidan entered.

"So..."

"It finally accepted you."

Aidan placed the journal on the table.

"It writes."

"It observes."

"It remembers."

She gently ran one finger across the leather cover.

"Every Heir leaves behind a Legacy."

"But none of them write it."

"The Legacy writes them."

Aidan frowned.

"So Zee..."

The Librarian nodded.

"His journal still exists."

Aidan's eyes widened.

"Can I read it?"

"No."

"Why?"

"Because..."

She looked into his eyes.

"If you live through your journey by reading someone else's..."

"...then it stops being yours."

Silence.

Aidan slowly nodded.

He understood.
