Black Hollow had never been quieter.

Children laughed.

Families rebuilt homes.

The dead had been buried.

The missing had returned.

For the first time in centuries...

Cassian believed...

He had found peace.

He stood on the balcony overlooking the village he had rebuilt with his own hands.

His partner stepped beside him.

"You finally look happy."

Cassian smiled.

"...Maybe I am."

For one perfect moment...

The Darkheart Brand upon his arm did not burn.

It did not ache.

It was silent.

Cassian looked toward the sunrise.

"I think..."

"...it's finally over."

---

Hundreds of miles away...

Inside House Darkheart...

Aidan stopped walking.

The second heartbeat echoed through his soul.

**THUMP.**

The Wolf lifted its head.

Not with anger.

With certainty.

> **"...It begins."**

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

Not with a quest.

Not with a reward.

Only four words.

---

> **SECOND JUDGMENT ACTIVE**

---

Aidan stared at the page.

"...So the decree has chosen."

Rez stepped into the room.

"You felt it too?"

Aidan slowly nodded.

"...Cassian has found peace."

The room became silent.

Lyra looked between them.

"What does that mean?"

Aidan's expression grew heavy.

He remembered the words spoken long ago...

The Final Decree of House Darkheart.

The judgment was never about chasing Cassian.

It was about peace.

Whenever Cassian found it...

The decree demanded payment.

Aidan closed the Legacy.

His voice carried no triumph.

Only sorrow.

> **"Prepare the horses."**

Lyra searched his face.

"...Do you know where we're going?"

Aidan looked toward the northern horizon.

"...Black Hollow."

"...The decree has called."

Far away...

Cassian smiled at the life he had rebuilt.

He had no idea...

That destiny had already begun walking toward him.
