Aidan escaped the archive by pure luck.

Or maybe because Lyra guided him.

Either way, he burst from the maintenance door and slammed it shut behind him.

The moment it closed, silence returned.

The trail of glowing footprints vanished.

The hidden archive disappeared.

As if it had never existed.

Aidan collapsed against the wall.

His chest burned.

His shoulder still ached from the Keeper's touch.

Then he opened Lyra's folder.

Inside was a photograph.

The same girl.

Dark hair.

Gray hoodie.

Pale eyes.

But the photo wasn't normal.

Every face around Lyra had been scratched out.

Every student.

Every teacher.

Everyone.

Only Lyra remained untouched.

Written beneath the photo:

> SUBJECT 731

> ERASURE FAILED

Aidan's stomach dropped.

Failed.

Meaning someone had tried.

And failed.

Then another page slipped free.

A hand-drawn map.

At the bottom was an address.

An abandoned church near the oldest part of the city.

And written beneath it:

> If you're seeing this, I stayed hidden too long.

> Don't trust the ones who smile.
