The seventh night arrived.

The moon...

Turned crimson.

Every Blood Sovereign gathered.

Every ancient vampire kingdom watched.

Even the Veil Dominion waited in the shadows.

House Darkheart stood ready.

Aidan walked out of the castle wearing a long black coat.

The Crimson Crown rested upon his head.

Crimson markings pulsed beneath his skin.

Three steps behind him...

The Crimson Assassin followed in complete silence.

Its eyes...

Still closed.

It carried no fear.

No anger.

No emotion.

Only purpose.

Protect the Crimson King.

Aidan stopped at the castle gates.

He looked toward the northern horizon.

Where Valerius waited.

Inside his mind...

The Legacy gave one final notification.

---

> **ROYAL QUEST**

## **THE CROWN OF VALERIUS**

---

**Victory Condition**

Defeat the First Blood Sovereign.

---

**Failure Condition**

Death.

---

**Hidden Reward**

**King's Ascension II**

**???**

---

The notification vanished.

Aidan smiled.

Not with joy.

With anticipation.

Then...

He took one step forward.

The Crimson Assassin followed.

House Darkheart watched their king leave.

None of them knew whether he would return.

But every one of them believed the same thing.

If anyone could walk into the realm of ancient kings...

And leave wearing another crown...

It was the man the Crimson Legacy itself had begun calling...

**Its greatest heir.**
