Morning came to House Darkheart.

The celebration had ended.

The music was gone.

The banners still waved proudly above the Crimson Castle.

But...

Everything felt different.

Not because the kingdom had changed.

Because the world had.

---

Aidan stood alone on the highest balcony.

The Crimson Legacy floated before him.

It no longer looked like the book he had first opened all those years ago.

The cover had changed.

The old crimson leather had become darker.

Ancient silver runes wrapped around its spine.

A faint crimson aura pulsed from every page.

It had evolved.

Just as he had.

---

Behind him...

Footsteps echoed.

Rez walked onto the balcony carrying two cups of coffee.

"I figured kings still need caffeine."

Aidan laughed.

"I was hoping the Legacy would've unlocked unlimited energy."

Rez handed him a cup.

"Nope."

"Still human enough to be tired."

The two stood in silence, looking over House Darkheart.

Children ran through the streets.

Merchants opened their shops.

Knights trained in the courtyard.

For the first time...

This wasn't merely a castle.

It was the heart of a growing civilization.
