Hours passed.

Aidan crossed abandoned highways...

Forgotten forests...

Ancient battlefields.

The further north he traveled...

The heavier the air became.

Then...

His footsteps stopped.

The Crimson Assassin stopped at exactly the same moment.

Without opening its eyes...

It slowly turned its head toward the trees.

Something...

Was watching.

---

Aidan spoke calmly.

"...Come out."

Silence.

Then...

Dozens of figures stepped from the darkness.

Not hunters.

Not Sovereigns.

They wore no crest.

No armor.

Only black robes.

An old man stepped forward and bowed.

"We have waited..."

"...for House Darkheart."

Aidan remained silent.

"We are..."

"...the Keepers of the Crimson Road."
