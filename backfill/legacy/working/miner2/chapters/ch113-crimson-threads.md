Hours passed.

The swamp remained silent.

Then...

Something happened.

Not an explosion.

Not a surge of magic.

The air itself seemed to breathe.

Aidan opened his eyes.

Crimson threads stretched across the entire bayou.

Millions of them.

Connecting everything.

Trees.

Animals.

Water.

People.

Even himself.

The Witch Authority wasn't showing him magic.

It was showing him...

Connection.

He slowly reached toward one glowing thread.

The moment his fingers touched it—

He sensed Lyra laughing somewhere in the city.

Another thread.

Malik arguing with someone over a parking ticket.

Another.

Sienna sharpening a blade alone in a dim apartment.

He let go immediately.

His breathing quickened.

"...I can feel them."

The System responded.

---

**Curse Sense Evolved**

**New Function Unlocked**

### Crimson Thread Network

You may now weave temporary connections with those you trust.

Current Limit:
One active thread.

Warning:
Connections share more than distance.

---

Aidan frowned.

"What does that mean?"

The System didn't answer.

It simply recorded the new ability.

---

As dawn broke over New Orleans, the Guardian watched from across the water.

For the first time since meeting Aidan...

He allowed himself a genuine smile.

"The Witch..."

"...is finally beginning to awaken."

---

And deep below the city, inside a cathedral forgotten by history, the First Fang pulsed once more.

Not yet.

But soon.

Very soon.

The Crimson Cathedral would call its heir.

And when it did...

The Witch Arc would race toward the tragedy that no one in the Crimson Circle could yet imagine.
