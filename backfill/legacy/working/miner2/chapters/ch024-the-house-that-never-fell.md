Rain continued falling over New Orleans.

The city slept peacefully.

But beneath the city...

History had awakened.

---

Aidan stood once more inside the Sanctuary.

He hadn't entered through a door.

He hadn't summoned it.

He had simply...

Opened his eyes.

The Sanctuary had called him.

No Guardian.

No Lyra.

No echoes.

Only silence.

The Crimson Throne stood before him.

For the first time...

Aidan noticed something carved into the stone beneath it.

Ancient words.

Hidden beneath centuries of dust.

He knelt.

Slowly brushing the dust away.

The inscription revealed itself.

> **"A House does not die when its king falls..."**

A second line appeared beneath it.

> **"...it dies when its legacy is forgotten."**

Aidan whispered the words aloud.

"...House Darkheart..."

The Sanctuary answered.

Not with magic.

With memory.

The throne room changed.

Not into another place...

Into another time.
