The Ash King watched the battle without moving.

Then...

He smiled.

"You still don't understand."

Aidan cut down another echo.

"What?"

The Ash King looked toward Lyra.

Then toward the Wolf within Aidan.

Finally...

Back to Aidan.

His voice echoed through the Hall.

> **"Every king..."**

> **"...believes the greatest battle..."**

A pause.

> **"...is the one standing before him."**

His gray eyes dimmed.

> **"...It never is."**

Then he turned and began walking back toward his throne.

Before disappearing into the darkness, he spoke one final sentence.

> **"The day your queen falls..."**

> **"...the world you built..."**

> **"...will demand to know what kind of king survives it."**

Silence.

No one answered.

The words lingered in the Hall like a prophecy.

Lyra looked toward Aidan.

Aidan reached for her hand.

He squeezed it gently.

Neither of them spoke.

But both understood.

The road ahead would test every promise they had made.

And somewhere deep within the Crimson Legacy...

A blank page waited.

Not yet written.

Waiting for the chapter that would decide the fate of House Darkheart forever.
