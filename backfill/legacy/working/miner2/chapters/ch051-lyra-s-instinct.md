Inside the castle...

Lyra suddenly stopped reading.

Her heartbeat quickened.

She looked toward the window.

Someone...

Was watching.

She stepped onto the balcony.

The night was calm.

Nothing moved.

Still...

Her instincts refused to settle.

She quietly whispered,

"...Whoever you are..."

"...come out."

Only silence answered.

Far away...

Cassian lowered himself behind the rocks.

She hadn't seen him.

But somehow...

She had felt him.

For the first time...

His resolve wavered.

He remembered Aidan's words.

> *"Walk away."*

Cassian closed his eyes.

His feet turned toward the forest.

One more step...

And he could leave.

One more step...

And the Third Judgment would never happen.

He lifted his foot.

Then his gaze fell upon the burned ring still resting in his hand.

His home.

His peace.

His future.

Gone.

Slowly...

He lowered his foot.

Not toward the forest.

Toward House Darkheart.

The decision...

Was made.
