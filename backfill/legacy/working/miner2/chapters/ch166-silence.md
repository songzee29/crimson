No one moved.

The swamp...

Was still.

The birds had stopped singing.

The wind refused to blow.

Even the river seemed afraid to continue flowing.

It was as if the world itself...

Was mourning.

---

Cassian remained on his knees.

Aidan rested in his arms.

His head leaned against Cassian's shoulder.

His eyes...

Were closed.

The Crimson King looked...

Peaceful.

For the first time in months.

---

"...No..."

Cassian whispered.

His voice barely existed.

"...Get up..."

Nothing.

---

"...Fight me..."

Nothing.

---

"...Please..."

Silence.

---

Across the battlefield...

Lyra finally broke free.

She ran.

Faster than she ever had.

Barefoot across shattered stone.

Ignoring every cut.

Ignoring every scream from Malik behind her.

She collapsed beside Aidan.

"...No..."

Her hands shook violently as she grabbed his face.

"Aidan..."

No response.

---

She placed trembling fingers against his neck.

Nothing.

---

Against his chest.

Nothing.

---

His heart...

Was gone.

---

She let out a scream so full of grief that the swamp echoed with it for miles.

---

Malik arrived seconds later.

He looked at Aidan.

Then slowly fell to his knees.

"...Brother..."

He couldn't say anything else.

---

The Guardian finally reached them.

He didn't speak.

He simply removed his crown...

And lowered his head.

A king...

Had fallen.

---

Even Azrath stood silently beneath the trees.

Watching.

Not smiling.

Not celebrating.

Simply...

Watching.

"...So..."

he whispered.

"...this is where legends die."
