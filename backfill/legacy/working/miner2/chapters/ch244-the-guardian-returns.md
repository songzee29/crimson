Late that evening...

The warehouse door slowly opened.

Everyone jumped to their feet.

Lyra gasped.

"...Guardian?"

He looked terrible.

His clothes were torn.

His arm hung in a sling.

Deep scars covered the left side of his face.

He smiled weakly.

"I've..."

"...had better weeks."

Before anyone could say another word—

Aidan appeared in the doorway.

The Guardian looked at him.

Then froze.

"...You've changed."

Aidan simply answered,

"...So have you."

The Guardian studied him carefully.

Then sighed.

"The curse..."

Aidan nodded once.

"It's finished."

The Guardian closed his eyes.

"...I'm sorry."

Aidan looked away.

"So am I."

The Guardian noticed the empty chair.

He didn't ask.

He already knew.
