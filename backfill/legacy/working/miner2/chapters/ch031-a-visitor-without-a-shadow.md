Three days later...

A man entered New Orleans.

No one remembered seeing him arrive.

He simply...

Appeared.

He wore a charcoal-gray suit.

Black gloves.

His hair was neatly combed.

His smile...

Perfect.

The city guards approached.

"Sir..."

"We'll need your name."

The man smiled politely.

"I've had many."

He looked toward the cathedral.

"But today..."

"...I'm simply a traveler."

One guard frowned.

"Business?"

The man looked toward the skyline where Aidan stood speaking to children.

His smile widened.

"I came..."

"...to meet history."

---

No shadow followed him.
