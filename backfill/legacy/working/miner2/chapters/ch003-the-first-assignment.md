Three hours later...

The command center buzzed with activity.

Maps covered every wall.

Ancient relics rested in reinforced glass.

Hunters and witches worked side by side.

The Guardian stood before everyone.

"The operation officially begins tonight."

A large map lit up.

Red circles appeared across North America.

"These are the last major cursed territories."

Twenty-three locations.

Twenty-three ancient threats.

"The goal..."

He looked directly at Aidan.

"...isn't extermination."

Aidan nodded.

"Liberation."

The Guardian smiled.

"Exactly."

No more endless wars.

No more killing everything supernatural.

The mission had changed.

Save those who could be saved.

Destroy only what refused salvation.

That had become the Crimson King's law.
