Just before Aidan's fangs reached Cassian's neck...

A whisper drifted through the darkness of his mind.

Faint.

Almost gone.

> **"...Aidan..."**

His body froze.

Not because he understood.

Because something inside him recognized the voice.

Another whisper.

> **"...Don't..."**

Aidan's grip tightened.

Then...

Loosened.

Only for a second.

Enough.

Cassian drove both feet into Aidan's chest.

The force threw them apart.

Cassian hit the ground and rolled.

Panting.

He touched his neck.

No bite.

Just two tiny cuts where the fangs had grazed him.

---

Azrath narrowed his eyes.

"...What interrupted him?"

The Guardian stared at Aidan.

"...Someone..."

"...is reaching him."

He didn't know who.

No one did.

---

Aidan stood motionless.

His crimson eyes remained fixed on Cassian.

But for the first time...

They flickered.

Just once.

As if somewhere...

Deep beneath the hunger...

A fragment of the man named Aidan was still fighting to surface.

It wasn't enough to restore him.

Not even close.

But it was enough to stop one bite.

For now.

---

The swamp fell silent again.

Cassian breathed harder than he ever had before.

For the first time since becoming a vampire...

He understood a terrifying truth.

The man he had killed...

Had returned.

And somehow...

He was far more dangerous than before.
