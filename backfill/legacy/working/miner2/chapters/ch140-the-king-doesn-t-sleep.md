Another sleepless night.

Aidan stood alone on the palace balcony.

The city below glittered beneath the moon.

He remembered when he used to enjoy this view.

Now...

He only counted escape routes.

He only counted rooftops.

He only imagined where an enemy would strike.

He hadn't slept peacefully in months.

---

A voice interrupted his thoughts.

"You've changed."

The Guardian.

Aidan didn't turn around.

"So has the world."

The old man walked beside him.

"Not like this."

Silence stretched between them.

Finally...

The Guardian asked,

"When was the last time you laughed?"

Aidan searched his memory.

He couldn't answer.

The silence...

Answered for him.
