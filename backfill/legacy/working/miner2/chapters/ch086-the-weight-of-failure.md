The city had never been quieter.

No music.

No celebrations.

No children running through Jackson Square.

Only whispers.

Not hateful whispers.

Worried ones.

---

Aidan walked through the streets alone.

No royal escort.

No Circle.

Just...

A king carrying the weight of promises he couldn't keep.

People still smiled.

But it wasn't the same.

Now they looked at him with one question.

**Can you still save us?**

---

That night...

Aidan stood before the Crimson Throne.

He didn't sit.

He couldn't.

His hands rested against the stone.

"...I'm failing."

Silence.

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

No rewards.

No quests.

Only words.

> **EVERY KING FAILS.**

Aidan lowered his head.

"...Then why does this hurt so much?"

The Legacy answered.

> **BECAUSE YOU STILL CARE.**
