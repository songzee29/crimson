The staircase seemed to descend forever.

Each step echoed through the darkness with a hollow knock that sounded far too loud for such an ancient place. Dust drifted through the stale air, disturbed for the first time in generations.

Malik held up his phone.

"No signal..."

He frowned.

"...and my flashlight just died."

Lyra looked down at her own.

"So did mine."

Darkness swallowed the stairwell completely.

Only one thing remained visible.

The faint crimson glow surrounding Aidan.

Not bright.

Barely enough to illuminate the steps beneath his feet.

The System quietly appeared.

---

**《CRIMSON LEGACY》**

**Legacy Environment Detected**

**Crimson Witch Authority Resonance Increased**

---

Another line appeared.

---

**The Crimson remembers this place.**

---

Aidan frowned.

"What does that mean?"

No response.

The message faded.

Lyra glanced toward him.

"...Did it say something?"

Aidan nodded.

"It knows this place."

Malik laughed nervously.

"That's... somehow not comforting."

The three continued downward.

Five minutes.

Ten.

Twenty.

The staircase never ended.

Then—

Aidan stopped.

"What?"

Lyra whispered.

He pointed at the wall.

Ancient claw marks.

Hundreds of them.

Deep enough to cut through solid stone.

None of them looked human.

The further they looked...

The more claw marks appeared.

Some were fresh.

---

Malik swallowed.

"...Something lives down here."

Aidan shook his head slowly.

"No..."

His voice barely rose above a whisper.

"...Something hunted down here."
