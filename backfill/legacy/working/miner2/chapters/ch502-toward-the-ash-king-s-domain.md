Everything beyond it was gray.

Trees stood without leaves.

Rivers flowed black.

Ash drifted endlessly through the air.

The rescued boy pointed into the distance.

"...That's where they went."

Far beyond the valley...

Not built.

Grown.

Its towers looked as though they had been carved from solid ash.

Lightning flashed above it.

The Wolf stood inside Aidan's soul.
