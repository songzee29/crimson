Black Hollow no longer felt like home.

It felt...

Haunted.

Not because ghosts wandered its streets.

Because questions did.

Every sunrise...

Another person was gone.

No blood.

No broken doors.

No screams.

Only empty homes.

Empty beds.

Empty chairs.

Entire families vanished as though history itself had erased them.

---

Cassian stood in the village square.

The people who once smiled at him now looked at him with uncertainty.

One elder finally stepped forward.

"You promised we'd be safe."

Cassian lowered his eyes.

"...I know."

"You said the wars were over."

"...I know."

A mother stepped into the crowd, clutching a child's toy.

"My son..."

Her voice broke.

"...Find my son."

Cassian couldn't answer.

Because...

He didn't know where to begin.
