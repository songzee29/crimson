Rain began falling over New Orleans.

Aidan stood outside the cathedral, letting it soak through his clothes.

Something felt...

Wrong.

Not dangerous.

Empty.

As though the world had just taken its first breath after holding it for centuries.

He looked up.

Across the street...

A man stood beneath a black umbrella.

Perfect suit.

Perfect posture.

Perfect smile.

He wasn't hiding anymore.

He inclined his head ever so slightly.

Not as an enemy.

As an equal acknowledging another ruler.

Then he turned...

And disappeared into the crowd.

Aidan's heartbeat quickened.

For reasons he couldn't explain...

Every instinct told him the same thing.

The real war...

Had just begun.
