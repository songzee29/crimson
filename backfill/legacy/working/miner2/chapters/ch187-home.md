The gates of House Darkheart slowly opened.

No guards moved.

Every soldier simply stared.

Their king had returned.

Covered in blood.

Without a heartbeat.

---

Aidan crossed the courtyard.

Every Darkheart banner fluttered.

Almost...

As if bowing.

The massive doors to the throne room opened by themselves.

He entered.

Alone.

The throne waited.

Exactly where he left it.

Aidan stood before it.

His crimson eyes stared at the crown.

Minutes passed.

Then...

Without a word...

He sat.

The Crimson Throne accepted him.

The moment he touched it...

Every crimson rune throughout the castle ignited.

---

> **VAMPIRE AUTHORITY... STABLE**

---

> **KING STATUS... CONFIRMED**

---

> **HOUSE DARKHEART... RECOGNIZED**

---

A line no one had ever seen.

---

> **WELCOME HOME... KING DARKHEART**

---

The castle shook.

Not violently.

Respectfully.

As if something ancient...

Had finally recognized its rightful ruler.
