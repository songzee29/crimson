Before dawn...

The Crimson Assassin landed silently on the castle wall.

It never spoke.

It never needed to.

It knelt before Aidan.

Then raised one hand.

Images flowed directly into Aidan's mind.

Dense forests.

Broken campsites.

Cassian.

Watching House Darkheart from miles away.

Never sleeping.

Never entering.

Simply...

Watching.

Aidan looked toward the north.

"He's studying us."
