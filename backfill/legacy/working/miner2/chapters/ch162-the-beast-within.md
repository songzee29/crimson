The abandoned watchtower trembled.

Stone cracked beneath Aidan's feet.

His breathing had become uneven.

Slow...

Then fast.

Then slow again.

Like two different hearts were trying to claim the same body.

---

Rez didn't move.

He had seen battlefields.

He had seen kings fall.

He had seen monsters born.

But this...

This was different.

This was Aidan fighting himself.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Darkness.

Endless darkness.

Then...

Two voices.

One was smooth.

Cold.

Patient.

**Feed.**

The other rumbled like thunder through an ancient forest.

**Hunt.**

Aidan stood between them.

*No.*

The vampire laughed.

**You smell it... don't you?**

The scent of warm blood flooded his senses.

Every heartbeat from House Darkheart echoed through the night.

Not one.

Hundreds.

The wolf interrupted.

**Forget the city.**

**The forest.**

**There are predators waiting.**

**Run.**

**Chase.**

**Claim your territory.**

---

Aidan grabbed his head.

"No..."

His voice echoed across the empty hills.

"I decide."

---

The vampire whispered again.

**You're hungry.**

**You've denied me for too long.**

The wolf answered with a growl.

**You've caged me for too long.**

**Run with me.**

**Feel the earth beneath your feet.**

**Stop pretending you're only a king.**

---

Outside...

Crimson energy burst from Aidan.

Trees bent under the pressure.

The ground split.

The moon seemed brighter.

---

Rez finally spoke.

"Aidan."

No response.

"Aidan!"

Still nothing.

---

The Crimson Assassin closed his eyes.

Their Soul Link flared.

**Listen to me.**

Aidan heard him.

Barely.

*I'm trying...*

**Don't fight the voices.**

Aidan frowned.

*What?*

**Understand them.**

---

The vampire hissed.

**He's lying.**

The wolf snarled.

**He's afraid of us.**

Rez remained calm.

**No.**

**I'm afraid of what happens if you let either one rule alone.**

Silence.

---

Aidan slowly lowered his hands.

His breathing steadied.

Not because the hunger disappeared.

Because he finally listened.

The vampire wasn't evil.

It was hunger.

The wolf wasn't evil.

It was instinct.

Neither one was meant to wear the crown.

He was.

---

Then...

Something changed.

The wolf stepped closer.

No longer a voice.

A massive crimson wolf emerged from the darkness of Aidan's mind.

Its fur looked like flowing crimson smoke.

Its eyes burned with ancient intelligence.

It didn't bare its fangs.

It simply watched him.

The vampire's presence gathered into a tall shadow with glowing crimson eyes.

Silent.

Patient.

Watching.

For the first time...

Neither force attacked him.

They waited.

---

Aidan looked at them.

"So..."

"You've finally decided to show yourselves."

The wolf's voice shook the darkness.

**We were always here.**

The shadow spoke next.

**You finally stopped drowning us out.**

---

Outside the watchtower...

Aidan's body stopped shaking.

His eyes slowly returned to normal.

His fangs retracted.

The wild Crimson aura settled.

Rez let out a long breath.

"There you are."

Aidan opened his eyes.

"They're not trying to destroy me."

Rez smiled.

"I know."

"They're trying to be heard."

---

**Ding.**

The wolf tilted its head.

The vampire remained silent.

Aidan's crimson eyes gleamed.

"I don't serve either of you."

"I am the one who wears the crown."

The wolf lowered its head in acknowledgment.

The vampire smiled.

Not in defeat...

In respect.

For the first time...

The King...

The Vampire...
