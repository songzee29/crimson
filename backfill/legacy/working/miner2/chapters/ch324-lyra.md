That night...

Lyra found Aidan sitting on the rooftop.

He hadn't noticed she was there.

She quietly sat beside him.

"You've been avoiding me."

Silence.

"...Because I love you."

He finally looked at her.

His eyes were tired.

"...Exactly."

She frowned.

"What does that mean?"

Aidan looked toward the city.

"If something is happening to me..."

"...I don't want it happening to you."

Lyra's expression hardened.

She grabbed his hand.

Held it tightly.

"I don't care."

He tried to pull away.

She wouldn't let him.

"I choose you."

Silence.

"If you're walking into darkness..."

"...then you're not walking alone."

For the first time in days...

Aidan cried.

Not loudly.

Quietly.

His forehead rested against hers.

"I don't know what's happening."

She whispered...

"You don't have to."

"We'll figure it out."

Together.
