Three days later...

New Orleans gathered.

Not because the government asked them to.

Because they chose to.

The funeral wasn't held in a church.

It was held in Jackson Square.

The place Elias had spent years volunteering.

Thousands came.

Firefighters.

Paramedics.

Children he had rescued.

Families he had helped.

People whose names he never even knew.

One little girl placed a stuffed bear on the casket.

"You said heroes always come back."

Her mother quietly pulled her close.

Unable to answer.

---

The Circle stood together.

Dressed in black.

Aidan hadn't spoken in three days.

Not one word.

He simply stared at the casket.

The Journal opened in his coat pocket.

It wrote by itself.

---

> **Some people leave behind power.**

Another line.

> **Some leave behind kingdoms.**

Another.

> **Elias left behind people who learned how to hope because he lived.**

The page closed.
