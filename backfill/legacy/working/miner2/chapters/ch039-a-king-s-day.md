Meanwhile...

Aidan's day couldn't have been more different.

He spent the morning helping rebuild homes damaged during the last battle.

By noon...

He was carrying lumber alongside ordinary workers.

By evening...

He was eating beignets with Lyra, Malik and Sienna while children chased pigeons across Jackson Square.

Malik laughed.

"I still think you're terrible with a hammer."

Aidan rolled his eyes.

"It worked."

"The roof is crooked."

"It has character."

Everyone laughed.

Even Lyra.

She looked at Aidan.

Not the Crimson King.

Just...

Aidan.

For a brief moment...

The world felt normal.

Cassian watched from another rooftop.

His expression never changed.

Below him...

People laughed with the man he hated most.

The whisper returned.

> **"Look carefully."**

Cassian didn't answer.

> **"That life..."**

> **"...should have been yours."**
