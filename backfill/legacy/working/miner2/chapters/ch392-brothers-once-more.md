For a while...

Neither spoke.

The silence between them wasn't awkward.

It was familiar.

Finally, Rez looked across the kingdom.

"I missed all of this."

His voice grew quieter.

"I missed watching House Darkheart grow."

"I missed Zee."

"I missed everyone."

Aidan didn't offer false comfort.

He understood loss better than most.

Instead...

He spoke honestly.

"You didn't choose to leave."

Rez nodded slowly.

"No."

Aidan turned toward him.

"But you chose to come back."

Rez smiled.

"And I'd do it again."

The two clasped forearms.

Not as king and servant.

Not as commander and soldier.

As brothers bound by House Darkheart.
