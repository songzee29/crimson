The rain finally began falling again.

Slowly.

Softly.

The crimson storm still circled above New Orleans.

Elias lay motionless in Aidan's arms.

His blood had become part of the street.

No one moved.

Not Lyra.

Not Malik.

Not Sienna.

Not even the Veil.

The entire battlefield waited.

Aidan slowly lowered Elias to the ground.

With more care than anyone had ever seen him use.

He closed Elias' eyes.

Straightened his jacket.

Then...

He stood.

His movements were calm.

Too calm.

He turned.

Looking directly at Cassian.

For the first time...

The two men truly saw each other.

Cassian saw the tears.

Aidan saw the regret.

Neither mattered anymore.

The Witch Authority pulsed beneath Aidan's skin.

Not exploding.

Listening.

Waiting.

Aidan's crimson eyes never left Cassian.

When he finally spoke...

His voice was almost a whisper.

> "Look at him."

Cassian didn't move.

Aidan took one step forward.

The ground beneath his feet blackened with crimson runes.

> "Look..."

> "...at what your choice has done."

Cassian slowly looked at Elias.

His breathing became uneven.

He couldn't deny it.

He couldn't explain it.

He couldn't fix it.

Aidan nodded once.

Almost as if confirming something to himself.

Then...

The System appeared.

Broken.

Glitching.

Barely functioning.

It tried to issue another emergency protocol.

The screen shattered before finishing.

Aidan didn't even look at it.

Instead...

He looked at Cassian and spoke words that would echo for the rest of the series.

---

> **"Hear my decree."**

The entire city seemed to hold its breath.

Even the Veil hunters lowered their weapons.

The Witch Authority responded.

Not with power.

With silence.

Aidan continued.

> **"From this night forward..."**

> **"...I do not forgive you."**

Another step.

> **"I do not hate you..."**

Cassian's eyes narrowed.

That wasn't what he expected.

Aidan's voice became colder.

> **"...Hatred fades."**

> **"...I won't allow myself that mercy."**

The rain began turning crimson as it touched the ground.

Every witch present felt the words resonate through their magic.

> **"Instead..."**

> **"...I will remember."**

Another step.

> **"Every breath you take..."**

> **"...every victory you celebrate..."**

> **"...every sunrise you live to see..."**

> **"...will exist only because I have not yet decided to take it from you."**

Cassian's hand slowly tightened around his sword.

Not in anger.

In acceptance.

Aidan's final words fell over the ruined street like a sentence carved into stone.

> **"Live."**

A long silence followed.

Then...

> **"Live long enough to understand exactly what you stole from me."**

> **"When that day comes..."**

His crimson eyes glowed brighter than ever before.

> **"...I will come for you."**

> **"Not out of revenge."**

> **"...But because some debts can only be answered."**

The Witch Authority erupted once.

Not violently.

Like a king's seal being pressed into wax.

The decree was spoken.

The Legacy recorded it.

---

**《CRIMSON LEGACY》**

**Oath Recorded**

---

**The Crimson Decree**

**Status: Irrevocable**

---

Cassian stood in complete silence.

He did not answer.

Because deep inside...

He knew.

This wasn't the promise of an angry young man.

It was the birth of a destiny neither of them could escape.

### Why I like this version

I made one deliberate change.

Instead of Aidan saying:

> **"I'm going to kill you."**

He says something far colder.

> **"Live."**

That line becomes a curse in itself.

Cassian is forced to carry the weight of what happened. Every time they meet afterward, those words are still hanging between them. It also fits the theme we've been building: Aidan doesn't become consumed by blind revenge in the Witch Arc. He makes a conscious decree that will shape the rest of his life.

Then, much later in the Vampire Arc—after a century of grief, bloodlust, and a humanity switch turned off—that decree can become even more frightening because readers will remember the quiet promise that started it all.
