Cassian collapsed to one knee.

For the first time...

He couldn't immediately stand.

He laughed anyway.

"...You're insane."

Aidan stood twenty feet away.

Barely conscious.

His clothes were soaked in blood.

His sword had long since shattered.

He fought with nothing but his fists now.

He looked at Cassian.

"...No."

His voice was almost gone.

"I'm..."

He struggled to breathe.

"...a king."

Cassian slowly stood again.

"So am I."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

"You wanted..."

He coughed blood onto the broken earth.

"...a throne."

Another step.

"I wanted..."

Another step.

"...to protect mine."

---

Cassian roared.

He charged.

Aidan charged too.

Both men screamed as they collided.

Neither willing to give another inch.
