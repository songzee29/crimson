Far across the Atlantic...

Beneath the ruins of an ancient city forgotten by history...

A throne carved from black crystal cracked.

A single crimson eye opened.

Unlike the Blood Sovereigns...

This being carried no scent of vampire.

No scent of witch.

No scent of wolf.

Only Authority.

A voice echoed through the ruins.

"...The Blood Sovereigns..."

"...have crowned another."

Silence.

"...Then I shall test him myself."

The throne room trembled.

Ancient chains snapped.

One...

Then another.

Then another.

A war that had slept for centuries...

Had begun moving again.
