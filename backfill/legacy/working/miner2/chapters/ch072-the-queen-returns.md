Lyra's body rose gently from Aidan's arms.

Crimson runes spiraled around her.

Her heartbeat had stopped.

Now...

Something else began.

**THUMP.**

Not the heartbeat of a vampire.

Not the heartbeat of a wolf.

Not the heartbeat of a witch.

A new heartbeat.

The heartbeat of the Crimson Bloodline.

Her wound slowly closed.

The blood vanished.

Life returned to her skin.

The crimson light surrounding her became brighter.

Then...

Everything stopped.

She slowly opened her eyes.

They weren't the same.

Within them burned crimson light laced with silver and gold.

She took her first breath.

A calm...

Steady...

Living breath.

She looked down at Aidan.

"...You..."

Aidan laughed through tears.

"I told you..."

"...I'm not losing you."

Lyra smiled weakly.

"You never listen."

"I know."

She reached out.

He took her hand immediately.

Neither of them spoke.

They didn't need to.
