The howl ended.

Silence followed.

Aidan slowly lowered his head.

His breathing had changed.

His pupils were now vertical.

Long black claws extended from every finger.

Silver veins spread beneath his skin.

His fangs had grown longer.

His muscles became denser.

Not a full transformation.

Not yet.

But enough.

The beast that had attacked him...

Stepped backward.

Fear.

Real fear.

It recognized something older than itself.

Something greater.

It lowered its head.

Not to Aidan.

To the Authority sleeping inside him.

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf stood behind the cracked seal.

It looked directly at him.

Then spoke clearly.

> **"I am your Third Authority."**

A pause.

> **"I have waited since the day you died."**

Another crack spread across the Sacred Seal.

Not enough to free it.

Enough...

To let its voice reach its king.

Outside...

Rez smiled.

He could feel it.

The final Authority had begun answering its rightful master.

And for the first time since Aidan became the Crimson King...

The road toward **True Crimson Blood** no longer felt distant.

It had begun.
