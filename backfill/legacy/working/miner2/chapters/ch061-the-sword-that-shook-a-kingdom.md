The doors exploded inward.

Stone shattered across the chamber.

A crimson blur crossed the room.

Aidan.

"CASSIAN!"

Cassian's head snapped toward the voice.

For one fraction of a second...

Time stopped.

His sword was already moving.

Aidan reached for Lyra.

Lyra reached for Aidan.

Neither could reach the other in time.

Steel flashed through the room.

**SHHHHK!**

Silence.

The rain outside was the only sound.

Cassian froze.

His sword trembled in his hands.

Aidan stopped only inches away.

His crimson eyes widened.

"No..."

Lyra looked down.

A thin line of crimson spread across her dress.

One drop.

Then another.

Then—

Blood poured onto the stone floor.

The room seemed to lose all sound.

Lyra slowly lifted her hand to the wound.

Her fingers became stained crimson.

She looked at Cassian.

There was no hatred in her eyes.

Only sadness.

"You..."

She struggled for breath.

"...still had a choice..."

Cassian staggered backward.

His sword slipped from his fingers.

It crashed onto the floor.

"...No..."

His voice cracked.

"...No..."

"I..."

"I didn't..."

He stared at the blood.

He had imagined revenge for weeks.

For months.

But now that it had happened...

There was no satisfaction.

Only horror.
