Deep within the forest...

Cassian knelt beside a blackened clearing.

The trees hadn't burned.

The grass hadn't withered.

Only the center of the clearing...

Had turned into crimson ash.

He picked some up.

It dissolved before reaching his fingertips.

"No scent..."

"No blood..."

"No magic..."

His partner looked around nervously.

"What is it?"

Cassian slowly stood.

"...A message."

"From who?"

He looked toward the mountains.

"...I don't know."

Hidden high above...

The hooded figure watched without moving.

Not a sound.

Not a breath.

Only observation.
