The battle erupted.

Echoes charged from every direction.

Steel clashed.

Magic ignited.

The Hall shook.

Aidan fought at the center.

Not chasing glory.

Holding the line.

Each time an echo slipped past...

He intercepted it.

Each time Lyra was threatened...

He moved without hesitation.

Each time one of his companions staggered...

He was there.

Inside his soul...

The Wolf suddenly walked through one of the cracks in the Sacred Seal.

Not fully free.

One paw.

Then another.

It now stood with half its body beyond the barrier.

It looked at Aidan.

Its voice carried absolute certainty.

> **"...Soon..."**

> **"...King..."**

> **"...Very soon..."**

Aidan could feel it.

The final awakening was drawing closer.

Not today.

But no longer far away.
