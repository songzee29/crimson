The doors of House Darkheart exploded open.

Not from an attack.

From the inside.

Stone cracked.

Iron bent.

The palace guards instinctively reached for their weapons.

Then froze.

Aidan stepped into the courtyard.

His crimson eyes swept across everyone.

No smile.

No anger.

Nothing.

The soldiers slowly lowered their swords.

Not because they trusted him.

Because something inside them understood...

The king standing before them...

Was no longer human.

Not to command.

To record.

Aidan looked beyond the castle walls.

Thousands of heartbeats.

Every street.

Every alley.

Every living creature.

He could hear them all.

His head slowly tilted.

The city...

Sounded alive.

Hungry.

---

Then...

He disappeared.
