Cassian stared at his own hands.

They were covered in Aidan's blood.

The Darkheart Brand glowed brightly across both forearms.

Burning.

Not painfully.

Heavier than before.

Like chains no one else could see.

---

His breathing became uneven.

"I..."

He looked at Aidan again.

"...I won."

The words sounded hollow.

Empty.

Wrong.

---

He waited.

For satisfaction.

For relief.

For triumph.

Nothing came.

---

Only...

Emptiness.

---

"...Why..."

A tear rolled down his face.

"...doesn't it feel like I won?"

---

No one answered.
