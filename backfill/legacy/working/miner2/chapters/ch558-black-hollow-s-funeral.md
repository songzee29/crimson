Black Hollow.

The last of the victims who couldn't be saved were laid to rest.

The village gathered beneath gray skies.

Cassian stood before the graves.

His voice was barely above a whisper.

"I failed you."

No one answered.

The wind carried only silence.

After the ceremony...

Cassian remained alone.

His partner found him.

"You can't blame yourself forever."

Cassian looked at the graves.

"...Watch me."

The Darkheart Brand pulsed once beneath his skin.

Not with pain.

With sorrow.

The curse wasn't speaking.

It was waiting.
