One quiet night...

The Circle gathered on the warehouse roof.

Malik had somehow burned hamburgers.

Again.

Everyone laughed.

Lyra looked at Aidan.

"So..."

"You ever gonna tell me?"

"What?"

"You love this city."

"I do."

"No."

She smiled.

"I mean..."

"...me."

Silence.

Aidan blinked.

"I..."

Before he could finish...

Lyra stepped forward.

Placed one hand against his face.

And kissed him.

Not dramatic.

Not passionate.

Gentle.

Patient.

Like she'd waited years.

When she stepped back...

She smiled.

"Took you long enough."

For the first time in years...

Aidan laughed so hard he nearly cried.

Below them...

The city lights shimmered.

Above them...

The cracked Vampire Seal pulsed again.
