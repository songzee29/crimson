Cassian stood on the roof of an old hotel.

The entire city stretched beneath him.

Explosions.

Smoke.

Chaos.

Behind him, one of his officers approached.

"Commander."

"The eastern district is secured."

"The western district?"

"Resistance is heavier than expected."

"And the Crimson Circle?"

"They split up."

Cassian closed his eyes.

Exactly as he expected.

One by one.

Separated.

Vulnerable.

The officer hesitated.

"...Your brother has been located."

Silence.

Cassian didn't move.

"...Where?"

"French Quarter."

Another pause.

"He is protecting civilians."

Cassian looked toward the burning skyline.

"I know."

The officer quietly asked,

"Should we engage?"

Cassian's answer came immediately.

"...No."

The officer looked surprised.

"Continue the operation."

"I'll handle him myself."
