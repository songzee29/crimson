Inside the cathedral...

Azrath placed another object on the altar.

Not blood.

A black crystal.

It pulsed once.

Like a heartbeat.

Cassian frowned.

"What is that?"

"A prison."

Cassian looked confused.

"A prison?"

Azrath nodded.

"For your humanity."

Silence.

Cassian laughed.

"I don't need humanity."

Azrath's smile disappeared.

"Oh..."

"...you will."

He slowly picked the crystal back up.

"But not yet."
