That evening...

Aidan and Rez sat together inside Aidan's mind.

For the first time in days...

Neither discussed training.

Neither discussed the Legacy.

Aidan asked a simple question.

*"Before Zee..."*

*"Who were you?"*

Rez became unusually quiet.

"I..."

"...don't remember everything."

"The Legacy sealed parts of my memories long before you were born."

Aidan frowned.

*"Even from you?"*

Rez nodded.

"The Legacy protects everyone."

"Even me."

He looked away.

"I remember becoming the Crimson Assassin."

"I remember standing beside Zee."

"I remember making a promise."

"But before that..."

He shook his head.

"...it's just fragments."

Aidan smiled.

"Then one day..."

"We'll uncover them together."

Rez looked at him.

Then smiled.

**"I'd like that."**
