The Guardian stood before the Wall of Names inside the Sanctuary.

Something...

Was wrong.

He couldn't explain it.

For centuries...

The Sanctuary had felt peaceful.

Now...

It felt as if someone was breathing just outside its walls.

He placed one hand against the stone.

The crimson runes flickered.

Then...

One rune turned black.

Only for a second.

The Guardian's heart stopped.

"...Impossible."

He immediately checked the prison seals recorded within the Legacy.

Every one appeared intact.

Every one...

Except one.

The smallest seal.

It wasn't broken.

It was...

Worn.

As though time itself had begun erasing it.
