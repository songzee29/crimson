Six figures stepped from the shadows.

Black combat uniforms.

Silver masks.

Each carried a different weapon.

A spear.

Twin blades.

Chains.

A war hammer.

A bow.

And at the center...

A young man with a sheathed sword.

He couldn't have been much older than Aidan.

His posture was relaxed.

Almost lazy.

But his eyes...

Never left Aidan.

One of the Veil hunters stepped forward.

"By order of the Veil Council..."

He unrolled an ancient scroll.

"...Aidan of the Crimson Bloodline..."

"...you are ordered to surrender the Legacy."

Silence.

Malik laughed.

"...Yeah."

"No."

The Veil hunter rolled the scroll back up.

"I expected that."

He looked toward the young swordsman.

"Proceed."

---

The swordsman didn't move.

He simply looked at Aidan.

Studying him.

Then quietly asked...

"Did you save everyone?"

Aidan frowned.

"...What?"

"The civilians."

"They all lived?"

"...Yes."

The young man nodded once.

"Good."

He turned toward the Veil squad.

"Leave."

Every hunter stared.

"What?"

"I said..."

His voice became ice.

"...leave."

The commander frowned.

"Those aren't our orders."

The young swordsman finally looked at him.

"...And?"

The commander hesitated.

Everyone in the squad slowly stepped backward.

Without another word...

They disappeared into the swamp.

Leaving only Aidan...

And the mysterious swordsman.
