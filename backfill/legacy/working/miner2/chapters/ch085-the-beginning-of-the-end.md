That night...

Aidan finally returned to New Orleans.

Exhausted.

Covered in dust.

Unable to save everyone.

The little girl from Jackson Square ran toward him.

"Mister King!"

He forced a smile.

"Hey..."

She looked up at him.

"...My aunt was in one of those villages."

Aidan froze.

She wasn't crying.

She genuinely believed he had saved her.

"Did you find her?"

Silence.

Aidan couldn't answer.

The girl slowly lowered her head.

"...Oh."

She walked away.

Not angry.

Just...

Disappointed.

Aidan stood completely still.

That hurt more than any wound he'd ever suffered.

---

High above the city...

Azrath watched the little girl disappear into the crowd.

He quietly whispered,

"...Now..."

"...you're beginning to understand."

He wasn't talking to Aidan.

He was talking to Cassian.

Standing beside him.

Watching the Crimson King break...

Without a single sword being drawn.

Cassian looked at Aidan below.

For the first time since Elias died...

He didn't see a king.

He saw...

A man.

One who could fail.

One who could bleed.

One who could be beaten.

A slow smile spread across Cassian's face.

Azrath noticed.

He said nothing.

He simply looked toward the moon.

The hook had set.

Now...

He only needed to reel it in.
