That evening...

Aidan stood before hundreds gathered in Jackson Square.

People had heard the rumors.

Fear spread through the crowd.

One woman shouted,

"Did you abandon those villages?"

Another voice joined.

"Are we next?"

The whispers grew louder.

Aidan slowly raised one hand.

Not to cast a Law.

Just to ask for silence.

The crowd obeyed.

"I can't promise..."

He looked across every face.

"...that I will always arrive in time."

Silence.

"I can't promise no one else will ever be hurt."

Another silence.

"But I can promise this..."

His voice carried across the square.

"If you call for House Darkheart..."

"...I will come."

No speech.

No magic.

Just a promise.

The crowd erupted.

Hope returned.

---

Far away...

Azrath watched from a rooftop.

His smile never faded.

"...Excellent."

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

Azrath answered without taking his eyes off Aidan.

"He just made a promise."

Cassian didn't understand.

"So?"

Azrath's smile widened.

"The bigger the promise..."

"...the greater the disappointment."

He finally looked at Cassian.

"And disappointment..."

"...creates monsters."

Cassian's heartbeat slowed.

For the first time...

He began seeing the world...

The way Azrath did.

Not as people.

As pieces.
