The traveler sat quietly inside a small café.

Outside...

Life continued.

Children laughed.

Music played.

People danced.

He stirred his tea.

Slowly.

Patiently.

A waitress smiled.

"Anything else, sir?"

He looked up.

His smile never changed.

"No."

"I've already found what I came for."

She nodded politely.

Then walked away.

She never noticed...

Her reflection in the window wasn't smiling back.
