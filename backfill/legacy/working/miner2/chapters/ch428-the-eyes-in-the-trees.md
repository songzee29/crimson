Night fell over Black Hollow.

Cassian refused to stop searching.

His partner pleaded with him.

"You need to rest."

"...Later."

"You've said that for five days."

"...Later."

He walked deeper into the forest.

Then...

He felt it.

Someone was watching.

He spun.

Nothing.

Silence.

Then...

Between two ancient trees...

A pair of crimson eyes appeared.

Not glowing with bloodlust.

Watching.

Calmly.

Cassian lunged.

The figure didn't run.

It simply stepped backward...

And vanished into the darkness.

Leaving behind...

Another handful of crimson ash.

Cassian stopped.

His breathing became heavy.

He whispered into the empty forest.

"...Who are you?"

No answer came.

Only the wind.

Far away...

Unknown to Cassian...

Aidan looked toward the same northern sky from the balcony of House Darkheart.

Rez stepped beside him.

"You feel it too."

Aidan nodded once.

"...Something is calling."

The Crimson Legacy remained silent.

Not because it was hiding the truth.

Because...

The answer had not yet reached House Darkheart.

But it was coming.

With every disappearing family...

With every trail of crimson ash...

With every step the unseen figure took...

History moved closer to the moment when the Crimson King would be forced to leave the safety of his throne...

And answer a mystery that threatened not only Black Hollow...

But the balance of the entire supernatural world.
