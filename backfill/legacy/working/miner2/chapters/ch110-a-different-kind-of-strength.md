Dawn.

Cassian stood beside a small stream.

Blood stained his hands.

Not human blood.

A deer's.

Its body lay nearby.

Completely drained.

Cassian stared at his reflection.

His eyes...

Had changed.

For a heartbeat...

They glowed a deep crimson.

Then faded.

He whispered,

"...What am I?"

Azrath's voice came from behind him.

"The beginning."

Cassian turned.

"I killed it."

Azrath nodded.

"You fed."

"I lost control."

"You survived."

Cassian looked down at the lifeless deer.

"I never wanted this."

Azrath walked beside him.

"No."

"You wanted power."

He looked toward the rising sun.

"This..."

"...is its price."
