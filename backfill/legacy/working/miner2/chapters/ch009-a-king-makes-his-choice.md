Aidan looked at Cassian.

Then toward the village.

Children laughed in the distance.

Families gathered around warm fires.

People who had nothing to do with the hatred between two men.

He clenched his fists.

"No."

Everyone looked at him.

"I will not let innocent people pay for a decree I wrote."

The Crimson Legacy glowed.

It did not reject his words.

Instead...

A new line appeared.

---

> **Royal Interpretation Acknowledged**

---

> **The Judgment must be fulfilled.**

> **The innocent need not bear its cost.**

Rez's eyes widened.

"You changed it..."

Aidan slowly shook his head.

"No."

"I understood it."

He looked at Cassian.

"The decree was never about revenge."

"It was about judgment."

Cassian met his eyes.

For the first time since Aidan's death...

Neither of them saw an enemy standing before them.

Only two kings...

Trying to keep their people from repeating the mistakes of the past.

Far away...

Hidden in the darkness...

A cloaked figure watched the fire between them.

The figure whispered with quiet satisfaction.

> **"Good..."**

> **"The Crimson King has finally learned..."**

> **"...that true power is not writing a decree."**

> **"...It is carrying its weight."**

The Second Judgment had begun.

But how it would be fulfilled...

Had yet to be decided.
