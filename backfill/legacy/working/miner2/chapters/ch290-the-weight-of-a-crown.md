Darkness.

Absolute silence.

Then...

Voices.

Thousands of them.

Every life Aidan had taken echoed around him.

Every enemy.

Every innocent casualty of war.

Every decision.

Every drop of blood.

Not to judge him.

To ask one question.

> **"Would you do it again?"**

The bridge waited.

Aidan answered without hesitation.

> **"...Yes."**

Not proudly.

Not angrily.

Simply...

Truthfully.

The voices vanished.

The bridge accepted his answer.
