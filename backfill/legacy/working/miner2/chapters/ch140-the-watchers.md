Far beyond House Darkheart...

High upon a frozen mountain...

Three cloaked figures overlooked the kingdom from an enchanted mirror.

The first folded his arms.

"So..."

"The Founder has begun."

The second nodded.

"The First Generation has awakened."

The third remained silent.

Watching.

Studying.

Finally...

The oldest among them spoke.

"Do we move?"

The leader slowly shook his head.

"No."

"Not yet."

"He still believes..."

"...he has time."

The mirror shifted.

Showing Aidan laughing with Malik.

Training beside Kael.

Speaking with Lyra.

Standing shoulder to shoulder with Rez.

The leader smiled.

"Enjoy this peace, Crimson King."

"Because once we step into your world..."

"...everything changes."

The mirror went dark.

Back in House Darkheart...

Aidan looked toward the setting sun.

For reasons he couldn't explain...

He felt as though someone...

Somewhere...

Had just made a decision.

Inside his mind...

Rez spoke quietly.

**I felt it too.**

Aidan nodded.

*Then let's make sure we're ready.*

The wind swept across the Crimson Kingdom.

The age of peace...

Was quietly coming to an end.
