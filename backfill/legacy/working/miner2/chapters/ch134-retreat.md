Cassian wiped blood from his mouth.

He looked at Aidan.

Then...

For the first time...

He stepped backward.

Not out of fear.

Out of calculation.

Azrath had already taught him.

Don't win the battle.

Win the war.

Cassian smiled.

"...Next time..."

"...I'll finish what I started."

He disappeared into the swamp.

Aidan took one step after him.

Then another.

Then another.

His body wanted to chase.

To finish it.

To kill him.

His hand instinctively began forming another Witch Law.

Then...

He stopped.

Lyra's voice echoed in his memory.

> **"You're still you, Aidan."**

His hand trembled.

The runes disappeared.

He fell to one knee.

The rain washed blood from his hands.

He looked down at his reflection in the water.

For a split second...

He didn't recognize the man staring back.

---

Far away...

Cassian stood atop a cliff overlooking the swamp.

His broken jaw had already healed.

He looked back one last time.

Smiled.

Then reached into his coat.

He pulled out another small crimson vial.

The first one had only opened the door.

Azrath's voice drifted from the darkness behind him.

> **"Soon..."**

> **"...you won't need those anymore."**

Cassian uncorked it.

Without hesitation...

He drank.

The night answered with thunder.

And somewhere deep inside House Darkheart...

The Crimson Legacy turned a page by itself.

One line slowly wrote itself in crimson ink:

> **WARNING**

> **THE HUNTER HAS TASTED DEATH...**

> **THE PREY HAS BEGUN TO HUNGER.**
