---

The hunt had begun.

And for the first time since finding the Crimson Book, Aidan truly understood what that meant.

Before, everything had felt distant.

The System.

The hidden world.

The erased people.

The mysteries.

They were terrifying, but they were still ideas.

Stories.

Questions.

Now there was a face attached to it.

Or rather...

the absence of one.

The man in the black coat.

The scout.

The thing that had smiled at him despite having no face at all.

And according to Lyra...

he wasn't even important.

He was only the one sent ahead.

---

The underground station sat in complete darkness.

Old tracks disappeared into black tunnels.

Water dripped steadily from broken pipes overhead.

The city above felt a thousand miles away.

Aidan sat against a rusted pillar.

Trying to process everything.

Trying not to panic.

Failing.

---

"The Veil."

The name felt heavy in his mouth.

Dangerous.

Like saying it too loudly might summon something.

Lyra sat across from him near an old maintenance lantern.

Its dim flame painted tired shadows across her face.

She looked older tonight.

Not physically.

Emotionally.

Like someone who had been running for a very long time.

---

"How many are there?"

Aidan asked.

---

Lyra laughed softly.

Not because the question was funny.

Because it wasn't.

---

"Enough."

---

"That's not an answer."

---

"No."

She looked down at the floor.

"It's the only answer that matters."

---

The station fell silent again.

---

Eventually Aidan spoke.

"What do they want from me?"

---

This time Lyra didn't hesitate.

---

"The same thing they wanted from me."

---

"And that's what?"

---

Her eyes met his.

---

"To make sure you never existed."

---

The words hit harder than he expected.

---

Aidan looked away.

---

Because deep down...

a part of him still hoped she was wrong.

Still hoped there was some misunderstanding.

Some mistake.

---

But every day that hope got smaller.

---

Every day reality became stranger.

---

Every day the System proved itself real.

---

Every day the hidden world opened a little wider.

---

Eventually there wouldn't be any room left for denial.

---

"Aidan."

---

He looked up.

---

Lyra was studying him carefully.

---

"Do you know what scares me?"

---

He shook his head.

---

"It's not that they found you."

---

A pause.

---

"It's how quickly they found you."

---

The statement hung in the air.

---

Aidan frowned.

"What does that mean?"

---

"It means they were already looking."

---

A cold chill crawled through his chest.

---

"For me?"

---

"No."

---

The answer came instantly.

---

"For Zee."

---

Silence.

---

Aidan's pulse quickened.

---

"The heir activates the System."

---

Lyra leaned back against the wall.

---

"The System leaves a mark."

---

"What kind of mark?"

---

"The kind monsters can smell."

---

The lantern flickered.

---

The shadows stretched.

---

And for a brief moment...

Aidan remembered something the System had said.

---

**ALL LEGACIES REQUIRE VERDICTS.**

---

**ALL VERDICTS REQUIRE COST.**

---

The words felt different now.

---

More threatening.

---

As if they weren't a warning.

As if they were a promise.
