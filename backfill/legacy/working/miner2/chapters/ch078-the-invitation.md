Azrath stood alone inside the abandoned cathedral.

The crimson vial rested in his hand.

"...Not yet."

He placed the vial upon the altar.

"You still think becoming stronger..."

"...will make you greater than him."

His voice echoed through the empty church.

"When the day comes..."

"...that you're willing to lose yourself..."

"...I'll know you're finally ready."

One.

By one.

By one.

Leaving the vial behind.

Not as a gift.

As a temptation.
