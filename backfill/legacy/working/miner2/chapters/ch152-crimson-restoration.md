The swamp echoed with heavy breathing.

Neither king nor monster moved.

Rain mixed with blood beneath their feet.

Cassian wiped crimson from his mouth.

"...You're done."

Aidan didn't answer.

His left arm hung uselessly.

Three ribs were broken.

His breathing rattled.

His vision blurred.

He could barely keep his feet beneath him.

Cassian laughed.

"I can hear your heartbeat slowing."

"You've got nothing left."

---

Aidan slowly looked down at his own body.

Every movement hurt.

Every breath felt like broken glass.

He quietly whispered...

> **"...Sixth Witch Law..."**

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

Aidan pressed one blood-covered hand over his chest.

The Witch Authority spread beneath him.

Not across the battlefield...

Across himself.

Ancient crimson symbols crawled over his skin.

The swamp shook.

---

> **"Crimson Restoration."**

BOOM!!

Crimson light erupted around Aidan.

His broken shoulder snapped back into place.

His ribs shifted.

Cracked bones reformed.

The bleeding slowed.

His breathing steadied.

The cut across his face closed.

Not perfectly.

Not completely.

Enough.

---

The System appeared.

Only Aidan could see it.

---

**《SIXTH WITCH LAW》**

**Crimson Restoration**

**Physical Condition Restored: 28%**

**Authority Reserves Remaining: 41%**

**Warning: Mental Fatigue Increased**

---

Cassian stared.

"...You healed..."

Aidan slowly rolled his shoulder.

It still hurt.

But it worked.

He flexed his fingers.

Then looked at Cassian.

"...No."

He took one step forward.

"I borrowed more time."

---

Azrath watched from the shadows.

His smile grew.

"...Interesting..."

"He made healing..."

"...another judgment."
