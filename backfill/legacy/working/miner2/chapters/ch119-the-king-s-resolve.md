Dawn broke over New Orleans.

Golden light poured through stained-glass windows.

For everyone else...

It was another morning.

For Aidan...

It felt like the beginning of something he couldn't name.

He hadn't slept.

The dream from the previous night lingered.

Not images.

A feeling.

As if something terrible had already happened...

And he simply hadn't caught up to it yet.

---

He stood before the Crimson Throne.

His right leg shook unconsciously.

The habit he'd inherited from Zee.

His eyes never left the empty seat.

"...What am I becoming?"

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

For several long seconds...

It remained blank.

Then words slowly wrote themselves.

> **A KING IS NOT MEASURED BY THE ENEMIES HE DEFEATS...**

Another line formed.

> **...BUT BY THE DARKNESS HE REFUSES TO BECOME.**

Aidan stared at the words.

Then quietly closed the Legacy.

"...We'll see."

For the first time...

He didn't answer with confidence.

He answered with uncertainty.
