The throne room had grown quiet.

Everyone had gone to rest.

Only Aidan remained.

Rez stood beside one of the tall windows in his Crimson Assassin body.

He stared at the moon.

For once...

He wasn't smiling.

Aidan walked over.

"You've been quiet."

Rez didn't answer.

"I know."

Another moment passed.

Then—

Rez suddenly grabbed the edge of the window.

His breathing became uneven.

Aidan stepped forward.

"Rez?"

The Crimson Assassin closed his eyes.

"...No..."

"...Not again..."

His body tensed.

A sharp pain shot through his head.

Then—

A voice.

Not Aidan's.

Not the Legacy's.

A voice from centuries ago.

> **"If he ever awakens..."**

> **"...protect him..."**

Another voice answered.

> **"Even if I forget?"**

> **"Especially if you forget."**

Rez's crimson eyes snapped open.

He staggered backward.

Aidan caught him before he fell.

"What did you see?"

Rez stared at him.

Not through him.

At him.

His voice trembled for the first time.

"...I remember..."

Aidan waited.

"...Someone..."

"...asked me to protect you..."

Silence.

Rez frowned.

"I don't know who..."

"I don't know when..."

"But I remember making the promise."

He looked down at his own hands.

"I chose this."

"I wasn't imprisoned by the Legacy..."

"I volunteered."

Inside Aidan's mind, the Soul Link trembled.

Aidan could feel Rez's emotions.

Confusion.

Relief.

Grief.

Hope.

Rez slowly laughed.

"...After all this time..."

"...I finally remembered my first promise."

---

**Ding.**

Rez looked at the notification and shook his head with a laugh.

"One percent?"

Aidan smirked.

"You've been making fun of my percentages."

"I guess it's your turn."

Rez groaned.

"...I deserved that."

Both of them laughed.

For the first time...

The mystery of Rez had finally begun to unravel.

---

I think **1%** is perfect.

Not because the percentage itself matters, but because it tells the reader:

- Rez's memories are recoverable.
- The process has begun.
- Every major milestone in Aidan's journey can unlock another piece of Rez's past.

That way, by the time you reach the later books, Rez's full identity won't be an exposition dump—it will be something the readers have been piecing together alongside Aidan for hundreds of chapters.
