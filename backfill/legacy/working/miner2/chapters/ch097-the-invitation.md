Azrath lit a single candle.

The abandoned cathedral glowed faintly.

Cassian arrived.

Without being called.

Azrath noticed.

He smiled.

"You came on your own."

Cassian didn't answer.

His eyes stayed on the vial resting upon the altar.

Azrath followed his gaze.

"You're getting closer."

"I haven't touched it."

"No."

Azrath agreed.

"But you're no longer afraid of it."

Silence.

Then...

Azrath picked up the vial.

Held it between them.

"This isn't a curse."

Cassian looked unconvinced.

"No?"

"No."

"It's a door."

"To what?"

Azrath smiled.

"To the man..."

"...you've always believed you deserved to be."

Cassian stared at the crimson liquid.

His reflection stared back.

Only...

The reflection was smiling.

He wasn't.
