House Darkheart welcomed its king home.

The gates opened.

The people cheered.

Children ran beside his horse.

Aidan smiled...

But it didn't reach his eyes.

Lyra met him at the castle steps.

"You came back."

"I did."

She embraced him.

For a brief moment...

The weight disappeared.

Inside his soul...

The Wolf quietly watched.

It saw something the others could not.

Aidan was no longer worried about himself.

He was worried about **her**.

The Wolf whispered only one sentence.

> **"...Protect the Queen."**

Aidan didn't know why those words unsettled him.

But they did.

Far away...

Cassian looked one last time toward the crimson banners of House Darkheart.

He turned.

And disappeared into the forest.

Not as a man seeking peace.

Not yet as a man seeking blood.

But as a man standing between those two roads.

The Third Judgment had not begun.

The choice that would awaken it...

Had been placed entirely in Cassian's hands.
