Peace.

It had become unfamiliar.

Weeks passed without bloodshed.

Without betrayal.

Without war.

House Darkheart flourished.

Children filled the Academy.

The First Generation grew stronger every day.

The kingdom laughed.

Smiled.

Lived.

...

Aidan couldn't.

---

It started as a whisper.

A feeling.

A strange emptiness deep inside his chest.

Every day...

It became louder.

Not painful.

Hungry.

---

One night...

Aidan stood alone on the highest tower.

His crimson eyes overlooked the sleeping kingdom.

Inside his mind, Rez immediately noticed.

**You haven't slept in three days.**

*I'm not tired.*

**Don't lie to me.**

Aidan remained silent.

Rez stepped beside him in the real world.

The Crimson Assassin didn't joke.

Didn't smile.

He simply watched his nephew.

**What's wrong?**

Aidan answered honestly.

*I don't know.*

*Something feels... off.*

---

The wind shifted.

A familiar scent drifted through the night.

Blood.

A hunter several miles away had caught a deer.

The scent should have faded.

Instead...

Aidan smelled it perfectly.

His pupils narrowed.

His fangs slowly extended.

His breathing deepened.

---

Rez saw it immediately.

His expression hardened.

**Aidan.**

No response.

**Aidan.**

Slowly...

The Crimson King turned his head.

His crimson eyes had become darker than Rez had ever seen.

Not glowing.

Burning.

---

**Ding.**

---

Aidan looked at the notification.

Then...

It vanished.

Not because the Legacy dismissed it.

Because Aidan did.

---

*I'm fine.*

Rez didn't believe him.

**No...**

**You're hiding something.**

---

Aidan finally admitted it.

*Every heartbeat...*

*I can hear them.*

He looked down toward the city.

*Every person.*

*Every child.*

*Every soldier.*

*Every heartbeat.*

He closed his eyes.

*And every one of them...*

He swallowed.

*Tastes different.*

---

Silence.

---

Rez slowly stepped in front of him.

The Crimson Assassin had seen this before.

Centuries ago.

Not in Aidan.

In someone else.

**How long?**

*A week.*

**...Why didn't you tell me?**

Aidan laughed bitterly.

*Because I'm the Founder.*

*I'm supposed to have control.*

---

The silence between them became heavy.

---

Then...

For the first time in years...

Rez looked afraid.

Not for himself.

For Aidan.

**This isn't training anymore.**

**This is bloodlust.**

---

Aidan clenched his fists.

His nails dug into his palms.

"I can handle it."

Rez's voice became firm.

**Don't say that.**

Aidan looked at him.

"I can."

**That's exactly what every powerful vampire says...**

**...right before they lose someone they love.**

---

Below...

A child laughed in the marketplace.

The sound reached Aidan's ears.

Instantly.

His head turned.

His breathing changed.

For a fraction of a second...

His instincts screamed.

**Feed.**

---

Aidan vanished.

**BOOM!**

The tower cracked beneath the force of his movement.

Rez's eyes widened.

"AIDAN!"

The Crimson Assassin disappeared after him.

---

The streets below erupted into confusion.

People looked around.

They never saw the crimson blur racing across the rooftops.

Aidan wasn't chasing a person.

He was running from himself.

Every heartbeat echoed inside his skull.

Every pulse.

Every drop of blood.

His hunger became unbearable.

---

Inside his mind...

Everything became distorted.

The Soul Link trembled violently.

**Aidan!**

Rez's voice echoed through the chaos.

**Listen to me!**

*I can't...*

**You can!**

*Make it stop!*

---

Aidan slammed into the roof of an abandoned watchtower outside the city.

The stone exploded beneath him.

He dropped to one knee.

Both hands gripping his head.

His fangs fully exposed.

His breathing wild.

His eyes...

No longer looked like a king.

They looked like a predator fighting the oldest curse in existence.

---

Rez landed a moment later.

He didn't attack.

He didn't reach for a weapon.

He simply stood in front of Aidan.

Ready.

If his nephew won the battle...

Rez would be there.

If his nephew lost the battle...

Rez would be there.

No matter the outcome...

He would not leave him alone.

The moon rose over House Darkheart.

For the first time since becoming the Founder...

Aidan Darkheart faced an enemy no army could defeat.

The monster within himself.

And the night...

Had only just begun.
