The following morning...

Cassian burned the page.

Or at least...

He tried to.

The ancient paper refused to catch fire.

He held it over an open flame until the candle melted into nothing.

The page remained untouched.

His jaw tightened.

"...What are you?"

For the first time...

The whisper answered immediately.

> **"A question."**

Cassian's blood ran cold.

"Show yourself."

Silence.

Then...

> **"Earn it."**

The voice disappeared.

Cassian looked around the room.

Empty.

Yet somehow...

He no longer felt alone.
