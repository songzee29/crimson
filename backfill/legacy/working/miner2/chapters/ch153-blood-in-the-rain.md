Aidan stopped beneath an old streetlamp.

The city was strangely quiet.

Too quiet.

Curse Sense activated.

Nothing.

Then...

Everything.

Crimson lines erupted across his vision.

Twenty...

No...

Thirty magical signatures.

Surrounding him.

He whispered,

"...Ambush."

A voice answered from the darkness.

"Correct."

Silver chains exploded from every rooftop.

Aidan barely activated Hex Step.

The chains slammed into the pavement where he'd been standing.

Another hunter appeared behind him.

A spear thrust toward his spine.

Aidan twisted.

The spear grazed his side.

Pain shot through his body.

Blood mixed with the rain.

Within seconds...

He was surrounded.

Not monsters.

Elite Veil Hunters.

None of them spoke.

They simply attacked.

---

The battle became chaos.

Hex Step.

Shackles.

Threads.

Curse Sense.

Everything flowed together.

Aidan wasn't thinking anymore.

He was surviving.

One hunter swung a hammer.

Aidan ducked.

Another fired enchanted arrows.

He redirected them with Crimson Thread.

Two hunters charged together.

Shackles erupted beneath their feet.

They shattered the chains.

Immediately.

"They're adapting..."

Aidan muttered.

No...

They already knew his techniques.

Someone had been studying him.
