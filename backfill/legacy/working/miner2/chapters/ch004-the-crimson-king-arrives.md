By sunset...

House Darkheart reached the outskirts of Black Hollow.

Aidan dismounted.

He didn't wear battle armor.

He didn't bring an army.

Only his closest companions.

Rez.

Lyra.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

They walked through the gates.

The villagers saw them.

Their celebration slowly quieted.

Not from hatred.

From uncertainty.

Cassian stepped forward.

His eyes met Aidan's.

Neither reached for a weapon.

Neither spoke.

For a long moment...

They simply looked at one another.

Finally...

Cassian broke the silence.

"...The decree called you."

Aidan nodded.

"...Yes."

"You didn't come to fight."

"...No."

Cassian looked down at the Darkheart Brand.

Then back at Aidan.

"...Neither did I."
