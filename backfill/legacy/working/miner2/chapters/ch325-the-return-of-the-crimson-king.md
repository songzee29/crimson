The snowstorm faded behind him.

The Valley of Sovereigns disappeared into the distance.

Aidan never looked back.

A king who constantly looked behind him...

Would never see the future approaching.

Three silent steps behind...

The Crimson Assassin followed.

Its eyes remained closed.

Its sword remained sheathed.

Its footsteps remained soundless.

Yet every ancient vampire watching knew one thing.

The Crimson King no longer walked alone.
