The forest shook.

Branches snapped as the Crimson Wolf tore through the ancient wilderness.

Aidan wasn't chasing anything.

Not yet.

He was running.

Every stride released another piece of the tension he had carried for years.

The wind rushed through his fur.

The earth answered every step.

For the first time...

The Wolf wasn't trapped behind a human body.

It was exactly where it belonged.

Free.

---

Far behind him...

Rez smiled.

"There he goes."

Lyra watched the crimson blur disappear between the trees.

"He looks..."

She searched for the right word.

"...happy."

Rez nodded.

"He is."

---

A long, echoing howl rolled across the mountains.

It wasn't a cry of anger.

It wasn't a challenge.

It was freedom.

The sound traveled for miles.

Every creature in the forest froze.

Then...

Something unexpected happened.

Malik suddenly grabbed his chest.

"...Whoa..."

Kael looked at him.

"You alright?"

"My wolf..."

He frowned.

"...It's answering."

Darius inhaled sharply.

"I feel it too."

Even Kael's breathing became heavier.

Deep inside each of them...

Their wolves stirred.

Not with rage.

With excitement.

---

Rez looked at the First Generation.

A knowing smile crossed his face.

"It's calling you."

Malik blinked.

"Calling us?"

Rez nodded.

"The Founder's wolf has begun the hunt."

"The Bloodline answers."

---

Mara tilted her head.

"What about me?"

"You carry a wolf as well."

Rez looked around at all of them.

"If your Crimson Blood carries the Wolf Authority..."

"Then tonight..."

"You run."

---

Without another word...

Malik smiled.

"...I've been waiting for this."

His Crimson Blood answered.

Bones shifted.

Muscles expanded.

Crimson energy wrapped around him.

Seconds later...

A massive wolf stood where Malik had been.

Its coat was dark charcoal with streaks of crimson running through its fur.

He threw his head back.

**Awooooo!**

The forest answered.

---

Kael transformed next.

Unlike Malik...

Ancient Crimson runes glowed across his wolf form.

Every step left faint runic marks in the earth before fading away.

---

Mara smiled.

"So this..."

"...is what everyone's been talking about."

She closed her eyes.

Her body shifted gracefully.

A sleek crimson wolf emerged, glowing faintly with witch runes woven through her fur.

She laughed the moment her paws touched the ground.

"I understand now."

---

Darius didn't even wait.

He exploded into his wolf form with enough force to crack the ground beneath him.

His wolf was enormous.

Broad-shouldered.

Built more like a living fortress than a hunter.

Malik looked at him and mentally laughed through the instincts they all now shared.

*You're still showing off.*

Darius wagged his tail once.

*Obviously.*

---

Lyra smiled as the Crimson Blood flowed naturally through her.

Her own transformation was calm.

Elegant.

A silver-black wolf with soft crimson markings stepped forward.

She looked toward Rez.

"Coming?"

Rez chuckled.

"I think I'll let you young wolves have this one."

He crossed his arms.

"Someone has to make sure none of you destroy my forest."

---

The five wolves looked toward the horizon.

Far ahead...

Aidan's scent still lingered on the wind.

The Founder's trail.

Without needing to speak...

They all understood.

The pack had already chosen its leader.

One by one...

They launched into the forest.

The hunt had become something greater than chasing prey.

It had become a reunion with the oldest instincts carried in Crimson Blood.

Miles ahead...

Aidan slowed to a stop atop a rocky cliff.

His ears twitched.

Behind him...

Five distinct scents.

Five familiar heartbeats.

Five wolves.

He turned.

One by one, they emerged from the trees.

Not as soldiers.

Not as nobles.

Not even as descendants.

As a pack.

The great Crimson Wolf looked over them.

For a long moment...

No one moved.

Then Aidan threw his head toward the crimson moon.

A powerful howl echoed across the wilderness.

Five more voices answered.

The sound rolled through the valleys like thunder.

Every predator in the forest lowered its head.

The true rulers of the hunt...

Had arrived.

Far in the darkness...

A pair of enormous golden eyes slowly opened.

Whatever had been watching the forest...

Had just heard the Crimson Pack announce its presence.

And now...

It was coming to meet them.
