The memory walked slowly around the throne.

"I left this place..."

"...because kings should never worship crowns."

He looked directly at Aidan.

"They should serve people."

The twelve kings behind him nodded silently.

Zee continued.

"Every Crimson King who sat here..."

"...failed."

Aidan blinked.

"...Every one?"

"Every one."

"Even you?"

Zee smiled sadly.

"...Especially me."

Silence.

"You know why?"

Aidan shook his head.

"Because..."

"...we believed strength made us kings."

He pointed toward Aidan's chest.

"It doesn't."

Another pause.

"Loss doesn't make you a king."

"Power doesn't make you a king."

"Blood doesn't make you a king."

He smiled proudly.

"You became one..."

"...the moment you carried Elias home."

Aidan's eyes filled with tears.

"...I couldn't save him."

"I know."

"I failed."

"I know."

"I wasn't enough."

Zee stepped closer.

His hand almost reached Aidan's shoulder.

Almost.

"If you had saved everyone..."

"...you would've learned nothing."

The room became silent.

Then Zee spoke the words Aidan would carry forever.

> **"A perfect king is feared."**

> **"A broken king is followed."**
