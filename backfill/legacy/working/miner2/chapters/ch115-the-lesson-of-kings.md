Azrath walked around the shattered pillar.

Running one finger across the broken stone.

"You know why you'll lose?"

Cassian frowned.

"I won't."

Azrath ignored him.

"You think killing Aidan..."

"...will make you greater."

Cassian's eyes narrowed.

"It will."

Azrath looked directly at him.

"No."

"It will only make you..."

"...the man who killed Aidan."

Silence.

"Legends aren't born by destroying kings."

"They're born..."

"...by replacing them."

Cassian's breathing slowed.

For the first time...

He actually listened.

---

Azrath continued.

"If you kill him today..."

"...history will remember the Crimson King."

"You'll only be remembered..."

"...as his murderer."

Cassian gripped his sword tighter.

"...Then what do I do?"

Azrath smiled.

"There."

"Now..."

"...you're asking the right question."
