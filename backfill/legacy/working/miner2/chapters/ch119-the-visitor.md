The old man entered without hesitation.

His eyes moved across the throne room.

The crimson banners.

The ancient pillars.

The throne.

Then...

They settled on Aidan.

The Founder of the Crimson Bloodline.

For several long moments...

Neither man spoke.

The room became completely silent.

Kael stood to Aidan's right.

Darius to his left.

Malik folded his arms near the entrance.

Lyra remained beside Aidan.

Rez stood a few steps behind the throne in his Crimson Assassin body, watching every heartbeat the old man made.

*Meaning?*

**He's not afraid... and he isn't lying.**

"My King."

"Why?"

"To see if the stories were true."

Aidan leaned back in the throne.

"And?"

"They were incomplete."
