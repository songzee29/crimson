Nothing happened.

No explosion.

No magic.

No music.

Only silence.

Aidan stared.

Unable to breathe.

Unable to think.

Unable to accept what had just happened.

His right leg...

Stopped shaking.

For the first time since childhood.

It became completely still.

Because something inside him had broken.

A single drop of Elias' blood slid from Aidan's fingertips...

Onto the ground.

The System appeared.

---

**...**

---

It froze.

The words refused to form.

---

**...**

---

Again.

Nothing.

The interface distorted.

Notifications overlapped each other.

Ancient Crimson symbols replaced familiar text.

The air around Aidan began vibrating.

Lyra's eyes widened.

"...Aidan?"

Malik finally reached the street.

He looked down.

Saw Elias.

Saw the blood.

Then saw Aidan.

"...No..."

Sienna covered her mouth.

The entire Circle stood frozen.

---

Deep inside the Crimson Legacy...

Something ancient cracked.

Not the Witch Authority.

Not the Vampire Authority.

The barrier between them.

---

The System suddenly screamed.

Not with sound.

With light.

Every crimson screen shattered across Aidan's vision.

---

**《CRITICAL ERROR》**

---

**SOUL TRAUMA DETECTED**

---

**WITCH CURSE CONDITION...**

---

**FULFILLED**

---

The words glitched violently.

One final notification struggled to appear.

---

**HOST...**

**SURVIVE...**

---

Before the sentence could finish...

Everything exploded.

A pillar of crimson magic erupted into the sky above New Orleans.

Windows shattered across the city.

The ground split beneath Aidan's feet.

The Veil hunters were thrown backward.

Cassian was hurled through a stone wall.

The rain evaporated before it touched the crimson storm.

Magic no longer flowed from Aidan.

It poured from him like a broken dam.

The Witch Authority had awakened.

Not under Aidan's control.

Not under the System's control.

But under the raw, unbearable weight of grief.

And as the crimson storm consumed the battlefield, the first cracks spread across the ancient seal that had held the Vampire Bloodline asleep for centuries.

The Witch Arc had reached the moment history would remember.

Not as the birth of a king.

But as the night the Crimson Legacy itself realized...

**its heir could break the rules.**
