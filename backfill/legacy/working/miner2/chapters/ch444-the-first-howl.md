The beast charged one final time.

Aidan didn't move.

He simply inhaled.

Deeply.

Then...

He threw his head toward the sky.

A sound erupted from his soul.

It wasn't a scream.

It wasn't a roar.

It was...

A howl.

**AAAAAAAAAWWWWWWOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!**

Every wolf within hundreds of miles answered.

One.

Then hundreds.

Then thousands.

Across mountains...

Across forests...

Across forgotten kingdoms...

The wolves answered their king.

Even House Darkheart heard it.

The Guardian slowly looked toward the northern horizon.

His face turned pale.

"...No..."

"He has begun."
