Three mornings passed.

For the first time...

The System remained silent.

No quests.

No warnings.

No hidden objectives.

Nothing.

Aidan actually found himself checking for it.

"...That's weird."

Lyra looked over from the driver's seat.

"What is?"

"It's quiet."

She smiled.

"I wish my life was that quiet."

Aidan laughed.

"You'd get bored."

"...Probably."

The silence between them was comfortable now.

Neither noticed the old Guardian watching from a nearby rooftop.

He smiled to himself.

*"Good."*

*"They're learning to live between battles."*
