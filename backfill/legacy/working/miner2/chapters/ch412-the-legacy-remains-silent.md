Back at House Darkheart...

Aidan stood in the Crimson Archive.

He waited.

The Legacy did not issue a quest.

It did not reveal the disappearances.

It did not warn him.

It simply...

Remained silent.

Aidan closed the ancient journal in his hands.

He understood.

Not every tragedy belonged to him.

Kings could not solve every problem in the world.

Some roads...

Had to be walked by others.

Far away...

Cassian stood alone in the center of Black Hollow.

Another family had vanished.

No answers.

No enemy.

Only loss.

For the first time...

The people of Black Hollow looked to Cassian...

Not with confidence.

But with fear.

And hidden in the shadows beyond the village...

A pair of crimson eyes watched.

Patiently.

Waiting.

The Second Judgment...

Had finally begun.
