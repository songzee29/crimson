The fourth floor.

Room 417.

The crying suddenly stopped.

Aidan slowly opened the door.

The room was empty.

Except...

For one man.

Sitting calmly in a wooden chair.

Perfect black suit.

White gloves.

A porcelain smiling mask covered his face.

The little boy stood silently behind him.

Completely unharmed.

The masked man slowly clapped.

"Wonderful."

Aidan immediately stepped in front of the child.

"Who are you?"

The man tilted his head.

"Names..."

He laughed softly.

"...are dangerous."

His voice was calm.

Almost friendly.

Which somehow made it terrifying.

"You've been looking for me."

Aidan frowned.

"I don't even know you."

"No."

"You've been looking for what I represent."

The masked man slowly stood.

"You carry the Crimson Legacy."

"I carry..."

He spread his arms.

"...its consequences."

---

The System violently appeared.

Not one notification.

Dozens.

---

**DANGER**

**DO NOT ENGAGE**

**RETREAT**

**RETREAT**

**RETREAT**

---

The messages overlapped.

Flickered.

Almost glitched.

It was the most urgent reaction the System had ever shown.

---

The masked man looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

Then...

He smiled beneath the porcelain mask.

"I can see it."

He whispered.

"The Witch..."

"...is almost awake."

---

Without warning—

He snapped his fingers.

Every window in the hotel exploded inward.

The entire building shook.

Walls cracked.

The floor collapsed beneath Aidan.

He grabbed the little boy just before they fell.

Lyra leapt after them.

Sienna and Malik rushed from opposite hallways.

The hotel began collapsing around them.

Dust filled the air.

The masked man never moved.

He simply watched.

Watching Aidan choose.

Fight...

Or save.

Aidan made his choice instantly.

He wrapped one arm around the child.

Reached for Lyra with the other.

"Move!"

The Crimson Circle escaped seconds before the hotel collapsed into itself.

The roar echoed across the Hollow District.

When the dust finally settled...

The masked man was gone.

No body.

No footprints.

Nothing.

Only one thing remained where he had been sitting.

A single porcelain chess piece.

A black king.

The System appeared one last time.

---

**Unknown Individual Recorded**

**Threat Assessment: Beyond Current Host**

**Status: Survive.**

---

Aidan picked up the black king.

For the first time since receiving the Crimson Book...

He didn't feel like he had met an enemy.

He felt like he had met someone who had been waiting for him.

Someone who wasn't interested in defeating him today.

Someone who was studying him.

Testing him.

Waiting for the day Aidan would become strong enough to face him again.

And miles away, inside a forgotten cathedral buried beneath New Orleans, the First Fang pulsed a little brighter.

Not because the Witch Authority had awakened.

Because destiny had just taken another step toward it.
