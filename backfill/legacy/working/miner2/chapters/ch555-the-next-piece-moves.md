Deep beneath the earth...

Far beyond the Ash Dominion...

A massive stone door slowly opened.

Ancient chains broke apart.

A figure stepped into the light.

Its face remained hidden.

Its voice echoed through the cavern.

> **"The Crimson King has survived the Ash King's first trial."**

Another voice answered from the darkness.

> **"Then the Second Judgment will proceed."**

The first figure nodded.

> **"Cassian's heart is almost ready."**

Silence.

Then the final order came.

> **"Do not force revenge."**

> **"Let grief choose it."**

The cavern fell silent once more.

Far away...

Aidan looked out across his kingdom.

He didn't know it yet.

But invisible hands were no longer trying to conquer House Darkheart.

They were arranging events.

Waiting...

For one heartbreaking choice.

The kind of choice that would change a man forever.

And somewhere within Aidan's soul...

The Wolf opened one eye.

It looked toward the horizon.

Then quietly whispered...

> **"...The hunt is not over..."**

The second heartbeat echoed through the Crimson King one more time.

**THUMP.**

**THUMP.**

This time...

It sounded less like a warning.

And more...

Like a countdown.
