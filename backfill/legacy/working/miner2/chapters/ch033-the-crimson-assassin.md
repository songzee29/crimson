On the highest tower...

The Crimson Assassin suddenly opened its eyes.

It looked toward the northern forest.

Then vanished.

A heartbeat later...

It stood miles away.

Blocking a narrow mountain pass.

A hooded traveler stopped walking.

Neither moved.

Neither spoke.

The Crimson Assassin slowly drew one crimson blade.

The hooded figure smiled beneath the hood.

"...So..."

"...the guardian has found me."

The Crimson Assassin attacked.

The mountain exploded.

Stone rained down the cliffs.

The battle lasted only moments.

When the dust settled...

The hooded figure was gone.

Only drifting ash remained.

The Crimson Assassin looked toward House Darkheart.

Its instincts screamed.

Danger.
