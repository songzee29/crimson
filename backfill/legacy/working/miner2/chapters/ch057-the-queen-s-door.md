The corridor was silent.

Too silent.

Cassian stood before the carved oak doors leading to the Queen's chambers.

His hand hovered over the handle.

It wouldn't move.

Inside his mind...

A thousand memories fought one another.

He remembered Lyra smiling beside Aidan.

He remembered her treating wounded villagers after the war.

He remembered her refusing to hate him, even when she had every reason to.

His hand slowly fell away from the door.

"...No..."

He turned to leave.

Then he saw the ring in his hand.

The blackened ring from the ashes of his home.

His breathing grew heavy.

His jaw clenched.

"...Why..."

"...do you still get a future..."

"...when mine burned?"

He closed his eyes.

When they opened...

The hesitation was gone.

He reached for the door.
