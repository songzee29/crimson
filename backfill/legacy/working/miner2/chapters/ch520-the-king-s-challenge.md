The Ash King stopped only a few feet away.

He folded his hands behind his back.

"I have no desire to destroy House Darkheart."

Everyone froze.

Rez frowned.

"...What?"

"I came for something else."

Aidan's voice remained steady.

"What?"

The Ash King's eyes drifted toward the Hall of Names.

"Memory."

He gestured toward the endless pillars.

"Every kingdom remembers its victories."

"Every bloodline remembers its heroes."

"But eventually..."

"They forget everyone else."

His voice became quieter.

"I remember them."

The forgotten names upon the pillars began glowing.

Children.

Soldiers.

Queens.

Farmers.

Entire families.

Names that history had abandoned.

The Ash King looked back at Aidan.

"You wish to save them."

"...Yes."

"So do I."

Silence swept through the hall.

The difference between them...

Wasn't the goal.

It was the method.
