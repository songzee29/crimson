The first Sentinel swung its stone spear.

Too slow.

Aidan stepped inside the attack before it completed.

His hand wrapped around the spear.

He twisted.

The weapon snapped.

The Sentinel had only enough time to widen its eyes before Aidan struck.

The impact launched it through three cathedral walls.

The ancient creature rose again.

Cracks spread across its stone-like skin.

It laughed.

"You cannot destroy us."

Aidan tilted his head.

"...Can I not?"

The Witch Authority ignited across his arm.

Crimson symbols wrapped around his fist.

One precise strike.

The Sentinel froze.

Then...

It crumbled into fragments.

The remaining Sentinels fell silent.

For the first time in centuries...

One of them had truly died.
