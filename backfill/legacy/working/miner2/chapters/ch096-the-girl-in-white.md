The cemetery was strangely quiet.

No tourists.

No caretakers.

Only rows of weathered tombs disappearing into the evening fog.

Aidan immediately noticed something strange.

Small footprints.

Children's footprints.

They stopped...

In the middle of an open pathway.

No return tracks.

No signs of struggle.

Just...

Gone.

Malik knelt beside them.

"That's impossible."

Lyra shook her head.

"No."

"It's magic."

The System appeared.

Then—

A little girl appeared between two crypts.

White dress.

Bare feet.

Long black hair.

She couldn't have been older than eight.

She simply stood there.

Watching Aidan.

He smiled gently.

"Hey..."

"Are you okay?"

She didn't answer.

Instead...

She pointed deeper into the cemetery.

Then vanished.

Not running.

Not teleporting.

Simply...

Gone.

Malik rubbed his eyes.

"...Did everyone else see that?"

Lyra nodded slowly.

"I did."

Aidan looked toward where she had pointed.

The pull inside his chest returned.

The same pull that had once led him to the Sanctuary.

He took a slow breath.

"I think..."

"...we're supposed to follow her."

---

Far beneath the oldest mausoleum in the cemetery...

A pair of crimson eyes slowly opened.

Not human.

Not spirit.

Something ancient.

Something hungry.

It smiled in the darkness.

Because for the first time in centuries...

The heir had willingly walked into its hunting ground.
