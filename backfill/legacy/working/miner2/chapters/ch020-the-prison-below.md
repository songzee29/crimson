Miles beneath the Veil...

Ancient chains stretched through an endless cavern.

Every chain was covered in crimson seals.

Every seal carried the mark of House Darkheart.

At the center...

001 slowly lifted his head.

His fingers traced the glowing crest burned into the iron around his wrists.

He laughed.

"...Still using his family's chains..."

His laughter echoed through the prison.

"...How poetic."

He pulled once.

Nothing happened.

He pulled again.

One seal flickered.

Then stabilized.

A voice echoed through the darkness.

"You cannot break them."

001 smiled.

"I know."

Silence.

"I'm waiting for someone else to."
