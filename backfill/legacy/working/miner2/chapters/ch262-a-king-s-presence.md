The following morning...

The Circle went into the city.

Something strange happened.

People kept moving out of Aidan's way.

Without realizing it.

Not fear.

Instinct.

A little boy accidentally bumped into Aidan.

He looked up.

"...Sorry, mister."

Aidan smiled gently.

"It's okay."

The little boy grinned and ran back to his mother.

His mother stared at Aidan for several seconds.

Then quietly bowed her head.

Not deeply.

Just enough to show respect.

She didn't know why she did it.

Neither did Aidan.

The Witch Authority had become so deeply woven into him that even ordinary people felt it.

Not as danger.

As presence.
