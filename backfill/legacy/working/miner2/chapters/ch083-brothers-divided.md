Cassian rose without another word.

He picked up his sword.

Not as a weapon.

As a burden.

He looked at Lyra one final time.

"...I'm sorry."

Then he looked at Aidan.

The words almost refused to leave his mouth.

"...Brother..."

Aidan's expression never changed.

"You lost the right to call me that tonight."

Cassian lowered his head.

"I know."

He turned.

And walked out of the royal chamber.

No one stopped him.

No one followed him.

The doors closed behind him with a deep echo.

As Cassian disappeared into the darkness beyond House Darkheart...

The rain finally stopped.

Inside the chamber...

Aidan continued holding Lyra's hand.

He had saved her life.

But he knew...

Nothing would ever return to what it had been.

The Second Judgment was over.

The Fourth Judgment had begun.

And somewhere beyond the horizon...

Cassian walked alone.

For the first time in centuries...

With nothing but the consequences of his own choice.
