Meanwhile...

Aidan had no idea another battle had already begun.

He spent the afternoon helping reopen an old orphanage damaged during the wars.

Children laughed as he painted walls alongside volunteers.

Lyra walked in carrying supplies.

She laughed.

"You've got paint all over your face."

Aidan grinned.

"So do you."

She wiped her cheek.

"...I hate you."

"No you don't."

"...No."

She smiled.

"I don't."

For one peaceful afternoon...

There were no curses.

No decrees.

No monsters.

Just people rebuilding together.

Outside...

An old man watched from across the street.

Perfect suit.

Perfect smile.

Azrath quietly turned away.

"...Now I understand."

He wasn't looking at Aidan.

He was studying...

What would hurt him most.
