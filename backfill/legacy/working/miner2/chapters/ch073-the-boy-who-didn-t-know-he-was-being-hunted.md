Three days passed.

For the first time since the Crimson Book disappeared...

Nothing happened.

No Veil.

No Sanctuary.

No strange visions.

No crimson doors.

Just life.

Aidan almost convinced himself that everything had slowed down.

Almost.

The System hadn't spoken once.

Not because it was gone.

Because it had nothing to say.

It was learning.

Watching.

Waiting.

---

Aidan sat alone on a park bench overlooking the Mississippi River.

The evening sun reflected across the water in streaks of orange and crimson.

People walked by without paying him any attention.

Children chased pigeons.

Street musicians played jazz a block away.

New Orleans felt alive.

For the first time in weeks...

Aidan smiled.

A real smile.

"You actually look happy."

Lyra dropped onto the bench beside him carrying two cups of coffee.

"I figured you needed this."

Aidan accepted one.

"...Thanks."

They sat in comfortable silence.

Neither of them realized someone across the street had taken a photograph.

The man lowered the camera.

He wasn't a tourist.

He wasn't press.

Hidden beneath his jacket was a silver Veil insignia.

He quietly spoke into a small radio.

"Target confirmed."

"He smiles."

A voice answered.

"Continue observation."

"No engagement."
