Far beyond the borders of House Darkheart...

Beyond mountains...

Beyond kingdoms...

Beyond the reach of ordinary maps...

A ship sailed through black waters.

Its sails bore no kingdom's banner.

Only a silver eclipse.

A lone figure stood at the bow.

Watching the horizon.

An old messenger knelt before them.

"My lord..."

"The reports have been confirmed."

"The Crimson Heir has awakened True Crimson Blood."

Silence.

"The Queen survived the First Dark Blooding."

More silence.

Finally...

The figure spoke.

"So..."

"It has begun."

The messenger lowered his head.

"Shall we prepare for war?"

The figure slowly shook their head.

"No."

"Not yet."

"They've only just entered history."

The figure smiled faintly.

"Let them build."

"Let them dream."

"Because every civilization..."

"...is eventually tested."

The ship continued through the darkness.

Its destination remained unknown.

Its master...

Still hidden.

But somewhere...

A new chapter of the Crimson Legacy had quietly begun.
