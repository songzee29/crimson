Weeks later...

Aidan and Lyra sat on the roof once again.

The city glowed beneath them.

She leaned against his shoulder.

"You ever think about leaving?"

He smiled.

"No."

"Why?"

He looked across New Orleans.

"Because this city gave me everything."

She looked up.

"And me?"

He turned toward her.

"You are my home."

Lyra smiled.

"So..."

"What happens after all this?"

Aidan laughed softly.

"I don't know."

"Maybe..."

"...a little house."

She laughed.

"You?"

"Yeah."

"With a garden."

"You'd kill every plant."

"I absolutely would."

They laughed together.

For a moment...

The world felt perfect.
