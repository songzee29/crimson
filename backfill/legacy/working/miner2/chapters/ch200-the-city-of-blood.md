New Orleans...

Had stopped sleeping.

Every television blared emergency broadcasts.

Every phone carried the same alert.

> **CITYWIDE EMERGENCY**
>
> **Remain indoors.**
>
> **Do not travel alone after sunset.**

Nobody knew why.

Only that people...

Kept disappearing.

---

The French Quarter had become a ghost town.

Blood stained the sidewalks.

Abandoned cars blocked intersections.

Police lights painted empty streets in flashing shades of blue and red.

Yet...

No officers remained.

Only silence.

---

Above the city...

Aidan watched.

Perched atop the rusted iron cross of St. Louis Cathedral.

His black coat drifted in the wind.

His crimson eyes scanned thousands of heartbeats.

Every pulse...

Called to him.

Every heartbeat...

Sounded like a drum.

Every drop of blood...

Smelled intoxicating.

His humanity was gone.

Only instinct remained.

---

Then...

He vanished.
