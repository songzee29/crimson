The abandoned cathedral was silent.

Cassian stood before the altar.

Minutes passed.

Then hours.

The vial never moved.

Neither did he.

He looked down at his own reflection in the dark crimson liquid.

"...I'm tired..."

No whisper answered.

No manipulation.

No Azrath.

Just...

His own voice.

He remembered Elias.

He remembered Aidan's First Decree.

> **"I condemn you... to survive."**

His jaw tightened.

He remembered standing beneath Aidan's shadow.

Watching the city cheer.

Watching children choose Aidan.

Watching House Darkheart become a legend...

Without him.

His hand slowly reached toward the vial.

He stopped.

His fingers trembled.

One thought echoed through his mind.

> **"Why should he be the only king?"**

His hand closed around the glass.

The crimson liquid inside immediately began spinning.

As if it had been waiting.

Cassian stared at it.

Then...

Very slowly...

He pulled the cork free.

The scent of ancient blood filled the cathedral.

He raised the vial toward his lips.

...

And the chapter ended.
