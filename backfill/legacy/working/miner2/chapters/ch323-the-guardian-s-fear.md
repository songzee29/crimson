The Guardian stood alone in the Hall of Kings.

He stared at the sealed vampire door.

Then quietly spoke.

"...Answer me."

Silence.

"...Please."

Nothing.

Then...

Ancient crimson letters slowly carved themselves into the stone.

---

> **Why do you fear him?**

The Guardian lowered his head.

"...Because I loved his father."

Silence.

Another sentence appeared.

---

> **Wrong answer.**

Another.

---

> **You fear him...**

> **...because he may surpass Zee.**

The Guardian didn't deny it.

Because...

It was true.
