The abandoned cathedral.

Cassian's sword struck stone again.

And again.

And again.

Each swing faster than the last.

His strength had already grown.

His speed had doubled.

His reflexes had become unnatural.

Yet...

It wasn't enough.

He imagined Aidan standing before him.

Every strike aimed at his throat.

His heart.

His head.

Again.

Again.

Again.

The stone pillar finally split in half.

Cassian breathed heavily.

"...Still not enough."

A slow clap echoed through the cathedral.

Azrath.

"You've improved."

Cassian didn't turn around.

"I don't need compliments."

Azrath smiled.

"No."

"You need discipline."
