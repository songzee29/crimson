The footsteps stopped.

Silence consumed the forgotten road.

The ancient stone gate groaned.

Not because someone pushed it.

Because something...

Was walking through it.

Ash drifted like snow.

Slowly...

A towering figure emerged.

Nearly nine feet tall.

Wrapped in robes the color of dying embers.

Its face was hidden behind a cracked stone mask.

Thousands of names glowed faintly across its body.

Each name pulsed...

Like a heartbeat.

The little boy behind Lyra screamed.

"That's him!"

"The one who took my family!"

---

Aidan stepped forward.

His crimson eyes never left the figure.

"...Who are you?"

The giant tilted its head.

Then answered.

Its voice sounded like a thousand people speaking at once.

> **"I..."**

> **"...am the Keeper."**

Another step.

> **"...I remember..."**

> **"...those history abandoned."**
