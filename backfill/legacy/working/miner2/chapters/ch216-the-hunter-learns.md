The battle changed.

Aidan stopped overpowering them.

Instead...

He began studying them.

Every swing.

Every spell.

Every formation.

The Sentinels noticed.

One shouted,

"He's adapting!"

Another answered,

"No..."

"...He's learning."

Every exchange made him more efficient.

Every defense became an attack.

Every mistake they made...

Never worked twice.

The oldest Sentinel finally understood.

"We're teaching him."

His voice cracked.

"...Retreat!"

No one moved.

Because it was already too late.
