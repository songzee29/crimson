The figure didn't attack.

Instead...

It slowly removed its hood.

Everyone stared.

The face beneath wasn't old.

Nor young.

Its skin was pale like ash.

Its eyes shimmered silver.

Not vampire.

Not witch.

Not wolf.

Something else entirely.

Rez narrowed his eyes.

"...Who are you?"

The stranger ignored him.

Instead...

It looked only at Aidan.

"You've grown."

Aidan remained calm.

"We've met?"

"No."

"But I knew your father."

Silence.

"You knew Zee."

The stranger nodded.

"I watched him refuse the same road."

The Wolf growled inside Aidan's soul.

Low.

Threatening.

The stranger heard it.

Its smile widened.

"I remember you too..."

"...Old Wolf."
