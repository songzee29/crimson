Rez studied Aidan as they continued walking.

The boy he remembered...

Was gone.

The hesitation...

Gone.

The uncertainty...

Gone.

Even the way Aidan walked had changed.

Every movement carried certainty.

Every decision had already been made before his foot touched the ground.

Rez smiled.

"...You've grown."

Aidan answered without looking at him.

"...I had to."

"You always were stubborn."

"...So I've been told."

Rez laughed.

A real laugh.

It echoed through the mountains.

The first genuine laughter the road had heard in centuries.

---

Then...

Rez stopped laughing.

His expression became serious.

"...Your eyes."

Aidan looked toward him.

"They're different."

"...I died."

"...I know."

"No."

Aidan's voice remained calm.

"...I really died."

"...Cassian killed me."

The wind fell silent.

Rez's smile disappeared.

"...He..."

Aidan nodded once.

"...Yes."

Nothing else needed to be said.
