On the lonely road to Black Hollow...

The Crimson Assassin suddenly froze.

Its sword slipped from its hand.

The blade struck the ground.

For the first time...

Its closed eyelids slowly parted.

Just enough...

For crimson light to shine through.

Aidan watched silently.

The Assassin looked around.

Confused.

Its breathing became uneven.

It looked down at its own hands.

Turned them over slowly.

Almost...

As if seeing them for the very first time.

Then...

Its crimson eyes met Aidan's.

Recognition.

Confusion.

Relief.

All at once.

Its lips parted.

Its voice was rough.

Weak.

As though unused for centuries.

> **"...Aidan..."**

Silence.

The Crimson King looked at the man who had protected him through countless battles without ever knowing why.

For the first time...

The Crimson Assassin smiled.

Not as an assassin.

As Rez.
