Hundreds of miles away...

Cassian stood before the rebuilt foundation of his home.

His partner walked beside him.

"We'll rebuild."

Cassian slowly shook his head.

"...No."

His partner looked confused.

"No?"

Cassian's eyes drifted south.

Toward House Darkheart.

Toward Aidan.

Toward Lyra.

His voice was almost a whisper.

"...Now..."

"...he needs to understand."

The wind carried his words into the darkness.

Inside Aidan's soul...

The second heartbeat exploded.

**THUMP!**

The Crimson Legacy appeared before Aidan.

Only he could see it.
