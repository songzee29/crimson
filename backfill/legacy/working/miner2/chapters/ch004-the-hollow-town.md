The Circle's first destination was a forgotten town in northern Louisiana.

Population...

Zero.

Or so the records claimed.

As they crossed the city limits...

Every clock stopped.

Every bird vanished.

Even the wind disappeared.

Malik slowly reached for his weapon.

"...I hate quiet places."

Sienna whispered,

"...Something's watching us."

Aidan raised one hand.

His fingers formed the familiar sign.

"Reveal."

The Sixth Law answered.

Crimson runes spread across the abandoned streets.

The illusion shattered.

The town wasn't empty.

Hundreds of people stood frozen exactly where they'd been ten years earlier.

A barber.

A teacher.

A mother holding her child.

None of them had aged.

None of them had moved.

They looked like statues...

With beating hearts.

Lyra's voice trembled.

"They're alive..."

The Crimson Legacy appeared.
