That evening, the company stopped beside an ancient river.

The campfire burned low.

Everyone else slept.

Only Lyra remained awake.

She watched Aidan sitting alone at the edge of the water.

His reflection stared back at him.

Then...

For only an instant...

The reflection changed.

One eye...

Glowed a deep crimson gold.

It wasn't the eye of a vampire.

It wasn't the eye of a witch.

It was...

A wolf.

Lyra's heart stopped.

She knew that eye.

She had seen it only once before.

The day she performed the Sacred Seal.

She quietly whispered to herself...

"...It's beginning."
