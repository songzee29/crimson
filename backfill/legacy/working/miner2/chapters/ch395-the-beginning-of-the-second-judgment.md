Far away...

Black Hollow.

Cassian sat alone in his home.

The fire crackled softly.

His partner placed a meal in front of him.

"You haven't eaten."

Cassian stared at it.

"I know."

"You barely speak anymore."

"I know."

"You leave every night."

"I know."

His partner finally asked the question that had been growing for weeks.

"Who is Aidan?"

Cassian froze.

Silence filled the room.

At last...

He answered.

"The man..."

"...I should never have underestimated."

His partner reached for his hand.

Cassian instinctively pulled away.

Not because he didn't love them.

Because...

He was afraid.

Afraid that one day...

The decree would demand another piece of his peace.

Outside...

The wind carried crimson leaves through the valley.

The Darkheart Brand beneath Cassian's skin glowed faintly.

Not painfully.

Patiently.

The Second Judgment had not yet arrived.

But history...

Was quietly arranging the pieces.
