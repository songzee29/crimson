High above the battlefield...

Azrath stood beneath a dying cypress tree.

Hands behind his back.

Watching.

Not interfering.

The rain rolled off his black coat without leaving a mark.

He quietly whispered to himself.

"Excellent..."

Neither of them noticed him.

Neither realized...

This wasn't the battle.

This was the beginning of one.

He watched Aidan's Witch Authority grow more unstable with every strike.

He watched Cassian lean harder into the hunger that now fueled his strength.

Two storms.

Colliding.

Azrath smiled.

"One will lose his humanity..."

"...the other already has."

Lightning split the sky.

For the briefest instant...

The swamp looked blood-red.

The war between the Crimson King and the murderer of Elias...

Had finally begun.

And neither of them intended to leave that swamp unchanged.
