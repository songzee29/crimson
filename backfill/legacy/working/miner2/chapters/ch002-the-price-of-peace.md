The journey to Black Hollow was silent.

No one joked.

No one laughed.

Even Rez remained unusually quiet.

He rode beside Aidan, studying him.

"...You're not angry."

Aidan kept his eyes on the road.

"No."

"Then what are you?"

A long silence followed.

Finally...

Aidan answered.

"...Tired."

---

Lyra looked over.

"Tired?"

Aidan nodded.

"I wrote that decree as a dying man."

"I wanted Cassian to understand what it felt like to lose everything."

The wind carried his words into the forest.

"I never imagined the day would actually come."

---

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf watched quietly.

For the first time...

It saw its king hesitate.

Not out of fear.

Out of compassion.

The Wolf spoke gently.

> **"...Mercy does not weaken a king."**
