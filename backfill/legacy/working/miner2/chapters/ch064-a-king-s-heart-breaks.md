**THUMP!**

**THUMP!**

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf stood before the Sacred Seal.

It watched Lyra through Aidan's eyes.

The woman who had never feared it.

The woman who had spoken to it.

The woman it had accepted as its Queen.

The ancient beast lowered its head.

For the first time in thousands of years...

The Wolf wept.

One tear struck the Sacred Seal.

A crack spread across its surface.

Then another.

Then another.

The entire seal began trembling.

Not from rage.

Not from bloodlust.

From grief.

The Wolf raised its head and released a howl that shook Aidan's soul.

A howl of loss.

A howl that echoed across every Authority.

The Witch Authority answered.

The Vampire Authority answered.

The Sacred Seal glowed brighter than ever before.

The cracks spread farther.

But...

It did not break.

Not yet.

It held.

Barely.

The Wolf looked at Aidan.

Its voice carried the same grief.

No explanation followed.

Only silence.

Something greater...

Was about to happen.

But at that moment...

He cared about only one thing.

"You're not leaving me."

And the entire supernatural world...

Not because a queen was dying.

Because the bloodline of House Darkheart...
