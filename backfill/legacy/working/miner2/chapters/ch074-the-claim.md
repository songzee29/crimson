The chamber fell into complete silence.

Ash drifted through the air like falling snow.

The Ash King stood motionless.

His ancient eyes never left Lyra.

She stood behind Aidan.

Alive.

Breathing.

Changed.

The first woman in history reborn through **True Crimson Blood**.

For the first time...

The Ash King's calm expression shifted.

Not to anger.

To surprise.

---

He took one step forward.

The entire castle groaned beneath his presence.

Every guard outside the chamber fell to one knee.

Not by force.

By instinct.

Even Kael struggled to remain standing.

Mara's runes flickered uncontrollably.

Darius planted his shield into the floor just to keep from falling.

Cassian remained on his knees.

He couldn't even lift his head.

---

Only Aidan remained standing.

Not because he resisted.

Because something inside him had changed forever.

The Crimson Blood flowing through his veins answered the Ash King's presence.

Not with submission.

With defiance.

---

The Ash King finally spoke.

> **"She crossed the threshold."**

His eyes settled on Lyra.

> **"By every law that came before..."**

> **"...her soul belongs to death."**

He looked back at Aidan.

> **"I have come to claim what is mine."**
