The Ash King ignored everyone except Aidan.

His ancient gaze shifted to Lyra.

> **"Stand beside your King."**

The Ash King nodded.

> **"Look carefully..."**

He gestured toward them.

> **"History changes..."**

> **"...not when kings are born."**

His eyes rested upon the crimson glow flowing beneath Lyra's skin.

A long silence followed.

Realizing...
