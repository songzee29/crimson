The Mirror Witch laughed as dozens of reflections stepped from the broken glass.

Every copy attacked.

The Circle split apart.

Aidan fought three.

Lyra fought two.

Malik was pinned beneath collapsing debris.

Before Aidan could move—

Elias was already there.

He lifted a concrete support beam with everything he had.

His muscles trembled.

Blood ran down his arms.

"Come on!"

Malik crawled free.

"Elias!"

The beam finally gave way.

Aidan appeared beside him.

Together...

They threw it aside.

Elias collapsed onto one knee.

Breathing hard.

Aidan offered him a hand.

"You didn't have to do that."

Elias looked up.

A grin spread across his face.

"Yeah..."

"I kinda did."

Aidan frowned.

"Why?"

Elias stood.

Brushed the dust from his jacket.

Then simply said...

> **"Because that's what family does."**

Silence.

No one said anything.

But something changed inside Aidan.

He had lost his parents.

He had never really known Zee.

He had spent most of his life alone.

Yet somehow...

This man...

Who had only known him for a short time...

Had begun filling a place in his heart he didn't realize was empty.
