The fight lasted nearly an hour.

Roots erupted from beneath the swamp.

Entire trees became weapons.

Malik was thrown into the water.

Sienna was nearly crushed beneath a collapsing cypress.

Lyra burned through almost all of her magic protecting civilians trapped in the marsh.

The Guardian fought like a man thirty years younger.

But even together...

They weren't winning.

The Hollow King kept regenerating.

Again.

Again.

Again.

Aidan landed dozens of attacks.

Nothing worked.

Then...

He remembered.

The Living Curse.

Every curse had a heart.

He closed his eyes.

Ignored the chaos.

Curse Sense expanded.

Everything became crimson.

Then...

He found it.

Not in the creature's chest.

Not in its head.

Buried beneath the swamp.

An ancient stone altar feeding it power.

His eyes opened.

"I found the source!"

The Guardian shouted,

"Then GO!"

"I'll hold it!"
