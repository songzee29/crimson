Cassian burst free.

His fist slammed into Aidan's jaw.

The Crimson King flew backward across the swamp.

Nothing moved.

Cassian slowly approached.

One step.

Then another.

He stood over Aidan.

"...Get up."

"...Come on."

Still nothing.

Cassian kicked him.

"...GET UP!"

Aidan coughed violently.

Then...

He smiled.

"...Still..."

"...not enough."
