That night...

The Guardian sat alone with Aidan.

Neither touched the tea growing cold between them.

Finally...

The Guardian spoke.

"I met Zee once."

Aidan looked up.

"He was older than you are now."

"He'd just lost someone."

"What happened?"

The Guardian smiled sadly.

"He disappeared."

Aidan's expression tightened.

"...Like me."

The Guardian nodded.

"For a while."

Silence settled between them.

Then the Guardian leaned forward.

"Do you know what brought him back?"

Aidan shook his head.

"People."

"...The people he still had."

The words lingered.

The Guardian continued.

"Pain tells you to close every door."

"But love..."

"It keeps knocking."
