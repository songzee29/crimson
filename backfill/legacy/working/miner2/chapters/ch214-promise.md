Elias saw the spears.

He didn't think.

He didn't hesitate.

He smiled.

Just like always.

Then...

He moved.

Throwing himself between Aidan and the attack.

The first spear pierced his shoulder.

The second tore through his side.

The third...

Went straight through his heart.

Silence.

The world stopped.

Rain continued falling.

Elias staggered backward.

Looking down at the crimson spreading across his shirt.

Then...

He laughed softly.

"...Guess..."

"...I won the race."

Aidan caught him before he hit the ground.

"No..."

"No..."

"No..."

Blood covered Aidan's hands.

"Elias..."

"Stay with me."

"Please."

Elias looked up.

His vision already fading.

He reached up with shaking fingers...

And rested his hand against Aidan's face.

"I told you..."

"...family..."

"...protects each other."

Tears streamed down Aidan's face.

"I'm sorry..."

"I'm so sorry..."

Elias smiled.

"No."

His voice barely existed now.

"This..."

"...was my choice."

He struggled to breathe.

One final sentence.

The words they'd one day echo through every part of Aidan's life.

> **"...Don't lose yourself..."**

His hand slipped away.

His eyes closed.

His heartbeat...

Stopped.
