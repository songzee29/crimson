The forgotten road ended before a massive stone archway.

No doors.

No walls.

Just an ancient gateway standing alone in the forest.

Its surface was carved with names.

Thousands of them.

Some fresh.

Most faded beyond recognition.

Mara stepped closer.

"These are people..."

Darius placed his hand on the stone.

"...Every missing person."

Kael looked higher.

"There are older names too."

Rez's expression hardened.

"This has been happening..."

"...for centuries."

Then...

The names began to disappear.

One by one.

Letters faded from the stone.

As though unseen hands were wiping them away.

The little boy Aidan had rescued cried out.

"My mother's name!"

He ran toward the gate.

Her name vanished...

Completely.

The forest trembled.

The ground shook beneath their feet.

Beyond the ancient archway...

A slow, heavy footstep echoed from the darkness.

**...THUD...**

Then another.

**...THUD...**

The Wolf Authority surged against the Sacred Seal.

Not to escape.

To protect.

Its voice thundered through Aidan's soul.

> **"...He comes..."**

Aidan's crimson-and-gold eyes ignited.

He drew his sword.

Not for Cassian.

Not for revenge.

For everyone standing behind him.

The unseen figure beyond the gate took one more step.

The air itself turned to drifting ash.

And for the first time...

The Crimson King came face to face...

With the shadow of an enemy so ancient that even history struggled to remember its name.
