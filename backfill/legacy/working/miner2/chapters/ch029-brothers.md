Later that night...

Aidan stood on the highest balcony.

Rez joined him.

Neither spoke for a while.

Finally...

Rez broke the silence.

> **"You're blaming yourself."**

Aidan didn't deny it.

*"I wrote the decree."*

> **"A different Aidan wrote it."**

*"He's still me."*

Rez leaned against the stone railing.

> **"Maybe."**

> **"...But this Aidan has something that one never did."**

*"What's that?"*

Rez smiled.

> **"People worth protecting."**

Silence.

Then Aidan quietly laughed.

*"You always know what to say."*

> **"That's literally my job."**

The two shared a quiet smile.

Brothers.

Bound by soul.

Bound by Legacy.
