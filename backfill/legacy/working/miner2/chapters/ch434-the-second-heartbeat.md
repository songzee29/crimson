The journey north had changed.

Not because the road was different.

Because Aidan was.

For three days...

He hadn't slept.

Not because he couldn't.

Because every time he closed his eyes...

He heard it.

**THUMP.**

He opened his eyes.

Silence.

Then again...

**THUMP.**

Not his vampire heart.

Something...

Older.

Something trapped.

Something alive.

"You've been hearing it too, haven't you?"

Aidan looked over.

"...You know."

Rez nodded.

"I know that look."

"What look?"

"The one Zee used to have..."

"...right before something inside him changed forever."
