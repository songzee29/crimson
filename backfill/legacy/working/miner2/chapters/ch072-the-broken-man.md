Azrath appeared without warning.

Leaning against the wall.

Perfect suit.

Perfect smile.

Perfect patience.

He looked around Cassian's room.

"So empty."

Cassian frowned.

"What do you want?"

Azrath ignored him.

Instead...

He picked up an old photograph.

The Circle.

Everyone smiling.

Aidan in the center.

Cassian standing near the edge.

Azrath looked at it for several seconds.

Then quietly asked,

"...Do you know where you're standing?"

Cassian looked confused.

"What?"

Azrath pointed.

"Everyone is facing him."

Another pause.

"Even you."

Cassian's face hardened.

"So?"

Azrath placed the picture back exactly where he found it.

"...Even in your happiest memory..."

"...you were still living in his shadow."
