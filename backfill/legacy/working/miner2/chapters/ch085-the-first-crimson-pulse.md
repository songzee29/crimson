Suddenly...

Lyra's body stiffened.

Her eyes closed.

A deep crimson light spread beneath her skin.

**THUMP.**

Everyone in the room felt it.

Not heard it.

Felt it.

Another pulse.

**THUMP.**

The heartbeat echoed through the castle.

Then across House Darkheart.

Every vampire stopped.

Every witch looked toward the castle.

Every wolf lifted its head.

The pulse carried only one message.

**The Crimson Bloodline lives.**

Lyra gasped.

Her eyes opened.

They burned brighter than before.

Not with hunger.

Not with rage.

With power.

She looked at her own hands.

Crimson runes flowed beneath her skin like living veins.

"What..."

"...am I?"
