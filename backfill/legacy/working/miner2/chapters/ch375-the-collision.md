Night descended.

A narrow mountain pass cut through the cliffs.

Only one path crossed it.

Aidan and Rez entered from the south.

Cassian entered from the north.

The wind stopped.

The stars disappeared behind clouds.

For several long moments...

No one moved.

Cassian's eyes locked onto Aidan.

He barely recognized him.

The frightened young man he once killed...

Was gone.

Before him stood a king wearing two crowns.

Authority rolled from Aidan like an ocean.

Then Cassian looked beside him...

His eyes widened.

"...Impossible..."

Rez.

Alive.

Standing beside the Crimson King.

The color drained from Cassian's face.

His voice barely escaped as a whisper.

> **"...You..."**

Rez slowly stepped forward.

His crimson eyes never left Cassian.

After everything...

After centuries...

After death...

He spoke only four words.

> **"...You've been hard to find."**

The mountain pass trembled.

Not from magic.

Not from power.

From history itself.

The past...

Had finally caught up with the present.
