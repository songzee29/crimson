The wind picked up.

Neither moved.

Neither attacked.

Two rulers.

Standing above a sleeping city.

Azrath broke the silence.

"You think I'm your enemy."

"You are."

"No."

Another smile.

"I'm your examination."

Aidan raised one crimson-lit hand.

The Witch Authority spread beneath his feet.

"Leave my city."

Azrath looked down at the crimson runes.

Almost amused.

"Not yet."

His eyes met Aidan's.

"I haven't finished teaching you."

Aidan's voice dropped.

"I don't need your lessons."

Azrath nodded.

"I know."

"...That's why you'll remember them."

Without another word...

He stepped backward.

There was no portal.

No spell.

The shadows simply welcomed him.

He disappeared.

Leaving only one black feather drifting onto the rooftop.

Aidan picked it up.

The moment his fingers touched it...

The Crimson Legacy flashed violently.

> **WARNING**
>
> **DIRECT CONTACT CONFIRMED**
>
> **HOUSE DARKHEART PROTOCOLS INITIATED**

Then the screen vanished.

Aidan stood alone beneath the crimson moon.

Far below...

Cassian watched from the darkness.

His eyes never left the Crimson King.

He reached into his coat.

The crimson vial was still there.

His grip tightened around it.

For the first time...

He wasn't wondering **if** he would use it.

He was wondering...

**When.**

And high above them all...

Azrath watched from the bell tower.

His smile never wavered.

The game had entered its next phase.

Not one of swords.

Not one of spells.

But one of **choice**.

Because Azrath knew something neither Aidan nor Cassian had yet realized:

The day Cassian drank from that vial...

History would begin marching toward the moment Aidan's heart stopped beating.

And when that day came...

The Vampire Curse would awaken.

Not as an accident.

But as the final consequence...

Of every choice made since Elias fell.
