Outside...

Aidan hadn't moved.

He still held Elias.

Blood dripped through trembling fingers.

His head remained lowered.

No one could see his eyes.

Lyra slowly stepped forward.

"Aidan..."

Nothing.

Malik followed.

"...Brother?"

Nothing.

The crimson storm became stronger.

Buildings around them began cracking.

Streetlights exploded.

Car alarms screamed.

Magic poured from Aidan's body in violent waves.

Not attacking anyone.

Simply...

Escaping.

Like it couldn't remain inside him anymore.

---

Then...

Aidan spoke.

So quietly...

Nobody almost heard him.

> "...I promised..."

Silence.

> "...I said..."

His shoulders shook.

> "...I'd save you..."

Another pause.

Then...

A scream.

Not loud.

Not angry.

A scream so broken...

That everyone who heard it felt their own hearts hurt.

Even the Veil hunters lowered their weapons.

Because this wasn't rage.

It was grief made audible.
