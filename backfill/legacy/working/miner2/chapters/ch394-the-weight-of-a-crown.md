That afternoon...

Aidan walked through the city alone.

No royal escort.

No guards.

People greeted him naturally.

A baker offered fresh bread.

Children waved.

An elderly woman stopped him.

"My King."

He stopped.

She smiled warmly.

"My grandson starts training next week."

Aidan nodded.

"He'll have good teachers."

She reached out and squeezed his hand.

"Thank you."

No speeches.

No ceremonies.

Just gratitude.

As Aidan continued walking...

Rez watched from a distance.

He smiled to himself.

"Now I understand..."

He whispered.

"You didn't become powerful because you're king..."

"You became king because of who you are."
