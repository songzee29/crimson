Miles away...

Cassian collapsed to one knee.

The Darkheart Crest covering his forearms glowed brighter than ever before.

His partner rushed toward him.

"It hurts again?"

Cassian nodded through clenched teeth.

"...Every hour now."

The burning wasn't physical anymore.

It was deeper.

His soul felt restless.

Every peaceful moment became unbearable.

Every smile felt empty.

Every laugh ended too quickly.

He hadn't slept through the night in weeks.

His dreams were all the same.

A crimson throne.

A young king.

One sentence.

Over...

And over...

And over.

> **"You shall never know peace..."**

Cassian slammed his fist into the ground.

"...Enough."

His partner stepped back.

Concern filled their face.

"Cassian..."

He slowly stood.

His eyes had changed.

Not with fear.

With obsession.

"...If peace won't come to me..."

He looked toward the southern horizon.

"...Then I'll go to the one who stole it."

---

At that exact moment...

The Darkheart Brand erupted in crimson fire.

Not because Aidan had activated it.

Because...

Cassian had finally made the choice the decree had been waiting for.

He chose judgment.
