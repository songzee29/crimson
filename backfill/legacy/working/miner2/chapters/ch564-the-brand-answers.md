Hundreds of miles away...

Cassian awoke screaming.

The Darkheart Brand blazed across his arm.

Not a single mark.

The entire decree burned crimson beneath his skin.

He stumbled out of bed.

His partner ran after him.

"Cassian!"

He didn't stop.

His feet carried him into the forest.

He didn't know why.

He couldn't control it.

The Brand wasn't pulling him toward Aidan.

It was pulling him...

Toward a choice.

Deep within the woods...

He collapsed to one knee.

The burning finally stopped.

Before him...

Someone stood beneath the trees.

Hidden by a hood.

The stranger spoke only one sentence.

> **"How much longer..."**

> **"...can you watch innocent people suffer..."**

> **"...because of the House of Darkheart?"**

Cassian clenched his fists.

"I won't be manipulated."

The stranger smiled beneath the hood.

> **"Good."**

> **"...Then make your own decision."**

The figure vanished into drifting ash.

Cassian remained alone.

The question...

Did not.
