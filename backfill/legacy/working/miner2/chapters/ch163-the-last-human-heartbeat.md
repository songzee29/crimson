Silence.

No thunder.

No rain.

No wind.

The swamp...

Simply watched.

---

Aidan stood alone.

His breathing had become shallow.

Blood dripped from both hands.

His knuckles had long since split apart.

Every muscle in his body begged him to stop.

The System appeared one last time.

---

> **Authority Reserves: 2%**

> **Multiple Organ Failure**

> **Cardiac Rhythm... Unstable**

> **Recommended Action...**

The words stopped.

As if the System itself already knew.

There was no recommendation left to give.

---

Across the battlefield...

Cassian slowly stood.

His regeneration had almost stopped.

One eye remained swollen shut.

His breathing was ragged.

His vampire body was nearing its own limit.

He looked at Aidan.

Then quietly whispered...

"...Why..."

"...won't you fall?"

---

Aidan smiled.

Weakly.

Because the answer was simple.

"...Because..."

His voice cracked.

"...if I fall..."

He looked past Cassian.

Toward New Orleans.

Toward everyone waiting for him.

"...who stands..."

Another cough.

Blood spilled onto the ground.

"...for them?"

---

Lyra collapsed to her knees.

"...Please..."

She couldn't stop crying.
