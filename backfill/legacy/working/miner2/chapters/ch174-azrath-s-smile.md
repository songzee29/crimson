For the first time since Aidan died...

Azrath smiled.

Not because he had won.

Because...

Everything had unfolded exactly as he predicted.

He quietly whispered...

"...Welcome..."

"...to eternity."

He turned.

Beginning to walk away.

The plan was complete.

Cassian had killed the king.

The curse had awakened.

There was nothing left to watch.

---

Then...

He stopped.

Not because he heard something.

Because...

He didn't.

There should have been silence.

Instead...

There was...

Nothing.

An absence so unnatural that even Azrath slowly turned back toward the battlefield.

"...What...?"
