The Ash King slowly lowered his hands.

The battle paused.

Ash drifted quietly between them.

He looked around the hall.

The names.

The pillars.

The crimson light.

Then back to Aidan.

For the first time...

He bowed.

Not deeply.

Just enough to acknowledge an equal.

> **"...Now I understand..."**

He looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

> **"...Why history refused to erase House Darkheart."**

Aidan returned the bow.

Neither king had won.

Neither had surrendered.

But something had changed.

The Ash King no longer saw a child carrying Zee's blood.

He saw...

**Aidan Darkheart.**

The Crimson King.

And deep within Aidan's soul...

The Wolf smiled.

> **"...Brother..."**

> **"...You are becoming complete."**

Far beyond the fortress...

The crimson moon rose higher into the sky.

The next phase of the journey was approaching.

The duel had become more than a contest of strength.

It had become a clash between two visions of what a king should leave behind:

One sought to preserve the past by holding onto it forever.

The other sought to protect the future by allowing people to live, choose, and be remembered.

The outcome of that conflict would shape every generation that followed.
