That night...

Aidan stood atop the castle walls.

The wind rolled across the kingdom.

Rez's voice broke the silence.

**"Can I ask you something?"**

*"Since when do you ask permission?"*

Rez laughed.

**"Fair enough."**

A pause.

Then—

**"When you first met me..."**

**"...did you trust me?"**

Aidan answered honestly.

"No."

Rez pretended to sound offended.

**"That hurts."**

"You were a mysterious voice living in my head."

"I think I was justified."

Rez laughed harder.

**"Fair point."**

Aidan leaned against the stone wall.

"But somewhere along the way..."

"You stopped feeling like the Legacy."

"You started feeling like family."

For several seconds...

Rez said nothing.

Then quietly answered,

**"...Thank you."**
