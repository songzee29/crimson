The forgotten chamber had not been opened for centuries.

Ancient Darkheart seals still covered the stone doors.

The moment Aidan stepped inside...

Every seal burned away on its own.

The chamber recognized its king.

At its center...

The cocoon split open.

**CRACK.**

Crimson fluid spilled across the floor.

A hand emerged.

Then another.

A tall figure slowly stood.

Black hair.

Ancient crimson markings stretching across pale skin.

A long crimson cloak draped from its shoulders.

Its eyes...

Remained closed.

It did not breathe.

It did not speak.

It simply stood.

Waiting.

The System scanned it.

Aidan watched silently.

The Crimson Assassin slowly turned toward him.

For several long seconds...

Nothing happened.

Then...

It lowered one knee.

Not out of loyalty.

Not out of respect.

Instinct.

The body recognized the blood standing before it.

The heir to the Crimson Legacy.

---

Without warning...

The chamber exploded.

A crimson spear burst through the ceiling.

The attack was aimed directly at Aidan's head.

Neither Aidan nor the Assassin had been expecting an ambush.

The Assassin moved first.

Its eyes never opened.

Its body simply reacted.

**SLASH!**

The spear split cleanly in two before reaching Aidan.

Silence returned.

The Assassin resumed kneeling.

As though nothing had happened.

---

Aidan looked at the shattered weapon.

Then at the silent figure.

"...Interesting."
