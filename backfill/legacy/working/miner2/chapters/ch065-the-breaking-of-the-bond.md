The chamber was silent.

No one moved.

No one dared to speak.

Aidan held Lyra against his chest.

Her blood soaked through his clothes.

His hands.

His heart.

Every second...

Her heartbeat became weaker.

"No..."

His voice trembled.

"You promised me forever."

Lyra smiled faintly.

"...Forever..."

"...isn't measured..."

"...by time..."

She coughed.

Blood stained her lips.

"...It's measured..."

"...by love..."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

"We still have QuinZ..."

"We still have Zuko..."

"We still have Azula..."

"Our family..."

"Our future..."

Lyra's eyes softened.

"They'll still come..."

"...because you'll never stop loving me..."

A tear rolled down her face.

Then another.

She slowly reached toward Aidan's chest.

Not toward his heart.

Toward the place...

Where she had secretly bound the Wolf so long ago.

Her fingertips rested there.

"Aidan..."

"...there's something..."

"...I have to tell you..."
