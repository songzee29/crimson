The moon hung low over New Orleans as Aidan, Lyra, and Malik walked away from the cemetery.

No one spoke for several minutes.

The old man's words refused to leave their minds.

*"We fought... because we kept burying children."*

Malik finally broke the silence.

"...You think we'll see him again?"

Aidan nodded.

"Definitely."

"You don't know that."

"I don't."

He looked back toward the cemetery.

"...But someone like him doesn't just disappear."

Lyra watched Aidan carefully.

He had changed.

Not physically.

His posture was different.

He wasn't walking like a frightened teenager anymore.

He was walking like someone beginning to carry responsibility.

That both comforted and terrified her.
