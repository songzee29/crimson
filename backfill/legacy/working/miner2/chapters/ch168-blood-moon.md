The feast was over.

The forest should have become quiet again.

It didn't.

Aidan slowly lifted his head.

Something...

Was wrong.

The wind had stopped.

The insects had vanished.

Even the trees stood unnaturally still.

---

The Crimson Wolf's ears twitched.

Every instinct screamed the same warning.

**Leave.**

The wolf inside him growled.

**No...**

**Something is watching us.**

---

The entire pack felt it.

Malik stopped mid-step.

Darius' hackles rose.

Kael's runes faintly glowed beneath his crimson fur.

Lyra's crimson eyes scanned the darkness between the trees.

Not one of them could see anything.

Yet...

They all knew.

They weren't alone.

---

Rez emerged from the shadows.

He didn't smile.

Not this time.

His eyes slowly traveled across the forest.

"I know this feeling..."

Mara stepped beside him.

"What is it?"

Rez answered quietly.

"A predator..."

"...that doesn't breathe."

---

Silence.

Then...

A howl.

Not a wolf.

Not any creature the pack recognized.

It echoed through the mountains.

Long.

Ancient.

Wrong.

The sound carried no life.

Only hunger.

---

The Crimson Wolf answered instinctively.

A thunderous howl exploded from Aidan's chest.

The forest shook.

The Crimson Pack answered immediately.

Five voices became one.

The sound rolled across the wilderness.

A declaration.

A warning.

This territory belonged to House Darkheart.

---

Nothing answered.

For several seconds...

There was only silence.

Then...

Every tree around them began to move.

Not from the wind.

From the shadows climbing across them.

The darkness itself twisted.

Flowed.

Gathered.

The forest became unnaturally dim.

The morning sun disappeared behind a veil of crimson clouds.

---

**Ding.**

The notification froze.

Then vanished.

---

Rez's face hardened.

"I don't like that."

Aidan didn't take his eyes off the trees.

*Neither do I.*

---

A branch snapped.

Then another.

Then another.

Heavy footsteps echoed through the valley.

Slow.

Deliberate.

Every step carried enough weight to shake the ground.

BOOM...

BOOM...

BOOM...

---

Malik instinctively moved closer to Aidan.

Not from fear.

From instinct.

The Alpha stood at the center.

The pack closed ranks around him.

Lyra took Aidan's left.

Kael the right.

Darius guarded the rear.

Mara raised both hands, Crimson Laws already forming around her fingertips.

Rez slowly drew one of his crimson blades.

The metal sang as it left the sheath.

"I was hoping I'd never hear those footsteps again."

---

The footsteps stopped.

A pair of enormous eyes opened between the trees.

Not red.

Not gold.

Black.

So black they seemed to swallow the light around them.

Then another pair opened.

Then another.

Then dozens.

The forest wasn't hiding one creature.

It was hiding an entire pack.

---

Malik's breathing became heavier.

"...How many..."

Rez answered without looking away.

"Too many."

---

The first creature stepped into the clearing.

It resembled a wolf...

If a wolf had been carved from living shadow.

Its body was scarred with glowing crimson cracks.

Its claws were longer than swords.

Its mouth dripped black blood onto the forest floor.

Behind it...

Another emerged.

Then another.

Then another.

An entire pack of nightmare wolves surrounded the Crimson Pack.

---

The leader stepped forward.

Larger than all the others.

It stood nearly eye-to-eye with Aidan's Crimson Wolf.

Its black eyes locked onto the Crimson King.

Then...

It smiled.

Not like an animal.

Like something intelligent.

Something ancient.

Something that had been waiting.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf growled so deeply the mental landscape trembled.

**Ancient enemy.**

The vampire's voice lost all amusement.

**I've tasted blood like this once...**

**Long ago.**

Aidan's eyes narrowed.

*Do you know them?*

Neither answered.

---

The leader of the shadow wolves took one slow step forward.

Its voice echoed through the clearing.

Not spoken.

Felt.

**Founder...**

The Crimson Pack stiffened.

The creature knew exactly who Aidan was.

**...We have come...**

Its black eyes burned into Aidan's crimson-and-gold gaze.

**...to see if your blood is worthy of ruling the hunt.**

Silence.

Aidan slowly stepped in front of his pack.

His crimson fur seemed to glow brighter beneath the darkened sky.

The crimson-and-gold eyes of the Founder never wavered.

He lowered his head.

A low growl rolled through the valley.

Not born of fear.

Not born of anger.

A king accepting a challenge.

The shadow alpha answered with its own growl.

The sound split the mountains.

The Blood Moon rose above the forest...

Though it wasn't supposed to rise until nightfall.

The hunt was over.

The trial...

Had just begun.
