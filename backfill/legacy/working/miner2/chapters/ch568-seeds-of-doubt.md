Black Hollow.

Cassian stood in the rebuilt village square.

Children laughed around him.

Families rebuilt homes.

Life had returned.

Yet every smile reminded him of those who never came back.

An elder approached.

"My Lord..."

Cassian turned.

"Yes?"

"Do you think..."

The elder hesitated.

"...the Ash King will return?"

Cassian looked toward the mountains.

"...Yes."

"And if he does?"

Cassian answered honestly.

"...Then we'll stand."

The elder nodded.

"But..."

He lowered his voice.

"...Can we stand alone?"

Cassian didn't answer.

Because...

For the first time...

He wasn't sure.
