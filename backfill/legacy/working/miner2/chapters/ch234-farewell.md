The city belonged to the Circle once more.

But victory tasted like ash.

Aidan knelt beside Elias again.

He carefully picked up the old firefighter badge Elias always carried.

It was bent.

Covered in blood.

Aidan closed his hand around it.

"So this..."

He whispered.

"...is all that's left."

The Journal suddenly appeared beside him.

Opening on its own.

Ink slowly spread across the page.

---

> **A brother gave his tomorrow...**

Another line appeared.

> **...so another brother could have one.**

The page stopped writing.

Aidan stared at it.

Then quietly closed the journal.

"No."

He whispered.

"...There has to be more."
