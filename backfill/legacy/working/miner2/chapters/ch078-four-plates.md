An hour later...

The three of them were sitting inside a small restaurant tucked between two centuries-old buildings.

Malik talked enough for three people.

"...So then my professor tells me I can't keep a pet crow inside the dorm."

Lyra nearly choked on her drink.

"You had a crow?"

"I had two."

Aidan blinked.

"...Why?"

"They found me."

"That's not an answer."

"It was enough for them."

Lyra laughed.

"I don't even know if you're joking."

Malik shrugged.

"I don't either."

For the first time in what felt like forever...

Aidan forgot about the Crimson Legacy.

Forgot about the Veil.

Forgot about curses.

He was just...

A guy eating dinner with friends.

The System appeared for only a heartbeat.
