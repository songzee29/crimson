Cassian finally reached the border of House Darkheart.

He didn't cross it.

He simply stood there.

Watching.

The kingdom was alive.

Children laughed.

Merchants traded.

Families smiled.

He remembered...

Once...

He had wanted exactly this for everyone.

Then he looked down at his hand.

The ring from the ashes rested in his palm.

His entire rebuilt life...

Reduced to one memory.

He slowly closed his fist.

His voice was barely a whisper.

> **"...Aidan..."**

> **"...You made me understand loss..."**

A long pause.

Then...

For the first time...

He spoke the words that terrified even himself.

> **"...Now..."**

> **"...it's your turn."**

The wind carried those words toward House Darkheart.

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf's eyes snapped open.

The second heartbeat thundered.

**THUMP!**

Only Aidan saw the Crimson Legacy.

Aidan closed his eyes.

He had hoped...

There would still be another path.

Now...

That hope was beginning to fade.

The road toward the Third Judgment had truly begun.

And somewhere in the darkness...

The Ash King watched in silence.

He did not manipulate.

Because he knew something neither king was yet willing to admit:

The greatest battles are not won by strength.

They are won...

Or lost...

By the choices made before the swords are ever drawn.
