Valerius threw seven punches.

Aidan blocked six.

The seventh struck his shoulder.

Instead of retreating...

Aidan stepped deeper into the attack.

Valerius' eyes widened.

"...Interesting."

Aidan's Witch Authority surged.

Not to cast.

To calculate.

The System remained silent.

It wasn't teaching.

It was watching.

Recording.

Learning.

Exactly as the Master Decree intended.

---

Aidan twisted.

Caught Valerius' wrist.

Blood Dominion answered.

The blood scattered across the battlefield rose into the air.

Not as weapons.

As mirrors.

Thousands of floating crimson reflections surrounded the arena.

Each reflected a different angle.

A different opening.

A different possibility.

Valerius looked around slowly.

"...You're using the battlefield..."

"...to think."

---

Aidan released his grip.

"...Every drop remembers."
