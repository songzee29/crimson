Darkness.

Cold water swallowed everything.

The current slammed Aidan against ancient stone as he fought to stay conscious.

His lungs burned.

The roar of the underground river drowned out every sound.

Then—

A hand grabbed his arm.

Elias.

Without saying a word, he forced Aidan toward the surface.

Seconds later—

They burst into the open air.

Aidan gasped violently.

"Elias!"

"I'm good!"

"Lyra!"

"I'm here!"

One by one the Circle surfaced, clinging to broken pieces of stone as the river carried them deeper beneath New Orleans.

Then...

The Guardian never surfaced.

Silence.

Aidan looked around desperately.

"...Guardian?"

Nothing.

Only rushing water.

His right leg began bouncing uncontrollably as panic settled into his chest.

"No..."

"I'm going back."

Aidan dove beneath the water.

Elias immediately grabbed him.

"No!"

"He could still be alive!"

"So could you!"

Aidan struggled.

"LET ME GO!"

Elias refused.

"You don't get to die trying to save everyone!"

The words echoed through the cavern.

For the first time...

Aidan stopped fighting.

Not because he agreed.

Because he knew Elias was right.

That truth hurt more than anything.
