Neither moved.

The night itself seemed unwilling to interrupt.

Rez looked around.

"The last thing I remember..."

He paused.

"...was the Book."

Aidan gave a small nod.

"...A lot has happened."

Rez looked at the sword lying on the road.

Then back at Aidan.

"...Was I..."

He hesitated.

"...protecting you?"

Aidan answered simply.

"...Every step."

Rez lowered his head.

A quiet laugh escaped him.

"So..."

"...even asleep..."

"...I couldn't stop watching your back."

For the first time in a long while...

The corner of Aidan's mouth lifted.

Not a grin.

Just enough to show recognition.

"...No."

He looked directly at Rez.

"...You never left."

Those words struck harder than any blade.

Rez closed his eyes for a moment.

When he opened them again...

The confusion was gone.

Only resolve remained.

He picked up the crimson sword.

Its weight felt familiar.

Like an old friend.

He rested it across his shoulder.

"So..."

He looked down the road.

"...where are we going?"

Aidan turned toward the mountains.

His expression became cold once more.

"...To fulfill a decree."

Rez followed Aidan's gaze.

Far beyond the horizon...

He felt it.

The lingering stain of the Darkheart Curse.

He smiled.

Not because he wanted blood.

Because he finally understood.

The old world...

Had returned.

And this time...

It would stand beside the Crimson King.
