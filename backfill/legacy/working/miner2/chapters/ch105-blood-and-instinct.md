The creature struck before anyone reacted.

It wasn't a wolf.

It wasn't a bear.

It was something twisted between both.

Its body was covered in black fur threaded with crimson veins.

Its eyes glowed with cursed symbols.

Nearly twelve feet long.

Aidan barely activated **Hex Step** in time.

The beast's claws carved through the concrete where he had stood.

Malik shouted,

"How do we kill that?!"

The Guardian folded his arms.

"You don't."

Lyra frowned.

"What?"

"It isn't your enemy."

The beast roared again.

Charging.

Aidan instinctively formed **Crimson Shackles**.

Chains erupted.

Wrapped around its legs.

For two glorious seconds...

The creature stopped.

Then—

Instead of breaking free...

It looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

Confused.

Like it recognized something.

The Guardian noticed.

"...Interesting."
