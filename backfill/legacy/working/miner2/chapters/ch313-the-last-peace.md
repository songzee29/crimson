The next morning...

Everything felt normal.

Too normal.

Children laughed.

Markets opened.

Street musicians played jazz.

Malik complained about paperwork.

Sienna teased him.

The Guardian drank tea.

Lyra quietly watched Aidan.

He smiled back.

She smiled too.

Neither of them knew...

This ordinary morning would become the memory they both clung to for the next one hundred years.

The Journal slowly opened.

Its pages turned by themselves.

Not one.

Not two.

Dozens.

As if searching for something.

Finally...

It stopped.

Ink spread slowly.

Almost reluctantly.

---

> **The happiest day...**

The ink hesitated.

Then continued.

> **...is often the last page before tragedy begins.**

The Journal closed.

Hard.

The sound echoed through the warehouse.

No one heard it.

Except the Crimson Legacy.

Deep within its core...

Another ancient chain shattered.

The Witch Authority flared.

The Soul Anchor pulsed.

The empty Witch Hybrid vessel twitched for the first time.

And in the endless darkness beyond the broken seal...

A single crimson eye...

Opened.

Not fully.

Just enough to see.

Just enough to whisper.

> **"...Aidan..."**

The whisper faded into silence.

But the darkness answered with one terrible truth:

**The Vampire Arc was no longer approaching.**

**It had already begun.**
