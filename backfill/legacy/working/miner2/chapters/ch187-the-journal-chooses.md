Morning never came beneath the Forgotten Sanctuary.

Only the soft glow of crimson crystals reflected across the underground lake.

The Circle had finally found a moment to rest.

Malik was asleep against a broken pillar.

Lyra sat beside the water, quietly skipping smooth stones across its surface.

Elias was repairing everyone's damaged equipment with surprising patience.

Only Aidan remained awake.

The blank journal rested in his lap.

He stared at it.

"...Record me..."

"What does that even mean?"

The System appeared.

---

**《LEGACY JOURNAL》**

**Status: Active**

---

Nothing else.

No explanation.

No instructions.

Aidan sighed.

"You really like making me figure things out."

He closed the journal.

The moment his hand left the cover—

Ink appeared.

By itself.

Slowly.

One word at a time.

---

> **The Heir questions before he commands.**

---

Aidan's heartbeat quickened.

He opened the journal again.

The words remained.

He hadn't written them.

No one had.

The journal had.
