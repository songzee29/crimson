Nobody moved.

Nobody breathed.

The swamp itself seemed frozen beneath the crimson moon.

Aidan stood.

Slowly.

The hole through his heart had vanished.

His clothes remained soaked in blood.

His chest...

Never rose.

No heartbeat.

No breath.

Nothing.

---

Lyra's tears continued falling.

"...Aidan..."

She took one step forward.

The Guardian gently caught her wrist.

"No."

She looked at him.

"But—"

"He knows you're there."

"...He simply can't hear you."

Her heart shattered.

---

Aidan's crimson eyes drifted across the battlefield.

They passed over Malik.

Over Lyra.

Over the Guardian.

Over Azrath.

None of them held his attention.

Then...

His gaze settled on Cassian.

It stayed there.

Unblinking.

Unwavering.

---

Cassian instinctively stepped backward.

"What..."

"...are you?"

Aidan said nothing.

---

Then...

He disappeared.
