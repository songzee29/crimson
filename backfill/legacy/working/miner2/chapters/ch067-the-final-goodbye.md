She could see it in Aidan's eyes.

"The Wolf..."

"...understands..."

"It loves you."

"I know..."

Looking toward Cassian.

He couldn't meet her eyes.

"I'm..."

"...sorry..."

"I never wanted..."

"...this..."

"I know..."

Even now.

Even here.

His forehead struck the stone floor.
