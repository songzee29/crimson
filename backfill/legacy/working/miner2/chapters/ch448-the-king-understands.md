For several long moments...

Aidan said nothing.

The second heartbeat echoed softly.

Then...

He reached out.

Lyra flinched.

Not from fear.

From guilt.

Instead of anger...

Aidan gently pulled her into his arms.

He hugged her.

"You saved my life."

Lyra looked up at him in disbelief.

"...You're not angry?"

He slowly shook his head.

"No."

"You made the only choice you believed could keep me alive."

"I understand."

Tears finally fell down her face.

"I was so afraid you'd hate me."

Aidan smiled softly.

"I don't."

"You gave me more time."

"Without that..."

"There wouldn't be a Crimson King."
