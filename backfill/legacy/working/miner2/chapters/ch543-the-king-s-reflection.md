Aidan stood before the throne.

He never sat.

Instead...

He reached out and rested one hand on its armrest.

The stone responded.

A vision flooded his mind.

He saw Zee.

Standing before the same throne.

He watched his father refuse to sit.

Then turn away.

The vision shifted.

Countless rulers across history had made the same choice.

Some sat...

Seeking power.

Others walked away...

Seeking purpose.

The throne never forced a decision.

It simply revealed the heart of the one standing before it.

The vision faded.

Aidan quietly stepped back.

"I don't need another throne."

Lyra smiled.

"You already have one waiting at home."
