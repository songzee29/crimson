That evening...

As the Circle returned to the warehouse...

Someone was already waiting.

An old woman.

Elegant.

Dressed completely in black.

She wasn't afraid.

She wasn't surprised.

She simply watched Aidan approach.

When he stopped before her...

She bowed.

Not deeply.

Respectfully.

"My King."

Aidan immediately shook his head.

"I'm not a king."

The woman smiled.

"No."

"Not yet."

She reached into her cloak and produced an ancient crimson scroll sealed with black wax.

She held it out with both hands.

"This was entrusted to my family..."

"...to be delivered only when the Crimson King returned."

The Guardian's eyes widened.

"...Returned?"

The woman looked at Aidan.

Not with fear.

With certainty.

"It has waited for generations."

Aidan accepted the scroll.

The seal bore a symbol he had never seen before.

A crown.

Surrounded by thirteen crimson stars.

The moment his fingers touched the wax...

The seal melted on its own.

The scroll slowly unrolled.

Only one sentence was written.

In handwriting Aidan had never seen...

Yet somehow recognized.

> **"If you are reading this... then you survived the Witch Curse. I prayed you would. — Zee."**

Aidan's breath caught.

The world around him faded.

For the first time...

His father wasn't a memory.

He had left him a message.

And somewhere deep within the Crimson Legacy, the Journal quietly opened and wrote:

> **"The next lesson comes not from power... but from a father who believed his son would one day be strong enough to hear the truth."**

The page turned by itself.

The next chapter of Aidan's life had begun.
