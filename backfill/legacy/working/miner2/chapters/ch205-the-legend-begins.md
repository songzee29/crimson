By dawn...

News channels across the country carried the same image.

A blurry frame.

A silhouette standing atop St. Louis Cathedral.

A crown.

Crimson eyes.

Black coat.

Someone called him...

**The Crimson Devil.**

Another called him...

**The Blood King.**

An old woman in the Ninth Ward quietly turned off her television.

She looked toward the city skyline and whispered,

"They've got his name wrong..."

Her granddaughter asked,

"Who is he?"

The old woman closed her eyes.

"No..."

"...that's not a devil."

A long silence.

"That's..."

"...the Crimson King."

---

Far away...

Azrath watched the broadcasts.

A slow smile crossed his face.

"They're afraid."

He looked toward New Orleans.

"But fear..."

"...is only the beginning."

He turned and disappeared into the night.

Because his work here was done.

The city now had a monster.

Soon...

It would need a king.

And House Darkheart had no idea that an even greater tragedy was still waiting for them.
