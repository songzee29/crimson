Caelum stood before an ancient stone tablet hidden beneath the cathedral.

Runes glowed faintly.

One hunter hurried toward him.

"The disappearances continue."

Caelum nodded.

"Good."

"But..."

"We found crimson ash."

Caelum's expression changed for the first time.

"...Where?"

"Black Hollow."

Silence.

He slowly approached the tablet.

"No..."

He whispered.

"...It can't be."

One of the oldest runes upon the stone...

Began glowing crimson.

Not because of Aidan.

Not because of Cassian.

Because something much older...

Had awakened.

Caelum's calm composure vanished.

"Seal every northern passage."

The hunters looked confused.

"Master?"

He turned.

For the first time...

Fear could be seen in his eyes.

"If I'm right..."

"...then none of us are hunting anymore."

"...We're the prey."
