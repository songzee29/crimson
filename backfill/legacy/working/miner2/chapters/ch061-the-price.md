Azrath laughed quietly.

"I was hoping you'd say that."

He reached into his coat.

Removing a tiny crystal vial.

Inside...

One drop.

Dark crimson.

Almost black.

It moved on its own.

Like it was alive.

Cassian frowned.

"...Blood?"

Azrath corrected him.

"No."

He held it toward the light.

"...Possibility."

Cassian reached for it.

Azrath pulled it away.

"Not yet."

Confusion crossed Cassian's face.

"You haven't earned it."

Cassian's expression darkened.

"What do you want?"

Azrath answered immediately.

"Nothing."

A pause.

"I want..."

"...to watch."
