Later that night...

The castle slept peacefully.

Rez stood alone atop the highest tower.

The moonlight reflected from his crimson eyes.

His long coat moved gently with the wind.

He wasn't standing watch because anyone asked him to.

He simply always had.

Inside Aidan's mind...

*Can't sleep?*

Rez smiled.

**Never really needed much of it.**

*Liar.*

**Alright...**

**I'm thinking.**

*About your memories?*

Rez nodded.

**About the people I couldn't remember.**

A long silence followed.

Then Aidan answered.

*We'll find them.*

Rez looked toward the stars.

**Even if they're gone?**

Aidan didn't hesitate.

*Then we'll remember them.*

The Crimson Assassin smiled.

"...Thank you."
