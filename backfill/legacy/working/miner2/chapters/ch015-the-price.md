The next morning...

House Darkheart rode out of Black Hollow.

No battle had taken place.

No innocent blood had been spilled.

Cassian watched them leave.

Confused.

Relieved.

Then...

Smoke rose in the distance.

His heart stopped.

Not from the village.

From the hill.

His home.

The small house he and his partner had spent years building together was burning.

Cassian ran.

Faster than he had in decades.

By the time he arrived...

It was already collapsing.

Every letter.

Every keepsake.

Every memory they had collected over centuries...

Gone.

His partner stood beside him.

Alive.

Safe.

Crying.

Cassian fell to his knees.

The house...

Was beyond saving.

Hundreds of miles away...

Aidan stopped riding.

He looked back only once.

Inside his mind, the Crimson Legacy appeared.
