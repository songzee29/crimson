The Guardian led them back toward the crimson entrance.

Before they reached the door, he stopped.

"You cannot stay here."

Aidan looked disappointed.

"I know."

"No."

The Guardian smiled sadly.

"I mean..."

"The Sanctuary itself won't allow it."

As if answering him—

The candles throughout the Sanctuary began extinguishing themselves.

One...

By one...

By one...

Until only the crimson doorway remained illuminated.

The Sanctuary was going back to sleep.

Waiting.

For the next time its heir returned stronger.

---

The System confirmed it.

> **SANCTUARY CLOSING**

> **RETURN CONDITION:**
>
> **Complete Crimson Witch Authority**

---

Aidan read it twice.

Then quietly whispered,

"So... I really have to pay the price."

No response came.

Because the Legacy had already answered that question long ago.

There were no shortcuts.
