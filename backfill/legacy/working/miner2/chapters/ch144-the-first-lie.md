Later that evening...

Lyra found Aidan alone.

She sat beside him on the palace steps.

Neither spoke for a while.

Finally...

She asked,

"...Are you okay?"

Aidan smiled.

The same smile he'd given the city.

The children.

His friends.

"I'm fine."

The Crimson Legacy flashed.

Only Aidan could see it.

---

Aidan's smile slowly disappeared.

He stared at the message.

His chest tightened.

Because...

The System wasn't accusing him.

It was simply...

Recording the truth.

He wasn't fine.

He hadn't been for a very long time.

And for the first time...

He had lied about it.

The moon drifted higher over New Orleans.

Far away...

Cassian sharpened his sword.

Azrath watched from the darkness.

Lyra cried where no one could see her.

And the Crimson Legacy quietly recorded every step.

Not toward victory.

Not toward defeat.

Toward a fate that none of them could stop.

Except...

Perhaps one.

Deep within the sealed pages of the Crimson Legacy...

Something shifted.

Not enough to awaken.

Not yet.

But for the first time in months...

The sleeping presence inside the book stirred.

Just once.

Then...

Silence.
