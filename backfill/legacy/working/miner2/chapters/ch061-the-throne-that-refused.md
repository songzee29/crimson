The Guardian stepped away from the throne.

"Come."

Aidan hesitated.

"...Me?"

"Yes."

Slowly, Aidan walked toward it.

The throne was carved from black stone threaded with deep crimson crystal.

It wasn't beautiful.

It was heavy.

Every scratch looked earned.

Every crack carried history.

The Guardian spoke softly.

"Sit."

Lyra looked shocked.

"What?"

The old man didn't repeat himself.

Aidan slowly lowered himself toward the throne.

The moment he was inches away—

The entire Sanctuary trembled.

The candles exploded into crimson flames.

The System flashed violently.

Not violently.

Firmly.

The trembling stopped.

Silence.

The Guardian nodded.

"...Good."

"Good?"

"If you had been allowed to sit..."
