The seal split apart.

Stone groaned.

A staircase slowly descended into darkness.

Torch after torch ignited by itself.

No magic circles.

No traps.

No monsters.

Only silence.

Aidan looked downward.

"...Guess we're doing this."

He stepped onto the first stair.

The moment his foot touched it...

The staircase lit completely.

One thousand candles.

One thousand years of darkness.

Gone.

---

Every wall carried carvings.

Not of wars.

Not of monsters.

Of ordinary people.

A king helping rebuild homes.

A king planting trees.

A king carrying children through floodwaters.

A king sitting beside the sick.

Aidan stopped.

"...These aren't battles."

A familiar voice answered.

Not inside his head.

Not the System.

A recording.

Old.

Gentle.

Zee.

> **"No..."**

> **"...because wars make history."**

> **"...but kindness makes kingdoms."**

Aidan's throat tightened.
