That evening...

Aidan stood on the balcony overlooking New Orleans.

Lights filled the streets below.

Music echoed through the French Quarter.

Lyra stepped beside him.

"You've been quiet."

He smiled.

"I was thinking."

"About?"

He looked over the city.

"How easy it would be..."

She looked at him.

"...to lose all of this."

Lyra frowned.

"You won't."

Aidan slowly shook his head.

"No king gets to decide that."

Silence settled between them.

Then Aidan smiled again.

"But while I'm here..."

He looked back toward the city.

"...I'll protect every last one of them."

Far below...

Unseen by anyone...

Cassian stood in the crowd.

Watching.

The whisper returned one last time.

> **"Listen to him."**

Cassian remained silent.

> **"...He believes he can save everyone."**

A long pause.

Then the voice spoke the words that would begin the end.

> **"Prove him wrong."**

Cassian closed his eyes.

For the first time...

He didn't tell the voice to leave.

He simply stood there.

Listening.

And far beneath the world...

Azrath slowly opened one eye.

Not because his prison had broken.

Because...

For the first time in centuries...

Someone had opened the door from the outside.
