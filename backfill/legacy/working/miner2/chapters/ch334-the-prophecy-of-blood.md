The old priest called Aidan back to the monastery one last time.

Without a word...

He led him beneath the chapel.

Deep underground.

Hidden beneath centuries of stone...

An enormous mural stretched across the wall.

It showed three kings.

The first...

Surrounded by crimson flames.

The second...

Covered in silver chains.

The third...

Standing over a world painted completely red.

The third king's face had been destroyed.

Scraped away.

Only one inscription remained beneath him.

---

> **"When the Crimson King drinks from love..."**

Aidan frowned.

"...Drinks?"

The priest slowly nodded.

Then finished the prophecy from memory.

---

> **"...the Vampire King shall awaken."**

Silence.

Lyra looked toward Aidan.

Neither of them understood.

The priest's voice became almost a whisper.

"You think this prophecy speaks of blood."

He slowly looked at Lyra.

"It doesn't."

Aidan's stomach tightened.

The old priest closed his eyes.

"The greatest feast..."

"...isn't the blood of an enemy."

He looked back at Aidan.

"It's the blood of the one you would die to protect."

The room fell completely silent.

Even the Witch Authority stopped moving.

The Crimson Legacy appeared.

Not with a quest.

Not with a warning.

Just one line.

---

**Future Probability Updated.**

---

Then...

It vanished.

Aidan didn't understand.

Lyra didn't understand.

The Guardian did.

His face turned pale.

Because for the first time...

He finally understood what Zee had feared.

The Vampire Curse wasn't designed to destroy Aidan.

It was designed to force him to choose between **his humanity... and the person he loved most.**

And somewhere, in the darkness beneath the Hall of Kings, another chain broke.

Not with a roar.

With a whisper.

The sound was so quiet that only the Crimson Legacy heard it.

And for the very first time in its existence...

The Legacy recorded something it had never recorded before.

> **The Vampire Authority is no longer waiting.**

> **It is hunting.**
