The Valley of Ash had earned its name long before kingdoms existed.

Nothing grew there.

No birds nested among its dead trees.

No rivers crossed its cracked earth.

Only ash.

Endless...

Gray ash.

---

Cassian stood alone.

His sword remained planted before him.

His hands rested upon the pommel.

The wind carried thousands of flakes of ash through the valley.

He didn't move.

He waited.

---

Hours passed.

Then...

A familiar pressure settled over the land.

Not loud.

Not violent.

Heavy.

The kind of presence that made the world instinctively become quiet.

Cassian slowly opened his eyes.

"He came."

---

Across the valley...

A lone figure walked through the ash.

No army.

No guards.

No pack.

Only Aidan.

His long crimson coat moved gently with the wind.

Every step was calm.

Measured.

Purposeful.

He stopped several yards away.

Neither man reached for a weapon.

---

Silence.

---

Cassian gave a tired smile.

"I figured you'd come alone."

Aidan nodded.

"So did you."

---

The wind carried ash between them.

For a long time...

Neither spoke.

Finally...

Cassian broke the silence.

"How long has it been?"

"A century."

Cassian laughed quietly.

"Feels longer."

---

Aidan looked into Cassian's eyes.

The anger that had once burned there...

Was gone.

All that remained was exhaustion.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez watched carefully.

**He came here expecting to die.**

*I know.*

---

Cassian slowly pulled his sword from the earth.

He didn't raise it.

He simply held it.

"I've spent centuries asking myself one question."

Aidan remained silent.

"What would happen..."

"...if I stopped fighting?"

---

Aidan took one step forward.

"The curse wouldn't end."

Cassian nodded.

"I know."

---

Another silence settled over the valley.

---

Cassian looked toward the sky.

"I killed Lyra."

The words hung in the air.

"I can never undo that."

"No."

"I deserve whatever happens next."

Aidan didn't answer immediately.

He simply watched the ash drift across the valley.

Then...

"I spent a hundred years believing I wanted revenge."

Cassian lowered his head.

"And now?"

Aidan looked directly at him.

"Now I want something else."

---

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

---

Aidan's crimson eyes seemed almost impossible to read.

"Freedom."

---

Cassian blinked.

"...Freedom?"

"For both of us."

---

The wind stopped.

Even the ash seemed suspended in the air.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf stood.

The vampire watched.

Neither spoke.

This choice belonged only to the King.

---

Cassian slowly tightened his grip on his sword.

"I don't understand."

"You will."

Aidan raised one hand.

Not to attack.

To invoke a Crimson Law.

His fingers traced a single Crimson symbol through the air.

The mark lingered for only a moment before fading.

The valley itself seemed to acknowledge it.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Living Blueprint**

**Crimson Law Recorded**

**Binding Field**

---

Cassian immediately recognized the feeling.

His body wouldn't move.

Not because he lacked strength.

Because the Crimson Law itself had anchored him in place.

He struggled.

Nothing happened.

He looked at Aidan.

"What... did you do?"

Aidan continued walking.

"I don't need a battle."

"I need your answer."

---

Cassian laughed bitterly.

"You've already taken away my ability to fight."

"No."

"I took away your ability to run."

---

Aidan stopped directly in front of him.

Only inches separated them.

"You've spent centuries as my enemy."

Cassian nodded.

"Yes."

"You've spent centuries carrying my judgment."

"Yes."

Aidan's voice became almost a whisper.

"I'm tired of it."

---

Cassian stared at him in confusion.

"What are you saying?"

---

Aidan slowly raised his hand.

A single drop of glowing **True Crimson Blood** formed at the tip of his finger.

It shone like liquid ruby beneath the gray sky.

Its power alone caused the ash around them to swirl.

Cassian's eyes widened.

"What... is that?"

Aidan looked into his eyes.

"The future."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez finally understood.

His eyes widened.

**Aidan...**

**You're not going to kill him...**

Aidan never looked away from Cassian.

*No.*

*I'm going to end this.*

The glowing drop of True Crimson Blood floated between them.

The Fourth Judgment had reached its turning point.

Not through vengeance.

Not through execution.

But through a choice that would forever change the fate of both men.
