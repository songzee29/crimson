Aidan reached the end of the bridge.

The Keepers all knelt.

The old man smiled.

"You answered correctly."

Aidan looked at him.

"...There was another answer?"

The old man shook his head.

"No."

"There were lies."

"You told the truth."

The Keeper stepped aside.

"The Crimson Road belongs to you."

As Aidan walked past...

The old man quietly whispered...

"One warning..."

"The Blood Sovereigns aren't gathering..."

"...to see who wins."

Aidan stopped.

"They're gathering..."

"...to witness whether the Crimson King deserves to exist."

Aidan never turned around.

He simply continued walking.

His answer came only after several silent steps.

> **"Then let them watch."**
