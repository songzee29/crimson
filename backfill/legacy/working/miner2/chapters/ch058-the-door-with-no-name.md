At the end of the hall stood another door.

Unlike the crimson entrance...

This one was made of black stone streaked with crimson veins.

No handle.

No keyhole.

Only a single symbol.

A broken crown.

The moment Aidan approached—

The System froze.

Every menu vanished.

Every notification disappeared.

Silence.

Then—

One sentence appeared.

---

**ACCESS FORBIDDEN**

---

Aidan frowned.

"For me?"

Another sentence.

---

**NOT YET.**

---

The words lingered.

Then another line slowly wrote itself.

As if someone else—not the System—was writing through it.

---

**The Founder turned back here.**

---

Aidan's eyes widened.

"...Turned back?"

---

**He chose another path.**

---

Nothing else.

The message erased itself.

The normal interface returned.

Aidan stepped away from the door.

His heart raced.

If that message was true...

Then even Zee...

Had reached a point where he made a choice.

Not because he lacked power.

Because the Legacy demanded something he wasn't willing to pay.
