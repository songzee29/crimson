The castle courtyard filled before sunrise.

Not with an army.

Only a handful of riders.

The Guardian frowned.

"My King..."

"Take more soldiers."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

"The kingdom still needs protecting."

Lyra stepped forward.

"Then let me go with you."

Aidan looked at her.

After a long silence...

He nodded.

"Together."

Rez smiled.

"Looks like the old days."

The Guardian sighed.

"I have a feeling..."

"...history is about to get very loud."

The great gates of House Darkheart slowly opened.

The Crimson King rode out.

Beside him...

Lyra.

Just behind them...

Rez.

Behind them...

Only a small royal escort.

Not an invasion.

Not a declaration of war.

A rescue.

Far away...

High upon a mountain cliff...

The hooded figure watched them leave.

For the first time...

A smile appeared beneath the hood.

"...Good."

"...Now every piece is moving."

The figure turned toward Black Hollow.

Then whispered into the wind...

> **"...Judgment Two..."**

> **"...begins."**

The crimson ash scattered into the sky.

And somewhere deep within the ancient world...

Something that had slept longer than the oldest vampire...

Opened its eyes.
