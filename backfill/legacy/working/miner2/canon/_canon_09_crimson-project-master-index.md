THE CRIMSON TERMINOLOGY GUIDE

Version 1.0

The Official Dictionary of Crimson Legacy

---

PURPOSE

Every official term has one meaning.

One spelling.

One definition.

Only this guide may define official terminology.

---

AUTHORITY

A supernatural foundation governing a specific aspect of power.

Authorities are unique and cannot be casually duplicated.

---

ASCENSION

A permanent evolutionary advancement that fundamentally transforms an individual beyond their previous state.

---

TRUE CRIMSON BLOOD

The complete Founder Blood possessed by Aidan after fulfilling the requirements established by the Crimson Master Decree.

---

CRIMSON BLOODLINE

The supernatural lineage created through True Crimson Blood.

---

HOUSE DARKHEART

The Founding Royal House established by Zee Darkheart.

---

CRIMSON CIVILIZATION

The nation founded by Aidan Darkheart.

---

FOUNDER

The individual responsible for creating something that permanently changes history.

---

FOUNDER AUTHORITY

The supreme Authority held only by the Founder recognized under the Crimson Master Decree.

---

LIVING ARCHIVE

The eternal record preserving Crimson history.

Nothing important is ever erased.

---

CRIMSON LEGACY

The complete universe consisting of the Master Decree, Codex, novels, history, and future generations.

---

DARK BLOODING

The official process through which a qualified individual joins the Crimson Bloodline.

---

CRIMSON FOUNDATION

The unified state formed when the Three Authorities become one.

---

MASTER DECREE

The supreme constitutional authority of the Crimson Legacy universe.

---

CODEX ENTRY

The official encyclopedia record for a person, place, object, event, race, or concept.

---

LEGACY

The lasting impact left upon future generations.

---

NOTE

Future terminology is added only after becoming official canon.

THE CRIMSON QUALITY CHECKLIST

Version 1.0

---

Before approving any chapter...

Answer every question.

□ Does it follow the Crimson Master Decree?

□ Does it agree with the Crimson Codex?

□ Does it respect the Timeline?

□ Does every character behave consistently?

□ Does every power follow established rules?

□ Does every conversation sound natural?

□ Does every scene move the story?

□ Does every chapter contain emotional progression?

□ Does every chapter contain meaningful conflict?

□ Does the world continue growing?

□ Does this strengthen Crimson Legacy?

□ Would future readers remember this chapter?

If any answer is No...

The chapter returns for revision.

---

QUALITY NOTE

Great chapters are rewritten.

Not rushed.
