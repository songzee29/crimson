Add this alone with the master decree  together to start the next chapter…📜 Crimson Legacy — Authority & System Constitutional Rule

The Crimson Legacy is not the source of Aidan’s power.

It is the Living Archive of House Darkheart.

Its purpose is to record, preserve, judge, and document Aidan’s journey, Authorities, Crimson Laws, Blood Arts, Evolutions, Ascensions, and all future achievements.

The System never activates Aidan’s powers.

Only Aidan does.

The Three Authorities

Aidan possesses three independent Authorities:

* Crimson Witch Authority
* Crimson Vampire Authority
* Crimson Wolf Authority

Each Authority functions according to its own nature.

Crimson Witch Authority

Aidan casts through his own will.

He forms Crimson Laws with his hands and speaks one-word or short decrees.

The System records the Law after it is created.

Crimson Vampire Authority

This Authority is completely instinctive.

Speed, strength, regeneration, Blood Arts, blood manipulation, and all vampire abilities flow naturally through Aidan without commands or System activation.

Crimson Wolf Authority

This Authority is also instinctive.

The wolf speaks directly to Aidan, sharpening his instincts, senses, hunting ability, awareness, and combat.

The wolf guides.

Aidan chooses.

First Crimson Ascension

Upon achieving the First Crimson Ascension, the Three Authorities cease functioning as separate active powers.

They become one unified Crimson existence.

From that moment forward:

* There is no visible separation between Witch, Vampire, and Wolf powers.
* There are no different colors or different energy signatures.
* Every ability manifests as Crimson.
* The world can no longer distinguish which Authority Aidan is using because they have become one complete power.

The Crimson Legacy may continue listing the Three Authorities separately for historical and archival purposes, but Aidan no longer experiences or uses them separately.

He simply commands… crimson
