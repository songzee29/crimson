# Chapter Seventy-Two: The Last Road

Blackwood Academy did not have time to mourn.

By sunset, another rider reached the gates.

He did not carry a plea.

He carried a warning.

"The northern shelters..."

His voice shook.

"...they won't survive the night."

Rowan closed his eyes for only a moment.

Then he opened them again.

"We move."

No one objected.

Not even Helena.

She simply looked at Aidan.

His face had lost what little color remained.

His left arm stayed bound beneath his cloak.

Every breath lifted only one side of his chest.

His side bled through another layer of fresh bandages.

He looked as though the mountain had never released him.

Still...

He picked up his staff.

"I'm coming."

Helena almost spoke.

Then stopped.

She already knew.

Nothing she said would outweigh another life waiting to be saved.

...

The northern shelters stood against a narrow pass between two cliffs.

Hundreds of refugees crowded behind hastily raised barricades.

Children.

The elderly.

Families who had already fled more than once.

The moment they saw Blackwood arrive...

Relief spread across exhausted faces.

Not because an army had come.

Because Aidan had.

He noticed.

The weight settled deeper inside him.

They still believed.

...

The first impact shattered the outer barricade.

Stone exploded across the pass.

The defenders rushed forward.

Aidan walked past them.

"Stay behind me."

Only four words.

No speech.

No promise.

Only a command.

Cassian looked at him.

"You can barely stand."

"I know."

"I'll take the front."

Aidan shook his head.

"No."

His voice remained calm.

"They need time."

He stepped into the narrowest part of the pass.

Both hands rose.

Slowly.

Pain shook every muscle before he even cast.

"Hold."

Crimson light spread across fractured stone.

The cliff answered.

Weakly.

Cracks continued crawling beneath the Law.

Aidan pushed harder.

Blood spilled freely from his nose.

Then his mouth.

His vision dimmed.

The Law flickered.

Not because his will had weakened.

Because his body had.

Behind him...

Families ran.

Cassian, Lyra, Malik, Elias, Rowan, and Helena guided them through the narrowing escape.

No one looked away from Aidan.

They all saw it.

His hands shaking.

His knees failing.

His breathing breaking apart.

The Crimson Witch had reached the end of what flesh could bear.

...

The cliff exploded.

Aidan's Law collapsed beneath the weight.

Stone crashed through the barrier.

The shockwave hurled him backward across broken ground.

His staff flew from his hand.

He struck the earth hard enough that he didn't immediately rise.

Cassian reached him first.

"Aidan!"

Aidan opened his eyes.

Slowly.

"The people?"

"They're still moving."

Aidan nodded once.

"Good."

He tried to stand.

His legs folded beneath him.

Cassian caught him again.

"You can't."

"I have to."

"No."

"I have to."

He forced himself upright anyway.

Every step became an act of will.

He walked back toward the collapsing pass.

Not because he believed he could stop it.

Because someone still hadn't crossed.

A mother.

Holding a little girl.

Pinned beneath fallen timber.

Without hesitation...

Aidan reached them.

His injured shoulder screamed as he lifted the beam.

His ribs shifted beneath torn flesh.

The wound along his side burst completely open.

Warm blood poured onto the stone.

"Go."

The woman hesitated.

"Go."

She ran.

The little girl looked back only once.

Aidan gave the smallest nod.

Then turned back toward the mountain.

Another section broke loose.

Cassian saw it first.

His voice tore across the battlefield.

"Aidan!"

Too late.

The cliff came down.

Dust swallowed everything.

When it cleared...

Aidan still stood.

Barely.

Holding enough of the collapse back with his body and failing Law for the last refugees to escape.

The crimson light around his hands flickered like a dying flame.

Rowan stared in silent horror.

Helena's hands trembled.

Malik lowered his weapon.

Lyra covered her mouth.

Cassian took one step forward.

Then another.

The realization settled over every one of them at the same time.

This could not happen again.

There was nothing left for Aidan's body to give.

And yet...

He gave it anyway.

# Chapter Seventy-Three: The Final Breath

The last child crossed the pass.

Aidan saw him reach Cassian.

Only then...

He let out the breath he had been holding.

The Law failed.

Completely.

The mountainside roared.

Cassian ran.

"Aidan!"

Stone swallowed the place where he had been standing.

The force of the collapse threw everyone backward.

When the dust finally began to settle...

Aidan lay beneath shattered rock.

Still.

Cassian reached him first.

He dropped to his knees.

"Aidan."

No answer.

Helena arrived beside him.

Her healing magic poured into wounds that had nothing left to answer it.

Rowan stood motionless.

For the first time...

He gave no orders.

Lyra knelt beside Aidan, her hands shaking as she tried to stop blood that would not stop flowing.

Malik looked away.

Elias lowered his head.

Aidan's eyes slowly opened.

They found Cassian.

Then Rowan.

Then Lyra.

Then the line of frightened survivors standing behind them.

Every one of them alive.

A faint smile touched his face.

Small.

Peaceful.

He had counted them.

They were safe.

Cassian gripped his hand.

"We're getting you home."

Aidan's lips moved.

Almost no sound came.

"It's..."

He struggled for another breath.

"...okay."

Helena's magic faltered.

Not from lack of skill.

From the terrible truth before her.

There was nothing left in his body to save.

Years of wounds.

Years of blood loss.

Years of broken bones reopened before they healed.

Years of choosing everyone else.

Every battle had taken something.

Nothing remained.

Aidan looked once more toward the people behind Cassian.

A little girl clutched her mother's hand.

The mother held her close.

They lived.

That was enough.

His fingers slowly loosened around Cassian's hand.

His breathing became quiet.

Then quieter still.

One final breath escaped him.

Silence.

Nothing else followed.
