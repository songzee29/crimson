# Chapter Twelve  
## The First Climb

The mountain answered with another violent tremor.

Stone burst from the walls.

A crack raced beneath Elias's crutch.

Before anyone shouted—

Aidan moved.

Not because he decided to.

Because his body already had.

His hand caught Elias by the shoulder and pulled him backward a heartbeat before half the corridor collapsed into the widening chasm.

Broken stone thundered into the darkness below.

The echo continued...

Long after the rock had disappeared.

Elias stared.

"I... didn't even see it."

Aidan looked at his own hand.

Neither had he.

He had simply...

Known.

"Everyone move!" Rowan barked.

The group retreated through the shaking passage while dust poured from the ceiling.

A second collapse erupted ahead.

The tunnel split.

Malik reacted immediately, throwing his weight beneath a falling support beam despite his injured leg.

The effort nearly drove him to one knee.

"I've got it!"

His muscles trembled.

The beam continued sinking.

Aidan reached him.

He didn't think.

His shoulder slid beneath the timber beside Malik's.

Broken ribs screamed.

White-hot pain shot across his chest.

Still...

He lifted.

Not because the pain disappeared.

Because his body refused to stop.

Together they shoved the beam high enough for Helena, Lyra, and Elias to escape beneath it.

Cassian followed last, dragging his useless arm close to his body.

The support collapsed the instant they cleared it.

Everyone stumbled into the next chamber breathing hard.

No one spoke.

The silence lasted only a moment.

"Thank you," Malik finally said.

Aidan nodded once.

He hadn't even realized how much the beam weighed.

Only afterward did he notice blood seeping through the fresh bandages around his ribs.

Again.

...

The deeper corridor narrowed.

Ancient air drifted upward.

Cold.

Wet.

Old.

Aidan paused.

"There."

He pointed into complete darkness.

Cassian frowned.

"I don't see anything."

"There are three passages."

"No," Helena replied. "There should only be one."

"There are three."

Without waiting, Aidan quietly crossed the chamber.

His footsteps made almost no sound.

He didn't notice.

The others did.

Lyra watched him.

"You're... quieter."

He looked back.

"What?"

"When you walk."

He glanced toward the stone beneath his boots.

He hadn't tried to move silently.

He simply had.

The walls ahead opened exactly as he'd said.

Three passages.

Two hidden by collapsed stone.

Helena stared.

"How..."

"I smelled fresh air."

Everyone looked at him.

The answer sounded strange even to himself.

"I didn't..."

He hesitated.

"...I didn't realize I knew that."

...

Another vibration rolled through the mountain.

Closer now.

Not random.

Rhythmic.

Dig.

Silence.

Dig.

Silence.

The surviving creature was climbing.

Not alone.

Aidan heard something else.

Breathing.

Heavy.

Slow.

Different rhythms.

Five.

No...

Six.

"No."

He corrected himself quietly.

"Seven."

Cassian looked sharply toward him.

"You counted them?"

"I..."

He listened again.

The breathing stopped.

The mountain fell silent.

His brow furrowed.

"They heard us."

...

Without warning—

A deafening shriek tore through the tunnel.

Not the bell.

Not the stone giant.

Something entirely different.

Thin.

Piercing.

Predatory.

Black shapes burst from the ceiling.

Winged creatures with elongated limbs and pale eyeless faces dove through the darkness.

"They're above us!" Lyra shouted.

Most of the group instinctively looked upward.

Aidan didn't.

His eyes locked onto the one diving directly toward Helena.

The greatest threat.

His body lunged before thought.

He shoved Helena aside.

The creature's claws ripped across his shoulder instead of her throat.

Pain exploded through him.

He caught himself before falling.

Another creature swept silently behind Elias.

Again...

His body moved first.

He grabbed Elias's cloak and yanked him backward.

Claws missed by inches.

"Aidan!" Rowan shouted.

"Center!"

The group formed around the injured almost instinctively.

This time...

Exactly as they had trained.

Cassian defended the left despite one functioning arm.

Malik anchored the front, refusing to let his injured leg buckle.

Lyra's runes illuminated the darkness.

Aidan listened.

Not watched.

Listened.

Every wingbeat.

Every breath.

Every scrape of claw against stone.

"They're circling!"

He pointed.

"Three behind us!"

Everyone turned just as the creatures descended.

Prepared.

The attack failed.

The battle shifted.

Within minutes the surviving creatures fled back into the darkness.

Not defeated.

Driven away.

Silence returned.

Only their breathing remained.

Aidan leaned against the wall.

His shoulder bled freely.

Another wound.

Another scar.

Helena reached him.

"You need stitches."

"I know."

"You've said that a lot lately."

He gave a tired smile.

"It keeps being true."

...

The group finally emerged into the cold night outside Blackwood Academy.

No one looked victorious.

Every step hurt.

Every injury remained.

As Aidan crossed the academy gate...

A young apprentice accidentally scraped his palm against broken stone while helping reinforce the walls.

One bright drop of blood fell onto the ground.

Aidan stopped walking.

Not intentionally.

His eyes found it immediately.

His breathing slowed.

The scent reached him before anyone else reacted.

Warm.

Fresh.

Alive.

His stomach tightened.

For one terrifying heartbeat...

He wanted to walk toward it.

Instead...

He clenched both fists until his wounded shoulder throbbed.

Lyra quietly stepped beside him.

Without asking why.

Without drawing attention.

She simply blocked his view.

"Aidan."

He blinked.

The hunger receded.

He released a slow breath.

"Thank you."

She didn't ask what for.

...

Late that night...

Alone beneath the dim light of the infirmary...

Aidan changed the bandages across his shoulder.

The wounds from weeks of relentless battles covered his body now.

Broken ribs.

Claw scars.

Deep cuts.

Bruises that never fully faded.

He barely recognized his own reflection.

Then—

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**CRIMSON SYSTEM**

**SURVIVAL FOUNDATION ACCEPTED**

**Foundational Adaptations Granted**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification lingered in complete silence.

No explanations appeared.

No descriptions followed.

No tutorials.

Only the ancient certainty that something long earned had finally been acknowledged.

The words faded.

The room became quiet once more.

Aidan touched the edge of a scar across his ribs.

He didn't feel stronger.

He felt...

Different.

Outside...

Deep beneath Blackwood Academy...

Seven sets of slow, deliberate footsteps continued climbing toward the surface.
