# Chapter Seven  
## The Hollow Bell

The bell rang once.

Not from Blackwood Academy.

From somewhere beyond its walls.

The sound rolled through the mountains like ancient iron struck beneath the earth.

Every ward surrounding the academy vibrated.

Students stopped walking.

Conversations died.

Professor Helena slowly looked toward Rowan.

"I've never heard that bell before."

Neither had he.

Rowan's expression hardened.

"Seal the inner grounds."

Within moments golden barriers rose around the academy.

Faculty guided younger students indoors while older witches reinforced defensive circles without needing to be told.

No panic.

Only discipline.

Blackwood had learned.

...

Aidan stood atop the eastern watchtower.

The bell echoed again.

Closer.

His Crimson Witch Authority spread across the surrounding forests.

No magical army.

No gathering curse.

No visible enemy.

Just...

Movement.

One.

Single.

Presence.

Walking directly toward Blackwood.

It wasn't hiding.

It didn't need to.

"It knows we're watching," Aidan murmured.

Lyra climbed onto the tower beside him.

"What is it?"

"I don't know."

That answer unsettled him more than any monster could.

...

They met it before it reached the academy.

Aidan.

Lyra.

Cassian.

Malik.

Elias.

Rowan.

Five hundred yards beyond Blackwood's eastern boundary.

Mist drifted between ancient pines.

The figure finally emerged.

Tall.

Wrapped in a weathered black cloak.

Its face hidden beneath a hood stitched together with faded crimson thread.

It carried no weapon.

Only an old iron bell hanging from one hand.

It stopped.

The bell rang a third time.

Dong.

Every bird in the forest exploded into flight.

The hood slowly lifted.

There was no face beneath it.

Only darkness.

Then—

The empty hood turned toward Aidan.

The thing moved.

Not quickly.

Instantly.

It appeared directly before him.

His body reacted before thought.

"Hold!"

Invisible force erupted outward.

The spell struck...

Nothing.

The cloaked figure simply wasn't there anymore.

"Aidan!"

Cassian shouted.

Too late.

Pain tore through Aidan's back.

Invisible claws ripped across his shoulders.

He stumbled forward, blood spraying across fallen leaves.

No enemy.

Only another bell.

Dong.

"There!" Elias yelled.

The figure stood thirty feet away.

Malik charged.

His shoulder passed straight through the cloaked body.

An illusion.

"No," Rowan barked.

"It isn't an illusion!"

Another bell.

Dong.

The forest changed.

Mist thickened.

Trees shifted.

Paths disappeared.

Each member of the group suddenly stood alone.

...

Aidan spun in every direction.

No voices.

No footsteps.

Only endless gray fog.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

Something moved behind him.

He turned.

Nothing.

Again.

Closer.

His breathing slowed.

His hearing sharpened.

He could distinguish individual raindrops striking distant branches.

He could hear water running beneath stone.

He could hear...

Blood.

His own.

Slowly dripping from the wounds across his back.

The scent reached him.

Warm.

Familiar.

Comforting.

His fingers twitched.

The fog whispered.

Drink.

He clenched both fists.

"No."

The whisper vanished.

Something struck him from the side.

Invisible force hurled him into an ancient oak.

His ribs screamed.

The impact stole every breath from his lungs.

He slid to one knee.

Crack.

At least one rib.

Maybe two.

He pushed himself upright anyway.

"I'm not..."

He coughed blood.

"...finished."

The bell answered.

Dong.

The cloaked figure appeared again.

Closer than before.

Still faceless.

Still silent.

Watching him.

Waiting.

Aidan raised trembling hands.

His Witch Authority surged.

Not to destroy.

To understand.

The spell spread through the fog.

Searching.

Listening.

The answer came all at once.

"There..."

He whispered.

"It's never standing still."

The creature wasn't teleporting.

Each bell forced everyone's senses to perceive it somewhere different.

It hunted through confusion.

Not strength.

The realization reached him a heartbeat before another strike.

This time...

He moved first.

His body twisted on instinct.

Invisible claws missed his throat by inches.

Aidan caught empty air with both hands.

"Still."

The Law expanded in every direction.

Not toward the enemy.

Toward the forest itself.

The mist stopped moving.

The leaves stopped falling.

The echo of the bell died.

For one perfect heartbeat...

Everything became still.

The cloaked figure appeared.

Real.

Only six feet away.

"Cassian!"

Steel flashed from behind.

Cassian burst through the fading mist.

One precise strike severed the iron bell from the creature's hand.

The bell struck the ground.

Silence.

The hood collapsed.

The empty cloak crumpled into dead leaves.

Nothing remained beneath it.

...

The journey home felt heavier than any before it.

Aidan could barely breathe.

Each step sent agony through broken ribs.

Blood soaked the back of his coat.

Lyra stayed beside him without speaking.

Halfway to Blackwood...

His legs failed.

Again.

This time Malik caught him.

"You don't always have to carry everybody," Elias muttered through clenched teeth from his own crutches.

Malik laughed quietly despite everything.

"Seems like I do."

For the first time since entering the forest...

Aidan smiled.

Small.

Tired.

Real.

...

The infirmary fell silent after midnight.

Helena confirmed three broken ribs.

Deep lacerations across his back.

Significant blood loss.

"No training," she ordered.

"No patrols."

"No arguments."

Aidan looked away.

"I'll try."

"You'll obey."

She walked out before he could answer.

The room darkened.

Outside, rain began falling once more.

Aidan stared at the ceiling.

His chest rose painfully.

The second heartbeat remained.

Steady.

Patient.

Then...

His wounded body moved before he gave it permission.

His tongue instinctively brushed across the blood gathered at the corner of his split lip.

The metallic taste touched his mouth for only an instant.

Warm.

Rich.

His eyes widened.

He jerked backward as though burned.

Disgust.

Confusion.

Fear.

All at once.

Then...

Something inside him wanted another taste.

No comfort.

Outside the infirmary window, dawn remained hours away.

And somewhere in the endless forest beyond Blackwood Academy...

Another hunt had already begun.
