## Chapter One Hundred Thirty-Eight — The Raven's Message

The raven did not fly away.

It remained perched upon the ancient oak, its crimson-black feathers rippling gently in the morning breeze.

Aidan stepped outside before the rest of the estate had awakened.

The world felt strangely still.

Even the birds that usually greeted the sunrise remained silent.

He approached slowly.

The raven never moved.

Its crimson eyes watched him with unsettling intelligence.

When Aidan stood within arm's reach, the bird lifted one leg.

A narrow strip of weathered parchment had been tied there with crimson thread.

The knot unraveled on its own.

The raven spread its wings.

Instead of taking flight...

It bowed its head.

Then disappeared into a cloud of crimson feathers that dissolved into the morning wind.

No magic exploded.

No dramatic display followed.

Only silence.

Aidan unfolded the parchment.

The handwriting was elegant.

Ancient.

Every letter appeared as though carved rather than written.

*If you wish to understand why history erased one King...*

*Come to Ashfall Abbey.*

*Come alone.*

*Bring no weapons.*

*Bring only your judgment.*

No signature waited beneath the message.

Only a single symbol.

A crimson crown surrounded by three unfinished circles.

The same symbol he had begun seeing throughout the Sanctuary.

The same symbol that appeared upon forgotten stone.

The same symbol history refused to explain.

His crimson-and-gold eyes lingered upon it.

Not with excitement.

With caution.

Someone...

Had begun leading him.

The question was why.

One of the council members stood immediately.

The Hunter.

No.

The Hunter followed orders.

Someone else sent the invitation.

Murmurs spread throughout the chamber.

Another Heir?

An enemy?

A survivor?

The oldest council member slowly rose from his seat.

Or...

Someone who remembers a history even we have forgotten.

Silence consumed the room.

No one challenged him.

The possibility frightened them more than any known enemy.

The man with the crimson ring finally spoke.

Prepare the Archives.

If Ashfall Abbey has reopened...

Then sealed history has begun moving again.

That should not have been possible.

Yet...

History had never promised to remain buried forever.

## Chapter One Hundred Forty — Ashfall Abbey

Blackwood Library had no record of Ashfall Abbey.

Neither did the academy archives.

Even the city's historical society had never heard its name.

The place simply...

Didn't exist.

Yet as evening approached, Aidan found himself following an old forest road beyond the western hills.

No map guided him.

No voice directed him.

Only instinct.

The deeper he walked, the quieter the forest became.

Even the wind seemed unwilling to disturb the ancient trees.

Finally...

The path ended.

Before him stood the weathered ruins of an enormous abbey reclaimed by time.

Towering stone walls stood cracked beneath creeping ivy.

Stained-glass windows had long since shattered.

Ancient statues lined the entrance.

Every face had been deliberately chiseled away.

Just like the portraits beneath House Darkheart.

Just like the Founder.

Aidan felt a chill run through him.

Someone...

Had erased more than one person from history.

The massive wooden doors stood open.

Waiting.

He stepped inside.

Dust covered the floor.

Moonlight poured through the broken ceiling.

Rows of empty pews stretched toward an altar carved from black stone.

Upon the altar rested only one object.

A book.

Bound in faded crimson leather.

Not the Crimson Legacy.

Older.

Much older.

As Aidan approached...

The cover slowly revealed three words.

**Book of Founders.**

Before he could touch it...

A voice echoed through the ruined abbey.

You've come farther than expected.

Aidan turned.

Standing in the shadows between two ancient pillars...

The Hunter finally stepped into the light.

For the first time...

The two stood face to face.

No distance.

No rooftops.

No crowds between them.

Only silence.

The Hunter studied Aidan carefully.

Then nodded once.

Good.

You chose to come.

Now...

Let's discover whether history chose correctly.

Outside...

Thunder rolled across the darkening sky.

Deep beneath the Sanctuary...

One of the twelve ancient seals glowed faintly for the first time in countless ages.

The First Trial of Judgment...

Had begun.
