# Chapter Seventy-Eight: The Chase

Blackwood Academy had never felt so small.

The scream had already stopped.

Only pounding footsteps echoed through the courtyards.

Broken doors hung from twisted hinges.

Stone cracked beneath impossible impacts.

Guards reached the eastern corridor first.

They found only shattered masonry.

No attacker.

No victim.

Aidan had already moved.

Too fast.

Far too fast.

Cassian followed the trail of destruction.

"Aidan!"

No response came.

Only another distant crash.

Then...

The unmistakable sound of a heartbeat.

Not his own.

Not one of the inner circle.

Fast.

Panicked.

Running.

Aidan heard it too.

His entire world had become that rhythm.

Every heartbeat in Blackwood sounded different now.

Every breath.

Every drop of blood.

The nearest one eclipsed everything else.

His body moved before thought.

Walls became obstacles to pass through rather than around.

He struck the outer gate.

The heavy timber doors burst outward.

Beyond them, the injured porter stumbled across the academy road, one hand pressed desperately against the bleeding wound in his forearm.

He looked back.

Their eyes met.

The man saw Aidan.

Then the fangs.

He turned and ran.

Aidan followed.

Not with anger.

Not with purpose.

With hunger.

Behind him, the academy bells began ringing again.

Not for an invasion.

Not for a siege.

For something no lesson had ever prepared them to face.

Lyra reached the shattered gate seconds later.

She watched the two figures disappear into the forest.

One fleeing.

One hunting.

Her voice broke into the night.

"Aidan!"

He never slowed.

He never looked back.

The darkness swallowed them both.

And Blackwood Academy understood, with growing horror, that the greatest danger beyond its walls...

Was no longer waiting for them.

It had once called the academy home.
