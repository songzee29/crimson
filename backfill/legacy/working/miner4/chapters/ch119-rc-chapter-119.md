# Chapter One Hundred Nineteen

The vampires did not flee.

They guided him.

Aidan crossed the rooftops overlooking the Mississippi with relentless speed.

The Inner Circle stayed close behind.

None of them questioned where he was going.

They knew.

The enemy wanted to be found.

"Thirty-two years ago."

"You were here."

"I arrived too late."

"I found an entire district emptied overnight."

"No bodies."

"No survivors."

"Only blood."

Draco looked toward Aidan.

"'The Heir never came.'"

Silence settled over the group.

"You've been searching for him ever since," Rowan realized.

"I wasn't looking for a weapon."

Its stained-glass windows had long since shattered.

Every entrance stood open.

No guards.

No traps.

Only invitation.

"They want us inside," Malik said.

"Then we stop making them wait."

No one noticed.

The doors opened on their own.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Hundreds of candles burned.

No vampires waited.

Only one man.

His hair had turned silver with age.

His crimson eyes never left Aidan.

"So..."

"The Crimson Heir finally arrives."

Aidan studied him.

"You've been watching me."

"For years."

"You've killed thousands."

"I've ruled thousands."

"My name is Lucien Vale."

"I built this city's hidden covens while the Ancient Gates slept."

"I have interest in what you became."

"You murdered innocent people."

"I sacrificed pieces."

"Logical."

Lucien smiled wider.

"You understand."

"No."

Aidan's voice remained flat.

"I understand how you think."

"And?"

"I've already calculated your death."

For the first time...

"Excellent."

"I was hoping the monster was real."

# Chapter One Hundred Twenty

Their collision shattered rows of wooden pews.

Neither gave ground.

Lucien smiled as he struck again.

"Faster."

Aidan answered with a brutal elbow that fractured Lucien's jaw.

The vampire healed before he landed.

"Better."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Unlike before...

These vampires weren't trying to kill everyone.

Cassian found himself separated from Aidan.

Lyra was forced toward the opposite side of the sanctuary.

"No."

Lucien struck again.

"But that's gone now."

Another exchange.

Stone burst apart.

"You've finally become dangerous."

Everything stopped.

Not toward Lucien.

Toward Elias.

"There it is."

"The real battle."

Watching.

Waiting.

Testing.

"Aidan!"

But it wasn't the one the monster should have made either.
