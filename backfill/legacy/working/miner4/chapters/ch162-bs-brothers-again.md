## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Two — Brothers Again

The valley seemed lighter.

Not because the sun had climbed higher into the sky.

Because laughter had returned.

For the first time since leaving House Darkheart, Aidan felt a piece of himself settle back into place.

Malik threw an arm around Elias' shoulders.

So...

You're the guy I've spent months competing against without even meeting.

Elias laughed.

Competing?

Aidan compared you to me so much I started wondering if I had another brother I didn't know about.

Darius folded his arms with a grin.

Trust me.

It got annoying.

Kael nodded enthusiastically.

Very annoying.

Aidan rubbed the back of his neck.

Was I really that bad?

The four answered together.

Yes.

Everyone burst into laughter.

Even Rowan smiled.

Cassian watched quietly from the edge of the group.

His smile was genuine.

But his eyes lingered on Aidan longer than necessary.

Studying him.

Measuring him.

---

As the group walked through the valley, Elias seemed to know everyone.

Children ran toward him.

Keepers greeted him by name.

Even the elders stopped their work long enough to welcome him home.

You became popular.

Aidan teased.

Elias shrugged.

Not really.

People just remember the idiot who kept getting lost.

One elderly Keeper overheard him.

Three times.

Elias sighed dramatically.

I was hoping nobody would mention that.

The old woman chuckled.

You found your way eventually.

That's what mattered.

Aidan looked at Elias.

You really walked the Pilgrim Roads alone?

Not alone.

Elias smiled.

The roads always send someone when you need them.

Sometimes they're strangers.

Sometimes...

They're friends you haven't met yet.

Those words stayed with Aidan.

## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Three — A Promise Remembered

That evening, Rowan prepared a simple meal for everyone inside the great hall.

The tables were built from ancient cedar.

Their surfaces carried countless knife marks and scratches from generations of shared meals.

No throne sat at the head.

Only another chair.

Aidan noticed immediately.

Even here...

No one ruled from above.

Everyone sat together.

Halfway through dinner...

Elias quietly reached into his backpack.

I've been carrying this for years.

He placed a small wooden box on the table.

Aidan frowned.

What's this?

Open it.

Inside rested two carved wooden swords.

Old.

Worn smooth by countless hands.

Aidan laughed.

No way...

You still have these?

Of course I do.

Elias picked one up.

Remember when we were eight?

You said we'd become the strongest swordsmen in history.

Aidan smiled.

You corrected me.

You said...

Not the strongest.

The ones people could trust the most.

Elias nodded.

Strength fades.

Trust doesn't.

Malik looked between them.

You two have been having deep conversations since you were kids?

No.

We mostly hit each other with sticks.

Everyone laughed.

Cassian smiled politely.

But something inside him stirred.

Not with anyone.

## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Four — Cassian's Shadow

The moon rose high above the valley.

Unable to sleep, Cassian wandered alone through the gardens surrounding the Second Foundation.

His footsteps stopped before an ancient pool of perfectly still water.

His reflection stared back at him.

For a moment...

It wasn't his own face.

A different man looked back.

Older.

Crueler.

Covered in blood.

Cassian blinked.

The reflection returned to normal.

Not again...

He whispered.

A familiar voice emerged from the darkness.

Still seeing him?

Cassian didn't turn.

No.

Only remembering.

Rowan stepped beside him.

The past becomes heavier when ignored.

Cassian looked toward the stars.

Some things should stay buried.

Rowan's expression grew thoughtful.

Perhaps.

But buried things have a habit of growing roots.

Neither spoke again.

Far away...

The Hunter watched the exchange from the cliffs.

Interesting...

The cracks have already begun.

## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Five — The Scroll Opens

At sunrise, every bell throughout the valley rang together.

Not loudly.

Harmoniously.

Keepers emerged from workshops.

Children stopped their lessons.

The entire valley walked toward the Temple of the Second Foundation.

Rowan stood beside the stone table.

Today...

History makes another choice.

He looked toward Aidan.

Come forward.

The sealed scroll still rested exactly where it had for centuries.

Its crimson wax remained untouched.

Aidan approached slowly.

What if it doesn't open?

Rowan smiled.

Then we'll close the temple...

Share breakfast...

And continue living.

History has waited nine hundred years.

It can wait one more day.

The answer eased Aidan's heart.

He placed both hands upon the stone table.

Not reaching for the scroll.

Simply resting them there.

Nothing happened.

A full minute passed.

Then another.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody rushed him.

Suddenly...

The crimson wax seal cracked.

Not from force.

From recognition.

The scroll slowly unrolled itself.

Ancient writing appeared across the parchment.

Not everyone could read it.

But Aidan could.

The first line simply said:

**The Second Foundation welcomes the Pilgrim.**

The second line appeared moments later.

**The road ahead will ask what no classroom ever could.**

Then...

The writing stopped.

Blank parchment stretched across the remaining scroll.

Rowan bowed his head.

The Foundation has accepted your arrival.

Not your journey.

You've only taken your first step.

The scroll wrote once more.

Only one sentence.

**Find the Broken Bridge before the next full moon.**

Below it...

A simple drawing appeared.

An ancient bridge spanning a deep canyon.

Beside the bridge stood the same crimson tree carved into the First Keeper's key.

Rowan looked at the image.

So...

It survived.

Elias stepped closer.

I've heard stories about that place.

No one has crossed it in generations.

Cassian studied the drawing carefully.

If the bridge truly exists...

Someone is guarding it.

The Hunter, still hidden high above the valley, lowered his hood.

Not someone.

Something.

His eyes narrowed toward the distant eastern mountains.

The Second Foundation has chosen well.

The Pilgrim Road has truly begun.

For the first time...

Aidan would not walk toward forgotten history.

History...

Would test whether it was willing to walk beside him.
