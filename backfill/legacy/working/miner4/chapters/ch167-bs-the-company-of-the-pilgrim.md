## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Seven — The Company of the Pilgrim

By noon, the valley buzzed with quiet preparation.

Maps were spread across wooden tables.

Travel packs were filled.

Ropes, lanterns, dried food, and climbing gear were carefully inspected.

Unlike soldiers preparing for war...

The Keepers prepared as though they were safeguarding something precious.

Rowan gathered Aidan, Elias, Cassian, Lyra, Darius, Kael, and Malik beneath the great cedar overlooking the valley.

This journey is not a mission.

It is a responsibility.

He pointed toward the map.

The Broken Bridge was once part of the oldest Pilgrim Road.

For centuries it connected the First and Second Foundations.

Then...

It disappeared.

Cassian folded his arms.

Collapsed?

Rowan shook his head.

No.

Forgotten.

Elias raised an eyebrow.

There's a difference?

A sad smile crossed Rowan's face.

A bridge that collapses can be rebuilt.

A bridge the world forgets...

May never exist again.

The words settled heavily over the group.

The Hunter, hidden high among the cliffs, quietly nodded to himself.

The old Keeper still teaches well.

---

That evening...

Aidan and Elias sat beside a small stream flowing through the valley.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Eventually Elias skipped a stone across the water.

Five skips.

Still rusty.

Aidan laughed.

It used to be seven.

Eight.

Don't cheat.

The two smiled.

Then Elias became unusually quiet.

You've changed.

Aidan looked toward the flowing water.

I know.

No...

Elias shook his head.

Not in the way you think.

When we were kids...

You always tried solving everyone's problems.

Now...

You carry everyone's pain too.

Aidan didn't answer.

Because Elias was right.

Elias leaned back against a tree.

You know what I missed most?

What?

Arguing with you.

Aidan blinked.

Seriously?

Absolutely.

Nobody else is stubborn enough to keep up.

Their laughter echoed across the valley.

From a distance, Lyra watched with a warm smile.

She finally understood.

This wasn't just Aidan's friend.

This was his brother.

## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Eight — Cracks in the Stone

While the others prepared supplies, Cassian wandered through the temple alone.

The silence comforted him.

Until he reached the chamber containing the opened scroll.

He stopped.

The parchment had changed.

One new line had appeared.

Only he could see it.

**Judgment follows those who flee from it.**

Cassian's breathing quickened.

No...

The words slowly faded.

When Rowan entered moments later...

The scroll appeared completely normal.

Cassian immediately stepped away.

Everything alright?

Rowan asked.

Cassian forced a smile.

Just thinking.

Rowan looked toward the scroll for a long moment.

Sometimes...

The Foundations show us what we need.

Not what we want.

Cassian nodded politely.

Inside...

His unease deepened.

Something in the Foundations recognized him.

And he wasn't sure that was a good thing.

## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Nine — The Pilgrim's Oath

Before dawn, the travelers gathered at the eastern gate.

Dozens of Keepers stood silently on either side of the path.

Not to celebrate.

To witness.

Rowan stepped forward carrying an old wooden staff.

Every Pilgrim who leaves this valley makes the same promise.

Not to us.

To history itself.

He looked toward Aidan.

Repeat after me.

I will seek truth...

Even when it burdens me.

Aidan repeated the words.

The others followed.

I will protect knowledge...

Without claiming ownership of it.

Again...

The company repeated the oath.

Finally...

Rowan spoke the final promise.

I will leave every generation stronger than the one before it.

Silence followed.

The mountain wind carried the words across the valley.

The old Keeper smiled.

Go.

Walk well.

Remember...

Roads reveal character long before destinations do.

The eastern gate slowly opened.

The company stepped beyond the Second Foundation together.

The Pilgrim Road truly began.

## Chapter One Hundred Seventy — Shadows on the Road

The first two days passed peacefully.

Dense forests surrounded the ancient trail.

Stone markers bearing the crimson tree appeared every few miles.

Most had been damaged.

Some deliberately broken.

Someone had been trying to erase the Pilgrim Road.

Elias knelt beside one shattered marker.

This damage is recent.

Cassian crouched beside him.

Within a year.

Maybe less.

Aidan studied the broken stone.

Why destroy a road almost nobody knows exists?

Nobody destroys something they fear.

Cassian answered quietly.

They destroy something they understand.

The group exchanged uneasy glances.

Far ahead...

Hidden beyond the trees...

Several cloaked figures silently watched them pass.

None made a move.

Not yet.

One whispered.

The Heir walks exactly as the prophecy described.

Another answered.

Then let him reach the bridge.

Everything begins there.

They vanished into the forest without leaving a trace.

## Chapter One Hundred Seventy-One — The Broken Bridge

Late on the third afternoon...

The trees abruptly ended.

Before the company stretched an enormous canyon.

Wind howled through its depths.

Ancient stone pillars rose from the abyss like the broken ribs of a forgotten giant.

Across the gap...

The remains of an immense bridge clung precariously to the cliffs.

Half its span had collapsed centuries ago.

The other half still stood.

Impossible.

Malik whispered.

Nobody could cross that.

Elias slowly stepped toward the edge.

Look.

Everyone followed his gaze.

Suspended above the abyss...

Almost invisible beneath centuries of dust...

A narrow path of crimson stone extended through the empty air.

Not broken.

Hidden.

The visible bridge had been the illusion.

The true bridge...

Had always remained intact.

Aidan took a slow breath.

The Foundation wasn't asking them to rebuild history.

It was asking them to uncover it.

At that exact moment...

A deep horn echoed from somewhere beneath the canyon.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

The wind stopped.

The crimson stones beneath Aidan's feet began to glow.

Then...

A colossal figure slowly emerged from the shadows below.

An armored guardian taller than the bridge itself lifted its head.

Its eyes ignited with ancient crimson light.

For the first time in a thousand years...

The Keeper of the Broken Bridge had awakened.

And it was looking directly at Aidan Darkheart.
