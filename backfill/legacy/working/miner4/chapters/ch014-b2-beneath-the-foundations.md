# Chapter 14: Beneath the Foundations

Aidan did not leave the old oak.

He remained standing in the cool night air, listening.

Seven heartbeats.

Slow.

Heavy.

Uneven.

They continued climbing through the mountain beneath Blackwood Academy.

Not wandering.

Not searching.

Ascending.

The distance between each beat told him something.

They were spreading apart.

One leading.

The others following.

Like hunters moving through familiar tunnels.

His breathing became shallow.

He closed his eyes.

Not to use the Crimson Witch Authority.

To listen.

The mountain spoke through tiny sounds no one else seemed to notice.

Stone scraping against stone.

Dust shifting through ancient cracks.

The groan of overloaded supports.

Water dripping somewhere impossibly deep.

Every sound fit together.

A map built from echoes.

His eyes opened.

"They're changing direction."

...

Before dawn Rowan gathered the senior faculty.

No one questioned why Aidan was already waiting.

"What did you hear?" Rowan asked.

"They've stopped climbing."

Helena frowned.

"Stopped?"

"They're moving sideways."

Cassian unfolded one of the academy's oldest tunnel maps.

"If they're avoiding the central foundations..."

His finger stopped.

"...they're heading toward the eastern dormitories."

Silence filled the room.

Hundreds of students still slept there.

"They know," Elias whispered.

"They're avoiding the strongest wards."

"They're learning," Rowan corrected.

"And every hour we wait..."

He looked toward Aidan.

"...they learn more."

Aidan nodded.

"We go underground."

...

The academy had barely awakened when the six of them descended once more beneath Blackwood.

This time every step carried greater urgency.

Aidan's ribs protested every breath.

His shoulder remained stiff beneath fresh stitches.

Malik's limp had worsened.

Cassian still couldn't fully lift his left arm.

Elias leaned heavily on his reinforced crutch.

No one had recovered.

They simply kept moving.

Darkness swallowed the tunnel.

Lanterns illuminated only a few yards ahead.

Aidan slowed.

"Extinguish them."

Malik blinked.

"We'll be blind."

"No."

Aidan looked ahead.

"They'll see us first."

The lanterns disappeared.

Darkness claimed the corridor.

Everyone except Aidan hesitated.

He could still distinguish the outlines of broken pillars.

Collapsed walls.

Fresh claw marks.

A narrow crack hidden behind fallen stone.

"There."

He pointed.

Lyra stared into the darkness.

"I can't even see the wall."

"It's there."

Cassian carefully reached forward.

His fingers touched cold stone exactly where Aidan indicated.

A hidden passage.

Rowan quietly looked at Aidan.

He said nothing.

But concern settled deeper into his eyes.

...

The hidden tunnel narrowed quickly.

Fresh scratches lined both walls.

Some low.

Some nearly touching the ceiling.

Seven different heights.

Seven creatures.

They were no longer guessing.

Aidan knelt beside one deep gouge.

The stone still felt warm.

"They passed recently."

Malik frowned.

"How can you tell?"

Aidan touched the rock again.

"I..."

He hesitated.

"I don't know."

He simply knew.

The answer disturbed him.

...

A faint cry echoed ahead.

Human.

Weak.

"Eastern patrol," Cassian whispered.

The group hurried forward.

The passage opened into a collapsed guard chamber.

Three academy guards lay trapped beneath shattered beams.

One still conscious.

"They came through..."

He coughed violently.

"...didn't even stop..."

Lyra immediately began healing the wounded.

Helena stabilized another.

The third...

Was already gone.

Aidan stood silently over the fallen guard.

Another life.

Another cost.

The mountain rumbled.

Closer now.

His head turned instantly toward the eastern wall.

"They're moving again."

The others heard nothing.

Seconds later...

The stone trembled.

Everyone looked up.

"You heard that before it happened," Rowan said quietly.

Aidan didn't answer.

Because Rowan was right.

The adaptations weren't waiting for his permission anymore.

They had become part of him.

# Chapter 15: The Hunters Turn

The eastern wall exploded inward.

Stone became shrapnel.

A massive claw burst through the rock.

Then another.

One of the creatures forced itself into the chamber.

Not the armored giant.

This one moved differently.

Lean.

Long-limbed.

Its pale hide clung tightly to powerful muscle while crimson eyes searched the room with terrifying intelligence.

It ignored everyone.

Its gaze locked onto Aidan.

Predator met prey.

Neither moved.

Then...

The creature lunged.

Its speed shocked everyone.

Not Aidan.

His body shifted before thought.

The claws carved empty air where his chest had been.

"Aidan!" Lyra shouted.

He was already moving.

Not faster than before.

Sooner.

Every instinct screamed where the next strike would come from.

Left.

Low.

Turn.

He obeyed without understanding.

The second attack missed.

The third grazed his injured shoulder.

Pain flared.

His body kept moving.

"Hold!"

Invisible force crashed into the creature.

It slid backward but remained standing.

Stronger.

Much stronger.

Cassian attacked from its blind side.

The creature ducked.

Not by chance.

It had heard him.

Malik charged.

The creature twisted away before impact.

"It's reading us!" Elias shouted.

"No."

Aidan's eyes never left it.

"It's listening."

The realization struck everyone at once.

Sound.

Footsteps.

Breathing.

Every movement warned it.

"Don't speak!" Rowan ordered.

Silence.

The battle transformed instantly.

Aidan lowered his breathing.

His footsteps faded naturally against the stone.

The creature hesitated.

It had lost him.

Predator's Focus narrowed his world.

Not the chamber.

Not the fear.

Only the creature.

Only the next movement.

Its weight shifted slightly onto its right leg.

Weakness.

He saw it.

No...

He felt it.

"There."

He whispered.

Cassian understood immediately.

Despite his injured arm he drove his blade toward the weakened stance instead of the creature's chest.

Steel pierced deep.

The beast shrieked.

Malik slammed into it before it recovered.

Together they drove it through the broken wall.

The tunnel collapsed behind it.

Silence returned.

Only heavy breathing remained.

Aidan leaned against the nearest pillar.

His vision blurred.

Blood dripped steadily from his reopened shoulder.

The metallic scent reached him.

His stomach tightened.

His eyes lingered on the crimson drops running across his fingertips.

Too long.

"Aidan."

Lyra's voice.

Gentle.

Close.

He looked up immediately.

The moment passed.

Barely.

Far beyond the collapsed tunnel...

Six remaining heartbeats continued through the mountain.

The wounded creature still lived.

It hadn't been abandoned.

The others...

Had kept moving.

They had never intended to rescue it.

They had only needed it to slow Blackwood's defenders.

The realization settled over the group like ice.

The attack had never been the objective.

It had been the distraction.

And somewhere deeper beneath the academy...

The true hunt continued.
