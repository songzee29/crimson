The corridor fell silent.

Only the distant groan of shifting stone echoed up from the chasm.

Dust drifted through the lantern light.

Nobody moved.

Aidan stared into the darkness beneath Blackwood Academy as the strange chorus below slowly faded back into silence.

Then—

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**CRIMSON SYSTEM**

**SURVIVAL MILESTONE ACHIEVED**

**Judgment:**

*The Heir has repeatedly survived injuries, blood loss, exhaustion, impossible battles, and overwhelming odds.*

*The System has completed its Survival Evaluation.*

**Survival Foundation Accepted.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The words remained longer than any notification he had ever seen.

One by one...

New lines appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Rewards Granted**

• Enhanced Battle Instinct

• Crimson Vitality I

• Night Perception

• Predator's Focus

• Enhanced Hearing

• Enhanced Smell

• Crimson Endurance

• Iron Stomach

• Pain Resistance

• Accelerated Reflexes

• Survival Adaptation I

• Blood Sense

• Threat Assessment

• Silent Movement

• Rapid Recovery I

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The final line appeared alone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Notice**

*These abilities strengthen the body.*

*Their true purpose remains unknown.*

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The light dissolved.

No explanation followed.

No tutorial.

No description.

Only silence.

Aidan frowned.

"...Strengthen the body?"

"What is it?" Rowan asked.

Aidan hesitated only a heartbeat.

"The System."

"What did it say?"

"It acknowledged the battles."

Nothing more.

Nothing less.

The rest remained his burden.

Another crack echoed from below.

The mountain trembled.

Aidan instinctively looked toward the darkness.

Before he consciously focused...

He heard it.

Not the collapsing stone.

Not the shifting earth.

Breathing.

Slow.

Measured.

Far below.

His eyes narrowed.

"I hear something."

Malik frowned.

"I don't hear anything."

Cassian listened.

"Neither do I."

Aidan stepped closer to the edge.

The darkness seemed... clearer.

Shapes separated naturally despite the absence of light.

Broken pillars.

Collapsed bridges.

Fresh claw marks.

He shouldn't have been able to see that far.

He realized it only after he'd already done it.

His gaze shifted.

The scent of damp stone reached him.

Old earth.

Fresh dust.

Then...

Blood.

Very faint.

Not nearby.

Deep below.

His stomach tightened.

He swallowed hard.

Not now.

Not here.

His body leaned forward almost imperceptibly.

As if something inside him wanted to follow the scent into the abyss.

Lyra's hand gently closed around his wrist.

"Aidan."

He blinked.

The moment broke.

"I'm here."

She searched his face.

"You disappeared again."

"I'm alright."

She didn't believe him.

Neither did he.

A low rumble rolled through the mountain.

The chasm widened another inch.

A section of the opposite ledge collapsed into darkness.

Several enormous claw marks became visible where the rock had broken away.

Not climbing upward.

Descending.

Rowan's expression hardened.

"It isn't digging toward the academy anymore."

"Then where is it going?" Elias asked, leaning heavily on his crutch.

Nobody answered.

Because no one knew.

Aidan's breathing slowed.

The new instincts settled around him like they had always belonged there.

His aching body still hurt.

Every broken rib still protested.

The wounds across his back still burned.

The blood loss still left him pale.

But despite all of it...

He found himself unconsciously calculating distances.

Weak points.

Escape routes.

Who was most vulnerable.

Who needed protecting first.

The thoughts came effortlessly.

Too effortlessly.

Cassian noticed.

"What are you looking at?"

"The ceiling supports."

"Why?"

"If something comes out..."

Aidan's eyes moved across the corridor.

"...that pillar falls first."

Then another.

"And Helena and Elias need to move left before it collapses."

The words left his mouth before he realized he had spoken them.

The group exchanged glances.

Rowan looked at him with quiet concern.

"You worked all of that out already?"

Aidan stared at the corridor.

"I..."

He hadn't.

His body had.

A violent impact erupted from somewhere beneath them.

The entire corridor lurched.

A deafening roar echoed upward through the depths.

Not one voice.

Many.

Stone exploded from the walls.

Fresh cracks raced across the floor toward the group's feet.

Rowan raised his staff.

"Everyone back!"

The mountain answered with another thunderous roar.

And somewhere far below...

The hunt was climbing toward them.
