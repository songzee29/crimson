For several long moments…

Nobody reached for a weapon.

Rez broke the silence.

“You look older.”

Cassian laughed bitterly.

“…So do you.”

“I’ve been asleep.”

“I’ve been trying to live.”

Rez’s smile disappeared.

“…Has it worked?”

Cassian opened his mouth…

Then closed it again.

He couldn’t answer.

Not honestly.

Every peaceful memory of the last several years now carried a shadow.

The Darkheart Crest burned beneath his sleeves.

Every day…

The curse had taken a little more.

Sleep.

Joy.

Contentment.

Hope.

Not all at once.

Little by little.

Until he no longer remembered what true peace felt like.

⸻

Aidan finally spoke.

His voice was calm.

“You built a life.”

Cassian nodded slowly.

“…Yes.”

“You found people worth protecting.”

“…Yes.”

“You discovered what peace feels like.”

Cassian looked away.

“…I did.”

Silence.

Aidan took one step forward.

His eyes never left Cassian’s.

“Now you understand…”

”…what you took from me.”
