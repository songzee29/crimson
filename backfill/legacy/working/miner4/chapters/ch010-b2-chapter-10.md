# Chapter Ten  
## What Refused to Stay Buried

The claw scraped against stone.

Far beneath Blackwood Academy...

Once.

Then again.

The sound was too deep for ordinary ears.

Too distant.

Yet Aidan's eyes snapped open.

Pain shot through his ribs as he tried to sit upright.

His chest tightened.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

Someone was already beside him.

Lyra gently pushed him back against the bed.

"Don't."

"I heard something."

"You were asleep."

"I wasn't."

She searched his face.

"You haven't slept since the northern pass."

He didn't argue.

Because she was right.

Every time exhaustion dragged him toward unconsciousness...

He dreamed of darkness.

Not nightmares.

Hunger.

Always hunger.

Professor Helena entered carrying another tray of fresh bandages.

Her expression darkened immediately.

"You reopened them again."

"I didn't move."

"You did."

She carefully removed the wrappings.

Fresh blood stained every layer.

The claw wounds across Aidan's back had split apart while he slept.

His ribs had shifted again.

"This doesn't make sense," Helena whispered.

"He should be healing."

Nobody answered.

Because everyone had begun wondering the same thing.

...

Across the infirmary...

Cassian struggled to move his left arm.

The limb barely responded.

He clenched his jaw as Malik helped fasten the leather brace around his shoulder.

"It'll heal."

Cassian shook his head.

"Not soon."

Malik hid his own limp poorly.

Every step forced him to favor his right leg.

Elias remained on enchanted crutches.

Even standing exhausted him.

Nobody had recovered.

The last battle had followed them home.

...

The academy bell rang.

Not the ancient bell from the forest.

Blackwood's.

One clear tone.

Every senior instructor immediately assembled inside the strategy hall.

Rowan placed a weathered map across the table.

"The collapse failed."

Nobody looked surprised.

"The creature survived."

Aidan stared quietly at the shattered section beneath the academy.

"It wasn't trying to escape."

Rowan nodded.

"It was digging."

Silence.

Professor Helena slowly traced the tunnel beneath Blackwood.

"If it keeps moving upward..."

"It reaches the academy."

"No."

Cassian spoke for the first time.

"It reaches him."

His gaze settled on Aidan.

"It ignored everyone else."

Nobody disagreed.

...

"We end it underground," Aidan said.

"No."

Lyra answered immediately.

"You're barely standing."

"I'm still standing."

"Barely."

The room fell silent.

Rowan watched both of them.

Finally he spoke.

"We don't hunt it."

Everyone looked toward him.

"We force it to hunt us."

...

Preparations began immediately.

Not for battle.

For a trap.

Runes spread through the lower tunnels.

Barrier circles layered one above another.

Collapsed passages deliberately reopened.

Every surviving instructor joined the effort.

Students noticed something was wrong.

No one was told what.

Only that the lower academy had become forbidden.

...

By nightfall...

The trap was ready.

Aidan stood alone inside the central chamber beneath Blackwood.

His breathing remained shallow.

Every inhale stabbed broken ribs.

Bandages wrapped tightly around his chest beneath his coat.

He could still smell dried blood beneath them.

The scent lingered.

Comfortably.

His stomach twisted.

No...

Not hunger.

Something else.

A quiet longing he couldn't understand.

The chamber trembled.

Dust drifted from the ceiling.

The creature was coming.

...

It emerged slowly.

Not charging.

Watching.

Its stone-like body now carried deep fractures from the cave-in.

One arm hung shattered.

Yet it walked without hesitation.

Toward Aidan.

Only Aidan.

"So..."

Aidan whispered.

"You survived too."

The creature lowered itself slightly.

Almost...

Curious.

Then it attacked.

The first blow shattered the floor.

Aidan rolled aside.

Pain exploded through his ribs.

Too slow.

Far too slow.

His injuries mattered now.

The creature struck again.

Another barrier broke.

Another support pillar cracked.

Not enough.

He couldn't overpower it.

He knew that now.

The second heartbeat thundered.

THUMP.

Without thinking...

His eyes tracked every pulse of movement inside the creature.

Every shift of weight.

Every fracture spreading through damaged stone.

His body moved before his thoughts.

One step.

One turn.

One impossible dodge.

The claws missed by less than an inch.

"Aidan!"

Malik's voice.

Now.

The hidden runes ignited.

Golden circles erupted throughout the chamber.

Barrier after barrier locked into place.

The creature roared.

For the first time...

It couldn't move freely.

"Lyra!"

Crimson runes wrapped around its remaining arms.

"Elias!"

Binding circles reinforced the prison.

"Cassian!"

Ignoring the agony in his ruined shoulder...

Cassian drove his blade directly into one of the fractures left by the cave-in.

The creature screamed.

Not in pain.

In rage.

The stone around its body began splitting apart.

"No..."

Helena whispered from the tunnel entrance.

"It isn't breaking."

The cracks widened.

Black stone peeled away.

Something pale moved underneath.

The shell...

Had never been its body.

It had been armor.

The entire chamber shook.

"Aidan!"

Rowan's voice carried unmistakable urgency.

"Everyone out!"

Too late.

The prison exploded.

Stone became shrapnel.

Aidan threw himself toward Lyra.

The blast hurled them both across the chamber.

Broken rock slammed into his back.

His ribs cracked again.

Everything went white.

...

When sound returned...

Someone was screaming.

Elias.

Malik struggled beneath fallen debris.

Cassian knelt beside him with only one usable arm.

Blood ran freely down Rowan's forehead.

Helena fought to maintain collapsing barriers.

The creature...

Was gone.

Not dead.

Gone.

Only a tunnel ripped through solid stone remained behind it.

It hadn't escaped.

It had continued digging.

Deeper into Blackwood.

...

No one celebrated surviving.

There had been no victory.

Only another delay.

Aidan forced himself upright.

Every muscle trembled.

Blood soaked through fresh bandages.

His breathing came in ragged gasps.

Then—

The scent reached him.

Malik's leg had reopened.

Blood soaked through torn cloth.

Warm.

Fresh.

His head turned before he realized it.

His eyes fixed on the wound.

His mouth went dry.

For one terrifying heartbeat...

Nothing else existed.

"Aidan."

Lyra's voice.

Soft.

Concerned.

He blinked.

The moment shattered.

He immediately looked away.

"I'm fine."

She didn't answer.

Because they both knew he wasn't.

# Chapter Eleven  
## The Shape Beneath the Mountain

Nobody returned to bed.

The academy no longer felt safe.

Engineers reinforced lower foundations while professors evacuated every classroom above the damaged tunnels.

Students carried supplies.

Nobody complained.

They could feel it.

Something beneath Blackwood had awakened.

...

Aidan sat alone against a stone wall outside the infirmary.

Helena had finished treating him.

Again.

His ribs were bound so tightly breathing itself hurt.

Fresh stitches crossed old wounds.

He had lost enough blood to leave his hands pale.

Still...

He refused to rest.

The mountain vibrated.

Very faintly.

Every few minutes.

Dig.

Silence.

Dig.

Closer.

He heard every scrape.

No one else reacted.

His hearing had changed again.

...

Lyra quietly sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

Finally she asked,

"When did you stop telling me everything?"

He stared ahead.

"I haven't."

"You have."

She wasn't angry.

Only hurt.

"You hide every time something scares you."

He closed his eyes.

"I don't know what's happening to me."

The confession barely escaped.

"I know."

She reached for his hand.

"I don't need you to understand it."

"I just need you to stop carrying it alone."

For a long time...

He simply held her hand.

The second heartbeat remained.

Patient.

Watching.

It did not disappear.

But for one brief moment...

It became quieter.

...

A crash echoed beneath the academy.

Not nearby.

Far below.

Then another.

The entire floor shifted.

Dust rained from the ceiling.

A messenger burst into the corridor.

"Master Rowan!"

"The lowest foundations just collapsed!"

Everyone moved.

Aidan stood too quickly.

Pain tore through his ribs.

Darkness flooded his vision.

He nearly fell.

Cassian caught him with his good arm.

"You can't keep doing this."

"I have to."

"No."

Cassian looked him directly in the eyes.

"If you fall..."

His voice lowered.

"...we all do."

The words followed Aidan all the way to the collapsing tunnels.

...

The lower foundations had split open.

A jagged chasm now divided the ancient corridor.

Cold air rose from impossible depths.

At the bottom...

Fresh claw marks climbed the walls.

Not one creature.

Many.

Nobody spoke.

The surviving monster had not been fleeing.

It had been making a path.

For something else.

Aidan stared into the darkness.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

Far below...

Something answered it.

Not a heartbeat.

A chorus.

Slow.

Ancient.

Waiting.
