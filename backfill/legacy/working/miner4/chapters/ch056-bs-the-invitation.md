## Chapter Fifty-Six — The Invitation

Monday arrived beneath clear blue skies.

For the first time in weeks...

Aidan woke before his alarm.

His body felt different.

Not stronger.

Lighter.

His movements flowed naturally as he dressed, every action deliberate without requiring thought.

The Guardian's lessons had begun reshaping him in ways invisible to everyone else.

As he reached the front door of the estate, his aunt called after him.

There's a letter for you.

Aidan frowned.

A letter?

She handed him a thick black envelope.

No stamp.

No return address.

His full name was written across the front in elegant silver ink.

Aidan Darkheart.

Nothing else.

The paper felt strangely warm.

He opened it.

Inside rested a single crimson card.

No signature.

No explanation.

Only one message.

You have been invited.

Come if you seek truth.

Do not come if you seek power.

The location appeared beneath the message.

Blackwood Library.

Tonight.

8:00 PM.

The card instantly crumbled into crimson dust.

His aunt looked up from the kitchen.

Everything okay?

Aidan quietly slipped the remaining dust into his pocket.

Yeah.

Just... a strange advertisement.

She smiled.

Those companies get more creative every year.

Aidan forced a small smile.

If only it were that simple.

---

The rest of the school day dragged by.

His attention wandered.

Every lesson.

Every conversation.

Every passing minute brought him closer to eight o'clock.

Even Malik noticed.

You're distracted today.

A little.

Something happen?

Maybe.

Malik nudged his shoulder.

Whatever it is...

Don't overthink it.

You always look like you're solving the world's biggest mystery.

Aidan laughed.

Maybe I am.

Neither of them realized how true those words had become.

---

That evening...

The city looked completely different beneath the fading sunlight.

Streetlights flickered to life.

Shops closed one by one.

People hurried home.

Aidan followed the address written on the invitation until he reached one of the oldest districts in the city.

At the end of a narrow cobblestone street stood a three-story brick building.

BLACKWOOD LIBRARY.

The sign above the entrance had nearly faded away.

The windows were dark.

The building appeared abandoned.

Yet the front door slowly opened before he touched it.

A warm light spilled into the street.

Someone had been expecting him.

Aidan stepped inside.

Shelves stretched endlessly into the distance.

Far larger than the building should have allowed.

Books climbed toward a ceiling hidden in darkness.

The scent of aged paper and polished wood filled the air.

An old clock quietly ticked somewhere beyond the shelves.

Then...

Footsteps.

Slow.

Measured.

Familiar.

The elderly bookseller emerged from between the towering shelves carrying a lantern.

His gentle smile returned.

I wondered how long it would take before you accepted the invitation.

Aidan stared.

You sent it?

The old man chuckled.

No.

The invitation belongs to the Legacy.

I merely delivered it.

He motioned toward a pair of worn leather chairs beside a crackling fireplace.

Sit.

Tonight...

You ask questions.

I'll answer the ones you've earned.

Aidan slowly sat down.

His pulse quickened.

For weeks...

Every answer had remained locked behind trials and cryptic messages.

Now...

Someone was finally willing to speak.

The old man carefully poured two cups of tea before taking his own seat.

Steam curled into the warm light between them.

He looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

Before we begin...

There is one question I must ask you.

Not because I need the answer.

Because you do.

He folded his hands.

Why did you open the Crimson Book?

The room became perfectly silent.

The fire crackled softly.

Outside...

Rain began tapping against the old library windows.

Aidan opened his mouth to answer...

Then stopped.

For the first time since his journey began...

He realized he didn't truly know.
