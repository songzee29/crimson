# Chapter Twenty-Three: The Observer

The academy changed again.

Not because anyone wanted it to.

Because it had to.

The western shelters were abandoned.

Dormitories rotated every night.

Patrol routes were no longer written down.

Students learned evacuation drills before breakfast instead of spell theory.

Professor Helena converted two classrooms into emergency treatment halls.

Master Rowan had every instructor teach combat in pairs rather than individually.

Blackwood learned.

Because the hunters did.

...

Aidan barely noticed the changes.

He hadn't slept more than an hour at a time in over a week.

When he did...

He dreamed of running through forests he had never seen.

Always at night.

Always chasing something.

Always waking before he reached it.

He sat alone in the Great Hall.

Breakfast covered the table.

Fresh bread.

Fruit.

Stew.

He stared at it.

Malik dropped into the seat beside him.

"You gonna eat?"

"I will."

"You said that yesterday."

"I know."

Malik pushed the bowl closer.

"You've had three bites in two days."

"I'm not hungry."

Malik laughed.

"You're kidding, right? After everything we've been doing?"

Aidan looked down.

He wasn't lying.

He simply...

Wasn't hungry.

The realization unsettled him.

He forced himself to eat anyway.

Three spoonfuls.

Nothing more.

Across the hall...

Lyra watched quietly.

She remembered when Aidan had always been the first to complain about training on an empty stomach.

Now...

He barely touched his meals.

...

That afternoon Rowan changed patrol formations again.

"No more fixed leaders."

The assembled guards looked confused.

"If they study us..."

He glanced toward Aidan.

"...then we stop giving them patterns."

Pairs became trios.

Trios became rotating circles.

Signals changed.

Meeting points changed.

Nothing stayed predictable.

Blackwood adapted.

...

The forest adapted too.

Aidan noticed first.

"They stopped watching from the trees."

Cassian looked around.

"You can tell?"

"They're higher."

Everyone instinctively looked into the branches.

Nothing.

Aidan looked toward the cliffs instead.

"They're using the rocks now."

A small pebble rolled down the slope.

Then another.

No attack followed.

Only observation.

"They wanted us to look up."

Aidan's voice remained calm.

"So we'd stop watching the ground."

Cassian slowly nodded.

"They're testing where our attention goes."

Neither side attacked.

Both sides learned.

...

That evening the patrol reached an abandoned watchtower overlooking Blackwood's northern valley.

No movement.

No creatures.

No blood.

Only silence.

Too much silence.

Aidan crouched near the broken doorway.

Someone had carved fresh marks into the stone.

Not random scratches.

Rows.

Straight.

Measured.

Each mark ended with a small symbol.

An eye.

Cassian joined him.

"They've been counting."

"No."

Aidan traced one symbol with his fingertips.

"They've been recording."

"What?"

Aidan looked toward the academy.

"Our responses."

He didn't realize someone was already watching him.

High above...

Hidden against the cliff face...

A pale figure remained perfectly still.

Unlike the other hunters...

It carried no claws stained with blood.

Instead...

Thin black lines covered its arms.

Each line matched another carved into the tower below.

It never attacked.

It only watched.

Then it disappeared.

# Chapter Twenty-Four: The Divider

The next attack came two nights later.

Exactly when Blackwood expected nothing.

No alarms.

No explosions.

Only a single frightened student pounding on the infirmary doors.

"Please!"

"My brother's missing!"

The academy moved immediately.

Within minutes patrols searched every building.

Every classroom.

Every tunnel.

Nothing.

The student had simply...

Vanished.

Aidan stood in the center of the courtyard.

Eyes closed.

Listening.

The academy breathed around him.

Hundreds of footsteps.

Panicked voices.

Doors opening.

Armor clattering.

Then—

One heartbeat.

Fast.

Terrified.

Underground.

"Library."

He was already running.

Cassian, Lyra, Malik, and Elias followed.

The hidden staircase beneath the library had been forced open from below.

Fresh claw marks lined the walls.

The missing student cried out again.

Closer now.

Aidan descended first.

Not recklessly.

Instinctively.

The tunnel split.

Left.

Right.

Ahead.

He stopped.

His body wanted the right passage.

His mind chose left.

"Left!" Malik shouted.

Aidan didn't move.

"No."

"Why?"

"I..."

He frowned.

"...I don't know."

He trusted the feeling.

"Right."

Cassian nodded without hesitation.

"We follow him."

They ran.

Minutes later...

The tunnel opened into a circular chamber.

Empty.

Except for the missing student.

Alive.

Bound.

Crying.

"Aidan..."

Lyra whispered.

"Something's wrong."

He knew.

The room smelled...

Clean.

Too clean.

No blood.

No fear.

Only stone.

The student looked up.

"Help me!"

Aidan took one step.

His body stopped.

Every instinct screamed.

Trap.

"Nobody move."

The words came quietly.

The student began sobbing.

"Please..."

"I said..."

Aidan slowly raised one hand.

"...don't move."

The ceiling exploded.

Stone crashed between him and the others.

The chamber split apart.

Aidan fell through collapsing rock.

Cassian lunged.

Too late.

Lyra screamed his name.

The ground sealed shut between them.

Silence.

Deep below the academy...

Aidan slammed into cold stone.

Every rib screamed.

His shoulder struck first.

The air vanished from his lungs.

He forced himself upright.

Darkness surrounded him.

No voices.

No Lyra.

No Rowan.

No Cassian.

No Malik.

No Elias.

Only silence.

Then...

Slow footsteps echoed through the tunnel ahead.

Not rushing.

Measured.

Patient.

A pale hunter emerged.

Not large.

Not heavily built.

Its claws remained lowered.

It looked at Aidan.

Then...

It looked back toward the collapsed tunnel separating him from everyone else.

It had succeeded.

Not by overpowering him.

By isolating him.

For the first time since the hunt began...

The Crimson Witch stood completely alone.

And somewhere beyond the darkness...

The rest of the hunters had already begun moving toward Blackwood Academy without him.
