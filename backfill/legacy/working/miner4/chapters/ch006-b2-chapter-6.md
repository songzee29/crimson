# Chapter Six  
## The Cost of Returning

Three days passed before Blackwood Academy dared to believe the forest had gone quiet again.

Three days without another breached ward.

Three days without another alarm.

No one relaxed.

Not after the eastern gate.

Additional patrols now circled the academy from dawn until well after sunset. Rowan no longer walked them himself.

He assigned them.

And for the first time...

Aidan led them.

Not because Rowan was unwilling.

Because he trusted him.

"You know what you're looking for," Rowan said as the patrol assembled before sunrise.

"I'll report before dusk."

"I know."

The answer carried complete confidence.

Aidan nodded once.

"Let's move."

Lyra, Cassian, Malik, and Elias followed without question.

...

The eastern forest remained unnaturally still.

Every broken branch.

Every disturbed patch of earth.

Every fading trail.

Aidan noticed them all before anyone else.

His Witch Authority spread naturally through the woods.

Magic whispered.

The land remembered.

Then...

His body stopped walking.

Not his mind.

His body.

His hand shot outward.

"Down!"

The others reacted instantly.

A massive tree crashed across the path exactly where Malik had been standing.

The impact shook the forest.

Silence followed.

Malik stared at the shattered trunk.

"I didn't even hear it..."

Aidan hadn't either.

He only...

Moved.

His heartbeat quickened.

That wasn't Witch perception.

It had felt...

Different.

Cassian's eyes lingered on him.

"You reacted before the tree moved."

"I know."

Neither of them understood how.

...

The trail eventually led into a narrow ravine carved between ancient cliffs.

Fresh claw marks covered the stone.

Long.

Deep.

Recent.

"They're nesting," Elias whispered.

"No."

Cassian crouched beside the marks.

"They're waiting."

The attack came from above.

Two beasts hurled themselves from opposite cliffs.

Another exploded from beneath loose stone.

A fourth burst through the trees behind Lyra.

"Split!"

The ravine erupted into chaos.

Aidan thrust both hands forward.

"Hold!"

Invisible force slammed one creature against the cliff wall.

Rock exploded outward.

The second beast reached him anyway.

Its claws ripped across his side before he could turn completely.

White-hot pain tore through him.

He staggered.

Blood soaked through his coat.

The third creature crashed into Elias.

Both disappeared down the rocky slope.

"Elias!"

Malik charged after him.

Cassian intercepted another beast, steel clashing against bone-like claws.

Lyra unleashed a storm of glowing runes that forced the fourth creature backward.

Aidan pressed one hand against his side.

Warm blood poured between his fingers.

Too much.

Far too much.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

The world sharpened.

Every movement slowed.

The wounded beast limped.

Its pulse fluttered.

Its breathing became uneven.

He knew exactly where to strike.

Without thinking...

He smiled.

The expression disappeared almost immediately.

But Lyra saw it.

"Aidan!"

Her voice broke through whatever had settled over him.

He blinked.

The strange calm vanished.

Pain returned all at once.

His knees nearly buckled.

The beast lunged.

Aidan raised one trembling hand.

"Still."

The world around the creature froze.

Not the beast.

Everything surrounding it.

The air itself became impossibly heavy.

Its momentum collapsed.

Cassian appeared beside it a heartbeat later.

One clean strike.

The battle ended.

...

"Elias!"

Malik's shout echoed from below.

Everyone rushed down the ravine.

They found him pinned beneath fractured stone.

His left leg twisted unnaturally.

His breathing came in short, painful gasps.

Cassian and Malik lifted the boulder together while Lyra immediately began stabilizing the injury with runes.

Aidan knelt beside him.

"I'm sorry."

Elias forced a weak grin despite the pain.

"You planning... on apologizing every time someone gets hurt?"

Aidan looked away.

"No."

"Good."

Elias winced.

"Because we'd never leave this forest."

...

The journey home was slow.

Malik carried Elias.

Cassian walked silently behind them.

Lyra refused to leave Aidan's side.

Blood continued soaking through the bandages around his ribs.

"You need to stop walking," she said quietly.

"I'm fine."

"No."

She looked directly into his eyes.

"You're lying."

He wanted to argue.

Instead...

His vision blurred.

The next step failed.

His legs gave out beneath him.

Cassian caught him before he struck the ground.

...

When Aidan awoke...

White stone ceiling.

The academy infirmary.

His ribs burned with every breath.

Professor Helena finished wrapping fresh bandages around his chest.

"You lost enough blood to kill an ordinary witch."

"I'm not ordinary."

"No."

Her expression remained firm.

"But you are not invincible either."

Across the room...

Lyra sat quietly beside the window.

She had not left.

Malik stood with folded arms.

Cassian leaned against the wall.

Elias rested in the next bed with his injured leg suspended in enchanted bindings.

No one laughed.

No one joked.

The silence spoke for them.

They had nearly lost each other.

Again.

...

Long after everyone had fallen asleep...

Aidan remained awake.

His side throbbed.

Every heartbeat hurt.

Then—

His nose caught something.

Fresh blood.

One of his wounds had reopened beneath the bandages.

The scent reached him instantly.

His breathing slowed.

His hand drifted toward the soaked cloth.

Not to stop the bleeding.

To touch it.

His fingertips brushed the warm crimson stain.

A pulse of satisfaction spread through him before he jerked his hand away in horror.

"What..."

The second heartbeat answered.

Closer.

Clearer.

Patient.

**CRIMSON SYSTEM**

**Behavioral Analysis Complete.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification vanished.

Another appeared.

**Passive Evolution Recorded.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Another.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

**Dormant Synchronization Progress Increased.**

**Access Restricted.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The final message lingered for only a heartbeat before dissolving into darkness.

No explanation.

No guidance.

Only observation.

Only preparation.

Aidan stared into the quiet room, listening to the breathing of the people who had risked their lives beside him.

For the first time since becoming the Crimson Witch...

He was afraid.

Not of dying.

But of the growing certainty that the next battle might demand something from him...

That magic alone would no longer be enough to resist.
