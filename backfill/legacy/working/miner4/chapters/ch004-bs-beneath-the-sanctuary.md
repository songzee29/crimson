## Chapter Four — Beneath the Sanctuary

The staircase spiraled endlessly into the depths of the earth.

Each step carried Aidan farther from the world he had known and deeper into one that should not have existed.

The air became warmer.

The walls no longer resembled carved stone. They shimmered with veins of crimson crystal that pulsed like living arteries beneath the mountain, casting long shadows that seemed to move when he wasn't looking directly at them.

The deeper he descended, the stronger the heartbeat became.

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

It wasn't echoing through the cavern.

It was echoing through him.

Every beat caused the crimson mark hidden beneath the skin of his right hand to burn for a brief moment before fading again.

Aidan reached the bottom of the staircase and froze.

An enormous underground city stretched before him.

Towering black structures reached toward a ceiling so high it disappeared into darkness. Bridges connected massive towers suspended above rivers of glowing crimson energy. Ancient banners hung from broken walls, untouched by time.

Yet there wasn't a single living soul.

It was beautiful.

It was abandoned.

It felt... lonely.

Aidan slowly walked through the empty streets.

Stone statues lined the roads.

Some depicted warriors.

Others showed scholars, healers, children, and kings.

No one looked victorious.

Every face carried determination.

As though they understood that greatness was never free.

He stopped before the largest statue in the city.

It portrayed a man standing with one hand extended toward the horizon while the other rested against the shoulder of a small child.

The man's face had been deliberately destroyed.

Not weathered.

Destroyed.

Someone had erased it.

The child's face remained untouched.

Aidan frowned.

Who would erase only one face?

Before he could examine it further, crimson light gathered beneath his feet.

LOCATION DISCOVERED

THE SANCTUARY OF THE FIRST AGE

Historical Classification:

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The words faded into the air.

Locked.

Everything important seemed to be locked.

Every answer demanded another question.

As he continued through the silent city, he noticed something strange.

The buildings weren't damaged by war.

There were no collapsed homes.

No burned streets.

No scattered weapons.

The city hadn't been destroyed.

It had simply been... left behind.

As though every person had walked away on the very same day.

His footsteps echoed across an empty plaza until he reached its center.

There stood another pedestal.

Unlike the first, nothing rested upon it.

Only an inscription carved into black stone.

The letters shimmered as Aidan approached.

Blood opens the door.

Character determines what waits beyond it.

He slowly placed his hand upon the pedestal.

Nothing happened.

Then the crimson mark beneath his skin ignited.

A deep rumble shook the city.

The ground split apart before him as an enormous circular platform rose from beneath the earth.

At its center stood a single doorway crafted from black crystal.

It had no handle.

No lock.

Only a handprint glowing with crimson light.

Aidan hesitated.

Something beyond that door terrified him.

Not because it felt evil.

Because it felt important.

He slowly placed his palm against the glowing mark.

The crystal doorway dissolved into thousands of crimson particles.

A wave of ancient power rolled through the city.

Far above, every statue lowered its head simultaneously.

Not by magic.

By recognition.

Aidan stepped into the chamber beyond.

The room was surprisingly small.

Circular.

Empty.

Except for a single object resting upon a stone table.

It wasn't a weapon.

It wasn't a crown.

It wasn't treasure.

It was a simple wooden practice staff.

Worn smooth by countless hands.

Splintered near one end.

Ordinary.

Confused, Aidan picked it up.

The moment his fingers closed around the wood, another memory exploded into existence.

A young boy struggled to lift the very same staff.

Again and again he fell.

Again and again he stood.

A calm voice corrected his stance.

Not harder.

Better.

Strength fades.

Skill remains.

The memory shifted.

The boy became older.

Faster.

Stronger.

Yet the same voice repeated the lesson.

Power earned without discipline creates monsters.

Power mastered creates protectors.

The vision vanished.

Aidan stared at the plain wooden staff in disbelief.

The Legacy hadn't offered him a legendary weapon.

It had offered him the first lesson.

A foundation.

Not power.

Discipline.

Before he could process the realization, the chamber began to shake violently.

A piercing alarm echoed throughout the Sanctuary.

Not from the System.

From the city itself.

Every crimson crystal flashed at once.

Deep beneath the Sanctuary...

Something had awakened.

Aidan turned toward the entrance just as a roar unlike anything he had ever heard ripped through the underground kingdom.

It wasn't the cry of a beast.

It wasn't the voice of a man.

It was something ancient enough to remember the beginning of the Crimson Legacy.

For the first time since entering the hidden world beneath House Darkheart...

Aidan realized he was not alone.
