## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Two — The Road Turns Home

The stranger's final words lingered long after he had vanished.

The greatest curses...

Never begin with hatred.

They begin...

With love.

No one spoke as the company continued along the Pilgrim Road.

Even Malik, who usually found a joke for every awkward silence, simply walked beside Elias.

Cassian remained several steps behind the others.

His thoughts were elsewhere.

Aidan noticed.

He said nothing.

Sometimes people needed someone to walk beside them...

Not questions.

By sunset, Rowan stopped beside an ancient milestone.

The weathered stone carried a familiar symbol.

The crimson tree.

Beneath it, another inscription had survived the centuries.

**Every road eventually returns to its beginning.**

Elias frowned.

Returns?

I thought we were moving forward.

Rowan smiled.

Sometimes...

Moving forward requires returning to what you left unfinished.

Aidan looked toward the fading sunlight.

For reasons he couldn't explain...

His thoughts drifted back to Blackwood Academy.

To House Darkheart.

To the unfinished life he had left behind.

History wasn't pulling him away from home.

It was preparing him to return differently than he had left.

## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Three — Brothers

Camp was quiet that night.

After dinner, Malik wandered into the trees with Kael to gather firewood.

Lyra sketched the old milestone in her notebook while Darius sharpened everyone's blades without being asked.

Aidan and Elias sat together overlooking the valley below.

It reminded them of another hill.

Another sunset.

Another time.

Remember the old watchtower?

Elias asked.

Aidan laughed immediately.

The one we weren't supposed to climb?

Exactly.

You slipped halfway up.

You caught me.

Elias smiled.

You would've done the same.

I would've.

Silence settled comfortably between them.

Then Elias spoke again.

When I left...

I kept wondering if you'd be angry.

Why would I be?

Because I disappeared.

Aidan looked at his oldest friend.

You didn't disappear.

You went where you had to go.

There's a difference.

Elias let out a slow breath.

I still missed you.

Aidan chuckled.

You think you had it bad?

Do you know how many times I almost said...

Wait until Elias gets back...

Only to remember you weren't there?

Elias laughed so hard he had to wipe tears from his eyes.

Seriously?

Every week.

Maybe every day.

For the first time since returning...

The guilt Elias had carried for years began lifting.

Home wasn't a place.

Sometimes...

Home was simply the person who never stopped saving your place beside them.

## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Four — Cassian's Question

The fire had burned low.

Most of the company had already gone to sleep.

Cassian remained awake.

Watching the flames.

Aidan quietly sat beside him.

Couldn't sleep?

Cassian shook his head.

Do you ever wonder...

If people are born to become something they don't want to be?

Aidan considered the question carefully.

Sometimes.

Cassian stared into the fire.

What if no matter what you choose...

You still become the person everyone fears?

The question felt strangely personal.

Aidan answered honestly.

Then I'd judge you by the choices you make...

Not by the future someone else predicts.

Cassian finally looked at him.

You really believe that?

Yeah.

People aren't prisoners of tomorrow.

They're responsible for today.

Cassian smiled faintly.

I hope you're right.

Neither realized...

History itself had quietly recorded the conversation.

## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Five — Blackwood

Three days later...

The company reached the final ridge overlooking the eastern valley.

Rowan stopped.

Your journey through the Second Foundation ends here.

Malik looked around in confusion.

We're going back?

Rowan nodded.

You've learned what this Foundation was meant to teach.

Now...

Life must test whether you actually learned it.

The old Keeper looked directly at Aidan.

Knowledge that never leaves the classroom...

Dies in the classroom.

The Pilgrim Road always returns its travelers to the world.

Only then does history discover what they truly became.

Below them...

Far beyond the forests...

A familiar skyline appeared.

Blackwood.

The academy.

House Darkheart.

Home.

Elias smiled.

Race you there.

Before Aidan could answer...

Elias was already running down the mountain.

Malik shouted.

That's cheating!

Then took off after him.

Kael followed.

Darius laughed.

Lyra shook her head before joining them.

Even Cassian smiled.

For a little while...

They weren't future legends.

They were simply young people racing each other home.

Aidan watched them disappear down the trail.

Then looked back at Rowan.

Will I see you again?

The old Keeper smiled.

Foundations never truly leave those who remember them.

Go home, Aidan.

Your greatest lessons...

Will not happen here.

With that...

Rowan turned and walked back toward the mountains.

Without looking back.

Teachers.

Students.

Even the birds.
