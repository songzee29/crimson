## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Seven — The New Semester

The first bell of the new semester echoed across Blackwood Academy.

Students filled the courtyard with excited conversations about vacations, tournaments, examinations, and rumors gathered over the past several weeks.

For Aidan...

It felt strangely different.

He had left as a student.

He returned carrying memories no one else could see.

Beside him, Malik stretched his arms over his head.

I missed this place...

Never thought I'd actually say that.

Elias laughed.

Give it until the first assignment.

You'll change your mind.

Lyra rolled her eyes.

That'll take five minutes.

Kael smiled quietly while Darius shook his head.

Some things...

Never changed.

And for a brief moment...

Aidan was grateful they didn't.

---

As the group crossed the courtyard, conversations slowly began fading.

Students recognized Aidan.

Some remembered the bridge rescue.

Others had heard stories that had already grown larger than the truth.

Most simply watched.

Aidan ignored the whispers.

Elias noticed immediately.

Still hate attention?

Always will.

Good.

Someone has to keep your ego under control.

Malik immediately pointed toward himself.

That's my job.

No...

Elias replied with a grin.

You're the bad influence.

I'm the responsible one.

The entire group laughed.

Even several nearby students couldn't help smiling.

Watching them together...

It was obvious.

This wasn't just a group of classmates.

They were becoming a family.

---

Near the academy entrance stood Headmaster Aldren.

His silver robes moved gently in the morning breeze.

He greeted each student with the same calm smile.

When Aidan approached, the Headmaster studied him for several quiet moments.

Welcome home, Mr. Darkheart.

Thank you, Headmaster.

I trust your journey answered some questions.

Aidan smiled faintly.

It gave me better ones.

The Headmaster chuckled.

Then it was worthwhile.

His gaze shifted toward Elias.

Mr. Ashbourne.

Welcome back to Blackwood.

Elias bowed respectfully.

It's good to be home.

Then...

The Headmaster looked toward Cassian.

His expression became almost impossible to read.

Mr. Vale.

Cassian inclined his head.

Headmaster.

Only one word.

Yet Aidan noticed something.

For the first time...

The Headmaster seemed cautious.

Not fearful.

Thoughtful.

As though he recognized something in Cassian that no one else yet understood.

---

The morning assembly gathered every student inside the Grand Hall.

Sunlight poured through towering stained-glass windows depicting the history of Blackwood.

The Founding of the Academy.

The First Great Coven.

The Treaty of the Three Clans.

None mentioned House Darkheart.

None mentioned the Crimson Legacy.

History...

Was still incomplete.

Headmaster Aldren stepped onto the stage.

Welcome to another year at Blackwood Academy.

Knowledge remains our greatest inheritance.

Power without wisdom has destroyed kingdoms.

Wisdom without courage has saved them.

Remember why you are here.

Not simply to become stronger...

But to become worthy of the strength you pursue.

His words settled across the hall.

Aidan found himself remembering the journal inside the First Keeper's Vault.

*Build people.*

*Empires rise and fall.*

*People become civilizations.*

The lesson had followed him home.

---

As the assembly ended...

A young instructor hurried toward the Headmaster and quietly whispered something into his ear.

The Headmaster's calm expression changed ever so slightly.

He looked across the hall.

His eyes stopped on Aidan.

Then Elias.

Then Cassian.

Finally...

He addressed the entire academy.

There has been a change to this semester's curriculum.

Beginning tomorrow...

Selected students will participate in a new historical field program.

Attendance...

Will not be optional.

A murmur spread throughout the hall.

Malik leaned toward Elias.

Why do I suddenly feel like that's bad news?

Elias smiled.

Because whenever someone says attendance isn't optional...

It usually is.

Just not for us.

Aidan looked toward the Headmaster.

Something told him...

The program had nothing to do with history lessons.

And everything to do with preparing them for something no one at Blackwood was yet ready to face.
