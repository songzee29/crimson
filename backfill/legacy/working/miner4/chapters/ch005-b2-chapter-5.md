# Chapter Five  
## Crimson on the Stone

The alarm bell rang just before sunrise.

One strike.

Then another.

Not the frantic rhythm of invasion.

The measured warning of a breached ward.

Students poured from their dormitories as instructors shouted orders across the academy grounds.

"Second-years to the eastern shelter!"

"Faculty to the outer barriers!"

"No one leaves the academy alone!"

Aidan was already moving.

By the time he reached the eastern gate, Rowan, Helena, Lyra, Cassian, Malik, and Elias had gathered beside the cracked wardstone.

The protective barrier shimmered unevenly.

Not broken.

Disturbed.

Professor Helena rested her palm against the ancient rune.

"...Something crossed the outer boundary."

"What?" Malik asked.

"I don't know."

"There aren't any magical signatures," Rowan added quietly. "It's as though whatever passed through... left almost no trace."

Aidan knelt beside the fractured earth.

There.

A single footprint.

Too large for any ordinary person.

Yet impossibly light.

The grass surrounding it hadn't even bent.

"We follow it," Aidan said.

Rowan looked toward him.

"You'll lead?"

Aidan met his gaze.

"Unless you have another plan."

The old master smiled faintly.

"I was hoping you'd say that."

...

The seven of them crossed beyond Blackwood's restored wards for the first time since the Witch Curse ended.

The surrounding forest had changed.

Branches twisted unnaturally.

Birdsong had disappeared.

Even the wind seemed reluctant to pass through the ancient trees.

Nobody spoke.

Every footstep echoed louder than it should have.

Cassian suddenly raised a fist.

"Stop."

Everyone froze.

A thin strand of silver thread stretched between two trees.

Almost invisible.

Aidan studied it.

"A trap."

Cassian nodded.

"But old."

No sooner had the words left his mouth than the forest erupted.

Three massive wolf-like creatures burst from the undergrowth.

Not werewolves.

Not ordinary beasts.

Scarred predators with crimson eyes and jaws lined with jagged bone.

They attacked without hesitation.

"Scatter!" Rowan ordered.

The first beast lunged toward Elias.

Aidan moved before he consciously decided to.

One instant he stood beside Rowan.

The next—

He was already between Elias and the creature.

His hand struck the beast's throat.

"Hold."

Invisible force slammed the monster into the ground.

The earth exploded beneath the impact.

Before it could recover, Lyra carved glowing runes through the air while Malik crashed into another beast with enough force to splinter nearby trees.

Cassian intercepted the third.

Steel flashed.

The creature dodged with unnatural speed.

It raked crimson claws across Cassian's chest before disappearing into the brush.

"Aidan!"

He turned instinctively.

Too late.

The beast beneath him wasn't trapped.

It had been waiting.

Its jaws closed around his forearm.

Pain exploded through his body.

Hot.

Sharp.

The creature's teeth tore through flesh before Aidan drove it backward with another Law.

Blood splattered across wet stone.

His blood.

Bright crimson drops scattered over the forest floor.

Time slowed.

He stared.

The scent reached him instantly.

Warm.

Rich.

His own blood.

Something deep inside him stirred.

Not fear.

Not pain.

Recognition.

The second heartbeat thundered once.

THUMP.

His vision sharpened.

Every heartbeat around him became suddenly clear.

Malik's.

Lyra's.

Rowan's.

Cassian's.

Elias's.

Even the wounded beast's pulse.

They echoed through the forest like distant drums.

"Aidan!"

Lyra's voice shattered the moment.

He blinked.

The strange clarity vanished.

The beast charged again.

This time Aidan didn't hesitate.

He stepped inside its attack.

One precise strike.

One whispered Law.

The creature collapsed without another sound.

Silence returned.

Heavy breathing filled the clearing.

Rowan approached first.

"You were bitten."

"I'm fine."

"You are bleeding."

Only then did Aidan notice blood still running down his arm.

It should have bothered him.

Instead...

He found himself staring at it.

The crimson trail running across his skin.

His fingers twitched.

A strange impulse flickered through him.

Touch it.

Taste—

"No."

The word escaped before he realized he'd spoken aloud.

Everyone looked at him.

Aidan quickly wrapped his sleeve around the wound.

"It was nothing."

Nobody argued.

But Lyra continued watching him.

...

The journey back to Blackwood was quieter.

The creatures had vanished.

No tracks remained.

Only torn earth and blood marked the battle.

Back inside the academy infirmary, Helena carefully stitched the wound closed.

"The bite isn't poisoned," she said.

"That's the good news."

"And the bad?"

"It should have hurt more than you're letting on."

Aidan glanced at the bandages.

"I've had worse."

"You have."

Helena tied the final knot.

"That doesn't make this harmless."

...

Night settled over Blackwood.

Most students never learned how close the battle had come.

The academy remained safe.

For another day.

Aidan stood alone beside the infirmary window.

Rain tapped softly against the glass.

His arm ached.

Not unbearably.

Just enough to remind him he was alive.

Then—

A droplet of blood escaped through the fresh bandage.

It slid slowly across the back of his hand.

Without thinking...

His thumb moved toward it.

He stopped less than an inch away.

His breathing grew uneven.

Why...

Why had he reached for it?

The second heartbeat answered.

Steady.

Patient.

Closer than ever.

**Instinct Recorded.**

No explanation followed.

No reward.

No guidance.

Only silence.

Aidan stared at the empty air for several moments before lowering his hand.

Outside, thunder rolled across the distant mountains.

Inside, Blackwood Academy slept under the protection of the Crimson Witch.

Yet somewhere beneath flawless magic...

Beyond reason...

Beyond understanding...

A quiet hunger had survived its first taste of blood.

And it had not been satisfied.
