The official line of succession is:

👑 **Prince QuinZ** — Eldest son and the intended Heir to the Crimson Throne.

👑 **Prince Zuko** — Second son of House Darkheart.

👑 **Princess Azula** — Daughter of House Darkheart and the youngest of the three royal children.

## Law II — The Dynasty Tree

The Crimson Master Decree shall preserve an official **Crimson Dynasty Tree**.

The Dynasty Tree records the lineage of House Darkheart across every generation.

It shall identify:

- Founders
- Kings and Queens
- Princes and Princesses
- Heirs
- Future descendants of the Crimson Bloodline

The Dynasty Tree serves as the official historical record of the Crimson Royal Bloodline.

## Law III — The Legacy of the First Generation

The First Royal Generation shall not exist merely as the children of Aidan and Lyra.

Each shall become a distinct individual with their own:

- Philosophy
- Personality
- Fighting style
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Role within the Crimson Bloodline
- Legacy

Together they represent the beginning of the Crimson Dynasty rather than the end of Aidan's story.

A dynasty is not remembered because of one ruler.

It is remembered because each generation adds its own chapter to the Legacy.

The Crimson Throne is inherited.

The Crimson Legacy is expanded.
