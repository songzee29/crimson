# THE CRIMSON LEGACY BIBLE DECREE

## BOOK II — CANON AMENDMENTS

### DECREE XXXI — THE CRIMSON LEGACY IS A CHARACTER

The Crimson Legacy is not merely a power system.

It is a living will.

It observes.

It remembers.

It tests.

It adapts.

It has one purpose: to ensure that no heir ever repeats the Founder's greatest mistakes.

The Legacy does not choose the strongest.

It chooses the one willing to bear the greatest burden.

---

### DECREE XXXII — THE SYSTEM NEVER LIES

The Crimson System may refuse to answer.

It may seal information.

It may delay the truth until the proper moment.

But it will never intentionally deceive its heir.

When the System speaks, its words are true.

When it is silent, there is a reason.

---

### DECREE XXXIII — THE PRICE CANNOT BE CHEATED

Every awakening requires a genuine cost.

A sacrifice cannot be substituted with an illusion.

It cannot be bypassed by another person's choice.

It cannot be undone by time travel, resurrection, or convenience.

The cost must permanently change the heir.

---

### DECREE XXXIV — REZ'S PURPOSE

Rez is not inside the Legacy to make Aidan stronger.

He is there to keep Aidan from losing himself.

His greatest victories will come through wisdom, restraint, and hard truths—not overwhelming power.

Even Rez does not know every secret hidden within the Legacy.

---

### DECREE XXXV — AIDAN'S IDENTITY

Aidan will inherit Zee's blood.

He will inherit Zee's Legacy.

He will never inherit Zee's identity.

Every major decision must reflect Aidan's own heart, fears, hopes, and flaws.

If Aidan begins acting as a copy of Zee, the story has violated its own foundation.

---

### DECREE XXXVI — THE FOUNDER'S SHADOW

Zee's influence will always remain present.

His memory will guide.

His choices will echo.

His sacrifices will shape the world.

But Zee must never overshadow Aidan's journey.

The Founder built the road.

The Heir walks it.

---

### DECREE XXXVII — THE MYSTERY PRINCIPLE

Every revelation must answer one important question while creating new questions.

The world must grow larger with every discovery.

Mystery is not confusion.

Mystery is controlled curiosity.

---

### DECREE XXXVIII — THE WORLD DOES NOT REVOLVE AROUND AIDAN

The world continues moving whether Aidan is present or not.

Kingdoms rise.

Wars begin.

Enemies plan.

Friends struggle.

The Veil evolves.

Every major faction has goals independent of the heir.

This keeps the world alive.

---

### DECREE XXXIX — POWER MUST CHANGE THE PERSON

Every evolution changes more than abilities.

It changes perspective.

Relationships.

Emotions.

Responsibilities.

Power without personal transformation is forbidden within the Crimson Legacy.

---

### DECREE XL — THE FINAL LAW

The Crimson Legacy is a story about inheritance—not destiny.

Aidan inherits power.

He inherits history.

He inherits enemies.

He inherits impossible expectations.

He does **not** inherit his future.

His future belongs to him alone.

## OFFICIAL CANON PRIORITIES

When creating any future chapter, the following order of authority shall always apply:

1. The Crimson Legacy Bible Decree.
2. Locked Canon established by previous Decrees.
3. The internal logic of the world.
4. Character consistency.
5. Rule of sacrifice before rule of spectacle.
6. Mystery before exposition.
7. Emotional truth before displays of power.

If any future idea contradicts a higher authority, it must be revised or rejected.

---

## THE SCRIBES' DECREE

From this point forward, every new idea introduced into the Crimson Legacy shall be placed into one of four categories before becoming canon:

**CANON** — Official and binding.

**PENDING** — Under development and not yet binding.

**ARCHIVED** — Older concepts preserved for reference but replaced by newer canon.

**REJECTED** — Ideas intentionally excluded from the universe and never to be reintroduced unless formally restored by a future Bible Decree.

This process ensures that the Crimson Legacy remains internally consistent as the universe expands.
