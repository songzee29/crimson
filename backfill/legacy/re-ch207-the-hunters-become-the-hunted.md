Word spread quickly.

Every supernatural faction in Louisiana answered the call.

Veil Hunters.

Independent witches.

Mercenary vampires.

Hybrid trackers.

Everyone wanted the bounty on the Crimson King.

Some came for justice.

Others came for glory.

Most came believing...

It was only one vampire.

---

They gathered beneath an abandoned hotel.

Forty-seven fighters.

Weapons blessed.

Magic prepared.

Plans made.

The oldest hunter looked around.

"If we stay together..."

"...we live."

A young witch nodded.

"He can't kill all of us."

The room suddenly became cold.

Not from magic.

From presence.

One by one...

Every candle went out.

Darkness swallowed the room.

Then...

A single pair of crimson eyes opened.

Silence.

One heartbeat.

Then another.

Then...

Chaos.

The windows exploded inward.

Concrete cracked.

Bodies flew through walls before anyone saw who struck them.

Silver flashed.

Magic erupted.

Gunfire echoed.

None of it mattered.

Aidan moved like a shadow between them.

One strike.

Another.

Another.

No wasted motion.

No speeches.

No anger.

Just efficient violence.

The strongest hunter managed to slash Aidan across the chest.

The wound hissed.

Closed.

The hunter smiled.

"...I got him—"

His sentence ended as Aidan's hand closed around his throat.

The room went silent.

One by one...

The survivors dropped their weapons.

Not because they surrendered.

Because they realized...

They were standing before something beyond their understanding.

Aidan released the hunter's lifeless body.

Then disappeared.

When the dust settled...

Only seven people remained alive.

None of them ever spoke of that night without trembling.
