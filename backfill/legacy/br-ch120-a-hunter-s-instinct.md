Malik burst through the command hall doors.

"We found him."

Aidan looked up immediately.

"Cassian?"

Malik nodded.

"Outside the city."

"He hasn't attacked anyone..."

"...but something's different."

The Guardian slowly stood.

His expression turned grim.

"Different..."

"...how?"

Malik hesitated.

"Our scouts said..."

"They couldn't hear his heartbeat."

Silence.

The room froze.

Aidan's eyes narrowed.

"What?"

"They said..."

"...Cassian was breathing."

"...Walking."

"...Talking."

"...But none of them could hear a heartbeat."

The Guardian's cane struck the floor.

Once.

"No..."

Twice.

"No..."

A third time.

"...he's already begun."

---

Aidan didn't wait for another explanation.

He grabbed his coat.

His sword.

And walked toward the door.

Lyra stepped in front of him.

"Aidan."

He stopped.

"What if it's a trap?"

His answer came instantly.

"I know."

"Then why go alone?"

He looked out toward the storm gathering over the city.

"Because if Cassian has become what I think he has..."

His voice dropped.

"...this isn't a mission."

"It's a judgment."

The doors opened.

Thunder rolled across New Orleans.

And somewhere beyond the city limits...

Cassian looked toward the horizon.

He smiled.

He had been waiting.

The day both men knew was coming...

Had finally arrived.
