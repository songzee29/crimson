That night...

Cassian couldn't sleep.

Every time he closed his eyes...

He saw Elias.

Not screaming.

Smiling.

As if asking one question.

> **"Was it worth it?"**

Cassian sat upright.

"Damn it..."

He grabbed his head.

"I said stop!"

Silence.

Then...

The whisper returned.

Calm.

Patient.

> **"You're asking the wrong question."**

Cassian froze.

"...Who are you?"

The voice chuckled.

Low.

Ancient.

> **"Ask yourself..."**

A pause.

> **"...why he was chosen."**

Cassian's breathing slowed.

The question lingered.

Why...

Why Aidan?
