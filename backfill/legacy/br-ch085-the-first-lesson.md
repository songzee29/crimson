The morning breeze carried the sounds of House Darkheart below.

For the first time in what felt like forever...

The kingdom wasn't preparing for war.

It was healing.

Lyra watched the people smiling in the streets.

"They still believe in us."

Aidan followed her gaze.

"They always have."

She smiled.

"No..."

"They believe in you."

Aidan shook his head.

"They believe in what we're building."

**"You're getting better."**

*"At what?"*

**"Accepting that you don't have to carry everything by yourself."**

*"Don't get used to it."*

Aidan's expression became focused.

This wasn't an unlock.

It wasn't a reward.

It was knowledge waiting to be learned.

He mentally selected the blueprint.

Instead of granting him an ability...

The Legacy showed him principles.

The movement of Crimson Blood through his body.

The balance between intent and control.

The flow required to shape the Blood without wasting it.

No commands.

No activation.

Only understanding.

The blueprint faded.

---

Rez smiled.

**"See the difference?"**

Aidan nodded.

*"It's teaching me..."*

**"Exactly."**

**"The Legacy isn't fighting for you anymore."**

**"It's treating you like a Founder."**

---

Aidan slowly raised his right hand.

He closed his eyes.

Remembering every principle the blueprint had shown him.

His Crimson Blood responded.

Not because the Legacy commanded it.

Because he did.

A thin strand of crimson energy flowed across his fingertips.

It trembled.

Then collapsed.

The technique disappeared.

Aidan opened one eye.

"...Well."

"...That didn't last long."

Lyra laughed.

"You make failing look dramatic."

He smiled.

"I'll take that as encouragement."

---

Rez folded his arms.

**"Again."**

Aidan nodded.

This time...

He slowed his breathing.

He stopped forcing the Blood.

Instead...

He listened to it.

The Crimson Blood moved naturally through his body.

He guided it instead of controlling it.

The crimson strand appeared again.

Longer this time.

Steadier.

It circled his hand like a ribbon before dissolving harmlessly into the air.

Aidan smiled.

"I felt the difference."

**"Good."**

Rez nodded.

**"Power isn't about forcing Crimson to obey you."**

**"It's about understanding how Crimson already wants to flow."**

Aidan looked out across House Darkheart once more.

His kingdom was at peace.

His Queen was alive.

True Crimson Blood flowed through his veins.

And despite everything he had accomplished...

He still had so much left to learn.

For the first time...

That thought excited him.

Not because he sought more power.

But because every new lesson meant he was becoming the kind of king future generations could look to...

Not as someone who was born perfect.

But as someone who never stopped growing.
