Three days passed after the Burning Quarter.

Aidan's ribs were still bruised.

His hands still ached whenever he used Crimson Thread.

The Witch Authority hadn't become easier.

If anything...

It felt heavier.

Like it was slowly settling deeper into his soul.

The Guardian found the Circle before sunrise.

His expression alone told them this wasn't another ordinary mission.

He threw a folded newspaper onto the warehouse table.

Across the front page—

### **FOURTEEN PEOPLE VANISH DURING MARDI GRAS PREPARATION.**

Malik slowly lowered the paper.

"...Fourteen?"

The Guardian nodded.

"No bodies."

"No witnesses."

"No curse residue."

Sienna frowned.

"That's impossible."

"No."

The Guardian looked toward Aidan.

"It's deliberate."

The System appeared.

---

**《MAIN QUEST》**

## The Hollow King

Location:
Bayou Lafourche

Difficulty:
Very High

Objective:

Locate the missing civilians.

Destroy the source.

Failure...

**Unknown.**

---

Aidan noticed something.

There was still no reward listed.

The System was changing.

Or perhaps...

It was trusting him to stop asking what he would gain.

He looked around the room.

Everyone was waiting.

Again.

Not because he was the strongest.

Because they trusted him.

He stood.

"We leave now."
