The crimson-black sphere shrieked.

Not with rage.

With fear.

Every heartbeat it had stolen…

Every grief it had nurtured…

Every lonely soul it had twisted…

Found nothing left to cling to.

The academy stood together.

Not unafraid.

United.

The Curse struck anyway.

The sphere exploded outward.

A tidal wave of crimson-black magic crashed directly toward Aidan.

Everyone moved at once.

Cassian stepped in front of him.

Lyra reached for him.

Malik raised his staff.

Elias anchored the nearest barrier.

“No!”

Aidan shouted.

Circle Weaving ignited instinctively.

Golden threads connected them all.

The attack struck.

The world disappeared into crimson light.

⸻

Silence.

Then…

Pain.

Not physical.

Memory.

Aidan stood alone.

No academy.

No Rowan.

No Lyra.

No Cassian.

No Malik.

No Elias.

Only endless darkness.

The Curse spoke clearly for the first time.

Choose.

Save yourself…

…or lose them forever.

Aidan smiled.

“You still don’t understand.”

The darkness hesitated.

“I already chose.”

He closed his eyes.

Not reaching for power.

For them.

The Circle answered.

Golden threads appeared within the darkness.

One by one.

Lyra.

Cassian.

Malik.

Elias.

Then Rowan.

Then Professor Helena.

Then every heart refusing to abandon another.

The darkness cracked.

⸻

The Crimson Legacy appeared one final time.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE CRIMSON WITCH

Final Judgment Complete.

The Heir has fulfilled the purpose of the Witch Authority.

Power served protection.

Strength served love.

Authority served others.

The Witch Curse can no longer define the Heir.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A final message appeared.

Smaller.

Quieter.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

THE WITCH CURSE

Breaking…

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The darkness shattered like glass.

⸻

The academy courtyard returned.

The crimson-black sphere fractured into thousands of tiny lights.

Each carried a whisper.

Then…

Peace.

The old oak stopped bleeding darkness.

Its shattered trunk slowly began glowing with warm crimson-gold light.

Every broken ward stabilized.

Every barrier settled.

The impossible pressure vanished.

Students slowly looked around.

Some cried openly.

Others laughed through tears.

Professor Helena sank to one knee in exhausted relief.

Headmaster Aldren removed his hat.

Master Rowan simply watched Aidan.

Pride filled his tired eyes.

“You don’t need me anymore.”

Aidan immediately shook his head.

“I always will.”

Rowan smiled.

“Good.”

“That means I taught you well.”

He stepped forward and embraced Aidan.

Not as teacher and student.

As family.

Around them…

Cassian, Lyra, Malik, and Elias closed the circle without a word.

Five friends.

One family.

The Crimson Legacy appeared one final time before fading into the quiet night.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WITCH ARC

Complete.

The Crimson Witch has been born.

Preparing transition…

VAMPIRE AUTHORITY

Dormant.

The next Curse waits.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The crimson light dissolved into the night sky.

For the first time since opening the Crimson Book…

There were no new lessons.

Only the quiet certainty that one journey had ended.

And another…

Far darker one…

Had just begun.
