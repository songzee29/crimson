Cassian walked through House Darkheart.

No one recognized him.

To the citizens...

He was only another traveler.

Children laughed as they ran past him.

Merchants greeted him warmly.

An old woman offered him fresh bread.

"Long journey?"

Cassian accepted it.

"...Yes."

"Then welcome to House Darkheart."

She smiled.

"We're always happy to help strangers."

Cassian stood frozen.

His hand trembled.

These weren't soldiers.

These weren't enemies.

These were the people Aidan had spent years protecting.

His anger cracked...

For just a moment.

---

Then he looked at the burned ring in his hand.

The crack closed.

He continued walking.
