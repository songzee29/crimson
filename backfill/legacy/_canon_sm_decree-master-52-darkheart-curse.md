**The Darkheart Curse**

To establish the eternal judgment placed upon Cassian by the Final Decree of House Darkheart and define the laws governing the Darkheart Curse.

### Law

Aidan's Final Decree creates an **Authority-Born Curse** known as the **Darkheart Curse**.

It is not a spell.

It is not a blood curse.

It is not a supernatural ability.

It is a judgment pronounced through the authority of the Final Decree.

The Final Decree declares:

> **"By the Final Decree... of House Darkheart...**
>
> **You shall never know peace...**
>
> **For your soul shall never outrun my judgment."**

---

### The Nature of the Curse

The Darkheart Curse does **not** function as a tracking ability.

It is **not** a GPS.

It does **not** reveal Cassian's location.

It does **not** compel Aidan to hunt Cassian.

It does **not** compel Cassian to seek Aidan.

Instead, it forges an eternal bond between their destinies.

Whenever Cassian finally finds peace...

Whenever he builds a kingdom...

Whenever he creates a family...

Whenever he believes the judgment of House Darkheart has ended...

Fate itself shall eventually bring the Darkheart Legacy back into his life.

His peace shall never remain untouched forever.

Cassian's immortality does not free him from the Darkheart Curse.

It strengthens it.

Every century he lives...

Every home he builds...

Every throne he claims...

Every happiness he earns...

May one day stand before the judgment of House Darkheart.

His immortality becomes part of his sentence.

---

### Foundational Principle

The Darkheart Curse is **not** a sentence of eternal pursuit.

It is a sentence of **eternal judgment**.

Justice shall arrive in its own time.

It cannot be rushed.

It cannot be escaped.

It cannot be outrun.
