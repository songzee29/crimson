The metallic clang still echoed through the underground cathedral.

Silence followed.

Not true silence.

The sound of blood.

It surrounded Aidan.

The dried crimson names carved across the walls began to pulse.

One after another.

Thousands of ancient heartbeats filled the chamber.

Not living.

Remembered.

The walls themselves carried the echoes of every vampire who had stood before the Ancient Gates.

Aidan’s crimson eyes swept across the endless inscriptions.

None bore the name Darkheart.

The realization passed through him without emotion.

His humanity remained gone.

Only instinct remained.

The massive doors rumbled again.

A second crimson seal emerged across their surface before drifting away from the stone.

It floated into the center of the chamber.

A perfect circle of blood hovered in the air.

Then it shattered.

The entire cathedral transformed.

The pillars vanished.

The walls dissolved into darkness.

Only countless crimson silhouettes remained.

Every figure possessed glowing crimson eyes.

Every figure watched him.

Without warning—

The nearest Blood Echo attacked.

It moved almost exactly as Aidan did.

Crimson Dash.

A straight punch.

Aidan twisted.

The fist shattered stone where he had stood.

Another echo appeared.

Then another.

Five.

Ten.

Twenty.

Each carried different movements.

Different instincts.

Different fighting styles.

The chamber wasn’t testing strength.

It was testing adaptation.

Aidan disappeared.

Crimson Dash exploded beneath his feet.

His fist tore through the nearest Blood Echo.

It burst into crimson mist.

Immediately another replaced it.

The Hall never stopped.

Every victory created another challenge.

Minutes passed.

Then an hour.

The echoes became faster.

Smarter.

More aggressive.

A crimson blade sliced across Aidan’s ribs.

Another opened his shoulder.

His regeneration responded—

Slowly.

Too slowly.

Blood reserves remained critically low.

Every wound lingered longer than the last.

Yet he continued moving.

Every instinct sharpened.

Every mistake became rarer.

The Hall slowly forced the Crimson Vampire to evolve.
