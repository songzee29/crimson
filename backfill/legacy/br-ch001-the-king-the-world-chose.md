*"Every legend has one final sunrise before the darkness remembers its name."*

---

Ten years.

That was how long it had been since Elias died.

Ten years since the Witch Curse.

Ten years since New Orleans watched a broken young man carry his brother through burning streets.

Now...

That same young man stood atop St. Louis Cathedral.

The wind carried his black coat behind him.

His silver-and-crimson ring caught the morning light.

Below...

Nearly half a million people filled the streets.

Not because of a festival.

Not because of a holiday.

Because today...

The world would witness something it had never seen before.

---

Operation...

**Rise of the King.**

---

Every major supernatural faction had answered the call.

The Veil.

Independent witches.

Spirit clans.

Hunter guilds.

Eastern covens.

Western covens.

Northern tribes.

Even kingdoms that had refused to acknowledge one another for centuries stood side by side.

For one reason.

Aidan.

---

Below the cathedral...

The Circle gathered together.

Malik adjusted his jacket before looking toward the crowd.

"...This many people?"

Sienna laughed.

"I've seen smaller countries."

Lyra walked beside Aidan.

Her hand naturally found his.

Not because they needed comfort.

Because after ten years...

It had become instinct.

She smiled.

"You nervous?"

Aidan looked over the city.

"...Terrified."

She laughed softly.

"Good."

He looked at her.

"You think that's funny?"

"I think..."

"...it means you're still human."

Those words lingered.

Neither of them knew...

How precious they really were.

---

The bells of St. Louis Cathedral rang.

Once.

Twice.

Seven times.

The entire city became silent.

The Guardian stepped onto the balcony overlooking New Orleans.

His hair had become completely white.

Time had finally caught him.

But his voice...

Still carried the strength of a kingmaker.

---

"People of New Orleans..."

His words echoed across loudspeakers enchanted by witches.

"For generations..."

"We feared the night."

"We feared curses."

"We feared monsters."

He slowly turned toward Aidan.

Then smiled.

"But one young man..."

"...refused to fear."

Cheers erupted.

The Guardian raised one hand.

Silence returned immediately.

"Today..."

"We begin the largest supernatural peace operation in recorded history."

More applause.

"This operation has one purpose."

He looked toward Aidan.

"To ensure..."

"...that no child ever loses their family to darkness again."

Aidan lowered his head.

Elias flashed through his mind.

His promise.

His failure.

His brother.

He quietly whispered...

"I'm still trying."

---

The Guardian extended one hand toward him.

"Aidan Darkheart..."

"Step forward."

He did.

Every camera.

Every witch.

Every hunter.

Every citizen watched him.

The Guardian spoke the words history would remember forever.

> **"By the will of those gathered..."**

> **"...and by the trust of those you have protected..."**

> **"...I ask you..."**

A pause.

Then...

> **"...will you lead Operation: Rise of the King?"**

Silence.

Aidan looked over the crowd.

Children sat on their parents' shoulders.

Firefighters stood in dress uniforms.

Police officers saluted.

The families Elias had once saved stood together in the front row.

Even Commander Cassian stood among the allied leaders.

He and Aidan locked eyes.

No hatred.

Not today.

Only understanding.

Two men carrying the same ghost.

Aidan finally answered.

"...I will."

The city exploded into cheers.

---

Deep beneath the Hall of Kings...

The Vampire Seal pulsed.

Once.

---

**Containment: 89%.**

---

The Crimson Legacy hid the message.

---

Above the city...

The Journal opened.

Its ink moved without hesitation.

---

> **Today...**

Another line formed.

> **The world crowns its protector.**

One final sentence appeared.

> **History prepares to crown something else.**

The page closed.

---

Far away...

Beyond Louisiana...

Beyond America...

In forgotten castles.

Abandoned crypts.

Sunken temples.

Ancient vampire prisons.

Thousands of crimson eyes opened at exactly the same moment.

One sentence echoed through every chamber.

> **"...He accepted."**

---

The Smiling Man stood alone before his chessboard.

For the first time in years...

He stopped smiling.

He gently picked up the Crimson King.

Held it over the board.

Then whispered...

> **"Forgive me..."**

> **"...because the only way for a king to rise..."**

He slowly placed the piece onto the center square.

> **"...is for the world to watch him fall."**

Outside...

The first drop of crimson rain struck the roof of St. Louis Cathedral.

Only one drop.

No one noticed.

Except Aidan.

He looked up at the clear blue sky.

Touched the crimson drop on the back of his hand.

His smile faded.

The blood vanished before anyone else could see it.

Deep inside his soul...

The Witch Authority stirred.

The Vampire Authority answered.

For the first time...

They did not fight.

They looked at each other.

And the Crimson Legacy recorded a sentence it prayed would never become true:

> **"Operation: Rise of the King has begun."**

**Book II had officially begun.** 👑🩸
