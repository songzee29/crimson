The crimson thread floated silently through the empty streets of New Orleans.

Aidan followed.

The city slept around him.

Jazz drifted through distant alleyways.

The Mississippi rolled quietly beside ancient brick buildings.

Every few steps...

The ring grew warmer.

As if it recognized places before Aidan did.

The thread finally stopped.

Not in front of a church.

Not beneath a cemetery.

But...

In the middle of Jackson Square.

Aidan frowned.

"...Here?"

Nothing.

Just old stone.

Tourists' footsteps from earlier that day.

Street performers' forgotten chalk drawings.

The thread disappeared into the ground.

For Aidan.
