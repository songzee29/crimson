Back in the living world...

The Crimson Assassin stopped walking.

For the first time since it had been reborn...

It didn't immediately continue following Aidan.

Its head slowly lifted.

Its closed eyes trembled.

Its heartbeat...

No longer echoed once every few minutes.

Now...

It beat steadily.

Slowly.

Like a heart remembering how to live.

Aidan stopped.

He turned.

The Crimson Assassin stood perfectly still.

Its hands slowly tightened into fists.

Then relaxed.

Aidan simply watched.

He didn't speak.

He understood.

Something was changing.

Inside the prison...

Rez frowned in his sleep.

A voice echoed across the darkness.

Not the System.

Not Aidan.

His own.

> **"...Why..."**

Another heartbeat.

> **"...am I..."**

The words caught in his throat.

Then...

Memory.

A boy.

A crimson book.

A promise.

A king.

Everything returned at once.

His eyes snapped open.

Brilliant crimson.
