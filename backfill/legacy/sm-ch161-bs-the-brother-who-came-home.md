## Chapter One Hundred Sixty-One — The Brother Who Came Home

The valley of the Second Foundation had not yet awakened.

Morning mist drifted across the stone paths while students, Keepers, and craftsmen quietly began another day.

Aidan stood alone outside the temple, studying the mountains beyond the valley.

Something felt different.

Not dangerous.

Familiar.

Footsteps approached from behind.

Rowan smiled before Aidan even turned around.

I was wondering how long it would take you to notice.

Notice what?

He's late.

Just like always.

Aidan frowned.

Who?

Before Rowan could answer...

A voice echoed across the valley.

Still staring at mountains before breakfast?

Some things never change.

Aidan froze.

No...

That voice...

Slowly...

He turned.

A young man stood at the entrance to the valley.

He looked about Aidan's age.

Dark skin weathered by years beneath the sun.

Curly black hair that had grown longer than Aidan remembered.

An old traveler’s cloak covered well-worn leather armor.

A sword rested across his back.

Not an ornate weapon.

One that had clearly seen years of use.

Across his shoulder sat a faded canvas backpack held together by countless repairs.

His amber eyes locked onto Aidan.

Then...

He smiled.

Not politely.

Not cautiously.

Like someone who had finally found family.

You still make that stupid face whenever you're surprised.

Aidan couldn't breathe.

Elias...

The name escaped as little more than a whisper.

The traveler laughed.

There he is.

Thought I'd lost you for a second.

Aidan crossed the distance before his mind had time to think.

The two embraced so hard they nearly lost their balance.

Years disappeared in a heartbeat.

Neither cared who was watching.

Rowan quietly looked away.

Some reunions belonged only to those who had earned them.

---

When they finally stepped apart...

Aidan punched Elias lightly in the shoulder.

You disappeared.

Elias rubbed the spot dramatically.

First thing you do after three years is hit me?

Three years?

Try saying welcome home first.

Aidan laughed harder than he had in months.

You look terrible.

I've been hiking across half the continent.

What's your excuse?

Apparently carrying ancient history around.

Fair enough.

The two grinned at each other.

It was effortless.

As though the years between them had never existed.

---

Several familiar faces approached from across the courtyard.

Lyra blinked.

Wait...

Is that—

Darius smiled.

So the stories were true.

Kael looked between the two of them.

I've never seen Aidan smile like that.

Malik folded his arms with a grin.

So...

You're the famous Elias.

The one we've heard about every other week.

Elias looked toward Aidan.

You talked about me?

Malik burst out laughing.

Talked?

Brother...

He compared all of us to you.

Every single time.

Aidan immediately protested.

I did not.

Malik pointed at him.

You absolutely did.

Elias would've climbed that tree.

Elias would've solved that puzzle.

Elias always beat me at chess.

Lyra covered her mouth trying not to laugh.

Darius nodded.

I honestly started wondering if you were real.

Elias looked back at Aidan with a raised eyebrow.

You really missed me that much?

Aidan answered without hesitation.

Yeah.

I did.

The smile slowly faded from Elias's face.

For a moment...

Neither spoke.

Sometimes the simplest truth carried the greatest weight.

---

Rowan stepped forward.

I suppose introductions are finally in order.

He looked toward the gathered group.

Everyone...

This is Elias Ashbourne.

One of the youngest Keepers ever entrusted with walking the Pilgrim Roads.

He left these mountains three years ago carrying messages between the hidden Foundations.

His journey ended only yesterday.

Elias rubbed the back of his neck.

Actually...

It technically ended this morning.

I got lost twice.

Malik laughed so hard he nearly doubled over.

Finally.

Someone worse with directions than Aidan.

Elias pointed at Malik.

I already like you.

---

As the laughter settled...

Another traveler emerged from the forest path.

Unlike Elias...

He walked without hurry.

Without expression.

His clothing was immaculate despite the long road behind him.

A silver pendant rested against his chest.

His dark eyes swept across the valley before settling briefly upon Aidan.

Calculating.

Observant.

Almost unreadable.

Rowan's smile disappeared.

You weren't expected until tomorrow.

The newcomer bowed respectfully.

Plans changed.

His voice was calm.

Controlled.

My name...

Is Cassian.

His eyes met Aidan's.

Neither smiled.

Neither frowned.

It lasted only a heartbeat.

Then Cassian offered his hand.

I've heard your name many times during the journey.

It's good to finally meet you.

Aidan accepted the handshake.

Likewise.

Neither of them realized...

The other...

The judgment he could never escape.

Hidden among the cliffs...

Good.
