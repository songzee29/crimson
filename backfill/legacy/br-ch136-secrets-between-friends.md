Back in New Orleans...

Lyra waited on the palace balcony.

The moment she saw Aidan...

She smiled.

Then noticed the blood.

The smile vanished.

"You fought him."

Aidan nodded.

"He became a vampire."

Lyra's heart skipped.

"So..."

"...it's begun."

Aidan looked at her.

"What?"

She forced a smile.

"Nothing."

He studied her for a moment.

She looked away too quickly.

Aidan noticed.

But said nothing.

---

That night...

Lyra couldn't sleep.

She stepped into an abandoned chamber beneath the palace.

Ancient runes covered the walls.

In the center...

A faded wolf crest glowed faintly.

She knelt before it.

"...Please..."

"...don't make me regret it."

The light pulsed once.

Then faded.
