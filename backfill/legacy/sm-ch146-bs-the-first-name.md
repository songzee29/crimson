## Chapter One Hundred Forty-Six — The First Name

The thunder faded into the distance.

Rain continued falling through the shattered roof of Ashfall Abbey, washing centuries of dust from the ancient stone.

Neither Aidan nor the Hunter spoke.

The silence had changed.

It no longer belonged to strangers.

It belonged to two people who now understood that every answer carried another question.

The Hunter walked toward the faded mural.

His gloved fingers brushed across the weathered stone.

Do you know why every face has been erased?

Aidan stepped beside him.

To hide their identity?

The Hunter slowly shook his head.

No.

To protect their purpose.

He traced the outline where the King's face should have been.

People worship faces.

They forget ideals.

The Founder feared that more than death.

Aidan stared at the empty space.

So...

He erased himself?

The Hunter's eyes softened.

No.

He ordered others to.

There's a difference.

A ruler seeking immortality preserves his image.

A true Founder preserves only his vision.

Lightning flashed again.

This time, another line beneath the mural became visible.

**Remember the House.**

**Not the man who built it.**

Before Aidan could read further...

Darkness swallowed the words once more.

## Chapter One Hundred Forty-Seven — The Library Beneath the Abbey

The Hunter placed both hands upon the ancient altar.

Stone groaned.

Hidden gears awakened after centuries of silence.

The altar slowly slid aside.

A spiral staircase descended into darkness.

Cold air rose from below.

Not stale.

Ancient.

The Hunter picked up a weathered lantern resting beside the stairs.

Its crimson flame ignited without flint or oil.

He handed it to Aidan.

Carry the light.

Aidan accepted it.

The Hunter smiled faintly.

Good.

You're learning.

They descended together.

The staircase seemed impossibly deep.

Every wall had been carved with thousands of names.

Some complete.

Others partially erased.

None carried titles.

None claimed greatness.

Only names.

Lives remembered.

At the bottom waited an immense circular library.

Unlike Blackwood Library...

These shelves were broken.

Books lay scattered across the floor.

Several had turned completely to dust.

Others bore deep claw marks.

Someone had searched this place.

Violently.

Not looking for knowledge...

Looking for something specific.

The Hunter quietly surveyed the destruction.

We were too late.

We?

The Hunter nodded.

The Keepers.

Aidan looked around.

Keepers of what?

History.

## Chapter One Hundred Forty-Eight — The Crimson Chronicle

At the center of the ruined library rested a stone pedestal.

Upon it sat one surviving book.

Untouched.

Its crimson leather remained flawless despite the devastation surrounding it.

The Hunter didn't approach.

Neither did Aidan.

Instead...

The book slowly opened by itself.

Elegant handwriting appeared across the first page.

Not as ink.

As light.

**The Crimson Chronicle**

**Volume One**

**House Darkheart**

Not quickly enough to read every word.

Just enough to reveal fragments.

The First House...

Built not for dominion...

...

Every gift carries judgment...

...

Protect the old races...

Never replace them...

When the Crimson Age arrives...

Another page turned.

The writing stopped.

Every remaining page remained blank.

The Hunter quietly lowered his head.

It recognizes you.

Aidan frowned.

Why stop there?

Because...

The Chronicle only reveals history...

When the future is ready to carry it.

Its cover became blank once again.

## Chapter One Hundred Fifty — The Keeper's Oath

The altar sealed itself just as the two emerged back into the abbey.

The violent shaking stopped instantly.

Rain continued falling peacefully outside.

Nothing suggested the chaos beneath their feet.

The Hunter remained motionless.

His breathing was calm.

His eyes were not.

What was that?

For the first time since they had met...

The Hunter hesitated.

Then he answered.

I don't know its name.

Only its purpose.

He looked toward the faceless mural.

It hunts history.

Not people.

History.

Aidan felt a chill.

How do you fight something like that?

The Hunter met his gaze.

You don't.

You preserve what it wants erased.

Silence settled over Ashfall Abbey once more.

The Hunter slowly removed a small object from inside his coat.

It was an old iron key.

Worn smooth with age.

Its bow had been forged into the shape of a crimson tree.

He placed it into Aidan's hand.

This belonged to the last Keeper before me.

Now...

It belongs to the next one.

Aidan looked down at the key.

I'm not a Keeper.

The Hunter smiled sadly.

No.

You're something history has been waiting much longer for.

He turned toward the ruined entrance of the abbey.

From this day forward...

You are no longer searching for the Crimson Legacy.

Others have begun searching for you.

Outside, lightning illuminated the forest.

Far beyond the trees...

Several distant figures stood silently watching the abbey before disappearing into the storm.

The world had changed.

Not because Aidan had grown stronger.

Because hidden powers had finally begun moving.

The age of quiet observation was over.

History had taken its first irreversible step toward awakening.
