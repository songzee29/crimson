The rain hadn't stopped.

Lightning split the Louisiana sky as the warehouse lights flickered.

Inside...

Everyone slept.

Everyone except Aidan.

Sleep had become difficult.

Every mission replayed inside his head.

Every civilian.

Every curse.

Every mistake.

His right leg bounced beneath the old wooden table.

Faster.

Faster.

He rubbed his eyes.

"...I need air."

The warehouse door quietly closed behind him.

He walked into the rain.

---

Miles away...

Inside the Veil Citadel...

An ancient bell rang.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

Every hunter stopped what they were doing.

A woman dressed in silver armor stepped into the center of the great hall.

"The Council has spoken."

Rows of hunters knelt.

"The Crimson Heir is no longer under observation."

She slowly unsheathed a silver sword.

"Operation..."

She drove the blade into the stone floor.

"...Crimson Eclipse..."

"...has begun."

The chamber erupted into motion.

Hundreds of hunters.

Messengers.

Scouts.

Every branch of the Veil came alive.

For the first time...

The Veil wasn't watching Aidan.

It was coming for him.
