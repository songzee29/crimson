As Aidan and Lyra turned to leave, a voice echoed through the sanctuary.

"You've grown."

They spun around.

Standing between two massive pillars was the hooded figure from before.

This time, they didn't disappear.

The figure slowly lowered the hood.

An older man.

Silver hair.

Sharp crimson eyes.

Scars crossed his face, but none looked fresh.

He carried no weapon.

Yet the air around him felt impossibly heavy.

Lyra instinctively stepped in front of Aidan.

"Who are you?"

The old man smiled faintly.

"I have had many names."

His eyes settled on Aidan.

"But the last one your father gave me..."

He stopped himself.

A shadow crossed his face.

"...isn't important yet."

Aidan's breath caught.

"You knew him."

The old man looked toward the empty throne hidden in the darkness.

"I served him."

Not as a soldier.

Not as a follower.

"As a friend."

Silence filled the sanctuary.

Finally, the old man looked back at Aidan.

"I've waited a very long time to see if anyone would open that crimson door."

"You were waiting for me?"

The old man shook his head.

"No."

"I was waiting to see if the Sanctuary would recognize you."

Aidan frowned.

"What's the difference?"

The old man's expression hardened.

"I could have been fooled."

"The Sanctuary cannot."

He took one slow step forward.

"The moment it called you **Heir**..."

His voice grew quieter.

"...history changed."

The System suddenly appeared.

---

**UNKNOWN INDIVIDUAL DETECTED**

**Threat Assessment: Impossible**

**Legacy Recognition: Confirmed**

---

Aidan blinked.

The Guardian noticed the slight change in his eyes.

He smiled.

"So..."

"You've already met the Legacy."

Aidan said nothing.

The Guardian nodded.

"Good."

"Keep its trust."

Then his smile disappeared.

"Because there will come a day when the Crimson System asks something of you..."

"...that it never asked of Zee."

The sanctuary fell silent.

And somewhere far beneath the stone, the Crimson Legacy stirred once more—as if it, too, was wondering what answer its heir would give when that day finally arrived.
