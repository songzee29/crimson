Black Hollow.

The annual Blood Moon Festival came to an end.

Families returned home.

The fires burned low.

Cassian walked through the quiet streets.

Then...

He noticed something.

A house.

Empty.

He frowned.

The family who lived there had left days ago.

Without saying goodbye.

He continued walking.

Another home.

Abandoned.

No struggle.

No blood.

Simply...

Gone.

The next morning...

Three more families had disappeared.

No signs of violence.

No bodies.

No messages.

Only silence.

Fear spread quietly through Black Hollow.

Cassian organized search parties.

Nothing.

The forests revealed no tracks.

The rivers carried no scent.

The mountains answered with silence.

For the first time since rebuilding his life...

Cassian couldn't protect the people who depended on him.

And that failure settled deep in his chest.
