The kingdom glowed beneath the evening sky.

Then Lyra smiled.

"Race you?"

Aidan looked at her.

"...Seriously?"

"Last one to the western tower buys breakfast."

She jumped.

Midair...

Her body transformed.

Crimson energy surrounded her.

The First Crimson Runefang soared over the palace walls in her magnificent wolf form.

"That's cheating."

Inside his mind...

The wolf growled playfully.

**She's challenging you.**

**You can't refuse now.**

*Fine.*

Crimson energy erupted around him.

In a heartbeat...

The Crimson Wolf launched from the balcony.

His crimson coat blazed beneath the moonlight.

His crimson-and-gold eyes gleamed as he landed on the castle wall before springing forward again.

Below...

The people of House Darkheart looked up in amazement.

Two crimson wolves raced across rooftops beneath the stars.

Not hunting.

Not fighting.

Simply...

Enjoying the freedom they had earned.

---

Far beyond the kingdom...

A lone figure watched from the cliffs.

A crimson raven landed on their shoulder.

The figure listened.

Then slowly smiled.

"So..."

"The Founder has finally accepted all three Authorities."

The smile disappeared.

"Then our time has come."

The raven dissolved into crimson mist.

The stranger turned toward House Darkheart.

One step.

Then another.

The journey had begun.

And this visitor...

Wasn't coming to ask questions.

They were coming to test the Crimson King.
