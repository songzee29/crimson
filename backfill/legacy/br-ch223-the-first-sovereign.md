Deep beneath an abandoned plantation...

Something laughed.

The walls were covered in dried blood.

Ancient vampire symbols.

Broken crowns.

Skulls of monsters.

A throne made entirely from black bone.

Upon it sat...

A man.

Perfectly dressed.

Perfectly calm.

Perfectly ancient.

He slowly opened one crimson eye.

"...So..."

"...the child found his fangs."

He smiled.

"...Come."

The entire plantation shook.

Thousands of bats burst into the sky.

The Blood Sovereign had no intention of hiding.

He wanted the hunt.
