At the very bottom of the scroll...

One final sentence waited.

Smaller than the others.

Almost as if Zee had written it after everything else.

---

> **"One day..."**

> **"...you will hate me."**

Lyra gasped.

---

> **"When that day comes..."**

> **"...I ask only one thing."**

---

Aidan's hands trembled.

---

> **"Learn the whole truth..."**

> **"...before you judge your father."**

The scroll slowly dissolved into crimson dust.

Its purpose fulfilled.

Gone forever.

---

No one moved.

Finally...

Aidan looked toward the night sky beyond the warehouse window.

His voice was barely above a whisper.

"...I don't hate you."

Another long silence.

Then...

"...I just wish I had known you."

The Journal opened by itself.

Ink flowed slowly across the page.

---

> **A father does not always leave behind his presence.**

Another line formed.

> **Sometimes...**

> **...he leaves behind the strength for his child to walk farther than he ever could.**

The Journal closed.

Outside...

The bells of St. Louis Cathedral rang seven times.

No one had touched them.

The Guardian smiled faintly.

"The city knows."

Aidan looked at him.

"What does it know?"

The Guardian's eyes reflected the moonlight.

"That the Crimson King has finally heard his father's voice."

---

Far away, hidden in the shadows of an abandoned Veil outpost, Commander Cassian stood alone.

He removed his gloves and looked down at his hands.

He could still see Elias' blood in his memory.

He whispered into the darkness:

> "You told me to live..."

> "...I'm trying."

Unseen by him, a figure wearing a porcelain smile watched from the rooftop above.

The Smiling Man gently moved another piece across his invisible chessboard.

A black king.

Then he whispered:

> **"Good..."**

> **"...because the next move belongs to Aidan."**
