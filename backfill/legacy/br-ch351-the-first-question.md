The beast slowly lowered itself onto one knee.

The ground shook beneath its weight.

Not a bow.

Not submission.

Respect.

It looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

> **"...Answer me..."**

> **"...King..."**

Aidan answered calmly.

"...Ask."

The beast's ancient eyes narrowed.

> **"...Why... do you seek more power?"**

The question echoed through the forest.

No one moved.

Even the Crimson Assassin remained completely still.

Aidan didn't answer immediately.

He looked toward the distant horizon.

Then quietly spoke.

> **"...Because power without purpose..."**

He paused.

> **"...belongs to tyrants."**

Another pause.

> **"...Purpose without power..."**

His crimson eyes hardened.

> **"...belongs to the dead."**

The beast closed its eyes.

For several long seconds...

It said nothing.

Then...

It smiled.

A genuine smile.

> **"...House Darkheart still remembers..."**
