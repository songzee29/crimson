As the old man prepared to leave...

The little girl appeared again.

This time...

She walked directly toward him.

She smiled.

"Dad."

Malik froze.

Lyra covered her mouth.

The old man knelt.

His hard expression softened instantly.

"Hello, little moon."

She hugged him.

Then...

She disappeared into glowing crimson particles.

Not a ghost.

Not alive.

A memory.

The old man remained kneeling.

For several long seconds.

Finally...

He stood.

Without looking at anyone.

"My daughter."

His voice barely rose above a whisper.

"The Veil called her collateral damage."

Silence.

"I called her everything."

Aidan didn't know what to say.

The old man looked toward him.

"You think Zee fought because he enjoyed it?"

He laughed bitterly.

"No."

"We fought..."

"...because we kept burying children."

---

Without another word...

He walked into the darkness.

Gone.

Leaving only silence behind.
