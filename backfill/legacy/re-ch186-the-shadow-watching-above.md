Far above the Forgotten Sanctuary...

Inside the flooded remains of the cathedral...

The enormous crimson hand slowly withdrew beneath the earth.

The heartbeat faded.

The Judge still stood.

Barely.

His ancient armor was shattered.

His sword had broken in half.

He looked toward the river where Aidan had disappeared.

Then...

For the first time in centuries...

He smiled.

> **"...He chose his people."**

> **"...Just as you did, my King."**

The ancient warrior finally knelt.

His body slowly became stone.

Returning to the cathedral he had protected for generations.

His final duty...

Complete.

Miles away...

The Veil captain stood alone beneath the rain.

An elder approached quietly.

"The cathedral collapsed."

"I know."

"The Judge has fallen."

"I know."

"The heir escaped."

The captain finally turned.

"...Good."

The elder frowned.

"You were supposed to intercept him."

"I chose not to."

The elder's expression hardened.

"You disobeyed the Council."

The captain's eyes became ice cold.

"I obeyed..."

"...my judgment."

Silence.

The elder slowly walked away.

Unseen by either of them...

Someone else stood atop the cathedral ruins.

The Smiling Man.

Watching.

He placed a white bishop onto a weathered chessboard.

Then whispered into the rain:

> **"The king has survived another move."**

He looked toward the distant city lights of New Orleans.

> **"...Now let's see what his heart does next."**

And somewhere deep beneath the Forgotten Sanctuary...

A single page inside Aidan's blank journal turned by itself.

Ink slowly appeared.

Just one sentence.

---

> **"A king is not remembered for the throne he inherited... but for the people he refused to abandon."**

The page stopped writing.

The journal closed.

Aidan never saw it.

But the Crimson Legacy had.

And for the first time since choosing its heir...

It had begun recording **not just Aidan's power... but the kind of man he was becoming.**
