That evening...

Aidan walked through Jackson Square.

The little girl from before waved excitedly.

"King Aidan!"

He smiled and waved back.

People greeted him everywhere he went.

Not because they had to.

Because they wanted to.

Azrath watched from a nearby rooftop.

His smile never changed.

He wasn't jealous.

He was calculating.

"Interesting..."

He whispered.

"They don't follow the crown."

"They follow..."

"...the man."

That made Aidan far more dangerous than Azrath expected.

And far harder to destroy.
