That evening...

Lyra found Aidan standing on the western wall.

The sun dipped below the mountains.

The kingdom glowed beneath the crimson sky.

She stepped beside him.

"You've been carrying a lot lately."

"...Part of being king."

"You don't always have to carry it alone."

Aidan looked at her.

For a moment...

Neither spoke.

Then Lyra smiled.

"When all of this is finally over..."

"I want to see what this kingdom looks like fifty years from now."

Aidan raised an eyebrow.

"Fifty?"

She laughed.

"A hundred."

"I want children to grow up hearing stories about the people who built House Darkheart..."

"...without ever having to see another war."

Aidan looked across the kingdom.

"...I'd like that."

Neither of them noticed...

High above the castle...

A lone raven watched.

Then silently disappeared into the night.
