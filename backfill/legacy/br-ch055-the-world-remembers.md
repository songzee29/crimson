Across the globe...

Every ancient being stopped what they were doing.

An elder vampire dropped his glass.

A witch extinguished her own ritual.

The Guardian collapsed to one knee inside the Sanctuary.

The Crimson Legacy...

Glitched.

Not like before.

This time...

The entire interface turned black.

One line slowly carved itself across the screen.

No crimson.

No light.

Only letters that looked as though they had been etched into obsidian.

> **PRISON STATUS: EMPTY**

Silence.

Then another line appeared.

> **WARNING**

A long pause.

> **THE FIRST FORGOTTEN WALKS AGAIN**

The message disappeared.

Not because the System closed it.

Because...

It no longer knew what to do.
