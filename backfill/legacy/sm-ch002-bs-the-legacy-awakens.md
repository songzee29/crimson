## Chapter Two — The Legacy Awakens

Darkness stretched endlessly around Aidan.

There was no floor beneath his feet.

No sky above his head.

Only an infinite sea of crimson light drifting through the void like stars scattered across an endless night.

Symbols floated around him in slow circles. Ancient runes burned with a quiet brilliance, each one carrying a language his mind had never learned yet somehow understood.

As he reached toward one of them, it dissolved into countless memories.

Cities built from black stone.

Kings kneeling before crimson thrones.

Armies marching beneath banners stained with blood and gold.

Then came war.

The sky itself split apart as witches unleashed storms of living fire. Wolves the size of castles crashed into legions of armored giants. Rivers turned crimson beneath the feet of immortal warriors whose names had been erased from history.

The visions came faster.

A man stood alone before an army.

His face remained hidden within the light, but everything about him radiated impossible authority.

He lifted one hand.

The battlefield fell silent.

Not from fear.

From obedience.

The world itself recognized him.

Aidan's chest tightened.

For reasons he could not explain, the stranger felt familiar.

Not like someone he had met.

Like someone his soul had always known.

Before he could step closer, the vision shattered.

The crimson symbols exploded into a storm around him.

One by one they began flowing toward his body, disappearing beneath his skin.

Knowledge.

Languages.

Histories.

Magic.

Battles.

Laws.

Thousands of years of forgotten civilization flooded into his mind.

The pain was unbearable.

He fell to one knee, clutching his head as if it might split apart.

The crimson light surrounding him condensed into a single point before his eyes.

Then a voice echoed through the endless darkness.

Not loud.

Not commanding.

Ancient.

Welcome, Heir.

The words vibrated through his entire being.

Aidan spun around.

No one stood behind him.

Who's there?

Silence answered.

Then the crimson light expanded into a vast palace suspended within the void.

Massive pillars reached beyond sight.

Ancient banners hung unmoving despite the absence of wind.

Countless empty thrones surrounded one enormous seat carved from crimson crystal.

It remained vacant.

Waiting.

Aidan slowly climbed the black steps leading toward it.

Each footstep echoed across the endless palace.

The closer he came, the stronger the strange pressure inside his chest became.

When he reached the final step, another vision erupted before him.

A pair of powerful hands gently placed the crimson book upon the throne.

The same unseen man spoke, though Aidan still could not see his face.

Power without character destroys everything it touches.

If you have come seeking strength...

Leave.

If you have come seeking dominion...

Turn back.

But if you seek the burden of protecting what others cannot...

Then inherit my Legacy.

The throne burst into crimson light.

Aidan instinctively shielded his eyes.

When the brilliance faded, floating windows appeared before him.

Unlike ordinary screens, they seemed alive, their edges formed from flowing crimson energy.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON LEGACY

Founder Verification Complete

Bloodline Confirmed

Heir Accepted

Initializing Legacy Interface...

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The words lingered only a moment before disappearing.

More windows replaced them.

Not numbers.

Not levels.

Questions.

What kind of King will you become?

How much of yourself are you willing to sacrifice?

When power demands your humanity...

Which will you choose?

Aidan stared at the questions.

There were no correct answers.

Only silence.

The palace itself seemed to be waiting.

Finally he spoke.

I don't want to rule anyone.

The moment the words left his mouth, the palace trembled.

Another notification appeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Response Recorded.

Character Evaluation Has Begun.

The Legacy Does Not Seek Perfection.

It Seeks Truth.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Without warning, every pillar inside the palace began illuminating one after another.

Hidden doors appeared throughout the massive hall.

Some radiated warmth.

Others felt impossibly cold.

One door seemed wrapped in chains forged from crimson light.

Another leaked shadows that moved like living creatures.

Every path promised answers.

Every path demanded a price.

Before Aidan could move, the entire palace shook violently.

A deafening roar echoed somewhere beyond the great doors.

Not human.

Not animal.

Something ancient.

Something imprisoned.

The chained door pulsed once.

Then again.

Tiny fractures spread across the glowing chains.

A single crimson eye opened within the darkness beyond the seal.

It stared directly at Aidan.

The entire palace fell silent.

Then the eye slowly smiled.

Far away in the abandoned room of the Darkheart estate, Aidan's physical body suddenly opened its own eyes.

Both irises had turned a deep crimson.

For the briefest moment, every candle inside the mansion extinguished at once.

And somewhere deep beneath the estate, where no living soul had walked for centuries, something answered the Legacy's awakening with a single heartbeat.
