By the end of the week...

The news had stopped calling the incidents accidents.

Across New Orleans, people whispered about a mysterious group that always arrived before the police.

A warehouse fire.

A cursed church.

The abandoned amusement park.

The Bayou.

No one knew who they were.

Only that...

People survived.

An elderly woman was interviewed outside a hospital.

"I never saw his face..."

She smiled through tears.

"...but the young man with the crimson eyes carried my granddaughter out of that fire."

Another man laughed.

"He disappeared before anyone could thank him."

Children started drawing pictures.

A figure wearing a crimson coat.

Standing between monsters...

And people.

Aidan never saw any of it.

He was too busy training.
