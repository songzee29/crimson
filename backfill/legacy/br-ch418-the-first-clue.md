The eighth day.

Black Hollow had become silent.

No children played outside.

No music echoed through the village.

Doors remained shut.

Every knock made hearts race.

Every sunrise was feared.

Cassian stood before the village council.

"We've searched everything."

One elder slammed his fist onto the table.

"Then search again!"

Another spoke.

"They trusted you."

"You promised us this place would never become another battlefield."

Cassian didn't defend himself.

Because...

They were right.

---

A scout burst through the doors.

"My Lord!"

Everyone stood.

"We found something."
