His eyes flew open.

Every sound became unbearable.

He could hear rats beneath the cathedral.

Rain striking rooftops miles away.

Thousands of heartbeats across New Orleans.

Every heartbeat...

Calling him.

His throat burned.

Not like fire.

Like starvation.

He collapsed to one knee.

"...What..."

His fingernails dug into the stone floor.

"...did..."

He gasped.

"...you..."

His veins darkened beneath his skin.

The hunger grew.

Every heartbeat outside became louder.

Faster.

Sweeter.

He covered his ears.

It didn't help.

The sound wasn't outside.

It was inside him.

---

Footsteps echoed through the cathedral.

Azrath emerged from the darkness.

Hands behind his back.

Watching.

Not helping.

Not smiling.

Simply observing.

"You survived."

Cassian glared at him.

"What did you do to me?!"

Azrath answered calmly.

"I opened the first door."
