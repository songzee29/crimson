Asterion turned toward the northern mountains.

Hidden behind clouds...

An enormous stone gateway stretched into the sky.

It was so massive...

A mountain had been carved into its frame.

Four empty circles decorated its surface.

The same symbol Aidan had seen before.

The beast spoke quietly.

> **"...Beyond that gate..."**

> **"...the Monarchs wait."**

A pause.

> **"...They won't care what crowns you wear."**

> **"...Only whether your Authority..."**

> **"...deserves to exist."**

Stone grinding against stone echoed across the entire forest.

Not carrying death.

Carrying challenge.

Three steps behind him...

The Crimson Assassin followed.

Not because he believed Aidan would win.

For the first time in countless centuries...

Someone worthy had finally walked the Road of Monarchs.

And somewhere beyond that gate...

Unseen by Aidan...

A figure seated upon an obsidian throne slowly opened its eyes.

A calm voice echoed through the darkness.

> **"...The Crimson King has arrived."**

The throne room fell silent.

Then another voice answered.

> **"...Let him enter."**

The first trial of the Monarchs...

Had begun.
