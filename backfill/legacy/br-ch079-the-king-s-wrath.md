No one spoke.

The Ash King had vanished.

Only drifting ash remained.

The room still carried the scent of blood.

Lyra leaned against Aidan.

Her breathing was steady now.

The True Crimson Blood had saved her.

But the memory of her death...

Remained.

---

Cassian slowly looked up.

His eyes met Aidan's.

For several long moments...

Neither king moved.

Finally...

Cassian stood.

His legs trembled beneath him.

His sword still lay where it had fallen.

He didn't reach for it.

He couldn't.

"I..."

His voice broke.

"...There is nothing I can say."

Aidan said nothing.

Cassian looked toward Lyra.

"I took your life..."

"...and he gave it back."

Tears streamed down his face.

"I have become the very thing I hated."

Silence.

No one defended him.

No one condemned him.

His own conscience had already become his prison.
