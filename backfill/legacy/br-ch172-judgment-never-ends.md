Several months passed.

House Darkheart continued to grow.

The Academy expanded.

The First Generation grew stronger.

The Crimson Civilization flourished.

To everyone else...

It looked like peace.

To Aidan...

It felt like a countdown.

---

One evening...

The throne room stood empty.

Aidan sat alone upon the Crimson Throne.

His fingers rested against the armrest.

His crimson eyes stared into the roaring fireplace.

Inside his mind...

Rez's voice broke the silence.

**You're thinking about Cassian again.**

*I never stopped.*

---

Silence.

---

Aidan slowly stood.

He walked toward the enormous balcony overlooking House Darkheart.

Families laughed below.

Children chased one another through the streets.

For a brief moment...

He allowed himself to smile.

Then...

The smile disappeared.

---

**Ding.**

### **Darkheart Curse**

**Judgment Progress**

**Phase Two**

**Complete**

---

The notification lingered.

Then another appeared.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

### **Darkheart Curse**

**Preparing Constitutional Transition...**

---

Rez frowned.

**...That wasn't there before.**

*I know.*

---

The entire Legacy froze.

Then...

One final notification appeared.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

### **Darkheart Curse**

**Judgment Phase Three**

**Active**

---

The air inside the throne room became cold.

Aidan closed his eyes.

He already knew what Phase Three meant.

He had written the decree himself.

He had lived with it for centuries.

Cassian would come.

Not for revenge.

For vengeance.

---

Far away...

Beyond the borders of House Darkheart...

An ancient manor stood hidden beneath endless snowfall.

Inside...

A man quietly placed flowers on a grave.

He wore black.

His face carried centuries of exhaustion.

Peace.

For the first time in generations...

He had finally found peace.

---

A single crimson rose rested upon the grave.

The inscription read:

**Elena**

**Beloved Wife**

---

The man knelt.

"I finally kept my promise."

"I rebuilt everything."

"I stayed away from war."

"I stayed away from him."

He closed his eyes.

"I hope..."

"...wherever you are..."

"...you've found peace too."

---

A cold wind swept through the cemetery.

The candles surrounding the grave extinguished.

One by one.

The man slowly stood.

His expression changed.

He recognized that feeling.

No...

Not the feeling.

The presence.

---

He turned.

Standing at the entrance to the cemetery...

A lone figure watched silently.

A long crimson coat moved with the wind.

Crimson eyes.

Calm.

Unshaken.

Aidan Darkheart.

Neither man spoke.

Neither needed to.

Cassian looked toward Elena's grave.

Then back at Aidan.

A hollow laugh escaped him.

"So..."

"...the curse found me again."

Aidan's voice was quiet.

"You found peace."

Cassian nodded.

"I did."

"And you knew what would happen."

"I did."

Silence.

Snow continued falling between them.

Cassian looked at the flowers on the grave one last time.

Then back at Aidan.

"I won't beg."

"I didn't come here to make you beg."

"I won't run."

"I know."

Cassian's eyes slowly filled with centuries of pain.

"So why are you here?"

Aidan's expression never changed.

"Because the decree continues."

The wind howled across the cemetery.

Cassian slowly drew his sword.

Not because he believed he could win.

Because he refused to kneel.

Aidan didn't move.

His hands remained behind his back.

His crimson eyes never left Cassian's.

Inside his mind...

Rez spoke quietly.

**This is where everything changes.**

Aidan answered without looking away.

*No...*

*This is where the Third Judgment begins.*

The snow fell harder.

The cemetery disappeared beneath white.

Two immortal enemies stood facing one another once more.

One bound by revenge.

The other...

Bound by his own decree.

The Darkheart Curse had entered its third phase.

And this time...

It would demand a price neither of them wanted to pay.
