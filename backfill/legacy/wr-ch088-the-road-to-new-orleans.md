Rain fell before dawn.

Warm.

Heavy.

It washed blood from the roads.

It did nothing to erase its scent.

Aidan stood atop the rusted frame of an abandoned water tower overlooking miles of wilderness.

His crimson eyes tracked movement impossible for any human to perceive.

A fox.

A passing freight train.

A fishing boat miles away.

Then—

A line of vehicles traveling the southern highway.

He listened.

Twenty-three heartbeats.

One driver had a fresh wound across his forearm.

Another passenger coughed violently.

A pregnant woman’s heartbeat echoed beside the smaller rhythm of an unborn child.

The sounds layered together until his head throbbed.

The hunger wanted all of them.

His body leaned forward.

His feet cracked the steel beneath him.

He vanished.

Only to stop a hundred yards away, standing atop another ruined structure.

His own speed startled him.

He had crossed the distance before consciously deciding to move.

The world felt slower again.

Every movement around him unfolded with unnatural clarity.

Far to the south…

Something else called to him.

Not blood.

Not prey.

A pull.

Ancient.

Silent.

His crimson gaze lifted toward the distant horizon.

New Orleans.

He had never seen the city.

Yet something beneath it recognized him.

The sensation did not soothe the hunger.

It merely gave it direction.

Aidan looked south one final time.

The fire inside his throat spread through every vein.

His blood reserves continued falling.

His regeneration slowed.

He did not need to.

The hunger drove him onward.

The Ancient Gates awaited.

And with every impossible stride through the darkness…

The Crimson Vampire drew closer to the city where the next chapter of his inheritance would begin.
