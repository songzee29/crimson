That night...

Aidan entered the Crimson Archive alone.

He expected the Legacy to speak.

It didn't.

No quest.

No reward.

No warning.

Only silence.

He closed his eyes.

"Why?"

The pages finally appeared.

A single sentence slowly wrote itself.

---

> **Some choices belong to kings.**

> **Not to Legacies.**

---

The book closed.

For the first time since inheriting the Crimson Legacy...

Aidan realized something.

The Legacy could guide him.

It could teach him.

It could record history.

But it would never make the decisions that defined him.

Those...

Were his alone.

Outside the Archive...

The crimson moon slipped behind dark clouds.

Inside his soul...

The Wolf lifted its head.

Its ears twitched.

Then...

It whispered.

> **"...The hunt has changed..."**

Far away...

Cassian looked toward House Darkheart from the edge of the forest.

For the first time in years...

He wasn't thinking about revenge.

He was thinking about responsibility.

And that made him even more dangerous.

Because the path toward the **Third Judgment** would not be forced upon him.

It would be chosen.

One decision...

At a time.
