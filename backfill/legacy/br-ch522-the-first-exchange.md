No one moved.

The Hall of Names had become perfectly silent.

Two kings stood only twenty feet apart.

One...

Carried the weight of the past.

The other...

Carried the hope of the future.

Ash drifted between them.

Neither blinked.

---

The Ash King finally spoke.

> **"Show me..."**

> **"...what kind of king you truly are."**

Aidan slowly removed his crimson coat.

He folded it carefully.

Then handed it to Lyra.

She understood.

This wasn't arrogance.

This was respect.

A king should meet another king...

Without hiding behind ceremony.

Lyra held the coat tightly.

"Come back to me."

Aidan smiled softly.

"I always do."
