The crimson bridge ended before an enormous circular chamber.

No throne.

No guardian.

No monster.

Only...

A mirror.

It stretched nearly fifty feet into the air.

Its surface wasn't glass.

It was liquid crimson.

The Circle stopped walking.

No one spoke.

The System remained completely silent.

Not one notification appeared.

For the first time...

Even the Crimson Legacy waited.

---

The Librarian quietly spoke behind them.

"This Seal..."

"...doesn't test power."

"It judges the future."

Aidan stepped forward.

The crimson mirror rippled.

Then...

It spoke.

Not with a voice.

Inside his mind.

---

> **"Darkheart..."**

> **"...what are you unwilling to become?"**

---

The question echoed through his soul.

Images exploded across the mirror.

Cities burning.

The Crimson Throne.

Countless bodies.

A man wearing the Crimson Crown.

His eyes completely empty.

Blood dripped from his fingertips.

The man slowly looked up.

It was Aidan.

Older.

Colder.

Alone.

The mirror asked again.

---

> **"Would you become this..."**

> **"...to save everyone you love?"**

---

The entire chamber waited.

Lyra couldn't hear the question.

Neither could Malik.

Neither could Elias.

Only Aidan.

He stared at the monster wearing his own face.

Then quietly answered.

"...Never."

The mirror didn't argue.

It simply rippled.

Then another sentence appeared.

---

> **"Every king says that..."**

---

Silence.

Then...

The mirror bowed.

Not to Aidan.

To his answer.

The crimson surface slowly dissolved.

Behind it...

The Second Seal floated peacefully.

No battle.

No explosion.

Only acceptance.

Aidan reached out.

The moment his hand touched the Seal—

The Witch Authority trembled.

Not violently.

Like it had just learned something.

---

The System finally appeared.

---

**《SECOND CRIMSON SEAL》**

**Accepted.**

---

No new spell.

No new power.

Only one line.

---

> **Law Recorded**

### **"The greatest curse begins with believing you are the only one who can save everyone."**

---

Aidan read it twice.

He didn't fully understand it.

Elias did.

He quietly looked away.
