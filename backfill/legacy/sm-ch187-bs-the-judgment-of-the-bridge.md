## Chapter One Hundred Eighty-Seven — The Judgment of the Bridge

Darkness swallowed the abandoned village.

No one reached for a weapon immediately.

Rowan had taught them well.

Fear invited mistakes.

Judgment prevented them.

The crimson glow around the old well slowly returned, illuminating weathered stones that had not seen daylight in centuries.

The voice did not speak again.

Instead...

Footsteps echoed from somewhere beneath the earth.

Slow.

Measured.

Neither hurried nor threatening.

Aidan instinctively stepped toward the well.

Elias caught his shoulder.

Not because he doubted Aidan.

Because brothers protected each other without asking permission.

Aidan glanced sideways.

You still do that.

Elias smiled.

You still walk toward danger before thinking.

Malik laughed despite the tension.

Good.

I was worried I was the only one telling him that.

The moment eased everyone's nerves.

Only Cassian remained silent.

His eyes never left the well.

Something about the voice had unsettled him in a way he could not explain.

The stones surrounding the well shifted.

A hidden stairway slowly appeared beneath the ancient masonry.

Rowan lowered his head respectfully.

The village has accepted us.

No one celebrated.

Every Keeper knew...

Only the beginning.

## Chapter One Hundred Eighty-Eight — Beneath the Village

The staircase descended into a circular chamber supported by enormous cedar pillars blackened with age.

Hundreds of shelves lined the walls.

Most were empty.

Some still held journals carefully wrapped in faded crimson cloth.

Not treasures.

Memories.

Aidan gently removed one.

Its cover simply read:

**Pilgrim Journal 203**

Inside were ordinary entries.

Weather.

Road conditions.

A bridge repaired after spring floods.

A child born during winter.

A Keeper teaching three young witches how to safely record village history.

No legendary battles.

No impossible heroes.

Just lives.

Elias looked over Aidan's shoulder.

History isn't only kings...

It's everyone.

Rowan nodded.

That is why civilizations survive.

Someone always remembers the ordinary.

At the center of the chamber stood a stone pedestal.

Upon it rested a single iron bell.

Cracked.

Silent.

Its inscription had survived the centuries.

**Ring only when history is in danger of being forgotten.**

Cassian read the words twice.

His expression tightened.

He didn't tell the others why.

## Chapter One Hundred Ninety-One — The Man Who Smiled

The forest slowly awakened around them.

Birdsong returned.

The wind moved through the crimson trees once more.

Hours later they reached a ridge overlooking the next stretch of the Pilgrim Road.

Standing alone at the overlook...

Waiting as though he had known exactly when they would arrive...

Was the man dressed in white.

His silver hair moved gently in the mountain breeze.

His crimson eyes rested on each traveler before finally meeting Aidan's.

Unlike before...

He did not disappear.

He smiled.

You've done well.

Better than most.

His voice was calm.

Almost kind.

Rowan's expression hardened immediately.

Everyone stay behind me.

The stranger inclined his head respectfully.

There's no need for fear, Keeper.

Not today.

His gaze shifted toward Elias.

Then to Cassian.

Finally...

Back to Aidan.

You have gathered people willing to walk beside you.

Protect them well.

The road ahead will ask a price.

The greatest curses...

Never begin with hatred.

They begin...

With love.

Silence lingered long after he was gone.

No one knew his name.

But every person in the company felt the weight of his warning.

Far beyond the Pilgrim Road...

**Blackwood Academy.**
