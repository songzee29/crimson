The crimson thread floated silently through the empty streets of New Orleans.

Aidan followed.

The city slept around him.

Jazz drifted through distant alleyways.

The Mississippi rolled quietly beside ancient brick buildings.

Every few steps...

The ring grew warmer.

As if it recognized places before Aidan did.

The thread finally stopped.

Not in front of a church.

Not beneath a cemetery.

But...

In the middle of Jackson Square.

Aidan frowned.

"...Here?"

Nothing.

Just old stone.

Tourists' footsteps from earlier that day.

Street performers' forgotten chalk drawings.

The thread disappeared into the ground.

---

Aidan slowly knelt.

His right hand formed the familiar sign of **Authority**.

His fingers spread against the stone.

One quiet word escaped his lips.

> **"Reveal."**

The Sixth Crimson Law answered.

Ancient crimson runes ignited beneath the entire square.

The earth trembled.

Hundreds of years of illusion peeled away like dust.

Hidden beneath Jackson Square...

An enormous circular seal slowly emerged.

The Guardian had walked across this place hundreds of times.

He had never known it was there.

No one had.

Because it had never been waiting for them.

It had been waiting...

For Aidan.
