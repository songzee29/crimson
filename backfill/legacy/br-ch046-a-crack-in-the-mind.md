That night...

Cassian stood before a mirror.

He hadn't slept.

Dark circles formed beneath his eyes.

He stared at his own reflection.

"...Who are you?"

The reflection smiled.

Cassian hadn't.

He stumbled backward.

The smile remained.

Then...

The reflection spoke.

Not with lips.

With thought.

> **"Say my name."**

Cassian slammed the mirror with his fist.

Glass exploded across the room.

His breathing became ragged.

"No!"

Silence.

The reflection returned to normal.

But one word remained carved into the broken glass.

**AZRATH**
