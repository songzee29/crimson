User message: `8211805e-38d4-4176-bf30-deb2b58c5c2e`

Author intent:

**Continuity implication:** A prose draft may show Aidan reacting to an internal prompt, but should not make another character read the prompt or see Rez standing beside him.

## C. Canon/alternate handling rules for the archive

1. **Current House name:** House Darkheart.
2. **Historical alternate:** House Drakhar remains attached to *The Forgotten Blood* / earlier alternate continuity; do not silently rename it into current canon or erase it from the archive.
3. **Opening book:** crimson, physical touch and blood first, then activation.
4. **Curse order:** Witch/System first; Vampire later through death; Wolf later through the author's intended devastating trigger.
5. **Rez:** dormant in the book at the beginning; no visible Rez; inner-voice communication; Vampire Curse is the awakening gate.
6. **System:** Aidan alone sees it. Lyra may notice Aidan looking at something, but must not see the System itself. No one Aidan turns receives a System.
7. **Power:** Aidan inherits access to Zee's broader evolution path, not just three basic species. Evolutions must have triggers/costs and should not be selected like a menu item.
8. **Archive method:** preserve all distinct source prose; label exact, alternate, superseded, truncated, or unverified. Do not invent missing passages or silently merge versions.
