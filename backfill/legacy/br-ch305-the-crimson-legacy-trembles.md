That night...

The Journal opened on its own.

Not because of Aidan.

Because of the blood.

Ink spread across the page faster than ever before.

---

> **The Witch protects.**

A second line.

> **The Wolf endures.**

A third.

The ink hesitated.

Almost refusing to continue.

Finally...

It wrote:

> **The Vampire remembers.**

The page shook violently.

The System immediately tried to erase the sentence.

It couldn't.

The words remained.

Permanent.

Deep within the Crimson Legacy, beyond the Witch Authority, beyond the Soul Anchor, beyond the sleeping vessel...

The cracked Vampire Seal pulsed again.

This time...

It did not stop immediately.

One ancient chain snapped.

Not enough to free what lay behind it.

Just enough for a single drop of crimson light to seep through the crack.

It drifted into the darkness...

Like the first drop of blood falling before a storm.

And in that endless darkness...

For the first time in centuries...

Something smiled back.
