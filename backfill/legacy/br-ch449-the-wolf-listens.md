Inside Aidan's mind...

The cracked Sacred Seal shimmered.

Beyond it...

The enormous wolf watched everything.

It had heard every word.

It had felt every emotion.

Its massive ears slowly lowered.

Not in anger.

Understanding.

It looked at Lyra through Aidan's eyes.

The wolf remembered.

It remembered the night of the seal.

It remembered the fear in her heart.

It remembered the trembling in her hands.

She had never sealed it out of hatred.

She had sealed it...

Out of love.

The great wolf slowly walked toward the cracked barrier.

It placed one massive paw against it.

Then...

To Aidan's surprise...

It bowed its head.

> **"...She protected my king."**

A pause.

Its deep voice became almost gentle.

> **"...I judged her wrongly."**
