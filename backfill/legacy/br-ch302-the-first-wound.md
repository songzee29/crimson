Valerius attacked.

Not to test.

To win.

His fist carried the weight of thirteen centuries.

It struck directly toward Aidan's chest.

Aidan didn't move.

The Dominion moved.

A Blood Chain erupted from beneath the arena.

A Witch Sigil strengthened it.

The chain wrapped around Valerius' arm.

For half a heartbeat...

The First Blood Sovereign stopped moving.

That half-second...

Was enough.

Aidan stepped forward.

**Scarlet Rift.**

The crimson blade crossed Valerius' shoulder.

Silence.

The battlefield froze.

A single line of blood appeared across Valerius' chest.

The wound...

Did not close.

Valerius slowly looked down.

Then...

Touched the cut.

Blood stained his fingertips.

He stared at it.

The Blood Sovereigns stared with him.

One whispered...

"...He..."

Another finished the sentence.

"...made Valerius bleed."

---

For the first time in centuries...

The First Blood Sovereign had been wounded in a Royal Duel.
