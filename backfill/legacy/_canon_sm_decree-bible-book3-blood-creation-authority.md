# 🔒 DECREE XLI — THE THREE AUTHORITY REQUIREMENT

The Crimson Heir cannot create, transform, or sire any supernatural being until all Three Crimson Authorities have been fully awakened.

The Three Crimson Authorities are:

• Crimson Witch Authority

• Crimson Vampire Authority

• Crimson Wolf Authority

If even one Authority remains sealed, Aidan's blood remains **Incomplete Crimson Blood**.

Incomplete Crimson Blood possesses **no Blood Creation Authority**.

No vampire, werewolf, witch, hybrid, tribe, bloodline, or supernatural descendant can be created through the Crimson Heir until all Three Authorities have become complete.
