Lyra stepped in front of Aidan.

The battlefield watched.

She wasn't protecting herself.

She was protecting him.

The Keeper spoke calmly.

> **"...Move."**

Lyra stood her ground.

"No."

"You know what happens when the seal finally breaks."

"...Yes."

"You know..."

"...what it will cost."

Tears filled her eyes.

"...Yes."

The Keeper remained silent.

Then asked one final question.

> **"...Will you still choose him?"**

Lyra never hesitated.

> **"...Every lifetime."**

The second heartbeat thundered.

**THUMP!**

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf closed its eyes.

Then slowly lowered its head toward Lyra.

Not because it was commanded.

Because it chose to.

> **"...Then I swear..."**

> **"...When I finally run free..."**

> **"...I shall never allow harm to come to my Queen..."**

A crimson-and-gold aura surrounded Lyra for only a moment.

The Wolf had made its own oath.
