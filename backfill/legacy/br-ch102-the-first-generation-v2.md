The sun dipped below the mountains.

Training had ended hours ago.

The soldiers returned to their homes.

The castle settled into its usual evening calm.

---

Malik was halfway to his room when he heard footsteps behind him.

He turned.

Aidan.

"My King?"

Aidan looked at him.

"Come with me."

Malik raised an eyebrow.

"...That's all I get?"

Aidan nodded once.

"That's all you need."

Malik laughed.

"Fair enough."

---

Aidan continued through the halls of House Darkheart.

Before long...

Kael joined them.

"What's going on?"

"You'll see."

A few turns later...

Mara stepped out of the library.

"Aidan?"

"Come with us."

She looked between everyone.

"...Should I bring my spellbook?"

"No."

"...Now I'm curious."

---

Darius eventually caught up carrying a training sword over his shoulder.

"You people walk too fast."

Aidan never slowed down.

Lyra smiled as she quietly joined the group.

One by one...

Everyone followed.

No questions.

No hesitation.

If Aidan called...

They answered.

---

He stopped before a pair of ancient stone doors deep beneath House Darkheart.

Doors almost no one had entered.

Runes glowed faintly across their surface.

Mara's eyes widened.

"...I've never seen these before."

Aidan rested his hand against the stone.

The runes recognized his Crimson Blood.

The doors slowly opened.

A cool breeze drifted from within.

Rows of crimson crystals illuminated the chamber.

It wasn't a throne room.

It wasn't a vault.

It was quiet.

Sacred.

As though it had been waiting for this moment.

Everyone stepped inside.

The massive doors closed behind them.

Silence filled the chamber.

---

Malik finally broke it.

"...Alright."

"...Now you have my attention."

Darius nodded.

"I was starting to think you were taking us to a dungeon."

Kael smirked.

"I still think he might."

Everyone laughed.

Even Aidan.

Inside his mind...

Rez folded his arms.

**You really enjoy keeping people confused.**

*It keeps life interesting.*

**Or stressful.**

*Same thing.*

Rez sighed dramatically.

**You're impossible.**

---

Aidan looked around the room.

Then at the people standing before him.

Malik.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

The people who had stood beside him before he wore a crown.

The people who never abandoned him.

His family.

He crossed his arms.

"...Who's tired of being weak?"

The room went quiet.

Malik snorted.

"You really opened with that?"

Kael chuckled.

"I thought there'd be a speech."

"There isn't."

Darius shrugged.

"I respect that."

Mara smiled.

"So..."

"...what are you actually asking?"

Aidan looked each of them in the eyes.

"I'm asking if you want more."

Silence.

He continued.

"More strength."

"More speed."

"More life."

"More than this world says you're allowed to have."

Malik stepped forward first.

"...Is this even possible?"

Aidan smiled.

"It is now."

---

**Ding.**

Malik looked at Aidan.

"So..."

"What's the catch?"

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"It changes everything."

Kael folded his arms.

"No going back?"

"No."

Mara spoke next.

"Will we still be... us?"

Aidan didn't answer immediately.

Then...

"Yes."

"You'll still be yourselves."

"You'll just become more."

The room fell silent again.

Darius laughed first.

"I was hoping for something more terrifying."

Malik nodded.

"I've followed you this far."

"I'm not stopping now."

Kael stepped beside him.

"I'm in."

Mara smiled.

"I was already wondering when you'd finally ask."

Lyra watched quietly from the back of the chamber.

A proud smile spread across her face.

Inside his mind...

Rez smiled.

**No army.**

**No audience.**

**Just family.**

Aidan nodded almost imperceptibly.

*Exactly.*

He slowly raised his right hand.

Crimson Blood flowed from the small wound on his wrist.

It glowed softly beneath the crystal light.

No one spoke.

Everyone understood.

This wasn't simply power.

This was the birth of a new chapter in House Darkheart's history.

Aidan looked at Malik.

A small grin crossed his face.

"Come here."

Malik walked forward.

Aidan held out his wrist.

Malik stared at it.

"...You know..."

"...I have absolutely no idea what's about to happen."

Aidan smirked.

"Neither do you."

The room burst into laughter.

Even Malik couldn't help but grin.

Then Aidan's expression became calm again.

"Trust me."

Malik met his eyes.

"I do."

He stepped closer.

The room held its breath.

The First Crimson Generation...

Was about to be born.
