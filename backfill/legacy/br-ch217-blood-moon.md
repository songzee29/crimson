Outside the cathedral...

The moon turned crimson.

Across New Orleans...

Every vampire felt it.

Some dropped to one knee.

Others fled without understanding why.

An ancient vampire whispered from miles away,

"...A king..."

"...has awakened."

Even Cassian stopped walking.

The Darkheart Brand burned beneath his sleeves.

He looked toward the cathedral.

His expression changed.

Not fear.

Recognition.

"...He's changed."

Far behind him...

Azrath stepped from the shadows.

"You expected the boy."

Cassian didn't answer.

Azrath smiled.

"You created the king."
