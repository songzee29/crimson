A cold wind swept across the rooftops overlooking the Mississippi.

Aidan stood at the edge.

Below, New Orleans continued moving as though nothing beneath its streets had changed.

Behind him…

The Inner Circle kept their distance.

Not because they feared he would attack.

Because they no longer knew how close they were allowed to stand.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco broke the silence.

“They weren’t hunting civilians.”

Everyone looked toward him.

“They were hunting you.”

Cassian frowned.

“Then why slaughter innocent people?”

“They didn’t.”

Draco pointed toward the streets below.

“They fed.”

“They compelled.”

“They recruited.”

“The killings were committed by the ferals.”

“They’ve been cleaning the board.”

Rowan’s expression hardened.

“So someone has spent years building an organized vampire network beneath this city.”

Draco nodded once.

“And now they know the Crimson Heir has finished the Ancient Gates.”

“They won’t hide anymore.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A sharp chime echoed inside Aidan’s mind.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Primary Objective Updated

Locate and Eliminate

Vampire Command Structure

Progress Required

Before Regional Expansion

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The notification vanished.

No reward.

Only another hunt.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Malik stepped forward.

“I’ve been thinking.”

Cassian sighed.

“That usually means trouble.”

Malik ignored him.

“They didn’t retreat because they lost.”

“They retreated because they confirmed something.”

“What?”

Malik looked directly at Aidan.

“They confirmed you’ll chase them.”

Aidan met his gaze.

“Correct.”

“So they’re choosing the battlefield.”

“Also correct.”

Malik’s shoulders lowered.

“The old you would’ve questioned why.”

“The current you only cares where.”

Aidan answered without hesitation.

“Results matter.”

“Motives don’t.”

No one argued.

Because that was exactly how he had been fighting since his humanity disappeared.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A scream echoed from three streets away.

Every head turned.

Another followed.

Then several more.

Aidan disappeared.

The rooftop cracked beneath the force of his jump.

Cassian cursed.

“There he goes.”

They followed immediately.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The attack was already underway.

A city bus had overturned.

Several vampires surrounded the wreckage.

Not feeding.

Waiting.

One of them smiled the instant Aidan landed.

“Right on time.”

The vampires attacked civilians.

Not to feed.

To force choices.

One rushed a terrified child.

Another charged an elderly couple.

A third sprinted toward a trapped police officer.

Aidan’s mind calculated instantly.

Three targets.

Insufficient time.

Maximum survivability.

He moved.

The child lived.

The elderly couple survived.

The police officer’s throat was torn open before Aidan reached him.

The vampire escaped into the night.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Silence settled over the street.

The officer collapsed.

Blood spread across the pavement.

The scent struck Aidan like a physical blow.

Every instinct screamed at him to kneel.

To feed.

His pupils narrowed.

His breathing slowed.

Cassian saw it immediately.

“Everyone back.”

Paramedics rushed forward.

Aidan didn’t move.

His eyes never left the blood pooling beneath the dying officer.

Lyra quietly approached him.

Not too close.

“You saved two.”

No response.

“You couldn’t save everyone.”

Still nothing.

She took another careful step.

“This isn’t your failure.”

Aidan finally spoke.

“I didn’t fail.”

She blinked.

“The objective changed.”

His gaze remained on the blood.

“I achieved the highest probability of survival.”

The words were perfectly logical.

Perfectly calculated.

And completely devoid of grief.

Lyra’s heart sank.

This wasn’t denial.

He truly saw the world that way now.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before anyone could speak again—

Draco looked toward the river.

“They’re watching.”

Across the Mississippi, silhouettes stood motionless along the opposite bank.

Dozens of them.

None crossed the water.

None attacked.

They simply watched Aidan.

Then, one by one…

They turned and disappeared into the darkness.

Draco’s face grew grim.

“They’ve finished measuring you.”

“They’re reporting back.”

Aidan wiped a single drop of the officer’s blood from his glove before turning away.

“Good.”

His crimson-gold eyes fixed on the dark horizon beyond the river.

“Now they’ll lead me to whoever commands them.”

The hunt had changed.

No longer scattered.

No longer reactive.

For the first time since the Vampire Arc began…

Aidan had a target.

And somewhere in the darkness…

Something ancient had decided the Crimson Heir was finally ready to meet it.
