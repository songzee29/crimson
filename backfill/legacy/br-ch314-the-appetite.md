The warehouse slept.

Outside...

Rain quietly washed over New Orleans.

Inside...

Aidan's eyes opened.

Exactly at **3:33 a.m.**

No nightmare.

No scream.

He simply...

Awakened.

His breathing remained calm.

His face showed no emotion.

He slowly sat up on the edge of the bed.

Listening.

Silence.

Then...

A heartbeat.

Not his own.

Another.

Another.

Another.

Hundreds.

Thousands.

The entire city beat like one enormous living heart.

He closed his eyes.

Trying to make it stop.

Instead...

It became louder.
