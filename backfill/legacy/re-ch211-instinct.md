The shattered cathedral stood silent.

Moonlight poured through the broken ceiling.

Stone dust drifted through the air.

Aidan slowly lowered his fist.

The altar no longer existed.

His crimson eyes remained fixed on the empty space before him.

Then...

Footsteps.

Seven.

No...

Eight.

His hearing corrected itself instantly.

Eight heartbeats.

Approaching from beneath the cathedral.

Not civilians.

Not hunters.

Something else.

---

The stone floor cracked.

Ancient symbols ignited beneath the church.

A forgotten seal...

Began opening.

Aidan didn't move.

He simply watched.

The air became cold.

Not naturally.

Magically.

One by one...

Eight figures climbed from the darkness.

Their skin was gray.

Ancient runes covered their flesh.

Their eyes glowed pale blue.

Not vampires.

Not witches.

Not wolves.

The oldest looked directly at Aidan.

Then smiled.

"...The Crimson King..."

"...has finally returned."

---

The System appeared.

---

> **NEW ENEMY DETECTED**

> **Ancient Veil Sentinels**

> **Status: S-Class Threat**

> **Originally Created To Execute Original Vampires**

---

Aidan read the words.

His face remained emotionless.

"...Sentinels."

The oldest raised a massive stone spear.

"We have waited four hundred years..."

"...for a king worthy of execution."

---

Aidan's coat fluttered in the wind.

He slowly cracked his neck.

Then...

For the first time since dying...

He spoke more than a single sentence.

"...Come."

No rage.

No excitement.

Just...

Permission.

---

The first Sentinel disappeared.

Its spear aimed directly for Aidan's skull.

Before the attack landed...

Aidan's body moved.

Not consciously.

Naturally.

His left hand caught the spear.

His right elbow shattered the Sentinel's jaw.

He spun.

Threw the massive creature through three cathedral pillars.

The Sentinel crashed into the far wall.

It didn't get back up.

---

Aidan looked at his own hand.

For a fraction of a second...

He frowned.

"...I..."

"...don't remember learning that."

The System immediately answered.

---

> **HYBRID COMPATIBILITY... INCREASING**

> **Combat Synchronization... 9%**

---

Aidan didn't question it.

He simply accepted it.

The remaining seven Sentinels surrounded him.

The oldest smiled.

"Good..."

"...Fight."

---

High above...

Lyra, Malik, and the Guardian arrived at the edge of the cathedral ruins.

They looked down...

And froze.

Not because of Aidan.

Because of what surrounded him.

The Guardian's eyes widened.

"...Impossible..."

Malik whispered,

"What are those things?"

The old man answered without taking his eyes off the battlefield.

"...The Veil's first executioners..."

"...I thought they were extinct."

Below them...

Aidan stood alone.

Eight ancient monsters circled the Crimson King.

His humanity remained off.

His emotions remained silent.

But his mind...

Sharper than ever.

He calmly shifted his footing.

One sentence escaped his lips.

"...Eight."

A pause.

"...Efficient."

Then he disappeared.

The cathedral exploded.
