No one spoke.

Not Lyra.

Not Malik.

Not Sienna.

The streets that had once echoed with jazz now echoed only with footsteps.

Aidan carried Elias in both arms.

Not because no one offered to help.

Because no one could have taken him.

Every person they passed instinctively stepped aside.

Firefighters removed their helmets.

Police officers lowered their heads.

Paramedics stopped pushing stretchers.

They didn't know Elias.

They didn't know Aidan.

But somehow...

They understood.

Someone important had been lost.

---

An old firefighter walked toward Aidan.

Gray hair.

Weathered face.

He looked at Elias for several seconds.

Then quietly removed the badge from his own chest.

Without saying a word...

He pinned it beside Elias' broken badge.

He saluted.

Then walked away.

One by one...

Every firefighter in the street did the same.

No orders.

No speeches.

Just respect.
