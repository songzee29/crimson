## 3. Gaps and editorial traps in the source

1. **Chapter 8 title collision:** “The Cost of a Word” and “Lines We Can’t See” both have the number 8. Preserve both.
2. **Chapter 13 response collision:** the 07:26 response is not interchangeable with the 07:40 response that contains Chapters 13–17.
3. **Chapter-only extraction loses context:** several responses include author-facing editorial statements, recap, or next-step options. The chapter extraction is useful for reading, but the original response is the evidence for what was actually delivered.
4. **Non-text payloads:** one Chapter 8 response ends with an audio asset marker. The text export does not expose the audio's full content as ordinary prose. Mark it as a payload, not as a chapter ending authored in words.
5. **Chapter numbering jumps:** after Chapters 29–30 on June 12, the next source batch appears June 16 at Chapters 31–32. This is a time gap in the conversation, not proof that unseen chapters 31–32 are missing—the source has them.
6. **Assistant suggestions are not author decisions:** option lists at the ends of chapters are possible next beats only. They should not be used as canon without a user selection.
7. **Rez's state must be read precisely:** faint internal presence or ominous closing thought is not equivalent to Rez waking, speaking openly, or becoming visible.
