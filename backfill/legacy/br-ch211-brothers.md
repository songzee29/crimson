The fire hissed beneath the rain.

Cassian stood in the middle of the ruined street.

His hand rested on the hilt of his sword.

Across from him...

Elias slowly stepped forward.

The support beam still pinned one of his legs, yet somehow he remained standing.

Blood dripped steadily onto the cracked pavement.

For a long moment...

Neither brother spoke.

Finally...

Cassian broke the silence.

"...You should have stayed gone."

Elias smiled sadly.

"I tried."

"You chose him."

"I chose people."

Cassian's eyes hardened.

"You abandoned your family."

Elias slowly shook his head.

"No."

"I finally understood what family meant."

The words struck deeper than any blade.

For the first time...

Cassian looked away.
