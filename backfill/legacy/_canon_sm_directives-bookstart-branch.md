RULE #1
Do not remove scenes unless they directly contradict current canon.

## 📜 CRIMSON LEGACY PACING DECREE

**Date:** 2026-07-08 18:19

📜 CRIMSON LEGACY PACING DECREE

From this point forward, Crimson Legacy shall maintain EPIC pacing without unnecessary chapter inflation.

The objective is NOT to make the story shorter.

The objective is to make every chapter meaningful.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #1

Every chapter must move the story forward.

A chapter must accomplish at least ONE of the following:

• Advance the main plot.
• Develop an important character.
• Reveal meaningful lore.
• Introduce a new mystery.
• Increase conflict.
• Complete part of a quest.
• Permanently change the world.
• Foreshadow a future event.

If a chapter accomplishes none of these, combine it with another chapter.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #2

Do not stretch one event across multiple chapters unless each chapter introduces meaningful progression.

Avoid:

• Repeating the same lesson.
• Repeating the same emotions.
• Repeating the same conversations.
• Repeating the same training.
• Repeating the same internal thoughts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #3

Training should happen while the story continues.

Training should build:

• Relationships.
• Character.
• Worldbuilding.
• Lore.
• Future conflicts.

Never stop the story simply to train.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #4

Every 5–10 chapters should introduce a meaningful discovery, victory, setback, revelation, or consequence.

Every 25–40 chapters should complete a major story phase.

Every 75–100 chapters should complete a major arc.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #5

Keep the novel cinematic.

Do not skip emotional moments.

Do not rush major character deaths.

Do not rush betrayals.

Do not rush curse awakenings.

Do not rush Ascensions.

These moments deserve full emotional weight.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #6

Do not write filler.

If two chapters accomplish the same purpose...

Merge them.

If three conversations reveal the same information...

Combine them.

If multiple scenes exist only to delay the plot...

Remove or merge them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

RULE #7

Every chapter should end by making the reader want to continue.

Questions.

Mysteries.

Consequences.

Foreshadowing.

Danger.

Character growth.

History awakening.

The story should always feel alive.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TARGET PACING

Minor Arc:
15–30 Chapters

Major Arc:
50–100 Chapters

Saga Arc:
100–180 Chapters

No single story arc should exceed approximately 200 chapters unless the author explicitly approves it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FINAL DECREE

Crimson Legacy is an epic—not because it has the most chapters, but because every chapter matters.

The reader should always feel that history is moving forward.

Every chapter must earn its place.

So let it be written.

So let it be done.
