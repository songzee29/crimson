Far beyond Louisiana...

Cassian stood before dozens of vampires.

His injuries had healed.

His hatred had not.

The Darkheart Brand rested quietly beneath his sleeves.

He never looked at it anymore.

He hated what it represented.

A young vampire approached.

"What now?"

Cassian slowly looked toward the east.

Toward New Orleans.

Toward House Darkheart.

His jaw tightened.

"We grow."

The vampire smiled.

"And then?"

Cassian's crimson eyes narrowed.

"Then..."

"...we make kings bleed."

High above the gathering...

Hidden among the broken rafters...

Azrath watched in silence.

His smile returned.

Everything was moving exactly as it should.

Not toward peace.

Toward destiny.

And somewhere...

Beyond even the Crimson Legacy...

Something ancient stirred.

Not because of Azrath.

Not because of Cassian.

But because House Darkheart had begun waking up.

The first Authority Born...

Would not be the last.
