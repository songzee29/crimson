Boom!

Glass showered across the floor.

Three Veil hunters rushed inside.

Malik met the first one with a tackle.

Sienna disarmed another before he could cast.

Lyra's magic lit up the warehouse in brilliant crimson.

Aidan didn't hesitate.

Hex Step.

One hunter fell.

Crimson Thread wrapped another's weapon.

Aidan intercepted him.

The hunter raised a silver dagger—

Aidan caught his wrist.

For the first time...

He wasn't fighting to survive.

He was fighting to protect his home.

His emotions surged.

The Witch Authority responded.

Every light inside the warehouse shattered.

Crimson energy erupted from the floor.

The Veil hunters froze.

Not from fear.

From pressure.

The entire building felt heavier.

Like gravity itself had changed.

The Guardian stepped through the broken doorway.

His eyes widened.

"...He's leaking."

Not blood.

Magic.

The Witch Authority was overflowing.

Aidan felt it too.

His heartbeat accelerated.

His breathing became ragged.

The crimson runes beneath his skin briefly became visible.

Then...

Just as suddenly...

Everything stopped.

The pressure vanished.

Aidan stumbled forward.

Confused.

"What..."

The Guardian caught him before he hit the floor.

His expression had become grave.

"No..."

"It shouldn't be happening this early."
