# Chapter One Hundred Seven

The eleven Guardians surged forward.

Their formation tightened.

Each movement forced them closer to Aidan.

Not because he stood at the center of the courtyard.

Because every ancient warrior had chosen him as its target.

Cassian met the first strike.

Twin swords flashed.

Stone splintered.

Another Guardian slipped past him.

Aidan stepped sideways.

Its blade missed his throat by a fraction.

He answered with a single punch.

The Guardian's chest exploded inward.

Crimson light leaked through the fracture.

Before it could recover—

Elias appeared behind it.

His blade pierced the exposed core.

The crimson light shattered.

The Guardian collapsed.

"One down!" Malik shouted.

"Nine," Rowan corrected.

"Stay focused!"

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Another Guardian drove a spear toward Aidan's back.

Lyra intercepted it in mid-lunge.

Her wolf form slammed into the stone warrior with enough force to send both crashing across the courtyard.

She ripped at its shoulder.

Stone flew.

The crimson core remained hidden.

The Guardian threw her aside.

She landed hard.

Cracked stone burst beneath her paws.

Aidan looked toward her.

Only for a second.

"Can you stand?"

Lyra rose immediately despite the impact.

"I always can."

There had once been a time when he would have crossed the battlefield to help her.

She remembered it.

For the briefest moment...

She waited.

He had already turned back toward the enemy.

The hesitation in her eyes disappeared.

She charged again.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cassian fought beside Aidan.

Back to back.

The way they had hundreds of times over the past years.

He blocked another crushing blow.

"You remember our first hunt?"

No answer.

Aidan broke a Guardian's arm without looking at him.

"It was a disaster."

Silence.

Cassian laughed despite himself.

"You nearly got us both killed."

Another Guardian attacked.

They defeated it together.

Still...

Nothing.

Not even a glance.

Cassian's smile slowly faded.

He lowered his voice.

"I miss arguing with you."

Aidan's expression never changed.

"We're in combat."

Three words.

Cold.

Practical.

Nothing more.

Cassian swallowed whatever else he had planned to say.

Then he threw himself back into the fight.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Malik watched the exchange.

His chest tightened.

He remembered the old Aidan.

The one who joked after battles.

The one who argued over strategy.

The one who laughed whenever Cassian exaggerated a story.

That man felt impossibly far away.

"Left!" Rowan shouted.

Malik reacted instantly.

Golden runes exploded beneath another Guardian.

The battle continued.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco's blade carved through stone.

He glanced toward Aidan.

"They're trying."

Aidan didn't answer.

"They haven't stopped."

Still...

Nothing.

Draco nodded once.

Almost to himself.

"I didn't expect you to."

He turned and severed another Guardian's arm.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The fountain trembled.

Beyond the courtyard walls...

Rainwater gathered along the edge of the ancient stone.

For only a heartbeat...

The tiny streams curved in impossible patterns before flowing normally once again.

No one looked away from the battle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Only seven Guardians remained.

They abandoned all defense.

Every strike became more desperate.

More relentless.

One Guardian ignored Cassian's blades completely.

It charged straight toward Aidan.

Rowan stepped between them.

His staff struck the stone warrior.

Golden magic flared.

The impact barely slowed it.

Aidan moved.

One hand caught the descending sword.

The other drove directly into the Guardian's fractured chest.

The crimson core shattered.

Stone collapsed around Rowan's feet.

The old Headmaster looked up.

For just a moment...

Hope crossed his face.

"Thank you."

Aidan released the broken Guardian.

"It was in the way."

Nothing more.

No concern.

No reassurance.

No warmth.

Rowan quietly lowered his eyes.

"I know."

He did.

And somehow...

That answer hurt more than if Aidan had said nothing at all.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The remaining Guardians formed a circle around Aidan once again.

The kneeling Guardian still had not moved.

Its head remained lowered.

Watching.

Waiting.

The ancient voice beneath the courtyard echoed one final time.

"The trial..."

A long silence followed.

"...continues."

Every Guardian attacked together.
