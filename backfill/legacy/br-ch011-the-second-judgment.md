Across the fire...

Cassian finally broke the silence.

"I know why you're here."

Aidan met his eyes.

"I wish I wasn't."

Cassian nodded slowly.

"The decree."

Aidan didn't deny it.

For several moments...

Neither man spoke.

Then Cassian asked the question that had haunted him for centuries.

"...Can it be stopped?"

Inside Aidan's mind...

The Legacy remained silent.

It offered no answer.

No quest.

No guidance.

This...

Was a king's burden.

Aidan answered honestly.

"I don't know."

It was the first time Cassian had ever heard those words from him.

Not certainty.

Not judgment.

Only truth.
