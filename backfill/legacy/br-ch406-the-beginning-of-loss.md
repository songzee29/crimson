Hundreds of miles away...

Black Hollow celebrated its annual Night of Blood Moon.

Music filled the valley.

Families gathered around great bonfires.

Cassian watched children dancing.

His partner found him in the crowd.

"You've been smiling more tonight."

Cassian looked around.

For the first time in a long while...

The emptiness had gone quiet.

Maybe...

Just maybe...

The decree had finally ended.

He reached for his partner's hand.

"I think..."

Before he could finish...

The Darkheart Brand beneath his skin flared.

Not with pain.

With warning.

Cassian froze.

His smile disappeared.

Somewhere...

Far beyond the mountains...

History had begun to move again.

The Second Judgment had taken its first unseen step.

The curse had not been sleeping.

It had been waiting.
