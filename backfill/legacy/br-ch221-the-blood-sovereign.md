Far beyond Louisiana...

Hidden beneath the frozen mountains of northern Canada...

An ancient castle trembled.

Deep within its throne room...

A coffin sealed in black iron slowly opened.

A pale hand reached into the air.

Crimson eyes opened.

Older than kingdoms.

Older than House Darkheart.

Older than history itself.

The ancient vampire king slowly stood.

Chains wrapped around his wrists fell to the floor.

CLANG.

CLANG.

CLANG.

He smiled.

Not kindly.

Hungrily.

"...At last..."

He looked south.

Toward New Orleans.

"...A king worthy of my blood."

Behind him...

Hundreds of ancient vampires opened their eyes.

One by one.

The Blood Sovereigns...

Had awakened.

And they weren't coming to rule beside Aidan.

They were coming...

To see if the Crimson King deserved to survive them.
