## 🔒 Decree 101 — The Crimson Bloodline Classifications

### Purpose

To establish the official classifications of descendants created through **True Crimson Blood**.

---

## Law

The descendants of the Crimson Heir are not classified as ordinary hybrids or tribrids.

They are recognized as distinct Crimson races born through **True Crimson Blood**.

The terms **Hybrid** and **Tribrid** shall not be used as the official classifications of Crimson descendants.

Instead, the Crimson Bloodline shall recognize the following races:

### Crimson Hexborn

**Crimson Witch + Crimson Vampire**

A descendant who inherits the Crimson Witch and Crimson Vampire bloodlines.

---

### Crimson Runefang

**Crimson Witch + Crimson Wolf**

A descendant who inherits the Crimson Witch and Crimson Wolf bloodlines.

---

### Crimson Blood Fangclaw

**Crimson Vampire + Crimson Wolf**

A descendant who inherits the Crimson Vampire and Crimson Wolf bloodlines.

---

### Crimson Trinity

**Crimson Witch + Crimson Vampire + Crimson Wolf**

A descendant who inherits all three Crimson bloodlines and is recognized as a complete threefold descendant of the Crimson Heir.

---

## Foundational Principle

These classifications are not combinations of existing supernatural races.

They are the first recognized races of the **Crimson Bloodline**.

Each possesses its own identity, history, and future independent of ordinary supernatural classifications.

From the First Blooding onward, these names become the official constitutional classifications of all descendants born through **True Crimson Blood**.
