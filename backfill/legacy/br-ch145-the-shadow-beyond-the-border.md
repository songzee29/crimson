Miles away...

A lone traveler stopped walking.

A hood concealed their face.

They slowly turned toward House Darkheart.

Then smiled.

"So..."

"You finally noticed me."

They removed one black glove.

Their palm bore an ancient crimson mark.

Not identical to Aidan's.

Older.

Weathered.

As though it belonged to another age.

The traveler quietly laughed.

"Good."

"I was beginning to think..."

"...the Founder wasn't paying attention."

They pulled the hood back over their head.

And continued walking.

Toward House Darkheart.

Toward the Crimson King.

Toward a meeting that would change everything Aidan believed about the origins of the Crimson Bloodline.

Far away...

Aidan still watched the western horizon.

Inside his mind...

Rez spoke only three words.

**They're coming now.**

Aidan nodded once.

"So are we."

The Crimson King turned back toward his castle.

Whatever waited beyond his borders...

House Darkheart would face it together.
