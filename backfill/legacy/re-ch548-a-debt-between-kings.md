That evening...

Cassian stood outside his home.

Aidan approached alone.

No guards.

No witnesses.

Just the two of them.

For a long time...

Neither spoke.

Finally...

Cassian broke the silence.

"You saved my people."

Aidan nodded.

"They deserved saving."

Cassian looked down.

"I've spent years blaming you for everything."

"...I know."

"And today..."

He sighed.

"...you still came."

Aidan answered honestly.

"I didn't come because of our past."

"I came because innocent people shouldn't suffer."

Cassian looked into Aidan's eyes.

For the first time...

There was no hatred.

Only conflict.

The Darkheart Curse still bound him.

But the man standing before him...

Was no longer the enemy he remembered.
