At the center of the platform sat a small object.

Not a weapon.

Not a crown.

Not an artifact of power.

A simple wooden box.

Dark crimson.

Worn by time.

Waiting.

---

The moment Aidan saw it—

his chest hurt.

Badly.

Like grief without context.

Like longing without memory.

---

Lyra noticed immediately.

"Aidan?"

He ignored her.

Not intentionally.

The box had all of his attention now.

Every step toward it felt inevitable.

Like fate was no longer asking permission.

---

Then he reached it.

And stopped.

Because carved into the top of the box were three words.

Words that shouldn't mean anything.

Words he had never seen before.

Yet somehow they shattered him.

---

**FOR MY SON**

---

Aidan couldn't breathe.

The world tilted.

Not memory.

Not a vision.

Just raw emotion crashing through a hole in his soul.

---

His right leg started shaking violently.

Harder than ever before.

---

"Aidan?"

Lyra's voice sounded distant.

Far away.

Like she was underwater.

---

Because the moment his fingers touched the box—

the sanctuary came alive.

---

A memory exploded around them.

---

Not his memory.

Someone else's.

---

A man stood in the sanctuary.

Tall.

Dark-haired.

Covered in blood.

A crown rested beside him.

Not on his head.

Beside him.

As though he no longer cared about it.

---

His eyes were exhausted.

Broken.

Terrified.

---

And Aidan immediately knew who he was.

Not because the System told him.

Not because anyone explained it.

Because every instinct inside him screamed the truth.

---

**Zee.**

---

The memory-Zee looked toward someone outside the vision.

Someone Aidan couldn't see.

---

His voice cracked when he spoke.

---

"I don't care what happens to me."

---

The statement echoed through the sanctuary.

Raw.

Honest.

Real.

---

"Just promise me he lives."

---

Aidan's knees nearly gave out.

---

The memory continued.

---

"They're coming."

---

A pause.

---

"They already found the others."

---

Silence.

Then something happened that shook Aidan more than anything else.

---

Zee started crying.

---

Not dramatically.

Not loudly.

Quietly.

Like a father who had already lost too much.

---

"If he stays with me..."

---

His voice broke completely.

---

"They'll kill him."

---

Aidan's chest felt like it was being ripped apart.

Because he didn't understand why it hurt.

He only knew that it did.

---

Then Zee looked directly toward the memory.

Directly toward where Aidan was standing.

---

And for one impossible second—

it felt like he could actually see him.

---

The memory-Zee whispered:

---

"I'm sorry, little prince."

---

Everything shattered.

---

The vision collapsed.

The sanctuary returned.

The candles steadied.

The silence came back.

---

Aidan fell to one knee.

Breathing hard.

Heart hammering.

Eyes wide.

---

Because for the first time since opening the Crimson Book—

he wasn't chasing a mystery anymore.

---

He had found proof.

---

Not proof that Zee existed.

Not proof that the Crimson Dynasty was real.

---

Proof that someone had loved him enough to erase him from the world.

And somewhere deep within the sanctuary, beyond doors not yet opened and truths not yet revealed, something ancient stirred.

Because the heir had returned.

And the Sanctuary of Truth had only just begun telling its story.
