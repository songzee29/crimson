When the council ended...

Rez and Aidan walked through the castle gardens.

Moonlight reflected across crimson flowers.

Rez finally asked the question that had been waiting.

"...Are you happy?"

Aidan didn't answer immediately.

He looked across House Darkheart.

Children trained with wooden swords.

Young witches practiced under lantern light.

Families walked the castle grounds without fear.

"...Happy?"

He considered the word.

"...Sometimes."

Rez nodded.

"That's enough."

Aidan looked toward him.

Rez smiled.

"If you were happy all the time..."

"...you'd stop growing."

The two continued walking.

Not as teacher and student.

Not as legend and heir.

But as two men carrying the weight of House Darkheart together.
