Commander Cassian descended beneath an abandoned fortress.

Steel doors.

Silver chains.

Ancient runes.

At the center...

Specimen 001.

Still smiling.

Cassian stepped forward.

"You know something."

The vampire laughed softly.

"I know many things."

"Who are you?"

The prisoner ignored the question.

Instead...

He looked directly into Cassian's eyes.

"The King has started seeing red..."

"...hasn't he?"

Cassian froze.

"You've been watching him?"

The vampire smiled wider.

"No."

"I've been feeling him."

Silence.

"He doesn't know yet..."

"...but his blood is beginning to remember."
