Three weeks later...

The emergency bells rang across New Orleans.

Not once.

Not twice.

Seven times.

The same number they rang every year for Elias.

Only this time...

It wasn't a memorial.

It was a warning.

The Circle rushed outside.

Everyone stopped.

The sky...

Was raining blood.

Not everywhere.

Only over one neighborhood.

The French Quarter.

Lyra whispered,

"...Again?"

The Guardian slowly shook his head.

"No..."

"This is different."

---

The blood never touched the ground.

Every drop stopped inches above the streets.

Floating.

As if waiting.

For someone.
