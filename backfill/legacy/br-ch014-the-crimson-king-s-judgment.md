That evening...

Aidan walked to Cassian's doorstep alone.

No sword.

No army.

No threats.

Cassian opened the door.

"I knew you'd come."

Aidan nodded.

"I need to show you something."

The two men walked in silence until they reached a hill overlooking Black Hollow.

The village glowed warmly beneath the stars.

Aidan finally spoke.

"When I died..."

"I wanted you to hurt the way I hurt."

Cassian lowered his head.

"I know."

"I thought pain would teach you."

A long silence followed.

"I was wrong."

Cassian looked up.

"What?"

Aidan's voice was heavy.

"Pain only teaches pain."
