To everyone's surprise...

Orion walked past Aidan.

Past Lyra.

Until he stopped in front of Rez.

The Crimson Assassin remained perfectly still.

"You know me."

Rez answered honestly.

"I don't."

"But I know... something."

Orion nodded.

"I thought so."

He smiled warmly.

"I've missed your terrible sense of humor."

Rez blinked.

"...What?"

Malik looked between them.

"...Hold on."

"You two actually know each other?"

Rez slowly shook his head.

"I..."

"I don't remember."

---

Orion's smile faded.

"I was afraid of that."

He reached into his coat.

Not for a weapon.

For a small wooden carving.

He held it out.

A wolf.

Simple.

Hand-carved.

Time-worn.

Rez froze.

His hand trembled.

"I..."

He slowly accepted it.

The moment his fingers touched the carving—

Another memory exploded into his mind.

---

A young boy laughed.

"You kept it!"

Rez smiled.

"I told you I would."

The boy hugged him.

"I knew you wouldn't throw it away."

The memory disappeared.

---

Rez looked down at the carving.

His voice cracked.

"...The wolf..."

"I remember..."
