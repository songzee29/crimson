The Ash King disappeared into the darkness.

The echoes stopped attacking.

One by one...

The ancient warriors lowered their weapons.

Then...

They faded.

Not destroyed.

Released.

The Hall of Names became quiet once more.

Only the sound of breathing remained.

---

No one celebrated.

The battle hadn't been won.

It had become...

Personal.

"You've been holding your side this entire time."

The wound from the Ash Spear still hadn't healed.

"I'm fine."

"...You keep saying that."

Rez laughed.

"And she keeps proving you wrong."

For a brief moment...

The weight of kingship disappeared.

He was simply surrounded by people who cared about him.
