The grinding of ancient stone continued long after the stairway finished opening.

No one moved.

The air rising from below was unnaturally cold.

It carried the scent of damp earth…

Ancient blood…

And centuries of silence.

Aidan stepped toward the opening without hesitation.

Cassian caught his arm.

“Wait.”

Aidan looked down at the hand gripping his sleeve.

Then at Cassian.

“What?”

“We’ve walked into too many places like this.”

Cassian glanced at the darkness below.

“We catch our breath.”

“We make a plan.”

“For once.”

Aidan pulled his arm free.

“We already have one.”

“And what’s that?”

“We go down.”

Cassian exhaled through his nose.

“You used to argue with me.”

“There isn’t anything to argue about.”

He descended the first step.

Stone steps spiraled deeper beneath the sanctuary.

Ancient crimson crystals embedded within the walls slowly awakened as Aidan passed.

Their dim light revealed carvings unlike those in the Guardian Court.

Not battles.

Not kings.

Hunters.

Groups of men and women armed with unfamiliar weapons pursuing towering, twisted creatures through underground tunnels.

Many carvings ended the same way.

The hunters vanished.

The monsters remained.

Draco slowed.

“I’ve never seen these.”

“These weren’t made to celebrate victories.”

It reached the first landing…

Paused for the briefest instant…

Then quietly flowed around Aidan’s footprints before continuing downward.

No one noticed.

The descent continued.

The staircase ended inside an immense circular chamber.

Broken pillars stretched toward a ceiling hidden by darkness.

Some ended abruptly.

Others disappeared into the blackness overhead.

The center of the chamber held only a massive stone door.

No handle.

No lock.

Only a single crimson handprint carved into its center.

The kneeling Guardian entered behind them.

Its footsteps echoed once.

Then it stopped.

It lowered its head again.

It would go no farther.

“The trial changes here,” Draco said quietly.

Aidan approached the door.

His second heartbeat struck once.

The crimson handprint answered.

Light spread through ancient cracks running across the enormous stone.
