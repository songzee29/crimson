Cassian lunged.

Sword drawn.

The blade stopped one inch from Azrath's throat.

It refused to move.

Not because Azrath blocked it.

Because Cassian's own arm had frozen.

Azrath looked at the blade.

Then back at Cassian.

"You still think like a man."

Another pause.

"That..."

"...will pass."

Cassian growled.

"You tricked me."

"No."

Azrath stepped around the blade.

"You asked for strength."

"I gave you the opportunity to earn it."

---

The hunger struck again.

Harder.

Cassian dropped his sword.

His breathing became ragged.

"...Make it stop."

Azrath's expression remained unreadable.

"I can't."

"You can."

"No."

Azrath looked directly into his eyes.

"Only blood can."
