He caught himself.

His body screamed for rest.

His mind screamed louder.

Then...

He did something he had never done before.

He raised both hands.

The Witch Authority spiraled around him.

The System flashed violently.

---

---

Even Azrath's smile disappeared.

"...Another one?"

Aidan's crimson eyes never left Cassian.

Blood ran down both arms.

His voice echoed across the shattered swamp.

> **"...The King's Burden."**

The Witch Authority exploded outward.

Not as an attack.

As a field.

Everything inside it became heavier.

The air.

The water.

Even gravity itself.

Cassian immediately dropped to one knee.

His vampire strength fought against the invisible weight.

His muscles trembled.

"What..."

"...did you do?"

Aidan stood in the center of the crimson field.

Barely standing himself.

He answered through bloodied lips.

> **"I made you..."**

He took one more step.

Each step looked impossible.

> **"...carry..."**

Another step.

> **"...what I carry..."**

The weight doubled.

Cassian's arm hit the ground.

The swamp cracked beneath him.

Even Azrath narrowed his eyes.

Because for one brief moment...

He felt it too.

Not the spell.

The meaning.

A king's burden wasn't armor.

It wasn't a crown.

It was weight.

And for the first time in his life...

Cassian was forced to bear even a fraction of it.

He screamed.

Not from pain.

From pressure.

Aidan looked at him.

Exhausted.

Broken.

Still standing.

Then quietly whispered...

> **"Now..."**

> **"...fight me."**
