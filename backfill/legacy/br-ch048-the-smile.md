Deep beneath the Veil...

Darkness.

Silence.

Ancient chains stretched endlessly through the abyss.

One seal flickered.

A hand reached toward it.

Not enough to break it.

Just enough...

To touch it.

Not a cruel smile.

Not an angry one.

A patient one.

He whispered into the darkness...

As if speaking directly to Cassian.

Tiny.

Almost invisible.

But House Darkheart's prison...

Had begun to fail.
