Cassian couldn't move.

His breathing became ragged.

His knees hit the floor.

He looked at his own hands.

Covered in Lyra's blood.

The blood of an innocent queen.

The woman who had never once raised a weapon against him.

His voice broke completely.

"...What..."

"...have I done?"

The Darkheart Brand burned across his arm.

Not as punishment.

As recognition.

The Third Judgment...

Had been fulfilled.

Not by fate.

Not by magic.

By Cassian's own choice.

He screamed.

The sound echoed through every hallway of House Darkheart.

Guards rushed toward the royal chambers.

Kael arrived first.

Then Mara.

Then Darius.

Every one of them stopped at the doorway.

No one entered.

No one spoke.

The sight before them stole every word.

Their Queen...

Dying in the King's arms.
