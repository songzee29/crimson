An elder of the Veil stepped forward.

"Commander!"

Cassian never looked away from Elias.

"Finish the mission!"

No response.

"Commander!"

His face had changed.

Cold.

Empty.

He looked at the elder.

Then...

At Aidan.

Then back again.

In one smooth motion...

He drew his sword.

The blade stopped at the elder's throat.

Every Veil hunter froze.

"If one more person..."

"...raises a weapon tonight..."

"...I'll execute them myself."

Silence.

No one argued.

No one questioned him.

The elder slowly stepped back.

Cassian lowered his blade.

"Retreat."

Within seconds...

The Veil vanished into the smoke.

Not because they had been defeated.

Because even they understood...

Nothing good would come from staying.
