The widening gap between the Gates released ancient air into the cathedral.

It smelled of stone.

Dust.

And blood that had never truly dried.

Aidan stepped forward.

The chamber answered immediately.

The floor trembled.

Crimson veins spread through the black stone.

At the center of the cathedral, the ground burst apart.

A colossal armored figure slowly emerged.

It stood nearly fifteen feet tall.

Its armor resembled dark crimson steel fused directly to its body.

No face.

Only two glowing crimson eyes.

Its enormous sword rested against the stone.

The Guardian looked directly at Aidan.

Then raised the blade.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON SYSTEM

Blood Trial III Accepted

The Crimson Guardian

Objective:

Defeat the Guardian.

Reward:

Access to the Third Seal.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Guardian moved.

The sword descended.

The impact split the cathedral floor for hundreds of feet.

Aidan vanished.

Crimson Dash carried him clear.

Fragments of stone exploded around him.

He countered immediately.

His fist struck the Guardian’s chest.

The impact echoed.

Nothing happened.

The armor never cracked.

The Guardian answered with another strike.

Blood Step activated instinctively.

Aidan slipped beside the descending blade by following the faint trail of his own blood lingering in the air.

His claws carved across the Guardian’s shoulder.

A shallow mark.

The first damage.

The Guardian stopped.

Then smiled.

Its armor shifted.

The shallow wound vanished.

It regenerated.

The trial had become something entirely different.

Not a contest of strength.

A contest of endurance.
