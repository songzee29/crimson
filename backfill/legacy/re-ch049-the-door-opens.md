The moment the memory shattered, silence swallowed everything.

Not ordinary silence.

The kind that falls over a place that has been waiting a very long time.

Aidan stared at the crimson wooden door.

His hand still rested against it.

The grain of the wood felt warm.

Alive.

Like it recognized him.

Beside him, Lyra couldn't move.

The color had completely drained from her face.

"Aidan..." she whispered.

The door answered before he could.

A deep sound echoed from within.

Not a creak.

Not a groan.

A lock.

Opening.

---

**CLACK.**

---

The old crimson door slowly swung inward.

Darkness waited on the other side.

Not blackness.

Not emptiness.

Just a place untouched by time.

Aidan's heart pounded.

The pull inside his chest nearly doubled.

Every instinct told him the same thing.

Go.

Lyra grabbed his sleeve.

"Wait."

Aidan looked at her.

"What?"

Her voice was shaking.

"The Veil was terrified of this place."

A pause.

"And now I know why."

Aidan looked back at the open doorway.

"What is it?"

Lyra swallowed.

"I think it's proof."

"Proof of what?"

She looked directly into his eyes.

"That somebody lied about your existence."

---

The air grew colder.

Aidan felt something twist inside him.

Not memory.

Not knowledge.

Recognition.

Like a locked room inside his mind had just heard its own name.

---

The System suddenly appeared.

For the first time in hours, every line of text was unstable.

---

> UNKNOWN LOCATION ENTERED

> RECORDS UNAVAILABLE

> PRIORITY DIRECTIVE UPDATED

---

Aidan frowned.

Then another line appeared.

One he had never seen before.

---

> WELCOME HOME, HEIR

---

Everything froze.

The text remained for only a second.

Then vanished.

As if the System immediately regretted showing it.

---

Aidan stared at the empty air.

His pulse quickened.

Lyra noticed.

"What did it say?"

Aidan hesitated.

Then answered honestly.

"It called me heir."

---

Lyra went completely still.
