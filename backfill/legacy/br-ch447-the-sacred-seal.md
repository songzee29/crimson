Lyra slowly walked toward him.

She wasn't afraid.

She refused to be.

When she stood before him...

She gently placed one hand against his chest.

She could feel it.

Two heartbeats.

One human...

One wolf.

Tears gathered in her eyes.

"...I'm sorry."

Aidan's voice was calm.

"...For what?"

She closed her eyes.

"For lying to you."

The forest became still.

"I've carried this secret..."

"...since the day you died."

Aidan didn't interrupt.

He simply listened.

"The night your Vampire Authority awakened..."

"...your Wolf Authority awakened too."

"It was beautiful..."

"...and terrifying."

"It wasn't evil."

"It wasn't trying to destroy the world."

"It was trying..."

"...to protect you."

Her voice broke.

"But your body couldn't survive both awakenings at once."

"I was afraid..."

"...that if the wolf fully awakened..."

"...you would die before your journey even began."

"So..."

She lowered her head.

"...I used my magic."

"...I placed the Sacred Seal upon your Wolf Authority."

Silence.

"I never asked your permission."

"I chose for you."

"I'm so sorry."
