Later that morning...

Lyra entered the throne room.

She dismissed everyone except Aidan.

"We need to talk."

He smiled softly.

"I figured."

She walked until she stood directly before him.

"If Cassian comes..."

"...don't hide me."

Aidan's expression changed instantly.

"No."

"I'm serious."

"No."

"He isn't coming for the throne."

"No."

"He'll come for your heart."

Aidan stepped closer.

"And that's exactly why you'll stay protected."

Lyra reached up and gently placed her hand against his face.

"You can't protect me from destiny forever."

His voice became firm.

"I can try."

She smiled sadly.

"And that's why I love you."
