# Chapter Eighty-Two: The First Blood

The forest fell silent.

Only one heartbeat remained.

Wild.

Terrified.

Aidan's fangs pierced flesh.

The first taste of living blood struck him like lightning.

The fire inside his throat vanished instantly.

Power surged through every part of his body.

His shattered exhaustion disappeared beneath an overwhelming tide of strength.

The world became sharper.

Brighter.

Clearer.

The heartbeat weakened.

Still...

The hunger demanded more.

Drink.

The man's struggles slowed.

His hands slipped helplessly against Aidan's coat.

His heartbeat faltered.

Then—

"Aidan!"

Lyra's voice tore through the trees.

She burst into the clearing with Cassian, Rowan, Helena, Malik, and Elias close behind.

They stopped.

Too late.

The woodcutter's body fell lifeless into the leaves.

White.

Empty.

Completely drained.

Silence swallowed the clearing.

Aidan slowly lifted his head.

Crimson stained his lips.

His eyes met Lyra's.

For one impossible heartbeat...

Neither moved.

Then—

Pain.

A savage howl erupted from Aidan's chest.

He staggered backward.

His body convulsed violently.

Dark crimson energy burst around him.

Something ancient answered from deep within his soul.

Not the Vampire.

Something wilder.

Older.

More feral.

The ground split beneath his feet.

His fingers curled into claws.

The howl became deeper.

More animal than human.

Chains of living witchcraft wrapped around the crimson storm.

The feral presence fought with impossible fury.

Trees bent outward under the pressure.

Stone cracked.

The chains held.

Again they tightened.

Again.

Until—

The crimson storm folded inward.

The savage howl ended.

Aidan collapsed to one knee.

His breathing became unnaturally still.

The feral pressure disappeared beneath the seal.

The forest exhaled.

"I sealed it..."

Her eyes never left Aidan.

"For now."

Aidan slowly raised his head.

The hunger remained.

The crimson eyes remained.

But the wild force had retreated beneath the binding.
