Night covered New Orleans.

A raven landed upon the balcony outside the throne room.

Around its leg...

A silver scroll.

Not blood.

Not magic.

Authority.

Aidan opened it.

Only one sentence had been written.

> **"You have claimed the Blood Crown."**

> **"Now come claim the throne you do not yet know exists."**

No signature.

Only a symbol.

Four interlocking circles.

The same symbol from the ancient journal.

The same symbol connected to the mysterious Fourth Throne.

Aidan looked toward the horizon.

Then quietly folded the scroll.

"...Finally."

Behind him...

The Crimson Assassin stepped into position.

Three steps.

Exactly as always.

The hunt for crowns was over.

The hunt for truths...

Had begun.
