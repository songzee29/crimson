The impact shook the entire sanctuary.

Four Guardians struck at once.

Aidan met them head-on.

His movements remained precise.

No wasted motion.

No anger.

No fear.

Every dodge measured.

Every strike final.

Cassian crashed into the nearest Guardian.

“You’ll have to get through us first!”

The Guardian ignored him completely.

Its empty eyes never left Aidan.

Cassian drove both swords into its side.

Still…

It kept walking.

“Seriously?”

He ripped his blades free.

“What does it take?”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Lyra launched herself across the courtyard.

She landed between Aidan and another Guardian.

The stone warrior raised its blade.

She met it with fang and claw.

Stone shattered beneath her assault.

For one brief instant…

Their eyes met.

Not predator to predator.

Not warrior to warrior.

She searched his face.

The same face she had fought beside for years.

The same eyes that once softened whenever he looked at her.

“Aidan!”

Her voice cut through the battle.

“I know you’re still in there!”

His response came without hesitation.

“Move.”

Nothing else.

No recognition.

No warmth.

Only an order.

She stepped aside just as he passed.

His fist shattered the Guardian’s core.

Stone collapsed around them.

The battle continued.

Lyra remained frozen for only a heartbeat.

Then she forced herself back into the fight.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Elias noticed.

So did Malik.

Neither said a word.

There would be time later.

If they survived.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The remaining three Guardians suddenly stopped.

All together.

The courtyard fell silent.

Their weapons lowered.

Then…

They struck the ground with their fists.

The ancient sanctuary answered.

Crimson light spread through every carved line in the floor.

The fountain erupted.

Blood rose into the air without spilling.

Suspended.

Weightless.

The kneeling Guardian finally lifted its head.

For the first time…

It looked beyond Aidan.

Toward the fountain.

The ancient voice echoed through every stone.

“The First Trial…”

“…is complete.”

The three standing Guardians stepped backward in perfect unison.

Not defeated.

Yielding.

The crimson light faded from their eyes.

One after another…

They returned to stone.

The kneeling Guardian remained.

Still watching.

Still silent.

A low grinding sound echoed beneath the sanctuary.

The cracked fountain slowly divided down its center.

Ancient gears hidden beneath centuries of stone began turning.

A narrow stairway descended into darkness.

Cold air rose from below.

Carrying the scent of old blood…

And something far older waiting beneath the Ancient Gates.
