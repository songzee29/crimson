Outside the warehouse...

Rain poured harder than before.

The Veil hunter from earlier stood across the street.

His black coat swayed gently in the wind.

He hadn't left.

He had been waiting.

Another hunter approached him.

"You could have killed him."

The older hunter remained silent.

"I was ordered to observe."

"And?"

The older man finally spoke.

"...He's different."

"Different how?"

"He protects before he attacks."

Silence.

The younger hunter frowned.

"That makes him dangerous."

The older hunter shook his head.

"No."

"It makes him..."

He searched for the right word.

"...familiar."

The younger hunter looked confused.

"You knew Zee?"

A long silence.

Then—

"I fought beside him."

The younger hunter froze.

"...What?"

The older hunter looked toward the warehouse.

"Long before the Veil became what it is now."

He walked away.

Leaving the younger hunter speechless.

The past...

Was beginning to move.
