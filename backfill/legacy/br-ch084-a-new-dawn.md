The first rays of sunlight spilled across House Darkheart.

For the first time...

The Crimson King watched the sunrise without wondering if the woman beside him would see another.

Lyra stood quietly on the balcony.

Alive.

Her fingers traced the fresh scar where Cassian's blade had pierced her heart.

There was no wound.

Only a memory.

Aidan stepped beside her.

Neither spoke.

Some moments didn't need words.

Below them, the kingdom slowly awakened.

Children ran through the streets.

Merchants reopened their shops.

Blacksmiths reignited their forges.

Life continued.

Yet House Darkheart would never be the same.

Lyra finally broke the silence.

"...I died."

Aidan looked toward the horizon.

"You did."

"And you brought me back."

"I did."

She smiled softly.

"You really don't know how to let me go."

He finally looked at her.

"I never will."

She rested her head against his shoulder.

"And I don't want you to."

For several peaceful moments...

There was nothing but the sound of the wind.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

Rez's familiar voice echoed through the Soul Link.

**"She's adjusting."**

Aidan answered without moving his lips.

*"I know."*

**"How are you holding up?"**

Aidan was quiet for a long moment.

*"I almost lost everything."*

Rez didn't answer immediately.

He understood.

No advice could erase that pain.

After a while, he simply said,

**"But you didn't."**

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Bloodline Record Updated**

**Founder Status Confirmed**

**True Crimson Blood — Stable**

**Living Blueprint Updated**

---

Aidan read the notification in silence.

No reward appeared.

No new power awakened.

Only a record.

Exactly as the Legacy was meant to be.

Lyra noticed the small smile that crossed his face.

"The Legacy?"

He nodded.

"It recorded what happened."

"What did it say?"

Aidan looked back over the kingdom.

"That we're still here."

---

Rez chuckled.

**"The Legacy has always cared more about defining moments than victories."**

*"I know."*

**"It doesn't remember every battle."**

**"It remembers the choices that changed history."**

Aidan thought about Cassian.

About mercy.

About Lyra.

About the choice to save her instead of surrendering to grief.

Maybe...

Those were the moments that truly mattered.

---

**Ding.**

## **CRIMSON LEGACY**

**Living Blueprint Available**

**True Crimson Blood Synchronization**

**Progress: 1%**

**Recommendation: Train naturally.**

**Mastery cannot be rushed.**

---

Aidan raised an eyebrow.

*"One percent?"*

Rez laughed.

**"Did you think becoming the Founder of an entirely new bloodline would make you a master overnight?"**

Aidan couldn't help but laugh too.

*"Fair point."*

**"The Legacy isn't telling you to activate anything,"** Rez said. **"It's showing you where you stand. The rest is up to you."**

Aidan looked down at his hands.

He could feel the difference.

The Crimson Blood flowed through him more smoothly than ever before.

Not stronger...

Just...

More complete.

He smiled.

"Then let's see what this new beginning has in store."

Far below...

House Darkheart continued to thrive.

And for the first time since Judgment Phase Three...

The Crimson King wasn't preparing for another tragedy.

He was preparing to grow.
