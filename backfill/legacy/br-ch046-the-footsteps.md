Hours later they emerged into a forgotten section of the city.

The streets above were abandoned.

Buildings leaned at strange angles.

Streetlights flickered despite having no power source.

Even the air felt old.

As if nobody had visited this part of the city in years.

Maybe decades.

Lyra kept checking over her shoulder.

Something felt wrong.

Not hostile.

Wrong.

Then Aidan heard it.

A footstep.

He stopped instantly.

Lyra looked at him.

"What?"

Aidan frowned.

"You didn't hear that?"

"Hear what?"

Another step echoed.

Soft.

Distant.

Ahead of them.

Not behind.

Ahead.

Aidan turned toward the sound.

Nothing.

Empty street.

Broken buildings.

Silence.

Then another step.

---

Tap.

---

Aidan's heart sped up.

Someone was there.

He knew it.

But every time he focused—

the sound moved further away.

Like whoever was making it refused to be seen.

"Aidan?"

"I hear someone walking."

Lyra listened carefully.

Nothing.

"Aidan... there's nobody there."

Another step.

Closer this time.

Then silence.

---

And suddenly—

his right leg started shaking.

Rapidly.

Unconsciously.

The same nervous movement he'd done several times since opening the Crimson Book.

A habit he never noticed.

But Lyra did.

"What are you doing?"

Aidan looked down.

His leg immediately stopped.

"I didn't realize I was moving."

Lyra stared at him.

Something about that felt important.

She just didn't know why.
