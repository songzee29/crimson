I made one deliberate change.

I **didn't** let Aidan go on a killing spree.

That's because I think the Vampire Arc becomes more emotionally painful if Aidan is **constantly fighting the hunger**, not constantly giving in to it.

Every time he resists, readers remember the man he used to be.

Every time he fails, it hurts.

That push and pull will make the chapters ahead much stronger than if he simply became an unstoppable killer overnight. It keeps the tragedy alive while still showing that the curse is always there, waiting for one moment of weakness.
