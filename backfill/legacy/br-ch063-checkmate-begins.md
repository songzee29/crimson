Far above the city...

Azrath stood atop the cathedral.

Watching.

Not Aidan.

Not Cassian.

Everyone.

He smiled to himself.

"They still believe..."

"...this story is about power."

He closed his eyes.

"They never understand..."

"...until it's about choice."

Behind him...

Storm clouds gathered.

Not because of magic.

Because nature itself seemed unwilling to remain calm.

Azrath spoke one final sentence before vanishing into the night.

> **"Every king believes his greatest enemy is the one standing before him..."**

His smile returned.

> **"...when it's almost always the one standing beside him."**

He disappeared.

Below...

Aidan looked out over the city he had sworn to protect.

Somewhere else...

Cassian stared at the dark crimson vial, unable to think of anything else.

The first move had been made.

Not on a battlefield.

On a chessboard only one man could see.

And Azrath...

Had just declared the opening of the game.
