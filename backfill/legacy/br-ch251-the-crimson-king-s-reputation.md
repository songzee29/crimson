Outside...

The police officers watched in complete silence.

One officer lowered his weapon.

Another whispered,

"...Did you see his hands?"

"What about them?"

"He only moved once."

The firefighter standing beside them smiled.

"You'll get used to it."

The officer looked confused.

"You know him?"

The firefighter shook his head.

"No."

"But I've seen him work."

"He never wastes a movement."

---

Word spread quickly.

Not about the battle.

About the calm.

People stopped saying:

> "The Crimson King defeated another curse."

Instead they started saying:

> "The Crimson King touched the air..."

> "...and the curse surrendered."

Legends have a funny way of growing.
