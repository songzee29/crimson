Near the edge of the dock...

An old man stood watching the river.

Elegant black suit.

Silver hair.

A crimson cane rested in his hand.

He never turned around.

"You arrived too late."

Aidan stopped.

"You know what happened here?"

The old man smiled faintly.

"I know..."

"...what did."

He finally looked over his shoulder.

His eyes weren't crimson.

They were completely black.

"You carry Zee's blood."

Aidan stiffened.

"You knew him."

The old man chuckled.

"I defeated him."

Silence.

Even the wind seemed to stop.

Aidan's fists clenched.

"...You're lying."

The old man shrugged.

"Perhaps."

"Perhaps not."

He slowly tapped his cane against the wooden dock.

"You'll discover the truth eventually."

Then...

He walked away.

Every instinct screamed at Aidan to chase him.

The System appeared.

**Do Not Pursue**

---

That was all.

For some reason...

Aidan listened.
