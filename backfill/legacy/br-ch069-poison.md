That night...

Azrath and Cassian sat across from each other.

A chessboard rested between them.

Neither touched the pieces.

Azrath finally spoke.

"Do you know why poison is so effective?"

Cassian shrugged.

"It kills."

Azrath smiled.

"No."

"It spreads."

He gently moved one black chess piece forward.

"Nobody fears the first drop."

"They fear..."

"...what it becomes."

He looked directly at Cassian.

"I don't intend to destroy Aidan."

"I intend..."

"...to let his own world destroy him."

Cassian finally understood.

This wasn't a war.

It was an infection.
