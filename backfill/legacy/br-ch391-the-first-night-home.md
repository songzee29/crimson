House Darkheart slept peacefully.

For the first time in years...

There were no alarms.

No invading armies.

No burning cities.

The kingdom rested beneath a crimson moon.

Aidan stood alone on the highest balcony.

The wind carried thousands of heartbeats to him.

Children sleeping.

Guards changing shifts.

Blacksmiths working through the night.

This...

Was what he had fought for.

Not power.

The chance for others to live without fear.

---

Behind him...

Rez quietly walked onto the balcony.

He folded his arms against the stone railing.

"You still don't sleep much."

Aidan gave a faint smile.

"I sleep."

Rez raised an eyebrow.

"You meditate with your eyes closed."

"...That's different."

Rez laughed.

"Still stubborn."
