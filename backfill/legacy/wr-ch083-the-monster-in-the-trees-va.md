The forest belonged to him now.

Not because he ruled it.

Because nothing else dared breathe too loudly within it.

Aidan stood motionless atop a rain-soaked cliff.

His chest no longer rose.

His heart no longer beat.

Yet the world thundered around him.

Thousands.

No…

Tens of thousands.

Every living heartbeat echoed through the mountains.

Birds nesting inside hollow trees.

Foxes hunting beneath fallen leaves.

Villagers sleeping behind locked doors.

Children crying in distant cabins.

Each heartbeat carried its own rhythm.

Its own scent.

Its own promise.

His throat burned.

Not with pain.

With need.

The notifications vanished.

They required no explanation.

His body already understood them.

…

Miles away…

A hunter checked the edge of his trapline.

He paused.

The forest had become strangely quiet.

No birds.

No insects.

Only wind.

He slowly reached for his lantern.

Something watched him.

He knew it.

He couldn’t see it.

His pulse accelerated.

Aidan heard it instantly.

THUMP.

THUMP.

THUMP.

The sound drowned out everything else.

His body leaned forward.

One step.

Then stopped.

Another heartbeat interrupted it.

Smaller.

Closer.

A frightened child sleeping inside the nearby cabin.

His head turned.

Two heartbeats.

One old.

One young.

The hunger demanded both.

He disappeared.

The hunter looked up just in time to see crimson eyes between the trees.

“Monster…”

He stumbled backward into his own firewood.

By the time he stood…

Nothing remained.

Only silence.

He grabbed the child from the cabin and fled toward the nearest village without looking back.

By sunrise…

The story had already begun spreading.

Something with crimson eyes hunted the northern forests.

Something that moved without sound.

Something that listened to hearts.

The name came naturally.

The Crimson Monster.

…

Back at Blackwood Academy…

No one corrected the rumors.

Because no one knew how.
