Black Hollow.

Cassian followed the footprints.

Hours passed.

The trail ended beside an abandoned cabin.

Inside...

No bodies.

No blood.

Only silence.

In the corner...

A wooden cradle rocked gently.

Empty.

A tiny blanket remained folded inside.

Cassian slowly picked it up.

His hands shook.

Not because he knew the child.

Because...

He imagined the parents returning.

Finding nothing.

His jaw tightened.

For the first time...

Anger began replacing helplessness.

Whoever was responsible...

Wasn't just taking lives.

They were stealing futures.
