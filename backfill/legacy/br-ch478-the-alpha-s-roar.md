The battlefield shook.

Ash wolves poured from the ancient gate.

Hundreds.

Then thousands.

They weren't flesh.

They weren't spirits.

Every forgotten wolf...

Every forgotten hunter...

Every forgotten soul...

Turned into a weapon.

Yet...

He refused to move.

Ten enemies disappeared.

Another strike...

Twenty more.

Still...

The army kept growing.
