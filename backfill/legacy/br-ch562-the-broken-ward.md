Mara knelt beside the ashes.

She spread both hands over the ground.

Thousands of crimson runes spread through the royal gardens.

They searched every stone...

Every leaf...

Every grain of dirt.

Finally...

She gasped.

"I found it."

Everyone turned.

"What?"

Mara pointed toward the center of the garden.

"The wards weren't broken."

"They were..."

She swallowed.

"...Ignored."

Silence.

The Guardian frowned.

"Impossible."

"There are only three people alive who know how these wards were woven."

Lyra looked toward Mara.

"Who?"

"The one who cast them."

"The one who inherited them."

"And..."

"...someone who understands House Darkheart better than we do."
