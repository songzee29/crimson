Aidan walked until he stood directly before Cassian.

Only inches separated them.

"When I was human..."

"I wanted your death."

"When I became a vampire..."

"I wanted your suffering."

"When I became king..."

"I wanted justice."

He looked directly into Cassian's eyes.

"So hear my judgment."

The room became silent.

"You are exiled from House Darkheart."

"You will leave this kingdom before sunrise."

"You will not return..."

"...or the man who once called me brother."

Cassian couldn't speak.

He slowly fell to one knee.

Not because he was forced.

Because he had nothing left.

"I don't deserve mercy."

"I know."
