Black Hollow.

Cassian stood beside the river that flowed through the village.

Children splashed through the water.

His partner waved from across the bridge.

Everything should have made him happy.

Instead...

Every joyful moment felt...

Distant.

Like watching someone else's life.

His partner approached him.

"You've been somewhere else lately."

Cassian forced a smile.

"I'm just tired."

"No."

They gently touched his arm.

"You're disappearing."

Those words struck harder than any sword.

Because...

They were true.

The curse wasn't taking his memories.

It wasn't stealing his strength.

It was slowly separating him...

From the very life he had built.
