The Royal Guards stormed into the chamber.

Weapons drawn.

They stopped the moment they saw Cassian.

Every blade immediately turned toward him.

Kael stepped forward.

"My King..."

"Give the order."

Darius raised his shield.

"It ends tonight."

Mara's runes ignited around her hands.

The room waited.

Every eye turned toward Aidan.

Cassian slowly closed his eyes.

He did not resist.

"If you're going to do it..."

"...do it."

Aidan looked at the man who had killed him.

The man who had burned under the Darkheart Curse.

The man who had just killed his Queen.

Then he looked at Lyra.

She gently squeezed his hand.

Just once.

That was enough.

Aidan lowered his hand.

"Stand down."

Every guard froze.

Kael looked stunned.

"My King?"

"I said..."

"...stand down."

The swords lowered.

Not because they understood.

Because they trusted their king.

Cassian slowly opened his eyes.

"...Why?"

Aidan answered without anger.

"Because if I kill you today..."

"...the Darkheart Curse teaches nothing."

"It only creates another beginning."
