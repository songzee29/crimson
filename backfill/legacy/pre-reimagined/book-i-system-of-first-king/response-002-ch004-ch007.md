# 🩸 THE CRIMSON LEGACY 🩸
## Book I: *The System of the First King*
### Chapter 4 — The Bloodline Nobody Knew

Aiden didn't sleep.

How could he?

There was an immortal man floating upside down above his bed.

A glowing red screen kept appearing every few minutes.

And apparently he was descended from the most famous bloodline in existence.

Aiden sat on the edge of his mattress.

Staring at Rez.

Rez stared back.

Long silence.

Finally—

> "You're ugly when you think."

Aiden grabbed a pillow.

Threw it.

The pillow passed straight through Rez.

The immortal laughed.

> "Missed."

"You're not real."

> "Unfortunately for both of us..."
>
> "I am."

Aiden rubbed his face.

"No."

He pointed at the floating immortal.

"You said Mikaelson."

Rez nodded.

"THE Mikaelsons?"

> "There aren't many others."

Aiden stood up.

"But that's impossible."

Rez raised an eyebrow.

> "You think the talking immortal is the impossible part?"

Aiden ignored him.

"My parents weren't royalty."

> "Correct."

"They weren't powerful."

> "Also correct."

"They never even used magic."

Rez's smile faded slightly.

> "Most side branches didn't."

Aiden froze.

"...Side branches?"

For the first time...

Rez looked serious.

# Chapter 5 — Forgotten Blood

Rez snapped his fingers.

The room disappeared.

Suddenly Aiden stood inside a massive library.

Endless shelves stretched beyond sight.

Thousands of crimson books filled every wall.

Aiden looked around in amazement.

"Where are we?"

> "Inside the Codex."

Aiden slowly turned.

"...What?"

Rez spread his arms dramatically.

> "Welcome to the Crimson Archives."

The shelves began glowing.

Names appeared.

Thousands of them.

Mikaelsons.

Not kings.

Not rulers.

Just people.

Families.

Children.

Descendants.

Lives.

Aiden walked forward slowly.

One section suddenly lit up.

### VALE BLOODLINE

STATUS:
Forgotten

Aiden stopped breathing.

His last name.

Vale.

His family.

Rez sighed.

> "Thousands of years ago..."
>
> "One branch left the Crimson Kingdom."

Images appeared.

A young Mikaelson warrior.

A healer.

Their children.

Their grandchildren.

Generation after generation.

Living quietly.

Far away from power.

Far away from kings.

Far away from history.

Until eventually...

Nobody remembered them.

Except the Codex.

# Chapter 6 — The Truth About the Book

Aiden looked toward the endless shelves.

"So what is this thing really?"

Rez's expression changed.

For the first time since meeting him...

The sarcasm disappeared.

> "The Crimson Codex isn't a weapon."

Aiden frowned.

"But it gives powers."

> "Eventually."

Rez walked toward a massive sealed door at the center of the library.

Ancient symbols covered its surface.

A crown was engraved into the metal.

Zee's crown.

> "This book was created after the Dynasty ended."

Aiden listened carefully.

> "The family knew power alone wasn't enough."

Images appeared.

Zee.

Lucien.

Astranea.

Kaelis.

Rez.

The legends.

> "So they recorded everything."

More images.

Victories.

Failures.

Mistakes.

Lessons.

> "Every power."

Another image.

> "Every evolution."

Another.

> "Every sacrifice."

Another.

> "Every regret."

Aiden looked stunned.

"This whole thing is their history?"

Rez nodded.

> "And now..."
>
> "It's yours."

# Chapter 7 — Quest Failed

A notification suddenly appeared.

### WARNING

### QUEST DETECTED

Aiden jumped.

"What now?"

### SIDE QUEST

Stand Up For Yourself

Difficulty: Easy

Reward: Experience

Aiden blinked.

"...That's random."

Rez immediately started laughing.

> "No."
>
> "That's hilarious."

Before Aiden could ask why—

The apartment door burst open.

Three boys entered.

The same ones from yesterday.

The leader smirked.

> "Look who it is."

Aiden's stomach dropped.

The bullies.

Again.

They shoved him backward.

The leader laughed.

> "Still talking to yourself?"

Aiden froze.

Because Rez was standing right beside them.

Invisible.

Grinning.

> "Well?"
>
> "Quest's right there."

Aiden whispered:

"I'm gonna die."

> "Probably."

The screen flashed.

### QUEST ACTIVE

The boys stepped closer.

Aiden looked at them.

Then at Rez.

Then back at them.

His hands shook.

Every instinct told him to back down.

Like always.

Like he always had.

Then he remembered something.

His parents.

The book.

The bloodline.

The quest.

And for the first time in his life...

Aiden didn't move backward.

He stepped forward.

One step.

Then another.

The room fell silent.

The leader blinked.

Confused.

"What's your problem?"

Aiden swallowed hard.

His voice trembled.

But he said it anyway.

> "Leave."

The boys laughed.

But Aiden didn't look away.

For the first time...

He held his ground.

The leader shoved him hard.

Aiden hit the floor.

Pain exploded through his shoulder.

But something happened.

A notification appeared.

### QUEST COMPLETED

Stand Up For Yourself

Reward:
+10 Experience

Aiden blinked.

"...Wait."

Another notification appeared.

### LEVEL UP

Level 1 → Level 2

A pulse of crimson energy flowed through his body.

Tiny.

Weak.

Barely noticeable.

But it was there.

Rez's eyes widened.

Then he smiled.

For the first time.

Not sarcastically.

Proudly.

> "Look at that."
>
> "Maybe we're not doomed after all."

Outside...

Far beyond the city...

A pair of ancient eyes opened.

Watching.

Waiting.

Because someone had felt the Codex activate.

And they knew exactly what it meant.

A descendant had begun the Legacy.
