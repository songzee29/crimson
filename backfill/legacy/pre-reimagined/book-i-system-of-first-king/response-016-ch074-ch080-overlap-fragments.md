### Chapter 76 — The Guardian’s Whisper

Aiden pushed open the violet door, and a sudden hush fell over the world. The air inside the corridor was rich with magic, a soft hum like a distant heartbeat. At its end, a figure appeared—translucent, almost like a dream. She radiated violet light, and her voice was like a soft wind.

“I am Liora, Guardian of the Ashborn Bond,” she intoned, her eyes meeting Aiden’s. “You have passed the first trial, but your bond is still untested. To reach the second temple, you must confront the shadow within you.”

Aiden’s pulse quickened. The shadow—he’d felt it, that cold flicker of doubt, that voice whispering he wasn’t enough. But now, with Liora’s gaze upon him, he realized this wasn’t a curse—it was a trial of self.

“What must I do?” Aiden asked, his voice steady but soft.

Liora smiled faintly. “Close your eyes. Walk into the shadow, but don’t let it define you. Let the fear speak, and then show it you are stronger.”

Aiden took a breath and stepped forward, the light dimming as he crossed into a swirl of darkness. Inside, he saw a faceless figure—a younger version of himself, afraid, lost. The shadow raised a hand, and for a moment, Aiden hesitated.

But then, he raised his own hand—no longer a child, but a boy becoming something more. The shadow wavered, and with a final step, it dissolved, leaving Aiden standing in radiant light.

Liora reappeared, nodding. “You have chosen trust in yourself. Now, the second temple is within reach.”

Aiden exhaled slowly, feeling lighter than he had in years. He was no longer just a boy following a map—he was a bridge between the past and the future, and every step he took now was his own.
