### Chapter 36 — The Spell That Shouldn't Exist

The pebble exploded.

Again.

Aiden stared at the dust.

Then at the spellbook.

Then at Rez.

Rez stared back.

Long silence.

> "Well."

Aiden frowned.

> "Well what?"

> "At least it wasn't your face this time."

Aiden groaned.

Three days.

Three days of training.

Three days of failed spells.

Three days of explosions.

And somehow...

Everything kept blowing up.

# Chapter 37 — The Impossible Page

The Crimson Archives surrounded them.

Thousands of books stretched endlessly into the distance.

Most shelves glowed softly.

One shelf did not.

Aiden noticed it immediately.

An entire section hidden behind magical chains.

Black chains.

Ancient chains.

Chains covered in Mikaelson runes.

Aiden pointed.

> "What's that?"

Rez immediately looked away.

> "Nothing."

Aiden narrowed his eyes.

> "You're lying."

> "Correct."

Aiden stood.

> "What's behind the chains?"

Rez sighed.

A very long sigh.

The kind that said trouble was coming.

> "Forbidden records."

Aiden froze.

> "Forbidden?"

> "Meaning stay away."

Naturally...

Aiden walked toward them.

# Chapter 38 — The Lost Spell

The chains rattled.

Not violently.

Almost excitedly.

As Aiden approached.

Rez immediately noticed.

His expression changed.

> "Wait."

The chains began glowing.

Aiden stopped.

The entire shelf shook.

A single book slid free.

Falling directly into Aiden's hands.

Silence.

Rez looked horrified.

> "...No."

Aiden looked down.

The cover had no title.

Only one symbol.

A crimson crown.

The same crown carved onto Vault 001.

The same crown worn by Zee.

Aiden slowly opened the book.

The first page was blank.

The second page was blank.

The third page suddenly filled with words.

As if the book were writing itself.

### AUTHORIZED USER DETECTED

### BLOODLINE VERIFIED

### LEGACY SPELL AVAILABLE

Aiden blinked.

> "Legacy spell?"

Rez looked ready to have a heart attack.

> "Close it."

> "Why?"

> "Because that book shouldn't be opening."

> "Why?"

> "BECAUSE ZEE SEALED IT HIMSELF."

Silence.

Aiden slowly looked back down.

The next page turned.

A single spell appeared.

Only one.

### CRIMSON THREAD

Difficulty:
Unknown

Creator:
Zee Mikaelson

Status:
Incomplete

Aiden frowned.

> "What's Crimson Thread?"

Rez stared at the page.

Then quietly said:

> "A spell Zee never finished."

Stable.

Not monster.

Not spirit.

A guardian.

Ancient.

Powerful.

> "A descendant approaches."

Its armor covered in royal symbols.

Its sword taller than a man.

Like something was calling him.
