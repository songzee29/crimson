### Chapter 52 — The Ashborn’s Secret

The moment Aiden crossed the river, the world around him froze. The water shimmered and split, revealing a tunnel beneath the surface—glowing crimson, as if a heartbeat lay within. He plunged forward, following the tunnel’s pull until it opened into a vast chamber. Statues carved from obsidian lined the walls—kings of old, each bearing a crown of ash. At the far end, a pedestal rose from the ground, and atop it rested a single, silver key. It called to him.

Aiden reached out, and as his fingers grazed the key, the Codex surged, flooding his mind with fragmented memories. He saw the Ashborn king—a boy once like him—standing in a circle of fire. And in that moment, Aiden understood: the Ashborn weren’t just forgotten—they were the bridge between power and sacrifice.

### Chapter 53 — The Silver Key

The key was heavier than Aiden expected, and as he gripped it, the ground beneath him shifted. The obsidian statues began to crack, and the air filled with a low, humming vibration. Rez floated alongside him, whispering. “That key unlocks more than you know. It can open the first Ashborn spell—the one Zee never completed.”

Aiden took a deep breath and placed the key into a hidden slot in the pedestal. The chamber rumbled, and a door, invisible before, slid open behind the statues. It led into a spiral staircase, descending further into darkness. Aiden felt a strange mix of fear and purpose—this was his path, and he couldn’t turn back.

---

### Chapter 54 — The First Ashborn Spell

At the bottom of the staircase, the air was thick with anticipation. A circular platform rose from the floor, surrounded by runes that Aiden now recognized from his Codex lessons. He stepped onto the platform, and suddenly, the silver key dissolved in his hand, becoming a spark of pure energy. The Codex flared, and a new spell appeared in his mind—Ashfire.

The first Ashborn spell wasn’t just power; it was control over the essence of loss and rebirth. Aiden closed his eyes, feeling the flame inside him awaken. And as it did, a vision flashed: the Ashborn king standing tall, no longer a shadow, but a protector of a legacy waiting to be born.

### Chapter 55 — The Price of Power

As Aiden opened his eyes, he realized the Codex wasn’t done with him. The Ashfire spell had come with a cost—his mana core burned at the edges, and he felt a dizzying weakness pulling him down. Rez hovered nearby, concern creasing his brow. “Aiden, you need balance—too much power and you’ll lose yourself to the Codex.”

Aiden gritted his teeth. He knew it was true. This power was fragile—like a bridge between who he was and who he could become. He staggered back, determined to find a balance, to keep his feet on the ground while his power soared.
