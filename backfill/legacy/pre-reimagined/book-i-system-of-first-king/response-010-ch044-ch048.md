# 🩸 THE CRIMSON LEGACY 🩸
## Book I: *The System of the First King*
### Chapter 44 — The Echoes of Sacrifice

Darkness.

Then...

Voices.

Hundreds of them.

Thousands.

Whispering.

Calling.

Remembering.

Aiden floated through an endless crimson void.

His body felt weightless.

His thoughts felt distant.

And everywhere around him...

Ghostly figures stood watching.

Not ghosts.

Memories.

Fragments preserved by the Codex.

The history of the Crimson Bloodline.

Aiden slowly looked around.

Warriors.

Kings.

Queens.

Mages.

Hunters.

Generations of forgotten descendants.

All watching him.

Then the crowd suddenly parted.

Someone approached.

A tall figure.

Black armor.

Silver crown.

Ancient eyes.

Aiden's breath caught.

Even though he'd never met him...

He knew.

Zee Mikaelson.

The First King.

The legend himself.

Zee stopped several feet away.

Silent.

Studying him.

Aiden couldn't move.

Couldn't speak.

Couldn't even breathe.

The stories never prepared him for this.

Because the stories never captured what made Zee terrifying.

It wasn't his power.

It wasn't his immortality.

It wasn't the crown.

It was his presence.

The feeling that mountains would move if he asked them to.

That death itself would hesitate before taking someone he loved.

Zee finally spoke.

> "You're smaller than I expected."

Aiden blinked.

"...What?"

Zee smirked.

> "Rez always described future descendants differently."

Somewhere nearby—

> "HEY!"

Rez's voice echoed through the void.

Zee actually laughed.

A real laugh.

Aiden stared.

The legend laughing somehow felt stranger than the legend existing.

# Chapter 45 — The First King Speaks

The void shifted.

Suddenly they stood inside a massive throne room.

The Crimson Throne.

The original throne.

The one from the oldest records.

Aiden looked around in awe.

Zee sat upon the throne.

Not as a king.

As a man carrying a burden.

A very heavy burden.

For a moment...

He looked tired.

Ancient.

Lonely.

Then he looked back at Aiden.

> "Do you know why the Codex exists?"

Aiden shook his head.

Zee leaned forward.

> "Everyone assumes it's a collection of power."

Silence.

> "They're wrong."

The throne room vanished.

Images appeared around them.

Wars.

Battles.

Victories.

Funerals.

Loss.

More loss.

Then even more.

Aiden watched countless people disappear from history.

Friends.

Family.

Children.

People Zee had loved.

People he couldn't save.

The First King's eyes darkened.

> "Power wasn't enough."

The words carried centuries of pain.

> "No matter how strong I became..."
>
> "Someone still died."

Silence.

> "So I built the Codex."

The images changed.

Showing countless descendants.

Each learning.

Each growing.

Each becoming stronger than the generation before.

> "The Codex was never meant to create another Zee."

Aiden froze.

That surprised him.

Zee smiled slightly.

> "One of me was already too much trouble."

# Chapter 46 — The Real Purpose

The throne room dissolved.

The void returned.

Aiden looked toward Zee.

> "Then what am I supposed to become?"

For the first time...

The First King didn't answer immediately.

He studied Aiden carefully.

As if measuring something.

Looking beyond flesh.

Beyond blood.

Beyond power.

Then finally—

> "Yourself."

Silence.

> "Not me."
>
> "Not my son."
>
> "Not the Dynasty."

The void trembled.

> "You are not a replacement."

Aiden felt something shift inside him.

Because for weeks...

The weight of Zee's legend had been crushing him.

Every quest.

Every challenge.

Every power.

All compared to someone impossible.

But now...

The impossible himself was telling him otherwise.

> "Legends are dangerous."

Zee stood.

The void shook around him.

> "People spend so much time trying to become them..."
>
> "That they forget to become themselves."

The entire void erupted with crimson light.

His mana expanded.

Doubled.

Then doubled again.

Then stabilized.

Aiden gasped.

His connection to magic felt infinitely deeper.

More natural.

As if the world itself were speaking to him.

Zee smiled.

> "Better."

# Chapter 48 — The Enemy Behind the Curtain

Meanwhile...

Far beneath Old Crimson Territory...

The hooded figure watched everything.

Every trial.

Every reward.

Every conversation.

Then a crack appeared in their mask.

A small crack.

But enough.

A single crimson eye became visible.

The figure slowly stood.

For the first time in centuries.

> "So."
>
> "The boy met him."

Ancient power surged around the room.

The air shattered.

Reality bent.

The hooded figure looked toward Vault 001.

And smiled.

Not kindly.

Hungrily.

> "Good."

A second voice echoed from the shadows.

One that did not belong to the figure.

> "Are you certain?"

The hooded figure nodded.

> "Yes."

Silence.

Then they spoke a name.

A name even Rez had forgotten.

A name buried before the Dynasty began.

> "Wake the Ashborn."

Far away...

Deep beneath the oldest ruins in existence...

Something ancient opened its eyes.

And for the first time in ten thousand years...

It smiled.
