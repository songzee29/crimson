- **Current canon:** House Darkheart.
- **Historical alternate:** House Drakhar / Zee Drakhar / Lucien Drakhar appear in the early *Book II: The Forgotten Blood* continuity after the June 1 surname-change discussion.

## Earliest conception timeline

1. **May 28, 2026, 04:40 UTC — original Zee story begins.** In `Zee Aesthetic Vibes`, Zee asks for a start-to-finish story about Zee growing up as a human child in a toxic environment and becoming the first unkillable Original Tribrid. This is the origin of the larger saga, not yet the Aidan-led Legacy.
2. **May 28, 2026 — the Dynasty grows.** The same thread adds Zee's human boyfriend and son, Darez/Rez as best friend, family and friends, further evolutions, the next generation, and a conclusion followed by “To be continued.” It then moves into Book Two and screenplay/visual development.
3. **May 30, 2026, 15:36 UTC — “The Shadow of the First King.”** A next-generation book concept is proposed: descendants burdened by Zee's legend. This is the earliest recovered Legacy-like conception, before the Aidan/System premise.
4. **May 30, 2026, 15:40 UTC — explicit Legacy planning.** Zee asks how to create Crimson Legacy without undoing Crimson Dynasty and whether a new chat is needed.
5. **May 31, 2026, 02:40 UTC — Aidan/System premise.** Zee specifies a powerless young boy orphaned by war, left an old book containing the power of Zee and his son. The book is blood-locked to Zee's bloodline; Aidan does not initially know he belongs to a side branch. The System is private to Aidan, and only he can see/hear Rez.
6. **May 31, 2026, 03:03 UTC — full-book request.** Zee asks for the Crimson Legacy System book from start to finish. The thread then continues through Book I and Book II.
7. **June 1, 2026 — Book II surname reset.** Zee asks to change Zee's surname and directs the project toward an original universe, after which the *Forgotten Blood* branch uses Drakhar. This is a historical alternate; current canon is Darkheart.
