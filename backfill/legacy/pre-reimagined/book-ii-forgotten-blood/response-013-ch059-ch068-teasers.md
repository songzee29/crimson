## Chapter 59 — The Son Even Lucien Never Knew

The Hall of Kings trembled.

Not from Valthoros.

Not from war.

Not from the awakening of some ancient monster.

It trembled because a secret buried deeper than any other had finally been uncovered.

Kael Drakhar.

The firstborn son.

The lost heir.

The forgotten prince.

Aiden stood frozen before the gate while the message lingered in the air.

Alive.

Sealed.

Awakening.

The words repeated inside his mind.

Alive.

After all this time.

After all these centuries.

Alive.

Zee remained silent.

The old king's eyes never left the crack in the seal.

For the first time since Aiden had met him...

The legendary king looked broken.

Not weak.

Broken.

A father staring at a mistake he had carried for thousands of years.

The child's voice echoed again.

Soft.

Hopeful.

Painfully hopeful.

"Father..."

The sound nearly shattered the old king.

Aiden saw tears sliding down Zee's face.

Real tears.

The kind no warrior could stop.

The kind no king could hide.

The chains rattled.

The darkness beyond the gate moved.

And suddenly...

Aiden felt something.

Not evil.

Not malicious.

Lonely.

The loneliness hit him like a physical blow.

Thousands of years.

Alone.

Waiting.

Forgotten.

The feeling made his chest ache.

Then Kael laughed.

A small laugh.

A child's laugh.

And somehow...

That terrified Aiden more than anything else.

Because it sounded wrong.

Not fake.

Not evil.

Wrong.

Like someone who had forgotten how laughter worked.

## Chapter 60 — Outside The Gate

Far beyond the Hall of Kings...

The real world was falling apart.

The battle between Lucien and Valthoros continued across the heavens.

Entire storms exploded.

Mountains collapsed.

The oceans themselves seemed restless.

Every clash between them sent shockwaves across continents.

Yet Lucien was smiling.

Actually smiling.

After thousands of years imprisoned...

He was finally fighting again.

Not out of rage.

Not out of duty.

But because somebody had threatened his family.

And Lucien Drakhar took that personally.

A crimson slash carved across the sky.

Valthoros roared.

Darkness erupted from the wound.

Lucien laughed.

The sound echoed across the world.

"That's for my sister."

Another strike.

A mountain-sized scar appeared across the Devourer's body.

"That's for my father."

Another strike.

The heavens shattered.

"And that's for me."

Valthoros immediately retaliated.

A claw larger than kingdoms descended.

Lucien barely dodged.

The attack erased three mountain ranges from existence.

The prince stared at the destruction.

Then slowly looked back toward Valthoros.

His smile faded.

Just a little.

"Okay."

A pause.

"That one would've hurt."

## Chapter 61 — The Watcher Behind The Observer

Back inside the Hall of Kings...

The gate cracked further.

A thin line spread across the seal.

Then another.

Then another.

Every crack sent tremors through the city.

The ancestors had begun gathering.

Thousands of them.

Standing in the streets.

Watching.

Waiting.

Fear spreading through the Hall itself.

Aiden noticed something strange.

The ancestors weren't looking afraid of Kael.

They were looking afraid of what might follow him.

Zee noticed too.

His expression darkened.

Then another presence appeared.

Without warning.

Without sound.

Without explanation.

The Observer stood beside them.

Aiden nearly jumped.

The ancient being simply stared at the gate.

For once...

The Observer wasn't smiling.

That alone was alarming.

Zee slowly turned.

"You came."

The Observer nodded.

"I had to."

Silence followed.

Then Aiden asked the obvious question.

"What's behind that gate?"

The Observer's eyes never left the darkness.

For a long moment he said nothing.

Then he finally answered.

"The first door."

Aiden frowned.

"The first door to what?"

The Observer looked toward him.

And for the first time since meeting him...

Aiden saw fear.

Real fear.

"The place before creation."

The Hall fell silent.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody moved.

Then the Observer quietly added:

"The place where even gods refuse to look."

## Chapter 62 — The King's Sin

The crack widened.

The child's hand remained visible through the opening.

Still reaching.

Still waiting.

Still searching for the father he had lost.

Zee slowly approached the seal.

Every step felt heavy.

Every step felt painful.

Finally he stood directly before the gate.

And spoke.

"Kael."

Silence.

Then excitement.

Pure excitement.

The kind only a child could have.

"Father!"

The Hall shook.

The voice sounded happy.

Relieved.

Overjoyed.

And somehow...

That made everything worse.

Because Zee began crying.

Not loudly.

Not dramatically.

Quietly.

The tears of a father carrying guilt for thousands of years.

Aiden looked away.

It felt too personal.

Too painful.

Then Zee revealed the truth.

The truth nobody knew.

Not Lucien.

Not Seraphina.

Not even the Codex.

"When Kael was born..."

His voice trembled.

"The world began breaking."

The crack widened.

The darkness stirred.

"The stars disappeared."

Another crack.

"Reality weakened."

Another.

"Entire cities vanished."

The Hall shook violently.

Zee closed his eyes.

"I didn't seal him because I hated him."

A tear fell.

"I sealed him because I loved him."

Silence.

Painful silence.

Then the old king whispered:

"I couldn't find another way."

## Chapter 63 — The Crack In Reality

The seal exploded.

Not completely.

Just enough.

A hole appeared.

A small opening.

No larger than a doorway.

Darkness spilled through.

Not shadow.

Not magic.

Nothingness.

Pure nothingness.

Aiden stared.

The sight hurt.

Actually hurt.

His eyes watered.

His head pounded.

His soul screamed.

Because the darkness wasn't empty.

It was absence.

The absence of existence itself.

Then something moved within it.

Small footsteps.

Slow footsteps.

Approaching.

The Hall of Kings trembled.

The Observer took a step back.

Zee froze.

The ancestors stood silent.

Aiden's heart hammered against his chest.

Then—

A child emerged.

No older than ten.

Black hair.

Silver eyes.

A simple smile.

The smile of a boy finally seeing his father again.

"Father."

Zee's face crumpled.

The king fell to one knee.

Kael smiled wider.

And ran toward him.

For one beautiful moment...

Everything seemed normal.

A lost child returning home.

A father seeing his son again.

A family reunited.

Then Aiden noticed something.

The ground beneath Kael's feet wasn't touching him.

Reality was disappearing.

Every step erased the street.

Every step erased the city.

Every step erased existence itself.

And Kael didn't even realize he was doing it.
