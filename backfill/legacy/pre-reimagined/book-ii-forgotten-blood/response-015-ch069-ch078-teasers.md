## Chapter 69 — The Brothers of House Drakhar

Kael laughed.

The sound echoed throughout the Hall of Kings.

And reality stopped unraveling.

Not completely.

Not permanently.

But enough.

Enough for everyone to breathe again.

Enough for hope to survive.

Lucien stood with his hands in his pockets, looking far less concerned than someone facing the collapse of existence should have been.

Kael stared at him.

The little boy's silver eyes were wide.

Confused.

Curious.

Hopeful.

"You know me?"

Lucien smiled.

The smile was smaller this time.

Sadder.

"I should've."

Silence settled over the Hall.

The prince slowly walked forward.

Each step careful.

Measured.

As though approaching a frightened animal.

Or a wounded child.

Perhaps both.

The memories were still returning.

Thousands of them.

Years that had been stolen.

Years he should have shared with his brother.

Years he would never get back.

Lucien stopped a few feet away.

For several moments neither spoke.

Then Kael quietly asked:

"Did I really have a brother?"

Lucien's heart shattered.

Aiden could see it happen.

The question wasn't dramatic.

Wasn't angry.

Wasn't accusatory.

It was genuine.

The boy truly didn't know.

Thousands of years trapped behind a seal.

Thousands of years forgotten.

Thousands of years alone.

Eventually even memories fade.

Lucien knelt down.

Until they were eye level.

And smiled.

"Yeah."

His voice cracked.

"Actually..."

He laughed softly.

"You had the coolest brother in the family."

Kael giggled.

Seraphina immediately rolled her eyes.

"Absolutely not."

Lucien pointed at her.

"See? That's your sister."

Kael blinked.

"Sister?"

Seraphina's expression softened instantly.

The next second she was kneeling beside them.

"Hi."

The little boy looked between them.

His eyes slowly widened.

Family.

For the first time in thousands of years...

Family.

Then he started crying.

## Chapter 70 — Aiden's New Path

The Hall of Kings remained unstable.

The First Door remained open.

The thing beyond it remained awake.

Yet for the first time...

Nobody focused on the danger.

They focused on Kael.

Because they finally understood something.

Kael wasn't the threat.

The threat was the power attached to him.

The burden forced upon him.

The same lesson they learned with Lucien.

The same lesson they learned with Seraphina.

The same lesson they learned with Aiden.

Power wasn't the enemy.

Isolation was.

The Codex appeared before Aiden.

Golden pages turning slowly.

Then a new notification emerged.

### UNIQUE CLASS EVOLUTION AVAILABLE

Current Class:
Crimson Witch

Current Title:
The Crimson Heir

Evolution Path Available:
???

Aiden frowned.

The screen flickered.

Then Zee's handwriting appeared.

### Build Something New

The message lingered.

Then vanished.

Aiden suddenly understood.

The Codex wasn't asking him to become another Zee.

It wasn't asking him to become another Lucien.

It wasn't asking him to become another king.

It was asking him to become the first Aiden.

Something original.

Something new.

Something the bloodline had never seen before.

The realization felt strangely liberating.

For so long he had been chasing legends.

Now he finally understood.

Legends weren't meant to be copied.

They were meant to inspire.

## Chapter 71 — The Thing Beyond The First Door

The peace didn't last.

It never did.

The darkness beyond the First Door moved.

Slowly.

Deliberately.

Watching.

Waiting.

Learning.

Aiden felt it before he saw it.

A pressure.

An intelligence.

An awareness older than existence itself.

The eye beyond the Door opened wider.

The sight nearly broke reality.

Entire sections of the Hall began trembling.

The ancestors looked away immediately.

Even the Observer lowered his gaze.

Only Kael continued staring.

And somehow...

The thing stared back.

The silence felt endless.

Then a voice emerged.

Not loud.

Not terrifying.

Not angry.

A calm voice.

A curious voice.

A voice that somehow felt worse.

> "Little one."

Kael froze.

The darkness shifted.

> "You finally found them."

Aiden's blood turned cold.

Because the being wasn't talking to everyone.

It was talking to Kael.

Specifically.

Personally.

Like it knew him.

Like it had always known him.

The Observer immediately stepped forward.

Golden power erupted around him.

"Do not answer."

The ancient being's voice remained calm.

> "You still hide behind borrowed light."

The Observer's face darkened.

Aiden had never seen him angry before.

Now he looked furious.

The thing beyond the Door laughed softly.

And the sound made entire realities shudder.

## Chapter 72 — The Inheritance Of Choice

Kael stood between two worlds.

Behind him stood family.

Ahead of him stood the First Door.

Behind him stood hope.

Ahead of him stood something ancient.

Something impossible.

The little boy looked terrified.

Not because he wanted power.

Not because he wanted greatness.

Because he wanted none of it.

He just wanted a family.

A normal family.

A father.

A brother.

A sister.

Something life had stolen from him.

Then Zee walked forward.

The Hall became silent.

The First King stood before his son.

Before the child he failed.

Before the child he loved.

For several moments he said nothing.

Then he knelt.

Just like Lucien had.

Just like Seraphina had.

Not as a king.

As a father.

And finally said the words he'd waited thousands of years to say.

"I'm sorry."

Kael froze.

Tears filled Zee's eyes.

"I was afraid."

The Hall trembled.

"I thought I was protecting everyone."

Another tear fell.

"But I wasn't protecting you."

Silence.

Painful silence.

Then Kael ran forward.

And hugged him.

The Hall of Kings erupted with light.

Not power.

Not magic.

Something stronger.

Forgiveness.

## Chapter 73 — The Crimson Family Reunites

The moment Kael forgave him...

Everything changed.

The darkness stopped spreading.

The First Door stopped growing.

The Hall stabilized.

Reality healed.

Not completely.

But enough.

The Codex appeared once more.

Its pages glowing brighter than ever before.

### WORLD QUEST UPDATED

THE LOST SON

Status:
Complete

Reward:
Family Restored

The notification faded.

Then another appeared.

### BLOODLINE STATUS

House Drakhar

Restored

The Hall exploded into cheers.

Ancestors celebrated.

Warriors laughed.

Kings smiled.

Queens cried.

Generations of House Drakhar witnessed what none thought possible.

The family was whole again.

Lucien.

Seraphina.

Kael.

Aiden.

Together.

At last.

Then, naturally...

Lucien ruined the emotional moment.

He pointed toward Kael.

"So does this mean I'm still the favorite?"

Seraphina punched him.

Kael laughed.

Aiden laughed.

Even Zee laughed.

For a brief moment...

A family broken by destiny felt complete.

But beyond the Hall...

Beyond the Door...

Beyond creation itself...

Something was watching.

Patiently.

Quietly.

Waiting.

Because House Drakhar's story was far from over.

In truth...

It was only just beginning.
