## Chapter 48 — The Inheritance of the First King

The moment the notification appeared, the world froze.

Not physically.

Spiritually.

Aiden felt it.

Lucien felt it.

Seraphina felt it.

Even Valthoros felt it.

The name alone carried weight.

**Inheritance of Zee Drakhar.**

The First King's final legacy.

The hidden power he never passed to Lucien.

Never passed to Seraphina.

Never recorded in history.

Never mentioned in the Codex.

A power so dangerous that even after thousands of years it remained sealed.

Waiting.

Watching.

Choosing.

Far above them, Valthoros's golden eyes narrowed.

For the first time since his arrival...

The Devourer looked uneasy.

Lucien immediately noticed.

And that terrified him more than anything.

Because Lucien knew what it took to make Valthoros nervous.

Entire worlds had fallen before that creature.

Entire races had vanished.

Yet somehow...

The possibility of Zee's inheritance worried him.

Lucien slowly turned toward Aiden.

His silver eyes filled with concern.

"Whatever Father left behind..."

A pause.

"...don't rush to unlock it."

Aiden blinked.

Coming from anybody else, that warning would have been easy to ignore.

Coming from Lucien Drakhar?

It felt like prophecy.

---

Then the Codex activated.

A single page appeared.

Old.

Burned.

Damaged.

Unlike anything Aiden had seen before.

At the top of the page were six words.

Words written in Zee's own handwriting.

**To the heir who comes after me.**

Aiden's breath caught.

For the first time...

The message wasn't from the Codex.

It was from Zee himself.

---

## Chapter 49 — The Letter Beyond Time

The world disappeared.

Aiden found himself standing in a quiet room.

No throne.

No battlefield.

No kingdom.

Just a simple wooden table.

A fireplace.

A chair.

And a man.

Zee Drakhar.

Older than any version Aiden had seen before.

His hair carried streaks of silver.

Scars covered his hands.

His eyes looked tired.

Not weak.

Just tired.

The kind of tired earned after carrying the world for too long.

For several moments Zee simply sat there.

Staring into the fire.

Then he looked directly at Aiden.

As though he could actually see him.

And smiled.

"Well."

The king chuckled.

"If you're seeing this, then either everything worked..."

He paused.

"...or everything went horribly wrong."

Aiden laughed despite himself.

That sounded exactly like Zee.

The old king leaned back.

"I won't waste your time."

His expression became serious.

"The inheritance isn't a weapon."

Silence.

"It never was."

Aiden frowned.

Then what was it?

As if hearing the question, Zee answered.

"The greatest mistake I ever made was believing strength could solve everything."

The fire crackled.

"Strength wins battles."

Another pause.

"It doesn't save people."

For a moment sadness appeared in Zee's eyes.

Deep sadness.

Ancient sadness.

The sadness of someone remembering failures.

Then he looked back at Aiden.

"If Valthoros has returned..."

His jaw tightened.

"...then you're already carrying more than anyone should."

The king stood.

And pointed toward a distant doorway.

A doorway Aiden hadn't noticed before.

"Go there."

Aiden frowned.

"What's behind it?"

Zee smiled.

"The reason Valthoros fears House Drakhar."

---

## Chapter 50 — The Hall of Kings

The doorway opened.

Aiden stepped through.

And immediately stopped.

Because he wasn't standing in a room.

He was standing in a city.

A city made entirely from memories.

Thousands of people filled the streets.

Warriors.

Mages.

Kings.

Queens.

Hunters.

Scholars.

Generations of House Drakhar.

Every descendant.

Every ancestor.

Every life connected to the bloodline.

The sight nearly brought him to his knees.

Aiden stared in disbelief.

The city stretched beyond the horizon.

Impossible.

Beautiful.

Alive.

A voice spoke beside him.

"Welcome."

Aiden turned.

And nearly had a heart attack.

Zee was standing there.

Not the recording.

Not the memory.

Something else.

Something closer to a spirit.

A lingering imprint.

The king smiled.

"This is what Father never understood."

Aiden looked around.

"What is this place?"

Zee spread his arms.

"Our true inheritance."

The city brightened.

The people laughed.

Talked.

Lived.

Even though they were long gone.

And suddenly Aiden understood.

House Drakhar's greatest strength was never power.

Never magic.

Never evolution.

It was legacy.

Family.

Connection.

The shadow Valthoros consumed worlds.

The Drakhars preserved them.

Remembered them.

Carried them forward.

Two opposite forces.

Locked in eternal conflict.

---

## Chapter 51 — The Devourer's Fear

Outside the memory realm...

Valthoros roared.

The sky shattered.

The oceans trembled.

Entire continents felt it.

Because the Devourer had realized something.

Aiden wasn't unlocking power.

He was unlocking understanding.

And that was far more dangerous.

The golden eyes burned brighter.

The darkness around the world thickened.

Reality itself began weakening.

Lucien immediately drew his blade.

Seraphina prepared her magic.

Nyra activated every protective ward she knew.

The war was beginning.

Not tomorrow.

Not someday.

Now.

Then Valthoros spoke.

Its voice thundered across existence.

> "Enough."

The heavens cracked.

> "I will end the bloodline myself."

A massive claw emerged from the darkness above.

Larger than mountains.

Larger than kingdoms.

Descending toward the Hollow Throne.

Toward everyone.

Toward everything.

Aiden's family.

And for the first time...

Lucien looked excited.

---

## Chapter 52 — Like Father, Like Son

The claw descended.

Everyone prepared for impact.

Everyone except Lucien.

The prince looked up.

Cracked his neck.

Rolled his shoulders.

Then smiled.

The exact same smile Zee always wore before doing something completely insane.

Seraphina immediately groaned.

"Oh no."

Rez looked confused.

"What?"

Seraphina pointed.

"That's Father's smile."

Lucien launched himself into the sky.

The earth exploded beneath him.

Entire mountains shattered from the force.

A crimson shockwave raced across the continent.

And before anyone could react—

Lucien punched Valthoros's claw.

Silence.

Absolute silence.

Then—

The sky exploded.

The claw shattered into millions of pieces.

A shockwave crossed the ocean.

Clouds vanished.

Stars became visible.

The entire world witnessed it.

The son of Zee Drakhar had just punched an ancient cosmic horror.

And won.

Aiden stared.

Nyra stared.

Rez stared.

Finally Rez nodded.

"Yep."

"What?"

"Definitely Zee's kid."

---

## Chapter 53 — The Beginning of the End

High above the world, Lucien hovered in the storm.

His sword drawn.

His silver eyes blazing.

Below him stood Seraphina.

Below her stood Nyra.

Below her stood Aiden.

The last heirs of House Drakhar.

For the first time in thousands of years...

The bloodline stood together.

United.

Complete.

The Codex activated.

One final message appeared.

### BLOODLINE REUNIFICATION COMPLETE

### HOUSE DRAKHAR RESTORED

### NEW QUEST UNLOCKED

**THE LAST WAR**

Difficulty:
Impossible

Reward:
The Future

Failure:
Everything

The screen faded.

The storm intensified.

The darkness spread.

And somewhere within the Hall of Kings...

The spirit of Zee Drakhar watched his descendants stand together.

For the first time in centuries...

The First King smiled.

Because his family had survived.

And now...

The final chapter of their story was about to begin.
