# BOOK II: THE FORGOTTEN BLOOD

## Chapter 89 — The Meeting of Enemies

Nobody attacked.

That surprised Aiden more than anything.

Valthoros stood in the center of the forum while armies from a thousand realities stared at him with barely restrained hatred.

The Devourer looked completely relaxed.

Almost amused.

As though he were attending a family reunion instead of standing among people who had spent entire lifetimes trying to kill him.

The First Guardian's silver eyes burned with fury.

The Observer looked ready to throw him into another dimension.

Lucien looked like he was deciding whether punching him now would improve the meeting.

And somehow...

Valthoros seemed delighted by all of it.

The ancient destroyer slowly looked around the chamber.

Then smiled.

"Good turnout."

Nobody laughed.

Valthoros sighed dramatically.

"Tough crowd."

---

The throne figure finally stood.

The forum immediately fell silent.

Its gaze remained fixed on Valthoros.

"You shouldn't be here."

Valthoros shrugged.

"And yet."

The silence that followed felt dangerous.

Then the Devourer surprised everyone.

His smile disappeared.

For the first time since Aiden had met him...

He looked serious.

Truly serious.

Ancient.

Tired.

Concerned.

The change was so unexpected that even Lucien stopped glaring.

Then Valthoros spoke.

And the entire room froze.

> "Because if I wasn't here..."
>
> "You'd all already be dead."

## Chapter 90 — Why Valthoros Came Willingly

The forum exploded with outrage.

Ancient rulers shouted.

Reality Lords rose from their thrones.

Entire armies prepared for battle.

Only the throne figure remained calm.

It simply watched Valthoros.

Waiting.

Studying.

Listening.

Eventually the room quieted.

The throne figure spoke.

"Explain."

Valthoros crossed his arms.

Then looked toward Kael.

The child immediately became uncomfortable.

The Devourer's expression softened slightly.

Which was somehow terrifying.

"Because of him."

Every eye turned toward Kael.

Again.

The boy lowered his head.

Aiden immediately stepped beside him.

Lucien moved too.

So did Seraphina.

Without hesitation.

Without discussion.

Family.

The gesture did not go unnoticed.

---

Valthoros watched them.

Then slowly nodded.

"Good."

Aiden frowned.

"What does that mean?"

The Devourer sighed.

As though he were explaining something obvious.

"It means House Drakhar finally learned."

Nobody understood.

Then Valthoros looked toward the horizon beyond reality.

And for the first time...

Fear appeared in his golden eyes.

Fear.

Real fear.

The sight stunned everyone.

Because if Valthoros was afraid...

Something had gone horribly wrong.

The Devourer's voice became quiet.

> "The thing beyond the First Door..."
>
> "Isn't the oldest thing."

Silence.

Utter silence.

Then he spoke the words that changed everything.

> "Something is waking behind it."

## Chapter 91 — The Seven Ancient Thrones

The Golden City trembled.

Not from an attack.

Not from power.

From recognition.

The city itself reacted to Valthoros's warning.

Seven colossal pillars erupted from beneath the forum.

Each larger than worlds.

Each older than stars.

Each bearing an empty throne.

The throne figure slowly looked upward.

Its expression darkened.

"The Seven Thrones."

The Observer immediately stepped back.

As if seeing ghosts.

Ancient Ones throughout the forum became visibly uneasy.

Aiden noticed something strange.

Only six thrones remained empty.

One already had an occupant.

The throne figure.

---

The revelation struck him instantly.

The being wasn't merely ruler of the Golden City.

It was one of the Seven.

One of the oldest powers in existence.

One of the beings who existed before reality itself.

The throne figure slowly stood from its seat.

Golden light illuminated the forum.

Then ancient symbols appeared across the remaining thrones.

Names.

Forgotten names.

Buried names.

Dangerous names.

The First Guardian.

The Endless Witness.

The Keeper Eternal.

The Warden of Doors.

The Dreaming Flame.

The Final Silence.

Each name echoed through creation.

Each carried enough power to reshape worlds.

Yet one throne remained blank.

No name.

No title.

Nothing.

The empty throne at the center.

The largest of them all.

The oldest.

The one nobody seemed willing to acknowledge.

Aiden pointed toward it.

"What sits there?"

Nobody answered.

Which felt worse than any answer.

## Chapter 92 — The Secret Hidden in Zee's Blood

The Codex suddenly activated.

Without warning.

Without permission.

Without explanation.

Golden pages flooded the sky.

The book had not reacted this violently since the Hall of Kings was restored.

Aiden felt his heart race.

Because he knew what this meant.

Another secret.

Another piece of Zee's legacy.

Another truth hidden for thousands of years.

The pages turned.

Faster.

Faster.

Faster.

Then stopped.

A single image appeared.

Young Zee.

Standing before the Seven Thrones.

Aiden froze.

The forum froze.

Even Valthoros looked surprised.

---

The memory continued.

Young Zee stood alone.

Broken.

Exhausted.

Yet defiant.

The same way he always stood.

The same way House Drakhar always stood.

Before him sat six ancient beings.

The seventh throne remained empty.

Waiting.

Watching.

Silent.

Then the memory revealed the impossible.

One of the Seven stood.

And placed a hand upon Zee's chest.

Golden light entered him.

The ancient being spoke.

> "A fragment shall remain."

The memory shook.

> "A promise."
>
> "A safeguard."
>
> "A key."

The vision ended.

The Codex revealed new text.

### HIDDEN BLOODLINE TRAIT DISCOVERED

### THE SEVENTH MARK

Status:
Inherited

Current Bearer:
Aiden Drakhar

---

The forum fell silent.

Nobody moved.

Nobody breathed.

Then Valthoros slowly turned toward Aiden.

And muttered:

"Oh."

Aiden frowned.

"Oh what?"

The Devourer looked genuinely concerned.

Which was becoming a disturbing trend.

Then he answered.

"You are absolutely not supposed to have that."

## Chapter 93 — The Return of an Impossible Dragon

Before anyone could ask another question...

The horizon exploded.

Not metaphorically.

Actually exploded.

Stars scattered.

Dimensions bent.

Entire realities shifted aside.

Something enormous was approaching.

Something alive.

Something ancient.

The forum rushed toward the edge of the Golden City.

Everyone stared into the cosmic darkness.

And saw it.

A silhouette.

Larger than solar systems.

Larger than galaxies.

A creature moving through existence itself.

The thing drew closer.

Closer.

Closer.

Then golden eyes opened.

Thousands of them.

Running along a body that stretched across reality.

Scales made from constellations.

Wings forged from collapsing stars.

A roar echoed across creation.

And every Ancient One in existence froze.

The First Guardian immediately stood.

The throne figure's eyes widened.

Even Valthoros whispered a curse.

The impossible creature continued approaching.

Majestic.

Terrifying.

Ancient.

Then the Observer finally spoke.

His voice barely above a whisper.

"The Celestial Dragon."

Silence.

Then another whisper.

One carrying genuine disbelief.

"It returned."

---

The dragon lowered its colossal head.

Its countless golden eyes focused on one person.

Not Valthoros.

Not the Seven Thrones.

Not the Ancient Ones.

Aiden.

The Crimson Legacy.

The Seventh Mark.

The dragon stared at him for several seconds.

Then slowly spoke.

Its voice sounded like stars singing.

> "At last."
>
> "The Heir has arrived."

The Codex exploded with light.

The Seventh Mark began burning.

And somewhere deep beneath the Golden City...

The empty Seventh Throne awakened.
