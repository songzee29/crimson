## Chapter 79 — The Golden City Before Time

The road of living stars stretched endlessly before them.

Every step felt impossible.

Every breath felt strange.

Even the laws of reality seemed uncertain here.

Aiden could feel it.

The Codex could feel it.

The very blood inside his veins reacted to this place.

As though House Drakhar had been here before.

Long ago.

Before kingdoms.

Before civilizations.

Before history itself.

The massive golden city continued growing larger with every step.

Its towers pierced entire galaxies.

Its walls stretched beyond sight.

Its gates dwarfed worlds.

No kingdom Aiden had ever seen came remotely close to its scale.

Not even the greatest empires from the Hall of Kings.

Not even the legendary Crimson Dynasty.

This wasn't a city.

This was a civilization built by beings who viewed universes the way ordinary people viewed bricks.

Lucien stared upward.

Then upward some more.

Then even further upward.

Finally he whistled.

"Okay."

A pause.

"That's ridiculous."

For once nobody argued.

---

As they approached the gates, Aiden noticed something carved into the golden walls.

Names.

Millions of names.

Billions.

Entire bloodlines.

Entire species.

Entire civilizations.

All recorded.

All remembered.

The realization struck him immediately.

This place was doing exactly what House Drakhar believed in.

Preserving memory.

Preserving legacy.

Preserving existence itself.

Then he found a familiar name.

His breath caught.

**Zee Drakhar.**

The name was carved among countless others.

Nearby were more.

**Lucien Drakhar.**

**Seraphina Drakhar.**

Even his own.

**Aiden Drakhar.**

Yet he had never been here.

Which meant something terrifying.

The city knew them already.

## Chapter 80 — The Throne Beyond Creation

The gates opened.

Silently.

Slowly.

As though they had been expecting them.

Beyond lay a vast golden avenue leading toward the central throne.

The figure sitting there remained motionless.

Watching.

Waiting.

The closer they got...

The more wrong everything felt.

Aiden expected a god.

A monster.

An ancient cosmic being.

Instead...

The figure looked human.

Almost.

White robes flowed around the throne.

Silver eyes glowed softly.

Long black hair moved despite the absence of wind.

The being looked neither old nor young.

Neither male nor female.

Neither alive nor dead.

It simply existed.

And somehow...

That made it far more intimidating.

The Observer immediately stopped walking.

For the first time since Aiden met him...

The ancient watcher bowed.

Lucien stopped smiling.

Seraphina looked stunned.

Even Zee's spirit appeared shocked.

The figure slowly opened its eyes.

The universe seemed to pause.

Then the being spoke.

A calm voice.

A familiar voice.

One Aiden had heard before.

Somewhere.

Somehow.

> "Welcome back."

The words echoed through the city.

Aiden frowned.

"Back?"

The figure smiled.

A sad smile.

An ancient smile.

The smile of someone carrying endless memories.

Then it answered.

> "All souls eventually return here."

Silence.

Nobody knew what to say.

Then Lucien raised his hand.

"Quick question."

The being looked at him.

Lucien pointed.

"Are we dead?"

The figure blinked.

Then actually laughed.

A genuine laugh.

"No."

Lucien nodded.

"Good."

A pause.

"I had plans later."

## Chapter 81 — The Last Keeper's Secret

The figure's attention slowly shifted.

Toward Rez.

Immediately.

The entire city reacted.

The golden streets brightened.

The towers hummed.

The stars above stirred.

And for the first time...

Rez looked genuinely worried.

The being upon the throne stood.

Every light in the city brightened.

Then it spoke.

> "Keeper."

Rez blinked.

"Nope."

The figure smiled.

> "You always say that."

Silence.

Everyone turned toward Rez.

Rez slowly pointed at himself.

"Again..."

A pause.

"Who exactly are you talking to?"

The figure stepped down from the throne.

One step.

Then another.

Each movement sent ripples through existence itself.

Finally it stopped before him.

Its silver eyes filled with recognition.

With memory.

With affection.

As though greeting an old friend.

Then it gently placed a hand upon Rez's forehead.

Instantly—

The world exploded.

---

Memories flooded the city.

Ancient memories.

Impossible memories.

Memories older than stars.

Older than time.

Older than creation.

Everyone watched as visions filled the sky.

A young man standing before the Golden City.

A young man carrying a key made from living light.

A young man guarding the First Door.

The same man.

Again.

And again.

Across countless ages.

Different faces.

Different names.

Different lives.

Yet always the same soul.

Always the same purpose.

Always the same role.

The Last Keeper.

---

The vision ended.

Rez collapsed to one knee.

Breathing heavily.

Thousands of forgotten memories now burned within him.

Aiden stared.

Lucien stared.

Seraphina stared.

Then Rez slowly lifted his head.

His eyes glowing gold.

And whispered:

"I remember."

## Chapter 82 — Kael's True Purpose

The city fell silent.

The throne figure turned toward Kael.

The smile vanished.

Not from anger.

From sadness.

Deep sadness.

Ancient sadness.

The kind that comes from watching history repeat itself.

Kael looked nervous.

The little boy instinctively moved closer to Zee.

The figure watched him carefully.

Then quietly spoke.

> "You were never meant to exist."

The words struck like lightning.

Everyone froze.

Even Kael.

The figure immediately continued.

> "And yet..."

A faint smile appeared.

> "You are exactly what creation needed."

Confusion spread through the group.

The figure raised a hand.

A vision appeared above the city.

A vision of Valthoros.

Then another.

A greater darkness.

The thing beyond the First Door.

Then another.

An endless future of destruction.

Entire realities collapsing.

Entire timelines dying.

Entire universes ending.

Then the vision changed.

A single point of light appeared.

Small.

Fragile.

Yet growing stronger.

Kael.

The little boy stared at the vision.

The figure's voice echoed across the city.

> "You are not the end."

The vision brightened.

> "You are the correction."

The city glowed.

> "The universe created an answer."

Silence.

Then realization struck Aiden.

Kael wasn't an accident.

Kael wasn't a mistake.

Kael was a response.

A living answer to a problem older than creation itself.
