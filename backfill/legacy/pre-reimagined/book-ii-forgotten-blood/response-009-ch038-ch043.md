## Chapter 38 — The Choice Lucien Never Had

The scream echoed across Lucien's mind.

It wasn't the scream of a monster.

It wasn't the scream of a prince.

It was the scream of a man who was tired.

Tired of fighting.

Tired of carrying the burden.

Tired of being alone.

The sound shook Aiden more than any attack ever could.

Because for the first time...

He understood.

Lucien wasn't trapped because he failed.

Lucien was trapped because he succeeded.

Every single day.

For thousands of years.

The shadow watched him silently.

Waiting.

Patient.

Confident.

It believed Aiden would break.

Everyone eventually did.

That was the nature of fear.

The nature of sacrifice.

The nature of impossible choices.

But Aiden suddenly laughed.

The shadow frowned.

Aiden laughed harder.

"Wow."

The shadow narrowed its eyes.

"What is amusing?"

Aiden wiped blood from his lip.

"You really don't understand Zee."

Silence.

The shadow's expression darkened.

Aiden smiled.

"You think his answer would've been sacrifice."

The throne room trembled.

"You think he'd want me to throw my life away."

The shadow took a step forward.

Aiden took one too.

"And that's why you lost."

The creature froze.

For the first time...

It looked uncertain.

Because somewhere deep inside...

It knew he was right.

---

Aiden had spent months walking through Zee's memories.

Listening to stories.

Learning the truth.

Zee wasn't a king because he sacrificed everything.

Zee became a king because he protected everything.

There was a difference.

A massive difference.

And suddenly...

Aiden understood the lesson the Codex had been trying to teach him since the beginning.

There was always another way.

---

The quest screen appeared again.

### THE HEIR'S CHOICE

Option One:
Save Yourself

Option Two:
Save Lucien

Aiden looked at both.

Then raised his hand.

And tore the screen in half.

The entire memory realm froze.

The shadow stared.

"What did you do?"

Aiden grinned.

"Neither."

## Chapter 39 — The Third Option

The Codex erupted.

Golden pages filled the sky.

Ancient runes appeared everywhere.

The system trembled.

Not from fear.

From evolution.

From change.

From possibility.

The shadow stepped backward.

For the first time since meeting it...

It looked worried.

Because Aiden had done something no heir had ever done before.

He rejected the choices.

The throne room cracked apart.

The Codex pages turned faster and faster.

Thousands of years of Drakhar history unfolded around them.

Every king.

Every queen.

Every warrior.

Every sacrifice.

Every victory.

Every mistake.

Aiden saw them all.

Then he saw Zee.

Standing at the very beginning.

Watching.

Smiling.

The vision lasted only a second.

Yet it was enough.

Because suddenly Aiden realized something.

The seal wasn't built by one person.

It belonged to the bloodline.

And bloodlines could grow.

## Chapter 40 — Lucien's Prison

The memory realm shattered.

Aiden returned to reality.

Immediately.

Violently.

The moment his eyes opened, he was launched through a wall.

Lucien's fist had missed him by inches.

The resulting shockwave destroyed half the city.

Stone rained from the sky.

The Hollow Throne was dying.

Aiden climbed from the rubble.

Blood covered his face.

His ribs hurt.

Everything hurt.

Yet he was smiling.

Because he finally had a plan.

A terrible plan.

A dangerous plan.

A plan almost guaranteed to fail.

Which meant it was probably the right one.

Seraphina landed beside him.

"Please tell me you have something."

Aiden nodded.

"I do."

Her eyes widened.

"You found a way?"

Aiden hesitated.

"A partial way."

Seraphina sighed.

"That's usually the part that kills us."

---

Another roar erupted from Lucien.

The prince stood in the center of the kingdom.

Crimson energy swirling around him.

Entire storms obeying his emotions.

The shadow was winning.

Slowly.

Steadily.

Relentlessly.

But now Aiden understood the real battle.

This wasn't about defeating Lucien.

This wasn't about surviving Lucien.

This was about reaching him.

## Chapter 41 — Brother and Sister

Seraphina stepped forward.

Alone.

The storm immediately turned toward her.

Lucien's eyes burned crimson.

The ground shattered beneath his feet.

Yet she kept walking.

One step.

Then another.

Then another.

Until she stood directly before him.

The entire kingdom held its breath.

Aiden watched nervously.

Nyra watched nervously.

Even Rez looked nervous.

Which was saying something.

Lucien slowly raised his hand.

Power gathered around it.

Enough power to erase mountains.

Enough power to erase cities.

Enough power to erase her.

Yet Seraphina never stopped walking.

Tears filled her eyes.

"Lucien."

The prince froze.

Just for a moment.

A tiny moment.

But enough.

"You idiot."

Another pause.

A small one.

A human one.

"I looked for you."

The storm weakened.

Only slightly.

"I waited for you."

The crimson energy flickered.

Seraphina's voice broke.

"You left me alone."

Silence.

Then something impossible happened.

Lucien's hand began shaking.

The rage hesitated.

The shadow faltered.

And somewhere inside the monster...

The prince heard her.

## Chapter 42 — The Voice Behind the Storm

Inside Lucien's mind...

The black throne cracked.

The shadow stood abruptly.

"No."

The word echoed.

Angry.

Fearful.

Desperate.

Outside, Lucien fell to one knee.

The sky split apart.

The storms screamed.

Yet another voice emerged.

Weak.

Broken.

Familiar.

A voice Aiden had never heard.

But instantly recognized.

Lucien.

The real Lucien.

Not the prince.

Not the legend.

Just Lucien.

"Sera..."

The word barely escaped him.

Yet Seraphina immediately smiled.

Through tears.

Through fear.

Through centuries of separation.

She smiled.

Because her brother was still there.

Still fighting.

Still refusing to surrender.

And as the storm raged around them...

The Codex activated one final time.

Aiden stared at the screen.

Then at Lucien.

Then at the approaching darkness gathering above the kingdom.

Because something else was coming.

Something the Observer had warned about.

Something that had felt the awakening of House Drakhar.

Something ancient.

Something hungry.

And far beyond the clouds...

A pair of enormous golden eyes opened.

Watching.

Waiting.

Smiling.

Valthoros had finally noticed them.
