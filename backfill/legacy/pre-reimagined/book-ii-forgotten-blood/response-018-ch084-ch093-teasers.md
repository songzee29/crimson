# BOOK II: THE FORGOTTEN BLOOD

## Chapter 84 — The First Ancient Arrives

The darkness beyond the Golden City shifted.

Entire constellations moved aside.

Galaxies drifted from its path.

Even the living stars lining the celestial road retreated.

Whatever was approaching...

Reality itself was making room.

The Ancient Ones had awakened.

And now one of them was coming.

Aiden stood atop one of the city's golden towers beside Lucien, Seraphina, Kael, Rez, and the throne figure.

Nobody spoke.

Nobody joked.

Not even Lucien.

The approaching presence was too immense.

Too old.

Too powerful.

Then it appeared.

Far beyond the horizon.

A silhouette.

Humanoid.

Walking.

Each step crossed millions of miles.

Each movement caused universes to ripple.

Entire dimensions folded around its body.

The being was so large that worlds orbited around it like drifting dust.

Kael stared silently.

The Ancient One stopped.

Its eyes opened.

Silver.

The exact same color as House Drakhar.

The throne figure immediately frowned.

"Impossible."

The Observer appeared beside them.

For the first time...

He looked terrified.

"No."

The word escaped him.

"No."

Aiden turned.

"What is it?"

The Observer slowly answered.

"The First Guardian."

Silence followed.

Then the Ancient One spoke.

Its voice echoed through creation.

Through memory.

Through time itself.

> "Who awakened the Child of Correction?"

The question shook every realm.

And every eye turned toward Kael.

## Chapter 85 — The Keeper's Forgotten Weapon

Rez remembered more every hour.

More names.

More lives.

More centuries.

Fragments of forgotten existence flooded his mind.

The Last Keeper.

The title finally made sense.

Not a soldier.

Not a king.

Not a god.

A Keeper.

Someone whose duty was preserving balance between realities.

Protecting the Doors.

Maintaining the boundaries.

Preventing catastrophe.

The problem was...

He remembered something else too.

Something far worse.

A weapon.

---

Deep beneath the Golden City existed a vault.

A place sealed before creation began.

A place only the Keepers could access.

The throne figure led Rez there personally.

The rest followed close behind.

The deeper they traveled...

The older everything became.

The architecture changed.

The air felt heavier.

Even the Codex seemed nervous.

Finally they reached a massive chamber.

A single pedestal stood at its center.

Empty.

Almost.

Floating above the pedestal was a shard of crimson crystal.

Small.

Beautiful.

Terrifying.

Aiden frowned.

"That's the weapon?"

Rez nodded slowly.

His face pale.

"Part of it."

The chamber shook.

Lucien crossed his arms.

"What happened to the rest?"

Rez swallowed.

Then answered.

"It shattered."

Silence.

Aiden already hated where this was going.

Rez continued.

"The fragments were scattered across existence."

Another pause.

"And if someone reunites them..."

The entire vault darkened.

"...they can rewrite reality."

---

## Chapter 86 — Kael Meets His Destiny

Later that night...

If "night" even existed beyond creation.

Kael sat alone at the edge of the Golden City.

Watching living stars drift across endless darkness.

For the first time since being freed...

He wasn't scared.

Just thoughtful.

A small figure appeared beside him.

The throne figure.

Neither spoke immediately.

Eventually Kael asked:

"Am I dangerous?"

The question hurt.

Not because of how it sounded.

Because it came from a child.

A child who should have been worrying about games.

Friends.

Adventures.

Not whether his existence endangered reality.

The throne figure sat beside him.

Then quietly answered:

"Yes."

Kael lowered his head.

The figure continued.

"So am I."

The child blinked.

The figure smiled.

"So is Lucien."

A pause.

"So is Seraphina."

Another pause.

"So is Aiden."

Kael looked confused.

The figure laughed softly.

"Power isn't what defines you."

The stars glowed brighter.

"Your choices do."

The child sat silently.

Thinking.

Learning.

Growing.

And somewhere deep within reality...

Ancient forces took notice.

Because the Child of Correction had begun choosing his own path.

---

## Chapter 87 — The Crimson Legacy Army

The warning came three days later.

Ancient Ones were moving.

Multiple.

Not one.

Not two.

Hundreds.

The cosmic balance was shifting.

The Age of Awakening had begun.

And House Drakhar could not face it alone.

---

The Hall of Kings answered first.

Ancestors emerged from memory.

Not as ghosts.

Not as spirits.

As living echoes.

Warriors.

Mages.

Hunters.

Kings.

Queens.

Legends.

Thousands of them.

Then other realms responded.

Bloodlines long connected to House Drakhar arrived.

Forgotten kingdoms.

Lost civilizations.

Ancient allies.

Entire armies crossed reality to answer the call.

The Golden City became crowded.

Alive.

Prepared.

For the first time since the Crimson Dynasty...

House Drakhar wasn't standing alone.

---

Aiden watched everything from a balcony overlooking the city.

The scale was overwhelming.

Then Lucien appeared beside him.

Looking unusually serious.

"You're thinking too much."

Aiden rolled his eyes.

"You don't think enough."

"Fair."

The prince looked toward the gathered armies.

Then smiled.

"This is starting to feel like Father used to describe."

Aiden glanced toward him.

"The old wars?"

Lucien nodded.

The smile faded.

"Except bigger."

Aiden looked toward the horizon.

Toward the approaching darkness.

Toward the Ancient Ones.

Toward the future.

And somehow...

He knew Lucien was right.

---

## Chapter 88 — The Beginning of the Cosmic Age

The first battle never came.

Not immediately.

Instead...

A meeting was called.

A gathering unlike anything existence had ever seen.

Ancient Ones.

Keepers.

Observers.

Bloodlines.

Kings.

Reality itself seemed to pause.

Waiting.

Watching.

Listening.

The Golden City opened its central forum.

And one by one...

The powers of creation arrived.

The First Guardian.

The Throne Figure.

The Observer.

The Last Keeper.

The Crimson Legacy.

House Drakhar.

All seated together.

For the first time.

---

Then the final guest arrived.

Late.

Uninvited.

And completely unconcerned about either fact.

The sky split open.

Darkness poured into the city.

The stars dimmed.

The armies reached for their weapons.

Ancient Ones rose from their seats.

And through the darkness walked a familiar silhouette.

Tall.

Ancient.

Smiling.

Golden eyes glowing.

Valthoros.

The Devourer.

The destroyer of worlds.

The enemy of ages.

He looked around the gathering.

Then grinned.

"Wow."

Silence.

The Devourer placed his hands in his pockets.

"Everybody I hate is here."

Even Lucien couldn't help laughing.

The First Guardian looked furious.

The Observer looked exhausted.

The throne figure closed its eyes.

As if developing a headache.

And Valthoros simply smiled wider.

Because the true story was only beginning.

The wars of kings were over.

The wars of worlds were over.

Now...

The Cosmic Age had begun.
