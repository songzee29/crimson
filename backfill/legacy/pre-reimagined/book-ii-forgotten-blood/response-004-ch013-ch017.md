## Chapter 13 — The First Daughter of House Drakhar

The explosion threw Aiden, Nyra, and Rez backward.

Stone shattered.

Ancient pillars collapsed.

A storm of crimson energy erupted from the broken seal and tore through the underground chamber.

For several seconds nobody could see.

Nobody could hear.

The world became nothing but light.

Then suddenly—

Silence.

The dust slowly settled.

The crimson energy faded.

And standing where the seal once existed was a woman.

She appeared no older than twenty-five.

Long black hair fell past her waist.

A crimson crown rested upon her head.

Silver eyes glowed softly within the darkness.

She wore black armor covered in ancient Drakhar symbols that no longer existed anywhere else in the world.

Power radiated from her.

Not wild power.

Not violent power.

Controlled power.

The kind that came from someone who had mastered themselves completely.

Aiden immediately understood something terrifying.

This woman wasn't powerful because of what she could do.

She was powerful because she no longer needed to prove it.

Her gaze slowly moved across the room.

First to Nyra.

Then to Rez.

Finally—

To Aiden.

The moment her eyes met his, time seemed to stop.

She stared at him.

Studied him.

Examined every part of him.

Then she smiled.

A sad smile.

A familiar smile.

The same smile Zee had worn in countless memories.

"My father would have liked you."

The words hit Aiden harder than any attack.

Because for the first time...

Someone who had actually known Zee was standing before him.

Not a memory.

Not a vision.

Not a recording.

A real person.

A member of his family.

## Chapter 14 — The Weight of Time

For a long moment nobody spoke.

The chamber remained silent except for falling debris.

Seraphina slowly walked forward.

Each step echoed throughout the ruins.

Aiden expected the ground to crack beneath her power.

Expected the walls to shake.

Expected something dramatic.

Nothing happened.

And somehow that made her even more intimidating.

Thousands of years imprisoned.

Thousands of years forgotten.

Yet she moved as calmly as someone taking a walk through a garden.

Her eyes never left Aiden.

"You carry Father's blood."

It wasn't a question.

Aiden nodded.

"Yeah."

A strange emotion appeared in her eyes.

Relief.

Pain.

Longing.

All at once.

"I wondered if any of us survived."

The sentence broke something inside the room.

Because suddenly she wasn't an ancient legend.

She was family.

Family who had spent thousands of years believing everyone she loved was gone.

Nyra stepped forward carefully.

"You really are Seraphina Drakhar?"

Seraphina looked toward her.

Then smiled.

"You have the eyes of my mother's line."

Nyra froze.

Seraphina's expression softened.

"The Forgotten Branch."

A pause.

"They survived."

Nyra immediately began crying.

Years of fear.

Years of loss.

Years of believing she was alone.

Gone.

In a single moment.

For the first time in her life...

She had found someone who understood.

Someone who belonged.

Someone who remembered.

## Chapter 15 — What Happened to Zee

Hours later they sat within the ruins of the ancient throne room.

A fire burned nearby.

Nobody wanted to sleep.

Not when they finally had answers.

Aiden sat across from Seraphina.

His heart pounded.

There was one question he had wanted answered since the day he opened the Codex.

One question bigger than all the others.

He finally asked it.

"What was Zee really like?"

The fire crackled.

Seraphina stared into the flames.

And smiled.

Not at the legend.

Not at the king.

At the memory.

"He was annoying."

Aiden blinked.

Rez laughed.

Nyra looked confused.

Seraphina rolled her eyes.

"He thought every problem could be solved by punching it."

Rez immediately nodded.

"That sounds exactly right."

Aiden laughed.

For the next several hours Seraphina told stories.

Not about King Zee.

About Father Zee.

The man who stayed awake all night when his children were sick.

The man who secretly burned food when he tried cooking.

The man who pretended to be fearless while worrying constantly about the people he loved.

Aiden listened to every word.

Every story.

Every memory.

Because suddenly the impossible happened.

The legend became human.

The king became real.

And for the first time...

Aiden felt connected to him.

Not through power.

Not through blood.

Through family.

Then Seraphina's smile faded.

The mood shifted instantly.

Because she remembered something.

Something terrible.

Something she had tried very hard to forget.

"The day I was sealed..."

Silence filled the room.

Seraphina slowly looked toward Aiden.

"The Observer came."

Everything stopped.

## Chapter 16 — The Enemy Before History

Nobody spoke.

Even Rez looked stunned.

"The Observer?" Aiden asked.

Seraphina nodded.

Her expression darkened.

"The world thinks the Purifiers were our greatest enemy."

A bitter laugh escaped her lips.

"They were soldiers."

The fire suddenly felt colder.

"They served something much older."

Aiden's pulse quickened.

"The Observer."

Seraphina nodded again.

"The Observer existed before House Drakhar."

A pause.

"Before the Ashborn."

Another pause.

"Before the kingdoms."

Silence consumed the throne room.

The implications were horrifying.

Seraphina leaned forward.

For the first time all night...

Fear appeared in her eyes.

Real fear.

Ancient fear.

"The Observer doesn't fear power."

She looked directly at Aiden.

"It fears potential."

The words lingered.

Heavy.

Dangerous.

Then she continued.

"It watched Father."

"It watched Lucien."

"It watched me."

Her gaze hardened.

"And now..."

She looked directly into Aiden's eyes.

"It's watching you."

The Codex suddenly activated.

Not with a quest.

Not with a reward.

Not with a warning.

A single line appeared.

One sentence.

One message.

One impossible message.

### OBSERVER CONTACT REQUEST ACCEPTED

The room went silent.

Nobody breathed.

Because underneath the message...

A second line appeared.

### THE OBSERVER WISHES TO SPEAK WITH THE HEIR

And somewhere far beyond the stars...

Something ancient smiled.
