# BOOK II: THE FORGOTTEN BLOOD

## Chapter 64 — The Boy Who Unmade Reality

Kael ran toward his father.

Smiling.

Laughing.

Happy.

For the first time in thousands of years.

And the universe began dying beneath his feet.

Entire sections of the Hall of Kings vanished.

Buildings disappeared.

Streets unraveled.

Memories were erased.

Not destroyed.

Erased.

As if they had never existed.

Aiden watched in horror as one of the distant towers simply ceased to be.

No explosion.

No collapse.

No sound.

One moment it existed.

The next moment it didn't.

The space it occupied became empty.

Reality itself had been removed.

The Observer immediately moved.

For the first time since Aiden met him, he reacted without hesitation.

Golden symbols erupted around the Hall.

Ancient barriers formed.

Thousands of glowing chains appeared from the sky.

Attempting to contain the spreading nothingness.

The effort lasted exactly three seconds.

Then Kael stepped forward again.

The chains vanished.

The barriers vanished.

The symbols vanished.

Everything simply stopped existing.

The Observer's eyes widened.

"He has become stronger."

Aiden looked toward him.

"Stronger than what?"

The Observer remained silent.

Which was answer enough.

---

Meanwhile, Kael finally reached Zee.

The child threw his arms around his father.

The old king embraced him immediately.

Tightly.

Desperately.

As though he were afraid letting go would cause him to disappear again.

For several moments neither spoke.

Father and son remained together.

Thousands of years of separation.

Thousands of years of loneliness.

Thousands of years of regret.

Gone.

At least for a moment.

Then Kael quietly asked:

"Did I do something bad again?"

The Hall fell silent.

Zee's face broke.

Because the child genuinely didn't know.

## Chapter 65 — The First Door Opens

The darkness behind the seal expanded.

Slowly.

Relentlessly.

The crack in reality widened until it stretched across the horizon.

And for the first time...

Everyone saw what lay beyond.

Nobody was prepared.

Not Aiden.

Not the Observer.

Not even Zee.

Beyond the First Door was not a place.

It was the absence of places.

The absence of existence.

The absence of creation itself.

No stars.

No planets.

No dimensions.

Nothing.

Absolute nothing.

Yet something lived there.

Something ancient.

Something sleeping.

Aiden felt it immediately.

A presence.

Far larger than Valthoros.

Far older than the Observer.

Something so vast that merely sensing it made his soul tremble.

The Observer slowly stepped backward.

"No."

The word escaped him before he could stop it.

"No."

Aiden stared.

"What is it?"

The Observer's face had gone pale.

"It isn't supposed to be awake."

The darkness shifted.

Far beyond the First Door...

A single eye opened.

The Hall of Kings shook violently.

Every ancestor dropped to one knee.

Every memory trembled.

Every light flickered.

The eye looked toward Kael.

And smiled.

## Chapter 66 — Lucien Learns He Has A Brother

Outside the Hall of Kings...

The battle stopped.

Completely.

Lucien froze in midair.

Valthoros froze.

The oceans froze.

Even the storm clouds stopped moving.

Everyone felt it.

The awakening.

The First Door.

The thing beyond creation.

Lucien's silver eyes narrowed.

His instincts screamed.

His blood screamed.

Something was wrong.

Something was terribly wrong.

Then suddenly—

A new memory appeared inside his mind.

A memory that wasn't his.

A little boy laughing.

A little boy with silver eyes.

A little boy sitting on Zee's shoulders.

Lucien froze.

"What?"

More memories appeared.

A birthday.

A family dinner.

A garden.

A brother.

The prince nearly dropped his sword.

Impossible.

He would remember.

Wouldn't he?

Wouldn't he?

The memories continued flooding in.

Hidden memories.

Sealed memories.

Memories Zee himself had buried.

Lucien saw Kael.

Saw his smile.

Saw his power.

Saw the fear.

Saw the day everything changed.

Saw the day Kael disappeared.

Saw his own memories altered.

Saw himself forget.

The realization struck like lightning.

His brother hadn't died.

His brother had been erased.

From history.

From records.

From memory.

From him.

Lucien slowly lowered his sword.

A rare emotion crossed his face.

Shock.

Pure shock.

Then another followed.

Anger.

Not at Kael.

Not at Zee.

At fate itself.

Because somewhere out there...

His little brother had spent thousands of years alone.

## Chapter 67 — The Return of the Forgotten Prince

The Hall trembled again.

Kael slowly released his father.

The child turned toward Aiden.

Toward the Observer.

Toward the ancestors.

His silver eyes were innocent.

Curious.

Almost normal.

Yet reality continued disappearing around him.

Entire streets vanished.

Buildings unraveled.

The city slowly died.

And Kael finally noticed.

The smile left his face.

His eyes widened.

"No..."

The word barely escaped him.

The ground beneath him vanished.

Another section of the Hall disappeared.

Kael stepped backward.

Terrified.

"No."

More reality disappeared.

His breathing quickened.

His hands trembled.

"No no no no..."

Zee immediately stood.

"Kael."

The boy looked at him.

Tears filled his eyes.

The sight shattered Aiden's heart.

Because Kael wasn't evil.

Wasn't malicious.

Wasn't trying to hurt anyone.

He was scared.

Just scared.

A frightened child carrying power no one should possess.

Then Kael whispered:

"I can't stop it."

The Hall shook violently.

And for the first time...

Aiden realized the true tragedy.

Kael wasn't trapped by the seal.

He was trapped by himself.

## Chapter 68 — The Choice That Could Save Kael... Or Doom The World

The Codex appeared.

Larger than ever before.

Golden pages filled the sky.

Thousands of notifications flashed.

Then all of them vanished.

Leaving only one.

### WORLD QUEST UPDATED

### THE LOST SON

Objective:
Save Kael Drakhar

Difficulty:
Unknown

Failure:
Reality Collapse

Reward:
???

The screen remained.

Then another message appeared.

A hidden message.

One written in Zee's handwriting.

### There Is Still A Way

Aiden's heart skipped.

The message continued.

### The Inheritance Was Never Power

### The Inheritance Was Never Strength

### The Inheritance Is Choice

Silence filled the Hall.

Then one final quest appeared.

### LEGENDARY QUEST

### BUILD WHAT I COULDN'T

Objective:
Create A New Path

The Codex vanished.

Aiden stood motionless.

The meaning slowly settled into place.

Zee had failed.

Not because he lacked strength.

Not because he lacked courage.

Because he couldn't find another answer.

Seal Kael.

Or destroy him.

Those were the only options he saw.

But Aiden belonged to a different generation.

A different era.

A different story.

The story wasn't asking him to repeat Zee's choices.

It was asking him to do better.

And somewhere behind them...

A familiar voice suddenly echoed through the Hall.

A voice carrying equal parts confidence and chaos.

A voice Aiden immediately recognized.

Lucien Drakhar.

The prince stepped through a portal of crimson fire.

His silver eyes landed on Kael.

Then widened.

For several seconds nobody spoke.

Nobody moved.

Then Lucien smiled.

A genuine smile.

And said the last thing anyone expected.

> "So you're the little brat who stole all the family height."

Kael blinked.

The Hall blinked.

Even the Observer looked confused.

Then, for the first time in thousands of years...

Kael laughed.

A real laugh.

And the spreading destruction stopped.

For one brief moment.

One precious moment.

The world held together.

And hope returned.
