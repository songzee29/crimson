# BOOK II: THE FORGOTTEN BLOOD

## Chapter 8 — The Hollow Throne

The Valley of Princes disappeared behind them as Aiden, Nyra, and Rez continued east.

For days, nobody spoke much.

The vision haunted Aiden.

Not because he had seen Zee Drakhar.

Not because he had seen Lucien.

But because he had seen them together.

For so long, Zee had existed in Aiden's mind as a legend.

A king.

A monster.

A god among mortals.

Yet the vision showed something else.

A father.

A man willing to stand beside his son until the end of the world.

The realization made the legends feel real.

Dangerously real.

By the tenth day, the mountains parted.

And there it was.

The Hollow Throne.

A kingdom of black stone buried inside a crater so large it looked as though something had fallen from the heavens and shattered the earth itself.

Ancient towers leaned at impossible angles.

Bridges hung broken between cliffs.

Entire sections of the city floated in midair.

Defying gravity.

Defying reason.

Defying time.

Nyra stopped walking.

Her face turned pale.

"I've seen drawings."

Aiden looked toward her.

"Of this place?"

She nodded.

"My family called it the Kingdom of the Exiled."

Rez suddenly froze.

Aiden immediately noticed.

"Rez?"

The spirit looked uneasy.

Very uneasy.

"The Exiled Kingdom..."

His voice dropped.

"I haven't heard that name in thousands of years."

As they descended into the ruins, Aiden felt the Codex becoming restless.

The pages inside his mind turned on their own.

Faster.

And faster.

Until finally—

### LEGACY LOCATION DISCOVERED

THE HOLLOW THRONE

### WARNING

SEALED ENTITY DETECTED

### WARNING

DO NOT WAKE THE KING

Aiden stared at the message.

Then looked at Rez.

Rez stared back.

Neither liked that warning.

## Chapter 9 — The Throne Room

Finding the throne room wasn't difficult.

The entire kingdom seemed built around it.

At the center of the crater stood a massive palace.

Black.

Ancient.

Silent.

The structure towered over everything around it.

Even ruined, it felt powerful.

Like the kingdom still obeyed it.

The enormous doors stood open.

Waiting.

Almost inviting.

Aiden hated that.

Ancient places that invited people inside rarely had good intentions.

Still, they entered.

The throne room stretched for what seemed like miles.

Rows of shattered pillars lined the path.

Ancient banners hung in tatters from the ceiling.

And at the very end—

The throne.

A massive seat carved from crimson crystal.

A throne fit for a king.

Yet it sat empty.

Or so it appeared.

The moment Aiden stepped closer, his newly awakened King's Insight activated on its own.

The world shifted.

And suddenly—

He saw someone sitting there.

A shadow.

A figure.

Watching.

The figure wore a crown.

Silver eyes glowed beneath the darkness.

Then it smiled.

Aiden jumped backward.

The vision vanished instantly.

The throne became empty once again.

Nyra looked alarmed.

"What happened?"

Aiden slowly shook his head.

"I don't know."

But deep down...

He knew exactly whose eyes he had seen.

## Chapter 10 — The Crimson Seal

Behind the throne they discovered a staircase.

One that descended far beneath the kingdom.

Far beneath the earth.

The deeper they traveled, the older everything became.

The stone changed.

The symbols changed.

Even the air felt different.

Then they found it.

A door.

Not a normal door.

A seal.

Thousands of crimson chains crossed its surface.

Each chain glowing with ancient magic.

Each one connected to a single symbol.

The Drakhar Crest.

A dragon formed from blood and shadow.

Nyra stared in disbelief.

"My family records mentioned this."

Aiden looked toward her.

"What is it?"

She swallowed.

"The Crimson Seal."

Silence followed.

Then—

"The prison of the first heir."

The words echoed through the chamber.

Even Rez looked stunned.

"The first heir?"

Nyra nodded.

"The one before Lucien."

Everything stopped.

Aiden's heart skipped.

"There was another son?"

"No."

Nyra's voice trembled.

"There was another heir."

## Chapter 11 — The Heir That History Erased

The Codex exploded with warnings.

### FORBIDDEN HISTORY DETECTED

### ACCESSING SEALED RECORDS

### HOUSE DRAKHAR ARCHIVES OPENING

Aiden fell to one knee as memories flooded his mind.

Ancient memories.

Older than anything he had seen before.

He saw Zee.

Not as a king.

Not as a ruler.

But as a young man.

A father.

Holding two children.

One was Lucien.

The other...

Was a girl.

A little girl with crimson eyes.

A girl laughing as Zee lifted her into the air.

A girl who looked frighteningly similar to Nyra.

The vision shifted.

Years passed.

The little girl grew older.

Stronger.

Smarter.

Powerful.

Too powerful.

Then came fear.

Not from enemies.

Not from monsters.

From the bloodline itself.

Because she evolved into something never seen before.

Something even Zee could not fully understand.

Something history eventually erased.

The memory ended.

Aiden gasped.

The Codex revealed the final words.

### FIRST HEIR IDENTIFIED

### NAME:

SERAPHINA DRAKHAR

### STATUS:

SEALED

### STATUS:

ALIVE

Silence consumed the chamber.

Nobody moved.

Nobody breathed.

Because one thought entered every mind at the same time.

If Lucien was still alive...

And Seraphina was still alive...

Then the Drakhar bloodline was far larger than anyone imagined.

And somewhere behind the Crimson Seal...

Something had just awakened.

## Chapter 12 — The Sound Behind the Door

The chamber trembled.

At first it was subtle.

A vibration beneath their feet.

Then came the sound.

A single metallic crack.

One of the crimson chains snapped.

Aiden's blood ran cold.

The seal had remained intact for thousands of years.

Yet now—

Another chain broke.

And another.

And another.

The entire chamber shook.

Dust rained from the ceiling.

Ancient runes flashed wildly across the walls.

The Codex immediately reacted.

### EMERGENCY EVENT

### CRIMSON SEAL DESTABILIZING

### SURVIVAL RECOMMENDED

Rez stared at the screen.

"That's a new one."

Aiden slowly drew Ashfire into his hands.

Beyond the seal...

Beyond the chains...

Beyond the prison...

Something was moving.

Something ancient.

Something powerful.

Then a voice echoed through the darkness.

A woman's voice.

Calm.

Cold.

Ancient.

And unmistakably Drakhar.

> "Tell me..."

The chains trembled.

> "Which one of my father's descendants finally found me?"

The last chain shattered.

And the Crimson Seal exploded.
