## Chapter 43 — The Devourer Sees the Heir

The sky changed.

Not gradually.

Not naturally.

Instantly.

One moment there were storm clouds.

The next...

There was darkness.

A darkness so deep it swallowed the stars themselves.

The moon vanished.

The wind stopped.

Even the crimson lightning surrounding Lucien disappeared.

The entire world seemed to hold its breath.

Waiting.

Watching.

Terrified.

Aiden slowly looked upward.

And immediately wished he hadn't.

Far above the clouds...

Something moved.

Something larger than mountains.

Larger than kingdoms.

Larger than imagination itself.

Two golden eyes stared down from the darkness.

Eyes older than civilization.

Older than history.

Older than memory.

Valthoros.

The Devourer.

The First Destroyer.

The being responsible for countless fallen worlds.

And it was looking directly at him.

Not Lucien.

Not Seraphina.

Not the kingdom.

Him.

Aiden Drakhar.

The heir.

### THREAT LEVEL

The golden eyes narrowed.

Then a voice entered Aiden's mind.

Not through his ears.

Not through the air.

Directly into his thoughts.

A voice so ancient it sounded like continents grinding together.

> "Another Drakhar."

The words shook his soul.

Every instinct screamed at him to kneel.

To run.

To hide.

To disappear.

But he didn't.

Because he remembered something.

Zee had stood against impossible things too.

And he hadn't bowed.

Neither would Aiden.

## Chapter 44 — The Forgotten King

Inside Lucien's mind...

The black throne continued breaking apart.

The shadow screamed.

For the first time since Aiden met it...

It was afraid.

Actually afraid.

Huge cracks spread through the throne room.

Light poured through the darkness.

Memories flooded back.

Lucien remembered everything.

His childhood.

His family.

His father.

His sister.

The kingdom.

The wars.

The sacrifice.

Thousands of years of memories returned all at once.

And with them...

Came rage.

Not Crimson Rage.

Real rage.

The rage of a man who realized how much had been stolen from him.

The shadow backed away.

"Wait."

Lucien slowly stood.

The version of Lucien inside his mind looked different.

Older.

Wiser.

Dangerous.

His silver eyes glowed like stars.

The shadow stumbled backward.

"You need me."

Lucien smiled.

The smile looked eerily similar to Zee's.

"No."

The throne shattered.

The prison collapsed.

And for the first time in thousands of years...

The Prince of Crimson was truly awake.

---

Outside...

Lucien rose.

Slowly.

The storm around him vanished.

The crimson energy calmed.

His eyes shifted from crimson back to silver.

Clear.

Focused.

Aware.

Seraphina froze.

Tears filled her eyes.

"Lucien?"

The prince looked at her.

Actually looked at her.

Then smiled.

A real smile.

"Sera."

The single word broke her.

She ran forward.

And after thousands of years apart...

Brother and sister embraced.

For a brief moment...

The world felt peaceful.

For a brief moment...

Everything felt right.

Then Valthoros laughed.

And the sky split open.

## Chapter 45 — The Return of the Crimson Prince

The laugh rolled across the world like thunder.

Entire oceans shook.

Mountains cracked.

Kingdoms trembled.

And Lucien immediately stepped in front of his family.

Not because he had to.

Because that's who he was.

A protector.

A warrior.

A prince.

The silver-eyed heir of House Drakhar.

His gaze lifted toward the heavens.

Toward Valthoros.

Toward the monster responsible for centuries of suffering.

For a long moment neither moved.

Then Lucien spoke.

His voice calm.

Steady.

Dangerous.

"You look uglier than I remember."

Silence.

Then Aiden burst out laughing.

Rez nearly choked.

Even Seraphina smiled.

And somewhere above the clouds...

Valthoros became very quiet.

Which was somehow even more terrifying.

---

The Devourer's voice rumbled through reality.

> "Lucien."

The prince folded his arms.

> "Still alive."

The darkness shifted.

> "For now."

Aiden stared.

The confidence.

The attitude.

The complete lack of fear.

Suddenly he understood why legends were written about this man.

Lucien wasn't brave because he lacked fear.

Lucien simply refused to let fear decide for him.
