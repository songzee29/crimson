## Chapter 54 — The King's Secret Trial

The world expected war.

Valthoros expected war.

Even Lucien expected war.

But Zee Drakhar had always been unpredictable.

And apparently...

Death hadn't changed that.

Inside the Hall of Kings, Aiden stood beside the spirit of Zee while countless ancestors watched from the streets of memory.

The old king looked toward the sky where the battle outside was beginning.

Then he sighed.

"Lucien always punches things first."

Aiden laughed.

"He gets that from you."

"I know."

The old king didn't even try denying it.

For several moments they watched the city.

The Hall of Kings wasn't fading.

It wasn't a vision.

It was something real.

A place existing beyond life and death.

A place built from memory itself.

Then Aiden noticed something strange.

One section of the city was missing.

Not destroyed.

Missing.

As though someone had ripped an entire district from existence.

The streets simply stopped.

The buildings ended.

Beyond them stood darkness.

Aiden pointed.

"What's that?"

The smile disappeared from Zee's face.

Immediately.

The change was so sudden Aiden felt his stomach tighten.

Because this was the first time he had ever seen Zee look nervous.

Not afraid.

Nervous.

The king stared toward the darkness.

Then quietly answered.

"My greatest failure."

## Chapter 55 — The Empty District

The darkness stretched for miles.

Aiden followed Zee through the silent streets.

The deeper they traveled...

The fewer people remained.

Eventually there were no ancestors at all.

No buildings.

No lights.

No sounds.

Only fog.

Only silence.

Only darkness.

The Hall of Kings itself seemed unwilling to come here.

As if the city was afraid.

Aiden had never imagined fear existing inside a place built by House Drakhar.

Yet it was everywhere.

The sensation crawled beneath his skin.

Finally they reached the edge.

A massive gate stood before them.

Black.

Ancient.

Covered in chains.

The sight immediately reminded Aiden of Seraphina's prison.

Only this seal felt older.

Much older.

The spirit of Zee stopped walking.

His eyes remained fixed upon the gate.

Then he whispered something Aiden never expected to hear.

"I hoped nobody would ever find this."

Silence.

Aiden stared.

"What is it?"

The king took a long breath.

Then answered.

"The first heir."

Aiden blinked.

"Seraphina?"

"No."

The answer came instantly.

"Before Seraphina."

The world stopped.

Aiden's blood ran cold.

Because he remembered.

The forgotten heir.

The erased heir.

The impossible heir.

The one history never mentioned.

The one even the Codex had hidden.

Zee slowly looked toward him.

"There was another child."

## Chapter 56 — The Child History Erased

The chains rattled.

The darkness beyond the gate moved.

Aiden immediately felt something staring back.

Watching.

Waiting.

Listening.

The feeling reminded him of Valthoros.

But also didn't.

This presence felt familiar.

Painfully familiar.

Like blood calling to blood.

The king slowly approached the gate.

Thousands of years of regret filled his eyes.

"When people tell stories..."

Zee said quietly.

"They tell the stories of their victories."

His hand touched the ancient chains.

"They don't tell the stories of their failures."

The seal began glowing.

A memory appeared.

A younger Zee.

A woman Aiden didn't recognize.

A small child laughing.

A little boy.

No older than five.

Running through a garden.

Silver eyes.

Black hair.

A Drakhar.

Without question.

The child looked happy.

Innocent.

Normal.

Then the memory changed.

Darkness spread.

The boy screamed.

The sky cracked.

Entire buildings collapsed.

People died.

The child cried.

And reality itself bent around him.

Aiden stared in horror.

The power wasn't vampire.

It wasn't witch.

It wasn't wolf.

It wasn't anything known.

It was something else.

Something wrong.

Something impossible.

The memory ended.

The gate trembled.

And Zee closed his eyes.

"His name was Kael."

## Chapter 57 — The Firstborn

The name echoed throughout the darkness.

Kael Drakhar.

The firstborn son of Zee.

The heir before Lucien.

The brother before Seraphina.

The child erased from history.

Aiden felt sick.

Not because Kael existed.

Because nobody knew.

Not Lucien.

Not Seraphina.

Nobody.

The king slowly lowered his head.

"He wasn't evil."

A pause.

"He wasn't broken."

Another pause.

"He was my son."

Pain entered his voice.

Raw pain.

The kind no king could hide.

The kind no legend could escape.

Then Zee finally revealed the truth.

"Kael was born connected to something."

The darkness behind the gate stirred.

The chains tightened.

The seal groaned.

The spirit of Zee stared at it.

"Something older than Valthoros."

Aiden froze.

That wasn't possible.

Nothing was older than Valthoros.

Nothing.

Yet Zee looked completely serious.

"The Observer watches Valthoros."

The king's gaze hardened.

"But something watches the Observer."

The silence that followed was terrifying.

Because Aiden realized something.

Every secret they uncovered only revealed bigger secrets.

Every answer created larger questions.

The universe was much larger than they thought.

And much darker.

## Chapter 58 — The Boy Behind The Gate

The seal cracked.

Just once.

A tiny crack.

Yet the entire Hall of Kings trembled.

Every ancestor in the city turned toward the darkness.

Every light flickered.

Every building shook.

The reaction was immediate.

Instinctive.

Fear.

The Hall itself feared what was behind the gate.

Then a voice spoke.

Soft.

Young.

Lonely.

A voice belonging to a child.

"Father?"

Zee froze.

Completely froze.

Thousands of years.

Thousands of years dead.

Yet that single word nearly broke him.

Aiden saw tears forming in the old king's eyes.

The voice spoke again.

"Father..."

The chains rattled.

The darkness shifted.

A small hand appeared through one of the cracks.

Not monstrous.

Not evil.

Just a child's hand.

And somehow...

That was far scarier.

Because the sadness in that voice felt endless.

The loneliness felt endless.

The isolation felt endless.

The boy had been trapped.

Forgotten.

Erased.

For thousands of years.

Waiting.

Alone.

Then the Codex appeared.

Not in front of Aiden.

In front of Zee.

The spirit stared at it.

His eyes widened.

Then Aiden saw the message.

Far bigger than House Drakhar.

Because somewhere beyond the seal...

The firstborn son of Zee Drakhar was waking up.

And history had buried him for a reason.
