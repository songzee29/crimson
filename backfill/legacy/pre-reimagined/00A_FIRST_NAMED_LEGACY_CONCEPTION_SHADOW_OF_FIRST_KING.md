## The original proposal (preserved)

Honestly, I think you've reached a crossroads where **either choice can work**.

### If You End It Here

The story actually has a satisfying ending.

Zee started as:
- a broken human kid
- trapped in a toxic environment
- angry at the world

And he ended as:
- the First King
- a father
- a grandfather
- a legend
- someone who finally found peace

That's a complete character arc.

Most stories make the mistake of continuing after the emotional journey is finished. Zee's emotional journey was completed when he stopped fighting for survival and started living for love and family.

Ending it here would make **The Crimson Dynasty** feel like a true epic saga.

---

### If You Continue

Then I wouldn't continue with Zee as the main character.

His story is finished.

I'd move to:

# THE CRIMSON LEGACY

Following:
- Astranea
- Kaelis
- future Mikaelson descendants

Maybe thousands of years later.

Instead of asking:

> "Can Zee become stronger?"

The new question becomes:

> "Can anyone ever live up to Zee?"

That's a much more interesting conflict.

Imagine descendants constantly hearing:

> "You're the blood of Zee Mikaelson."

Every achievement compared to him.
Every failure judged against him.

Being the child of a legend can be its own curse.

---

### My Favorite Option

End **The Crimson Dynasty**.

Then begin:

# 🩸 THE CRIMSON LEGACY 🩸
### Book I: *The Shadow of the First King*

Where Zee is no longer the hero.

He's the legend everyone else is trying to live up to.

That way Zee remains special, the ending stays powerful, and the universe can keep growing without undoing the emotional ending you already earned.

So if this were my decision:

**The Crimson Dynasty ends.**
**The Crimson Legacy begins.** 👑🩸📖
