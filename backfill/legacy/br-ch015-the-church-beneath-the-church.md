The next evening Aidan followed the map.

Rain poured from dark skies.

The abandoned church sat forgotten between crumbling buildings.

Most people passed without noticing it.

Aidan almost did too.

Until his Witch Sight activated.

Then he saw it.

The church wasn't alone.

A second structure existed underneath it.

Invisible.

Buried.

Ancient.

A church beneath a church.

The hidden world was beginning to reveal itself.

As Aidan approached, he felt something watching him.

Not the Keeper.

Not the faceless man.

Something else.

Something cautious.

A survivor.

Someone waiting to see whether he was friend or enemy.
