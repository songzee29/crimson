The room beyond the door wasn't what either of them expected.

No throne.

No treasure.

No glowing artifacts.

Just a massive underground sanctuary carved from ancient stone.

Beautiful.

Silent.

Forgotten.

Rows of stone pillars stretched into darkness.

Ancient crimson banners hung unmoving despite the lack of wind.

At the center stood a circular platform surrounded by hundreds of candles.

Every candle was lit.

Every single one.

Despite nobody being there.

Aidan slowly stepped forward.

His footsteps echoed.

The sanctuary echoed back.

Not acoustically.

Emotionally.

The place felt...

relieved.

Like it had been waiting for him.

---

Lyra followed cautiously.

"This place is older than the city," she whispered.

Aidan barely heard her.

His attention was locked on the platform.

Something was there.

Waiting.

---

The closer he got—

the harder it became to breathe.

Not from fear.

From pressure.

The same pressure he had felt when touching the door.

Only stronger.

Much stronger.

---

> SANCTUARY RECOGNITION CONFIRMED

> BLOODLINE AUTHENTICATED

Aidan froze.

Bloodline.

His stomach tightened.

No.

Not bloodline.

Anything but that.

Because bloodline meant answers.

And answers meant the possibility that everything he believed about himself was wrong.

---

The candles suddenly flickered.

Every flame bent toward him.

As if bowing.

---

Lyra took a nervous step backward.

"Aidan..."

He couldn't answer.

Something was happening.

Something inside him.

Something waking up.
