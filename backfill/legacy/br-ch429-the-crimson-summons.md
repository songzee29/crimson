Three nights passed.

The disappearances continued.

Black Hollow no longer slept.

Parents stood watch outside their homes.

Warriors patrolled every road.

Witches placed protective wards around every child.

None of it mattered.

Every sunrise...

Someone else was gone.

No screams.

No battle.

No blood.

Only silence.

---

Cassian stood in the center of the village.

His eyes were sunken.

His clothes stained with dirt and ash.

He hadn't slept in days.

One of his captains approached.

"My Lord..."

Cassian never looked away from the forest.

"...Report."

"We've searched over two hundred miles."

"Nothing."

"The wards were never broken."

"Nothing."

"The scouts found no scent."

"Nothing."

Cassian closed his eyes.

"...Again."

The captain hesitated.

"My Lord..."

"...Our people are beginning to lose hope."

Cassian looked toward him.

"No."

"They're beginning to lose faith..."

"...in me."
