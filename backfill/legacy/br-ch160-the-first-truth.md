The embrace lasted only a moment.

When Aidan stepped back...

He noticed something he had never seen before.

Rez looked...

Peaceful.

Not because the burden had disappeared.

Because it finally had a name.

The Crimson Assassin was no longer searching for himself.

He had found his way home.

Silence settled over the council chamber.

Everyone could see it.

Even Malik, who usually found a joke in every situation, simply smiled.

"So..."

"...you're really his uncle."

Rez looked at him.

"I've been his uncle longer than you've been alive."

Malik scratched the back of his head.

"That explains why you boss him around."

Aidan looked at Malik.

"I was wondering how long it would take you."

The room laughed.

The tension finally disappeared.

---

Rez slowly turned toward Orion.

"You could've broken the seal years ago."

Orion nodded.

"I could have."

"Then why didn't you?"

"Because it wasn't my seal to break."

He folded his hands behind his back.

"It was yours."

Everyone frowned.

Mara stepped forward.

"I don't understand."

Orion looked toward her.

"The memories were never stolen."

"They were sealed."

"And only the man who made the oath could fulfill the conditions to remove that seal."

Rez lowered his eyes.

"...Which means..."

Orion nodded.

"You unknowingly fulfilled your own oath."

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

*So the Legacy wasn't punishing him...*

Rez smiled.

**No.**

**It was protecting what I asked it to protect.**

---

Aidan folded his arms.

"Then tell me."

Rez looked at him.

"What do you want to know?"

Aidan answered without hesitation.

"Everything."

Rez laughed.

"I was afraid you'd say that."

He walked toward the massive window overlooking House Darkheart.

The kingdom stretched beneath them.

Children played in the streets.

The Academy echoed with lessons.

Guards patrolled the walls.

For several moments...

He simply watched.

Then he spoke.

"I knew your father long before he became a legend."

"I knew Zee when he was reckless."

"When he thought he could solve every problem by charging straight through it."

Lyra smiled.

"Sounds familiar."

Everyone slowly looked toward Aidan.

Aidan sighed.

"I walked into that one."

Even Rez laughed.

"He hated listening."

"He argued with everyone."

"He believed he was always right."

Malik pointed toward Aidan.

"Definitely runs in the family."

---

The laughter faded.

Rez's expression became thoughtful.

"But there was one thing Zee understood better than anyone else."

"He believed power meant nothing..."

"...if there was no one left to protect."

Aidan listened without interrupting.

"That belief became the foundation of House Darkheart."

"It became the reason the Crimson Legacy was created."

**Ding.**

Aidan read the notification.

Then nodded.

"That feels right."

"It always has."

---

Rez turned away from the window.

"There is something else."

Something in his voice made the room fall silent again.

"I remembered one final promise."

Aidan's expression became serious.

"What promise?"

Rez met his eyes.

"The promise Zee made to me..."

"...the day before he disappeared."

The room seemed to grow colder.

"He told me..."

"If the day ever comes when Aidan surpasses me..."

"...don't let him become me."

Silence.

No one spoke.

Aidan frowned.

"What did he mean?"

Rez smiled sadly.

"Zee knew the temptation that comes with absolute power."

"He wasn't afraid of enemies."

"He was afraid that one day..."

"...you would have to choose between protecting the world..."

"...or ruling it."

Aidan lowered his head.

Then slowly smiled.

"I've already made that choice."

Rez raised an eyebrow.

"You have?"

Aidan looked through the window at House Darkheart.

At the children.

The families.

The civilization they had built together.

"I don't want subjects."

"I want a future."

Rez couldn't help but smile.

"Then your father was wrong about one thing."

Aidan looked at him.

"What's that?"

Rez laughed quietly.

"You didn't surpass him..."

"You became something different."

Outside...

The bells of House Darkheart rang across the kingdom.

Not for danger.

Not for war.

But for another day of peace.

For the first time since Book III began...

The shadows of the past no longer stood between Aidan and Rez.

The promise had been fulfilled.

The memories had returned.

And now...

The future could finally begin.
