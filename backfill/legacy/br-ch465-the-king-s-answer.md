The stranger looked at Aidan one final time.

"I've delivered my warning."

Aidan took one step forward.

"Wait."

The stranger paused.

"If you serve the Ash King..."

"...why help me?"

The stranger smiled sadly.

"I don't serve him."

"I survived him."

With those words...

Its body dissolved into drifting ash.

The wind carried it toward the northern mountains.

Gone.

Rez looked toward Aidan.

"What now?"

Aidan stood in silence.

Then the Crimson Legacy appeared.

Not with a quest.

Not with a reward.

Only one sentence.

---

> **A King is measured not by the enemies he defeats...**

> **...but by the people history remembers because he refused to let them be forgotten.**

---

The page closed.

Aidan turned toward Black Hollow.

His crimson eyes briefly flashed with gold.

Then he spoke.

"...We're too late to stop the beginning."

He looked toward his companions.

"...But we're not too late..."

"...to stop the end."

The company mounted their horses once more.

The road ahead no longer led only toward Cassian.

It led toward an ancient horror...

One capable of erasing names, memories, and entire bloodlines from history itself.

And for the first time...

The Crimson King wasn't riding to fulfill a decree.

He was riding...

To make sure there would still be a future...

For House Darkheart to inherit.
