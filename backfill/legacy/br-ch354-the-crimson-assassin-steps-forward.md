Without warning...

The Crimson Assassin moved.

Not toward Asterion.

Beside Aidan.

Its closed eyes trembled.

One hand slowly rose toward its own chest.

Almost...

As if remembering something.

Asterion looked at it.

His ancient expression softened.

"...So..."

"...the sleeping prince..."

"...still guards his king."

Aidan's eyes narrowed.

"...You know him."

The beast slowly nodded.

"...Not by that face..."

"...By the soul waiting to return."

Silence.

"...He kept his promise."

The Crimson Assassin lowered its hand.

Returning to complete stillness.

Yet...

Something had changed.

Deep within the dormant vessel...

A heartbeat echoed.

Twice.

Instead of once.
