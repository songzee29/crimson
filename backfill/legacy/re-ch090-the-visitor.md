That evening...

Aidan climbed onto the cathedral roof.

He already knew someone was waiting.

Azrath stood at the edge.

Looking over New Orleans.

Hands behind his back.

He never turned around.

"You've built something remarkable."

Aidan didn't answer.

"I expected more guards."

"I don't hide behind people."

Azrath nodded.

"I know."

Silence stretched between them.

Finally...

Aidan spoke.

"...Why are you doing this?"

Azrath smiled.

Still facing the city.

"Because hope..."

"...must always be tested."

Aidan's voice hardened.

"You've hurt innocent people."

"I've moved them."

Azrath corrected calmly.

"They're alive."

Aidan froze.

"What?"

"I said..."

"...they're alive."

For the first time...

Azrath looked directly at him.

"Did you ever ask yourself why there were no bodies?"

Silence.

"I wanted to see..."

"...whether your first instinct would be anger..."

"...or compassion."

Aidan frowned.

"...Where are they?"

Azrath's smile returned.

"I'll tell you."

A pause.

"...when you earn the answer."
