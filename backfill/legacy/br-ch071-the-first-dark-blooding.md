The room trembled.

Aidan looked at Lyra.

Then at his own glowing blood.

He remembered every promise he had ever made.

*"I'll never leave you."*

*"We'll build our kingdom together."*

*"Forever."*

The Wolf stood beside him inside his soul.

Not giving an order.

Only one sentence.

> **"...She is our Queen."**

Rez stepped forward.

He understood before anyone else.

"Aidan..."

His voice was calm.

"You have the choice now."

No one else understood what Rez meant.

Only Aidan.

Only the Crimson King.

He gently brushed Lyra's hair away from her face.

"I'm sorry..."

He whispered.

"I should've been faster."

His glowing blood gathered at his fingertips.

He rested his hand over Lyra's heart.

His voice echoed through the silent chamber.

Not loudly.

Not with rage.

With absolute love.

> **"By My Crimson Blood..."**

> **"...Live."**

The room exploded with crimson light.
