That afternoon...

Aidan visited the rebuilt orphanage.

The children ran to him.

One little boy tugged on his sleeve.

"King Aidan!"

He laughed.

"Yeah?"

The little boy looked serious.

"...Do kings ever get scared?"

Aidan smiled.

"Every day."

The children looked surprised.

"But..."

He knelt beside them.

"...being brave doesn't mean you're never scared."

"It means..."

"...you don't let fear decide for you."

The children nodded.

Satisfied.

As they ran away laughing...

Aidan whispered to himself,

"I hope that's true."

Far above...

Azrath watched from a church tower.

He smiled.

"You really do believe your own words."
