Far beneath the Alps...

In a fortress carved into black stone...

Ancient rulers gathered.

Not vampires.

Not witches.

Not wolves.

Monarchs.

Leaders of supernatural civilizations so old that even House Darkheart rarely spoke their names.

At the center of the room...

An empty throne waited.

A silver-eyed ruler spoke.

"The Blood Sovereigns have moved."

Another answered.

"The Veil Dominion has moved."

A third quietly added,

"...House Darkheart has begun rebuilding."

Silence filled the chamber.

Finally...

The oldest Monarch stood.

"If the Crimson King continues evolving..."

"...he will unite Authorities no ruler has ever united."

He looked around the room.

"We have two choices."

"Bow..."

"...or destroy him before he becomes complete."

No one answered.

Because every Monarch understood...

Either choice...

Would begin a war unlike anything the world had ever seen.
