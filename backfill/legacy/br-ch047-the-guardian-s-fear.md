The Guardian stood within the Sanctuary archives.

Ancient scrolls floated around him.

One by one...

They began opening on their own.

Every scroll carried the same warning.

Different languages.

Different centuries.

Different civilizations.

One sentence.

> **"Do not answer the whisper."**

The Guardian's hands began shaking.

"No..."

He rushed deeper into the archives.

Searching.

Searching.

Finally...

He found it.

An old record written by Zee himself.

The ink had almost faded away.

The title alone made his heart sink.

> **THE SILENT WAR**

He slowly opened the first page.

Only one sentence remained legible.

> **"If he ever finds another voice..."**

The rest of the page had been torn away.
