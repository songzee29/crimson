• Pain Resistance — Pain slows him but rarely stops him.

• Crimson Vitality — His body survives wounds and blood loss that should normally kill him, though he does not instantly heal.

• Silent Movement — His footsteps become naturally quieter without conscious effort.

• Survival Adaptation — His body gradually learns from repeated life-or-death experiences.

These adaptations strengthen his body.
