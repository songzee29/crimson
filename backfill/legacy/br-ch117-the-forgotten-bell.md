The next morning...

The Guardian summoned the Crimson Circle to an abandoned church on the edge of the French Quarter.

Dust covered every pew.

The stained-glass windows had long since shattered.

A massive bronze bell hung silently above them.

The Guardian looked unusually serious.

"The Veil is moving."

"So are others."

He pointed toward the bell.

"Ring it."

Malik looked confused.

"...Why?"

"Because if it rings..."

He looked directly at Aidan.

"...they'll know the heir has awakened."

Lyra frowned.

"Who's they?"

The Guardian smiled sadly.

"The ones who survived Zee."

Silence.

Aidan walked toward the rope.

His hand wrapped around it.

The System suddenly appeared.

Aidan stopped.

His heartbeat quickened.

Ring the bell...

And reveal himself.

Stay silent...

And remain hidden.

For several long seconds...

He stood there.

Then...

He let go.

The bell remained silent.

The Guardian smiled.

"Good."

Malik blinked.

"...Wait."

"You wanted him to ring it."

The Guardian shook his head.

"No."

"I wanted to know if he understood."

Lyra looked confused.

"Understood what?"

The Guardian looked toward Aidan.

"A true king doesn't announce himself."

"He waits until the world gives him no other choice."

Aidan didn't know why...

But those words felt right.

Deep inside him.

Like they had always been there.
