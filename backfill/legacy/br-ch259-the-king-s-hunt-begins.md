Night returned.

Aidan stood on the balcony overlooking New Orleans.

The city still feared him.

Sirens echoed in the distance.

The news continued calling him...

"The Crimson Devil."

Aidan looked over the skyline.

He didn't care what they called him.

Names didn't matter.

Power did.

Kings did.

Evolution did.

He slowly closed his eyes.

The Royal Blood Reservoir pulsed inside him.

His Blood Arts had grown stronger.

His body had changed.

His instincts had sharpened.

Yet...

It still wasn't enough.

He opened his eyes.

Crimson lightning flashed across the clouds.

One sentence left his lips.

Quiet.

Certain.

> **"...Valerius."**

> **"...I'm coming for your crown."**

Far away...

Across frozen mountains...

Valerius slowly stood from his throne.

He smiled.

Then laughed.

A laugh that echoed through his ancient castle.

> **"FINALLY!"**

He spread his arms wide.

> **"COME, CRIMSON KING!"**

> **"COME CLAIM WHAT YOU BELIEVE IS YOURS!"**

The ancient castle shook.

Blood Sovereigns gathered from every corner of the world.

Not to interfere.

To witness.

Because they all knew what was coming.

Not a battle.

Not a war.

A coronation.

One king would leave with his crown.

The other...

Would leave with nothing.

And hidden far beyond both kings...

Azrath watched in silence.

For the first time in centuries...

He whispered words that even surprised himself.

> **"...Don't die, Aidan."**

Because if Aidan fell now...

Azrath's true plan...

Would die with him.
