# 🔴 THE CRIMSON SYSTEM — CORE RULES

### 1. The Book Has Been Bound
The physical book no longer exists in the world.

Once Aidan bled on it, it dissolved into a **mind-bound system**.

- No one can see it
- No one can steal it
- No one can interact with it except Aidan

But it still exists somewhere…

just not in reality anymore.

---

### 2. Only the Heir Can Perceive the System
The Crimson System exists exclusively in Aidan’s perception.

- Visual overlays appear in his mind, not the air
- Text can distort based on emotion, fear, or injury
- Other people experience nothing unusual

If Aidan tries to explain it, it sounds like madness.

---

### 3. Power Must Be “Unsealed”
No ability is given freely.

Every power is locked behind a condition called a **Seal Condition**, such as:

- Blood sacrifice
- Emotional trigger (rage, grief, fear)
- Physical trial (survive something deadly)
- Moral cost (choose between two harmful outcomes)

No seal = no ability.

---

### 4. Every Ability Has a Cost
Power is never neutral.

Each ability has a **Legacy Debt**, such as:

- Memory loss
- Physical decay
- Emotional numbing
- Shortening lifespan of something important (relationships, sanity, identity)

The stronger the ability, the heavier the debt.

---

### 5. The System Adapts to the User
The system is not fixed.

It studies Aidan.

- His fears influence quests
- His decisions unlock different paths
- His moral choices shape future abilities

In short:
> The system is not just giving power… it is learning him.

---

### 6. Death Is Not Final (But It Is Not Free)
Aidan cannot permanently die while bonded to the system.

However:

- Each “resurrection event” corrupts something in him
- Something human is exchanged for something unknown
- The system records every death like progress, not tragedy

---

### 7. The Crimson System Reacts to Bloodline Energy
Anything tied to Zee’s lineage causes instability.

- Strong emotional surges can trigger hidden content
- Ancient memories may surface without warning
- Unknown “echoes” of Zee’s past may overwrite system logic briefly

The system does not fully understand Zee… and never admits it.

---

### 8. Quests Cannot Be Refused (Only Delayed)
When a quest appears:

- It becomes active immediately
- Aidan can delay it
- But delaying increases difficulty, cost, or consequences

Avoidance is not escape—it is escalation.

---

### 9. Truth Is Locked Behind Progression
The system does not reveal the full truth at once.

Information is gated:

- About Zee
- About Rez
- About the world’s collapse
- About the system itself

Even Rez does not have full access.

---

### 10. The Final Rule: The System Has an End Condition
The Crimson System is moving toward something.

Unknown to Aidan.

But there is a hidden directive:

> “WHEN HEIR REACHES FINAL FORM — THE SYSTEM WILL COMPLETE ITS PURPOSE.”

What that purpose is… is not revealed.

Even Rez cannot see this line clearly.

---

# 🧠 REZ — LIMITATIONS INSIDE THE SYSTEM

Rez is powerful in personality, but heavily restricted in reality.

---

### 1. No Physical Form
Rez cannot:

- appear in the real world
- manifest visually outside Aidan’s mind
- interact with objects or people

He is **pure cognition inside the system**.

---

### 2. Limited Knowledge Access
Rez does NOT know everything.

He only knows:

- fragments of Zee’s legacy
- partial system mechanics
- what the system allows him to see at that moment

Important truths are often “blurred” or missing for him too.

---

### 3. Cannot Directly Control Aidan (Usually)
Rez cannot force actions.

However:

- he can suggest
- he can warn
- he can distort perception slightly during extreme moments

But if Aidan’s will is strong, Rez is powerless.

---

### 4. Emotional Sync Limitation
Rez’s clarity depends on Aidan’s mental state.

- calm Aidan → clear Rez
- stressed Aidan → glitching, fragmented Rez
- near death Aidan → Rez becomes unstable or aggressive

Sometimes Rez doesn’t even realize what he’s saying.

---

### 5. Memory Fragmentation
Rez’s memories are incomplete.

He may:

- remember events differently each time
- contradict himself without knowing
- “unlock” memories only when Aidan reaches certain milestones

This makes him unreliable… even to himself.

---

### 6. Cannot Escape the System Alone
Rez is trapped.

Even if Aidan tries to free him:

- Rez cannot leave without a physical anchor
- The system will not release him willingly
- Any attempt risks corruption or system backlash

So freedom is possible… but not simple.

---

### 7. Communication Delay Effect
Rez is not always immediate.

Sometimes:

- Aidan hears Rez seconds too late
- or Rez reacts after decisions are already made
- or Rez appears “muted” during high system activity

This creates tension in critical moments.

---

### 8. Restricted Truth About Himself
Rez is blocked from fully accessing:

- how he ended up in the system
- his original identity (beyond what he claims)
- his connection to Zee

If he tries to think too deeply about it, the system interrupts him.

---

### 9. Cannot Lie Perfectly, But Can Misinterpret
Rez cannot directly lie with full intent.

But he can:

- misunderstand truths
- present incomplete information
- assume things that are wrong

So he’s not deceptive… just dangerously imperfect.

---

### 10. Attachment Restriction (Most Important)
The system monitors Rez’s bond with Aidan.

If Rez becomes too influential:

- the system weakens him
- silences him temporarily
- or “resets” parts of his memory

Because the system fears something growing between them.

---
