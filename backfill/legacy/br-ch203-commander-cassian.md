Deep beneath the Veil Citadel...

Commander Cassian stood before the Council.

"The Circle has assembled."

The oldest elder nodded.

"Proceed."

Cassian remained silent.

The elder noticed.

"...Speak."

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"There will be civilian casualties."

"They are acceptable."

"There are children in those districts."

"They are acceptable."

For the first time...

Cassian's eyes flashed with anger.

"...No."

The chamber fell silent.

The eldest elder slowly stood.

"You question the Council?"

"I question this order."

Another elder stepped forward.

"The Crimson Bloodline has always corrupted those around it."

"They are no longer civilians."

"They are collateral."

Cassian closed his eyes.

His fists tightened.

Then...

He gave a single nod.

Not because he agreed.

Because duty demanded obedience.

As he walked away...

His footsteps echoed through the empty hall.

Each one felt heavier than the last.
