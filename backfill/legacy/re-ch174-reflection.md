One hundred years...

The Crimson Kingdom had never been the same.

House Darkheart still stood.

The Academy still flourished.

The Crimson Civilization continued to grow.

But laughter...

Had become quieter.

The throne...

Had become heavier.

And the King...

Never smiled the same way again.

---

The great hall echoed with silence.

The Crimson Throne stood at the end of the chamber.

Aidan sat upon it alone.

No crown rested upon his head.

No celebration filled the room.

Only memories.

---

Inside his mind...

Rez remained quiet.

For once...

Even the Legacy Guardian had no words.

---

Aidan slowly opened the small wooden box resting on the throne beside him.

Inside...

A single silver ring.

Lyra's ring.

He picked it up carefully.

His thumb traced its worn edges.

A hundred years...

And he still couldn't bring himself to put it away.

The notification faded.

Aidan didn't even acknowledge it.

He already knew.

---

Far away...

Cassian watched a little girl run through a garden.

She couldn't have been older than eight.

Her laughter filled the air.

She chased butterflies beneath the afternoon sun.

Cassian smiled.

Not because he had forgotten.

Because, somehow...

Life had continued.

The little girl turned.

"Father!"

Cassian knelt and opened his arms.

She ran straight into them.

He hugged her tightly.

"What did I tell you about running so far from the house?"

She giggled.

"But you always find me."

Cassian smiled.

"I always will."

---

Hidden far beyond the garden...

A crimson raven landed silently on an ancient tree.

Its crimson eyes watched father and daughter.

It stayed only a moment.

Then it flew away.

---

Back in House Darkheart...

The raven landed on the castle balcony.

It dissolved into crimson mist.

Rez stepped forward.

His expression darkened.

He looked toward Aidan.

"I found him."

Aidan never looked up from Lyra's ring.

"I know."

"He has a daughter."

Silence.

"She looks..."

Rez stopped.

He couldn't finish the sentence.

Because he already knew who she resembled.

Lyra.

---

Aidan finally stood.

He closed the wooden box.

His grip tightened around it.

Inside his mind...

The wolf remained silent.

The vampire remained silent.

Neither tried to influence him.

This decision...

Belonged only to the King.

---

Rez stepped in front of him.

"You don't have to do this."

Aidan looked at his oldest friend.

"No."

"I don't."

"Then don't."

Another silence.

Longer this time.

"I made the decree."

Rez nodded.

"I know."

"I spoke the judgment."

"I know."

"And every judgment..."

Aidan looked out across his kingdom.

"...demands a price."

Rez's voice hardened.

"Then let the price be yours."

Aidan closed his eyes.

For the first time in a century...

Tears rolled down his face.

"I've paid it every day since she died."

---

The throne room fell silent.

No king.

No founder.

No legend.

Only a husband...

Still mourning the woman he loved.

---

Outside...

Storm clouds gathered over House Darkheart.

Far across the world...

Cassian tucked his daughter into bed.

She smiled up at him.

"Will you tell me another story?"

Cassian smiled.

"Of course."

"What about tonight?"

"The story of a King."

She closed her eyes.

"I like that one."

Cassian began speaking...

Completely unaware...

That thousands of miles away...

The King in his story...

Was preparing to make the hardest decision of his immortal life.

The Fourth Judgment had begun.

Not with blood.

Not with battle.

But with a choice.

And that choice...

Would determine whether Aidan Darkheart remained the man Lyra loved...

Or became the monster he had spent centuries refusing to become.
