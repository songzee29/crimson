The warehouse became silent.

No one spoke.

Not Lyra.

Not Malik.

Not Sienna.

Not even the Guardian.

Aidan carefully unfolded the ancient crimson scroll.

The ink was impossibly fresh.

As though it had been written yesterday.

He began reading.

---

> **"To my son..."**

Aidan froze.

His breathing stopped.

Not...

*"To my heir."*

Not...

*"To the next king."*

Simply...

*"To my son."*

A tear silently rolled down his cheek.

> **"If these words have found you..."**

> **"...then the Witch Authority accepted your heart."**

> **"If it had not..."**

> **"...this letter would have remained blank forever."**

Lyra looked toward the Guardian.

He whispered...

"The letter judged him..."
