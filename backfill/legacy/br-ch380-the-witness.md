The mountain pass remained silent.

Rez looked at Aidan.

Then at Cassian.

Neither reached for a weapon.

History itself had become the battlefield.

Cassian slowly stood.

His eyes were red.

Not from bloodlust.

From exhaustion.

"...What happens now?"

Aidan answered truthfully.

"I leave."

Cassian frowned.

"...That's it?"

"...No."

Aidan turned his back.

He began walking away.

Rez followed.

After several steps...

Aidan spoke one final time without looking back.

> **"Live."**

Cassian froze.

> **"Build another home if you wish."**

> **"Find love again."**

> **"Laugh."**

A long silence.

Then...

The final words echoed through the pass.

> **"...And every time peace finds you..."**

> **"...remember my decree."**

Aidan never looked back.

He didn't need to.

Judgment had already been delivered.

---

Cassian remained alone in the mountain pass.

The Darkheart Crest glowed faintly beneath his skin.

He finally understood.

The curse wasn't trying to kill him.

It was making him carry the memory of what he had done...

For as long as he chose to keep living.

Far in the distance...

The Crimson King and Rez disappeared beyond the mountains.

The old world had returned.

But it hadn't returned to repeat history.

It had returned to prove that House Darkheart never forgot its promises.
