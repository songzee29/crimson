At sunrise...

Aidan entered the royal training grounds.

No weapons.

No armor.

Only himself.

He closed his eyes.

Inside his soul...

The Wolf stood before him.

Not behind the Sacred Seal.

Before him.

The cracked barrier remained between them.

But the distance had grown smaller.

> **"...Fight me."**

Aidan smiled.

"...In here?"

The Wolf nodded.

> **"...Strength begins in the soul."**

The training began.

No fists.

No claws.

Only instinct.

Every time Aidan anticipated the Wolf's movement...

The Wolf changed.

Every time Aidan relied on vampire speed...

The Wolf slowed the battle.

Every time Aidan reached for witchcraft...

The Wolf simply waited.

Hours passed.

Finally...

The Wolf stopped.

> **"...You still seek the strongest attack."**

Aidan caught his breath.

"...Don't I need it?"

The Wolf slowly shook its massive head.

> **"...No..."**

Its ancient eyes softened.

> **"...A king needs the strongest judgment."**

Those words echoed through Aidan's soul.
