Days later...

Aidan saved another family from a cursed mansion.

A little boy hugged him tightly.

"Thank you."

Aidan smiled.

He knelt to the child's level.

Then...

The little boy accidentally scraped Aidan's wrist with a broken toy.

A tiny cut.

One drop of Crimson Blood landed on the boy's hand.

The world...

Stopped.

The child looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

His pupils dilated.

Not with fear.

Recognition.

Then...

Without understanding why...

The little boy whispered,

> **"...I know you."**

Lyra's blood ran cold.

The child had never met Aidan before.

The boy blinked.

Confused.

"...What did I just say?"

No one answered.

Because no one knew.

Except the Crimson Legacy.

Deep within its endless halls, the Journal opened by itself.

Ink spread across the page in jagged strokes.

---

> **The Bloodline is beginning to call.**

Another line appeared.

> **Soon...**

> **Blood will answer Blood.**

The page closed with a violent snap.

---

Far beneath the Hall of Kings...

The Vampire Seal cracked again.

This time...

No chain broke.

Instead...

Something reached through the crack.

Not a hand.

Not a claw.

A whisper.

It drifted through the darkness...

Past the sleeping Soul Anchor...

Past the dormant Witch Hybrid vessel...

Until it reached the edge of Aidan's dreams.

One sentence echoed in the black.

> **"How long... will you keep starving us?"**

Aidan bolted upright, drenched in sweat.

The room was empty.

The warehouse was silent.

But for the first time...

He wasn't afraid of the darkness.

He was afraid...

Of what was waiting for him inside it.
