Morning arrived.

The wolves were gone.

Only their footprints remained.

Aidan looked toward the northern mountains.

The disappearances.

Black Hollow.

Cassian.

The crimson ash.

None of those problems had vanished.

But now...

He no longer walked alone.

His Witch Authority guided his mind.

His Vampire Authority strengthened his body.

And deep within his soul...

His Wolf Authority had finally become...

His ally.

Not fully awakened.

Not yet.

But no longer silent.

Aidan mounted his horse.

Lyra climbed onto hers.

Rez tightened the crimson sword across his back.

The Guardian looked toward the road.

"My King..."

"...where to now?"

Then he answered.

> **"We finish what we started."**

The company rode north.

Toward Black Hollow.

Toward the mystery of the crimson ash.

Toward the Second Judgment.

And somewhere...

Hidden beyond the mountains...

A pair of ancient eyes slowly opened.

Watching.

Waiting.

Smiling.

Because the Wolf had finally answered its king.

But the true enemy...

Had only just begun to make its move.
