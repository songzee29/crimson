The weeks that followed were unlike anything the world had ever witnessed.

Operation: Rise of the King became more than a mission.

It became a movement.

The Circle liberated cursed territories one after another.

Ancient shrines were cleansed.

Forgotten villages awakened from centuries of enchantment.

Children born beneath curses laughed beneath open skies for the first time.

Every victory carried the same message.

**The Crimson King had come.**

---

But victory...

Was becoming exhausting.

Aidan stopped sleeping.

Not because he couldn't.

Because every time he closed his eyes...

He heard Elias.

Not words.

Just laughter.

The kind brothers shared.

The kind he could never hear again.

---

Lyra noticed first.

"You look tired."

Aidan smiled.

"I'm fine."

She frowned.

"You're lying."

He looked away.

"...I know."
