## Chapter One Hundred Fifty-Seven — The Pilgrim's First Night

The city disappeared behind Aidan long before the sun reached its highest point.

Stone roads became dirt paths.

Dirt paths became narrow forest trails.

By evening...

Only the mountains remained ahead.

The farther he traveled, the quieter the world became.

No traffic.

No distant sirens.

Only the sound of wind weaving through ancient pines.

For the first time in years...

Aidan was truly alone.

Yet he didn't feel lonely.

The Guardian's lessons.

Malik's compass.

Lyra's sketchbook.

His aunt's promise.

The First Keeper's key.

Every one of them traveled with him.

Not in his bag.

In his heart.

---

As dusk settled across the mountains, Aidan reached a weathered stone marker standing beside the old northern trail Lyra had marked on her map.

Most of the inscription had been worn away.

Only a few words remained.

**...Pilgrim Road...**

**...Walk with Honor...**

Someone had deliberately destroyed the rest.

Fresh chisel marks scarred the ancient stone.

Not centuries old.

Recent.

Aidan knelt beside the monument.

Who would erase a trail marker?

He brushed away loose dirt.

Hidden beneath moss, he found another carving.

Smaller.

Far older.

A simple crimson tree.

Its roots stretched downward.

Its branches reached toward the mountains.

The same tree engraved upon the First Keeper's key.

The Pilgrim Road had once belonged to the Keepers.

Someone wanted the world to forget that.

---

Deep beneath the mountain...

The Living Legacy quietly preserved another truth.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON RECORD UPDATED

The Pilgrim has found the First Marker.

Roads may be buried.

Truth remains.

History remembers every step.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The record sealed itself.

---

Night fell quickly.

Aidan gathered fallen branches and built a small fire beneath an ancient cedar.

The flames danced gently against the darkness.

He opened Lyra's sketchbook.

Between two pages rested the crimson lily the florist had given him.

It had dried beautifully.

Its crimson petals had darkened, but none had fallen away.

A folded note slipped from between the pages.

He frowned.

He hadn't noticed it before.

The handwriting belonged to Lyra.

*If you're reading this...*

*You probably remembered to pack the sketchbook before remembering to pack enough food.*

Aidan laughed quietly.

She wasn't wrong.

The note continued.

*Look up once in a while.*

*The road is beautiful too.*

He closed the note with a smile.

Then...

He looked up.

The night sky stretched endlessly overhead.

Thousands of stars shimmered across the darkness.

For the first time since leaving home...

He stopped thinking about where he was going.

And appreciated where he was.

---

Several miles away...

The Hunter watched the small campfire from the ridge above.

He made no effort to hide.

He knew Aidan couldn't see him at this distance.

The Heir chose the northern trail.

Good.

A second figure emerged silently beside him.

Unlike the Hunter, this newcomer wore weathered green robes.

An old bow rested across his back.

His hair had turned completely white.

You didn't tell him.

The Hunter kept watching the fire below.

It isn't my place.

The old archer nodded.

The Forest Keepers have waited a long time.

Will the mountain accept him?

The Hunter's answer came slowly.

That depends.

On what?

Whether he believes the Foundation exists for him...

Or whether he understands...

He exists to serve the Foundation.

The old archer smiled.

Then perhaps...

He truly is the right successor.

---

Just after midnight...

Aidan awoke.

Not from a sound.

From silence.

The forest had become unnaturally still.

The fire continued burning.

The wind had stopped.

Even the insects no longer sang.

His crimson-and-gold eyes slowly opened.

Every instinct within him sharpened.

Something was nearby.

Not hostile.

Watching.

He quietly stood.

One hand rested near the iron key inside his coat.

Not as a weapon.

As reassurance.

Then...

A single stag stepped into the edge of the firelight.

Its antlers were enormous.

Its coat shimmered silver beneath the moon.

It regarded Aidan calmly.

Neither moved.

For nearly a minute...

The two simply watched one another.

Then the stag lowered its head.

Not in fear.

In acknowledgment.

Before disappearing back into the darkness.

Aidan remained standing long after it was gone.

He couldn't explain why.

But deep inside...

He felt as though he had just been judged.

And somehow...

Accepted.

Far beyond the trees...

Hidden beneath the eastern mountain...

The massive stone gateway of the Second Foundation opened another inch.

Ancient dust drifted into the night air.

Inside the forgotten halls...

A deep bell rang once.

Not to summon.

To welcome.

The Pilgrim had crossed the first threshold.

The mountain had begun to recognize his footsteps.
