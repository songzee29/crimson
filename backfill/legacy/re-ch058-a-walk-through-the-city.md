Azrath wandered through New Orleans like an ordinary tourist.

He bought coffee.

Thanked the cashier.

Helped an old man pick up groceries after they spilled across the sidewalk.

A little girl accidentally bumped into him.

"I'm sorry!"

Azrath smiled warmly.

"No need."

He handed her dropped teddy bear back.

"Be careful."

She smiled.

"Thank you, mister!"

She ran away.

Azrath watched her leave.

"...Hope."

He whispered the word almost curiously.

"As fragile now..."

"...as it was then."
