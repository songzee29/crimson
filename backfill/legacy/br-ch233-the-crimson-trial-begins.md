The world disappeared.

There was no plantation.

No swamp.

No moon.

No stars.

Only an endless crimson sea stretching beyond the horizon.

Aidan stood alone.

Across from him...

Valerius.

The First Blood Sovereign.

The ancient king smiled.

"...Welcome."

"This is not my Domain."

"This..."

He spread his arms.

"...is where Vampire Kings are born."

---

The crimson sea suddenly erupted.

Thousands of crimson pillars burst from beneath the ground.

Each pillar held the skeleton of an ancient vampire king.

Crowns rested upon their skulls.

Some had swords buried in their chests.

Others were still chained.

Every one of them...

Dead.

Valerius looked at them.

"They all wanted eternity."

"They all wanted power."

"They all wanted my throne."

He turned back toward Aidan.

"They all failed."

---

The System appeared.

Only Aidan could see it.
