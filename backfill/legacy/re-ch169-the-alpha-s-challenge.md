The Blood Moon hung over the forest.

Impossible.

It was still morning.

Yet crimson moonlight washed over the trees, painting the ancient wilderness in shades of blood.

The Shadow Pack never broke formation.

Neither did the Crimson Pack.

Two Alphas.

Two packs.

One forest.

---

The Shadow Alpha slowly circled Aidan.

Its black eyes never blinked.

Every step left the earth frozen beneath its paws.

The Crimson Wolf never looked away.

His crimson coat glowed beneath the Blood Moon, while his crimson-and-gold eyes followed every movement.

No fear.

No hesitation.

Only absolute confidence.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf lowered its massive head.

**Do not look away.**

The vampire smiled coldly.

**Predators smell uncertainty before they smell blood.**

Aidan's thoughts became calm.

*Then let's give them neither.*

---

The Shadow Alpha stopped.

Only a few feet separated the two kings.

It inhaled deeply.

Then...

It laughed.

Not with joy.

With contempt.

**Your blood is young...**

**Yet it dares call itself Crimson.**

A low growl rolled from Aidan's chest.

The sound alone shook loose leaves from the trees.

The Shadow Alpha's smile disappeared.

---

Behind Aidan...

Malik's claws dug into the earth.

*Just say the word...*

Darius lowered his massive head.

*We'll tear them apart.*

Kael remained perfectly still.

His wolf instincts were calm.

Waiting.

Lyra's crimson eyes never left the Shadow Alpha.

She sensed something none of the others did.

This creature wasn't testing strength.

It was testing leadership.

---

Rez stepped beside Mara.

His crimson blade remained lowered.

"Don't interfere."

Mara looked at him.

"They outnumber us."

Rez nodded.

"I know."

"Then why?"

"Because this isn't our fight."

His eyes remained fixed on Aidan.

"This is an Alpha's Judgment."

---

The Shadow Alpha suddenly roared.

The entire Shadow Pack answered.

Hundreds of voices erupted across the mountains.

The sound struck like a physical force.

Trees splintered.

Animals fled in terror.

Even Malik took one involuntary step backward.

---

Aidan didn't move.

His breathing remained slow.

Measured.

Then...

He inhaled.

Deeply.

The Crimson Wolf threw back his head.

His howl exploded into the heavens.

It wasn't louder.

It was greater.

The sound carried command.

Authority.

Conviction.

The Crimson Pack answered immediately.

Five voices became one.

Their howl tore through the Shadow Pack's chorus.

Not by overwhelming it...

By silencing it.

One by one...

The Shadow Wolves stopped howling.

Their ears lowered.

Not in submission.

In confusion.

They had never heard an Alpha whose voice carried such certainty.

---

The Shadow Alpha's eyes narrowed.

**Interesting...**

It lunged.

Not at Malik.

Not at Lyra.

Straight at Aidan.

The ground exploded beneath both wolves.

Their jaws collided.

The impact echoed like thunder.

Neither yielded.

Their claws tore trenches through solid stone as each tried to force the other backward.

The Crimson Wolf's crimson-and-gold eyes burned brighter.

The Shadow Alpha's black eyes swallowed the light around them.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The vampire whispered.

**Break it.**

The wolf answered.

**Overpower it.**

Aidan's heartbeat slowed.

*No.*

Both voices became quiet.

*I don't need to overpower him...*

*I need to outlead him.*

---

Aidan suddenly released the bite.

Instead of forcing forward...

He sidestepped.

The Shadow Alpha stumbled for the briefest moment.

That single mistake was enough.

The Crimson Wolf wasn't hunting alone.

He had a pack.

Lyra moved first.

She darted across the Alpha's blind side, forcing it to turn.

Malik immediately attacked from the opposite direction.

Darius crashed into its shoulder like a living avalanche.

Kael's precise strike drove the beast back toward Aidan.

Not chaos.

Coordination.

The Shadow Alpha suddenly realized something horrifying.

It wasn't fighting one wolf.

It was fighting a family.

---

The Crimson Wolf leapt.

His forepaws slammed into the Shadow Alpha's chest.

The two Alphas crashed into the earth.

The forest shook.

Aidan's jaws stopped inches from the Shadow Alpha's throat.

He could end it.

Right now.

The Shadow Alpha stared into crimson-and-gold eyes.

Waiting.

Aidan didn't bite.

He stepped back.

The entire forest fell silent.

Even the Shadow Pack looked confused.

The Shadow Alpha slowly rose.

**Why...?**

Aidan's answer wasn't spoken.

It carried through instinct itself.

*The hunt is over.*

*You've already lost.*

The Shadow Alpha looked behind itself.

Its pack wasn't charging.

They weren't fighting.

They were watching.

Watching the Crimson Pack stand shoulder to shoulder around their Alpha.

No fear.

No ambition.

Only loyalty.

For the first time in countless centuries...

The Shadow Alpha lowered its head.

Not to surrender.

To acknowledge.

The Blood Moon began to fade.

Sunlight slowly broke through the crimson clouds.

The Shadow Alpha looked at Aidan one final time.

**Perhaps...**

**...the forest has chosen a new King.**

With a single turn...

It disappeared into the shadows.

One by one...

The Shadow Pack followed.

Until only silence remained.

Aidan stood at the center of the clearing.

His crimson coat shimmered beneath the returning sunlight.

His crimson-and-gold eyes slowly swept across his pack.

No victory cry came.

No celebration.

Only a quiet realization.

Today...

The wilderness had witnessed something it had never seen before.

A king who ruled not through fear...

But through the loyalty of those who chose to follow him.

And somewhere, far beyond the forest...

Another presence opened its eyes.

This one...

Smiled.
