Aidan woke the next morning in the Sanctuary.

The Guardian stood beside the bed.

He looked exhausted.

"You scared us."

Aidan slowly sat up.

"What happened?"

The Guardian didn't answer immediately.

Instead...

He placed an ancient scroll on the bed.

Its seal bore the symbol of the Sanctuary.

Broken.

"The elders have reached a decision."

Silence filled the room.

Aidan slowly looked up.

"...What decision?"

The Guardian's voice was barely above a whisper.

"They've watched your progress."

"They've watched the Witch Authority."

"They've watched the Veil."

He closed his eyes.

Then spoke the words Aidan never expected to hear.

> **"The Sanctuary has issued a verdict."**

Another silence.

> **"You are no longer permitted to train."**

Aidan blinked.

"...What?"

The Guardian looked directly at him.

> **"From this day forward..."**

> **"...your training ends."**

> **"...Your trials begin."**

The System appeared one final time.

No quest.

No reward.

Just one line.

**The Heir has entered the Trial Era.**

Somewhere far away...

The mysterious Veil captain stood alone beneath a waterfall, finally drawing his sword for the first time.

He looked at its silver blade and quietly said:

> "Don't die yet..."

> "...Aidan."

Then he resumed training.

Because he knew something the heir did not.
