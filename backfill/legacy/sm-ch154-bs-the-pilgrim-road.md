## Chapter One Hundred Fifty-Four — The Pilgrim Road

The following morning, Aidan left the Darkheart estate before sunrise.

He wasn't answering a summons.

He wasn't chasing another mystery.

He simply needed to think.

The city still slept beneath a blanket of pale mist as he wandered toward the oldest district, where narrow brick streets and weathered buildings whispered of a past few people bothered to remember.

For the first time in weeks...

He carried no books.

No journal.

No relics.

Only the iron key rested quietly in his pocket.

The Guardian's words echoed in his thoughts.

History does not remain within walls.

It walks the world.

Aidan finally understood.

If that was true...

Then the world itself had become his classroom.

---

The streets gradually came alive.

Shopkeepers unlocked their doors.

Bakers filled the air with the scent of fresh bread.

Children laughed as they hurried toward school.

An elderly woman struggled to lift crates outside her flower shop.

Without thinking, Aidan stepped forward.

May I?

She smiled gratefully.

These old hands aren't what they used to be.

Together they carried the crates inside.

The shop was unlike any Aidan had entered before.

Every flower bore a handwritten card.

Not with prices.

With names.

Curious, he picked one up.

The Crimson Lily.

The old woman noticed.

That one grows only where great sacrifices have been made.

She gently watered another flower.

Every flower remembers the ground that raised it.

Aidan smiled politely, though the words lingered in his mind.

Before he left, she handed him a single crimson lily.

No charge.

Why?

The old woman looked into his crimson-and-gold eyes.

Some people need reminders.

Of what?

She smiled.

That even beauty grows from hardship.

---

As Aidan stepped back onto the street...

The flower in his hand remained perfectly still despite the morning breeze.

He looked at it for a long moment before carefully placing it inside his notebook.

Not because it possessed magic.

Because someone had given it with kindness.

---

Across the street...

A man lowered the morning newspaper.

Black eyes.

Charcoal coat.

The Hunter.

He watched the exchange without interrupting.

Interesting...

Again...

No audience.

No reward.

The Heir continued making the same choice.

The Hunter turned away before Aidan noticed him.

His judgment grows.

Soon...

It will be tested.

Not by strangers.

By those he loves.

---

Far beneath Blackwood Library...

The old bookseller unlocked a hidden cabinet carved into the bedrock beneath the building.

Inside rested dozens of rolled maps.

Each bore a crimson seal.

He selected one.

Its edges had become brittle with age.

Carefully spreading it across an ancient table, he revealed a network of roads stretching across continents.

Some roads ended at mountains.

Others beneath forgotten cities.

Seven symbols had been marked upon the parchment centuries earlier.

The First Foundation now glowed faintly.

The old man closed his eyes.

Just as it was written...

The Pilgrim Roads are opening.

He looked toward the staircase leading back to the library above.

Soon...

The Keepers will begin arriving.

Not because they were summoned.

Because history remembers where to find its own.

---

That evening...

Aidan returned to the Sanctuary.

Instead of finding the Guardian waiting beside the Chamber of Threads...

He found him studying the carved map of the Seven Foundations.

One more change had appeared.

A new line had been etched into the stone.

It hadn't been there yesterday.

The line stretched from House Darkheart...

Toward the eastern mountains.

The Guardian looked over his shoulder.

You see it.

Aidan nodded.

A road.

No.

The Guardian smiled.

A choice.

Every Heir reaches this point.

Until now...

History has come to you.

From this moment forward...

You must choose whether to walk toward history.

He stepped aside.

The eastern road led toward the Second Foundation.

No voice commanded Aidan to follow it.

No ancient record declared it was time.

The Living Legacy remained silent.

As it always had.

Because the next step...

Would not belong to the Legacy.

It would belong to Aidan.

Far beyond the eastern mountains...

Hidden beneath forests older than recorded civilization...

A massive stone gateway slowly opened for the first time in nearly a thousand years.

Ancient crimson vines retreated from its surface.

A single inscription became visible beneath centuries of moss.

**Only those who seek wisdom without certainty...**

**May pass beyond this gate.**

Something inside stirred.

Not with malice.

With anticipation.

The Second Foundation had awakened.

And for the first time since the First Founder entrusted history to the Keepers...

It was preparing to receive another visitor.
