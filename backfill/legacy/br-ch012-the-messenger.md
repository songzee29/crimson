At sunrise...

A lone figure entered the city.

No weapons.

No armor.

Only white robes stained with old blood.

The city guards approached carefully.

"Identify yourself."

The stranger smiled politely.

"I've come to speak with the Crimson King."

Word spread quickly.

Within minutes...

The Circle surrounded him.

Aidan stepped forward.

"You wanted to see me?"

The man slowly looked up.

Then...

Without hesitation...

Dropped to one knee.

"My King."

Aidan sighed.

"You don't have to kneel."

"I know."

"...I want to."

He reached into his robes.

Everyone prepared for an attack.

Instead...

He removed an ancient scroll.

Its edges were burned.

Its wax seal carried no crest.

Only a single crimson eye.

"I bring a declaration."

"From who?"

The messenger looked directly into Aidan's eyes.

"...The First Vampire."

The world stopped.

Even the wind disappeared.

Malik frowned.

"...That's impossible."

The messenger calmly nodded.

"So was receiving a letter from a dead king."

No one argued.
