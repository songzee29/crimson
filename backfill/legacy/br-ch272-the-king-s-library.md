As Aidan turned away from the sealed chamber...

Something caught his eye.

One of the thirteen pillars had shifted.

Hidden behind it...

A narrow hallway.

Unlike the Hall of Kings...

This place felt...

Personal.

Books lined the walls.

Old maps.

Broken weapons.

Children's toys.

A tiny wooden wolf.

A cracked chessboard missing one piece.

A faded firefighter helmet.

Aidan stopped.

"...Why is that here?"

He slowly picked it up.

The helmet dissolved into crimson dust.

The Journal appeared.

The room wasn't filled with relics of Crimson Kings.

It collected memories.

Things kings refused to forget.

Not treasures.

People.

The realization struck Aidan harder than any battle.

Kings didn't preserve gold.

They preserved love.
