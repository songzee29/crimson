😮‍💨 **Now THIS feels like the Witch Authority.**

Notice what changed:

- It isn't just a threat anymore.
- It becomes **a decree**, almost like a supernatural law.
- The System doesn't *create* it—it can only **record** it.
- Aidan doesn't say, "I'm going to kill you."

Instead, he condemns Cassian to **live**, carrying the weight of what happened until the day judgment comes.

One thing I would still be careful about, though: I'd avoid making the decree literally control fate (for example, making death impossible for Cassian), because that would make the Witch Authority so absolute that it could undermine later conflicts. Framing it as a legendary oath that resonates through the Legacy and terrifies everyone who witnesses it keeps it dark, powerful, and still leaves room for the story to unfold naturally. I think that's the sweet spot.
