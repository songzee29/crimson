## Chapter Twenty-One — The First Real Fight

The tallest teenager rushed forward with a wild right hook.

Aidan didn't panic.

He didn't try to overpower him.

He remembered the Guardian's lesson.

Strength fades.

Skill remains.

The punch cut through empty air.

Aidan shifted one step to the side.

Not fast.

Efficient.

The bully stumbled past him, surprised that he had missed.

Before the teenager could recover his balance, Aidan gently pushed his shoulder.

Nothing flashy.

Nothing brutal.

Just enough.

The boy lost his footing and crashed into a puddle.

His friends froze.

What...

How did—

The second one charged.

Unlike the first, he tried grabbing Aidan instead of throwing a punch.

Aidan watched his shoulders.

His hips.

His feet.

Every movement announced itself before it happened.

He stepped backward.

Caught the attacker's wrist.

Turned.

The teenager's own momentum sent him tumbling over Aidan's hip before landing hard on the wet pavement.

Silence.

Rain continued falling.

The youngest student stared with wide eyes.

The final bully hesitated.

He looked at his friends.

Then back at Aidan.

Something had changed.

The confidence he wore moments ago had vanished.

Who... are you?

Aidan answered honestly.

Nobody.

He picked up the wrapped practice staff.

Helped the frightened student to his feet.

You should head home.

The younger boy nodded repeatedly before running toward the street.

The three bullies remained motionless.

Aidan looked at them one last time.

You had three people against one.

Next time...

Find the courage to pick on someone your own size.

Without another word, he walked away.

He never saw the expressions on their faces.

Not fear.

Confusion.

Nothing about that fight had made sense.
