Hundreds of miles away...

Cassian dropped to one knee.

The Darkheart Brand ignited.

Not a flicker.

Not a warning.

It burned hotter than ever before.

He clenched his teeth.

"What..."

"...is happening?"

His partner rushed to his side.

"Cassian!"

He couldn't answer.

Inside his mind...

A voice echoed.

Not Aidan's.

Not the Ash King's.

Only four words.

> **"Judgment is approaching."**

The pain vanished.

Cassian's breathing slowly steadied.

But the fear remained.

Because...

For the first time...
