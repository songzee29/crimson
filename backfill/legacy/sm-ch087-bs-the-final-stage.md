## Chapter Eighty-Seven — The Final Stage

The final stage of the Leadership Trials began beneath an overcast sky.

Only twelve students remained.

Faculty members gathered around the academy courtyard, clipboards in hand.

No one explained the rules.

No one announced a winner.

The principal simply stepped forward.

Today...

There will be no written examination.

No interviews.

No speeches.

He paused, allowing his words to settle.

Today...

You will simply live your day.

Several students exchanged puzzled looks.

That's it?

The principal smiled.

That is more difficult than you think.

He dismissed them with a wave of his hand.

The trial has already begun.

---

The remaining candidates scattered across campus.

Some immediately began holding doors open.

Others loudly volunteered to carry books.

One student helped a teacher across the courtyard while making sure everyone was watching.

Aidan noticed.

They were performing kindness.

Not living it.

He quietly continued toward his first class.

The Legacy had taught him something important.

Real character existed when no one seemed to be looking.

## Chapter Eighty-Eight — The Boy Without a Voice

Halfway through lunch...

Aidan noticed a small freshman sitting alone beneath the far oak tree.

Unlike most students, the boy wasn't eating.

His lunch tray sat untouched.

Three older students laughed as they walked past, intentionally knocking the tray onto the ground.

Food scattered across the sidewalk.

The freshman didn't react.

He simply knelt and began picking everything up.

Aidan walked over without hesitation.

Need some help?

The boy nodded quietly.

Together they gathered the spilled food.

The older students stopped walking.

One smirked.

Mind your own business.

Aidan calmly looked at them.

It became my business when you decided to make it someone else's problem.

The tallest student stepped forward.

You trying to be a hero?

No.

Just trying to stop someone from acting like a coward.

The hallway fell silent.

Students nearby stopped talking.

For several seconds...

No one moved.

Then one of the older boys looked away first.

Forget it.

Let's go.

The three walked off, pretending not to care.

Aidan turned back toward the freshman.

Are you okay?

The boy hesitated before speaking.

Thank you.

Most people...

Don't notice.

Aidan smiled.

Everyone deserves to be noticed.

The freshman quietly nodded.

My name's Kael.

Aidan.

Nice to meet you.

For the first time that day...

Kael smiled.

Only half remained.

What will you do?

Because it isn't full.

Exactly.

Because leaving it empty was never an option.
