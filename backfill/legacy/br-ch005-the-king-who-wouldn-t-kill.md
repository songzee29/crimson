The town square erupted.

Black vines burst from the ground.

A creature made entirely of cursed roots rose above the rooftops.

It had no face.

Only hundreds of mouths whispering at once.

> "Destroy..."

> "Consume..."

> "Forget..."

Young hunters panicked.

Several witches prepared lethal spells.

Aidan stepped forward.

"No."

Everyone looked at him.

"They're inside it."

The Guardian's eyes widened.

"You can feel them?"

Aidan nodded.

"Every heartbeat."

He lifted both hands.

Slowly.

Calmly.

The familiar crimson symbols formed between his fingers.

One word.

> **"Mercy."**

The Seventh Law spread across the entire town.

The monster screamed.

Not in pain.

In resistance.

Thousands of trapped souls poured from its body like crimson starlight.

One by one...

The frozen townspeople collapsed to their knees.

Alive.

Free.

The creature disintegrated.

Not because it had been destroyed.

Because it had nothing left to imprison.

Silence filled the streets.

Then...

An old woman walked toward Aidan.

She looked almost ninety.

Tears streamed down her face.

"I missed my granddaughter growing up..."

She gently touched his cheek.

"...Thank you for giving me the chance to meet her."

Aidan closed his eyes.

He thought of Elias.

This time...

Someone else's family had been returned to them.
