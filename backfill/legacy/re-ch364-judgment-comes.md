House Darkheart stood silent beneath a crimson moon.

The throne room doors opened.

A scout entered.

His armor was torn.

His breathing uneven.

He immediately dropped to one knee.

"My King..."

Aidan slowly looked down from the Crimson Throne.

"...Speak."

The scout swallowed.

"We found him."

Silence filled the room.

The Crimson Assassin remained three silent steps behind the throne.

Its eyes stayed closed.

Its hand rested calmly upon its sword.

The scout continued.

"...Cassian."

Not another word was needed.

The entire room became still.

Lyra looked toward Aidan.

The Guardian slowly lowered his head.

No one interrupted.

This wasn't military intelligence.

This was destiny.

---

The scout unfolded an old map.

He pointed toward a mountain valley far beyond House Darkheart's borders.

"He calls it..."

"...Black Hollow."

"A vampire settlement."

"A coven."

"He has followers."

Another pause.

"...And..."

He hesitated.

"...He isn't alone."

Aidan's crimson eyes never changed.

"...Family."

The scout nodded.

"...Yes, my King."

Silence.

No rage.

No grief.

No hesitation.

Only certainty.

---

Inside Aidan's mind...

The Crimson Legacy quietly appeared.

---

> **Darkheart Curse**

---

> **Authority Born**

---

> **Status**

Active

---

> **Target**

Cassian

---

> **Final Human Decree**

Still in Effect

---

The page slowly turned.

Nothing had weakened.

Nothing had faded.

The decree remained exactly as powerful as the day it had been spoken.

---

Aidan slowly stood.

The Crimson Crown caught the moonlight.

He spoke only one sentence.

> **"...Prepare nothing."**

Everyone looked confused.

He continued walking.

"...This judgment..."

"...belongs to me."

---

The Crimson Assassin immediately fell into place.

Three silent steps behind its king.

---

Far away...

Black Hollow.

A peaceful valley hidden beneath endless mountains.

Children laughed through the streets.

Vampires rebuilt old homes.

Torches burned warmly against the night.

Cassian stood outside a small stone house.

He smiled.

For the first time in years...

Life felt...

Quiet.

His partner stepped outside.

"You've been staring at the moon all night."

Cassian smiled faintly.

"...Just thinking."

His partner gently took his hand.

"Come inside."

Cassian started walking.

Then...

Stopped.

A burning pain shot across both of his forearms.

The Darkheart Brand.

The crimson crest carved into his flesh ignited.

He dropped to one knee.

His partner rushed toward him.

"Cassian!"

The burning became worse.

Not enough to injure him.

Enough to remind him.

The words he had spent years trying to forget echoed inside his mind.

> **"By the Final Decree of House Darkheart..."**

> **"You shall never know peace..."**

> **"...for your soul shall never outrun my judgment."**

Cassian's breathing became uneven.

He looked around.

Nothing was there.

No enemy.

No spell being cast.

No attack.

Just...

The curse.

Still alive.

Still waiting.

Still judging.

He whispered to himself...

"...He's coming."

---

Miles away...

Aidan walked alone beneath the crimson moon.

He didn't know Cassian's thoughts.

He didn't need to.

The Darkheart Curse wasn't leading him.

It wasn't tracking him.

It was simply doing what Aidan had unknowingly created on the day he died as a human.

Judgment had reached the point in history where it was finally time to be fulfilled.

The Crimson King never changed his pace.

He simply continued walking.

Toward Black Hollow.

Toward an old promise.

Toward a man who had mistaken time...

For forgiveness.
