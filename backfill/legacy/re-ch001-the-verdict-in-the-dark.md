The city beneath the city was not supposed to exist.

That was the first lie Aidan learned.

The second was that silence meant safety.

He stood in the drowned ruins below New Orleans, where the air tasted like old stone and forgotten things. The tunnels had collapsed in places, opening into chambers that should not have survived time, water, or collapse.

But they had.

Because something was keeping them intact.

Not structure.

*Intent.*

Aidan didn’t know why he had come here.

He just remembered waking up already moving toward it.

Like something inside him had decided before he did.

That was the part he didn’t like thinking about.

So he stopped thinking about it.

For now.

---

The chamber ahead was wrong.

Not broken.

Not abandoned.

*Preserved.*

A circular space carved into ancient stone, untouched by decay. Symbols lined the floor in rings that overlapped like stitched wounds. At the center sat something that did not belong in any world that made sense.

A black book.

No dust.

No damage.

Just waiting.

Aidan stepped closer slowly.

His breath tightened without reason.

“…This is it?”

His voice echoed too cleanly.

Too perfectly.

Like the room corrected it.

The moment his shadow crossed the first ring of symbols—

the world stopped responding normally.

The air didn’t move.

The water dripping behind him froze mid-fall.

Not visually.

Conceptually.

Like reality paused to listen.

Aidan froze.

“What the hell—”

The book opened itself.

No wind.

No motion.

Just decision.

And inside it—

there were no pages.

Only darkness shaped like thought.

Then it spoke.

Not aloud.

Not inside his ears.

Inside the structure of meaning itself.

> **“BLOODLINE DETECTED.”**

Aidan stepped back instantly.

His heartbeat spiked.

“Who’s there?”

No answer.

Only deeper pressure.

The room darkened—not visually, but like comprehension itself dimmed.

Then the second voice arrived.

Older.

Heavier.

Not human.

Not alive.

> **“HEIR IS INCOMPLETE.”**

Aidan’s chest tightened.

“Rez?” he called instinctively.

Silence.

Not even a flicker.

That was new.

That was wrong.

Then—

the book shifted.

And something inside it looked back at him.

Not a face.

A structure of presence.

Like a crown made of broken rules.

And it spoke again:

> **“INITIATING BLOODBOUND VERDICT.”**

Aidan stepped back—

but the symbols beneath his feet lit up.

Not glowing.

*Activating.*

The rings on the floor began to move—not physically, but like laws rewriting themselves in real time.

Aidan’s breath caught.

“I didn’t agree to anything.”

A pause.

Then:

> **“AGREEMENT NOT REQUIRED.”**

The book closed slightly.

Like it was focusing.

Then—

Aidan felt it.

Not power.

Not energy.

Something worse.

**Understanding forced into his mind.**

Suddenly he saw it:

Not spells.

Not magic.

But rules.

The hidden structure behind reality.

Bloodlines as locked chains.

Curses as enforceable laws.

Identity as something that could be rewritten under correct authority.

Aidan stumbled.

“What… is this…”

The symbols on the floor rose into the air around him like floating judgments.

Then the system finalized:

> **“ZEE WITCH LINE — CURSE AUTHORITY LAYER GRANTED.”**

Aidan froze.

“…Witch?”

He didn’t even understand what that meant yet.

But his mind did.

And that was the problem.

Because suddenly—

he *could see too much.*

Not clearly.

Not safely.

Too much.

The world had layers now.

And he could see one of them.

---

Rez finally flickered in.

But his voice wasn’t stable.

It sounded like it had to push through something to exist.

> “Aidan… don’t move.”

Aidan turned slightly.

“I can’t see you—what is this?”

Rez hesitated.

That hesitation was the first real warning.

> “You didn’t get power.”

A pause.

> “You got the system that decides what power is allowed to exist.”

Aidan swallowed.

“That’s not an answer.”

Rez’s voice dropped lower.

> “No…”

> “It’s a warning.”

---

The book opened fully again.

And this time—

it didn’t give him abilities.

It gave him **laws he could attempt to speak into reality.**

The first three formed slowly in his mind like carved sentences:

---

## 🩸 1. VEIN REALITY SIGHT

He could see what people were underneath reality.

Not emotion.

Not aura.

But structure.

Broken paths of inheritance.

Curses written into blood like language.

---

## 🔥 2. HEX WORD AUTHORITY

Single words could bend minor reality structure.

Not full control.

Not domination.

Just interruption.

Reality hesitation.

---

## 🩸 3. ECHO FRACTURE LINK

Fragments of Zee’s past bleed into his perception under stress.

Not memory.

Overlap.

Like two timelines brushing against each other.

---

Aidan shook.

“This isn’t normal…”

Rez cut him off.

> “Nothing about this is normal.”

A pause.

> “And you were never supposed to start here.”

---

The symbols began sinking back into the floor.

The chamber dimmed.

The book closed.

Not violently.

Not dramatically.

Like a decision had been made.

And then—

it disappeared.

Not destroyed.

Not moved.

Removed from physical reality entirely.

---

Aidan stood alone in the dark.

Breathing hard.

Hands shaking.

The world felt unchanged.

But he wasn’t.

Rez spoke one last time before fading again:

> “Aidan…”

> “Whatever you just got…”

A pause.

> “That’s not the beginning of your power.”

> “That’s the beginning of your judgment.”
