The ancient forest stood silent.

Mist drifted between towering trees older than kingdoms.

Every creature hidden beneath the canopy had already sensed it.

The Crimson King had entered their territory.

---

Aidan stood at the forest's edge.

His crimson eyes slowly closed.

The breeze carried thousands of scents.

Fresh rain.

Ancient oak.

Running water.

Prey.

Predators.

Everything.

Inside his mind...

The great Crimson Wolf stood beside him.

Its crimson eyes looked toward the endless wilderness.

**This is where we belong.**

Aidan smiled.

*Then let's stop pretending otherwise.*

---

He looked back at his family.

Lyra.

Malik.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

Rez.

A slow grin spread across his face.

"Today..."

"...we hunt."

Malik cracked his neck.

"I've been waiting to hear that."

Darius laughed.

"I was starting to think we'd only be chasing paperwork."

Even Rez smiled.

"It's about time."

---

Aidan stepped into the clearing.

The Crimson Blood answered him without resistance.

His body shifted naturally.

Bones reshaped.

Muscles expanded.

His senses exploded outward.

There was no pain.

Only freedom.

A wave of Crimson energy rolled through the forest.

Where a man had stood...

A colossal Crimson Wolf now stood beneath the morning sun.

His fur was black as midnight, threaded with flowing crimson beneath every strand.

His eyes carried the calm authority of a king.

He lifted his head.

A single howl thundered across the mountains.

Not a challenge.

A summons.

---

Miles around...

Every wolf carrying Crimson Blood felt it.

Not through magic.

Through instinct.

---

Lyra smiled.

"There you are..."

The Crimson Wolf lowered his head until it rested gently against hers.

She ran her fingers through the thick fur between his ears.

"I've missed seeing this side of you."

The wolf gave a low rumble.

Almost affectionate.

Then stepped back.

---

Lyra closed her own eyes.

The Wolf Authority within her answered.

Crimson runes shimmered briefly across her skin.

Her body shifted gracefully.

Moments later...

A magnificent she-wolf stood where the future Queen had been.

Her fur was deep black with elegant crimson markings flowing along her sides like living runes.

Unlike Aidan's overwhelming presence...

Lyra's wolf radiated wisdom.

Grace.

Balance.

The Crimson Wolf looked at her.

She met his gaze without fear.

Their noses touched briefly.

No words.

None were needed.

---

Malik laughed.

"Guess we're doing this."

His transformation exploded outward.

A massive Blood Fangclaw emerged with crimson eyes blazing beneath dark fur.

He shook himself once before looking toward Aidan.

Ready.

---

Darius transformed next.

His wolf was enormous.

Heavy.

Built like an unstoppable battering ram.

He let out an excited growl that echoed through the trees.

---

Kael stepped forward.

Ancient Crimson Laws briefly glowed around his arms before dissolving into his transformation.

A powerful Runefang stood in his place.

Dark fur covered his body while crimson runes flowed naturally across his shoulders and legs.

He flexed his claws against the earth.

Smiling.

---

Only Mara remained standing.

She crossed her arms as she watched the five wolves.

"Well..."

"I suppose someone has to keep all of you alive."

Rez laughed.

"The Crimson Hexborn speaks."

Mara smiled confidently.

"I'll support the hunt."

"If something survives five wolves..."

"...it gets to deal with me."

Even Aidan's wolf gave a low amused rumble.

---

Rez adjusted the sleeves of his crimson coat.

"I'll stay on two legs."

Malik's wolf tilted his head.

Rez shrugged.

"Someone has to remind you not to level half the forest."

---

The five wolves stood together.

Founder.

Queen.

Blood Fangclaw.

Runefang.

Blood Fangclaw.

For the first time...

The Crimson Pack was complete.

Aidan looked at each of them.

He didn't need to speak.

The Wolf Authority carried his intent through instinct alone.

Run.

Hunt.

Protect one another.

The entire pack understood.

Without hesitation...

Five wolves launched into the ancient forest.

The ground thundered beneath their paws.

Trees blurred past.

Streams became effortless leaps.

Every member of the pack found their own rhythm.

Aidan led from the front.

Lyra ran at his side.

Malik darted between the trees with youthful excitement.

Kael's movements were precise and controlled.

Darius simply smashed through anything too slow to move.

Behind them...

Rez sprinted in human form, moving with impossible speed through the shadows while Mara followed, her Crimson Laws quietly weaving protective barriers around the pack's path whenever danger approached.

For the first time since House Darkheart had been founded...

The Crimson Pack hunted together.

And deep within the oldest part of the forest...

A pair of enormous golden eyes slowly opened.

Then another.

And another.

Something ancient had heard the howls of the Crimson Pack.

It rose to its feet.

The hunt...

Was no longer one-sided.
