That night...

Aidan stood alone on the castle balcony.

Rez appeared beside him in his Assassin body.

The crimson eyes that had once remained closed now watched the kingdom below.

Neither spoke for several minutes.

Finally...

Rez broke the silence.

> **"Say the word."**

Aidan didn't look at him.

*"For what?"*

> **"I'll end this tonight."**

> **"Cassian never reaches the castle."**

The wind carried the words into the darkness.

Aidan slowly shook his head.

"No."

Rez wasn't surprised.

"You still believe he can stop."

Aidan answered honestly.

"I have to."

"If I stop believing people can choose differently..."

"...then I became the man who wrote that decree."

Rez smiled.

Not because he agreed.

Because Aidan had finally become the king Zee always hoped his heir would be.
