Aidan still held Elias.

He hadn't blinked.

Not once.

Blood continued dripping from Elias' chest.

Aidan whispered...

"...You're cold."

No one answered.

"...You hate the cold."

Another silence.

"...Wake up."

Lyra slowly walked forward.

Her own tears refusing to stop.

"Aidan..."

He didn't hear her.

She reached for his shoulder.

The moment her fingers touched him—

BOOM!

A wave of crimson magic erupted outward.

Not an attack.

A reflex.

The force threw everyone backward.

Lyra crashed through a broken storefront.

Malik slammed into an overturned police cruiser.

Cassian planted his sword into the street just to remain standing.

Aidan never moved.

His subconscious...

Had begun protecting his grief.
