Branches exploded apart.

Ancient trunks blurred into streaks of black and silver.

The forest no longer looked the way Aidan remembered.

Every leaf trembled with life.

Every creature carried a heartbeat.

Every frightened breath painted a trail through the darkness.

The world had become prey.

The wounded porter stumbled through the undergrowth.

Blood dripped from his forearm.

Each crimson drop struck the earth like thunder inside Aidan’s mind.

The scent drowned everything.

Reason.

Memory.

Grief.

Only hunger remained.

He closed the distance without effort.

The porter looked back.

His eyes widened.

“No…”

He tripped over an exposed root.

Aidan landed only a few paces away.

Silent.

Perfectly still.

The injured man scrambled backward through wet leaves.

“Please…”

The word barely escaped him.

Aidan tilted his head.

The heartbeat.

Faster.

Fear made it stronger.

The scent became intoxicating.

His fangs descended another fraction.

His fingers flexed against the bark of a nearby tree.

One step.

Then another.

Behind him…

The academy bells echoed faintly through the forest.

Far away.

Meaningless.

The notification disappeared.

No explanation followed.

No comfort.

Only instinct.

Aidan lunged.

The porter screamed.

A burst of crimson light slammed between them.

The impact hurled Aidan backward through two ancient trees.

Splintered wood erupted across the clearing.

He rose before the trunks finished falling.

Lyra stood between him and the terrified man.

Both hands remained raised.

Golden runes surrounded her.

Tears filled her eyes.

“Aidan…”

He didn’t answer.

His eyes never left the blood running down the porter’s arm.

Cassian emerged beside her.

Sword drawn.

Blood still stained the cut across his own forehead.

Aidan’s gaze flickered toward it.

Another heartbeat.

Another scent.

Another temptation.

His body lowered instinctively.

Predatory.

Ready.

Cassian’s grip tightened.

“He’s gone.”

“No.”

Lyra never looked away from Aidan.

“He’s still in there.”

The Crimson Vampire smiled.

Not kindly.

Not cruelly.

Hungrily.

Then he vanished.

Cassian barely turned in time to intercept him.

Steel met impossible strength.

The sword flew from Cassian’s hand.

He crashed across the clearing.

Before Aidan could reach him…

The scent changed.

The porter had tried to run again.

Fresh blood struck the forest floor.

Everything else disappeared.

Once more…

Hunger became stronger than vengeance.

Aidan abandoned Cassian without a second glance.

The chase began again.
