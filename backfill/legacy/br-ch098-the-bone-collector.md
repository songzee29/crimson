The stairs descended beneath the cemetery.

Unlike the warehouse catacombs...

These tunnels were lined with skulls.

Thousands of them.

Each one carefully placed into the walls.

Some still wore crowns.

Others...

Still wore children's clothing.

Lyra whispered,

"Who would build something like this?"

No one answered.

Because another voice already had.

A slow clap echoed through the chamber.

One.

Two.

Three.

An old man stepped from the darkness.

His skin was almost gray.

Long white hair reached his waist.

His eyes...

Were completely crimson.

Not glowing.

Simply red.

He smiled.

"So..."

"The blood returns."

Aidan instinctively moved in front of Lyra and Malik.

"Who are you?"

The old man bowed theatrically.

"I've had many names."

He laughed softly.

"But Zee always called me..."

He stopped.

His smile faded.

"...an annoyance."

Malik blinked.

"You knew Zee too?"

"Oh..."

The old man chuckled.

"We tried killing each other for almost two hundred years."

Silence.

Then he grinned.

"He cheated."
