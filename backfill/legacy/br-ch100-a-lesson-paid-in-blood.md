The old man attacked again.

This time...

He wasn't trying to win.

He was teaching.

Every mistake Aidan made...

He punished.

Every predictable movement...

Countered.

Every reckless decision...

Answered.

Within minutes—

Aidan was covered in bruises.

His lip split.

His shoulder dislocated.

His breathing became ragged.

The old man finally stopped.

"You rely on your curse."

He pointed toward Aidan's heart.

"Fight with this."

Then toward his head.

"And this."

Finally...

He pointed toward Aidan's chest.

"But never forget this."

Aidan frowned.

"My heart?"

The old man nodded.

"Power without purpose..."

He looked toward the skull-lined walls.

"...creates places like this."

---

The words hit Aidan harder than any punch.

Because they sounded exactly like something Zee would have believed.
