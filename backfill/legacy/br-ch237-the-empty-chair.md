Back at the warehouse...

Nobody wanted to open the door.

Malik finally reached for the handle.

The familiar creak echoed through the room.

Everything looked exactly the same.

Elias' jacket still hung by the entrance.

His coffee mug still sat on the table.

His boots remained beside the couch.

Like he'd be back any minute.

Lyra looked at the kitchen.

The pan from breakfast was still on the stove.

She slowly walked over.

Touched it.

Then broke down crying.

Sienna silently wrapped her arms around her.

Malik stood in the middle of the room.

Looking around.

Completely lost.

"...He's late."

Nobody answered.

"...He's just late."

His voice cracked.

"...Right?"

Silence.

He punched the wall.

Hard.

Again.

Again.

Until his knuckles split open.

"I SAID HE'S LATE!"

The room shook with his grief.
