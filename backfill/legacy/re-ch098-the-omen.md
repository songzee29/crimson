That same night...

The Crimson Legacy awakened Aidan from his sleep.

Not with alarms.

With silence.

A new page had appeared.

No quest.

No reward.

Only a prophecy.

> **HOUSE DARKHEART RECORD 001**

> **Beware the man who smiles before he strikes.**

Another line wrote itself.

> **For he does not seek your death...**

Aidan read on.

His heartbeat slowed.

> **...he seeks the death of the man you are.**

The room fell silent.

Aidan closed the book.

Outside...

The bells of New Orleans rang three times.

No one had touched them.

Deep beneath the city...

Where Azrath's prison had once stood...

Only one thing remained.

An empty chain.

Still warm.

As if it remembered the weight it had carried for thousands of years.

And somewhere beneath the sound of the bells...

A quiet voice laughed.

Not because victory had come.

Because...

It was getting closer.
