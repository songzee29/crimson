That evening...

Lyra found Aidan alone in the royal gardens.

The same gardens where the ancient tree had once turned to ash.

She quietly slipped her hand into his.

"You've been carrying this alone."

"I know."

"You don't have to."

Aidan looked at her.

"I wrote the decree."

Lyra gently shook her head.

"No."

"A grieving man wrote the decree."

"I married the king."

She rested her forehead against his.

"I know the difference."

For the first time in days...

Aidan smiled.

"I love you."

She smiled back.

"I know."

Neither of them realized...

High above the castle walls...

A pair of eyes watched them from the darkness.

Then disappeared.
