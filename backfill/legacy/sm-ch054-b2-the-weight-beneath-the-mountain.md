# Chapter Fifty-Four: The Weight Beneath the Mountain

Darkness pressed against Aidan from every side.

Stone covered his legs.

His right arm remained pinned beneath a slab of broken rock.

Every breath sent knives through his fractured ribs.

He opened his eyes.

Nothing.

Not even a sliver of light reached him.

The silence lasted only a heartbeat.

Then...

"Aidan!"

Lyra.

Faint.

Somewhere above.

"I'm here..."

The words barely escaped his throat.

Too weak.

No one answered.

He tried to move.

The stone didn't.

Pain exploded through his shoulder.

He stopped.

Not because he wanted to.

Because his body finally refused.

Blood slowly ran beneath his coat.

Warm.

He smelled it immediately.

His breathing changed.

His stomach tightened.

No.

Not now.

He shut his eyes.

Focused on the distant voices instead.

Cassian shouting.

Malik moving debris despite his damaged leg.

Rowan calmly directing everyone.

Helena ordering healers into position.

They hadn't stopped.

They never stopped.

...

Hours passed.

Or minutes.

He couldn't tell.

Time no longer mattered beneath the mountain.

The second heartbeat continued beneath his own.

Steady.

Patient.

Waiting.

His thoughts drifted.

Not to victory.

Not to the next battle.

To the old man in Red Creek.

*You came... just... too late.*

The words refused to leave him.

Another memory followed.

Grey Hollow.

The bridge.

The children.

The buried instructors.

Every face.

Every apology.

Every promise.

He had survived all of it.

Yet someone always paid.

What good was surviving...

If he kept arriving too late?

...

Above him...

The rescue continued.

Cassian slammed another stone aside with his good arm.

The effort nearly dropped him to one knee.

Malik shoved collapsed beams away despite his permanent limp.

Blood seeped through the brace around Elias's injured leg as he reinforced unstable tunnels with trembling runes.

Lyra ignored Helena's orders to rest.

She dug until her hands bled.

No one mentioned stopping.

Rowan quietly looked at the exhausted faces around him.

"He'd still be digging for us."

No one needed more reason than that.

...

Far below...

Aidan heard it.

Not his friends.

Something else.

One slow footstep.

Then another.

Not close.

Watching.

His body tensed instinctively.

He listened.

Nothing followed.

Had he imagined it?

He couldn't be certain anymore.

That uncertainty frightened him.

He reached weakly toward his staff.

Still there.

His fingers closed around it.

If something came...

He would stand.

Even if standing killed him.

...

Stone shifted above.

A beam of light pierced the darkness.

"Aidan!"

This time he heard Lyra clearly.

He managed the faintest answer.

"I'm..."

He coughed blood.

"...here."

The digging became frantic.

Hope surged through the tunnel.

Another stone moved.

Another.

Then—

The entire ceiling groaned.

Rowan's expression changed instantly.

"Back!"

Too late.

The weakened chamber collapsed again.

Fresh rock thundered into the opening.

Lyra disappeared beneath a cloud of dust.

Cassian barely pulled her backward before another section gave way.

The tunnel sealed itself again.

Silence returned.

Below...

Aidan stared at the place where the light had vanished.

They had been so close.

His hand slowly fell away from the opening.

Hope had reached him.

Then the mountain had taken it back.

# Chapter Fifty-Five: Another Scar

Blackwood Academy worked through the entire night.

No one slept.

Fresh rescue teams replaced exhausted ones every hour.

Even first-year students carried broken stone away from the collapse.

The academy itself seemed determined not to lose another person.

...

Professor Helena stood beside Rowan.

"He won't survive another collapse."

Rowan never looked away from the mountain.

"Then we won't give it one."

"He has internal bleeding."

"I know."

"He hasn't eaten properly in weeks."

"I know."

"He's exhausted."

"I know."

She lowered her voice.

"We're running out of time."

For the first time...

The old master's shoulders seemed heavier.

"So is he."

...

Below...

Aidan's breathing had become slower.

Not calmer.

Weaker.

His side no longer hurt the way it had.

That frightened him.

Pain meant he was still fighting.

The absence of it felt wrong.

He forced himself awake.

He refused to drift.

His hearing reached upward again.

Stone.

Voices.

Tools striking rock.

Then...

Something else.

A faint scratching.

Not above.

Behind him.

His body reacted before his thoughts.

He turned as much as the stone allowed.

Darkness.

Nothing visible.

The scratching stopped.

He waited.

Minutes passed.

No sound returned.

Had it been loose rock?

His imagination?

Or something watching from another tunnel?

He couldn't know.

The not knowing settled into him like another wound.

...

At last...

A narrow crack opened above his shoulder.

A hand reached through.

Cassian's.

"I've got you."

His voice was rough.

Exhausted.

Real.

Aidan reached back.

Their hands locked together.

Stone shifted.

More hands reached through.

Malik.

Rowan.

Together they widened the opening enough to pull him free.

The moment Aidan reached the surface...

His legs gave out.

He didn't even try to stand.

Helena was already beside him.

She pressed both hands against his side.

Blood soaked through immediately.

"We're losing him!"

Healers rushed forward.

Lyra dropped to her knees beside him.

Tears streaked through the dust covering her face.

"You idiot..."

Her voice shook.

"You told us you'd be right behind them."

Aidan looked at her.

A faint smile almost appeared.

"I'm..."

His voice barely carried.

"...sorry."

She shook her head fiercely.

"No."

She gripped his hand.

"No more apologizing."

His eyes slowly closed.

Not from peace.

From exhaustion.

As Helena fought to keep him conscious...

Aidan heard the academy around him.

Boots.

Voices.

Prayers.

Someone crying.

Someone thanking him for saving the apprentices.

He felt none of it.

Only emptiness.

He had survived again.

The cost...

Was another piece of himself he could no longer find.

Far beyond Blackwood's walls...

Rain began falling over the northern forests.

Whatever waited there remained unseen.

Unanswered.

And while Blackwood struggled simply to keep its Crimson Witch alive...

Another burden was already beginning somewhere beyond the rain.
