Two days later...

The gates of House Darkheart opened.

No announcement had been sent.

No messenger had arrived.

Yet everyone already knew.

Their King...

Had returned.

The moment Aidan crossed the front gate...

Every Darkheart banner hanging from the castle walls began moving despite the complete absence of wind.

Ancient enchantments woven into the stone awakened.

The castle itself recognized him.

The Guardian smiled faintly.

"...Welcome home..."

"...Your Majesty."

---

Lyra stood frozen.

She immediately noticed something different.

Not the crowns.

Not the crimson markings.

His presence.

Before...

Walking beside Aidan felt dangerous.

Now...

Standing near him felt like standing beside an entire kingdom.

The pressure wasn't oppressive.

It was absolute.
