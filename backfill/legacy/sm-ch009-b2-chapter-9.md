# Chapter Nine  
## The Fracture That Remained

The celebrations ended before dawn.

The rescued merchants slept beneath Blackwood Academy's protection.

No one noticed Aidan quietly leaving the infirmary.

Every breath hurt.

Three broken ribs wrapped tightly beneath fresh bandages protested each step, while the claw wounds across his back burned beneath his coat.

Helena had ordered him to remain in bed.

He lasted less than an hour.

The eastern courtyard stood empty beneath a pale morning sky.

Aidan rested one hand against a repaired stone pillar and slowly expanded his Witch Authority.

The academy unfolded within his mind.

Students sleeping.

Faculty changing patrol rotations.

Runes humming beneath ancient foundations.

Everything felt...

Normal.

Then—

A gap.

Not in the wards.

Not in the barriers.

In the mountain itself.

His eyes opened.

The sensation vanished immediately.

...

"You felt it."

Rowan stood beneath the old oak.

Aidan nodded once.

"Something beneath the academy."

"I felt it too."

Neither man spoke for several moments.

Finally Rowan said, "Whatever has begun outside our walls..."

He looked toward the distant mountains.

"...is pushing inward."

"It isn't attacking Blackwood."

"No."

Rowan's expression darkened.

"It's looking for something."

...

Later that morning Professor Helena gathered the senior students inside the Archive Hall.

Ancient maps covered every table.

Several had been unrolled for the first time in decades.

Cassian pointed toward the northern pass.

"The creatures that attacked the caravan weren't feeding."

"They were searching," Elias agreed.

"For what?" Malik asked.

Nobody answered.

Aidan stared quietly at one weathered map.

Beneath Blackwood Academy...

Old tunnels.

Most had collapsed centuries ago.

One remained unlabeled.

Just a faded passage disappearing beneath the mountain.

"There."

Everyone followed his finger.

Helena frowned.

"That section shouldn't exist."

"But it does."

She hesitated.

"The records stop."

"Why?"

"I don't know."

Silence settled across the room.

Blackwood Academy had survived countless generations.

Yet even its oldest records contained blind spots.

...

By afternoon they stood before an ancient stone staircase hidden beneath the western library.

Dust covered every step.

No footprints.

No recent movement.

Rowan rested his hand against the sealed entrance.

"I'll go."

Aidan stepped forward.

"I should."

"You were nearly killed yesterday."

"And if something is already beneath the academy?"

Their eyes met.

Neither man blinked.

Finally Rowan stepped aside.

"You're not going alone."

"I know."

Lyra moved beside him.

"So am I."

Cassian quietly drew his sword.

Malik rolled one shoulder despite lingering bruises.

Elias adjusted the fresh brace around his injured leg.

Nobody argued.

Nobody stayed behind.

...

The tunnel descended deep beneath Blackwood.

Cold air drifted upward.

The walls were carved from black stone older than the academy itself.

Every few yards strange grooves scarred the rock.

Not runes.

Claw marks.

Fresh ones.

"They came through here," Cassian whispered.

Aidan nodded.

His ribs throbbed with every step.

His body begged him to stop.

He ignored it.

The passage widened into an enormous underground chamber.

Ancient pillars stretched into darkness.

Collapsed bridges crossed a bottomless chasm.

Then...

They found them.

Three more merchants.

Alive.

Bound.

Unconscious.

"No guards?" Malik whispered.

"Too easy," Cassian answered.

Aidan didn't move.

Something felt wrong.

Not danger.

Expectation.

The second heartbeat answered.

THUMP.

His head turned sharply toward the darkness beyond the pillars.

"I know you're there."

His voice echoed through the chamber.

Silence.

Then—

A single stone struck the floor.

Click.

Another.

Click.

Then dozens.

Something enormous moved through the darkness.

Not rushing.

Walking.

Slowly.

Purposefully.

Each step shook dust from the ceiling.

The shape emerged.

Twice the height of any man.

Its body resembled carved black stone wrapped around pale bone.

Four long arms ended in hooked claws.

Its eyeless face slowly turned toward Aidan.

Unlike the creatures before...

This one did not scream.

It studied him.

Then...

It ignored everyone else.

Every claw pointed toward Aidan.

"It came for you," Lyra whispered.

The creature moved.

The chamber exploded.

Aidan barely raised a barrier before the first strike shattered the stone beneath his feet.

He flew backward across the cavern.

Broken ribs screamed.

His barrier cracked.

The creature never looked at another target.

Only him.

"Malik!"

Malik slammed into its side.

It didn't even stagger.

Cassian struck from behind.

One blade carved across black stone.

Sparks flew.

No blood.

The creature backhanded him through a pillar.

Stone exploded.

"Cassian!"

Lyra rushed toward him.

Elias unleashed binding runes.

Golden chains wrapped around two of the creature's arms.

They snapped apart almost instantly.

Too strong.

Far too strong.

"Aidan!"

Rowan's voice echoed from the tunnel entrance.

He had followed.

Not to lead.

To reinforce.

"Think!"

Aidan forced himself upright despite the agony ripping through his side.

Not stronger.

Smarter.

His Witch instincts searched.

The chamber.

The pillars.

The bridges.

The cracks running across the ceiling.

He smiled.

"There."

Both hands rose.

Not toward the creature.

Toward the cavern itself.

"Hold."

Every fractured support pillar froze exactly where it stood.

"Malik!"

The giant understood too late.

Malik lowered his shoulder and drove into one weakened column with everything he had.

The frozen stone remained standing.

For one heartbeat.

Then Aidan released the Law.

The cavern collapsed.

Thousands of tons of ancient rock thundered downward.

The creature vanished beneath the avalanche.

The entire mountain shook.

...

Nobody spoke during the climb back to the surface.

The rescued merchants survived.

Cassian's left arm hung uselessly at his side.

Malik limped.

Elias leaned heavily against Lyra.

Aidan's bandages had turned crimson again.

Halfway up the staircase...

His vision blurred.

Dark spots filled his sight.

He reached for the wall.

Missed.

The second heartbeat pounded.

THUMP.

For one impossible instant...

He smelled blood.

Not from himself.

From everyone.

Warm.

Alive.

His mouth went dry.

His heartbeat accelerated.

No...

Not his heartbeat.

The other one.

"Aidan."

Lyra's voice sounded distant.

"Aidan!"

He blinked.

The scent disappeared.

She stood directly in front of him, both hands on his shoulders.

"You stopped walking."

He stared at her.

"I..."

His voice failed.

She searched his face.

"What happened?"

He looked away.

"I don't know."

It wasn't true anymore.

He no longer knew what belonged to him...

And what didn't.

...

That night the infirmary overflowed again.

Fresh fractures.

Fresh stitches.

Fresh exhaustion.

Helena silently treated one patient after another.

When she reached Aidan she frowned.

"You reopened everything."

"I know."

"No."

She looked directly into his eyes.

"You aren't healing the way you should."

He had no answer.

Long after everyone else slept...

He remained awake.

Outside, rain struck the windows.

Inside, the academy breathed quietly around him.

Then—
