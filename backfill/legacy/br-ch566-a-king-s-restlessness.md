The castle slept.

Aidan did not.

The moonlight spilled across the Crimson Throne as he sat alone in the empty hall.

No guards.

No council.

Only silence.

His fingers rested against the arm of the throne.

The second heartbeat echoed.

**THUMP.**

Then another.

**THUMP.**

The Wolf opened its eyes.

> **"...You're waiting."**

Aidan nodded.

"...Yes."

> **"...For what?"**

Aidan looked toward the great doors.

"...For whoever walked through my kingdom without fear."
