Nobody wanted to open the door.

Malik finally reached for the handle.

The familiar creak echoed through the room.

Everything looked exactly the same.

Elias' jacket still hung by the entrance.

His coffee mug still sat on the table.

His boots remained beside the couch.

Like he'd be back any minute.

Hard.

Again.

Again.
