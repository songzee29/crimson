## 3. Additional chapter-number discontinuity

On June 30, the main Reimagined thread contains a Chapter 211 response titled **“Instinct”** (source `347bbd7e-4ff7-4d91-b0f8-447d6cc4a9b1`, 6,112 characters). This is another high-numbered entry appearing after the low-numbered reset. It should remain in its source run rather than be forced into the June 28 Chapters 2–42 sequence.

The same conversation later contains the July 7 Book III / Fourth Judgment sequence, including the First Demon Hexborn and Crimson Demon chapters. That transition is a new story state, not proof that every intervening chapter from 43 through 210 exists in the same track.

## 4. Why these jumps cannot be “repaired” by renumbering

The export preserves:
- a June 27 Chapter 175–180 batch;
- a June 28 high-numbered run at Chapters 279–334;
- a June 28 low-numbered restart at Chapters 2–42;
- a June 30 Chapter 211 response;
- July 7–8 Book III chapters 177–178 and Codex records.

This chronology proves that the source is a set of overlapping manuscript tracks, not one uninterrupted numerical sequence. The correct archival response is to assign separate track IDs and source provenance, not to rename chapters or invent missing transitions.
