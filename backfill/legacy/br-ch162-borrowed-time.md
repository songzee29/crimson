Cassian drove his fist into Aidan's stomach.

Blood sprayed across the mud.

Aidan dropped to one knee.

Cassian raised his sword.

For the first time since the battle began...

He had a clean shot.

He swung.

CLANG!!

Aidan caught the blade.

Bare-handed.

The steel bit into his palm.

Blood poured through his fingers.

He smiled.

"...Not..."

His arm shook violently.

"...yet."

Then...

He whispered again.

> **"...Sixth Law..."**

Cassian's eyes widened.

"...Again?!"

Crimson runes exploded across Aidan's body.

His shattered ribs pulled together.

His shoulder straightened.

His breathing steadied just enough.

The System answered.

---

> **《CRIMSON RESTORATION》**

> **Physical Recovery: 23%**

> **Authority Reserves: 11%**

> **Warning...**

> **Critical Depletion Imminent**

---

Aidan lowered his hand.

He could barely feel his fingers now.

His legs were numb.

His heartbeat had become irregular.

He looked at Cassian...

And smiled.

Not because he felt strong.

Because...

He had bought himself...

One more round.

---

Cassian just stared.

His sword slowly lowered.

For the first time...

He wasn't angry.

He was stunned.

He whispered to himself...

"...How..."

"...are you still standing?"

Aidan looked toward the dark sky.

Then back at Cassian.

His voice was barely audible.

> **"...Because..."**

A long pause.

> **"...someone has to."**

Silence.

Even the wind stopped.

Lyra covered her mouth.

Malik lowered his head.

The Guardian quietly closed his eyes.

Even Azrath...

Didn't smile.

Not this time.

Because standing before them...

Wasn't just the Crimson King.

It was a man who had gone far beyond his limits...

And somehow...

Still refused to fall.
