The warehouse had become louder.

Not because of battles.

Because of Elias.

He somehow managed to make every place feel alive.

He fixed the leaking roof.

Cooked dinner.

Actually cooked dinner.

Malik nearly cried after taking the first bite.

"...This..."

"...This has seasoning."

Elias laughed.

"That's usually how food works."

Lyra smiled.

"I think Malik thought salt was optional."

"I DID MY BEST!"

Even Sienna laughed.

Aidan sat quietly watching them.

Elias noticed.

"You thinking again?"

"...Maybe."

Elias tossed him an apple.

"You overthink."

"I don't."

"You do."

"I don't."

"You've been staring at that wall for five minutes."

"...I was thinking."

"My point exactly."

Everyone laughed.

Even Aidan.

Again.

---

Later that night...

The two of them sat on the warehouse roof.

Looking over the city.

Elias finally spoke.

"You know..."

"...it's okay to let people carry some of the weight."

Aidan looked over.

"I don't know how."

"I figured."

Silence.

Then Elias gently bumped his shoulder.

"So we'll teach you."

Aidan looked confused.

"We?"

Elias smiled toward the lights inside the warehouse.

"Your family."

Those words stayed with Aidan long after they stopped talking.
