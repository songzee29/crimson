Aidan stood in the middle of Royal Street.

Around him...

The three hunters struggled to breathe.

Not dead.

Broken.

One crawled backward.

Terrified.

"W-what..."

"...are you?"

Aidan stared.

He didn't understand the question.

He only heard the heartbeat.

The hunter's pulse accelerated.

Fear.

Blood pressure rising.

The scent became stronger.

The hunger answered.

Aidan reached down...

Grabbing the man by the throat.

The hunter screamed.

His heartbeat pounded.

Then...

Aidan stopped.

Not because of mercy.

Because...

Another heartbeat echoed louder.

Much louder.

Hundreds of them.

Emergency responders.

Police.

Soldiers.

More prey.

His head slowly turned toward the sound.

The hunter slipped from his grasp.

Forgotten.

Aidan disappeared before the man hit the ground.
