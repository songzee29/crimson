Caelum looked at Aidan one last time.

"This isn't over."

"The Veil Dominion will come."

"Not for you."

He pointed toward the Crimson Assassin.

"...For that."

Without another word...

He vanished.

The chamber finally became quiet.

The Guardian released a long breath.

"You have no idea..."

"...what you've awakened."

Aidan looked at the Crimson Assassin.

The silent figure slowly turned toward him.

Waiting.

The Legacy remained silent.

No quest.

No reward.

Just observation.

For a long moment...

Neither moved.

Then...

Aidan gave his first command.

"...Follow."

The Crimson Assassin lowered its head once.

It didn't kneel.

It didn't salute.

It simply took one silent step behind the Crimson King.

Exactly three paces back.

Never closer.

Never farther.

As if that distance had been engraved into its very existence.

The Guardian watched them leave.

One king.

One silent assassin.

Walking into the New Orleans night.

He whispered words he never thought he'd say again.

> **"House Darkheart... has two shadows once more."**

---

Far away...

Hidden beneath layers of forgotten magic...

An ancient throne carved from obsidian cracked.

A pair of silver eyes opened.

Not vampire.

Not witch.

Not wolf.

Something older.

A voice echoed through the darkness.

> **"The Crimson Assassin walks again..."**

A pause.

Then a smile.

> **"...Wake the Monarchs."**

Across the world...

Ancient seals began to break.

Not because Aidan had become stronger.

But because the return of the Crimson Assassin confirmed a terrifying truth.

House Darkheart...

Was rebuilding its dynasty.

And history had learned long ago...

When House Darkheart rebuilt...

The world bled.
