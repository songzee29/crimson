Two days later...

The warehouse door burst open.

A young man stumbled inside carrying an unconscious woman over his shoulder.

He couldn't have been older than twenty-two.

Dark brown skin.

Short dreadlocks tied behind his head.

A torn leather jacket covered in blood.

Not his.

He looked around desperately.

"I was told..."

"...the Crimson Circle helps people."

Everyone immediately moved.

Lyra rushed to the injured woman.

Sienna began treating the wounds.

Malik grabbed medical supplies.

Aidan walked over.

"What happened?"

The young man exhaled heavily.

"Veil hunters."

"They attacked our convoy."

"You survived?"

"Barely."

He finally set the woman down.

Then looked directly at Aidan.

"...You're him."

"The heir."

Aidan frowned.

"You know me?"

The young man smiled tiredly.

"I know your father."

Silence.

The room froze.

"I..."

"...served beside Zee."

The Guardian slowly stood.

Recognition filled his eyes.

"...No..."

"...It can't be."

The young man looked at him.

"It has been a long time."

The Guardian whispered one name.

"...Cyrus."

The young man nodded.

"So you remember."

---

Aidan's heart began racing.

Another person.

Another piece of Zee's past.

But this one...

Was different.

He wasn't a legend.

He wasn't mysterious.

He wasn't hiding.

He looked...

Broken.
