The meeting ended long after midnight.

Nobody slept.

Elias found Aidan sitting alone outside.

Again.

He smiled.

"You know..."

"...one day you're gonna wear a hole in that rooftop."

Aidan laughed quietly.

"I've had worse habits."

Elias sat beside him.

For several minutes...

Neither spoke.

Finally...

Elias broke the silence.

"You remember the first time we met?"

"The grocery bags?"

"You thought I had the wrong warehouse."

"You did."

"...Yeah."

They both laughed.

Then Elias became serious.

"If something happens..."

Aidan immediately interrupted.

"Don't."

"I'm serious."

"No."

"Listen to me."

"I said no."

Elias looked directly into his eyes.

"You don't get to decide when people love you enough to protect you."

Silence.

Aidan looked away.

"...I don't want to lose anyone else."

Elias smiled sadly.

"I know."

He reached over...

And pulled Aidan into a hug.

Not dramatic.

Not emotional.

Just...

A brother hugging his brother.

"I got you."

For the first time since losing his parents...

Aidan allowed himself to believe those words.
