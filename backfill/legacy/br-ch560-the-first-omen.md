Night fell over House Darkheart.

Lyra wandered into the royal gardens alone.

The moon reflected across a quiet pond.

She smiled.

For the first time in weeks...

She felt at peace.

Then...

A single crimson flower growing beside the water suddenly withered.

She frowned.

Another flower died.

Then another.

The entire garden began to wilt.

Not from disease.

Not from winter.

Something unseen passed through it.

Lyra slowly backed away.

"...Aidan?"

No answer.

A cold wind swept across the garden.

For just an instant...

She saw a shadow standing beneath an ancient tree.

Tall.

Still.

Watching.

She blinked.

It was gone.

The flowers remained dead.

Back inside the castle...

Aidan suddenly stood upright.

His Wolf Authority roared inside his soul.

> **"...RUN!"**

He vanished from the throne room in a crimson blur.

By the time he reached the gardens...

Lyra was safe.

But the ancient tree where the shadow had stood...

Had turned completely to ash.

Nothing remained.

Not even roots.

Only gray dust drifting into the night.

Aidan knelt beside the ashes.

His expression became colder than anyone had ever seen.

This wasn't an attack.

It was a message.

Someone...

Had reached the heart of House Darkheart.

Without crossing its walls.

The second heartbeat thundered.

**THUMP!**

Far away...

Cassian looked toward the same moon.

The Darkheart Brand burned hotter than ever.

And hidden somewhere between both kingdoms...

A cloaked figure smiled.

The pieces were almost in place.

The road toward the Third Judgment...

Had truly begun.
