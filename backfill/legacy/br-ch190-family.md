Back in the Sanctuary...

Elias found Aidan sitting alone.

Again.

He tossed him a sandwich.

"You skipped breakfast."

"I wasn't hungry."

"Liar."

"I wasn't."

"You've been staring at a blank journal for three hours."

Aidan looked away.

"...It isn't blank anymore."

Elias sat beside him.

Neither spoke.

After a while...

Elias quietly asked,

"You ever wonder what your parents would've thought?"

Aidan looked down.

"Every day."

"They'd be proud."

"You don't know that."

"I do."

"How?"

Elias smiled.

"Because I've watched you."

He pointed toward the city above them.

"You save people."

"You blame yourself."

"You keep trying."

"You still laugh."

Then he looked directly at Aidan.

"Good people usually come from good people."

Aidan couldn't answer.

Not because he disagreed.

Because no one had ever said something like that to him before.
