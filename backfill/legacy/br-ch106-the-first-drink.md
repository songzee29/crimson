The cathedral held its breath.

Rain tapped against broken stained glass.

The crimson vial hovered inches from Cassian's lips.

His hand trembled.

Not from fear.

From anticipation.

He could still walk away.

He knew that.

Azrath wasn't there.

No whisper.

No command.

No manipulation.

Just...

A choice.

Cassian closed his eyes.

"...I refuse..."

A long pause.

"...to stand beneath another man's crown."

He drank.

---

Nothing happened.

Cassian lowered the empty vial.

"...That's it?"

Silence.

He laughed.

"You call this power?"

The laugh stopped.

His heart skipped once.

Then again.

Then...

It stopped.

For one terrifying second...

Everything inside him died.
