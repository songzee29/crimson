The Ash King finally turned toward Cassian.

The warmth vanished from his face.

"Cassian."

Cassian slowly rose to one knee.

"I know."

"You came for me."

The Ash King shook his head.

"No."

"I came because Judgment has been fulfilled."

Cassian frowned.

"What?"

The Ash King pointed toward Aidan.

"His Judgment."

Then toward Lyra.

"Her sacrifice."

Finally...

Toward Cassian himself.

"And your choice."

Silence.

The Ash King spoke with absolute certainty.

> **"No curse forced your hand."**

> **"No king commanded you."**

> **"No god decided your path."**

> **"You chose."**

Cassian closed his eyes.

"...I know."

A single tear fell onto the stone floor.

The weight of that choice would remain with him forever.
