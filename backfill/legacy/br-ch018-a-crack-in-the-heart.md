That night...

Cassian sat alone beside the ruins.

He didn't sleep.

He didn't cry.

He simply stared.

Hours passed.

The moon climbed higher.

Then...

The Darkheart Brand burned again.

Not with pain.

With memory.

He saw Aidan...

As a young man.

Laughing.

Dreaming.

Believing they could one day change the world.

The vision shifted.

He saw himself.

Full of anger.

Full of certainty.

Certain that killing Aidan had been the only answer.

The memory shattered.

Cassian buried his face in his hands.

"...What have we done to each other?"

No one answered.
