As Aidan approached...

The throne awakened.

Not physically.

Spiritually.

Around it...

Transparent figures began appearing.

One after another.

Ancient kings.

Ancient queens.

Leaders from forgotten civilizations.

Each wore a different crown.

Each carried different burdens.

None spoke.

They simply watched.

Judging.

Waiting.

Then...

One by one...

They bowed to Aidan.

Not because he was stronger.

Because they recognized the burden he carried.

The burden of placing his people before himself.

The burden of making promises he intended to keep.

The Wolf lowered its head.

> **"...They acknowledge you."**
