Morning.

Aidan addressed the city.

More villages had been found.

All alive.

Hidden.

Confused.

Someone had moved them miles from their homes.

Families reunited.

The city celebrated.

People cheered louder than ever.

"The Crimson King found them!"

"He never gave up!"

Aidan smiled.

But inside...

Something bothered him.

Azrath could have killed them.

He didn't.

Why?

The question haunted him.

That evening...

The Guardian found him alone.

"I know that look."

Aidan sighed.

"He had every opportunity."

"...and chose not to."

The Guardian nodded slowly.

"That's why he's dangerous."

Aidan looked up.

"I don't understand."

The old man answered quietly.

"If a monster only destroys..."

"...you know how to fight it."

He looked toward the city lights.

"But if a monster makes you question..."

"...yourself..."

"...the battle has already begun."
