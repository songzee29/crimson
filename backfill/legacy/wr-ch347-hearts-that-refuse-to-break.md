Night settled over Blackwood Academy.

No stars pierced the clouds.

Only the crimson-black glow above the old oak illuminated the grounds.

Faculty witches rotated through the protective circles without rest.

Every hour…

Another ward weakened.

Every hour…

Another was restored.

The academy was enduring.

Not winning.

⸻

Aidan stood alone on the balcony overlooking the courtyard.

The whisper returned.

Clearer now.

You cannot protect them all.

He closed his eyes.

He didn’t answer.

Choose one.

Lose the rest.

His breathing remained steady.

Footsteps approached.

Lyra quietly stepped beside him.

“I knew you’d come here.”

He smiled faintly.

“How?”

“You always carry heavy things by yourself first.”

She rested her forearms on the stone railing.

“You don’t have to.”

Neither looked at the other.

They simply watched the old oak sway beneath an invisible wind.

After several minutes…

Aidan finally spoke.

“I’m afraid.”

Lyra didn’t pretend otherwise.

“So am I.”

The honesty itself felt like relief.

⸻

Elsewhere…

Malik and Elias reinforced another ward beneath Professor Helena’s guidance.

Neither complained.

Neither joked.

Until Elias finally looked sideways.

“You almost followed that voice today.”

Malik nodded.

“I know.”

“I almost did too.”

Malik blinked.

“You heard something?”

Elias quietly smiled.

“My brother.”

Silence settled between them.

Then Malik extended his fist.

“We don’t follow them.”

Elias bumped it.

“We follow each other.”

The ward beneath them immediately brightened.

Professor Helena watched without saying a word.

The Foundation had been right.

Trust strengthened magic.

⸻

Near the academy cemetery…

Cassian stood alone.

Not because he wanted solitude.

Because he wanted to prove he could choose it.

Aidan quietly joined him.

Neither spoke.

Finally Cassian laughed softly.

“I kept expecting you.”

“You did?”

“You always show up.”

Aidan smiled.

“I’m trying to make a habit of it.”

Cassian looked toward the dark cemetery.

“I don’t know if I deserve that.”

Aidan answered immediately.

“I wasn’t asked to judge whether you deserve it.”

“I chose.”

Cassian’s eyes quietly filled.

He looked away before anyone could notice.

“Thank you.”

⸻

Far above them…

The crimson-black light surrounding the old oak suddenly expanded.

Not violently.

Deliberately.

Its shadow stretched across the academy.

Across every dormitory.

Across every classroom.

Across every heart.

The whisper returned to Aidan one final time that night.

Not mocking.

Certain.

Soon…

The Crimson Legacy appeared before him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FINAL CRIMSON WITCH PREPARATION

The Heir continues choosing others before himself.

Character remains stronger than corruption.

One final lesson remains.

Prepare.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The message disappeared.

Aidan looked toward the ancient oak.

Toward the growing darkness surrounding it.

Then toward the lights still burning inside Blackwood Academy.

As long as those lights remained…

So would he.

Even if the Witch Curse had already begun its final judgment.
