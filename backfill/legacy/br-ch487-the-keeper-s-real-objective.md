The Keeper watched everything.

It never attacked again.

Instead...

It slowly began laughing.

Not loudly.

Softly.

Almost...

Satisfied.

Aidan frowned.

"...Why are you smiling?"

The Keeper pointed toward the wound on Aidan's side.

> **"...Because..."**

A pause.

> **"...The seal weakens."**

Silence fell.

Lyra's face turned pale.

The Keeper continued.

> **"...Every drop of Crimson Blood..."**

> **"...feeds what sleeps beneath it."**

Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf immediately snarled.

> **"...Liar."**

The Keeper slowly nodded.

> **"...Half truth."**

Then...

For the first time...

It looked directly at Lyra.

> **"...Your seal..."**

> **"...cannot last forever."**

Lyra tightened her fists.

"I know."
