Aidan didn't leave immediately.

He remained sitting in the center of the chamber long after the System went silent.

The word *pending* echoed through his thoughts.

Not because it was threatening.

Because it was honest.

The System hadn't promised power.

It hadn't promised destiny.

It hadn't even promised survival.

Only cost.

And somehow that felt more real than anything else it had said.

---

The tunnel lights above flickered weakly.

Aidan stared at the empty pedestal where the Crimson Book had once rested.

Part of him still expected it to return.

To explain itself.

To tell him he had misunderstood everything.

But the pedestal remained empty.

The book had done its job.

Now he was alone with what it left behind.

---

A sudden pressure built behind his eyes.

Not pain.

Information.

The sensation hit so fast that Aidan doubled over.

His hands slammed against the stone floor.

"What—"

The System appeared instantly.

---

> **NEW DIRECTIVE AVAILABLE**

---

Aidan froze.

The words continued.

---

> **BLOODBOUND VERDICT: QUEST GENERATED**

---

The chamber suddenly felt much colder.

---

> **QUEST I**

> **THE BROKEN NAME**

> **OBJECTIVE: FIND THE GIRL WHO DOES NOT EXIST**

---

Aidan blinked.

"What?"

---

The System ignored him.

---

> **TIME LIMIT: 7 DAYS**

> **REWARD: CURSE AUTHORITY GROWTH**

> **FAILURE: UNKNOWN**

---

The message vanished.

---

"Unknown?"

Aidan stood up immediately.

"No. No, we're not doing that."

His voice echoed through the chamber.

"Find the girl who doesn't exist? What does that even mean?"

Silence.

The System offered no explanation.

No guidance.

No map.

Nothing.

---

Aidan laughed once.

A short nervous laugh.

"Great."

The sound died quickly.

Because deep down...

he knew he wasn't going to ignore it.

---

The walk home felt different.

Every face he passed became suspicious.

Every stranger suddenly felt important.

The girl who doesn't exist.

How exactly was someone supposed to find a person that didn't exist?

---

The answer arrived the next morning.

Or rather...

the clue did.

---

It happened during first period.

Aidan was barely paying attention when a sheet of paper slid off a nearby desk.

Instinctively he glanced down.

And for a split second—

the world shifted.

---

A flash.

A crack.

A glimpse.

---

The classroom vanished.

Not completely.

Only partially.

Like two realities briefly overlapped.

---

Aidan saw an empty chair.

In the back corner.

A chair nobody used.

A chair nobody sat in.

Yet for one impossible moment—

someone was there.

---

A teenage girl.

Dark hair.

Pale skin.

Gray hoodie.

Staring directly at him.

---

Then she disappeared.

The classroom returned.

The chair was empty.

---

Aidan nearly stood up.

His pulse exploded.

"What the hell..."

---

Several students turned toward him.

The teacher frowned.

"Aidan?"

---

"Sorry."

He sat back down immediately.

Trying not to panic.

Trying not to look at the chair again.

---

He failed.

Every few minutes his eyes drifted back.

Empty.

Always empty.

---

Yet something lingered.

A feeling.

Like the space remembered her.

---

That afternoon he returned to the classroom after everyone left.

The room sat empty and silent.

The chair remained in the corner.

Exactly where he remembered.

---

Aidan approached slowly.

Nothing happened.

No vision.

No message.

No ghost.

---

Then he noticed something strange.

Written underneath the desk.

Tiny letters scratched into the metal.

Old.

Faded.

Easy to miss.

---

**LYRA**

---

Aidan stared.

The name sent a chill through him.

Because the moment he read it—

the System appeared.

---

> **QUEST TARGET IDENTIFIED**

---

His stomach dropped.

---

> **OBJECTIVE UPDATED**

> **FIND LYRA**

---

The message faded.

---

Aidan looked around the empty classroom.

The room suddenly felt much larger.

Much older.

---

"Who are you?" he whispered.

No answer.

---

But somewhere far away...

someone was listening.

---

Not the System.

Something else.

---

The air around the desk felt colder.

The shadows beneath it stretched slightly longer than they should have.

And for the briefest moment—

Aidan thought he heard a girl's voice.

Not clear enough to understand.

Just enough to know it was real.

---

Then it was gone.

---

That night Aidan searched every school record he could access.

Yearbooks.

Attendance lists.

Archived student files.

Social media.

Anything.

---

Nothing.

No Lyra.

No transfer student.

No dropout.

No missing person.

Nothing.

---

It was as if the girl had never existed.

---

Yet the System had identified her.

The desk carried her name.

And Aidan had seen her.

---

Which meant one thing.

Something had erased her.

Or worse.

Something was trying to.

---

For the first time since finding the Crimson Book...

Aidan felt genuine excitement beneath the fear.

A mystery.

A target.

A purpose.

---

He didn't realize it yet.

But this quest wasn't really about finding Lyra.

It was the System testing Zee's Witch Legacy.

Because the first lesson of curse authority was simple:

> **To change reality, you must first learn to see what reality is hiding.**

And somewhere in the city...

a girl who supposedly didn't exist opened her eyes.

As if she had just felt someone looking for her.
