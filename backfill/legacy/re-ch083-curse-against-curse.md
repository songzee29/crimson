The warehouse erupted into chaos.

One cursed entity leaped from the ceiling.

Another burst through a stack of crates.

Three more circled around Malik and Lyra.

"Aidan!" Lyra shouted.

Without thinking—

Aidan moved.

Not because the System told him to.

Because someone he cared about was in danger.

He slid across the warehouse floor, crimson energy spiraling around his feet.

The unstable **Hex Step** carried him farther than expected.

Too far.

He slammed shoulder-first into one of the creatures.

The impact shattered both of them through an old wooden shelf.

Pain exploded across his body.

"...Okay..."

He groaned.

"...That definitely needs work."

The shadow creature shrieked.

Its arm twisted into a blade before swinging toward his throat.

Aidan raised his hand instinctively.

A crimson circle appeared beneath the creature.

Dark chains erupted from the floor.

Not metal.

Living curses.

They wrapped around the monster's limbs.

The System flashed.

"Malik, DON'T—"

**Hex Step.**

Again.

Hungry.
