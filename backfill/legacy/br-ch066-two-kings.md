Cassian entered the abandoned cathedral again.

He hadn't been called.

He simply knew Azrath would be there.

He was.

Standing beneath broken stained glass.

Looking toward the moon.

Without turning around...

He spoke.

> "Tell me..."

"...What do you see when you look at Aidan?"

Cassian answered immediately.

"...A king."

Azrath smiled.

"No."

Silence.

"You see..."

"...the life you wanted."

Cassian's jaw tightened.

"...Maybe."

Azrath finally turned.

"And what does he see when he looks at you?"

Cassian didn't answer.

Because he already knew.

The man who killed Elias.

Nothing more.
