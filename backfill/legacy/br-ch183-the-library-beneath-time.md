At the center of the ruins stood a massive circular building.

Unlike everything else...

It remained untouched.

Not a single crack covered its walls.

The enormous doors slowly opened by themselves.

Waiting.

Inside...

Books.

Thousands.

No...

Hundreds of thousands.

Shelves stretched higher than the eye could see.

Ancient scrolls floated through the air without magic.

The smell of old parchment filled the room.

Even the System hesitated.

---

**Legacy Archive**

Access...

Denied.

Aidan blinked.

Denied?

The building had opened for him.

Yet the Legacy refused access.

Then...

Footsteps echoed through the library.

An old woman appeared between the shelves.

She carried no weapon.

Only a lantern.

She studied Aidan for a long moment.

Then smiled.

"So..."

"...another heir finally found his way home."
