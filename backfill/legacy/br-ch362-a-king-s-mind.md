The Monarch slowly circled Aidan.

"You've turned your humanity off."

"...Yes."

"You've become a monster."

"...According to many."

"You've become a king."

"...According to history."

The Monarch stopped.

"So answer me."

"If your humanity returned tomorrow..."

"...would you regret becoming this?"

For the first time...

The question lingered.

Not because Aidan doubted himself.

Because it deserved an honest answer.

He looked toward the empty throne.

Then answered quietly.

> **"...No."**

A pause.

> **"I cannot help that the world took the kind..."**

His crimson eyes hardened.

> **"...and made them kings."**

The chamber fell completely silent.

Even the other Monarchs...

Who had remained hidden...

Slowly opened their eyes.

One whispered...

"...That..."

Another finished the sentence.

"...is not Zee."

The first nodded.

"...No."

"...It's Aidan."
