The warehouse had barely finished being repaired.

Broken windows were replaced.

The walls had been rebuilt.

For the first time in weeks...

Everyone was eating together.

Malik had somehow burned scrambled eggs.

Again.

Lyra stared at the blackened pan.

"...How?"

"I honestly don't know."

Sienna took one bite.

Immediately set the fork down.

"...This is a crime."

Elias laughed so hard he nearly dropped his coffee.

"I've seen military rations with more flavor."

Malik pointed the spatula dramatically.

"Y'all are some haters."

For the first time in what felt like forever...

Aidan laughed until his stomach hurt.

Not because of magic.

Not because of destiny.

Because...

He finally had people around him.

For one peaceful morning...

The Crimson Legacy didn't exist.

Then—

The System appeared.

Not urgently.

Quietly.
