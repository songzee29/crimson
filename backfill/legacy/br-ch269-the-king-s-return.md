Word spread faster than wildfire.

Not through television.

Not through the internet.

Through fear.

Every supernatural faction felt it.

The pressure that now surrounded House Darkheart had changed.

It was no longer the pressure of one king.

It was the pressure...

Of a dynasty beginning to awaken.

---

Aidan walked through the halls of House Darkheart.

Silent.

The Crimson Assassin followed exactly three steps behind.

Its eyes remained closed.

Its footsteps made no sound.

Servants stopped working.

Veteran warriors instinctively lowered their heads.

Children hid behind pillars.

Not because of Aidan.

Because of the thing following him.

No one had ever seen anything like it.

---

Lyra stepped into the hallway.

She froze.

Her eyes moved from Aidan...

To the Crimson Assassin.

"...Who..."

"...is that?"

Aidan answered honestly.

"...I don't know."

It was the truth.

He knew what it was.

He didn't know...

Who it would become.

---

The Crimson Assassin slowly turned its head toward Lyra.

It didn't attack.

It didn't react.

Then...

It looked away.

As though recognizing that she wasn't a threat to its king.
