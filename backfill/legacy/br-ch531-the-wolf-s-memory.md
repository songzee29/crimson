Inside Aidan's soul...

The Wolf suddenly turned away from the battle.

It looked into the darkness behind itself.

Ancient memories emerged.

It saw Zee.

Young.

Standing before the same Ash King centuries ago.

Not as enemies.

As rivals.

The memory ended before the outcome could be seen.

The Wolf whispered,

> **"...He walked away..."**

Aidan heard it.

"My father fought him."

> **"...Yes."**

"And survived."

The Wolf lowered its head.

> **"...But he never finished the war."**

Aidan's expression hardened.

"Then I will."

The Wolf's eyes glowed.

Not with bloodlust.

With pride.
