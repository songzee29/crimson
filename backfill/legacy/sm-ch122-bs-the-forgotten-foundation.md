## Chapter One Hundred Twenty-Two — The Forgotten Foundation

The rain had stopped before dawn.

A thin layer of mist drifted across the grounds of the Darkheart estate, wrapping the ancient manor in a silence that felt older than memory itself.

Aidan stood alone beneath the eastern balcony, watching the first rays of sunlight break over the distant hills.

The warmth inside his chest had changed.

Ever since the Law of Protection accepted him, the world felt... sharper.

He heard birds before they landed.

He noticed servants smiling at one another before they spoke.

He could feel the weight people carried without understanding why.

It wasn't magic.

It wasn't supernatural sight.

It was awareness.

The Guardian had been right.

Authority did not begin by changing the world.

It began by changing the one who carried it.

His crimson-and-gold eyes reflected the sunrise.

The crimson never disappeared.

Neither did the gold.

Together they shimmered softly, becoming brighter only when emotion stirred within him.

They were not a transformation.

They were simply his eyes.

The eyes of House Darkheart.

Though Aidan himself did not yet know what that truly meant.

---

Breakfast passed quietly.

His aunt placed another serving of pancakes onto his plate before he could refuse.

You're thinking again.

Aidan smiled.

Is it that obvious?

Only when you forget your syrup.

He looked down.

She was right.

The pancakes remained untouched.

His mind had wandered back to the Sanctuary.

Back to the Hall of Laws.

Back to the faceless Founder.

The same question returned.

Who are you?

Not because he wanted power.

Because he wanted truth.

His aunt gently rested a hand on his shoulder.

Whatever answer you're searching for...

I hope it brings you peace.

Aidan smiled politely.

So do I.

Yet somewhere deep inside...

He already suspected peace would not be waiting at the end of this road.

---

That evening he returned beneath the estate.

The forgotten corridor welcomed him with its familiar silence.

Portraits watched from the walls.

Most depicted generations of House Darkheart.

Noble men.

Brilliant women.

Warriors.

Scholars.

Children.

Yet one portrait always drew his attention.

It hung farther down the corridor than the others.

Larger.

Older.

Its frame had been carved from black wood unlike anything else in the estate.

The canvas itself had almost completely faded.

Only a throne remained visible.

The person sitting upon it...

Had no face.

Not because time had damaged the painting.

Because someone had deliberately removed it.

Again.

Aidan stepped closer.

His fingers hovered just above the canvas.

The throne looked strangely familiar.

The same towering design.

The same crimson banners.

The same ancient architecture.

He had seen it before.

Inside the Living Legacy.

A chill ran through him.

Before he could study it further, the crimson mark beneath his skin grew warm.

Not a command.

Not a warning.

An observation.

Ancient crimson letters slowly formed before him.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRIMSON RECORD UPDATED

An inheritance is remembered before it is understood.

The Legacy preserves.

Time reveals.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The elegant words dissolved like drifting ash.

Nothing else happened.

The Legacy had not answered his question.

It had simply recorded that another piece of history had found him.

Aidan lowered his hand.

One day...

He would learn why every ancient image seemed determined to erase the same face.

---

Far beneath the Sanctuary...

Beyond the Hall of Laws...

Beyond the Chamber of Threads...

There existed another chamber.

No Guardian stood watch there.

No student had ever entered.

No record named it.

Twelve enormous seals covered a circular door forged from crimson-black metal.

Upon the door were carved three ancient symbols.

A crown.

A crescent moon stained crimson.

The head of a wolf.

The symbols pulsed once before falling silent again.

Hidden behind the first seal...

Something breathed.

Slowly.

Patiently.

Not awake.

Not asleep.

Waiting.

The silence surrounding it felt familiar.

As though an ancient soul dreamed beneath countless ages of stone.

No voice escaped.

No thoughts reached the surface.

Only a faint heartbeat echoed through the darkness.

Boom...

...

Boom...

...

Boom...

The Living Legacy observed.

It did not interfere.

For some inheritances could not be rushed.

They had to awaken when history itself was ready.

---

High above the Sanctuary, within the endless archive where every age had been faithfully preserved, another page quietly inscribed itself.

Not with haste.

With ceremony.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LAW PRESERVED

The First Crimson Witch Authority has accepted its bearer.

The bearer has not mastered the Law.

He has only begun walking beside it.

The First Foundation stands.

The remaining Foundations sleep beneath time.

History continues its patient vigil.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The page folded itself into a crimson-bound volume before sliding silently onto a shelf that seemed to stretch into eternity.

Millions of books rested there.

Some remembered civilizations.

Some remembered kings.

Some remembered monsters.

Only a handful remembered Founders.

At the very end of the endless aisle stood one empty shelf.

Waiting.

Not for the First Founder of House Darkheart.

His place had long since been preserved.

Not for the ancient names already written into history.

The empty place waited for another book.

A book that did not yet exist.

Its title had already been chosen.

**The Founder of the Crimson Civilization.**

But its pages...

Had not yet been written.
