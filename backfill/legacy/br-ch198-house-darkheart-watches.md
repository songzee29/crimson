Inside the palace...

Lyra stood at the tallest window.

Smoke rose from distant streets.

Sirens echoed endlessly.

Malik slammed his fist against the wall.

"We have to stop him!"

The Guardian never looked away from the city.

"No."

Malik turned.

"What do you mean 'No'?!"

The old man spoke quietly.

"You can't stop a newborn vampire with a sword."

"Then what do we do?!"

Silence.

A silence that hurt more than any answer.

Finally...

The Guardian whispered...

"We survive."

Lyra closed her eyes.

Tears rolled down her face again.

Not because she had hope.

Because she finally accepted...

The boy she loved...

Was gone.

For now.
