The circle collapsed inward.

Seven remaining Guardians abandoned every trace of defense.

Ancient blades converged on Aidan from every direction.

He didn’t retreat.

He stepped forward.

One sword passed over his shoulder.

Another missed his throat by inches.

His hand shot through the opening.

Stone exploded beneath his fist.

The Guardian staggered.

Before its crimson core could retreat beneath the fractured armor—

Lyra’s claws pierced straight through the opening.

The core shattered.

Another Guardian fell.

“Six!” Elias shouted.

“No.”

Aidan’s voice remained calm.

“They’ve changed.”

The remaining Guardians immediately shifted positions.

Two protected the front.

Three moved behind them.

One circled wide.

The kneeling Guardian remained perfectly still.

“They’re no longer trying to stop us,” Draco said.

“They’re delaying us.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A thunderous strike echoed beneath the courtyard.

The ancient fountain overflowed.

Dark crimson liquid spilled over the stone.

It flowed through channels carved into the ground.

Not toward the Guardians.

Toward the center.

Toward Aidan.

The blood stopped less than a foot from his boots.

For one impossible second…

The surface became perfectly smooth.

No ripple.

No movement.

Then it quietly withdrew back into the fountain.

Cassian frowned.

“Did anyone else—”

A Guardian attacked before he could finish.

The question died with the clash of steel.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Malik slammed both palms into the ground.

Golden runes spread beneath three Guardians.

“Collapse!”

The stone beneath them erupted upward.

One Guardian lost its footing.

Elias appeared instantly.

His blade drove into the exposed fracture.

The crimson core burst apart.

The Guardian crumbled.

“Five!”

Rowan nodded.

“Keep separating them.”

“They’re strongest together.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Draco crossed blades with another Guardian.

For the first time…

He was forced backward.

His coat tore across one shoulder.

Stone scraped flesh.

Blood stained the black fabric.

He ignored it.

Aidan noticed.

Nothing in his expression changed.

He intercepted the Guardian before it could finish Draco.

One strike.

A shattered arm.

A second.

The glowing core collapsed beneath his hand.

The Guardian fell.

Draco looked at him.

“You saved me.”

“It was the fastest path.”

No gratitude.

No reassurance.

Only efficiency.

Draco gave a slow nod.

“I figured.”

Then he returned to the fight.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Only four Guardians remained.

The courtyard shook harder than before.

Cracks spread through the ancient stone beneath the fountain.

The whisper returned.

Not louder.

Closer.

“The Trial…”

“…approaches judgment.”

Every surviving Guardian suddenly ignored everyone else.

Cassian’s sword slashed across one.

It never reacted.

Lyra tore another apart.

It continued crawling forward.

Every ancient warrior walked directly toward Aidan.

Nothing else mattered.

Aidan understood.

“So that’s the objective.”

He lowered into a fighting stance.

“They’re trying to reach me.”

Then all four charged together.
