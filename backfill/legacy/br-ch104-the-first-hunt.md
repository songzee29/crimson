Two nights later...

Aidan's phone rang.

It was the old Guardian.

Not through magic.

A normal phone.

Aidan almost laughed.

"I didn't know you used cell phones."

The Guardian sighed.

"I've lived long enough to learn."

His voice grew serious.

"Something escaped."

"What?"

"A Curse Beast."

Silence.

"They were once created to protect ancient Witch sanctuaries."

Aidan frowned.

"So what's the problem?"

"They no longer remember who they're protecting."

An hour later...

The Crimson Circle arrived at an abandoned sugar mill outside the city.

The air smelled of rust and old blood.

Broken machinery towered overhead.

Moonlight slipped through holes in the roof.

The Guardian waited near the entrance.

"So..."

He looked at Aidan.

"Let's see what you've learned."
