House Darkheart had become a fortress.

Every entrance was guarded.

Every window sealed.

Not to keep enemies out.

To keep hope alive.

Lyra hadn't slept.

Malik hadn't eaten.

Sienna stared at maps covered in missing-person reports.

The Guardian stood before the empty throne.

He reached toward the Crimson Crown.

Then stopped.

"...Not yet."

He couldn't bring himself to move it.

Because somewhere inside him...

He still believed its king would return.
