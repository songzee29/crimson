On the way back into the city...

Aidan heard shouting.

A young woman was pinned against the side of a brick building by two men wearing black Veil jackets.

"We asked you nicely."

She glared at them.

"My answer hasn't changed."

One of them raised his hand.

Aidan stepped forward.

"Hey."

The men turned.

"You've got the wrong person."

The older Veil agent sneered.

"Walk away."

Aidan didn't.

Lyra and Malik arrived just behind him.

The woman looked between them.

"...You really picked the worst time to be heroes."

The Veil agents attacked.

---

The fight was quick.

Not because Aidan was stronger.

Because he was smarter.

The Guardian's training was already changing him.

Instead of rushing...

He waited.

Watched.

Moved only when necessary.

Hex Step.

One strike.

Crimson Shackles.

A clean sweep.

The Veil agents retreated.

Not defeated.

Surprised.

"Aidan."

"Malik."

"So..."

"You've got a story."

"So do you."
