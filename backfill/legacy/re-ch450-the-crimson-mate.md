Outside...

A warm crimson light surrounded Aidan.

Not violent.

Not destructive.

Peaceful.

Lyra looked into his eyes.

For the first time...

She saw something looking back.

Not Aidan.

The Wolf Authority.

One crimson-and-gold eye glowed brighter.

The Wolf spoke through Aidan.

Its voice was ancient.

Powerful.

Yet calm.

> **"Lyra..."**

She froze.

"You can hear me?"

> **"Yes."**

Another long silence.

Then...

The Wolf surprised everyone.

> **"You bound me..."**

Lyra lowered her head.

"...I know."

> **"...to save him."**

"...Yes."

The Wolf closed its eyes.

When it opened them again...

There was no anger.

Only respect.

> **"Then hear my decree."**

The entire forest seemed to listen.

> **"From this night forward..."**

> **"...I shall never see you as my jailer."**

Another pause.

Then, with complete certainty...

> **"...I shall know you..."**

> **"...as the one who protected my king when I could not."**

Lyra's tears fell freely.

Then...

The Wolf smiled.

A true wolf's smile.

> **"...And because your heart chose him before yourself..."**

> **"...mine shall choose you as well."**

Aidan felt warmth spread through his soul.

Not domination.

Not control.

Acceptance.

The Wolf Authority had made its choice.

It did not merely tolerate Lyra.

It loved her because she had loved Aidan enough to bear the burden of a secret that protected his life.

The cracked Sacred Seal glowed softly.

It had not broken.

But it had changed.

It was no longer a prison.

It had become a promise between three hearts:

Aidan.

Lyra.

And the Wolf waiting patiently for the day they would all run together beneath the same crimson moon.
