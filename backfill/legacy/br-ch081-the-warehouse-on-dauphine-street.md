The warehouse looked abandoned.

Old brick.

Broken windows.

A rusted sign hung crooked above the entrance.

**RIVIÈRE IMPORTS**

Closed for decades.

Malik stood outside the chain-link fence, waving both arms.

"You guys finally made it!"

Lyra looked at the building.

"...Please tell me you didn't buy this."

Malik rubbed the back of his neck.

"So..."

"I can explain."

Aidan folded his arms.

"I'm waiting."

"It was an online auction."

"You bought a warehouse... online?"

"It came with free storage units."

Lyra sighed.

"You hear yourself, right?"

Malik grinned.

"It was a really good deal."

Aidan couldn't help laughing.

"You are definitely going to get us killed one day."

Malik pointed at him dramatically.

"See? That's friendship."
