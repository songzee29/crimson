Thousands of people gathered.

No panic.

No riots.

No screaming.

Instead...

They looked toward one person.

Aidan.

Nobody asked what to do.

Nobody questioned him.

A little girl tugged on her mother's sleeve.

"Is he going to fix it?"

Her mother smiled.

"He always does."

Aidan heard those words.

For a brief moment...

The weight felt unbearable.

He thought of Elias.

Then quietly whispered to himself,

> "I'll try."

---

The Witch Authority stirred.

His hands slowly rose.

His fingers moved through familiar signs.

Elegant.

Precise.

The crimson runes beneath the city awakened.

One word left his lips.

> **"Mercy."**

The Seventh Crimson Law answered.

The blood in the sky slowly turned into ordinary rain.

The clouds broke.

Sunlight poured through.

The city erupted into cheers.

Children laughed.

People cried with relief.

Someone shouted,

> **"Long live the Crimson King!"**

Soon...

Hundreds repeated it.

Aidan lowered his head.

He still wasn't used to hearing it.
