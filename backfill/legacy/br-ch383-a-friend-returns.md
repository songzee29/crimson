As the road stretched onward, Rez looked around with childlike curiosity.

"So much has changed..."

He stopped beside an abandoned roadside shrine.

Its stone had cracked with age.

Someone had carved House Darkheart's crest into it.

Freshly.

Rez brushed his fingers across the carving.

"They remember."

Aidan looked at the shrine.

"They remember Zee."

Rez smiled softly.

"No..."

He looked at Aidan.

"They're starting to remember you."

Aidan didn't answer.

Praise had never interested him.
