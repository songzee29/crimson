The twentieth Blood Echo fell.

The fortieth.

The seventieth.

Time lost meaning.

Every movement wasted less effort.

He predicted them.

The chamber adapted again.

Unlike the others…

It carried no weapon.

It simply walked.

Its crimson eyes matched Aidan’s exactly.

When it moved—

Blood Step.

The distance between them disappeared unnaturally.

Its hand struck Aidan’s chest.

Stone behind him exploded.

For the first time since awakening…

He landed hard against one of the cathedral walls.

The second heartbeat thundered.

The hunger surged.

Fresh blood flowed from his split lip.

Its scent reached his own senses.

Even that awakened the predator.

The Blood Echo attacked again.

This time—

Aidan didn’t react.

His body did.

Blood Step answered Blood Step.

The two crimson figures vanished repeatedly throughout the chamber.

Stone collapsed.

Ancient pillars shattered.

Every collision shook the cathedral.

The duel ended with one decisive movement.

Aidan’s hand pierced straight through the Blood Echo’s chest.

The crimson figure looked down.

Then smiled.

Not kindly.

Knowingly.

It dissolved into countless crimson particles that drifted toward the Ancient Gates.
