## Chapter Sixty-Two — The Walk Home

Aidan stepped out of Blackwood Library into the cool night air.

The door quietly closed behind him.

He turned almost immediately.

The building stood exactly where it had been moments before.

Warm light still glowed through its windows.

The old bookseller remained visible behind the front desk, calmly placing another book onto a shelf.

Nothing about it appeared supernatural.

Yet Aidan now understood something.

The strangest places often looked the most ordinary.

He slipped the small wooden token into his pocket.

It felt surprisingly heavy.

Not because of its weight.

Because of what it represented.

Questions.

Responsibilities.

Choices.

As he walked through the quiet streets, the city seemed different.

Not changed.

Revealed.

He noticed an elderly couple helping each other cross the road.

A firefighter carrying a sleeping child from a parked ambulance.

A teenager stopping to help a stray dog find its owner.

Small moments.

Ordinary moments.

The Founder hadn't built the Legacy to protect kingdoms.

He had built it to protect moments like these.

Aidan smiled.

For the first time...

He understood why compassion came before power.

Morning.

Aidan laughed awkwardly.

I hope that's a good thing.

Depends on who's talking.

The janitor leaned his broom against the wall.

Can I tell you something?

Sure.

People spend too much time trying to become important.

Not enough time trying to become useful.

History remembers both.

But only one leaves the world better than they found it.

Aidan stood silently.

Lately...

Every older person he met seemed to speak in lessons instead of conversations.

He wasn't sure if the world had changed...

Or if he had.
