A sound broke the silence.

A cough.

Everyone turned.

Inside a collapsed home...

A small boy crawled into the light.

He couldn't have been older than seven.

Covered in dirt.

Terrified.

Alive.

Lyra rushed forward.

"It's alright."

"We're here."

The child looked past her.

Directly at Aidan.

His eyes widened.

"The wolf..."

He whispered.

"...The wolf came."

The company froze.

Aidan knelt before him.

"What wolf?"

The boy's breathing became uneven.

"The giant one..."

"...It told us to run."

"...It said..."

The child burst into tears.

> **"It said the Ash King was coming."**

Silence consumed the village.
