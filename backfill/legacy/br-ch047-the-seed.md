Cassian stood alone after Aidan left.

The battlefield became quiet once more.

He walked to the sword planted in the earth.

His own sword.

The one that had ended Aidan's human life.

He pulled it free.

The steel reflected his face.

Tired.

Older.

Broken.

He whispered to himself...

"...Walk away."

His grip tightened.

"...Just walk away."

He turned toward Black Hollow.

He took three steps.

Stopped.

His hand slowly opened.

Inside it...

Rested the blackened ring recovered from the ashes of his home.

He closed his fist again.

"...Why does he still have everything..."

"...when I lost mine?"

There was no voice.

No magic.

No hooded figure.

No Ash King.

Only a wounded heart asking a dangerous question.

For the first time...

Revenge did not come from the curse.

It came from Cassian.
