One evening the Circle gathered on the roof overlooking New Orleans.

No meetings.

No missions.

Just friends.

Malik leaned back in his chair.

"You know..."

"...I miss when we used to almost die every other day."

Sienna threw a piece of bread at him.

"You are literally insane."

Cassian laughed.

"For once..."

"...I agree with her."

Everyone laughed.

Even Aidan.

For a brief moment...

There was no Crimson King.

No House Darkheart.

No Legacy.

Just...

Friends.

The family he had built.

The family he had chosen.

The Guardian watched from below.

A smile touched his face.

"...Good."

Because he knew...

Moments like these...

Were always the first thing war stole.
