Everyone in the room listened.

Even Cassian.

Still kneeling.

Still unable to comprehend what he had done.

Lyra struggled to breathe.

"When..."

"...your vampire side awakened..."

"...your Wolf awakened too..."

Aidan nodded slowly.

"I know."

She smiled weakly.

"No..."

"...you don't..."

Another painful breath.

"I was afraid..."

"...of what would happen..."

"...if all three Authorities awakened at once..."

"So..."

"...I used my own life..."

"...to bind your Wolf."

Silence.

Aidan stared at her.

"You..."

"...bound it?"

She nodded.

"I didn't imprison it..."

"I protected you..."

"...until..."

"...you were ready..."

Aidan looked inward.

Inside his soul...

The Wolf stepped forward.

It looked at Lyra through Aidan's eyes.

Not with anger.

Not with hatred.

With love.

The great beast lowered its head.

> **"...Queen..."**

> **"...Thank you..."**

Its deep voice carried through Aidan's soul.

> **"...You never chained me..."**

> **"...You protected my King..."**

Aidan felt tears streaming down his face.

"You knew..."

The Wolf answered.

> **"...I have always known..."**

> **"...That is why..."**

> **"...I chose her..."**

Another crack spread across the Sacred Seal.

Not from rage.

From acceptance.

The Wolf no longer wished to be free.

It wished to honor the woman who had protected both it...

And its King.
