## Canon Artifact History: System, Legacy, Authorities, Humanity-Off, Elias, and the Wolf Gate

**Classification:** Cross-document canon chronology. This report records how the rules developed; it does not amend the Master Decree.  
**Sources:** Original export, the dated canon artifacts in `backfill/legacy/_canon_*`, `legacy/canon.md`, and `chapter_map.md`.

## 1. Why the canon files cannot be read as if they were written at once

These are distinct artifacts with source dates, not interchangeable copies of one universal document.

## 2. System and Living Legacy: the apparent contradiction is a stage distinction

### Early opening rule — June 11
- The Crimson Book accepts Aidan's blood after physical contact.
- The physical Book disappears after opening.
- The System appears within Aidan's mind and is visible/audible only to Aidan.
- Rez remains dormant until the Vampire Curse.
- The System guides Aidan through the inheritance; it is not a public interface.

### June 28–July 8 constitutional distinction
The House Darkheart identity artifact (source dated June 28) describes House Darkheart, Crimson Bloodline, Crimson Legacy, and Royal Decrees as separate concepts. The July 5 Authority & System Constitutional Rule says the Legacy is the Living Archive, while Aidan activates powers through his own will/instincts and the System records what he does.

The July 8 System Evolution Directive adds the missing stage distinction:
- **System = interface:** informs, records, teaches, grants earned abilities, quests, skills, Witch Laws, rewards, and Authority progression during the Witch Arc.
- **Legacy = living intelligence/judgment:** decides what is earned, revealed, withheld, or permitted.
- **Aidan = user of the powers:** he activates and uses his abilities himself; the System is not the physical trigger for every cast or instinctive action.
- **Full-tri­brid stage and later:** when Aidan becomes a full tribrid, the System shifts away from granting new power and toward blueprints, Founder Techniques, records, and mastery.

This resolves the apparent contradiction between “the System does not activate Aidan's powers” and “the System grants powers.” Granting an ability/reward is not the same as activating it on Aidan's behalf. The current Story Bible reflects this stage-based reading.

### Permanent privacy rule
Only Aidan sees or hears the System/Living Legacy interface. He must never reveal it to friends. Other characters may notice him pausing or looking at empty space, but cannot see the System itself. This is not optional scene flavor; it is Rule #1.

## 3. Humanity-off: intelligence remains; empathy and restraint do not

The June 30 Humanity-Off artifact corrects a major characterization drift. When Aidan's humanity is off:
- compassion, empathy, guilt, remorse, and mercy are absent;
- intelligence, planning, personality, combat skill, leadership, and the ability to speak remain;
- Aidan is still Aidan, but does not care.

This rule rejects writing him as a mindless zombie or animal. Later author corrections intensify the Vampire Arc's dark-humor/monster characterization, but they do not remove intelligence or strategic capability. The exact “enjoys being a monster” tone should be linked to its July 16–17 source and current approved directive; do not infer it from the phrase “humanity off” alone.

The Humanity-Off artifact also distinguishes Rez's conscious self from the hybrid body: the body/hybrid compatibility can exist while Rez's conscious self remains asleep. This prevents the Crimson Assassin body from being used as proof that Rez is awake early.

## 4. Elias: older death drafts are superseded

The branch Codex Update Code (July 9) records the correction that Elias was not killed by Cassian and survives the Witch Arc. The July 19 Master Decree update, **Operation: Elias Continuity Restoration**, explicitly replaces earlier references to Elias's death and states that Elias remains alive, one of Aidan's closest companions.

Current handling:
- Elias survives the Witch Arc.
- Any older chapter, outline, or Codex entry that kills him remains preserved as historical draft material.
- Do not reintroduce his death as a current event without a formal new Zee amendment.
- Keep the original correction code and later decree update linked to the affected chapter track.

## 5. Wolf Authority: awakened/sealed is not the same as fully released

The current Story Bible and Chapter Map define a staged gate:
1. Aidan's first true death and rebirth as the Crimson Vampire unlock the Vampire Authority; Rez awakens at that constitutional moment.
2. The Wolf Curse awakens at that death but Lyra's sacred bond seals/restraints it before full manifestation.
3. Lyra's later death breaks the seal permanently and releases the Wolf Authority.
4. Only after all three Authorities are complete can the later True Crimson Blood / Blood Creation Authority / Dark Blooding sequence occur.
5. The Wolf Arc is about the Wolf's full release and mastery, not a first awakening invented at the beginning of that arc.

This must be distinguished from the September 8 Chapter 80–85 rewrite, where Lyra's death and Wolf release occur much earlier in a different numbering track. That older run is preserved; it does not override the October 12 current arc lock.

## 6. Four Authorities vs. six Authorities

Two dated records conflict:
- The September 8 Chapter 83 directive explicitly proposes six Authorities (Witch, Vampire, Wolf, Dragon, Siren, Crimson Phoenix) and demands an instant evolution without extra trials.
- The October 12 current Story Bible records four Authorities: Witch, Vampire, Wolf, and Crimson Demon Eclipse. It says these unify after the First Crimson Ascension.

Current treatment: the October 12 Story Bible controls present-tense continuity. The six-Authority directive and related Chapter 83 material remain preserved as an alternate/historical track. Do not silently rewrite that older directive to say four; do not use its six-Authority count in the current track unless Zee issues a newer ruling.

## 7. Cassian: several concepts must not be collapsed

The source history contains at least three separate kinds of Cassian material:
- early relationship/antagonist drafts;
- the July 7 transformation into the **First Crimson Demon Hexborn**, using Aidan's True Crimson Blood during the breaking of the Darkheart Curse;
- later canon-summary conflict between “permanent antagonist, never redeemed” and a path toward redemption in which Cassian survives the Witch Arc and remembers that Aidan chose him.

The current `legacy/canon.md` explicitly flags Cassian's arc as needing Zee's ruling. Do not resolve this conflict by treating a transformation chapter as proof of redemption, or by treating an older “never redeemed” line as automatically stronger than a later Master Record. Keep the question open.

Cassian is not Aidan's parent. Aidan's mother is Queen Nymeris Thalryn. These are current locked facts in the October 12 Story Bible.
