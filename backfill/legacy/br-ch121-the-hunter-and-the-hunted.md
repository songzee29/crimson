The rain returned.

Not a storm.

A slow...

Steady...

Cold rain.

Aidan walked alone through the Louisiana swamps.

No escort.

No Circle.

No Guardian.

This wasn't their burden.

It was his.

The wind carried a scent.

Blood.

Fresh.

Aidan's eyes narrowed.

"...Cassian."

His fingers instinctively moved.

Not to his sword.

To the familiar motions of the Witch Authority.

The crimson runes briefly flickered around his hands...

Then disappeared.

He kept walking.

---

Miles away...

Cassian stood in the middle of an abandoned plantation.

His hearing had become unbearable.

He could hear insects crawling beneath the earth.

He could hear fish swimming through muddy water.

He could hear...

One heartbeat.

Slow.

Steady.

Approaching.

Cassian smiled.

"...He's coming."

Azrath stood in the shadows of the old mansion.

Watching.

"You sound excited."

Cassian laughed.

"I've waited for this."

Azrath remained silent.

Then quietly said,

"Don't underestimate grief."

Cassian looked confused.

"What?"

Azrath's crimson-black eyes met his.

"The most dangerous version of Aidan..."

"...isn't the king."

A pause.

"...It's the brother."
