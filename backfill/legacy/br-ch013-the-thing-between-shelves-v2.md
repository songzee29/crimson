The Archive Keeper moved.

One second it stood at the far end of the aisle.

The next it was already halfway there.

Aidan's heart nearly stopped.

Instinct took over.

He grabbed Lyra's folder and ran.

The moment his fingers touched the black folder, the entire archive screamed.

Not with sound.

With pressure.

Every shelf shook violently.

Thousands of files rattled at once.

The Archive Keeper lunged.

Its long arm stretched unnaturally between shelves, fingers splitting into black thread-like tendrils.

One brushed Aidan's shoulder.

Agony exploded through his body.

Images flooded his mind.

Hundreds of faces.

Thousands.

People forgotten by the world.

People erased.

People screaming as their names vanished from reality.

Aidan crashed into a shelf and broke free.

The vision disappeared.

But one terrifying realization remained.

The Keeper wasn't made of names.

It was made of people.

Or what remained of them.

Behind him, Lyra's voice echoed again.

"Aidan! Left!"

Without thinking, he obeyed.

The Keeper crashed through the aisle where he would've been standing a second later.

For the first time, Aidan realized something impossible.

Lyra could see him too.
