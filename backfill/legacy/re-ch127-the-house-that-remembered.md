Portraits lined the walls.

Every painting showed a different family.

Different centuries.

Different faces.

One thing remained the same.

Each portrait had someone scratched out.

Not damaged.

Carefully removed.

Almost...

Erased.

Lyra stopped before one painting.

"That's strange."

"What?"

"This family..."

She pointed.

"...they're all looking at the empty space."

Aidan slowly approached.

His right leg began bouncing again.

Faster.

Faster.

Something felt wrong.

He reached toward the scratched-out figure.

The moment his fingers touched the canvas—

The entire mansion screamed.

Not a sound.

A memory.

Dozens of voices filled the air.

*"Erase him!"*

*"They can't know!"*

*"Hide the heir!"*

*"Protect the bloodline!"*

Aidan dropped to one knee.

His head felt like it would split apart.

The voices grew louder.

*"Hide him!"*

*"Hide him!"*

*"Hide him!"*

Then...

One voice.

Different from the others.

A man's voice.

Strong.

Steady.

Filled with pain.

> **"Forgive me... son."**

Everything stopped.

The mansion became silent once again.

Aidan remained frozen.

His breathing was ragged.

Lyra knelt beside him.

"Aidan!"

He looked up slowly.

"...I heard him."

"Hear who?"

He couldn't answer.

Because deep inside...

He already knew.
