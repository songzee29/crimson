The silence inside Aidan's mind didn't last.

The crimson wolf remained seated.

The vampire stood in the darkness with folded arms.

Neither spoke.

Neither moved.

They simply...

Waited.

Aidan frowned.

*So that's it?*

*You finally reveal yourselves... and now you're quiet?*

The vampire smiled.

**You've spent years blaming us.**

**Now you finally understand what we are.**

The wolf's deep voice followed.

**We are not your enemies.**

**We are your nature.**

---

Outside...

The wind rolled across the mountain.

Rez watched Aidan carefully.

He knew his nephew wasn't looking at him anymore.

He was somewhere deeper.

Somewhere only the Soul Link could reach.

---

**Talk to them.**

Aidan heard Rez immediately.

*You can hear them?*

**No.**

**But I know what comes next.**

---

The vampire slowly stepped forward.

Its crimson eyes glowed brighter.

**You fear me because you tasted what I can become.**

Aidan remembered...

The bodies.

The blood.

The century he had lived with his humanity gone.

The monster he became.

His jaw tightened.

"I won't become that again."

The vampire shook its head.

**That wasn't me.**

**That was you... without balance.**

Silence.

---

The wolf stood.

The ground beneath its paws cracked.

**And you fear me...**

**...because you think I only know rage.**

Aidan remembered Lyra sealing the wolf.

The curse.

The years of silence.

The uncontrollable instincts waiting beneath the surface.

"I won't lose myself."

The wolf lowered its massive head.

**Then stop treating me like something to lose.**

---

Aidan stood motionless.

For the first time...

He understood.

He hadn't been accepting them.

He had been tolerating them.

There was a difference.

---

The vampire extended a hand.

Not to control him.

To invite him.

**Walk with me.**

The wolf turned toward the endless crimson forest.

**Run with me.**

---

Aidan looked between them.

Then smiled.

"No."

Both looked back at him.

"I'll do both."

Without another word...

He walked toward the vampire.

Then continued past him.

He placed one hand against the wolf's neck.

Its crimson fur felt warm.

Alive.

The giant beast closed its eyes.

Accepting the touch.

Aidan looked back toward the vampire.

"You can come too."

For the first time...

The vampire laughed.

A genuine laugh.

**You've finally stopped choosing between us.**

---

Outside...

Aidan's heartbeat changed.

Not faster.

Steadier.

His Crimson Aura settled into a calm rhythm.

The violent surges disappeared.

Rez smiled.

"There it is..."

---

Aidan slowly opened his eyes.

The hunger was still there.

The instinct to hunt was still there.

But now...

He could separate desire...

From decision.

He looked toward the endless forest surrounding House Darkheart.

A slow grin spread across his face.

"I'm still hungry."

Rez nodded.

"I know."

"I'm not hunting people."

Rez raised an eyebrow.

"No?"

Aidan looked toward the mountains beyond the kingdom.

"There are creatures out there that have been terrorizing villages for years."

His crimson eyes gleamed.

"I think..."

"...it's time something started hunting them."

Rez couldn't help but laugh.

"Now that..."

"...sounds like a Darkheart."

Malik's voice suddenly echoed from the trail below.

"My King!"

Aidan looked over the cliff.

Malik stood at the bottom of the path with Kael, Mara, Darius, and Lyra.

He waved enthusiastically.

"We've been looking everywhere!"

Darius folded his arms.

"You disappear for one night..."

"...and the entire castle panics."

Lyra climbed the last few steps and stopped in front of Aidan.

She studied his face carefully.

Then smiled.

"You won."

Aidan looked at her.

"It wasn't a battle."

She gently took his hand.

"I know."

"It was something harder."

For the first time in days...

Aidan's smile reached his eyes.

He looked at his family.

Then at the wilderness stretching beyond the kingdom.

"The next lesson isn't inside these walls."

Kael adjusted the sword at his side.

"So..."

"Where are we going?"

Aidan's grin widened.

"On a hunt."

Malik immediately smiled.

"Now we're talking."

Inside Aidan's mind...

The wolf stood and stretched.

**Finally.**

The vampire smiled.

**Let's see if our King can keep his word.**

Aidan chuckled.

*Watch me.*

The Crimson King turned toward the untamed wilderness.

This time...

He wasn't running from the darkness within him.

He was taking it with him.

Toward whatever waited in the wild.
