The cathedral held its breath.

Rain tapped against broken stained glass.

The crimson vial hovered inches from Cassian's lips.

His hand trembled.

Not from fear.

From anticipation.

He could still walk away.

He knew that.

Azrath wasn't there.

No whisper.

No command.

No manipulation.

Just...

A choice.

"...I refuse..."

"...to stand beneath another man's crown."

"...That's it?"

He laughed.

"You call this power?"

Then again.

Then...
