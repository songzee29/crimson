Azrath stood alone inside an abandoned opera house.

Dust floated through broken sunlight.

He slowly walked onto the stage.

Thousands of empty seats stared back at him.

He smiled.

"I remember..."

"...when these were full."

He closed his eyes.

As though hearing applause that no longer existed.

Then...

Footsteps.

Cassian.

"You wanted to see me."

Azrath nodded.

"Yes."

Cassian looked around.

"What is this place?"

"A memory."

Azrath answered.

"Before kingdoms."

"Before the Veil."

"Before the Crimson Legacy."

He looked toward the ruined ceiling.

"There were only two names anyone feared."

Cassian frowned.

"The Crimson King..."

Azrath slowly shook his head.

"No."

A long silence.

"House Darkheart..."

"...and me."
