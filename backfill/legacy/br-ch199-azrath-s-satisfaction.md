High above the city...

Azrath stood on the roof of an abandoned hotel.

He watched the panic spread through New Orleans.

He watched soldiers establish barricades.

He watched helicopters search the skies.

He watched fear consume the city.

Then...

He smiled.

Not proudly.

Patiently.

"...Good."

"...Now..."

"...why kings should never become monsters."

Crimson.

And somewhere beneath the streets of New Orleans...

Not into the hero who protected the city.

But into the nightmare that hunted it.
