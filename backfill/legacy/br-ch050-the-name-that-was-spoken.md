Deep beneath the Veil...

Beyond the reach of kingdoms...

Beyond the reach of magic...

Beyond the reach of history...

There existed only one sound.

**Drip...**

**Drip...**

**Drip...**

Ancient crimson blood fell from the ceiling onto black stone.

Every drop carried one purpose.

To feed the prison.

To keep it sleeping.

For centuries...

It had been enough.

Until...

One name...

Was spoken.

---

The ancient chains surrounding the prisoner began vibrating.

Not violently.

Almost...

Excited.

One crimson rune faded.

Then another.

Then another.

The prison itself whispered...

> **"Authority... recognized."**

Silence.

Then...

A second voice answered.

Not from outside.

From within.

> **"...Finally."**

---

Azrath slowly opened his eyes.

They weren't crimson.

They weren't gold.

They were...

Completely black.

As though light itself refused to exist inside them.

He smiled.

The same patient smile.

The one history had spent centuries trying to forget.
