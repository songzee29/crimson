As dawn approached...

House Darkheart's banner rose over the camp.

Kael trained with Cassian in the clearing, testing techniques and learning from one another despite the uneasy history between their leaders.

Mara sat with Lyra, refining new protective runes inspired by what they had witnessed in the Ash Dominion.

Darius repaired damaged armor while quietly sharing stories with the rescued children, helping them feel safe again.

Rez watched them all.

Then smiled.

He walked over to Aidan.

"You know what I see?"

Aidan looked across the camp.

"What?"

Rez folded his arms.

"I see the beginning of something your father never had."

Aidan raised an eyebrow.

"A family?"

Rez shook his head.

"...A civilization."

Aidan's gaze drifted across the camp.

House Darkheart.

Former enemies.

New friends.

Children laughing again.

Kael.

Mara.

Darius.

Lyra.

Rez.

Even Cassian...

Beginning to question the hatred he had carried for so long.

The Crimson Legacy appeared one final time.
