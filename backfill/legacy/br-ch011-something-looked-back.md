The rain followed Aidan all the way home.

Cold drops tapped against bus windows.

Streetlights blurred into long streaks of gold and white.

The city moved around him.

Normal.

Unaware.

Safe.

At least that's how it looked.

But Aidan couldn't stop thinking about the message.

---

**HELP ME.**

**HE'S COMING.**

---

The second line shouldn't have existed.

He knew it hadn't been there.

He would have noticed.

There was no way he could have missed it.

Which meant one of two things.

Either someone entered the classroom after him.

Or reality itself was changing.

---

The second possibility scared him more.

---

That night he sat at his desk staring at a notebook.

For the first time since finding the Crimson Book, he began organizing everything he knew.

---

**Lyra exists.**

**School records say she doesn't.**

**The System wants me to find her.**

**I can see fragments of hidden reality.**

**Something else may be involved.**

---

Then he stopped writing.

His eyes drifted toward the final note.

---

**HE'S COMING**

---

"Who?"

The question slipped out naturally.

---

The System responded immediately.

---

> **UNKNOWN**

---

Aidan nearly jumped.

"Stop doing that."

---

> **REQUEST DENIED**

---

For a moment he almost laughed.

Almost.

---

Then the lights flickered.

---

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

---

The room darkened.

---

Aidan froze.

---

The air changed.

---

Every instinct inside him screamed.

Not fear.

Danger.

Pure danger.

---

The feeling lasted only two seconds.

Then it vanished.

The lights stabilized.

The pressure disappeared.

Everything returned to normal.

---

But Aidan remained frozen.

---

Because during those two seconds—

he had felt watched.

---

Not observed.

Hunted.

---

The difference mattered.

---

Slowly he stood.

Walked to his bedroom window.

Looked outside.

---

Nothing.

Just rain.

Cars.

People.

---

Then he noticed someone standing across the street.

---

A man.

---

Black coat.

Tall.

Still.

---

Watching his apartment building.

---

Aidan squinted.

Trying to make out his face.

---

The streetlight flickered.

---

For half a second—

the man's face disappeared.

---

Not hidden.

Gone.

---

A blank void occupied the space where features should have been.

---

Then the light stabilized.

And his face returned.

Normal.

Human.

---

Aidan stepped backward.

His pulse exploded.

---

"Nope."

---

The word escaped before he could stop it.

---

The instant he spoke—

the man looked directly at him.

---

Their eyes met.

---

Aidan felt ice crawl through his veins.

---

The stranger smiled.

---

Then turned.

And walked away.

---

Not hurried.

Not startled.

---

As though he had already accomplished what he came to do.

---

Aidan stood motionless for several minutes.

---

Eventually he whispered:

"System."

---

> **ACTIVE**

---

"Did you see that?"

---

Silence.

---

Then:

---

> **AFFIRMATIVE**

---

Aidan swallowed.

---

"What was he?"

---

Long pause.

Longer than usual.

---

The answer arrived slowly.

---

> **UNAUTHORIZED OBSERVER DETECTED**

---

Aidan frowned.

"Meaning?"

---

> **NOT PART OF CURRENT VERDICT**

---

That wasn't reassuring.

Not even slightly.

---

"So somebody's watching me?"

---

> **AFFIRMATIVE**

---

"Why?"

---

Silence.

---

Then:

---

> **INVESTIGATION RECOMMENDED**

---

Aidan stared at the ceiling.

---

"You're completely useless."

---

> **STATEMENT RECORDED**

---

Aidan groaned.

---

Yet beneath the fear...

something else was happening.

---

The System wasn't warning him.

It wasn't protecting him.

It wasn't intervening.

---

Which meant this was part of the test.

---

Whatever Zee had built...

it expected him to face danger.

Not avoid it.

---

The realization made him uncomfortable.

Because suddenly he understood something important.

---

The System was never designed to raise a hero.

---

It was designed to forge a survivor.

---

And survivors paid prices.

---

Outside, thunder rolled across the city.

---

Far away.

Near the river.

Near the oldest parts of New Orleans.

Near forgotten tunnels and buried secrets.

---

A young woman sat alone beneath a broken church.

Dark hair.

Gray hoodie.

Pale skin.

---

Lyra.

---

She stared at an old stone wall covered in strange symbols.

---

Then suddenly—

she looked up.

---

Directly toward the city.

Toward Aidan.

Though miles separated them.

---

Her expression changed.

---

Hope.

Fear.

Recognition.

---

"Someone found the trail."

---

The words barely escaped her lips.

---

Then her face hardened.

Because she felt it too.

---

Something had awakened.

---

Something that wasn't supposed to notice Aidan yet.

---

And if it reached him before she did...

the first quest of the Crimson Legacy might become a death sentence.

---

Meanwhile, deep within the hidden architecture of the Bloodbound Verdict System...

far behind layers of sealed authority...

a sleeping presence shifted again.

---

Not awake.

Not conscious.

---

But closer.

---

Much closer.

---

And for the briefest moment—

a single thought drifted through the darkness.

---

*...kid better not get himself killed before I wake up...*

---

Then silence returned.
