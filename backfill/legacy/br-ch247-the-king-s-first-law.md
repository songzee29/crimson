One month later...

New Orleans had begun healing.

The buildings could be rebuilt.

The streets could be cleaned.

But names...

Names were harder to repair.

Everywhere Aidan walked...

People recognized him.

Not because they knew his face.

Because they remembered the night crimson lightning split the heavens.

The night one young man carried another through a burning city.

---

The warehouse had become quieter.

Not sad.

Just...

Different.

Malik was making breakfast.

He burned the bacon.

Again.

Lyra laughed.

"You literally did this yesterday."

"I like consistency."

Sienna rolled her eyes.

"The smoke detector likes you too."

For the first time in weeks...

A small smile appeared on Aidan's face.

Not because the pain was gone.

Because Elias would've laughed at this.

---

The smile disappeared as the emergency phone rang.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

The Guardian looked up.

"...That's impossible."

Aidan stood.

"What is it?"

The Guardian answered quietly.

"Only one place uses that emergency frequency."

"Where?"

He looked directly at Aidan.

"St. Louis Cathedral."
