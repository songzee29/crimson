The Sovereign slowly stood.

No aura.

No dramatic speech.

Just presence.

The pressure inside the room became almost unbearable.

Even the walls groaned.

He looked Aidan directly in the eyes.

"...You possess power."

"...But you fight like a newborn."

Without warning...

He disappeared.

Aidan reacted instantly.

Too late.

A fist slammed into his stomach.

BOOOOOOM!!

Aidan crashed through the mansion.

Through stone.

Through ancient oak trees.

Across half a mile of swamp.

The impact carved a trench through the earth.

For the first time since his resurrection...

Someone had physically overpowered him.

Hands behind his back.

"...Stand."

No mockery.

No hatred.

Just expectation.

"...Again."

Blood dripped from his mouth.

His ribs regenerated immediately.

He smiled.

"...Interesting."
