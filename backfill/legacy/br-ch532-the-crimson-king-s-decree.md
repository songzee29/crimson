The Ash King launched another relentless assault.

This time...

Aidan didn't counter with brute force.

He planted one foot firmly against the fractured stone.

His voice rolled through the Hall of Names.

> **"By the Crimson King's Authority..."**

The crimson runes beneath his feet ignited.

> **"...This ground shall remember every soul that stands upon it."**

The arena shook.

The Hall responded.

Names that had begun fading on the towering pillars stopped disappearing.

Some even grew brighter.

The Ash King's eyes widened.

Not in fear.

In genuine surprise.

"...Impossible."

The Crimson Legacy appeared once more.
