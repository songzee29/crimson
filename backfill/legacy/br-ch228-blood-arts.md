The Sovereign vanished.

Not with speed.

With mastery.

Even Predator Sense lost him.

Aidan's crimson eyes searched the battlefield.

Nothing.

Silence.

Then—

Pain.

A fist buried itself into Aidan's ribs.

CRACK!!

Three ribs shattered.

Before he could recover—

Another strike.

His jaw snapped sideways.

A kick launched him through the plantation.

Stone exploded.

Trees collapsed.

The swamp trembled.

---

The Sovereign calmly walked toward him.

"You rely..."

"...on strength."

He kicked Aidan again.

"...I..."

Another kick.

"...Master..."

Another.

"...Blood."

---

Aidan rose.

His broken bones were already healing.

He wiped blood from his mouth.

Then...

He laughed.

Not loudly.

Just enough for the Sovereign to hear.

"...Good."
