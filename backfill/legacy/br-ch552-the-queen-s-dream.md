Lyra found Aidan watching the city.

She wrapped her arms around him from behind.

"You've been quiet."

"I've been thinking."

"Dangerous."

Aidan laughed.

"You've been spending too much time with Rez."

She smiled.

"Maybe."

Then her expression softened.

"I had another dream."

Aidan turned toward her.

"The same one?"

She nodded.

"There were three children..."

She looked toward the horizon.

"They were running through the castle gardens."

"The oldest carried a wooden sword."

"The second chased wolves through the forest."

"And our little girl..."

Lyra laughed softly.

"...She was teaching grown witches what they were doing wrong."

Aidan couldn't help but smile.

He already knew those names.

The future heirs of House Darkheart.

Prince **QuinZ**.

Prince **Zuko**.

Princess **Azula**.

The future still felt distant.

But for the first time...

It also felt possible.
