"Join me."

He looked at the millions of forgotten names carved into the pillars.

Then at the countless empty faces wandering outside.

He understood.

The Ash King wasn't lying.

He truly believed he was saving them.

But Aidan also saw the cost.

Lives without choice.

Memories without freedom.

Existence without hope.

"No."

The Ash King tilted his head.

"...Why?"

> **"Because people deserve to be remembered..."**

He looked toward the forgotten souls.

> **"...while they are still living."**

Another step.

> **"Not after everything that makes them who they are has been taken away."**

Then...

The Ash King closed his eyes.

Not in anger.

In disappointment.

"So..."

"...your answer is war."

"My answer..."

"...is freedom."

The Ash King opened his eyes.

For the first time...

The forgotten fortress shook from its foundations.

The first true battle between the King of Ash...

And the Crimson King...
