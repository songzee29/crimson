The ancient chalice hovered between Aidan and the partially opened Gates.

Its crimson contents remained perfectly still.

No ripple.

No movement.

Only an impossible scent.

It overwhelmed every instinct inside the First Crimson Vampire.

His fangs lengthened.

His pupils narrowed.

The second heartbeat pounded violently against the silence.

His blood reserves were nearly exhausted.

His regeneration had slowed.

Every wound from the Blood Trials remained open.

The chamber had waited until he reached his limit.

Only then had it presented its offer.

Aidan reached forward.

The instant his fingers touched the chalice—

The cathedral vanished.

Darkness swallowed everything.

No walls.

No floor.

No ceiling.

Only endless crimson mist.

His hand remained wrapped around the ancient cup.

A voice never spoke.

Yet understanding flooded directly into his instincts.

The Offering was not power.

It was permission.

Permission to continue.

Permission to survive the Gates.

Aidan lifted the chalice.

His humanity was gone.

There was no hesitation.

He drank.

The blood struck him like liquid fire.

Every vein ignited.

His body convulsed.

Ancient blood collided with newborn Crimson blood.

His second heartbeat erupted with overwhelming force.

Pain unlike anything he had endured tore through every inch of his body.

Bones cracked.

Muscles tightened.

His vision became completely crimson.

Then…

Silence.

His wounds began closing.

Not instantly.

Steadily.

His blood reserves no longer felt empty.

The burning hunger retreated.

Not satisfied.

Fed.

For now.
