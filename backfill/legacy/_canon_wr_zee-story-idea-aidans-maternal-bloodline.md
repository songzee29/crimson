STORY IDEA — AIDAN’S MATERNAL BLOODLINE

This is a brainstorming idea only.

Nothing below is canon until officially approved and added to the Master Decree.

⸻

Core Idea

Aidan has already inherited and awakened his father’s bloodline through House Darkheart and the Crimson Legacy.

However…

Very little is known about his mother.

The idea is to explore Aidan’s maternal heritage rather than introducing another Authority or replacing his existing progression.

⸻

The Mother

Possible concept:

Aidan’s mother is the Queen of an ancient Siren civilization hidden beneath the ocean.

She rules an underwater kingdom that has remained isolated from the surface world for centuries.

⸻

Story Direction

After Aidan completes his current major journey, he slowly begins experiencing something different.

Examples:

* Feeling naturally drawn toward oceans, rivers, and deep water.
* Feeling unusually calm while surrounded by water.
* Becoming stronger while submerged.
* The water itself seeming to recognize his presence.
* Ancient underwater magic reacting to him before he understands why.

Rather than learning a new power, the environment responds to his bloodline.

⸻

Underwater Kingdom

The underwater civilization eventually notices Aidan.

Something within their ancient magic recognizes the blood of their missing Queen.

At first, no one understands why.

The kingdom begins investigating the mysterious individual on the surface.

⸻

The Queen

One possibility is that Aidan’s mother is still alive.

If so, she may begin sensing someone carrying her blood.

She does not immediately realize that person is her son.

Instead, she only knows that someone with an impossible connection to her lineage exists.

⸻

Possible Conflict

Political tensions slowly rise between the Crimson Civilization and the underwater kingdom.

Neither ruler initially understands the true connection between them.

Different factions on both sides may encourage conflict before the truth is revealed.

The eventual revelation that the Crimson King and the Siren Queen are mother and son could become one of the emotional turning points of the story.

⸻

Worldbuilding Goals

This idea is intended to:

* Expand the Crimson Legacy Universe beyond the surface world.
* Introduce an entirely new civilization.
* Explore Aidan’s maternal heritage.
* Add political and emotional conflict instead of simply giving Aidan another transformation.

⸻

Questions Still Being Explored

1. Should Aidan’s mother be alive or deceased?
2. What makes Sirens unique within the Crimson Legacy Universe?

Examples to consider:

* Biology
* Culture
* Society
* Government
* Magic
* Military
* Relationship with the surface world

3. Should Sirens possess their own unique form of magic, or should their abilities be connected to existing magical systems?
4. Why did the Queen leave the surface world—or why was Aidan separated from her?
5. Why has no one in House Darkheart ever spoken about Aidan’s mother?
6. Why did the Crimson Legacy choose Aidan specifically? Was his unique combination of House Darkheart blood and Siren heritage necessary, or was it simply part of fate?
7. If the Queen is alive, why has she never searched for Aidan? Was she prevented, deceived, or did she believe he had died?
