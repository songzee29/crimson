Outside the plantation...

Hidden high among the ancient cypress trees...

A young man watched through binoculars.

No older than Aidan.

Black combat uniform marked with the Veil's crest.

A scar crossed one side of his face.

He lowered the binoculars.

"So..."

"The heir cries."

He smiled.

"Good."

Another hunter stepped beside him.

"Orders?"

The young man never looked away from the mansion.

"Not yet."

"He isn't ready."

The other hunter frowned.

"When do we strike?"

The young man slowly drew a silver blade.

He watched it reflect the moonlight.

Then quietly answered:

> "When he finally has something..."

A cold smile spread across his face.

> "...worth taking from him."

He sheathed the blade and disappeared into the trees.

The reader doesn't know his name.

Aidan doesn't know he exists.

But fate has already begun weaving their lives together.

One day...

This young Veil warrior will become the man who changes Aidan's life forever.

The man whose blade will fulfill the Witch Curse.

The man Aidan will one day call...

**His nemesis.**
