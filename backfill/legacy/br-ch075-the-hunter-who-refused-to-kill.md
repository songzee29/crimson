Across the street...

The Veil observer lowered his binoculars.

"Interesting."

He looked toward another figure standing beside him.

A woman wearing the white coat of an Execution-class officer.

"He sensed me."

She nodded once.

"Earlier than expected."

"Do we proceed?"

She was silent for several moments.

Then she surprised him.

"No."

The observer frowned.

"But the Council—"

"The Council wants certainty."

She continued watching Aidan through the rain.

"I want understanding."

The observer looked confused.

"You think he's dangerous?"

She answered honestly.

"I think..."

"...he isn't dangerous yet."

She turned away.

"But if we push him too soon..."

"...we'll create the very thing Zee feared."

Those words lingered in the cold night air.
