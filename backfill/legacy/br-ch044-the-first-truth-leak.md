The vision sharpened for half a second.

The woman’s face almost fully formed.

Tear-streaked.

Panicked.

Protective.

The child reached forward—

And Aidan felt something snap inside his chest.

Not memory.

Recognition without memory.

Lyra saw it.

“Aidan… what is it?”

He whispered:

“I think I knew them.”

Then the vision collapsed violently.

The subway room returned.

But Aidan didn’t.

Not fully.

He stood there breathing heavily, like he had just survived something without understanding what it was.

Lyra stared at him.

Because now she knew.

The System didn’t just take something from him.

It left something behind.

A direction.

A missing bond that still behaved like it was real.

And somewhere deep in Aidan’s fractured core—

something finally responded properly for the first time.

Not a memory.

Not a full thought.

Just one lingering truth-shaped instinct:

> *Find them.*
