## Zee Darkheart

- **Full name:** Zee Darkheart
- **Role:** Protagonist of *Crimson Dynasty* (the prequel); the First Tribrid King; the Vampire King. Zee is gay.
- **True nature (locked — report Part 5):** **First Natural Tribrid** — Witch, Vampire, and Wolf natures active **from birth**. **Dormant Dragon blood** from mother Aurelia = a 4th natural blood nature, NOT awakened at the start. **Age 10:** Dragon signs appear — crimson-black horns, permanent Crimson scales spreading with emotion/danger, Dragon energy; no adult Dragon form/wings yet. Zareth's Teal-and-Gold Dragon Mana + Dragon Link **catalyze** (not give) the dormant blood. Powers develop through **Crimson Evolutions**; never copies others' powers. Eventual revelation: the **First Natural Abysscin** in his true **Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix form** — a unified nature, not a collection of traits.
- **Power limits (locked):** Cannot permanently die after Victor's newborn attempt (one-time event, never repeated as plot device). Time/age power changes a LIVING person's biological age only — cannot undo history, reverse death, or bring Aurelia back (she dies). Crimson magic is Zee's alone, not House Darkheart's. Dragon blood distinct from Zareth's lineage. Evolutions must be EARNED through story.
- **Key traits / powers:** Unkillable — "Nature hated loopholes"; stakes can't kill him, magic barely contains him, wolf venom strengthens him, death itself rejected him. Crimson mana (unknown, leaks at birth, feared). Carries **Veyrath-derived Dragon blood**. Big evil villain vibes: kill first, ask questions later — but never hurts his own people. Dark phases never target his inner circle (Ner, Rez, Zareth, Kael, Tessa, Amara, family/close friends are absolute no-target boundaries).
- **Key relationships:** Father of three sons — Aidan Seyrion (with Queen Nymeris Thalryn — chosen family, not romantic), Zayrlen Keal and Azren Neyron (with Ner Azrion — Zee's lover; no love triangle). Closest/childhood best friend: Rez. One of the "Two Princes" (with Rez) under the Two Kings (Victor Darkheart, Lucien Vesper).
- **Status:** Alive; age 34 in Dynasty's main timeline. Per Zee (Oct 12, 2026), he will NOT be around for the later part of Aidan's life (when Aidan's own children are born).
