## 11. Zee's True Nature (locked — report Part 5, Oct 12, 2026)

- **First Natural Tribrid:** Witch, Vampire, and Wolf natures are active **from birth**.
- **Dormant Dragon blood** from mother Aurelia = a 4th natural blood nature, but it is **NOT awakened at the start**.
- **Age 10 — Dragon signs appear:** crimson-black horns, permanent Crimson scales that spread with emotion or danger, Dragon energy. **No adult Dragon form or adult Dragon wings yet.**

- Powers develop through **Crimson Evolutions**; Zee never copies others' powers.
- **Eventual revelation:** Zee is revealed as the **First Natural Abysscin** in his true **Crimson Black Abyss Phoenix form** — a unified nature, not a collection of separate traits.

## 12. Power Limits (locked — report Part 5)

- Zee **cannot permanently die** after Victor's newborn attempt. That attempt is a **one-time event** — never repeated as a plot device.
- **Time/age power:** changes a LIVING person's biological age only. It **cannot** undo history, reverse a death, or bring Aurelia back (she dies).
- **Crimson magic is Zee's alone** — not a general power of House Darkheart.
- Zee's Dragon blood is **distinct from Zareth's Dragon lineage**.
- New supernatural evolutions must be **EARNED through the story**, never granted without consequences.
- These limits preserve tension even when Zee becomes extraordinarily powerful.

## 13. The 8-Step Story Arc (locked direction — report Part 4)

1. Zee as a feared child.
2. The attempt on his life and his survival.
3. His pursuit by people who fear or want to control him.
4. His development from a hunted survivor into someone capable of ruling.
5. His relationships with Rez and Ner.
6. His growing understanding of love, loyalty, and family.
7. His eventual position as a powerful ruler.
8. His decision to protect his family by **concealing one of his sons**.

**Dynasty must stand ALONE.** Its major events and emotional resolutions cannot depend on readers knowing Aidan's future. It must not become Aidan's retelling or constantly reference Legacy's future events. **Zee remains the protagonist; the narrative stays focused on his experiences.**

## 14. Master Decree vs Dynasty Codex (locked — report Part 4)

- **Master Decree:** locked canon and rules; numbered decrees; protects established facts from accidental changes; corrections respected when new canon supersedes older material.
- **Dynasty Codex:** evolving character profiles; tracks relationships and timelines; stores story arcs, notes, and **ideas** — not locked canon.
- **Brainstorming ≠ canon.** A suggestion never automatically becomes a permanent story fact.
