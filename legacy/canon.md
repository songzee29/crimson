## 2. The System and the Legacy Are Separate

**Locked canon (Zee's correction, Jul 8, 2026):** the Crimson System and the Crimson Legacy are two **separate** things joined as one.

- **The System = the interface.** It informs, records, delivers, teaches. It does NOT command. Style: ancient, elegant, Crimson — never generic RPG. Milestone messages take decree form, e.g. **"CRIMSON RECORD UPDATED."**
- **The Legacy = the intelligence.** It judges and decides what the System is allowed to grant, reveal, or withhold.

**RULE #1: Only Aidan can see or hear the System.** He must **never** reveal it to his friends. Others may only notice him pausing or staring at empty space. (A draft that had Aidan telling his friends was a continuity violation — struck from canon.)

**The System's behavior across arcs (System Evolution Directive, locked Jul 8, 2026):**
- **Witch Arc:** System fully active — quests, updates, completion notices, hidden quests, rewards, Witch Laws, skills, abilities, tutorials, authority progression, founder and historical records. It **grants powers and abilities**; rewards are not limited to power.
- **Vampire Arc:** after Aidan's first true death, the Vampire Authority unlocks; Rez awakens at the constitutional moment.
- **Wolf Arc:** the System **stops granting power** and shifts to teaching mastery — blueprints, Founder Techniques, combat records — toward the complete Crimson Trinity. This shift happens only when Aidan becomes a full tribrid.

## 10. The Chapter Quality Control Directive

A locked file in ChatGPT's library ("CRIMSON LEGACY CHAPTER QUALITY CONTROL DIRECTIVE," Sep 28, 2026) — the final silent checkpoint before writing **every** chapter. Eight phases:

1. **Verify Canon** — check Master Decree, Codex, Creator's Handbook, Master Index, Locked Expansion Directives, current manuscript, in that order; higher document wins; never invent replacement canon.
2. **Verify the Story** — confirm book, arc, arc phase, chapter, scene, quest, emotional focus, mystery, location, timeline; never guess.
3. **Verify the Characters** — personalities consistent, relationships reflect history, nothing forced, natural growth.
4. **Verify the Crimson System** — System matches the story stage; visible ONLY to Aidan; Aidan NEVER reveals it.
5. **Verify Pacing** — every chapter must advance at least one of: main plot, quest, character growth, relationships, authority progression, mystery, worldbuilding, next milestone — otherwise rewrite it.
6. **Verify Continuity** — continue directly from the previous chapter/scene; no unnecessary time skips or unrelated scenes.
7. **Verify New Content** — nothing new (organizations, events, lore, locations, powers, races, mysteries, prophecies, side stories) unless already in canon or author-approved; when uncertain, do less, never improvise.
8. **Final Inspection** — 14-point checklist (Decree, Codex, Handbook, Index, locked directives, scene continued, arc & quest progressed, character consistency, System used correctly, emotional payoff, no filler, no continuity errors, no Writers' Room behavior).

**FINAL LAW:** *"Book Chat exists for one purpose: Write the novel. Nothing else… So let it be written. So let it be done."*

## 18. The Seven Laws of the Timeline (verbatim, from the Master Record, Oct 12, 2026)

1. **History Cannot Be Forgotten.** Official events receive permanent historical records.
2. **History Cannot Contradict Itself.** Established history cannot be rewritten without an official amendment.
3. **History Is Continuous.** Events must connect naturally, and unexplained gaps must be addressed.
4. **History Creates Legacy.** Every generation inherits consequences from those before it.
5. **History Has Perspective.** Historical records and character experiences may describe the same event differently.
6. **History Is Alive.** The timeline expands as the universe develops.
7. **History Is Permanent.** Official canon does not simply disappear.

These laws govern continuity across multiple books and generations. In particular: **Law 2** forbids re-resolving the same conflict without a formal amendment (see §23), and **Law 3** is what makes chapter gaps (§16's missing ranges and the Ch 80 question) addressable rather than ignorable.

## 23. Continuity Rules (from the Master Record, Oct 12, 2026)

- **Status rules:** character records must distinguish **current status** vs **future locked events** vs **historical accomplishments** vs **planned transformations** vs **unresolved questions**. Examples: Lyra is alive at the Witch Arc ending even though her later death and resurrection are locked future events; Elias is alive at the Witch Arc ending even though older Codex text once described his death.
- **System rules:** the System preserves progress and records milestones. Authority percentages, System messages, completed trials, and unlocked abilities must agree with the chapter timeline. **A completed milestone must not be repeated** as though Aidan never achieved it. **An ability must not appear before its established awakening** unless the canon is explicitly amended.
- **Chapter rules:** preserve the completed chapter's actual ending; distinguish a proposed outline from a finished chapter; keep future locked events in their proper order; carry character relationships forward; do not revive a superseded death or transformation; **do not invent missing chapters** to fill a gap; use the latest approved continuity recap when moving to a new chat.
- **Canon-correction rule:** the **latest locked Master Decree supersedes older conflicting material**. The clearest recorded example is Elias's survival: older Codex entries describing Elias as dead cannot override the later decree restoring his survival. The latest formally approved canon determines the story's current truth.
