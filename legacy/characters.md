## AIDAN SEYRION DARKHEART

- **Role:** Protagonist of *Crimson Legacy*. Heir of the Crimson.
- **Key traits/powers:** Crimson Witch (Witch Authority complete); Crimson Vampire (Authority unlocks after his first true death and rebirth); wolf side dormant by design. Wolf fur = crimson; eyes = crimson-and-gold. Performs the Dark Blooding. The only person who can see/hear the Crimson System — and he must never reveal it (RULE #1).
- **Book Two progression (from Zee's activity report, Jul 19–20, 2026 — recorded, not final):** became the **First Crimson Vampire** (humanity not yet activated); completed the **Ancient Gates** and **Blood Trials**; accepted the **Crimson Offering**; defeated the **Guardian**; endured the **King's Judgment**; reached **55% Authority**; completed **Authority Stabilization**; gained **Crimson Dominion** and **Greater Regeneration**; recognized as **Heir of Ancient Gates**. Next: ~2-year jump into Ch 101, transition into the "Years of Blood" arc.
- **Key relationships:** secretly the son of Zee Darkheart and Queen Nymeris Thalryn (oldest of Zee's three sons); anchor-bonded to Lyra (their sacred bond seals the Wolf Curse); permanent antagonist Cassian Vaelthorne; companions Malik, Elias, Rowan; uncle-figure Rez inside the System Book.
- **Titles/status:** Founder of Crimson Civilization (with Queen Lyra, of the modern Crimson Bloodline); King Aidan Darkheart; the Heir. Age varies across drafts (16 / 19 / 21) — canonical age at each point UNVERIFIED. Has brothers he never knew existed (Zayrlen, Azren) — he is hidden in Vesper, offstage from them.

## LYRA VEYNE / QUEEN LYRA DARKHEART

- **Role:** Aidan's anchor and partner; the future First Crimson Queen.
- **Key traits/powers:** Crimson Witch. Becomes the living seal of the Wolf Curse through her sacred bond with Aidan — her death breaks the seal forever (Decrees 12, 42, 43). After the Dark Blooding: the **First Crimson Runefang** (inherits Crimson Witch Blood + Crimson Wolf Blood; Decree Law III). "No Crimson Wolf existed before her."
- **Key relationships:** Aidan (sacred bond); killed by Cassian (Judgment of Revenge), dies in Aidan's arms; returns via Aidan's Dark Blooding; later in Aidan's life, mother of his children QuinZ, Zuko, Azula.
- **Titles/status:** Queen Lyra Darkheart, the Wolf Queen, the Crimson Huntress, Guardian of the Crimson Heir.
- **Timing restriction (Master Record, Oct 12, 2026):** alive at the end of the Witch Arc — her later death, resurrection, and Runefang transformation must NOT be written before their intended future storyline. She helps establish the Crimson Pack.

## CASSIAN VAELTHORNE

- **Role:** The permanent antagonist. Never redeemed; never Aidan's friend again. *(Older lock — canon.md §4.)*
- **Master Record (Oct 12, 2026):** Cassian's story includes a **path toward redemption**; his established titles include **The Cursed One** and **The First Crimson Demon Hexborn**. At the Witch Arc ending he is **alive**, having resisted the Curse's accusations by remembering that Aidan chose him. Later history: kills Lyra during the **Third Judgment**; becomes the First Crimson Demon Hexborn when Aidan breaks the Darkheart Curse. Those later events must remain in their intended timeline. **⚠️ Tension with the older "permanent antagonist, never redeemed" lock — needs Zee's ruling (canon.md §12, item 10).**
- **Key traits/powers:** Crimson Demon → the **First Crimson Demon Hexborn** (Ch 177; registered via True Crimson Blood at the breaking of the Darkheart Curse).
- **Key relationships:** bound by Aidan's Final Decree — the Darkheart Curse's Five Judgments (Fear; Loss; Revenge — he kills Lyra; Reflection — Aidan harms his child; Acceptance — he begs Aidan for death). Phase 3: kills Lyra. NOT Aidan's parent (locked ruling — "they're not even in the same book yet").
- **Status:** antagonist; "Cassian's punishment" is on the unwritten vampire-arc ending checklist.

## MALIK THORN

- **Role:** Companion; professor at Blackwood Academy; brother of Elias.
- **Master Record (Oct 12, 2026):** **"The Unshaken Shield."** A loyal companion who helps defend Blackwood Academy and stands beside Aidan when others cannot. During the Witch Arc's conclusion, Malik **survives** and helps break the Curse. His loyalty, courage, and ability to stand in the present rather than surrender to past failure are important to Aidan's victory.
- **Key traits/powers:** survived Book One; one of the pillars who helped break the Witch Curse (alternate track); heard his father's voice during the curse's grief attacks.
- **Key relationships:** Elias (brother — "My brother called me too"; they pledge to follow each other).
- **Status:** alive.

## ELIAS THORNE / ELIAS BLACKWOOD

- **Role:** Headmaster of Blackwood Academy; companion.
- **Key traits/powers:** survived Book One; warned Aidan that the System is waking faster than expected; chose character over power during the curse — stayed, supported, anchored, refused to abandon his brother. Planned future: Crimson Hexborn of a different identity — scholar/researcher/barrier specialist; leading title **"First Royal Crimson Scholar"** (candidates: Keeper of Knowledge, Royal Archivist, Master Runesmith, Guardian of the Living Archive, Architect of the Crimson Academy, Founder of the Royal Library).
- **Key relationships:** Malik (brother); Aidan (mentor-adjacent authority).
- **Status:** alive — **was NOT killed by Cassian** (locked correction, Jul 8, 2026; codex v1.1).
- **Master Record future titles (Oct 12, 2026):** Royal Archivist, Keeper of Knowledge, Master Runesmith, Barrier Scholar, Architect of the Royal Library, Guardian of Crimson Records. **His future development must NOT copy Rez's role** — Rez is the Founder-era Crimson Hexborn and assassin; Elias is intended to become the **scholarly Hexborn of Aidan's age**.
- **⚠️ Surname question:** appears as both "Elias Thorne" (Malik's brother) and "Elias Blackwood" (Headmaster) across threads — UNVERIFIED whether these are the same person or a naming inconsistency. Needs Zee's ruling.

## ROWAN BLACKWOOD

- **Role:** Aidan's teacher — **Master Rowan**, the **Founder Scholar**, the **Last Instructor** (Master Record, Oct 12, 2026). *(Earlier sheet described him as a Blackwood Academy student; the Master Record supersedes this — see canon.md §23 canon-correction rule.)*
- **Master Record (Oct 12, 2026):** an important member of Aidan's chosen family. He helps Aidan understand the laws of Witchcraft and prepares him to inherit the Crimson Legacy. **"Rowan did not simply teach Aidan magic. He helped raise the heart strong enough to survive it."** At the end of the Witch Arc, Rowan **survives and embraces Aidan as family** — he recognizes Aidan no longer needs him in the same way, but Aidan makes clear that Rowan will always matter to him.
- **Key traits/powers:** midnight-black hair; chosen by the System as important; seems to know Aidan's name before they meet; shows him the second crimson box (House Vesper's silver-and-black crest); reveals the Vespers once swore an oath to protect the Darkheart line; teaches Aidan's near-final Witch lessons (the curse only twists what lives in the heart; it is defeated by love and truth; "the Crimson Witch exists so others can keep living"); survived Book One.
- **Key relationships:** Aidan (guide/confidante); House Vesper connection.
- **Status:** alive.

## ZEE DARKHEART

- **Role:** Aidan's father; protagonist of the prequel *Crimson Dynasty* (see Dynasty bible). "The First Tribrid King" — first tribrid (vampire + witch + werewolf), unkillable ("Nature hated loopholes").
- **Key traits/powers:** kingdom beneath New Orleans; stakes can't kill him, magic barely contains him, wolf venom strengthens him, death rejected him; absorbed Seraphine's magic. His rule: **don't hurt his own people; kill everyone else who threatens them — no investigation first.**
- **Key relationships:** father of three sons — **Aidan Seyrion Darkheart** (oldest; with Queen Nymeris Thalryn), **Zayrlen Keal Darkheart** and **Azren Neyron Darkheart** (with Ner Azrion); partner of Ner Azrion; closest friend Rez. Grandfather of QuinZ, Zuko, Azula (Aidan & Lyra's children — he will NOT be around for that part of Aidan's life).
- **Status:** central to Dynasty; his shadow looms over Legacy (unwritten vampire-arc ending checklist item: "Zee's shadow"). Legacy canon treats him as deceased-but-influential by Aidan's era.
- **⚠️ Note:** Legacy's core rule is that Dynasty comes first — anything in Legacy touching Zee's story must wait for Dynasty to be finished.

## REZ

- **Role:** Aidan's uncle-figure; Zee's closest/childhood best friend; Soul-Linked protector of the Crimson Bloodline.
- **Master Record classifications (Oct 12, 2026):** **Crimson Assassin**; **Guardian of the Crimson Legacy**; **Keeper of the Founder**; **Soulbound Companion**; the **FIRST Crimson Witch–Vampire created by Zee** — he predates the Crimson Civilization. Carries knowledge from the Founder's Age; guides, protects, advises, and questions Aidan but **does not rule him** — Aidan retains the final decision. Eventually **regains his physical body while retaining the Soul Link**, recovers his lost memories, and returns to active service. A living witness to both Zee's founding era and Aidan's rise.
- **Key traits/powers:** blue-white mana (House Vesper signature — "A witch who cannot master their mind cannot master their mana"). Exists within the System Book and inside Aidan's mind; the voice of discipline against the bloodlust (forces Aidan to control himself and remember why he came). Awakens after the Vampire Curse — "Rez awakens at the constitutional moment." Identified in the alternate Drakhar continuity as **"The Last Keeper"** (BLOODLINE DETECTED, ACCESS RESTRICTED) — that continuity's canonicity is UNVERIFIED.
- **Key relationships:** Zee (best friend); Aidan (uncle, protector, inner voice).
- **Status:** dormant until the Vampire Arc's constitutional moment.
- **⚠️ NOT Zee's son.** Locked correction, Oct 12, 2026.
