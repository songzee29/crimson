# Job Posting — Continuity Assistant (Upwork-ready draft)

**Title:** Fiction Continuity Assistant / Lore Keeper for Fantasy Novel Series

**About the project:**
I'm the author of an epic fantasy saga — two interconnected series (a prequel, *Crimson Dynasty*, and the main saga, *Crimson Legacy*) spanning hundreds of chapters, multiple historical ages, complex bloodline/magic systems, and a large cast. The world bibles are extensive living documents. I need someone obsessive about consistency to keep them clean.

**What you'll do:**
- Maintain two series bibles (canon, characters, chapter maps) from my chapter drafts and notes
- Flag continuity conflicts (timeline, powers, relationships, established rules) before they spread
- Track which chapters exist, which are missing, and what's unverified
- Prepare continuity recaps before new writing sessions
- Keep a clean distinction: locked canon vs proposals vs unresolved mysteries — never invent missing content

**You must have:**
- Extreme attention to detail — you catch contradictions others miss
- Love of epic fantasy; experience with long series, TTRPG lore-keeping, wiki editing, or fandom archival work all count
- Comfortable working in long documents (Google Docs / Markdown)
- Reliable and communicative; you meet deadlines

**Nice to have:**
- Experience as a beta reader, editor, or writing assistant
- Familiarity with worldbuilding-heavy fantasy (Brandon Sanderson, Malazan, etc.)

**Logistics:**
- Part-time to start: ~5–10 hrs/week, ongoing
- Fully remote, async-friendly
- Long-term role if it's a good fit — this saga is years of work

**To apply:**
1. Tell me about the most satisfying continuity catch you've ever made (in any story).
2. Brief note on your fantasy background — reader, writer, lore-keeper, all welcome.
3. Your hourly rate and weekly availability.

I'll send shortlisted candidates a short paid trial: a sample chapter + bible excerpt with 2–3 planted contradictions to find.
