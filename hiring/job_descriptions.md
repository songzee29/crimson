# Hiring Plan — Crimson Projects

Prepared Oct 12, 2026. Goal: build a small team to keep Dynasty/Legacy moving while the ChatGPT export is pending.

## Roles

### 1. Continuity Assistant (hire FIRST — highest leverage)
**Why:** catches exactly the contradictions we've been finding (Cassian redemption, chapter numbering, duplicate characters). Keeps the bibles current.
**Does:**
- Maintains the Dynasty and Legacy bibles from chapter drafts
- Flags canon conflicts before they spread
- Tracks which chapters exist vs are missing
- Prepares continuity recaps for new writing sessions
**Must-have:** obsessive attention to detail, fantasy-genre familiarity, comfortable with long documents.
**Typical rates:** ~$20–50/hr freelance (Upwork). Part-time is fine to start.

### 2. Editor
**Why:** chapter drafts need professional eyes before they stack up.
**Does:**
- Developmental feedback (pacing, character arcs) on new chapters
- Copy editing / proofreading on locked chapters
**Must-have:** fantasy editing experience, sample edit before hiring.
**Typical rates:** developmental ~$0.03–0.08/word; copyediting ~$0.02–0.04/word. (Rough estimates — get quotes.)

### 3. Cover Artist / Illustrator
**Why:** the series already has book-art direction; a real artist takes it to publishable quality.
**Does:** ebook/print covers, character art, series branding consistency.
**Must-have:** portfolio with fantasy covers.
**Typical rates:** ~$200–800+ per cover depending on experience. (Rough estimate.)

### 4. Beta Readers (3–5 people)
**Why:** real-reader feedback on whether chapters land emotionally.
**Does:** reads chapters, answers guided questions (no editing required).
**Must-have:** actually reads fantasy, meets deadlines.
**Typical rates:** usually free/volunteer (r/BetaReaders, Goodreads groups, Discord writing communities).

## Where to post
- **Continuity assistant:** Upwork (post as "Fiction continuity assistant / lore keeper")
- **Editor:** Reedsy (vetted publishing pros) or Upwork
- **Artist:** Reedsy, ArtStation, Fiverr (vet portfolios hard)
- **Beta readers:** r/BetaReaders, Goodreads Beta Reader groups

## Suggested order
1. Continuity assistant — unblocks everything else, cheapest way to stop canon drift.
2. Beta readers — free, start building the group now.
3. Editor — once new chapters are flowing.
4. Artist — when covers are needed.

## First team meeting (draft agenda, once hires are in)
1. Project status: Dynasty first, Legacy paused — why.
2. Current blockers: export watch, chapter gaps, open canon questions.
3. How we work: bibles are the source of truth; Book Chat = drafting only; no inventing missing chapters.
4. Roles and who's doing what.
5. Cadence: weekly check-in while export is pending.
6. Next actions with owners.
